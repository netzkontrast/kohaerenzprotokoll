## Definition

The Witness-Funktion includes the Quantum Entanglement Witness, which decisively distinguishes entangled states between alters. ^[Sources/drive/technical-audit-research-mandate-the-kohaerenz-protokoll-fra.md:33-33]
It also includes the Zero-Knowledge Verifier, which verifies Kael's trauma (the "Prover") without revealing the destructive raw data to AEGIS's surveillance. ^[Sources/drive/technical-audit-research-mandate-the-kohaerenz-protokoll-fra.md:34-34]
A third component, the Husserlian Spectator, observes the transcendental ego without triggering a wavefunction collapse. ^[Sources/drive/technical-audit-research-mandate-the-kohaerenz-protokoll-fra.md:35-35]
