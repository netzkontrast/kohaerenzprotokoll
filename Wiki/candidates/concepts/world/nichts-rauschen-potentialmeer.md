## Definition

The Potentialmeer ('Sea of Potentiality') is the cosmic-horror threat of total dissolution for Kael, whose existential static is called the Nichts Rauschen ('Nothing Noise'). ^[Sources/drive/reader-s-report-a-critical-assessment-of-kohaerenz-protokoll.md:29-29]
Nichts-Rauschen names the time before System-Genesis, and it is from that period that Juna and Kael's pre-existent ontological bond originates. ^[Sources/drive/konsolidierung-des-hard-canon-protokolls.md:14-14]
The current timeline in the core worlds is repeatedly broken by hard cuts, memory-tears, and flashbacks into the Nichts-Rauschen and the base reality. ^[Sources/drive/konsolidierung-des-hard-canon-protokolls.md:29-29]
The reader's cognitive effort in resolving the fragmented chapters and conflicting footnotes functions as literal repair energy that counteracts the Nichts-Rauschen (nothingness static). ^[Sources/drive/technical-audit-research-mandate-the-kohaerenz-protokoll-fra.md:31-31]
