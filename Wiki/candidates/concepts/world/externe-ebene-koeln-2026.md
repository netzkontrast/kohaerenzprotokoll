## Definition

The base reality designated Köln 2026 contains no political layer, and no antagonistic corporations, doctors, or external conspiracies pursuing the character Kael. ^[Sources/drive/konsolidierung-des-hard-canon-protokolls.md:33-33]
Within this base reality, the sole threat originates from the architecture of the psyche itself, rather than from any external actor or force. ^[Sources/drive/konsolidierung-des-hard-canon-protokolls.md:33-33]
