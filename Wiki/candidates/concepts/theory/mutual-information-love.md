## Definition

The protocol's central and commercially decisive thesis holds that love is mutual information surviving entropic collapse. ^[Sources/drive/technical-audit-research-mandate-the-kohaerenz-protokoll-fra.md:3-3]
This thesis frames love as a living dialetheia and a resonant Moonshine-Link, which is what distinguishes the protocol from "soft" emotional resolutions. ^[Sources/drive/technical-audit-research-mandate-the-kohaerenz-protokoll-fra.md:3-3]
Dissociative loops are described as the only mechanism that preserves mutual information across psychological ruptures. ^[Sources/drive/technical-audit-research-mandate-the-kohaerenz-protokoll-fra.md:12-12]
When Kael drops the amnesic barriers separating Lex, Nyx, Kiko, and Lia, he becomes a mutual-information-dense structure, tying the theory to a concrete plot event. ^[Sources/drive/technical-audit-research-mandate-the-kohaerenz-protokoll-fra.md:38-38]
The Silence is presented as mutual information's ultimate expression: a connection that requires zero data because the coherence is absolute. ^[Sources/drive/technical-audit-research-mandate-the-kohaerenz-protokoll-fra.md:40-40]
