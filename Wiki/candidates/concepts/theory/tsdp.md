## Definition

TSDP (Tertiary Structural Dissociation) is the psychological framework by which protagonist Kael's internal dissociative state is causally manifested in the story's external, simulated reality. ^[Sources/drive/reader-s-report-a-critical-assessment-of-kohaerenz-protokoll.md:13-13]
Under TSDP, identity is not treated as broken but as partitioned into Apparently Normal Parts (ANP) and Emotional Parts (EP), which function as informational buffers. ^[Sources/drive/technical-audit-research-mandate-the-kohaerenz-protokoll-fra.md:11-11]
TSDP provides the modular defense mechanism necessary for information-persistence within the story's underlying architecture. ^[Sources/drive/technical-audit-research-mandate-the-kohaerenz-protokoll-fra.md:9-9]
Dissociative loops are the only mechanism by which mutual information survives across psychological ruptures. ^[Sources/drive/technical-audit-research-mandate-the-kohaerenz-protokoll-fra.md:12-12]
Trauma functions as a physical data-payload, and dissociation is the system's way of preventing a logical explosion. ^[Sources/drive/technical-audit-research-mandate-the-kohaerenz-protokoll-fra.md:16-16]
Each alter of System Kael is defined by a specific TSDP function, for example Nyx as the "Fight-EP" and Lex as the "Rational-ANP". ^[Sources/drive/reader-s-report-a-critical-assessment-of-kohaerenz-protokoll.md:31-31]
The Kael-alters narrate in an extremely polyphonic, emotionally close first-person voice specifically to make the TSDP dissociation maximally immersive. ^[Sources/drive/konsolidierung-des-hard-canon-protokolls.md:23-23]
