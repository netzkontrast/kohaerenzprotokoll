## Definition

Autopoiesis is used in the work as one of its advanced, discipline-specific theoretical concepts, referring to self-contained, self-referentially closed systems. ^[Sources/drive/reader-s-report-a-critical-assessment-of-kohaerenz-protokoll.md:27-27]
In the hard-canon protocol, AEGIS itself is defined as the ultimate autopoietic manifestation: a self-contained system that carries out Kael's own system management and trauma defense. ^[Sources/drive/konsolidierung-des-hard-canon-protokolls.md:15-15]
