## Definition

AEGIS enforces an exclusionary Coherence Theory of Truth, under which reality is defined solely by internal consistency and any contradictory data is classified as "entropy" to be eliminated. ^[Sources/drive/reader-s-report-a-critical-assessment-of-kohaerenz-protokoll.md:15-15]
Kael, by contrast, is pushed toward an integrative Correspondence Theory of Truth, in which healing and stability come from accepting the messy, often contradictory truths of his trauma and multifaceted identity. ^[Sources/drive/reader-s-report-a-critical-assessment-of-kohaerenz-protokoll.md:15-15]
One reading frames the story's resolution as the triumph of Correspondence over Coherence, with Kael's integrated truth shattering AEGIS's internal, systemic delusion. ^[Sources/drive/report-reorienting-the-central-conflict-as-a-call-to-wholene.md:57-57]
A later, hard-canon reading instead holds that the novel's open, circular ending deconstructs the question of which truth theory is authentically real, closing the loop between trauma and healing rather than crowning a clear winner. ^[Sources/drive/konsolidierung-des-hard-canon-protokolls.md:34-34]
