## Definition

Identity is not "broken"; it is partitioned into Apparently Normal Parts (ANP) and Emotional Parts (EP), which function as informational buffers. ^[Sources/drive/technical-audit-research-mandate-the-kohaerenz-protokoll-fra.md:11-11]
ANPs manage daily life while EPs hold trauma, and a cooperative outcome is described as "functional multiplicity," in which both part-systems are integrated rather than kept apart. ^[Sources/drive/report-reorienting-the-central-conflict-as-a-call-to-wholene.md:53-53]
ANP/EP structures must maintain informational firewalls until the story's climax, keeping what each part-system knows separated until that point. ^[Sources/drive/technical-audit-research-mandate-the-kohaerenz-protokoll-fra.md:15-15]
