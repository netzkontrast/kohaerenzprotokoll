## Definition

The reader is the literal thermodynamic substrate of the Kohärenz-Protokoll, providing the K1 repair energy required to maintain the story's Mind. ^[Sources/drive/technical-audit-research-mandate-the-kohaerenz-protokoll-fra.md:29-29]
The reader's cognitive effort in resolving the 39 fragmented chapters and conflicting footnotes is the literal "repair energy" that counteracts the "Nichts-Rauschen" (nothingness static). ^[Sources/drive/technical-audit-research-mandate-the-kohaerenz-protokoll-fra.md:31-31]
The polyphonic pronouns and mosaic structure are not "flavor" but mechanical tools that force the reader to synthesize the K1 kernel. ^[Sources/drive/technical-audit-research-mandate-the-kohaerenz-protokoll-fra.md:36-36]
