## Definition

The 39-Kapitel-Matrix is the work's chapter-architecture rule, deriving its 39 chapters from a mathematical structure of 3 acts multiplied by 13 shares (Anteile). ^[Sources/drive/konsolidierung-des-hard-canon-protokolls.md:13-13]
An architectural mandate requires immediate implementation of the 39-chapter mosaic and directs that every "logical crack" in it be treated as a dialetheic truth rather than an error to be resolved. ^[Sources/drive/technical-audit-research-mandate-the-kohaerenz-protokoll-fra.md:40-40]
