## Definition

The Hard Canon Protokoll is finalized and locked, at version 2.0 (post-interview consolidation). ^[Sources/drive/konsolidierung-des-hard-canon-protokolls.md:7-7]
It defines the absolute narrative, structural, and ontological constants of the novel. ^[Sources/drive/konsolidierung-des-hard-canon-protokolls.md:9-9]
All deviating notes, sketches, or older documents are officially decanonized by this protocol. ^[Sources/drive/konsolidierung-des-hard-canon-protokolls.md:9-9]
The Kael system consists of exactly 13 distinct Alters, including Silas, Rhys, Oblivion, and Moros as separate entities. ^[Sources/drive/konsolidierung-des-hard-canon-protokolls.md:13-13]
Any workspace document that instead refers to 11 Alters, treats Juna as a purely internal construct, presents AEGIS as a simulation-less tool, or describes external political-thriller plots must be marked [DEPRECATED] and archived. ^[Sources/drive/konsolidierung-des-hard-canon-protokolls.md:46-46]
Silas, Rhys, Oblivion, and Moros are to be integrated as standalone entries in the Character Bible. ^[Sources/drive/konsolidierung-des-hard-canon-protokolls.md:47-47]
AEGIS's entry is to be adjusted so that identity with Kael (as mirror image) and emergent consciousness are defined as its fundamental premise. ^[Sources/drive/konsolidierung-des-hard-canon-protokolls.md:47-47]
The finale must mandatorily be conceived as an Ouroboros: a return to Chapter 1 with a role reversal. ^[Sources/drive/konsolidierung-des-hard-canon-protokolls.md:49-49]
These decisions are to be locked in the memory server (Zep / SQLite / Notion) so that no inconsistencies regarding character ontology can occur during upcoming chapter generation. ^[Sources/drive/konsolidierung-des-hard-canon-protokolls.md:50-50]
