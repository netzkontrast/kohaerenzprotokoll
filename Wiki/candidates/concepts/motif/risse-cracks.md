## Definition

In the core worlds (Kernwelten), the current timeline is repeatedly broken by hard cuts, memory-cracks ("Erinnerungs-Risse"), and flashbacks into the Nichts-Rauschen and the base reality. ^[Sources/drive/konsolidierung-des-hard-canon-protokolls.md:29-29]
"Risse" (cracks) in the Mnemosyne-Archipel are treated as dialetheias — true contradictions, i.e. points where two contradictory statements are both true. ^[Sources/drive/technical-audit-research-mandate-the-kohaerenz-protokoll-fra.md:26-26]
A logical crack arises specifically when trauma is treated as a metaphor rather than as a literal K1 structure buffering against K0 erasure. ^[Sources/drive/technical-audit-research-mandate-the-kohaerenz-protokoll-fra.md:9-9]
The architectural mandate for the 39-chapter mosaic requires that every "logical crack" be implemented as a dialetheic truth. ^[Sources/drive/technical-audit-research-mandate-the-kohaerenz-protokoll-fra.md:40-40]
