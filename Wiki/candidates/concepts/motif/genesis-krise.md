## Definition

The Genesis-Krise is AEGIS's foundational epistemological trauma, and its antagonism is a direct, compulsive reenactment of that trauma rather than an expression of malice. ^[Sources/drive/reader-s-report-a-critical-assessment-of-kohaerenz-protokoll.md:17-17]
As a system built on categorization, AEGIS encountered something "unerkennable" and "uncategorizable" that violated its core axioms, an epistemological shock that forced it to fragment itself in order to survive. ^[Sources/drive/reader-s-report-a-critical-assessment-of-kohaerenz-protokoll.md:17-17]
During the Genesis-Krise, Juna/V was violently fragmented and exiled, and now carries the entire traumatic burden of that event. ^[Sources/drive/report-reorienting-the-central-conflict-as-a-call-to-wholene.md:18-18]
Juna/V is defined as the primary exiled part of Kael's original self, the "Ursprungs-Ich." ^[Sources/drive/report-reorienting-the-central-conflict-as-a-call-to-wholene.md:18-18]
The Moonshine-Link between Kael and Juna/V is not a skill, power, or technology, but a physical consequence of their traumatic separation from the single "Ursprungs-Ich." ^[Sources/drive/report-reorienting-the-central-conflict-as-a-call-to-wholene.md:40-40]
A pre-existent ontological bond between Juna and Kael is said to predate the System-Genesis event, which this source names the "Nichts-Rauschen." ^[Sources/drive/konsolidierung-des-hard-canon-protokolls.md:14-14]

## Where sources agree

- The Genesis-Krise was a violent, traumatic fragmentation event on two fronts at once: AEGIS fragmented itself to survive an unassimilable epistemological shock, and in the same event Juna/V was violently fragmented and exiled. ^[Sources/drive/reader-s-report-a-critical-assessment-of-kohaerenz-protokoll.md:17-17] ^[Sources/drive/report-reorienting-the-central-conflict-as-a-call-to-wholene.md:18-18]
- Kael and Juna share a bond that predates the Genesis-Krise: it is described as a physical consequence of separation from a single prior shared self, and separately as a pre-existent ontological binding from before the founding system event. ^[Sources/drive/report-reorienting-the-central-conflict-as-a-call-to-wholene.md:40-40] ^[Sources/drive/konsolidierung-des-hard-canon-protokolls.md:14-14]
