## Definition

The central conflict of Kohärenz Protokoll is reoriented from an external quest into a journey of internal reintegration, which realigns the narrative's core architecture around Kael's psyche rather than external stakes. ^[Sources/drive/report-reorienting-the-central-conflict-as-a-call-to-wholene.md:5-5]
The entire plot must be framed as Kael's internal "Call to Wholeness," a journey to find and reintegrate an essential, lost part of himself, and every scene, character interaction, and worldbuilding element is stress-tested against that principle. ^[Sources/drive/report-reorienting-the-central-conflict-as-a-call-to-wholene.md:22-22] ^[Sources/drive/report-reorienting-the-central-conflict-as-a-call-to-wholene.md:5-5]
The framework is anchored in the Internal Family Systems principle of "positive intent," which frames Kael's internal journey as diplomacy and understanding rather than a civil war against monstrous parts. ^[Sources/drive/report-reorienting-the-central-conflict-as-a-call-to-wholene.md:11-11]
The journey's "Herold" is the increasing "leakage of Juna/V's pain" through Kael's internal system, surfacing as disruptive emotional intrusions, fragmented memory flashes, and disproportionate reactions that his "Manager" parts can no longer suppress. ^[Sources/drive/report-reorienting-the-central-conflict-as-a-call-to-wholene.md:24-24] ^[Sources/drive/report-reorienting-the-central-conflict-as-a-call-to-wholene.md:24-24]
The goal of the Call to Wholeness is the psychological process of reintegration: finding the exiled Juna/V, befriending her, unburdening her trauma, and welcoming her back, culminating in her "homecoming" as the exiled core that completes Kael's system. ^[Sources/drive/report-reorienting-the-central-conflict-as-a-call-to-wholene.md:26-26] ^[Sources/drive/report-reorienting-the-central-conflict-as-a-call-to-wholene.md:53-53]
