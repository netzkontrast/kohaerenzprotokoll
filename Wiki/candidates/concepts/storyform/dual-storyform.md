## Definition

The narrative architecture uses a dual-storyform — two parallel storyforms running side by side — to generate a 5D interference pattern that is structurally grounded in the mathematical symmetry of the Monster Group. ^[Sources/drive/technical-audit-research-mandate-the-kohaerenz-protokoll-fra.md:17-17]
One of the two storyforms must track Kael's "Heuristics of Integration" as its Success/Good throughline, while the parallel storyform tracks AEGIS's "Phoenix Collapse" as its Failure/Bad throughline. ^[Sources/drive/technical-audit-research-mandate-the-kohaerenz-protokoll-fra.md:19-19]
