## Definition

Lex is the alter within System Kael who is mapped to Kernwelt KW1 and who functions as the system's rational Analytical/Normal Part (ANP). ^[Sources/drive/reader-s-report-a-critical-assessment-of-kohaerenz-protokoll.md:13-13] ^[Sources/drive/reader-s-report-a-critical-assessment-of-kohaerenz-protokoll.md:31-31]
Lex is one of four alters — alongside Nyx, Kiko, and Lia — that the technical audit requires to be treated as a discrete functional module. ^[Sources/drive/technical-audit-research-mandate-the-kohaerenz-protokoll-fra.md:12-12]
When Kael drops the amnesic barriers separating Lex from Nyx, Kiko, and Lia, he becomes a mutual-information-dense structure. ^[Sources/drive/technical-audit-research-mandate-the-kohaerenz-protokoll-fra.md:38-38]
