## Definition

Kiko is a child-alter within Kael's system whose paralyzing terror is something Kael hopes to see resolved as part of his personal future. ^[Sources/drive/reader-s-report-a-critical-assessment-of-kohaerenz-protokoll.md:44-44]
Kiko is one of four alters—Lex, Nyx, Kiko, and Lia—that must be treated as discrete functional modules. ^[Sources/drive/technical-audit-research-mandate-the-kohaerenz-protokoll-fra.md:12-12]
When Kael drops the amnesic barriers between Lex, Nyx, Kiko, and Lia, he becomes a mutual-information-dense structure. ^[Sources/drive/technical-audit-research-mandate-the-kohaerenz-protokoll-fra.md:38-38]
