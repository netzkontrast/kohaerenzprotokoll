## Summary

This is a strategic report that reorients the novel's central conflict from an external quest into an internal psychological journey, redefining the character Juna/V as an Internal Family Systems (IFS) 'exiled part' of the protagonist Kael rather than a separate external entity. It grounds their bond (the 'Moonshine-Link') in a synthesis of the ER=EPR conjecture and Whitehead's process philosophy to explain why the antagonist AEGIS cannot detect it, and redefines the story's climax as a causal result of psychological integration (via IFS 'functional multiplicity' and a 'living Gödel-Satz' framing) rather than physical conflict. The document functions as a project-internal directive intended to keep scenes, characters, and worldbuilding consistent with this reoriented conflict architecture.
