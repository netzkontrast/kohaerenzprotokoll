## Summary

An editorial/publisher-style critical assessment of the 'Kohärenz Protokoll' concept, written in the voice of a reader's report. It identifies core strengths (the protagonist-world causal architecture, the philosophical AEGIS conflict, the tragic antagonist) and weaknesses (risk of intellectual abstraction, unclear personal stakes, underdeveloped alter characters, Juna/V as a possible deus ex machina), then offers actionable recommendations for narrative development. Functions as a strategic development/revision document rather than source theory or in-world lore.
