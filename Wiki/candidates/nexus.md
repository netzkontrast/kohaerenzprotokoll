# Nexus

The meta-space the [[guardians|Guardians]] appear in. **11 occurrences, and a second name
used in the same sentences.**

**That is the 2025 document's Nexus.** Later sources move it: the worldbuilding concept puts it in KW4, a
„Übergangsort zwischen KW4 und Externer Ebene." ^[worldbuilding-konzept-kohaerenzprotokoll-md.md:L619], and its Guardians in the Überwelt.

## Reading — `guardians-und-kern-welten-konzept`, 2025-04-17

Every Guardian has a field called `Repräsentation im Nexus` ^[guardians-und-kern-welten-konzept.md:L29, L54, L79,
L106, L118] — five times, once each. It is where a Guardian takes a form:
[[logos|LogOS]] as „eine komplexe, sich ständig neu konfigurierende Struktur aus
Lichtlinien und Datenströmen" ^[guardians-und-kern-welten-konzept.md:L29], Sophia as „als ruhige, leuchtende
Präsenz" ^[guardians-und-kern-welten-konzept.md:L118].

The `Möglichkeits-Garten` is called its `Vorstufe` ^[guardians-und-kern-welten-konzept.md:L122] and may act as
„als Übergangszone, als Verbindungspunkt oder als Kommunikationskanal zwischen den Kern-Welten und dem übergeordneten Nexus"
^[guardians-und-kern-welten-konzept.md:L130] — so the Nexus sits **above** the Kern-Welten.

## The unresolved surface — `Überraum`

The field is named `Repräsentation im **Nexus**`, and **all five filled
instances open `Im Überraum…`**. `Nexus` 11 occurrences, `Überraum` 5.

The pattern is too regular to be accident — a field name in one vocabulary,
filled in another. **The document never states they are one space.** Kept as two
pages and cross-referenced rather than merged: J18.

calls „dem Nexus oder der Meta-Realität" ^[guardians-und-kern-welten-konzept.md:L124] — the document offers those as
alternatives and does not choose.

**It is inside KW4**, and this page's Nexus sits *above* the [[kern-welten|Kern-Welten]]. Neither
`Überraum` nor any word for a meta-level occurs in this document at all.

So the name now has three carriers across two documents: a meta-space
(`Nexus`, document 4), the space its occupants describe as `Überraum` (same
document, same field), and an interface inside a Kern-Welt (`Nexus-Interface`,
here). **No page is created for the third**, because creating one asserts that it
is a separate term, which is exactly what is unresolved.

Its `Source` column says `Plot Teil 1` — the name comes from a plot document, not
from this one — so the document that would settle this is probably that one.

### What would settle it

A passage saying whether the Nexus-Interface leads to the Nexus, is named after
it, or is unrelated. This document offers no link: its profile never mentions a
higher level, and document 4's Nexus is never reached from a Kern-Welt by an
interface.
