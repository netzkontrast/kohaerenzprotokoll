# ARS — Autopoietische Reentry-Segmentierung

One of eight protocols of an „AEGIS-Postulat" that the only source **analyses
rather than authors**. So every reading is doubly attributed: what the postulate
claims, and what this source says about the claim.

## What the postulate claims — **Beschreibung** ^[aegis-emergenz-aus-der-leere.md:L72]

self-segmentation of the system into submodules that are closed in themselves but recursively coupled.

That label marks the postulate's content, **not this document's position**.
Asserting it of this source would attribute to it the thing it goes on to reject.

## What the source says about it — **Bewertung** ^[aegis-emergenz-aus-der-leere.md:L75]

Transferring autopoiesis from biological cells to abstract modules needs a justification it does not give.

> „Der vorgestellte Formalismus konzentriert sich eher auf die Interaktionsdynamik als auf die Komponentenproduktion" ^[aegis-emergenz-aus-der-leere.md:L75]
