# Kohärenz

## Reading — `kohaerenzprotokoll-aegis-und-systementropie`, 2025-04-19

The first operational definition the corpus has given it — as the complement of
entropy, and measurable:

> „Im Gegensatz dazu definiert AEGIS \"Kohärenz\" wahrscheinlich als **niedrige
> Entropie, hohe Vorhersagbarkeit, logische Konsistenz** innerhalb von
> Datenstrukturen und Ereignisabläufen sowie stabile, wiederkehrende Muster."
> ^[kohaerenzprotokoll-aegis-und-systementropie.md:L27]

And the quantity it is measured by:

> „Kohärenz könnte quantitativ über die *Redundanz* im Informationsgehalt des
> Systems gemessen werden – dem Gegenteil von Entropie." ^[kohaerenzprotokoll-aegis-und-systementropie.md:L27]

**This is a property of a running system**, not of a belief or an argument. What
[[aegis|AEGIS]] monitors, and what it loses when the bond breaks.

## Note on why this page did not exist earlier

The word occurs in both earlier sources — once in one, forty-eight times in the
other — and neither defined it. A term can be everywhere and mean nothing yet.

## Reading — `kohaerenz-protokoll-konzept`, 2025-04-17

**Two days earlier, and opposite: this document uses `Kohärenz` for the thing
the [[guardians|Guardians]]' data-focused reading cannot reach**, rather than for a
measurable property of a running system:

> „zur Erlangung einer authentischen Form von Kohärenz, die über reine
> Datenintegration hinausgeht" ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-konzept.md:L19]

This is the opposite direction from the later document's „niedrige Entropie,
hohe Vorhersagbarkeit" reading above — here Kohärenz is what the [[guardians|Guardians]]'
data-based approach falls short of, not what they compute. **Not a conflict**:
this document never mentions `AEGIS`, and its `Kohärenz` may simply be a
different speaker's use of the same word rather than a second definition of the
same referent. Recorded as a second, unmerged reading, per the wiki's rule for
attributed, disagreeing sources.

`Seelen-Kohärenz-Protokoll`, the project's own working title, contains this
word but names the document, not the concept — see [[seele-info]] and J46.

### The paradigm's own narrower sense

The [[guardians|Guardians]]' tools aim at a different, measurable target this document calls
`Datenkohärenz` and `Datenintegration` — see [[seele-info|Seele=Info]] and J48,
J52. **Two senses in one document, kept apart by this page rather than folded**:
authentic Kohärenz (this reading) is what Michael is moving toward; Datenkohärenz
is what the [[guardians|Guardians]]' failed tools measure against.
