# Kern-Welten

**For two documents this page had no reading at all, and existed to record
that.** The third defines four worlds — see the bottom of this page.

## What the first source does

`entropie-aegis` uses the term **once**, inside a parenthesis, as something
already understood:

> „dem Management von Entropie innerhalb des Systems (Überwelt und **simulierte
> Kern-Welten**)" ^[entropie-aegis.md:L93]

That is the entire occurrence. It gives exactly two facts, both by position
rather than by statement: the Kern-Welten are simulated, and they sit inside the
same system as the [[ueberwelt|Überwelt]].

## Why this page exists at all

A term used once, in a parenthesis, as known, is the shape of term that gets
skipped — and skipping it is invisible, because nothing is missing from anywhere.

It is also load-bearing. The brief's whole premise is that [[kael|Kael]]'s fragmented
psyche is represented as simulated worlds ^[entropie-aegis.md:L21], and these are
those worlds. **The document depends on a term it never explains.**

## Reading — `romanplot-kohaerenz-protokoll-teil-1`, 2025-04-18, the Teil-1 plot — the four worlds as a proposed, not strictly linear sequence tied to Guardians and parts of Kael

The Teil-1 plot proposes the Kern-Welten as stages of Kael's journey and says so in a structural note: „Die Progression durch die Kern-Welten ist nicht streng linear“ ^[romanplot-kohaerenz-protokoll-teil-1.md:L51] and adds that Kael can revisit worlds or meet intrusions from others; and „Der primäre Fokus folgt jedoch tendenziell dieser Sequenz“ ^[romanplot-kohaerenz-protokoll-teil-1.md:L51]. The side characters are introduced to enrich them, as „Interaktionspunkte, Spiegelbilder von Kaels Zustand oder Repräsentationen der Systemlogik und ihrer Grenzen“ ^[romanplot-kohaerenz-protokoll-teil-1.md:L15].

Its matrix gives each world a mechanic: „Logik/Kausalität“ ^[romanplot-kohaerenz-protokoll-teil-1.md:L299] for the first, „Emotion/Erinnerungsresonanz“ ^[romanplot-kohaerenz-protokoll-teil-1.md:L300] for the second, „Sicherheit/Grenzen/Angst“ ^[romanplot-kohaerenz-protokoll-teil-1.md:L301] for the third, „Symbolik/Potenzial/Metamorphose“ ^[romanplot-kohaerenz-protokoll-teil-1.md:L302] for the fourth. It reads the whole as: „Diese Matrix verdeutlicht, wie jede Welt einen spezifischen Aspekt von Kaels Psyche und der Systemlogik repräsentiert“ ^[romanplot-kohaerenz-protokoll-teil-1.md:L304].

At the end of Part 1 the plot proposes that the worlds collapse into one another: „beeinträchtigen das Gefüge zwischen den Kern-Welten“ ^[romanplot-kohaerenz-protokoll-teil-1.md:L263] in Kap 12, then „Die eskalierende Instabilität gipfelt im Zusammenbruch der Grenzen zwischen den Kern-Welten“ ^[romanplot-kohaerenz-protokoll-teil-1.md:L281] in Kap 13.
