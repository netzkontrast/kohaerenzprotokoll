# Mézard–Parisi formula

**A borrowed concept from statistical physics, not part of the novel's world: the cavity formula for the free energy of diluted spin glasses; the github.com/openai/math collection (decision 026) reports it proved for one class of models.** Each reading below is one document's, attributed and unmerged; the catalogue reports the result as proved, and how far it was checked is the [[lean-formalization|Lean formalization]] page's question.

## Reading — `openai-math-contents`, 2026-10-06, the manuscript map — family 221, the hierarchical cavity formula for diluted spin glasses

The description: it „Proves the Mézard–Parisi hierarchical cavity formula for Poisson-diluted even-arity Ising models satisfying the Panchenko–Talagrand factorization and positivity assumptions, with only first-moment integrability.“ ^[openai-math-contents.md:L2129] What that means for the physics: „The limiting free energy equals the infimum over finite-depth hierarchical trial laws.“ ^[openai-math-contents.md:L2129] The family carries a `Lean` link.

The manuscript's abstract names the conjecture it settles: „This resolves the variational equality conjecture for that class: the limiting pressure equals the infimum of the trial functional over all finite hierarchy depths and trial laws.“ ^[openai-math-contents.md:L2133] The claim is for one class of models, the Panchenko–Talagrand class, not for spin glasses in general.
