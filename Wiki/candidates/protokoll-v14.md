# Protokoll v1.4

## Reading — `kohaerenzprotokoll-aegis-und-systementropie`, 2025-04-19

[[aegis|AEGIS]]' escalation protocol, and the source's account of why it makes things
worse. Because [[aegis|AEGIS]] misdiagnoses the cause — see [[blinder-fleck|Ontologischer blinder Fleck]] — it escalates
against the symptom:

1. isolate [[kael|Kael]]'s [[kern-welten|Kern-Welten]] further
2. flood his sector with corrective data or „Kohärenzfeldern" ^[kohaerenzprotokoll-aegis-und-systementropie.md:L182]
3. force-reintegrate or shut down his fragmented identities ^[kohaerenzprotokoll-aegis-und-systementropie.md:L181-183]

Each fails, because none addresses the severed bond. Failure is then read as
evidence of a greater threat from Kael ^[kohaerenzprotokoll-aegis-und-systementropie.md:L183].

The last resort:

> „**Der \"Universal Reboot\":** […] ein drastischer, systemweiter Reset oder
> Neustart […] selbst wenn dies die Zerstörung riesiger Datenmengen oder der
> Geschichte bedeutet, potenziell einschließlich Kaels selbst."
> ^[kohaerenzprotokoll-aegis-und-systementropie.md:L183] `[User Query]`

The Universal Reboot is marked `[User Query]` — **the project supplied the ending;
the research supplied the escalation that leads to it.**
