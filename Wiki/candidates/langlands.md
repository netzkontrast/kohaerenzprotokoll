# Langlands

**A borrowed concept from mathematics: the Langlands correspondence between number theory, representation theory and geometry. The novel's own sources write the name too; this page so far holds only the github.com/openai/math collection (decision 026), which reports two geometric forms proved in stated ranges.** Each reading below is one document's, attributed and unmerged; the catalogue reports the result as proved, and how far it was checked is the [[lean-formalization|Lean formalization]] page's question.

## Reading — `openai-math-contents`, 2026-10-06, the manuscript map — two families, the restricted and the quantum geometric Langlands equivalences

Family 014 is headed „Restricted geometric Langlands, global Arthur enhancements, and generic Ramanujan.“ ^[openai-math-contents.md:L149] Its description reports the equivalence proved under conditions: it „Proves the restricted geometric Langlands equivalence for connected reductive groups on smooth projective connected curves“ ^[openai-math-contents.md:L149] over finite fields, „under the four stated Lie-theoretic characteristic hypotheses“ ^[openai-math-contents.md:L149].

Family 069 is headed „Global quantum geometric Langlands at irrational level.“ ^[openai-math-contents.md:L739] Its description: it „Proves the unramified de Rham quantum geometric Langlands equivalence for every connected simple complex algebraic group on every smooth projective connected complex curve“ ^[openai-math-contents.md:L739], and it „identifies the full derived categories of twisted D-modules for the group and its Langlands dual“ ^[openai-math-contents.md:L739].

Both are geometric forms of the Langlands correspondence, each claimed in a stated range; the catalogue claims no form of the arithmetic programme in general.
