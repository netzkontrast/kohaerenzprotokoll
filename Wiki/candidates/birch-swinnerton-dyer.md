# Birch–Swinnerton-Dyer

**A borrowed concept from number theory, not part of the novel's world: the conjectured formula linking an elliptic curve's rank to its L-function; the github.com/openai/math collection (decision 026) reports it proved in Selmer corank zero and one.** Each reading below is one document's, attributed and unmerged; the catalogue reports the result as proved, and how far it was checked is the [[lean-formalization|Lean formalization]] page's question.

## Reading — `openai-math-contents`, 2026-10-06, the manuscript map — family 002, the full formula from low Selmer corank

The description: it „Proves the full Birch–Swinnerton-Dyer leading-term formula for every elliptic curve over ℚ whose full q-power Selmer group has corank zero or one for some prime q, including finiteness of the Tate–Shafarevich group.“ ^[openai-math-contents.md:L29] Joined with another family it widens: „With result 006, this gives full BSD for a density-one set of quadratic twists of every elliptic curve over ℚ.“ ^[openai-math-contents.md:L29]

The first manuscript's abstract repeats the claim for the same range: „We prove the full Birch–Swinnerton-Dyer leading-term formula for every elliptic curve over ℚ whose full q-power Selmer group has corank zero or one at some prime q.“ ^[openai-math-contents.md:L33] The claim is conditional on that corank; the catalogue does not say it proves the conjecture for every curve.
