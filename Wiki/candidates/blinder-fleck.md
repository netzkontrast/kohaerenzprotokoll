# Ontologischer blinder Fleck

## Reading — `kohaerenzprotokoll-aegis-und-systementropie`, 2025-04-19

AEGIS cannot perceive the Kael-Julia bond, and the source is explicit that this is
not a gap but a category:

> „AEGIS' Blindheit ist somit fundamental und nicht akzidentiell. Es handelt sich
> **nicht um einen fehlenden Sensor, sondern um eine kategoriale Unfähigkeit**"
> ^[kohaerenzprotokoll-aegis-und-systementropie.md:L61]

**This is the research's own contribution**, not a premise — it is argued, at
length, from five directions ^[L55–59]: sensor limits, method limits, the qualia
problem, an ontological barrier drawn from Bohm's implicate order, and a violation
of Ashby's Law of Requisite Variety.

The last is load-bearing:

> „AEGIS fehlt die \"erforderliche Varietät\" (Requisite Variety), um die
> Kael-Julia-Bindung zu modellieren oder zu verstehen." ^[kohaerenzprotokoll-aegis-und-systementropie.md:L59]

## The consequence the source draws

AEGIS reacts to symptoms it can see and misdiagnoses their cause ^[L82, L177],
which makes its escalation — see `protokoll-v14` — actively destructive.

## Open

Whether anything else in the project has a blind spot of this shape, and whether
it would be the same mechanism. Not answerable from this source.
