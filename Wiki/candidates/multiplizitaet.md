# Multiplizität

## Reading — `entropie-aegis`, 2025-04-17, brief

It appears once, and it appears as **one half of the distinction the whole
document turns on**:

> „Wie unterscheidet sich **gesunde Komplexität/Multiplizität** von destruktiver
> Entropie/Chaos?" ^[entropie-aegis.md:L95]

So: multiplicity is what a fragmented psyche looks like when it is *not* a
disorder. It is set against [[entropie|Entropie]]/Chaos, which is what [[aegis|AEGIS]] sees and acts
against.

**The question is asked and not answered.** But the framing is itself the
reading: the document asserts that the two are different and asks how — it does
not ask whether.

## Why this matters more than one occurrence suggests

If healthy multiplicity and destructive entropy cannot be told apart, then [[aegis|AEGIS]]
classifying `DID` as entropy is not an error, and the novel's central conflict
loses its ground. The single sentence at L95 is where that hangs.

## Surface drift

The corpus elsewhere carries **`funktionale Multiplizität`** — the same idea with
a qualifier. One surface here, another there, and nothing connects them but
reading both.

Recorded as an alias rather than a second term, provisionally. **If a source
turns out to distinguish them, this page is wrong and splits.**
