# Multiplizität

## Reading — `entropie-aegis`, 2025-04-17, brief

It appears once, and it appears as **one half of the distinction the whole
document turns on**:

> „Wie unterscheidet sich **gesunde Komplexität/Multiplizität** von destruktiver
> Entropie/Chaos?" ^[entropie-aegis.md:L95]

So: multiplicity is what a fragmented psyche looks like when it is *not* a
disorder. It is set against [[entropie|Entropie]]/Chaos, which is what [[aegis|AEGIS]] sees and acts
against.

**The question is asked and not answered.** But the framing is itself the
reading: the document asserts that the two are different and asks how — it does
not ask whether.

## Why this matters more than one occurrence suggests

If healthy multiplicity and destructive entropy cannot be told apart, then [[aegis|AEGIS]]
classifying `DID` as entropy is not an error, and the novel's central conflict
loses its ground. The single sentence at L95 is where that hangs.

## Surface drift

The corpus elsewhere carries **`funktionale Multiplizität`** — the same idea with
a qualifier. One surface here, another there, and nothing connects them but
reading both.

Recorded as an alias rather than a second term, provisionally. **If a source
turns out to distinguish them, this page is wrong and splits.**

## Reading — `kohaerenz-protokoll-konzept`, 2025-04-17 — the surface confirmed, and given a phase structure

**This document uses exactly `funktionale Multiplizität`, the alias recorded
above, never bare `Multiplizität` alone.** It grounds the term in a named
three-phase clinical structure it cites by source, ISSTD:

> „Die Phasen der ISSTD-leitlinienorientierten DID-Therapie (Stabilisierung,
> Traumabearbeitung, Integration/Neuorientierung )" ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-konzept.md:L63]

with the third phase named twice, once with `funktionale Multiplizität` folded
into it:

> „Phase 3 (Integration, funktionale Multiplizität, Neuorientierung) der
> DID-Therapie" ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-konzept.md:L281]

and earlier, as one of Teil 1's goals:

> „Phase 1 der DID-Therapie: Stabilisierung, Bewusstwerdung und
> Symptommanagement." ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-konzept.md:L267]

**So `funktionale Multiplizität` is not a free-standing state here — it is the
name for the third of three named phases**, `Integration`/`Neuorientierung`
being alternate names for that same phase rather than separate destinations.
Supporting vocabulary from this cluster —
`Stabilisierung`, `Traumabearbeitung`, `Neuorientierung`, `Bewusstwerdung`,
`Symptommanagement`, `Co-Bewusstsein` and `Integrationsversagen`
^[kohaerenz-protokoll-konzept.md:L109] — is recorded here rather than
promoted separately; J57 folds `Integrationsversagen` into this same
DID-therapy cluster and keeps it apart from the [[guardians|Guardians]]' `Datenintegration`,
recorded on [[seele-info]].

This document also states, independently of the entropy/multiplicity question
`entropie-aegis` leaves open, why no single "Kernpersönlichkeit" is assumed:

> „eine Konstellation von dissoziierten Selbstzuständen, die aus einem Mangel
> an frühkindlicher Integration resultieren" ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-konzept.md:L104]

**Not in tension with the entropy/multiplicity question above** — this
document never mentions `AEGIS` or `Entropie`, so it neither answers nor
contests „wie unterscheidet sich gesunde Komplexität/Multiplizität von
destruktiver Entropie/Chaos?".
