# Alters

## The single occurrence — `entropie-aegis`, 2025-04-17, brief

> „die verschiedenen Bewusstseinszustände oder \"**Alters**\" einer Person mit
> DID" ^[entropie-aegis.md:L69]

Quoted, once, inside a question about [[entropie-signatur|Entropie-Signatur]]. The gloss
„Bewusstseinszustände" is the only thing this source says they are, and it is
offered as an approximation rather than a definition.

## Why a page for one quoted word

Because this is the term the corpus disagrees about most.

The retired pipeline, run over four unrelated documents, found its largest
disagreement here: **how many alters System [[kael|Kael]] has — 11 or 13** — with three
sources per position and a second disagreement about whether two named alters
are distinct or resolved into others.

None of that is in this source and none of it is on this page. What belongs here
is the record that **the term enters the corpus as a parenthetical gloss inside a
question**, and that a page built only from documents where it looks important
would start somewhere else entirely and never see this first occurrence.

## Open

The count, the roster, and whether a Kern-Welt corresponds to an alter. All of it
lives in documents not yet ingested.

### Not one-to-one, unlike document 6's roster

Where `roman-lokalitaeten-konzept-und-ausarbeitung` names exactly one Alter per
Kern-Welt (see `q3-how-many-kern-welten-and-alters`), this document puts **two**
named alters in Welt 1 (Schatten/Persecutor and the unnamed Memory Holder/EP)
and **two** in Welt 4 (Host and, optionally, Funke) — a structurally different
shape, not a numerically different count of the same shape. Contributes to Q3
without settling it; see that page for the update.

### The clinical frame this document adds

The structural-dissociation ANP/EP model is named directly and mapped onto all
four worlds in one sentence — the only place in this document that states the
whole mapping at once:

> „Welt 3 (Zitadelle) und Welt 4 (Bibliothek/Maske) repräsentieren die
> Funktionen des anscheinend normalen Persönlichkeitsanteils (ANP) […] Welt 1
> (Zerbrochene Stadt) und Welt 2 (Spielplatz/Puppenhaus) verkörpern die
> Funktionen der emotionalen Persönlichkeitsanteile (EP)"
> ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-konzept.md:L254]

Supporting vocabulary from this document's DID-clinical grounding —
`Protector`, `Persecutor`(en), `Kind-Anteile`, `Gatekeeper`,
`Internal Self-Helper`/`ISH`, `Apparently Normal Part`/`ANP`, `EP`,
`Beobachter`, `Vogel` (the last two named as recurring archetypal
*readings of* an alter's function rather than alters themselves,
^[kohaerenz-protokoll-konzept.md:L160-162]) — is recorded here rather than
promoted separately; each is the seven-row table's vocabulary or generic
clinical terminology this document imports, not a distinct novel concept.

## Reading — `roman-lokalitaeten-konzept-und-ausarbeitung`, 2025-04-18

**The word is „wie", not „und".** Each level names an example, so this is four
Alters attested and no count claimed. That matters for the count this page exists
to record as disputed.

`Orakel/Muse` is always slashed — 10 occurrences, never `Orakel` alone and never
`Muse` alone — exactly as `Kairos/Sophia` is among the Wächter. What the slash
means is not stated.

### What they do to a place

The Alters are given causal power over the environment, and the document proposes
reading a room's state as evidence of which one was last there:

> „Ein perfekt geordneter Raum könnte den Einfluss eines bestimmten Alters
> widerspiegeln, während eine gestörte oder veränderte Version desselben Raumes
> auf die Präsenz oder den kürzlichen Durchgang eines anderen Alters hindeuten
> könnte." ^[roman-lokalitaeten-konzept-und-ausarbeitung.md:L93]

Two KW4 locations exist for them as a group: a `Forum des Dialogs`, „Raum für die
Kommunikation und Koordination zwischen Alters" ^[roman-lokalitaeten-konzept-und-ausarbeitung.md:L224], and a `Trainingsraum der
Alters` ^[roman-lokalitaeten-konzept-und-ausarbeitung.md:L225], whose profile calls the whole a „DID-System" ^[roman-lokalitaeten-konzept-und-ausarbeitung.md:L450].

### Still open

The count and the correspondence. Four named across four worlds looks like a
one-to-one rule and the document does not state one — „wie" leaves every level's
roster open, and [[kael|Kael]]'s own [[personas|Personas]] are never enumerated.

### The document's own most confident data breaks one-alter-one-world outright

Kael and the five rows without a `?` are this document's plainest assignments.
**Two of those five share a world** — Nox and Praetor both to Grenzfeste
(KW3), unhedged — **and a sixth is assigned to two worlds at once** — Oblivion,
`KW2 (isoliert)/ KW3`, also unhedged. Unlike the third roster
(`kohaerenz-protokoll-konzept`), which put two alters per world only in a
document that called itself a sketch, this document's contradiction sits in
its most confident rows. See
`Wiki/questions/q3-how-many-kern-welten-and-alters.md` for the full argument.

### The clinical vocabulary this document imports

**A named clinical theory, cited directly**, grounding the whole roster:

> „Im Rahmen der Theorie der strukturellen Dissoziation werden Hosts als
> \"Anscheinend Normale Persönlichkeitsanteile\" (ANPs) betrachtet […] während
> andere Anteile (Emotionale Persönlichkeitsanteile, EPs) oft die
> traumatischen Erfahrungen halten." ^[charakterkonzepte-fuer-kohaerenz-protokoll.md:L26]

`Host`, `ANP`/`Anscheinend Normale Persönlichkeitsanteile`,
`EP`/`Emotionale Persönlichkeitsanteile`, and the role-types
`Gatekeeper` (Limina), `Protector` (Praetor), `Persecutor` (Nox — with its own
named mechanism, `Täterlogik` ^[charakterkonzepte-fuer-kohaerenz-protokoll.md:L294]),
`Caretaker` (Silas), `Internal Self-Helper` (Index) and `Child Alter`/`Little`
(Echo, Flicker) are this document's clinical-role vocabulary, applied to named
characters rather than stated as free-standing theory — the same treatment
`kohaerenz-protokoll-konzept`'s DID-clinical grounding received above, and
recorded here rather than promoted separately for the same reason.

Three further clinical terms describe the system as a whole rather than any
one Alter: `Co-Bewusstsein` — „Der Grad an Co-Bewusstsein und interner
Kommunikation variiert stark zwischen Systemen"
^[charakterkonzepte-fuer-kohaerenz-protokoll.md:L230] (cross-reference
[[multiplizitaet]], which already carries this term from the third roster's
document); `Intrusionen` and `Amnesie`, both named as how the Host experiences
other Alters' influence before he knows the system exists
^[charakterkonzepte-fuer-kohaerenz-protokoll.md:L29] (cross-reference
[[risse|Risse]], where `Amnesie` is already one of `kohaerenz-protokoll-konzept`'s
four named Riss-types).

`frontet` — the document's own term for which Alter currently controls the
body, used in every profile's Externes-Auftreten field (e.g. „Wenn er
frontet, kann Kael plötzlich zynisch…"
^[charakterkonzepte-fuer-kohaerenz-protokoll.md:L307]) — and `Switching`,
named once directly (`Die Darstellung von Wechseln (Switching)`
^[charakterkonzepte-fuer-kohaerenz-protokoll.md:L664]), are the vocabulary for
that same event. The document also states, as a research-grounded constraint
on how switching should read on the page, that most DID is `verdeckt`
(covert): „Da die meisten Fälle von DID als verdeckt (covert) beschrieben
werden, sollten dramatische Wechsel […] eher die Ausnahme als die Regel sein."
^[charakterkonzepte-fuer-kohaerenz-protokoll.md:L231]
