# Guardians

## Reading — `entropie-aegis`, 2025-04-17, brief

Role only, in a subordinate clause:

> „Dies prägt auch das Handeln der Guardians als **spezialisierte Agenten dieses
> Entropie-Managements**." ^[entropie-aegis.md:L93]

They appear a second time as [[aegis|AEGIS]]' side of the central conflict: „Der Konflikt
zwischen AEGIS/Guardians und Michael/Julia" ^[entropie-aegis.md:L96].

**Three occurrences in the whole document, all in dependent clauses.** Nothing
says what a Guardian is, how many there are, or whether they are conscious.

## Do not confuse with `Integrity Guardian`

`Integrity Guardian` ^[entropie-aegis.md:L65] is one of AEGIS' four named
sub-functions and a different term — see [[aegis-teilfunktionen|AEGIS' four sub-functions]]. The two share a
substring and nothing else.

This is recorded on the page because it is the kind of thing that gets merged by
accident exactly once, and then stays merged.

## Open

Everything except the role. This is a first-rank term of the novel appearing as
an aside in a document about something else.

## Reading — `guardians-und-kern-welten-konzept`, 2025-04-17

**From a role in a subordinate clause to five named bearers, each fully
specified.** This document is dated the same day as `entropie-aegis` and does
what that one did not: it says who the Guardians are.

| Guardian | domain | blind spot | world |
|---|---|---|---|
| [[logos|LogOS]] | Struktur, Logik, Systemarchitektur | categorical | [[konstrukt-stadt|Konstrukt-Stadt]] |
| [[mnemosyne|Mnemosyne]] | Erinnerung, Emotion, Empathie | misreading | [[resonanz-landschaft|Resonanz-Landschaft]] |
| [[cerberus|Cerberus]] | Sicherheit, Abwehr, Grenzen | misreading | [[grenzfeste|Grenzfeste]] |
| [[kairos|Kairos]] | Potenzial, Kreativität, [[emergenz|Emergenz]] | contextual | [[moeglichkeits-garten|Möglichkeits-Garten]] |
| [[sophia|Sophia]] | Wissen, Weisheit, Integration | missing data | [[moeglichkeits-garten]] |

Nine fields filled for each, uniformly. See the individual pages.

### The structural claim

Every Guardian has a `Blinder Fleck`, and the document makes it a consequence
rather than a flaw:

> „wie die spezifische Natur des \"Blinden Flecks\" jedes Guardians logisch aus
> dessen Domäne und Funktion erwächst" ^[guardians-und-kern-welten-konzept.md:L137]

All five are blind to the same thing — the [[partnerin|Partnerin]] — and each is blind
differently, because each blindness is derived from that Guardian's own domain.
The document types the failures three ways itself
^[guardians-und-kern-welten-konzept.md:L117]: categorical, misinterpretation,
incomplete knowledge.

### Four pairs, five Guardians

„die vier zentralen Hüter" ^[guardians-und-kern-welten-konzept.md:L15] and „die
vier Guardian/Welt-Paare" ^[guardians-und-kern-welten-konzept.md:L135], against
five named. The document resolves it itself: [[kairos|Kairos]] and [[sophia|Sophia]] share one world
^[guardians-und-kern-welten-konzept.md:L96]. **The count is not an error, but
any tally taken from the framing text is off by one.**

### Still open, and now sharper

This document never mentions `AEGIS` — zero occurrences. Document 1 called the
Guardians „spezialisierte Agenten dieses Entropie-Managements", which is [[aegis|AEGIS]]'
work. **Whether the Guardians are inside AEGIS, beside it, or a design it
replaced is not answerable from the four documents read** — see
`Wiki/conflicts/c4-guardians-and-aegis.md`.
## Reading — `aegis-subplots-kapitelweise-system-exploration-docx`, 2025-08-05, brief

**The first document to place the Guardians relative to AEGIS in so many words —
and it does so while arguing that the placement is the open question.**

Its structural language is consistent and puts them inside. A Guardian

> „agiert als spezialisierter Agent innerhalb eines größeren Systems" ^[aegis-subplots-kapitelweise-system-exploration-docx.md:L49]

and the modelling instruction is explicit:

> „Modelliert den Guardian als funktionale Komponente innerhalb der
> AEGIS-Architektur, die auf programmierten Direktiven basiert." ^[aegis-subplots-kapitelweise-system-exploration-docx.md:L50]

It can also describe a Guardian's behaviour as untypical for

> „eine reine AEGIS-Erweiterung" ^[aegis-subplots-kapitelweise-system-exploration-docx.md:L280]

which only makes sense if being an AEGIS extension is the default reading.

### But the whole of chapter 20 exists to put that under pressure

> „Die Natur eines Guardians - Autonomer Agent oder bloßes Werkzeug?
> (Konfrontation)." ^[aegis-subplots-kapitelweise-system-exploration-docx.md:L272]

> „Führt er lediglich AEGIS' Code aus, oder zeigt er Anzeichen unabhängiger
> Ziele, Interpretationen oder sogar Einschränkungen basierend auf seiner
> spezifischen Domäne?" ^[aegis-subplots-kapitelweise-system-exploration-docx.md:L273]

The frame it proposes is the principal–agent problem: could a Guardian's goals
„selbst geringfügig, von AEGIS' Gesamtziel abweichen" ^[aegis-subplots-kapitelweise-system-exploration-docx.md:L274], given
delegation and Informationsasymmetrie.

**So this document's answer is: structurally inside, and whether that is all
they are is the novel's own unanswered question.** That is not the same as
settling `c4-guardians-and-aegis`, and it changes the conflict's status — see
that record.

### A fourth kind, and a taxonomy in one clause

> „AEGIS setzt einen neuen Typ von Guardian ein – nicht nur Vollstrecker oder
> Monitore, sondern 'Architekten' mit tieferem Zugriff auf den Kerncode der
> Simulation." ^[aegis-subplots-kapitelweise-system-exploration-docx.md:L414]

Three roles named, only `architekten` developed. `Vollstrecker` and `Monitore`
appear once each, in this clause, and nowhere else in any read document.

### The numbered worlds

This document numbers the [[kern-welten|Kern-Welten]] and attaches Guardians to two of them —
`LogOS` to KW1, `Cerberus` to KW3. Document 4 named the worlds without numbering
them. The join is on [[kern-welten|Kern-Welten]], flagged as an inference rather than either
document's claim.

**Everything above is proposal.** This document hedges once every 86 words and
puts 32 of its 91 question marks in the field closest to assertion.

## Reading — `roman-lokalitaeten-konzept-und-ausarbeitung`, 2025-04-18

**This document says `Wächter` and never `Guardian` — not once, in 12,022
words — and it is the first read source to use the German word analytically.**

The four bearers are given in a parenthesis, in the same order as the levels:

> „die Domänen der jeweiligen Wächter (LogOS, Mnemosyne, Cerberus,
> Kairos/Sophia)" ^[roman-lokalitaeten-konzept-und-ausarbeitung.md:L35]

They are seated in AEGIS' level:

> „Sitz von AEGIS und den Wächtern in ihrer Systemfunktion." ^[roman-lokalitaeten-konzept-und-ausarbeitung.md:L176]

and the [[ueberwelt|Überwelt]] holds their infrastructure — a `Kommunikations-Hub` for „die
Kommunikation zwischen Wächtern/AEGIS" ^[roman-lokalitaeten-konzept-und-ausarbeitung.md:L232] and a `Wächter-Registry`,
„Datenbank/Interface zur Verwaltung der Wächter-Parameter" ^[roman-lokalitaeten-konzept-und-ausarbeitung.md:L233].

### One of them is called a Wächter *of* something

> „Cerberus (als Wächter der Mauer)" ^[roman-lokalitaeten-konzept-und-ausarbeitung.md:L370]

which is the word in its ordinary German sense — keeper of a specific thing —
attached to one of the four. See [[grosse-mauer]].

### What this does to the register hypothesis, which was a measurement

Q4 recorded a distribution from document 5: `Guardian` standing alone 22 times
across every analytic field, `Wächter` three times and never in analysis. The
hypothesis was that **the English word is the analytic register and the German
one the fictional register**, and that if it held across more documents it was a
stance signal rather than a synonym.

**It does not hold.** Here `Wächter` carries all twelve analytic uses and
`Guardian` is absent. Two documents, opposite distributions, and the difference
tracks the document rather than the register. See
`q4-waechter-four-bearers`.

### On the relation to AEGIS, this document assumes rather than argues

Every placement puts the Wächter inside AEGIS' domain, and the document never
raises the question that document 5 built a chapter out of. It lists them beside
AEGIS as „Nutzer/Beobachter" of the system monitor ^[roman-lokalitaeten-konzept-und-ausarbeitung.md:L472], and it gives AEGIS a
registry of their parameters ^[roman-lokalitaeten-konzept-und-ausarbeitung.md:L233] — which is a stronger form of containment
than anything read so far, and it is stated in passing, as a location's function.

**That is placement, not a statement of the relation.** See
`q1-guardians-and-aegis`.
