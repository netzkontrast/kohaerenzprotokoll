> psychologische oder soziale Beziehungen; sie wird als *fundamentale
> Kohärenzstruktur* postuliert" ^[kohaerenzprotokoll-aegis-und-systementropie.md:L104] `[User Query]`

**Marked `[User Query]`, so this is a premise the commission supplied**, not a
finding the research produced. The page records it as the source does: as
something the project asserted and asked to have explained.

What the research adds is its function: the bond is a constant source of
**[[negentropie|Negentropie]]**, offsetting the entropy [[kael|Kael]]'s DID generates, holding a dynamic
equilibrium AEGIS does not manage ^[kohaerenzprotokoll-aegis-und-systementropie.md:L122]. Its loss removes „das zentrale
stabilisierende Element" and triggers the cascade ^[kohaerenzprotokoll-aegis-und-systementropie.md:L221].

open question ^[kohaerenzprotokoll-aegis-und-systementropie.md:L239].
