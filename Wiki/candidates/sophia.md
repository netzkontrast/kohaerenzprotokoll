# Sophia

One of **five [[guardians|Guardians]]** specified by a single document, each filled out across
the same nine fields. Twelve other read documents name Sophia — the readings below, the Inquiry file's the latest (it moved this count from eleven, the Hard-SF-Outline's from ten, the Ultra-Plot's from nine, the philosophischer Bericht's from eight). (Until that reading this lead said nothing else read named Sophia at all, which the second reading on this page had already made false.)

### Blinder Fleck — **missing data**

Unique among the five: „fehlt ihr die entscheidende Information" ^[guardians-und-kern-welten-konzept.md:L117]. The document types it explicitly — „keine kategorische wie bei LogOS oder eine Fehlinterpretation wie bei Mnemosyne/Cerberus" ^[guardians-und-kern-welten-konzept.md:L117] — a limit of knowledge inside an otherwise complete frame.

The document's closing claim makes this structural rather than incidental:
„wie die spezifische Natur des \"Blinden Flecks\" jedes Guardians logisch aus
dessen Domäne und Funktion erwächst" ^[guardians-und-kern-welten-konzept.md:L137].

### Repräsentation im Nexus

„als ruhige, leuchtende Präsenz […] als zentrale Bibliothek" ^[guardians-und-kern-welten-konzept.md:L118] — the field is named `Repräsentation im Nexus` and its text opens
`Im Überraum…`. Whether those are one space is open (J18).

### Beziehung zu anderen Guardians

Integrates LogOS, Mnemosyne and Cerberus and moderates their debates; works closely with Kairos ^[guardians-und-kern-welten-konzept.md:L119].
