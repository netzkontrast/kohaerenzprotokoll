# Möglichkeits-Garten

One of **four [[kern-welten]]** specified by a single document, each paired to a
Guardian and filled out across the same eight fields.

Until this document, `Kern-Welten` was a term two sources used and neither
defined — once inside a parenthesis, as already understood. This one names four
of them and gives each its own physics.

## Reading — `guardians-und-kern-welten-konzept`, 2025-04-17

**Grundkonzept:** „eine Realität, die Potenzial, Kreativität, symbolische Bedeutung und Transformation repräsentiert" ^[L124]

**Regeln/Physik/Logik:** „nicht-linear, assoziativ und metamorph" ^[L125]

Primarily experienced by the **Funke · Sucher**-Persona ^[L124].

### How Risse manifest here

„unkontrollierbare, destruktive Transformationen" ^[L128]

This is the world's own governing principle turned against itself — growth made destructive.
The document states one claim about `Risse` four times, in four vocabularies;
see [[risse]].

### Partnerin-Echos here

„mit außergewöhnlicher Klarheit und Kraft…" ^[L129]

Every world gets this field, and it is where the Partnerin is characterised —
by effect, never by identity. See [[partnerin]].

## Two Guardians, and a second name

This is the one world with **two** [[guardians]] — the document says so in a
parenthesis: „Kairos und Sophia werden als zwei distinkte, aber komplementäre
Guardians dargestellt, die gemeinsam über diese Domäne wachen." ^[L96]. That is
why four Guardian/Welt-Paare hold five Guardians.

Its heading also gives it a second name: `Der Möglichkeits-Garten /
Nexus-Vorstufe` ^[L122]. That slash is an alias, not a term boundary (J14) —
and `Nexus-Vorstufe` is a *role*, not the `Nexus` itself (J15).

## Guardian

`Kairos & Sophia` — see [[kairos]]. The pairing is the document's organising principle:
each section is a `Guardian/Welt-Paar`.

## Open

How many Kern-Welten exist in total, whether each corresponds to one alter, and
how these four relate to the worlds documents 1 and 3 refer to without naming.
**`AEGIS` does not occur in this document**, so the relation to [[aegis]]'
containment of the Kern-Welten (document 3) is not answerable here.
