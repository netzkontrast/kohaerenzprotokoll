# Potentialmeer

## Reading — `kohaerenzprotokoll-aegis-und-systementropie`, 2025-04-19

> „Die Beschreibung, die Bindung sei \"aus dem Potentialmeer geschöpft\",
> erinnert stark an Konzepte wie David Bohms *implizite Ordnung* und das
> *Quantenpotential*." ^[kohaerenzprotokoll-aegis-und-systementropie.md:L106] `[User Query]`

**The phrase is marked `[User Query]`** — the project supplied it. What the
research adds is the identification with Bohm: the bond would be „eine
stabilisierte Manifestation dieser zugrundeliegenden, vernetzten Potentialität",
a structure within the Holomovement ^[kohaerenzprotokoll-aegis-und-systementropie.md:L106].

The consequence for [[aegis|AEGIS]] is the point: it operates „ausschließlich innerhalb der
expliziten Ordnung" ^[kohaerenzprotokoll-aegis-und-systementropie.md:L58] and so cannot reach what is anchored in the
implicate one. See [[blinder-fleck|Ontologischer blinder Fleck]].

## Open

Whether `Potentialmeer` is the project's own term or a paraphrase of Bohm adopted
in passing. The source treats it as given and explains it by analogy — which is
not the same as defining it.
