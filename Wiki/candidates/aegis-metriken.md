# AEGIS' operative Metriken

## Reading — `kohaerenzprotokoll-aegis-und-systementropie`, 2025-04-19

Six quantities [[aegis|AEGIS]] monitors, each given **two interpretations** — under [[kael|Kael]]'s
DID, and under the loss of the bond ^[kohaerenzprotokoll-aegis-und-systementropie.md:L91-96]:

| metric | under DID | under bond loss |
|---|---|---|
| Rate logischer Widersprüche | contained inconsistencies in [[kael|Kael]]'s worlds | rapidly escalating, unresolvable, system-wide |
| Integrität von Datenstrukturen | manageable local noise | exponential system-wide corruption |
| Vorhersagbarkeits-/Abweichungswerte | predictable deviation within bounds | chaotic transitions, loss of control |
| Ressourcenfluktuationsanalyse | contained consumption tied to state changes | massive uncontrolled surges |
| Mustererkennung / Anomaliedetektion | recognisable, contained deviations | unprecedented collapse of every pattern |
| globaler Entropie-Score | gradual, manageable rise | exponential rise toward the maximum |

## Why one page and not six

The only thing known about any of them individually is its two readings in this
table. The finding is **the contrast**, not the metric — and it is the mechanism
by which [[aegis|AEGIS]] can see the catastrophe and still misread its cause.

Splits into six the moment a source says something about one of them alone.

## What no metric covers

**All six are system-level. None is per-alter.** So [[entropie-signatur|Entropie-Signatur]] — a
per-alter measure another source asks for — is not answered here, and this is the
closest the corpus has come to answering it.
