# Grenzfeste

One of **four [[kern-welten|Kern-Welten]]** specified by a single document, each paired to a
Guardian and filled out across the same eight fields.

Until this document, `Kern-Welten` was a term two sources used and neither
defined — once inside a parenthesis, as already understood. This one names four
of them and gives each its own physics.

## Reading — `guardians-und-kern-welten-konzept`, 2025-04-17

**Grundkonzept:** „eine Realität, die als Schutzraum, Quarantänezone oder Kontrollzentrum konzipiert ist" ^[guardians-und-kern-welten-konzept.md:L85]

**Regeln/Physik/Logik:** „rigiden Regeln, starken, oft undurchdringlichen Grenzen und Barrieren" ^[guardians-und-kern-welten-konzept.md:L86]

Primarily experienced by the **Wächter**-Persona ^[guardians-und-kern-welten-konzept.md:L85].

### How Risse manifest here

„unerklärliche Sicherheitslücken" ^[guardians-und-kern-welten-konzept.md:L89]

This is the world's own governing principle turned against itself — security made porous.
The document states one claim about `Risse` four times, in four vocabularies;
see [[risse|Risse]].

### Partnerin-Echos here

„Anomalien, die sich den Sicherheitsprotokollen widersetzen, aber keine offensichtliche feindliche Absicht zeigen…" ^[guardians-und-kern-welten-konzept.md:L90]

Every world gets this field, and it is where the [[partnerin|Partnerin]] is characterised —
by effect, never by identity. See [[partnerin|Partnerin]].

## Guardian

`Cerberus` — see [[cerberus|Cerberus]]. The pairing is the document's organising principle:
each section is a `Guardian/Welt-Paar`.

## Open

how these four relate to the worlds documents 1 and 3 refer to without naming.

containment of the [[kern-welten|Kern-Welten]] (document 3) is not answerable here.
