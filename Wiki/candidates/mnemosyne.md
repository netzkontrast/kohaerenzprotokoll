One of **five [[guardians|Guardians]]** specified by a single document, each filled out across
the same nine fields. dessen Domäne und Funktion erwächst" ^[guardians-und-kern-welten-konzept.md:L137].

`Im Überraum…`. Whether those are one space is open (J18).

Understands Kairos; supplies emotional context to LogOS and Cerberus, who may discount it; seeks perspective from Sophia ^[guardians-und-kern-welten-konzept.md:L55].
