| date | documents | Michael / Julia | Kael / [[juna|Juna]] |
|---|--:|--:|--:|

**A clean cut on a single day, with no mixed document on either side.** This is
not vocabulary drifting; it is a decision taken between two working days.

## And then it leaks for fourteen months

`Michael` appears in **16 documents dated after the cut**, the last on
2026-06-10. Nine of them use it exactly once — the residue of a find-and-replace
that missed a line. Two use it 16 and 20 times.

**So the rename was announced cleanly and never enforced.** Both facts matter: a
reading dated before 2025-04-18 is *expected* to say Michael, and a reading
dated after it that says Michael is a defect in that document rather than
evidence about the character.

## What this corrects

An earlier reading of a handful of documents concluded that the two names changed
at different times, from the observation that some documents mix the new name
with the old. **That was inference from a small sample and it is wrong.** Both
names changed on the same day; the mixing is incomplete enforcement afterwards,
not a second rename.

The correction is recorded here rather than silently applied, because *how* it
went wrong is the reusable part: a few documents looked like a timeline, and a
count over 346 showed a cliff.
