# Kohärenz-Programm

The system as a whole, in this document's vocabulary. Six occurrences ^[L24, L36, L49, L73].

## Reading — `guardians-und-kern-welten-konzept`, 2025-04-17

It is what the [[guardians|Guardians]] serve and what the [[risse|Risse]] damage:

- [[logos|LogOS]] monitors „der logischen Konsistenz und strukturellen Integrität des
  gesamten Seelen-Kohärenz-Programms" ^[L24]
- [[mnemosyne|Mnemosyne]] watches emotional coherence „innerhalb des Kohärenz-Programms und
  seiner Instanzen" ^[L49]
- [[cerberus|Cerberus]] is „die Firewall, das Immunsystem und der Grenzschutz des
  Kohärenz-Programms" ^[L73]
- The [[konstrukt-stadt|Konstrukt-Stadt]]'s faults come „Aufgrund der \"Risse\" im
  Kohärenz-Programm" ^[L36]

## Not `Kohärenz`, and not the novel's title

Three surfaces sit close together and are three things (J16, J17):

| surface | what it is |
|---|---|
| `Kohärenz` | the property the system maintains — see [[kohaerenz|Kohärenz]] |
| **`Kohärenz-Programm`** | the system itself, this page |
| `Das Seelen-Kohärenz-Protokoll` | the **novel's title**, in quotation marks after „Romanprojekts" ^[L15] |

The third is not a term of the fiction at all.

## Reading — `charakterkonzepte-fuer-kohaerenz-protokoll`, 2025-04-18 — a fourth surface, and this one does name AEGIS

**A different surface, `Kohärenz Protokoll` (two words, no hyphen), used once
in a sense that matches this page rather than [[kohaerenz|Kohärenz]] or the
novel's title** (`Plan/runs/judgements.jsonl` J79 — the wiki-lookup's own
near-match pointed at `kohaerenz.md`, and this reading overrides that with the
document's actual sense):

> „AEGIS wird ausschließlich durch seine Kernprogrammierung, das \"Kohärenz
> Protokoll\", angetrieben." ^[charakterkonzepte-fuer-kohaerenz-protokoll.md:L124]

**This is the system itself, called [[aegis|AEGIS]]'s own core programming — the same
referent this page already tracks as `Kohärenz-Programm`** — and, unlike the
first reading, it answers the open question above directly: this document
does name [[aegis|AEGIS]], and identifies the Kohärenz-Programm/Kohärenz-Protokoll with
[[aegis|AEGIS]]'s own programming in one sentence. **This is one document's own claim,
not yet corroborated by a second source that uses both `AEGIS` and either
hyphenated surface.**

Five of the document's seven occurrences of `Kohärenz Protokoll` are its own
title or headers — not a term of the fiction, the same exclusion this page's
first reading already applies to `Das Seelen-Kohärenz-Protokoll`.
