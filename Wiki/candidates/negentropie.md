# Negentropie

**Asked about, never asserted.** Both occurrences in this source are inside
questions.

## What the source asks — `entropie-aegis`, 2025-04-17, brief

> „Könnte die Verbindung zu einer externen, nicht-digitalen Ebene (im Roman
> repräsentiert durch \"Julia\") aus AEGIS' Sicht als eine massive Injektion von
> unkontrollierbarer Entropie **oder als eine Form von Ordnung/Negentropie
> erscheinen, die es nicht verstehen kann**?" ^[entropie-aegis.md:L71]

And the same idea as the frame of the central conflict:

> „eine Form von Komplexität/**Negentropie** (lebensfördernde Struktur), die
> AEGIS fälschlicherweise als gefährliche Entropie klassifiziert und bekämpft"
> ^[entropie-aegis.md:L96]

The second is closer to a claim — but it is a *proposal for how to frame the
conflict* („kann nun auch […] gerahmt werden"), which in a brief is still a
premise offered for research, not a finding.

## Why the page exists with no reading

Because the question gets answered, two days later, in a different document —
and **nothing in the corpus links the two.** `kohaerenzprotokoll-aegis-und-systementropie`
makes Negentropie a property of the Kael–Julia bond
^[kohaerenzprotokoll-aegis-und-systementropie.md:L122].

That answer is not on this page: it belongs to that source and goes on when that
source is ingested. What belongs here is the record that **the question predates
the answer by two days and neither document knows about the other.**

## Status

`readings: 0` is deliberate. A term that a source only asks about has no reading
in that source — and a page that recorded the question as a reading would turn
the project's uncertainty into its position.
