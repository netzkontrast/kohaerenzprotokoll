# TSDP

**A borrowed clinical model — the theory of structural dissociation of the
personality (van der Hart, Nijenhuis, Steele 2006) — which the sources apply to
[[kael|Kael]]: trauma splits a personality into parts that carry daily life (ANPs) and
parts fixed in the trauma (EPs), phobias between them hold the split, and Kael is
the tertiary case, several of each.** The [[did|DID]] page has where the sources set
TSDP against DID, and the [[alters|Alters]] page has the roster and the count. This
page has the model: what it is, what holds it, and how the sources map it onto the
novel's other structures.
