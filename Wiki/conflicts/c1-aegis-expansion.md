## Why this is not a wording problem

Position 1 is a regulator *inside* a system:

> „Regulation des Übergangs von Ordnung zu Unordnung (Entropie), um die Kohärenz
> und strukturelle Integrität des Gesamtsystems […] zu wahren"
> ^[entropie-aegis.md:L21]

Position 3 is what reality emerges *from*:

> „das nicht innerhalb einer bestehenden Realität operiert, sondern diese durch
> nicht-lineare, nicht-lokale Kohärenzstrukturen emergent formt"
> ^[aegis-emergenz-aus-der-leere.md:L126]

A gatekeeper that regulates a system it sits inside cannot be the substrate that
system emerges from.

## Positions 2 and 3 are in the same document, and it says so

> „Der Übergang von der technischen (AGI-System) zur ontologischen
> (Realitätsursprung) Formulierung ist nicht nachvollziehbar begründet."
> ^[aegis-emergenz-aus-der-leere.md:L173]

The source that carries both names the gap between them as a defect of the
postulate it is analysing. **It does not resolve it either.**

## What would resolve this

An author's decision, or a later source that expands the acronym and states which
earlier expansion it supersedes. **Neither source does that** — each writes as if
its expansion were the only one.

A source *claiming* authority over others does not count. Decision 003: a gather
step proposes; it never resolves.

## Open questions this raises

- Is position 2 a third thing, or position 3 under a technical description?
- Does any source use `Entropic Gatekeeper` and a substrate reading together?
