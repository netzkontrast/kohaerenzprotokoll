# C5 — a world in one document is a place inside that world in another

**Append-only.** This record decides nothing.

## The positions

| # | what it is | source | line |
|--:|---|---|--:|
| 1 | **one of four Kern-Welten** — „eine Realität, die Potenzial, Kreativität, symbolische Bedeutung und Transformation repräsentiert" ^[guardians-und-kern-welten-konzept.md:L124], paired to Kairos & Sophia | `guardians-und-kern-welten-konzept` | 124 |
| 2 | **one of eight locations in KW4** — „Symbol für Wachstum, Potenzial und zukünftige Pfade" ^[roman-lokalitaeten-konzept-und-ausarbeitung.md:L219], with gates to the other regions of that level (quoted below) | `roman-lokalitaeten-konzept-und-ausarbeitung` | 219, 436 |

Document 4 makes it a Realität. Document 6 makes it a Bereich with gates leading
to the other Bereiche of the level:

> „Kreuzungen, besondere Bäume oder Blumen (die spezifische Potenziale
> symbolisieren), Brunnen der Reflexion, Aussichtspunkte, Tore zu anderen
> Bereichen von KW4."
> ^[roman-lokalitaeten-konzept-und-ausarbeitung.md:L436]

**A world does not have gates to other parts of itself.** That clause is what
makes this a conflict rather than two compatible descriptions.

## The names differ too, and that is a second question

`Möglichkeits-Garten` in document 4; `Garten der Möglichkeiten` in document 6,
which contains **zero** occurrences of the hyphenated form. The surfaces do not
fold together — `fold()` keeps word order — so nothing mechanical joins them, and
the join above is a reading.

**Document 6's table marks the row `Konzept Doc`**, meaning this document took the
name from a concept document rather than inventing it
^[roman-lokalitaeten-konzept-und-ausarbeitung.md:L219]. Document 4 is a concept
document. That is suggestive of a shared origin and it is not a statement by
either source.

## What would settle it

- A source that places a `Garten` inside a named Kern-Welt, or that names KW4
  and a garden in the same sentence with a containment word.
- Any source using both spellings.
- Document 4's `Nexus-Vorstufe` alias resolved: that document's own heading calls
  this world a transition zone toward the [[nexus|Nexus]], which already makes it something
  other than a plain Kern-Welt in its own source. If so, the scale question may
  be one document's rather than two documents'. See [[nexus]].

## Not to be confused with

`c3-emergenz-origin`, which is about where a thing comes from. This is about how
big a thing is, and it is the first conflict of that kind in the wiki.

and it adds a second name for that world. It uses neither `Garten der

source for one side is not a settlement, and this source's own claim to be
„Source-of-Truth" ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-storyform-und-outline-2026-06-10-md.md:L11] is its claim, not the wiki's.
