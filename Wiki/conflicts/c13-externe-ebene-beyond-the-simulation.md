**What every source shares**: Köln 2026 is never a stage and appears only as a
memory fragment, a phone tone, a smell — the attributes judgement J54 matched
word for word when it made `Basisrealität` and `Externe Ebene` one term. **What
they do not share** is where it is.

**The positions split along the two names.** Both sources that write
`Basisrealität` put it beyond the simulation; all four that write `Externe Ebene`
deny that it is outside. J54 joined the names on the attributes; whether the
names now mark two conceptions of the level is the author's question, not the
ledger's.

**Rows 1 and 2 share a date**, 2026-05-08, so no date orders them. The later
sources' „Kein" negates a phrase in quotation marks, which reads as a correction of
an earlier framing; none of them names what it corrects.

**Found while reconciling `kohaerenz-protokoll-welt-sensorik-drafting-2026-06-10-md`.** Rows 1 to 5 were
already readings on [[externe-ebene]], each attributed, and no record held the
disagreement.
