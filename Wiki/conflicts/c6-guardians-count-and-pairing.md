## What changed besides the count

The earlier bearers' names reappear as **world names**: KW1 is „Konstrukt-Stadt
(Logos-Prime)" ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-storyform-und-outline-2026-06-10-md.md:L219], KW2 „Mnemosyne-Archipel" ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-storyform-und-outline-2026-06-10-md.md:L220], KW3
„Cerberus-Labyrinth / Überwelt-Nexus" ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-storyform-und-outline-2026-06-10-md.md:L221]. `Kairos` and `Sophia` do not
occur in the 2026 document. And two worlds are named differently: document 1
paired Mnemosyne with `Resonanz-Landschaft` and [[cerberus|Cerberus]] with `Grenzfeste`; the
2026 document's KW2 and KW3 are the Archipel and the Labyrinth.

## Why this is a conflict and not two facts

Both describe the same four-world frame, and they cannot both be the account of
who guards it. The 2026 document says „post-Reset", which suggests a design that
replaced an earlier one — **the wiki does not infer that**; it records the word.

## What this document claims about precedence — recorded, not applied

The 2026 document names itself „Source-of-Truth" ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-storyform-und-outline-2026-06-10-md.md:L11] and states „bei
Konflikt gewinnt das Neuere" ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-storyform-und-outline-2026-06-10-md.md:L13]. By its own rule it would win. **That is a
source granting itself authority.** Which version is canon is the author's call.
