it: *does AEGIS see the external connection as an entropy injection, or as an
order it cannot parse?* ^[entropie-aegis.md:L71]

Kael-Julia bond provides, offsetting the entropy the DID generates

which is the first time this step has produced something no single census could.

## `kohaerenz` finally means something

— and neither defines it. It got no page, correctly.

This source gives the first operational definition: „niedrige Entropie, hohe
Vorhersagbarkeit, logische Konsistenz […] messbar über die *Redundanz*"
^[…:L27].

**A term can be everywhere and mean nothing yet**, and the page is created at the
moment that stops being true rather than at first occurrence.

## New conflict — `C3`, and it is the subtle one

Emergence from `S₀ = ∅` against emergence **within** the simulation.

Both sources use the word correctly. Neither text marks the level it is applying
the word at. And what it decides is not terminology: **whether AEGIS can be wrong
about its own world.** Under emergence-within it can, and the entire blind-spot
argument follows; under emergence-from-nothing there is no outside to be wrong
about.
