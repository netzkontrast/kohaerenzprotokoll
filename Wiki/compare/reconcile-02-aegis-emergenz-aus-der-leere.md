## New conflicts — two

### `AEGIS` — a gatekeeper and a substrate cannot be the same thing

| source | expansion | what it does |
|---|---|---|
| `entropie-aegis` ^[L19] | Autonomous Entropic **Gatekeeper** for Integrity Systems | regulates the order→disorder transition *inside* a system |
| `aegis-emergenz-aus-der-leere` ^[L126] | Autonomous Entropic Generative Integrity **Substrate** | „nicht innerhalb einer bestehenden Realität operiert, sondern diese […] emergent formt" ^[aegis-emergenz-aus-der-leere.md:L126] |

A guardian that regulates a system it sits inside, and a substrate that reality
emerges from. **Not resolvable by wording**, and the second document's own third
expansion ^[L17] agrees with neither.

**Conflict `C1` — AEGIS, three expansions, two sources. Unresolved.**

### `Entropie` — disorder to be resisted, or the matrix things emerge from

| source | sense |
|---|---|
| `entropie-aegis` | „ständigen Kampf gegen die systemimmanente Tendenz zur Unordnung" ^[entropie-aegis.md:L21] |
| `aegis-emergenz-aus-der-leere` | „schöpferische Matrix" (maximaler Möglichkeitsraum) ^[aegis-emergenz-aus-der-leere.md:L126] |

Two days apart. The second document notices the deviation without knowing about
the first.

**Conflict `C2` — Entropie, two incompatible senses, two sources. Unresolved.**

## New surface — one, and it is not a conflict

**`Zero-Trust`** is a named AEGIS sub-function on `aegis-teilfunktionen`, from
^[entropie-aegis.md:L65]. This document uses **`Zero-Trust-Architektur (ZTA)`** —
the established cybersecurity architecture — as a yardstick, and distinguishes it
explicitly:

> „ZTA verifiziert jedoch Zugriffsanfragen anhand von Identitäten, Gerätehygiene
> und vordefinierten Policies, **nicht allein durch interne Selbstkonsistenz**"
> ^[aegis-emergenz-aus-der-leere.md:L67]

**The project named a sub-function after an existing architecture.** A naming
relation, not a disagreement — and the first false conflict a shared-string
detector would have produced.

## What deliberately gets no page

The imported terms — Autopoiesis, the Symbol Grounding Problem, Fixpunkttheoreme,
the Free Energy Principle, Ashby, Bohm, Popper, roughly forty of them.

They are **what the postulate is measured against, not what the project means.**
Their meaning is anchored outside this corpus, which is why they do not drift and
do not conflict. Recorded here as a decision so it is not re-taken per document:
**a borrowed term gets a page only when a source uses it as the project's own.**
