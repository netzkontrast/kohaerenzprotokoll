and the same probes. Comparison 001 covered the first pair; this one adds the
third and changes what the pair suggested.

**Two of the three mark their stance, by two different mechanisms, and the probe
sees only one of them.** `repeated labels` finds document 2's six labels exactly
and reports `none` for document 3's 26 markers, which are inline rather than
bold. That is what `none` means here: no convention this probe knows about.

## The rule comparison 001 proposed, sharpened

001 found that string identity was anti-correlated with semantic identity. With a
third document that holds, and a better statement of it is available:

**Of the five terms present in all three documents, the three the project owns
each carry a conflict, and the two it borrowed carry none.**

| term in all three | d1 | d2 | d3 | |
|---|--:|--:|--:|---|
| **AEGIS** | 19 | 50 | 123 | three incompatible expansions and one deliberate non-expansion |
| **Entropie** | 53 | 30 | 60 | disorder to be fought · „schöpferische Matrix" · Shannon uncertainty |
| **Kohärenz** | 1 | 48 | 25 | see below |
| Selbstorganisation | 1 | 10 | 2 | borrowed from systems theory, same sense throughout |
| Kybernetik | 1 | 3 | 6 | a field name, not a claim |

So the signal is not that a term is shared. **It is that a term is shared *and*
the project owns it.** A borrowed term is stable because its meaning is anchored
outside the corpus; a project term drifts because nothing anchors it.

That is a cheaper test than reading for conflicts, and it is directly checkable:
*does this term have a meaning outside this project?*

## `Kohärenz` is the third conflict, and it was invisible in the pair

Document 1 uses it once, as a goal — „um die Kohärenz und strukturelle Integrität
des Gesamtsystems […] zu wahren" ^[entropie-aegis.md:L21]. On the pair alone that
is too thin to compare.

Across three, two incompatible senses appear:

- **System stability.** „niedrige Entropie, hohe Vorhersagbarkeit, logische
  Konsistenz […] messbar über die *Redundanz*"
  ^[kohaerenzprotokoll-aegis-und-systementropie.md:L27] — a measurable property
  of a running system.
- **Truth by self-consistency.** In document 2's ZTV protocol, coherence *is* the
  validity criterion — „extreme Form des epistemischen Kohärentismus"
  ^[aegis-emergenz-aus-der-leere.md:L67], criticised for risking solipsism.

A system can be maximally coherent in the first sense while being wrong about
everything in the second, and document 2 says so explicitly. **The project uses
one word for both.** Document 2 also compounds it eight ways —
`Kohärenzmetrik`, `Kohärenzbalancierung`, `Kohärenzregulation`,
`Kohärenzakkumulation` — so the surface fans out while the sense splits.

**This is the case the whole step was built for**: a term that looked like
agreement in two documents and turns out to be a conflict in three.

## `Juna` appears in none of the three

Zero occurrences across all 6,700+ words of each. The page `Wiki/candidates/juna.md`
is therefore **titled by a name no read source uses** — it is attested in other
landed documents, not in these.

That is a defect in the page, not in the census: a term page named from outside
its own readings. Either the page is renamed to what its sources say, or it
carries the name with the count that justifies it. Not settled here.

## `Zero-Trust` stays a false conflict, and document 3 does not touch it

d1 = 2 (an AEGIS sub-function), d2 = 1 (the external ZTA architecture), d3 = 0.
Nothing in the third document bears on it, so the naming relation comparison 001
recorded stands unchanged.

## What this says about the process

**1. Two documents were not enough to see `Kohärenz`.** The pair showed 1 against
48 occurrences and no way to tell a thin usage from a different sense. Comparisons
get sharper with n, and a comparison written at n=2 should expect to be superseded
— as this one supersedes 001.

**2. The test for where conflicts live is now cheap.** Shared *and* project-owned.
It needs no reading, only a judgement that is made once per term: does this word
mean something outside this project?

**3. Independent extraction keeps paying.** `Kohärenz` was one line in document
1's census, recorded because the census is exhaustive rather than selective. A
reading would have dropped it — it carries nothing in that document on its own.
