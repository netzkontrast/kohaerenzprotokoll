# Q2 — vocabulary, or only ever criticised?

**Seven pages ask this, in identical words**, because one document introduced all
seven terms and did nothing but evaluate them:

[[ani|ANI — Äußere Nicht-Identifikation]] · [[ars|ARS — Autopoietische Reentry-Segmentierung]] · [[ecr|ECR — Entropische Kohärenzregulation]] · [[nullpunkt-protokoll|Nullpunkt-Protokoll]] · [[pms|PMS — Protomemetische Speicherbildung]] · [[rsa|RSA — Rekursive semantische Autogenese]] · [[snk|SNK — Strukturierte Nicht-Kontrolle]]

Each page says the same thing: **Whether any later source uses this protocol as
project vocabulary rather than as something being evaluated. Until one does, the
term exists here only as an object of criticism.**

## Why this is one question and not seven

They entered the wiki together, from one document, in one role. **Whatever
settles it for one settles it for all seven** — either a later source picks the
vocabulary up, or the whole family stays a critique's subject matter.

Seven pages carrying the same sentence is seven places to edit and no place that
says they move together.

## Why it matters more than it looks

Seven of 46 pages — **15% of the wiki** — may be terms the project does not
actually use. If no later source picks them up, that is not a mistake to correct
but a fact worth knowing: the census was right to extract them and the wiki is
right to hold them, marked.

It also sets a precedent the project has not needed yet: **a term that exists
only inside a critique.** Nothing in the page schema says how to mark that.

## What would answer it

Any later source using one of the seven as its own vocabulary — asserting with it
rather than about it. One instance answers it for that term; a category of
documents read without any instance answers it for the family.
