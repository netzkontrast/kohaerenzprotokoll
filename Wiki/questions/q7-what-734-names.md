# Q7 — what 734 names

Promoted from the `## Open` section of [[komponente-734|Komponente 734]], from the
number's readings on [[kaels-wohneinheit|Kaels Wohneinheit]] and [[kael|Kael]], and
from `NOW.md`, which has carried it as `Einheit 734` (J80) since document 16 and on its
chapter list under Kap 1. Judgement J80 left it open on purpose: its rule is that a
number shared by a dwelling and a designation identifies neither.

## Why it is one question

**The same three digits label at least four things across the read sources, and the
plans make the number a thread the reader follows.** The strukturierter Outline has it
return in Kap 2, Kap 10 and Kap 25, where Kael suspects it is an address or a name
(its L837, quoted below) — so whether it *is* an address, a name, or both is not a
detail of one page but the payoff of a planted motif. Two pages hold its two main senses; a single
sentence that says what the Kap-1 console line refers to answers both.

## What is already decided, and what is not

- The dwelling under its numbers is one term (J50, J58); `Komp 734` is the
  component (J92); `Component 734` is placed on the German page by the sentence
  (J101); `T-734` is its source's own sense, never the component (J112).
- **Not decided:** what a bare `Einheit 734` refers to (J80), and whether the
  sources that give the dwelling and the component one number mean the coincidence.
  When the component is made — before the [[trennungsprotokoll|Trennungsprotokoll]] or out of it — is C12's,
  and this page does not touch it.

## What would answer it

A source that states what the number labels in the world: that the dwelling carries
Kael's designation, or that the two share a number by accident of drafting, or what
the console reads in Kap 1. The strukturierter Outline's „eine Adresse oder ein Name“ ^[koharenz-protokoll-strukturierter-outline-2026-05-18-md.md:L837]
and the Plot-Konkretisierung's `[V]` are the two sides a later text would have to
choose between or keep together on purpose.

**Where to look.** Counts, not readings — a whole-word match over every landed file,
2026-09-29; none of these documents is read. `Einheit 734` stands in nine unread
documents; most often in `romanplot-uberarbeitung-kohaerenz-protokoll-teil-1`
(2025-04-18), 18 times. The Reset-Doc, `kohaerenz-protokoll-struktur-kanon-reset-2026-04-30-md`,
writes `734` four times.
