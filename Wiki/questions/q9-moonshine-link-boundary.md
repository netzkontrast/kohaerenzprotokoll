# Q9 — the Moonshine-Link's boundary

**An open point the read sources name again and again, with no page of its own.**
The [[moonshine-link|Moonshine-Link]] page carries it in reading after reading, each
time as the source's own open question: `Moonshine-Boundary`, OQ-F in the drafting
manual, the Alter profiles and the Plot-Konkretisierung, OQ-B in the philosophischer
Bericht, Q2 in the AEGIS-Analyse, C.2 of the Reset-Doc's Appendix C as the
Dual-Storyform background document restates it. By a whole-word count over the landed files
(2026-09-29) the surface `Moonshine-Boundary` stands in 16 documents, 15 of them read.

## Why it is one question

The sources ask it three ways, and every way is about the same edge:

1. **What crosses** — which kinds of thing the link carries and which it does not.
2. **Who feels it, and where** — whether [[silas|Silas]] alone inside [[kael|Kael]]'s system
   senses it, and whether it is felt everywhere or only at particular places.
3. **Whose it is** — the Kael–Juna pair's alone, or a property of the world's
   substrate that anyone could in principle use.

It also answers what the [[externe-ebene|Externe Ebene]] page has asked since
document 3 — whether anything crosses it in both directions, named there by the
source as needing definition ^[kohaerenzprotokoll-aegis-und-systementropie.md:L238] —
because two read sources say the link is how [[juna|Juna]], from that level, reaches
Kael (below).

## What would answer it

A source that locks any of the three: a list of what crosses stated as settled, a
rule for who feels the link and where, or a statement that it is the pair's alone or
the substrate's. The recommendations agree on the second and nearly on the first;
the third has one answer, from a research report, and one source that says what that
answer would oblige the novel to explain.

**Where to look.** The sources that say where a lock would stand point at the
Reset-Doc's Appendix C, which is landed and unread: `kohaerenz-protokoll-struktur-kanon-reset-2026-04-30-md`,
whose Appendix C has a heading C.2 on the Moonshine-Link's boundary (its L697; a
heading looked up for orientation, not read). The one other unread document that writes `Moonshine-Boundary` is
`research-prompt-dramatica-dual-storyform-synthese-aegis-thro` (2026-04-29), twice — by
its title a research prompt for a dual-storyform synthesis of [[aegis|AEGIS]]' throughline.
