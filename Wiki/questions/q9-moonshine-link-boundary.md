# Q9 — the Moonshine-Link's boundary

**An open point the read sources name again and again, with no page of its own.**
The [[moonshine-link|Moonshine-Link]] page carries it in reading after reading, each
time as the source's own open question: `Moonshine-Boundary`, OQ-F in the drafting
manual, the Alter profiles and the Plot-Konkretisierung, OQ-B in the philosophischer
Bericht, Q2 in the AEGIS-Analyse, C.2 of the Reset-Doc's Appendix C as the
Dual-Storyform background document restates it. By a whole-word count over the landed files
(2026-09-29) the surface `Moonshine-Boundary` stands in 16 documents, 15 of them read.

## Why it is one question

The sources ask it three ways, and every way is about the same edge:

1. **What crosses** — which kinds of thing the link carries and which it does not.
2. **Who feels it, and where** — whether [[silas|Silas]] alone inside Kael's system
   senses it, and whether it is felt everywhere or only at particular places.
3. **Whose it is** — the Kael–Juna pair's alone, or a property of the world's
   substrate that anyone could in principle use.

It also answers what the [[externe-ebene|Externe Ebene]] page has asked since
document 3 — whether anything crosses it in both directions, named there by the
source as needing definition ^[kohaerenzprotokoll-aegis-und-systementropie.md:L238] —
because two read sources say the link is how [[juna|Juna]], from that level, reaches
Kael (below).

**1 — What crosses.** Three documents of 2026-05-08 give the same list, each marked
open:

„Übertragbar: mutuale Information, atemporale Resonanz, Zeugenschaft" ^[koharenz-protokoll-konzept-konsolidiert-2026-05-08-md.md:L312]
„Nicht übertragbar: materielle Daten, klassische Nachrichten, Rettungsaktionen" ^[koharenz-protokoll-konzept-konsolidiert-2026-05-08-md.md:L313]

The master report writes it with its lock open (L597–L603, above), and the
worldbuilding concept under „Boundary Conditions (offen, Appendix C):" ^[worldbuilding-konzept-kohaerenzprotokoll-md.md:L330],
restating it as a proposal: „Moonshine-Link-Boundary. Vorgeschlagen: nur MI/Witness, keine Daten/Materie. Final-Lock-In offen." ^[worldbuilding-konzept-kohaerenzprotokoll-md.md:L891]
The Konzept-Iteration Genesis, of the same date, draws the line elsewhere:
„er überträgt Resonanz, nicht Information“ ^[koharenz-protokoll-konzept-iteration-genesis-md.md:L802] — where the three lists count mutual
information as carried. The philosophy catalogue gives the image: „Telefon-Stille als MI ohne Daten ist das prototypische Bild." ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-philosophie-im-detail-2026-06-10-md.md:L513]

Before the reset of 2026-04-30, the Hard-SF-Outline has Juna reach through the link
at the climax: „Inmitten des Hitzetods greift Juna ein letztes Mal durch den ER=EPR-Tunnel. Sie opfert ihre externe Superposition, ihre Unantastbarkeit als reiner Ghost." ^[hard-sf-roman-outline-dkt-physik-cosmic-horror.md:L190]
Whether that is what the later lists exclude as `Rettungsaktionen`, none of them says.

**2 — Who feels it, and where.**

„Beobachtbar nur durch K₀-blinde Seiten — Silas spürt es, AEGIS sieht es nicht" ^[worldbuilding-konzept-kohaerenzprotokoll-md.md:L336]

„Vorschlag: Silas zuerst, andere Alter im Verlauf von Akt II; räumlich global, phänomenal nur an Knotenpunkten." ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-anteile-profile-sprach-dna-2026-06-10-md.md:L1077]

„C.2 Moonshine-Boundary — Wer kann den Link spüren?" ^[dual-storyform-hintergruende-md.md:L426] „Empfehlung: Silas zuerst, andere Alter im Verlauf von Akt II" ^[dual-storyform-hintergruende-md.md:L426], „räumlich global aber phänomenal nur an Knotenpunkten (Telefon, Stille, Erinnerungsorte)" ^[dual-storyform-hintergruende-md.md:L426]

The same proposal twice, marked a recommendation both times.

**3 — Whose it is.**

„Ist die Verbindung exklusiv für das Kael-Juna-Paar, oder gilt sie prinzipiell universal, sobald die Leech-Lattice-Symmetrien erfüllt sind?" ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-philosophischer-bericht-md.md:L415] „Status: ungelöst (OQ-B in den offenen Fragen)." ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-philosophischer-bericht-md.md:L415]

The AEGIS-Analyse asks the same, „Q2 Moonshine-Boundary: Ist der Moonshine-Link strukturell exklusiv für das Kael-Juna-Paar, oder besitzt er universelle Anwendbarkeit über die Foundation hinweg?" ^[dramatica-storyform-synthese-aegis-analyse.md:L139], and
leaves it. The later run of the same analysis answers it, as its own recommendation:
„Daher ist er logischerweise nicht exklusiv, sondern eine fundamentale physikalische Eigenschaft des Substrats.“ ^[dramatica-storyform-synthese-aegis-analyse-2.md:L335]
„Kael ist lediglich der erste Host, der sie bewusst und erfolgreich nutzt.“ ^[dramatica-storyform-synthese-aegis-analyse-2.md:L335]
The philosophischer Bericht names what a universal link would cost: „Wenn universal, müsste der Roman erklären, warum sie *narrativ* nur dieses Paar betrifft." ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-philosophischer-bericht-md.md:L415]

**The link and the Externe Ebene.** „Juna ist keine Alterpersönlichkeit, sondern eine externe Entität/Anomalie der "Externen Ebene"." ^[charakter-kompilation-fuer-kohaerenz-protokoll.md:L284]
„Moonshine-Link: Dies ist eine nicht-lokale, sub-protokollarische Verbindung zu Kael." ^[charakter-kompilation-fuer-kohaerenz-protokoll.md:L286] The Inquiry, of 2025-10-15, the
same in English (its L25, on [[juna]]). Whether the level itself is outside the
simulation is conflict C13; this question asks only what the link carries across.

## What would answer it

A source that locks any of the three: a list of what crosses stated as settled, a
rule for who feels the link and where, or a statement that it is the pair's alone or
the substrate's. The recommendations agree on the second and nearly on the first;
the third has one answer, from a research report, and one source that says what that
answer would oblige the novel to explain.

**Where to look.** The sources that say where a lock would stand point at the
Reset-Doc's Appendix C, which is landed and unread: `kohaerenz-protokoll-struktur-kanon-reset-2026-04-30-md`,
whose Appendix C has a heading C.2 on the Moonshine-Link's boundary (its L697; a
heading looked up for orientation, not read). The one other unread document that writes `Moonshine-Boundary` is
`research-prompt-dramatica-dual-storyform-synthese-aegis-thro` (2026-04-29), twice — by
its title a research prompt for a dual-storyform synthesis of AEGIS' throughline.
