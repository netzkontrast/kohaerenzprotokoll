# Q8 — AEGIS after the Vortex, and who takes its function

**The sources name this question themselves**, which is the promotion rule's other
way in: a source naming something as needing definition. They cite it as the
Reset-Doc's Appendix C — „Offene Frage Reset-Doc Appendix C" ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-charakter-bibel-2026-05-08-md.md:L123] — and as open questions
of their own, OQ-A and OQ-G. [[algorithmische-melancholie|Algorithmische Melancholie]],
[[aegis|AEGIS]], [[vortex|Vortex]] and [[oblivion|Oblivion]] each carry a piece of it;
the [[vortex]] page's `## Open` sends it to the first.

## Why it is one question

**Two halves that the sources join.** What AEGIS is after the fifth beat, and what
takes over AEGIS' erasing function inside [[kael|Kael]]'s system. The Dual-Storyform
background document restates both from the Reset-Doc's Appendix C, side by side, and
ties both to the same chapters — C.1 to „was nach Beat 5 in Kap 37–39 ist“ ^[dual-storyform-hintergruende-md.md:L425],
C.5 to „A-Resolution Kap 37–39“ ^[dual-storyform-hintergruende-md.md:L428]. A lock of either changes what Kap 37–39 can
show.

What *happens* in Beat 5 is not this question: most read sources that name the state
put it in the Vortex's fifth beat, and the few that place it elsewhere or nowhere are
recorded with their readings on [[algorithmische-melancholie]]. The question is what
remains.

## What would answer it

A source that locks either half — names what AEGIS is in Kap 37–39, or says that
Oblivion takes over its function — rather than recommending. The recommendations
agree more than the answers: where a preference is stated, AEGIS goes on in the
state — the living relic (the master report, the worldbuilding concept), or the
Dauerzustand with traces of self-shutdown (the Dual-Storyform background document) —
and every source that names Oblivion's role after the Vortex gives the same one, a
choosing rather than an automatic erasure. **None of them is a lock by its own words**, and the wiki applies no document's
claim to settle it (decision 006).

**Where to look.** The sources that say where the lock stands point at the Reset-Doc's
Appendix C, and it is landed and unread: `kohaerenz-protokoll-struktur-kanon-reset-2026-04-30-md`,
