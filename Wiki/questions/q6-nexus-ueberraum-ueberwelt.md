# Q6 — the Nexus, the Überraum and the Überwelt

Promoted from the `## Open` sections of [[nexus]], [[ueberraum|Überraum]] and
[[ueberwelt|Überwelt]], which ask it in three forms, and from four judgements that
each ended by saying the document did not relate two of the names: J18 (`Nexus` and
`Überraum`, open), J36 (`Nexus-Interface`, unresolved), J63 (`Überwelt-Nexus` is
neither the Überwelt nor the Nexus) and J103 (`Überwelt / Nexus`, a reading on both
pages). `NOW.md` has carried half of it since 2026-09-17, as what the `nexus` page wants —
anything relating `Nexus`, `Überraum` and `Nexus-Interface` — and half since
document 31, under *The Überwelt inside the four worlds*.

## Why it is one question

**The three names keep being written in the same place and never said to be one.**
The 2025 document fills its field `Repräsentation im Nexus` five times with a
sentence that opens `Im Überraum` (J18). Three 2026 sources name KW3
`Cerberus-Labyrinth / Überwelt-Nexus`, a fourth sets the `Überwelt-Nexus` in Kap 33, and the philosophischer Bericht heads KW3
`Überwelt / Nexus`. And the thing all of them place there is the same: where the
[[guardians|Guardians]] are — they take a form in the Nexus in 2025, reside in the
Überwelt in the worldbuilding concept, and reside in KW3 `Überwelt / Nexus` as
`Wächterprogramme` in the philosophischer Bericht. **One answer — a sentence that
relates any two of the names — would move all three pages**, and the wiki has kept
them apart only because none has been read.

outside the worlds and `Überwelt-Nexus` as KW3 — and relate neither (J63). That is
the question inside one document.

## What is already decided, and what is not

- **The Möglichkeits-Garten is not the Nexus.** `Nexus-Vorstufe` is a role the
  heading assigns the garden (J14); a precursor is not the thing (J15).
- **A compound is not its head.** `Überwelt-Nexus` is neither the Überwelt nor the
  Nexus by surface (J63); the slash in `Überwelt / Nexus` is a reading on both pages
  and merges nothing (J103).
- **The Innere Weite is the Überwelt's alias** (J95), so what the Kap-0 texts say of
  the Innere Weite is on [[ueberwelt]] — it does not touch the Nexus.
- **Not decided:** whether the Überraum is the Nexus (J18), whether the
  Nexus-Interface leads to it (J36), and whether the KW3 name joins the Überwelt and
  the Nexus or only borrows both words.

## What would answer it

A passage that relates any two of the names: that the Guardians' Überraum is the
Nexus or a region of it; that the Nexus-Interface opens onto the Nexus or is named
after it; that KW3's `Überwelt-Nexus` is the Überwelt's part in the worlds, or the
Nexus moved there. One such sentence answers its pair; the three pairs answer the
question.

**Where to look.** Counts, not readings — a whole-word match over every landed file
in `Sources/drive/`, 2026-09-29; none of these documents is read, and a count says
nothing about what the lines say:

- `Überraum` stands in seven unread documents, all of 2025-04-17 — most often in

  41 times. `corpus.py where Überraum` lists three documents, because its derived
  index holds fewer files than are landed; the count here opened the files.
- `Nexus-Interface` stands in three unread documents: `orte-konzept-fuer-kohaerenz-protokoll`
  and `lokalitaeten-konzept-fuer-roman-simulation` (2025-04-18), and `welt`
  (2025-07-29). The Lokalitäten document's `Source` column gives the interface's
  origin as `Plot Teil 1` (its L218); `romanplot-uberarbeitung-kohaerenz-protokoll-teil-1`
  writes `Nexus` 11 times.
- `Überwelt-Nexus` stands in no unread document; every document that writes it is read.
