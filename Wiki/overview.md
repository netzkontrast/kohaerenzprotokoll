# Overview

This page is the versioned synthesis of the research wiki: what the
ingested sources, merged into concept pages, say the novel is — and where
that differs from what `Canon/` says. `/wiki-understand` rewrites it as a
candidate after every batch of 25 promoted sources; the author promotes it.
Version 0 has no ingested source, so it states the starting position only.

## Starting position

- The normative description of the novel is `Canon/` (seven documents,
  storyform and outline normative for manuscript work). Inside this loop
  every Canon claim is `unverified` until it has been checked against the
  populated wiki (D-W12); a Canon/research conflict is an open question with
  no default winner.
- 693 Drive documents are indexed (`Sources/manifest.jsonl`): 508 work
  documents, 172 theory documents, 13 out of scope and excluded (D-W9). None
  is exported yet.
- The first batch is the audit slice plus part of the Kernkonzept slice
  (about 25 documents), because those carry the most self-understanding of
  the project; storyform, characters, worldbuilding and plot follow; theory
  last.

## How to read the next versions

Each version lists, per category, the concepts the sources establish, the
disagreements between sources, the disagreements with Canon, and the
questions raised on the three axes (incompleteness, incorrectness,
redundancy). Numbers come from `graph/coverage.json`; the rendered
`concept-table.md` is the compressed map; `index.md` is the catalogue.
