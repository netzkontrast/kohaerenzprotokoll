## Definition

The Kohärenz-Protokoll adopts Graham Priest's Dialetheism and Chaitin's uncomputability in order to prevent "logical explosion" when the simulation encounters a contradiction. ^[Sources/drive/technical-audit-research-mandate-the-kohaerenz-protokoll-fra.md:23-23]
"Risse" (cracks) in the Mnemosyne-Archipel are treated as true contradictions—dialetheias, points where two contradictory statements are both true. ^[Sources/drive/technical-audit-research-mandate-the-kohaerenz-protokoll-fra.md:26-26]
Love is defined as a living dialetheia and a resonant Moonshine-Link, which differentiates the protocol from "soft" emotional resolutions. ^[Sources/drive/technical-audit-research-mandate-the-kohaerenz-protokoll-fra.md:3-3]
Logical "cracks" occur specifically when trauma is treated as a metaphor rather than as a literal K1 structure buffering against K0 erasure. ^[Sources/drive/technical-audit-research-mandate-the-kohaerenz-protokoll-fra.md:9-9]
Chaitin's Halting Probability (Ω) supplies the "algorithmic randomness" necessary for novelty. ^[Sources/drive/technical-audit-research-mandate-the-kohaerenz-protokoll-fra.md:27-27]
Novelty is treated as the only escape from AEGIS's deterministic clockwork. ^[Sources/drive/technical-audit-research-mandate-the-kohaerenz-protokoll-fra.md:27-27]
Integration is held to triumph over exclusion not through sentiment but through the superior efficiency of paraconsistent logic. ^[Sources/drive/technical-audit-research-mandate-the-kohaerenz-protokoll-fra.md:40-40]
The architectural mandate calls for immediate implementation of the 39-chapter mosaic, ensuring every "logical crack" is treated as a dialetheic truth. ^[Sources/drive/technical-audit-research-mandate-the-kohaerenz-protokoll-fra.md:40-40]
