## Definition

The redefinition of Juna/V is anchored in the Internal Family Systems (IFS) model's core principle of "positive intent," which reframes Kael's internal journey as diplomacy and understanding rather than a civil war against monstrous parts. ^[Sources/drive/report-reorienting-the-central-conflict-as-a-call-to-wholene.md:11-11]
Within this model, Juna/V is defined as the primary exiled part of Kael's original self, termed the "Ursprungs-Ich." ^[Sources/drive/report-reorienting-the-central-conflict-as-a-call-to-wholene.md:18-18]
In the IFS framing, Juna/V occupies the role of the "Exilant." ^[Sources/drive/report-reorienting-the-central-conflict-as-a-call-to-wholene.md:18-18]
Juna/V was violently fragmented and exiled during the "Genesis-Krise" so that she alone would carry the entire traumatic burden of that event. ^[Sources/drive/report-reorienting-the-central-conflict-as-a-call-to-wholene.md:18-18]
