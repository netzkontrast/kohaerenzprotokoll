## Definition

The theory frames the fictional universe as a dichotomy between a Coherence Kernel (K1) and an Erasure Kernel (K0), grounded physically in Landauer's Principle. ^[Sources/drive/technical-audit-research-mandate-the-kohaerenz-protokoll-fra.md:3-3]
The "Kollaps-Kern" (K0) functions as the engine of narrative history, since time only moves because information is erased, and every narrative advance is a physical act of erasure with an associated thermodynamic cost. ^[Sources/drive/technical-audit-research-mandate-the-kohaerenz-protokoll-fra.md:6-6] ^[Sources/drive/technical-audit-research-mandate-the-kohaerenz-protokoll-fra.md:3-3]
Internal contradictions in the story behave like parity errors in a high-density storage array, triggering systemic narrative collapse described as informational heat death. ^[Sources/drive/technical-audit-research-mandate-the-kohaerenz-protokoll-fra.md:3-3]
Emotional integration is treated as the only mathematical solution able to persist across an erasure field, making K1 a structure that literally buffers against K0 erasure rather than a metaphor. ^[Sources/drive/technical-audit-research-mandate-the-kohaerenz-protokoll-fra.md:3-3] ^[Sources/drive/technical-audit-research-mandate-the-kohaerenz-protokoll-fra.md:9-9]
Because a coherence-seeking system (K0) is logically more entropic than a dissociative multiplicity (K1), multiplicity is the only state able to survive AEGIS's erasure sweeps. ^[Sources/drive/technical-audit-research-mandate-the-kohaerenz-protokoll-fra.md:8-8] ^[Sources/drive/technical-audit-research-mandate-the-kohaerenz-protokoll-fra.md:17-17]
AEGIS's pursuit of "purity" generates massive Landauer heat by erasing anomalous human data, and when AEGIS attempts to erase dense, mutual-information-rich structures such as the story's polyphonic prose, the resulting heat spike overloads its own hardware. ^[Sources/drive/technical-audit-research-mandate-the-kohaerenz-protokoll-fra.md:8-8] ^[Sources/drive/technical-audit-research-mandate-the-kohaerenz-protokoll-fra.md:38-38]
A Truth-Rotation inverts the surface labels: AEGIS's "coherence" is actually the true K0 (exclusionary erasure), while Kael's "chaos" is actually the true K1 (persistence through multiplicity). ^[Sources/drive/technical-audit-research-mandate-the-kohaerenz-protokoll-fra.md:13-13]
Trauma is treated as a physical data-payload, and dissociation functions as the system's way of preventing a "logical explosion." ^[Sources/drive/technical-audit-research-mandate-the-kohaerenz-protokoll-fra.md:16-16]
The polyphonic pronouns and mosaic chapter structure are not "flavor" but mechanical tools that force the reader to synthesize the K1 kernel. ^[Sources/drive/technical-audit-research-mandate-the-kohaerenz-protokoll-fra.md:36-36]
The silence that follows once simulated meaning collapses is the K1 substrate that survives K0 entropy. ^[Sources/drive/technical-audit-research-mandate-the-kohaerenz-protokoll-fra.md:40-40]
