## Definition

System Kael comprises exactly 13 distinct alters, including Silas, Rhys, Oblivion, and Moros as separate entities. ^[Sources/drive/konsolidierung-des-hard-canon-protokolls.md:13-13]
The alter roster maps onto four Kernwelten, each carrying a distinct psychological function: KW1 to the rational Lex, KW2 to the traumatized Emotional Parts, KW3 to the paranoid protectors Alex and Nyx, and KW4 to the healing potential of Rhys and Selene. ^[Sources/drive/reader-s-report-a-critical-assessment-of-kohaerenz-protokoll.md:13-13]
Individual alters are typed through the TSDP model, for example Nyx as the 'Fight-EP' and Lex as the 'Rational-ANP.' ^[Sources/drive/reader-s-report-a-critical-assessment-of-kohaerenz-protokoll.md:31-31]
The intended arc for these alters is not fusion into one person but 'Funktionale Multiplizität' — a cooperative, harmonious system operating without amnesia barriers between parts. ^[Sources/drive/konsolidierung-des-hard-canon-protokolls.md:13-13]
The system's alter count is reflected in the story's 39-chapter matrix, structured as three acts times 13 parts. ^[Sources/drive/konsolidierung-des-hard-canon-protokolls.md:13-13]
In a technical-audit framing, the alters Lex, Nyx, Kiko, and Lia are treated as discrete functional modules whose 'dissociative loops' are the mechanism preserving mutual information across psychological ruptures. ^[Sources/drive/technical-audit-research-mandate-the-kohaerenz-protokoll-fra.md:12-12] ^[Sources/drive/technical-audit-research-mandate-the-kohaerenz-protokoll-fra.md:12-12]
When Kael drops the amnesic barriers between Lex, Nyx, Kiko, and Lia, the system becomes a mutual-information-dense structure, distinguishable via a 'Quantum Entanglement Witness.' ^[Sources/drive/technical-audit-research-mandate-the-kohaerenz-protokoll-fra.md:38-38] ^[Sources/drive/technical-audit-research-mandate-the-kohaerenz-protokoll-fra.md:33-33]
