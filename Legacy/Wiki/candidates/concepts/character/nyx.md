## Definition

Nyx is one of System Kael's alters, mapped together with Alex to the paranoid-protector Kernwelt KW3. ^[Sources/drive/reader-s-report-a-critical-assessment-of-kohaerenz-protokoll.md:13-13]
Under the TSDP model, Nyx is defined as the "Fight-EP." ^[Sources/drive/reader-s-report-a-critical-assessment-of-kohaerenz-protokoll.md:31-31]
A technical audit treats Nyx, together with Lex, Kiko, and Lia, as one of the alters that must be handled as a discrete functional module. ^[Sources/drive/technical-audit-research-mandate-the-kohaerenz-protokoll-fra.md:12-12]
When Kael drops the amnesic barriers separating Nyx from Lex, Kiko, and Lia, he becomes a mutual-information-dense structure. ^[Sources/drive/technical-audit-research-mandate-the-kohaerenz-protokoll-fra.md:38-38]
One reader's theory holds that Nyx's rage may be driven by a hidden vulnerability he is desperate to conceal. ^[Sources/drive/reader-s-report-a-critical-assessment-of-kohaerenz-protokoll.md:45-45]
