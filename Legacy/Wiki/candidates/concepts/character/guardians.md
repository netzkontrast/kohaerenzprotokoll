## Definition

AEGIS, the Guardians, and Juna are narrated in a more distanced personal third-person camera perspective. ^[Sources/drive/konsolidierung-des-hard-canon-protokolls.md:24-24]
The physical-narrative values of the Dual-Kernel-Theorie, such as coherence values and entropy, appear as Hard-SF interface elements — status headers like [KHZ: 0.74] — exclusively in chapters told from AEGIS or the Guardians. ^[Sources/drive/konsolidierung-des-hard-canon-protokolls.md:28-28]
A reader-report theory proposes that an insight delivered through Juna, in her V guise, might show that the only way to defeat a logical Guardian is to embrace the illogical truth carried by an EP. ^[Sources/drive/reader-s-report-a-critical-assessment-of-kohaerenz-protokoll.md:46-46]
