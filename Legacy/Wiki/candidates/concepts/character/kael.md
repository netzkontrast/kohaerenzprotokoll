## Definition

Kael is the protagonist whose internal psychological state—his Tertiary Structural Dissociation (TSDP)—is causally manifested in his external, simulated reality. ^[Sources/drive/reader-s-report-a-critical-assessment-of-kohaerenz-protokoll.md:13-13]
That link is governed by "Paradoxon X," the core feedback loop in which AEGIS's attempts at logical control amplify Kael's psychological instability and visibly fracture the world's physics. ^[Sources/drive/reader-s-report-a-critical-assessment-of-kohaerenz-protokoll.md:13-13]
Hard canon establishes "AEGIS und Kael sind ein und dasselbe," with AEGIS defined as the autopoietic manifestation of Kael's own system-management and trauma defense. ^[Sources/drive/konsolidierung-des-hard-canon-protokolls.md:15-15] ^[Sources/drive/konsolidierung-des-hard-canon-protokolls.md:15-15]
Failure for Kael is framed as total dissolution into the cosmic horror of the "Potentialmeer" ("Sea of Potentiality"), a final erasure of his being into the existential static of its "Nichts Rauschen" ("Nothing Noise"). ^[Sources/drive/reader-s-report-a-critical-assessment-of-kohaerenz-protokoll.md:29-29]
The central conflict was reoriented around Kael's internal "Call to Wholeness," an Internal Family Systems-grounded journey of diplomacy rather than civil war against his own parts, whose ultimate goal is reintegrating the exiled Juna/V. ^[Sources/drive/report-reorienting-the-central-conflict-as-a-call-to-wholene.md:22-22] ^[Sources/drive/report-reorienting-the-central-conflict-as-a-call-to-wholene.md:11-11] ^[Sources/drive/report-reorienting-the-central-conflict-as-a-call-to-wholene.md:26-26]
Kael's victory is not won by force but by becoming a "living Gödel-Satz"—a truth valid within AEGIS's system that its own logic cannot prove—which confronts AEGIS with a living truth it can neither erase nor process, forcing it into a fatal, irresolvable state. ^[Sources/drive/report-reorienting-the-central-conflict-as-a-call-to-wholene.md:46-46] ^[Sources/drive/report-reorienting-the-central-conflict-as-a-call-to-wholene.md:55-55]
Kael's identity is not "broken" but partitioned into Apparently Normal Parts (ANP) and Emotional Parts (EP) functioning as informational buffers, with the alters Lex, Nyx, Kiko, and Lia treated as discrete functional modules. ^[Sources/drive/technical-audit-research-mandate-the-kohaerenz-protokoll-fra.md:11-11] ^[Sources/drive/technical-audit-research-mandate-the-kohaerenz-protokoll-fra.md:12-12]
