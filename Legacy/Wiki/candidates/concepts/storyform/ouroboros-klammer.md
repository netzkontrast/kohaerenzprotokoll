## Definition

The novel's finale does not resolve in a classical way but instead flows directly into the backstory of the very first chapter, with the characters' roles reversed. ^[Sources/drive/konsolidierung-des-hard-canon-protokolls.md:34-34]
This structure is a mandatory design requirement: the finale must be built as an "Ouroboros" that returns to chapter 1 with a swap of roles. ^[Sources/drive/konsolidierung-des-hard-canon-protokolls.md:49-49]
The resulting open, circular ending finally dismantles the question of which reality is the true one, and in doing so it closes the loop between trauma and healing. ^[Sources/drive/konsolidierung-des-hard-canon-protokolls.md:34-34]
