## Definition

The Hard Canon Protocol defines functional multiplicity as the target end-state of character development: not fusion into a single person, but a cooperative, harmonious system operating without amnesia barriers between its parts. ^[Sources/drive/konsolidierung-des-hard-canon-protokolls.md:13-13]
In System Kael, Kael reaches this state by cooperatively integrating his Apparently Normal Parts (ANPs), which manage daily life, with his Emotional Parts (EPs), which hold his trauma. ^[Sources/drive/report-reorienting-the-central-conflict-as-a-call-to-wholene.md:53-53]
This cooperative model is also framed as a 'Dynamic Multiplicity' that triumphs over a 'Static Unity' ideal, positioning a cooperative, systemic self as superior to an idealized, monolithic one. ^[Sources/drive/report-reorienting-the-central-conflict-as-a-call-to-wholene.md:57-57]
