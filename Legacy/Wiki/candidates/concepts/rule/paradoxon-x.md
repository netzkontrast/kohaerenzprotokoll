## Definition

"Paradoxon X" names the core feedback loop linking Kael's psychology to the world's physics: AEGIS's attempts at logical control function as negative feedback that amplifies Kael's psychological instability, and that amplified instability functions as positive feedback that visibly fractures the world's physics. ^[Sources/drive/reader-s-report-a-critical-assessment-of-kohaerenz-protokoll.md:13-13]
The report proposes dramatizing "Paradoxon X" concretely rather than describing it abstractly: AEGIS enforcing the architectural laws of KW1-Konstrukt-Stadt (Lex's logical world) should directly cause a violent geological cataclysm in KW2-Mnemosyne-Archipel (the EPs' emotional world). ^[Sources/drive/reader-s-report-a-critical-assessment-of-kohaerenz-protokoll.md:43-43]
