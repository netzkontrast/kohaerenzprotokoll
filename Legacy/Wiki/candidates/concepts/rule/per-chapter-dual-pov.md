## Definition

Point-of-view in the Kohärenz-Protokoll is strictly bound to whichever character is active in a given chapter, so POV switches deterministically with the active-character assignment per chapter. ^[Sources/drive/konsolidierung-des-hard-canon-protokolls.md:19-19]
The thirteen Kael alters narrate their chapters in an extremely polyphonic, emotionally close first-person ("Ich-Perspektive") mode, chosen to make their dissociation (TSDP) maximally immersive for the reader. ^[Sources/drive/konsolidierung-des-hard-canon-protokolls.md:23-23]
AEGIS, the Guardians, and Juna are instead narrated in a more distanced personal third-person ("Kamera-Perspektive") mode. ^[Sources/drive/konsolidierung-des-hard-canon-protokolls.md:24-24]
The Dual-Kernel-Theorie's physical-narrative values, such as coherence values and entropy, surface as hard-SF interface elements like status headers (e.g. [KHZ: 0.74]) exclusively in AEGIS or Guardian chapters. ^[Sources/drive/konsolidierung-des-hard-canon-protokolls.md:28-28]
In Kael and Juna chapters the same underlying system instead manifests purely organically, through physical glitches, somatic markers, temperature drops (Landauer principle), and rifts in the environment. ^[Sources/drive/konsolidierung-des-hard-canon-protokolls.md:28-28]
This split is not stylistic flavor: the polyphonic pronouns and mosaic structure of the Kael chapters are mechanical tools that force the reader to synthesize the K1 kernel. ^[Sources/drive/technical-audit-research-mandate-the-kohaerenz-protokoll-fra.md:36-36]
