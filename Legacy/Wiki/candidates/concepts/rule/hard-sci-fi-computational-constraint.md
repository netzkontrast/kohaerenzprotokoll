## Definition

Within the Kohärenz-Protokoll, "Hard Sci-Fi" is not treated as a stylistic choice but as a computational constraint that governs the system. ^[Sources/drive/technical-audit-research-mandate-the-kohaerenz-protokoll-fra.md:3-3]
Under this constraint, internal contradictions are treated as equivalent to parity errors in a high-density storage array, and they trigger systemic narrative collapse, described as informational heat death. ^[Sources/drive/technical-audit-research-mandate-the-kohaerenz-protokoll-fra.md:3-3]
