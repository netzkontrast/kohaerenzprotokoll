## Definition

The narrative is explicitly structured as "nicht-linear" (non-linear) rather than following chronological order. ^[Sources/drive/konsolidierung-des-hard-canon-protokolls.md:29-29]
The current timeline within the "Kernwelten" is repeatedly interrupted by hard cuts, memory ruptures, and flashbacks into the "Nichts-Rauschen" and the "Basisrealität". ^[Sources/drive/konsolidierung-des-hard-canon-protokolls.md:29-29]
This non-linear, rupture-driven structure formally mirrors the "zeitlose Wesen von Traumata" (timeless nature of trauma). ^[Sources/drive/konsolidierung-des-hard-canon-protokolls.md:29-29]
