## Definition

Kael's "Manager" parts occupy a protective role and, in that role, function as the "authors of his unreliable narrative." ^[Sources/drive/report-reorienting-the-central-conflict-as-a-call-to-wholene.md:25-25]
These Manager parts actively create memory gaps ("Gedächtnislücken") in order to suppress trauma and censor Kael's own life story. ^[Sources/drive/report-reorienting-the-central-conflict-as-a-call-to-wholene.md:25-25]
When this suppression breaks down, it leaks out as disruptive emotional intrusions, fragmented memory flashes, and disproportionate emotional reactions that Kael's Manager parts can no longer suppress. ^[Sources/drive/report-reorienting-the-central-conflict-as-a-call-to-wholene.md:24-24]
