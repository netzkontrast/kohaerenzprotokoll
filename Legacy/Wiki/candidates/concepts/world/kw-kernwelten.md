## Definition

The Kernwelten are four internal worlds (KW1–KW4) that map onto Kael's alters: KW1 to the rational Lex, KW2 to the traumatized Emotional Parts (EPs), KW3 to the paranoid protectors Alex and Nyx, and KW4 to the healing potential of Rhys and Selene. ^[Sources/drive/reader-s-report-a-critical-assessment-of-kohaerenz-protokoll.md:13-13]
The current timeline inside the Kernwelten is repeatedly broken by hard cuts, memory-cracks (Erinnerungs-Risse), and flashbacks into the Nichts-Rauschen and the base reality. ^[Sources/drive/konsolidierung-des-hard-canon-protokolls.md:29-29]
In the high-entropy environment of KW2, the Mnemosyne-Archipel, a single monolithic consciousness is treated as a liability rather than a strength. ^[Sources/drive/technical-audit-research-mandate-the-kohaerenz-protokoll-fra.md:9-9]
"Risse" (cracks) that occur within the Mnemosyne-Archipel are treated as dialetheias: points where two contradictory statements are both simultaneously true. ^[Sources/drive/technical-audit-research-mandate-the-kohaerenz-protokoll-fra.md:26-26]
The story's climax is staged as an "ontological rotation" taking place inside the server core of the Mnemosyne-Archipel (KW2). ^[Sources/drive/technical-audit-research-mandate-the-kohaerenz-protokoll-fra.md:36-36]

## Where sources agree

- Rather than explaining the "Overhead" concept of the Protocol Ontology directly, Kael's profound psychological exhaustion should be dramatized as the physics of his Kernwelt decaying and losing energy. ^[Sources/drive/reader-s-report-a-critical-assessment-of-kohaerenz-protokoll.md:43-43]
- Rather than describing "Paradoxon X" abstractly, it should be dramatized as AEGIS enforcing the architectural laws of KW1 (Lex's logical world) and thereby directly causing a violent geological cataclysm in KW2 (the EPs' emotional world). ^[Sources/drive/reader-s-report-a-critical-assessment-of-kohaerenz-protokoll.md:43-43]
- The "Kollaps-Kern" (K0), tied to the Mnemosyne-Archipel, is treated as the engine of history in the Kernwelten: time only moves forward because information is erased. ^[Sources/drive/technical-audit-research-mandate-the-kohaerenz-protokoll-fra.md:6-6]
