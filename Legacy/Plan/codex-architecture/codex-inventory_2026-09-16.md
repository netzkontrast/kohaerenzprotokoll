# Codex/Wiki/Canon/NCP/graph inventory

First deliverable of the `todo.md` task "Codex- und Wiki-Architektur für
kontext-effiziente Romanarbeit". Scope is exactly item 1 of the "Reihenfolge
für die nächste Session" list: files, sizes, generators, graph entities,
manual vs. generated content, and every consumer/reference — **not** the
authority matrix (item 2) or anything downstream of it. Engineering language

## 7. Cross-cutting observations for the next step (authority matrix)

These are observations, not decisions — item 2 of `todo.md` ("Autoritätsmatrix
beschließen") is the next session's job, and per the migration rules
("Wie werden Widersprüche sichtbar gemacht, ohne sie automatisch
aufzulösen?") nothing below is resolved here:

1. **Two glossaries with different jobs, same name**: `Codex/GLOSSARY.md`
   (292 KB, 602 entries, generated, domain vocabulary) vs. `Wiki/GLOSSARY.md`
   (2.4 KB, hand-authored, operational/process terms). `CLAUDE.md` already
   distinguishes them in prose ("Operational terms are defined in
   `Wiki/GLOSSARY.md`; the domain glossary remains generated at
   `Codex/GLOSSARY.md`") but nothing enforces the split structurally.
2. **Two graphs**: `.agency/session.db` (canon/novel provenance, 5.2 MB,
   20 node labels) and `Wiki/graph/edges.jsonl` (research-wiki relations,
   currently empty). `CLAUDE.md` states "'the graph' always means the
   provenance graph … it never receives wiki page bodies (D-W2)" — so the
   split is a decision already made (D-W2), just not yet reflected in a
   single inventory document until now.
3. **The `concept` CodexEntry bucket is doing the work of several entity
   types** (§1 above) — the flattened `Kategorie:` convention is a
   workaround for Spec 132's closed 5-value enum, not a designed taxonomy.
   This is the most concrete input to architecture question 2.
4. **Codex is 100% generated from the graph; Wiki is currently 0% populated;
   Canon and NCP are 100% hand-authored.** The "verbindliches
   Informationsmodell" the task wants sits across all four, but they are
   at completely different lifecycle stages today — the authority matrix
   needs to account for Wiki's emptiness as a real current state, not
   design around a populated Wiki that doesn't exist yet.
5. **Process metadata dominates the graph** (`Invocation`/`Event` = 69% of
   3953 total nodes). Any future graph-size/context-cost concern should
   distinguish "graph is big" (true, 5.2 MB) from "novel content is big"
   (1214 nodes, much smaller) — they are not the same number.
6. **Five documented edge-creating verbs have zero instances** (§5) —
   worth an explicit decision (use them, or note them as not-yet-adopted)
   rather than silent absence.
