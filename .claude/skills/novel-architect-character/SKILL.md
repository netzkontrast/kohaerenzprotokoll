## Current model

Resolution is **functional multiplicity, never fusion**. Kael is the Host/ANP surface; inner differentiation must be recognizable through syntax, lexicon, somatics and action signatures rather than labels. Early Arc I preserves the multiplicity veil; later arcs progressively allow conscious cooperation.

## Workflow

1. Identify which character/function actually carries the scene's action.
2. Check the canonical profile before inventing motive, phobia, vocabulary or somatic cue.
3. Check reveal timing for the target chapter.
4. Use at most the cue density permitted by the current arc/brief.
5. Preserve distinction between character function and symbolic/cosmological role.
6. When a voice cue changes a later scene, update/check its downstream recognition and relationship state.

## Key locks

- AEGIS is tragically innocent, not malicious.
- Juna is a cosmological constant/witness function, not merely a love interest; early grammar is effect rather than explicit subject/action.
- Silas and Oblivion are mirror functions and should not be flattened into ordinary cast members.
- Oblivion is recognized by the **result of perfect removal/order**, not by a tagged voice.
- Kael's early prose remains Kael's register even when another function leaks through; Lex-style syntax in early Arc I is restricted to artefacts/somatic traces according to current decisions.
- No eliminated parts at resolution.
