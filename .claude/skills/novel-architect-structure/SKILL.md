## Structural invariants

- 13/14 and 26/27 are **mode changes**, not the true storyform turn.
- 35/36 = Vortex 1 and the operative B→A Truth-Rotation.
- 37 = Reward / false victory; it must genuinely improve conditions while remaining insufficient.
- 38/39 = Vortex 2 / synthesis beyond the old binary.
- 40 = Coda outside the Hero's Journey count.
- A's optionlock and B's timelock remain distinguishable and diegetically legible.

## Chapter design test

For every chapter, state:

1. What state enters?
2. What concrete event/action happens?
3. What unique structural job does this chapter do that no neighbor already does?
4. Which Storyform/arc pressure becomes more constrained?
5. What state exits?
6. What later payoff depends on this chapter?

If two adjacent chapters answer these identically, merge, differentiate or redesign them.

## Do not

- Use abstract theory labels as substitutes for events.
- Repeat the same escalation under different philosophical titles.
- Spend Vortex information early for spectacle.
- Resolve functional multiplicity as fusion.
