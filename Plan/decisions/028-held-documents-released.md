# 028 — the held documents are released, under four rules

**Date:** 2026-10-16 · **Decided by:** the author, answering four questions in the session · **Status:** in force

## What was asked

Seventy landed documents were held unread (`Plan/questions-for-the-author.md`, Part 2, of 2026-10-15),
each by a rule the project could not lift alone: documents that call figures by earlier names, the
album, material about a real diagnosis and about persons, the gold list of
`aegis-manifest-genesis-krise-reboot-2`, and one empty export.

## What was chosen

The author's answers, verbatim:

| group | answer | what it means here |
|---|---|---|
| the early names | „Lesen, Namen nie schreiben“ | read with the full pass; the earlier names of Kael, Juna and the parts are never written or quoted — not in a census, note, page, record or question; a span holding one is not quoted; pages carry the current names only |
| the album | „Lesen, leichter Durchgang“ | census and note, no readings on wiki pages |
| DIS and personal documents | „Lesen, leichter Durchgang“ | census and note of the novel's terms only, nothing on wiki pages; what a document holds about a real person or diagnosis is described in one neutral line of its kind, with no personal fact and no name |
| reboot-2's gold list | „Gold-Liste aufgeben, neu lesen“ | the frozen candidate list of 2026-09-30 is retired (kept under the run's `retired-gold-2026-09-30/`), and a new reader reads the document from scratch |

## What was rejected

- Accepting a case-only zero on a gold list: the checker keeps refusing it everywhere.
- Writing the early names on pages, either as written or as „the early name of …“.
- Readings from the album or the personal material on wiki pages.

## Still open

- `kohaerenz-protokoll-plot-entwurf-finalisierung` landed empty; a new export waits on a yes.
- `Coherence Protocol.mp3` waits on consent to a third-party transcription service.

## What would change our mind

A page or record that needs an early name to stay true — then the author is asked again, and the name
stays unwritten until answered.
