# 006 — Every draft is back in question; `Sources/` is the starting point

**Date:** 2026-09-24 · **Decided by:** the author · **Status:** chosen, applied to the records

## What was chosen

The author's words, after documents 7 and 8 were read and C6–C10 put to them:

> „Alle alten Entwürfe kommen wieder in Frage und müssen diskutiert werden —
> sources wird die neue Ausgangslage."

Three consequences:

1. **No source retires another.** A document calling itself „Source-of-Truth",
   stating „bei Konflikt gewinnt das Neuere", saying „Aktueller Kanon" or calling
   an earlier version „Frühere Drafts" makes a claim about authority. The wiki
   has always recorded such claims and not applied them; now nothing else applies
   them either. The five Guardians of 2025 are as much in question as the two of
   2026.
2. **A conflict is an agenda item, not a pending ruling.** C1–C10 and every
   conflict after them are discussed with the author, one at a time. A conflict
   closes when the author decides it, and the decision is written into the
   conflict record with its date.
3. **`Sources/` is the baseline.** What the project knows starts from the landed
   documents — the 2025 research and the 2026 canon-era documents alike — not
   from `Legacy/Canon/`, the NCP files, the reset of 2026-04-30 or any tiering
   that sorts documents before reading them.

## What this replaces

`GOAL.md` §3.2 proposes tiers (T0 locks … T5 „Steinbruch: alles vor dem Reset
2026-04-30") and a rule that the higher tier and then the newer date wins. That
rule is **suspended**: it would have settled C6–C10 automatically, in favour of
the newest document, and the author has said the old drafts come back into
question. The tiers stay in `GOAL.md` as the brief wrote them, marked with this
decision; they may still describe *where* a document came from, never *whether*
it counts.

## What was rejected

**Applying „newer wins" and marking the older side superseded.** It is what
documents 7 and 8 themselves ask for, and it is the predecessor's defect in a new
place: letting a source grant itself authority.

**Deleting or demoting the 2025 readings on pages like `guardians`.** They stay,
attributed and dated, beside the 2026 ones.

## What would change our mind

The author deciding individual conflicts — each decision recorded where the
conflict is — or naming a rule for a class of them.
