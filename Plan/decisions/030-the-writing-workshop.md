# 030 — The writing workshop: how the prose is written, and the tools built before the first scene

**Date:** 2026-10-26 · **Decided by:** the author, through ten questions the session put with suggestions · **Status:** in force, the tools provisional

## What was asked

> Think Hard and Long about the project - and how to proceed - so we can start developing the Tools we Need to Write the Story.
> And use askuser Tool to ask me your 10 Most important questions - with suggestions

## What was chosen

1. **A session writes the prose scene by scene; the author approves each scene's beats first and edits the scene before
   the next** (question A of `Plan/concept/novel-writing-plan_2026-09-29.md` §11: A2).
2. **Weichen are answered only when a chapter needs them**, from its briefing — no round of answers in advance.
3. **Kap 1 has no working base yet**: the ten drafts stay poles; the chapter starts from its briefing after its Weichen.
4. **The order: a pilot Kap 1–3, then Akt I in order.**
5. **A briefing carries the decided and the proposed, marked apart** (`[K]` / `[V]` / `[S]`), with the open Weichen as
   questions before the scene.
6. **Continuity is kept per chapter in four tables**: knowledge, time, heat and numbers, objects/places/names — and every
   draft is checked against them.
7. **The prose rules of the sources go into a register with status**; each is confirmed or rejected by the author's words,
   and the confirmed, checkable ones are checked by code.
8. **A chapter in the pilot is 3,000–5,000 words** (W16, answered for the pilot; several scenes per chapter).
9. **A scene is reviewed with marks per paragraph in the app; approval is the author's words**, recorded in `kanon.md`.
10. **Every tool is built before the first scene**: `prose_rules.py`, `weiche.py`, `continuity.py`, `brief.py`, `manuscript.py`,
    the Schreibtisch in the app (`Plan/concept/writing-workshop_2026-10-26.md`).

## What was rejected

- A1 (a session drafts whole chapters) — the September draft is what that produced, and the author rejected its quality.
- A3 (the author writes, sessions read) — more of the author's time than the author chose to give.
- A decision session over all 29 Weichen before the prose — the author prefers them per chapter.
- Draft J as the base of Kap 1, or a new draft from A–J — nothing is the base before briefing and Weichen.
- Short chapters (1,500–2,000) or no length — the author set 3,000–5,000.
- A button that approves — approval stays the author's words in the repository (P0).

## What would change our mind

The pilot. If after Kap 1–3 the author writes without the briefing, or the register is never consulted, the tools are demoted
(`Plan/concept/writing-workshop_2026-10-26.md` §7). If the scenes come out well under or over 3,000–5,000, W16 is answered again.
