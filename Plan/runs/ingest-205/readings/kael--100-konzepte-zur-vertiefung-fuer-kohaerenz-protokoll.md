## Reading — `100-konzepte-zur-vertiefung-fuer-kohaerenz-protokoll`, 2025-04-29, the hundred concepts list — the concepts proposed for deepening around Kael

The hundred concepts list is a list of topics worth deepening, each with a `Begründung`; it defines nothing about Kael, it gives reasons. Group I names the model for him: „Das Kernmodell für Kael“ ^[?], with the demand that the complexity be shown consistently. Integration is put as a question to answer: „Was ist das realistische Ziel für Kael?“ ^[?], followed by „Nicht unbedingt Fusion, sondern Kooperation.“ ^[?] Attachment theory is proposed because it „Erklärt Kaels (und der Anteile) Schwierigkeiten mit Vertrauen und Nähe“ ^[?]. Jung's individuation is proposed as „Bietet einen übergeordneten Rahmen für Kaels Reise zur Ganzheit“ ^[?], and Ricoeur's narrative identity because „Kael konstruiert seine Identität durch Erzählen“ ^[?], with the open question how AEGIS and Juna influence his story. The Selbst is named „Quelle der Heilung und Integration“ ^[?]. The Kernwelten are called „Manifestationen von Kaels Psyche“ ^[?].
