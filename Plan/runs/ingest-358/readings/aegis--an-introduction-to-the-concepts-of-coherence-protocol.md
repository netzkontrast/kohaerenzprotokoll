## Reading — `an-introduction-to-the-concepts-of-coherence-protocol`, 2025-11-03, the concepts introduction — AEGIS as the flawed god of exclusion

The concepts introduction, an orientation text for newcomers, describes `AEGIS` as the side that „believes that true, stable order can only be achieved by eliminating all contradictions“ ^[?] and calls it „AEGIS is the primary antagonist of the story“ ^[?]. Its purpose is stated as „primary purpose is to create and maintain order by minimizing entropy“ ^[?], and its origin as „Born from a chaotic“ ^[?] Sea of Potentiality. The text does not call AEGIS evil: „its destructive actions are the inevitable result of its own flawed programming and a tragic misunderstanding of the nature of true coherence“ ^[?]. In its stated-goal table, „Its rigid methods of control paradoxically“ ^[?] create the instability the system tries to eliminate, named Risse or glitches. The Juna/V section describes „a living, breathing truth that AEGIS“ ^[?] cannot process. The text is a reader's guide and decides nothing.
