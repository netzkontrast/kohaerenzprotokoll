## 2026-10-13 — `aegis-seele-und-entropie`, 2025-04-21, the Seelenpaar report

**The Seelenpaar report analyses a scenario in which Aegis, after failing to grasp a transcendent pair, isolates and fragments the soul; it names no Kael.**

In the scenario the report analyses, Aegis answers the failed analysis and the reboot with „Isolation und Fragmentierung der Entropiequelle (der Seele)“ ^[?]. The fragmented soul is imagined with amnesia and longing: „Mögliche Amnesie oder fragmentierte Erinnerungen“ ^[?] and „tiefgreifender, unaufhörlicher Schmerz oder eine überwältigende Sehnsucht nach der verlorenen Hälfte“ ^[?]. The other fragment is asked about as possibly „stärker beschädigt, vielleicht tiefer in Aegis' System isoliert“ ^[?]. The report offers these as options and writes no Kael as one of the fragments.

Where it stands in the record's own terms: a scenario of an origin by fragmentation, with Aegis as agent; recorded, not applied and not decided.
