## Reading — `roman-konzept-kael-aegis-simulation`, 2025-05-01, the simulation concept — Lex as the logic Alter who maps AEGIS

The simulation concept describes Lex as an Alter who emerges in part 1: „Lex (kühl, logisch, analytisch, aber emotional distanziert“ ^[?]. As the paper proposes the middle of the book, Lex takes on a task of his own: „Lex könnte besessen davon sein, die Logik des Systems AEGIS zu entschlüsseln“ ^[?]. In part 2 the system's mapping is done „durch den logikorientierten Alter Lex, versucht aktiv, die Funktionsweise von AEGIS zu kartieren“ ^[?]. The wording is conditional (`könnte`) in the first and active in the second; both are proposals of a concept paper.
