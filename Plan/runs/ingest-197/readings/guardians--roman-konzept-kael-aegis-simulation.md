## Reading — `roman-konzept-kael-aegis-simulation`, 2025-05-01, the simulation concept — the Guardians as AEGIS's enforcers, to be shown in more detail later

The simulation concept lists the Guardians among AEGIS's methods: „Einsatz von Wächtern ('Guardians'“ ^[?], next to surveillance and manipulation of reality perception. In part 1 they appear as low-ranking enforcers of rigid rules. In part 2 it proposes that they be drawn more finely: „Auch die Wächter ('Guardians') könnten differenzierter dargestellt werden“ ^[?]. Lex, it proposes, maps their behaviour along with AEGIS's interventions: „im Verhalten der Wächter und in der Struktur der Kernwelten“ ^[?].
