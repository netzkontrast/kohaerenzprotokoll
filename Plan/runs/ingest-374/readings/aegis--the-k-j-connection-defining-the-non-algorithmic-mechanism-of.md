The K-J report opens with „the persistent and inexplicable failure of the Autonomous Entropic Gatekeeper for Integrity Systems“ ^[?] to control System Kael, and gives the axiom „AEGIS is what AEGIS prevents from not being“ ^[?]. It states that the influence comes from what „AEGIS’s own logic classifies as an irrelevant subjective anomaly“ ^[?].

Its table of vulnerabilities says AEGIS is „structurally blind to any non-local or acausal phenomena“ ^[?], and, through its hybrid architecture, „incapable of recognizing or processing emergent, coordinated cooperation“ ^[?]. The Adaptive Layer is described as „reinforcing fragmentation and suppressing integration“ ^[?] while satisfying the coherence metric.

At the end the K-J report names two choices: „AEGIS is left with only two choices“ ^[?]. The second is a paraconsistent state, a system „capable of processing a truth“ ^[?] it can never experientially understand. The K-J report defines and proposes this account; it is recorded here, not applied.
