## Reading — `creative-expose-the-correspondence-principle-as-narrative-ar`, 2025-11-03, the correspondence exposé — Kael as the arena of coherence against correspondence

The correspondence exposé frames Kael as the side of correspondence. Its table gives him the column heading `Kael: Coherence *through* Integration` and says of it: „Kael’s journey is a quest to make his internal state correspond to the undeniable, objective facts of his past“ ^[?]. Of the protagonist, `System Kael`, it writes: „His very consciousness is the battlefield where the war between coherence and correspondence is waged.“ ^[?] It says: „Kael's condition is defined by the“ ^[?] `TSDP`, which the exposé applies.

Its four senses of correspondence are the exposé's own construction. On the physical one it reads Kael's arc this way: „Kael's character arc is a narrative metaphor for Niels Bohr's“ ^[?] `Correspondence Principle`. On the mathematical one: „The nature of Kael's healed identity is modeled on the concept of a mathematical“ ^[?] correspondence, a multifunction. For the climax it writes that „Kael confronts AEGIS with his healed, integrated self“ ^[?], a living Gödel-Satz, which forces AEGIS's system into collapse or transformation.

The exposé states this as the novel's design; it makes no canon claim for itself.
