## Reading — `creative-expose-the-correspondence-principle-as-narrative-ar`, 2025-11-03, the correspondence exposé — the Kernwelten as externalised alter landscapes

The correspondence exposé gives the `Kernwelten` as „These are specialized, externalized psychological landscapes“ ^[?], each mapped to the functions and phobias of Kael's alters. It lists four, with the atmosphere it gives each: the first is „The oppressive, rigid domain of the rational ANP“ ^[?] `Lex`; the second is the trauma-landscape of the Emotional Parts; the third is „The paranoid, zero-trust“ ^[?] bunker-world of `Alex` and `Nyx`; the fourth is „associated with the relational alters“ ^[?] `Rhys` and `Selene`. In its three-act plot, act II forces Kael into the Risse and the unstable Kernwelten.
