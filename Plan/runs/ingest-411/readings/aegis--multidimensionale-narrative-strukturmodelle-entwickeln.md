## Reading — `multidimensionale-narrative-strukturmodelle-entwickeln`, 2025-07-03, the multidimensional-models report — AEGIS as the controlling meta-narrative of a case study

The multidimensional-models report reads `System Kael` as a case study for its model of several parallel storyforms, and AEGIS as a case of the controlling meta-narrative. It writes that AEGIS „fungiert als ein übergeordnetes Meta-System, das versucht“ ^[?] to regulate and control the Kernwelten, which it takes to be autopoietic storyforms (L110). The central drama, the report's `Paradoxon der fehlausgerichteten Kohärenz`, arises because AEGIS does not understand these worlds' inner logic and reads their functions as errors or entropy; the report calls this a case of a controlling meta-narrative against autonomous sub-narratives.

In the report's dialectic of thesis, antithesis and synthesis, AEGIS stands in the thesis: the `Kohärenz Protokoll` of AEGIS is „die Erreichung von Stabilität und Ordnung durch rigide, logische Top-Down-Kontrolle“ ^[?] (L210).

Where it applies the postmodern critique of grand narratives, it reads AEGIS as „die fehlerhafte, traumatisierte Meta-Erzählung“ ^[?], and its origin as that of „ein System, das aus Angst und dem Bedürfnis nach defensiver Kontrolle geboren wurde“ ^[?] (L277). Kael's journey is read there as an uprising against the „fehlerhafte, totalisierende große Erzählung von AEGIS“ ^[?] (L279). The report gives these as illustrations of its model, citing a source (its footnote 1), not as statements about the novel itself.
