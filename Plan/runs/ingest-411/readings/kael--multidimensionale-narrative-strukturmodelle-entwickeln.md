## Reading — `multidimensionale-narrative-strukturmodelle-entwickeln`, 2025-07-03, the multidimensional-models report — Kael as the illustration of integration and the persona-shadow model

The multidimensional-models report uses `System Kael` as its case study and reads Kael as a case of several of its borrowed models. Of narrative entanglement it writes that the link between „Kaels innerem Zustand“ ^[?] and the stability of the external Kernwelten is a clear example, and that a breakthrough, such as accepting a traumatic part, could heal a rift in the outer world without a physical chain of cause (L129).

In its Jungian persona-and-shadow model, the report reads the ANPs „wie Kael (Host) und Lex (Analytiker)“ ^[?] as the persona, and Kael's whole journey as the process of loosening the identification with the persona and confronting and integrating the traumatised shadow parts: „Kaels gesamte Reise ist der Prozess, sich von der exklusiven Identifikation mit seiner Persona zu lösen“ ^[?] (L192).

In the dialectic it places Kael in the antithesis: „Kaels psychologischer Heilungsprozess“ ^[?], a chaotic, emotional bottom-up process that appears to AEGIS as a rise of entropy (L212). In its postmodern reading the nested storyforms are „Kaels innere Anteile“ ^[?] and their attempts at healing (L277), and the journey is „ein Aufstand des lokalen, subjektiven Narrativs“ ^[?] (L279). These are the report's illustrations of its model, citing a source (its footnote 1), not the novel itself.
