## Reading — `multidimensionale-narrative-strukturmodelle-entwickeln`, 2025-07-03, the multidimensional-models report — the Kernwelten as autopoietic storyforms

The multidimensional-models report reads the Kernwelten as a case of its autopoietic storyforms: „Diese Kernwelten (z.B. die Welt der Logik, die der Emotionen) können als autopoietische Storyforms verstanden werden“ ^[?], each representing a specific psychological function (L110). In its entanglement section the stability of the Kernwelten is tied to Kael's inner state (L129), and in the persona-shadow section it adds that they „als Externalisierungen dieser psychischen Domänen gesehen werden“ ^[?] (L192). These are the report's illustrations of its model, citing a source (its footnote 1), not the novel itself.
