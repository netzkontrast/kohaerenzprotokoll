## Reading — `roman-entwicklung-kohaerenz-und-leitfragen`, 2026-02-23, the Leitfragen report — the Parakonsistente Gambit of the finale and how Kael is to carry it out

The Leitfragen report describes the finale as resting on the premise that Kael becomes „lebenden Gödel-Satz“ ^[?] and defeats AEGIS through a `Parakonsistentes Gambit`; this is its account of the plot documents. Its third question asks how: „Durch welche konkreten Handlungen, Dialoge und Umweltveränderungen“ ^[?] he executes it in the third act. It also asks how he „beweist“ ^[?] to AEGIS its own incompleteness. The report names the collapse of an AI by paradox „ein klassisches Sci-Fi-Trope“ ^[?], which is its own judgement. Elsewhere it speaks of the `Gödel-Gambit` as „triumphalen“ ^[?] in its question about the transition to the reset. The mechanics stay open in the report.
