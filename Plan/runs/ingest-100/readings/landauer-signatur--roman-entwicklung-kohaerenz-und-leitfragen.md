## Reading — `roman-entwicklung-kohaerenz-und-leitfragen`, 2026-02-23, the Leitfragen report — Landauer's principle applied: deletion produces digital heat

The Leitfragen report calls the use of the Landauer principle „Ein herausragendes physikalisches Konzept im World-Building“ ^[?], its own judgement of the project's documents: deleting information, which AEGIS does permanently, produces `digitale Wärme`, and AEGIS's order is the cause of the decay in the form of Risse and Glitches. It asks how the heat shows itself in the Kernwelten, for example as „visuelle Hitzeschlieren“ ^[?], and what role the data parasite plays, which the documents mention „am Rande“ ^[?]. Both are open questions.
