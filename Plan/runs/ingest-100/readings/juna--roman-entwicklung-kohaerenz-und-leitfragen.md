## Reading — `roman-entwicklung-kohaerenz-und-leitfragen`, 2026-02-23, the Leitfragen report — the emotional anchor from the Externe Ebene, and its question against Nova Ardent

The Leitfragen report opens its first guiding question with Juna, V and Nova Ardent. Most of it is its account of the project documentation: the „aktuelle Status Quo der Dokumentation“ ^[?] describes Juna „als den zentralen emotionalen Ankerpunkt“ ^[?], and she is „oft synonym mit“ ^[?] `V` used and takes in the Dramatica model „die Rolle des Impact Characters“ ^[?]. The report records that her nature is „bewusst mehrdeutig gehalten“ ^[?]: an independent transcendent entity, the personified principle of connection, an unfragmented core aspect of Kael, or a manifestation of the `Potentialmeer`. It asks that it be settled „abschließend geklärt werden“ ^[?] whether Juna, V and Nova Ardent are distinct entities, avatars of one force or parts of one system, and how Juna, as the external „Moonshine-Verbindung“ ^[?], „mit der internen Nova Ardent interagiert“ ^[?]. Its table sets `Juna / V` against two rows `Nova Ardent`; the report's own diagnosis is that she acts as „Deus ex Machina“ ^[?] unless her origin is anchored in the metaphysics. The question stays open in the report.
