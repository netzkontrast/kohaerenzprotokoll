## Reading — `roman-entwicklung-kohaerenz-und-leitfragen`, 2026-02-23, the Leitfragen report — the host of the ten-alter table, and an open question about his origin

The Leitfragen report (an analyst's review of the project's master documents) names Kael as the protagonist whose story it audits: „Protagonisten Kael“ ^[?]. Most of what it says about him is its account of other documents. It reports that in one chapter he is „Elite-Cybersoldat der Aegis Coalition“ ^[?], ordered to eliminate Nova Ardent; it reports that a further character-concept document lists ten alters, the first of them „Kael (Host)“ ^[?], and its own table of that roster sets `Kael` as `Host / ANP` in the Konstrukt-Stadt. On his condition the report writes that the main figure and the clinical picture „Dissoziativen Identitätsstörung“ ^[?] have „einen tiefen, persönlichen Hintergrund“ ^[?], and that the psychological coherence is therefore to be treated with care. Its own open question: „Ist Kael ein simulierter Avatar, ein psychologisches Konstrukt oder ein Sub-Prozess von Dr. Aris Thorne?“ ^[?] The report asks this and answers nothing.
