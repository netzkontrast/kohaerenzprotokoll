## Reading — `roman-entwicklung-kohaerenz-und-leitfragen`, 2026-02-23, the Leitfragen report — two rosters reported: ten alters against 13 entities

The Leitfragen report finds a contradiction, in its account of two other documents. One, it says, specifies the Kael system as a TSDP system „bestehend aus 13 Entitäten“ ^[?] (ANPs and EPs); another „Charakter-Konzept-Dokument listet und profiliert jedoch exakt 10 Alters“ ^[?]. The ten it names are in one line, among them `Oblivion` and `Silas`. The report calls the break „kritisch“ ^[?] and asks which matrix „bildet den definitiven Kanon des Romans“ ^[?]. It asks and does not decide. Its own table, headed „Alter-Bezeichnung (10er System)“ ^[?], is its gloss of the ten-alter document: each alter with its function and Kernwelt, `Echo / Flicker` as child alters in one row. In the pacing question it names `Echo` and `Oblivion` as the alters through whom the childhood trauma is to be shown.
