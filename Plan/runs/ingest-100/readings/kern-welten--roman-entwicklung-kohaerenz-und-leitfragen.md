## Reading — `roman-entwicklung-kohaerenz-und-leitfragen`, 2026-02-23, the Leitfragen report — the four Kernwelten with their early names in brackets, and a transition mechanism it calls open

The Leitfragen report (an analyst's review of the project's master documents) says what follows as its account of other documents, not as the novel's settled state. It reports that the simulation rests on four Kernwelten, „ökologische Manifestationen von Kaels innerer psychologischer Landschaft“ ^[?], and finds that Kapitel 1 establishes the first well while the mechanics of the transition between the worlds is „unzureichend definiert“ ^[?].

Its table sets each Kernwelt beside the early name in brackets, with its psychology, Guardian, mathematical paradigm and an open transition mechanism, under the column heading „Transitions-Mechanismus (Vakant)“ ^[?]:

- „Konstrukt-Stadt (Co₁)“ ^[?], with „ANPs, Rationalität, Verdrängung“ ^[?] and „Initialisierung via Reboot“ ^[?];
- „Resonanz-Landschaft (McL)“ ^[?], with „EPs, Trauma, Subjektivität“ ^[?] and „Kaskadierender Glitch“ ^[?];
- „Grenzfeste (B)“ ^[?], with „Protektoren, Paranoia, Abwehr“ ^[?] and „Überwindung der Zero-Trust-Firewall“ ^[?];
- „Möglichkeits-Garten (Ly)“ ^[?], with „Kreativität, Potenzial, Emergenz“ ^[?] and „Gnosis-Durchbruch via Moonshine“ ^[?].

Its Leitfrage 4 asks how Kael can break through „hermetisch abgeriegelten Grenzen der Kernwelten“ ^[?]. The report only asks; it decides nothing.
