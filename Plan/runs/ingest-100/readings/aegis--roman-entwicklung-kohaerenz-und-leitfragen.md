## Reading — `roman-entwicklung-kohaerenz-und-leitfragen`, 2026-02-23, the Leitfragen report — AEGIS as the system the documents describe, and the report's questions about it

In the Leitfragen report AEGIS appears almost wholly as the report's account of other documents. It says that the documentation describes Juna as an entity who evades „Autopoiesis-System AEGIS“ ^[?]; that AEGIS is „ein Entropie-Torwächter“ ^[?] on a Zero-Trust-Architektur with Boundary Protocols against „Cross-Contamination“ ^[?]; that it „operiert nach der Kohärenztheorie der Wahrheit“ ^[?], which demands absolute formal freedom from contradiction; that its Guardians are „die Agenten von AEGIS“ ^[?]; and that it uses the dissociation of the host as a tool: „AEGIS nutzt diese Fragmentierung als Werkzeug zur Stabilisierung“ ^[?]. Of its deletion of information the report writes that AEGIS does it permanently, and that its striving for order is „die unmittelbare Ursache für den Zerfall der Welt“ ^[?]. In the further-developed model, it says, Kael wakes up after a `Universal Reboot` that AEGIS initiates. Its questions concern how Kael shows AEGIS its incompleteness (the report asks this: „Wie genau“ ^[?] he proves it) and how he crosses the borders AEGIS enforces; it settles none of them.
