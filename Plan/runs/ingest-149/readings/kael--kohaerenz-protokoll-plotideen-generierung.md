## Reading — `kohaerenz-protokoll-plotideen-generierung`, 2025-04-26, the plot-idea synthesis — Kael as avatar of a Monstergruppe-inspired structure, his DID induced by AEGIS

The plot-idea synthesis, an analysis that proposes plot seeds, calls Kael „ein Avatar einer Struktur, die von der Monstergruppe (M) inspiriert ist“ ^[?] and has him come from the Potentialmeer as a Kohärenz-Insel; of the Potentialmeer it says it contains such islands, „welche als Ursprung von Kaels Essenz angedeutet werden“ ^[?]. His dissociative disorder was induced by AEGIS's attempts at analysis: „Er leidet unter einer Dissoziativen Identitätsstörung (DID), die durch AEGIS' Analyseversuche induziert wurde“ ^[?]. The document's account of his arc is „Kaels Reise ist der Prozess von dieser durch AEGIS induzierten Fragmentierung hin zur Integration“ ^[?]; it reads this through the Internal Family Systems model, and holds that his success at integration is the failure of AEGIS's control. Among the plot seeds, Kael recognises the cause: „Kael erkennt, dass AEGIS' Analyse seine DID verursacht hat“ ^[?]. The document borrows its theory (IFS, second-order cybernetics) and states no canon claim.
