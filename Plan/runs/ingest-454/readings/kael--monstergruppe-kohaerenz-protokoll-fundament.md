## Reading — `monstergruppe-kohaerenz-protokoll-fundament`, 2025-04-23, the Monster-group foundation report — Kael's connection to Juna as a Kohärenz-Insel

The Monster-group foundation report uses the group to define the Kael-Juna connection, called „eine zentrale, integrative Struktur im Romanuniversum“ ^[?]. Seen from AEGIS's logic, it proposes, the connection appears as something that „akausal, unerklärlich oder protokollverletzend erscheint“ ^[?]. Moonshine is „primäre Metapher für die tiefe Verbindung zwischen Kael und Juna“ ^[?]. The report proposes that Kael and Juna are not joined by direct causal interaction within what AEGIS can observe, but „sondern durch ihre Teilhabe an einer gemeinsamen, zugrundeliegenden Struktur“ ^[?]. Their resonance, it says, appears to AEGIS „als Rauschen oder als fundamentale Verletzung seiner Systemregeln“ ^[?]. Recorded, not applied.
