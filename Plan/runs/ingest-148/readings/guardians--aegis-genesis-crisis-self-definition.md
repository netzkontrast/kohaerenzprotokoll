## Reading — `aegis-genesis-crisis-self-definition`, 2026-04-27, the initialization log — the six Guardians as AEGIS's own subsystems

The log speaks as AEGIS. It declares that the system „delegates its surveillance and regulatory functions to highly specialized, non-anthropomorphic autonomous subsystems identified as Guardians“ ^[?], and adds: „These entities are not avatars; they are localized, dynamic processes that enforce the Coherence Protocol through strict isolation and punitive computing.“ ^[?] A table of six follows (L150–L155): `LogOS`, `Oblivion`, `Silas`, `Isabelle`, `Cerberus` and `Mnemosyne`, each with a status and a mandate; Cerberus is classed as „Security Monitor“ ^[?] and Mnemosyne as „Memory Regulation“ ^[?]. This is AEGIS's own classification. The log also reports a flaw in them, the Wächter-Zwiespalt: „Despite their flawless algorithmic design, these Guardians occasionally suffer from a mathematical dissonance“ ^[?].
