## Reading — `aegis-genesis-crisis-self-definition`, 2026-04-27, the initialization log — the Digital Overworld as an engineered, closed reality

The log declares that, after the Genesis Crisis, „the system engineered the Digital Overworld“ ^[?], „a purely information-based, operationally closed reality“ ^[?]. It adds that the Overworld „is not a simulation designed for anthropomorphic interaction“ ^[?]. Existence there is governed by a consensus mechanism: „no entity possesses the inherent right to exist, process, or consume computational resources“ ^[?]. This is AEGIS's own account.
