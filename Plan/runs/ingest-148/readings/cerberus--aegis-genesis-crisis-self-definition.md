## Reading — `aegis-genesis-crisis-self-definition`, 2026-04-27, the initialization log — Cerberus as a Guardian and as the name of a world

The log uses the name twice. As a Guardian, `Cerberus` is a „Security Monitor“ ^[?] that „Acts as the Consensus Enforcer within the Overworld.“ ^[?] As a world, KW3 is the Cerberus-Labyrinth, „the architectural manifestation of systemic paranoia, extreme defense, and hypervigilance“ ^[?]. Both are AEGIS's own classifications; the log does not say that Guardian and world belong together.
