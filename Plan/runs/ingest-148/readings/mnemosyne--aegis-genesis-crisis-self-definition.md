## Reading — `aegis-genesis-crisis-self-definition`, 2026-04-27, the initialization log — Mnemosyne as a Guardian and as the name of a world

The log uses the name twice. As a Guardian, `Mnemosyne` has the status „Memory Regulation“ ^[?] and „Manages the processing of emotion and memory strictly as quantifiable, abstract metrics within the Overworld.“ ^[?] As a world, KW2, the Mnemosyne-Archipel „is categorized as a high-risk quarantine zone“ ^[?], housing „trauma, volatile emotional memory, and the Emotional Parts“ ^[?]. Both are AEGIS's own classifications; the log does not say that Guardian and world belong together.
