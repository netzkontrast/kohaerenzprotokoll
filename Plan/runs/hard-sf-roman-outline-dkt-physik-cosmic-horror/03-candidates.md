## Lines 11–31 — title, premise, the world's physics

„Das Kohärenz Protokoll: Architektonik eines multidimensionalen Romans", a „vollständigen,
detaillierten Bauplan" for a work of „neununddreißig Kapitel", hard SF and cosmic horror (Egan,
Watts, VanderMeer, Ligotti). The TSDP disguised for more than half the text as the hostile system of
a superintelligence, AEGIS. The DKT; four set-pieces: the Landauer-Prinzip as AEGIS' weapon (heat and
ozone), Verlinde's entropic gravity, the Bekenstein bound with the Island-Formeln, ER=EPR as the bond
between Kael and Juna — „Juna, das exilierte Ursprungs-Ich, existiert in einer B+C-Superposition", her
Moonshine-Link a non-local ER=EPR topology.
