## Reading — `roman-entwicklung-ontologie-trauma-horror`, 2026-04-16, the ontology-trauma-horror report — AEGIS as a borrowed statistical boundary and the macro-scale form of the protagonist's morning process

The ontology-trauma-horror report, an unsigned research report, translates a borrowed statistical boundary onto AEGIS: „AEGIS rechnet keine Simulation“ ^[?] and „Sie ist die absolute statistische Membran“ ^[?], which decides what may exist within experience (L39). In its synthesis the report writes „AEGIS ist die Cortisol Awakening Response (CAR) des Universums“ ^[?] and, of the same entity, „ist somit keine externe Rechenmaschine im Sinne einer klassischen Simulation“ ^[?] (L138); the report presents this as the macro-scale form of the protagonist's morning process.

For the morning loss, the report proposes that the protagonist's other identities are not lost because „Der Protagonist verliert die anderen Identitäten am Morgen nicht, weil sie spirituell verschwinden“ ^[?], but are forced into local sleep so the host can function in the world of AEGIS (L100). A table column on relevance for the novel gives the extinction of plurality in the morning as felt infection by the lawfulness of AEGIS (L63). A writing proposal places „Dies verankert die ontologische Prämisse der AEGIS-Welt“ ^[?] in the sentence structure (L76).

The report ends on an image: „durch die schiere, makellose Mechanik der universellen Existenz (AEGIS) sekündlich aussortiert und gelöscht werden“ ^[?] and „Es ist der ultimative kosmische Horror einer Welt, die absolut perfekt funktioniert“ ^[?] (L146). The report records these as proposals for the novel; this reading applies none of them.
