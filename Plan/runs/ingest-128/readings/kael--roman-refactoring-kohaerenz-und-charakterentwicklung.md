## Reading — `roman-refactoring-kohaerenz-und-charakterentwicklung`, 2026-02-26, the refactoring plan — Kael as host and as a system of eleven parts, in an assistant's plan

The refactoring plan is an assistant's proposal to the author, and what it says of Kael is its own mapping. It names him in the matrix as „(Primärer ANP)“ ^[?L52] and gives his function: „Bewahrt die Fassade der Normalität.“ ^[?] It states the structure of the system: „Das System Kael besteht aus elf hochspezialisierten Anteilen“ ^[?] and speaks of „die dissoziative Identitätsstruktur Ihres Protagonisten Kael“ ^[?]. His trauma is placed by the plan: „Die Trauma-Biografie Kaels wurzelt in der Genesis-Krise von AEGIS.“ ^[?] The physics metaphor the plan assigns to him is its proposal, not the world's physics: „Kael agiert innerhalb einer kausal begrenzten Blase.“ ^[?] The row also says „Leidet an Cache-Inkohärenz.“ ^[?], a term the plan does not define. On healing it recommends against fusion: „die Heilung für Kael nicht in einer vollständigen Fusion“ ^[?]. The plan's report of Heidegger (footnoted there) is applied to him in Akt III: „Gleichzeitig durchlebt Kael Heideggers“ ^[?]
