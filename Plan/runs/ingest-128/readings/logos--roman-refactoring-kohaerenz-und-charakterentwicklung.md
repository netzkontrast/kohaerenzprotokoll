## Reading — `roman-refactoring-kohaerenz-und-charakterentwicklung`, 2026-02-26, the refactoring plan — LogOS, guardian of logic, and the slingshot argument

The refactoring plan is an assistant's proposal to the author; in Kapitel 14 it has Kael meet LogOS: „trifft Kael auf LogOS, den Wächter der Logik“ ^[?]. It then has Kael turn the argument on him: „Die Eskalation erreicht ihren Höhepunkt, als Kael das Slingshot-Argument gegen LogOS richtet“ ^[?]. The slingshot argument (Davidson) and the correspondence theory are the plan's reported references.
