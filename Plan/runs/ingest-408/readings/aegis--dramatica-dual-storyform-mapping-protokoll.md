## Reading — `dramatica-dual-storyform-mapping-protokoll`, 2026-04-30, the dual-storyform mapping report — AEGIS as the kernel's guard, MC of its Storyform B, and the paradox it keeps open

The dual-storyform mapping report names AEGIS in the coherence kernel: „Systemisch wird dies durch AEGIS (Autonomous Entropic Gatekeeper for Integrity Systems) überwacht“ ^[?], the kernel being the foundation of the Konstrukt-Stadt. In its own Storyform B, the report says, „Hier fungiert AEGIS zwingend als Main Character (MC)“ ^[?]; in its throughline table the row Structure pairs „AEGIS / Konstrukt-Stadt“ ^[?] with Overall Story (They).

Under „Die Rolle von AEGIS in der Kern-Hierarchie“ ^[?] the report sets two placings against each other: the DKT standard „Ordnet AEGIS strikt dem“ ^[?] coherence kernel, while the Dialetheia-Toleranz proposal treats AEGIS as an instance of the other kernel despite its self-image. Its resolution is its own: the tolerance lets AEGIS be run functionally as a guard while its reason for existing binds it to the process it fends off, and the report writes the formula `AEGIS ist, was AEGIS verhindert` with italics markers in the export. „Dieser Konflikt bleibt als intendierte Paradoxie offen und wird nicht harmonisiert.“ ^[?]

In its Alternative 2 (Möbius-Verschränkung) the report has Kael and AEGIS share the MC slot: „AEGIS ist der MC in den geraden Kapiteln (System-Perspektive)“ ^[?]. In the closing section it asks that AEGIS' attempts to heal the dissociation be understood as an antagonistic act of the collapse kernel: „dass AEGIS' Versuche, diese Dissoziation zu“ ^[?] be read so, the act that provokes the total collapse. The isolation of the Verschränkungs-Insel is described as an effect that undermines „die AEGIS' Zugriffsberechtigungen mathematisch unterläuft“ ^[?].
