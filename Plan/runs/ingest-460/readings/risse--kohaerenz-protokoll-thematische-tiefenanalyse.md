## Reading — `kohaerenz-protokoll-thematische-tiefenanalyse`, 2025-04-28, the thematic blueprint — the Risse as system instabilities and limits of control

The thematic blueprint lists the Risse as system instabilities: they „Manifestieren sich als visuelle Glitches“ ^[?], and it says they represent the limits of AEGIS's control: „Sie repräsentieren die Grenzen von AEGIS' Kontrolle“ ^[?]; their frequency and intensity may rise over the phases: „Ihre Häufigkeit und Intensität können über die Phasen zunehmen“ ^[?] (L247). The matrix names life in the system and its Risse for Phase 2 and an epistemology of the Risse (L76, L77). Phase 1 plot events propose discovering the first Risse as unexplained phenomena, Phase 2 AEGIS trying to close them (L287). The world-design row for Phase 2 has them growing: „größer, häufiger, zeigen vielleicht alternative Möglichkeiten“ ^[?]. Recorded, not applied.
