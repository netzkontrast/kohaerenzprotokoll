## Reading — `kohaerenz-protokoll-thematische-tiefenanalyse`, 2025-04-28, the thematic blueprint — Kernwelten as diagnostic arenas and ontological laboratories

The thematic blueprint says of Phase 1: „Die Kernwelten dienen als diagnostische Arenen“ ^[?], and of the theme cluster on reality: „Die Kernwelten sind ontologische Laboratorien“ ^[?]. Its matrix maps four coded worlds to parts of Kael: Co₁ for order and logic, McL for network and relationship, B for chaos and shadow, Ly for potentiality and emergence (L65–L68); the logician part for Co₁, the shadow and child parts for B, the creative part for Ly (L65, L67, L68). The world-design row proposes: „Kernwelten sind so gestaltet, dass sie spezifische Anteile isolieren/analysieren“ ^[?].

Proposed designs per world: for Co₁ „Gestaltung mit steriler Ästhetik“ ^[?], for McL „Visuelle Darstellung von Verbindungen“ ^[?], for B „Düstere, verfallene oder surreale Landschaften“ ^[?] (L243–L245). The matrix assignments are labelled as examples elsewhere in the report (L56); recorded, not applied.
