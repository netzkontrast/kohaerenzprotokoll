## Reading — `ai-assisted-narrative-coherence`, 2025-10-15, the ARCHON proposal, the simple guide, the critical review, the three-act blueprint and the strategy paper of the English compilation — DID as the clinical name for Kael's condition

`DID` ^[ai-assisted-narrative-coherence.md:#5] stands in five lines. The ARCHON proposal says the protagonist's „Dissociative Identity Disorder (DID) is modeled on the“ ^[?] Theory of Structural Dissociation; the simple guide (L219 on) writes that tertiary dissociation is „the theory's model for what is clinically known as Dissociative Identity Disorder (DID).“ ^[?]

The critical review (L652 on) lists among the novel's strengths „Offers a respectful and credible depiction of DID.“ ^[?] The three-act blueprint (L831 on) reads the first symptoms of the awakening as „These are not symptoms of simple confusion but the first intrusions from the other Alters within his Dissociative Identity Disorder (DID) system“ ^[?]. The strategy paper (L1157 on) names an ethical task: „the profound challenge of sensitively representing trauma and“ ^[?] the disorder.
