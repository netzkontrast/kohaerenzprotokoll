## Reading — `ai-assisted-narrative-coherence`, 2025-10-15, the ARCHON proposal, the simple guide, the blueprint, the critical review, the strategy paper, the architecture analysis and its lexicon of the English compilation — TSDP as the clinical model of Kael's psyche, stated in the parts as tertiary dissociation

`TSDP` ^[ai-assisted-narrative-coherence.md:#30] stands throughout the compilation. The ARCHON proposal (L11–L122) names it as the model of the case-study protagonist: „whose Dissociative Identity Disorder (DID) is modeled on the“ ^[?] theory. The simple guide (L219 on) writes „Specifically, his case represents“ ^[?] Tertiary Dissociation and calls it „the theory's model for what is clinically known as Dissociative Identity Disorder (DID).“ ^[?]

The blueprint (L339 on) explains the theory: it „posits that severe trauma can prevent the integration of a single personality“ ^[?]. The critical review (L652 on) says of its role: „The novel uses the Theory of Structural Dissociation of the Personality (TSDP) for the“ ^[?] origin of fragmentation. The strategy paper (L1157 on) writes „Specifically, his internal world represents a case of“ ^[?] tertiary structural dissociation, and the architecture analysis (L1671 on) writes „Kael's psyche is modeled on the Theory of Tertiary Structural Dissociation (TSDP)“ ^[?]. The lexicon row, `TSDP (Tertiäre Strukturelle Dissoziation)`, defines it as „The Theory of Tertiary Structural Dissociation of the Personality.“ ^[?]
