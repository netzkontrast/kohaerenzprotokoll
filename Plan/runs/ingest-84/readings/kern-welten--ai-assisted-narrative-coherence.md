## Reading — `ai-assisted-narrative-coherence`, 2025-10-15, the English compilation (the blueprint, the concept document, the architecture analysis, the lexicon) — four simulated worlds made by AEGIS

The blueprint's section 5.2 says: „The four Kernwelten (Core Worlds) are simulated realities created by AEGIS as“ ^[?] laboratories, which also externalise Kael's psyche. The concept document: the Kernwelten are „specialized simulation environments created by AEGIS to manage, analyze, and control the primary fragment of its own fractured consciousness“ ^[?]. The architecture analysis: „The four Kernwelten are simulated realities created by AEGIS to manage and analyze Kael's fragmented psyche.“ ^[?] Its lexicon: „The four simulated realities (KW1-4) created by AEGIS to analyze and control Kael.“ ^[?]

The parts name the worlds differently. The concept document's table and the blueprint's headings give `KW1: Logos-Prime`, `KW2: Mnemosyne-Archipel`, `KW3: Cerberus-Labyrinth` and `KW4: Kairos-Potentialis`; the architecture analysis pairs each with a second name, „KW1 (Konstrukt-Stadt/LogOS-Prime)“ ^[?]; the critique labels KW4 „Kernwelt 4 (Kairos/Sophia)“ ^[?].
