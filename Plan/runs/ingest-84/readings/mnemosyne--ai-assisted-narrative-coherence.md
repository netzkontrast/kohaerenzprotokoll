## Reading — `ai-assisted-narrative-coherence`, 2025-10-15, the English compilation (the blueprint, the concept document, the three-act blueprint, the scene outline) — KW2 and its Guardian

The concept document's table names the world `KW2: Mnemosyne-Archipel` and the blueprint's section 5.2 describes it as „a fluid, dream-like world where the environment reacts directly to emotion and memory.“ ^[?] The three-act blueprint puts a Guardian in it: Kael confronts memories „actively manipulated and distorted by the Guardian Mnemosyne.“ ^[?] In the scene outline the Guardian first appears „not as a threat, but as a serene guide, offering to help him“ ^[?] organise his experiences, and later, when Kael's intent changes, „sensing this shift in intent, actively attempts to trap him in a memory loop of a childhood abandonment, weaponizing his grief to neutralize him.“ ^[?]
