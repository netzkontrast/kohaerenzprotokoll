## Reading — `ai-assisted-narrative-coherence`, 2025-10-15, the English compilation (the methodology report, the three-act blueprint, the scene outline) — Guardians as thematic probes

The methodology report treats the Guardians as functions of AEGIS: „The supporting characters, specifically the Guardians, do not function as independent actors with their own motivations and desires.“ ^[?] Its example is one named by world: „a confrontation with the Guardian of KW1 (Logik) is not a simple battle but a targeted assault on the logical frameworks of Kael's analytical part, Lex.“ ^[?]

The three-act blueprint names two by name in Act II: Kael „confronts traumatic memories, actively manipulated and distorted by the Guardian Mnemosyne.“ ^[?] and, in the next world, a struggle against Cerberus. After the transformation, „Guardians engage in bizarre, useless, but logically valid behaviors, such as endlessly building and deconstructing a perfect wall.“ ^[?]

The scene outline introduces a Guardian by a unit number: „A Guardian, designated Unit 734, detains him for a“ ^[?] random coherence check.
