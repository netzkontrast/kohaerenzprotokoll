## Reading — `ai-assisted-narrative-coherence`, 2025-10-15, the blueprint, the three-act blueprint, the critique, the Kael biography and the scene outline of the English compilation — Lex as the analyst ANP, and two incompatible placements of Dr. Aris Thorne

Most of the parts agree on Lex's function. The blueprint (L339 on) types the row „ANP - Intellectual Analyst. Seeks control through logic and understanding.“ ^[?] The critique (first copy, L931 on) ties the part to the first world: „He embodies the rigid logic of Kernwelt 1.“ ^[?] The methodology report (L732 on) uses it as the target of the KW1 Guardian: „a targeted assault on the logical frameworks of Kael's analytical part, Lex.“ ^[?] The Kael biography (L1073 on) calls the part a manager: „This Manager-part attempts to master the system using only analysis and control.“ ^[?] The scene outline (L1273 on) gives it a turn in Chapter 21: „attempts to analyze it using pure logic but fails.“ ^[?]

**Dr. Aris Thorne, as two parts give it.** The three-act blueprint (L831 on) opens its premise with a protagonist of that name: „the protagonist, Dr. Aris Thorne“ ^[?], and describes him as one „who goes by the name“ ^[?] Lex, „within the dissociated system known as Kael“ ^[?]. There the protagonist is the ANP and Lex the name he goes by. The scene outline (L1273 on) makes Dr. Thorne an agent of AEGIS: scene 1.6 is set in „Dr. Thorne's Office“ ^[?], where „Thorne's objective is to gaslight Kael, systematically convincing him that the glitches, emotional intrusions, and other“ ^[?] (L1376). The outline names this figure the `Dr. Aris Thorne` construct and not an alter. `Dr. Aris Thorne` ^[ai-assisted-narrative-coherence.md:#2] stands twice in the document, once in each part; neither marks itself as correcting the other.
