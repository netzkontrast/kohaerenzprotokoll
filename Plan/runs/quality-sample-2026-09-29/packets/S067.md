### The claim as written on the page

**One Kernwelt, unnamed and unnumbered, and no alter named at all.**
The oldest whole-novel plan read; it calls itself „verbindlich" ^[monstergruppe-primzahlen-plot-blueprint.md:L15]. Every chapter's Kernwelt is singular and generic: „Kael erlebt seinen Alltag in einer scheinbar normalen, von AEGIS verwalteten Kernwelt." ^[monstergruppe-primzahlen-plot-blueprint.md:L30] `KW1`–`KW4`, `Konstrukt-Stadt` and every Guardian name stand 0 times (`Plan/runs/monstergruppe-primzahlen-plot-blueprint/05-verify.txt`).
**One escalation names more than one, without a number.** Kapitel 22: „führen zu einer kaskadierenden Instabilität in der gesamten Kernwelt oder sogar in mehreren verbundenen Kernwelten." ^[monstergruppe-primzahlen-plot-blueprint.md:L244] — a possibility raised and left open, not a count; the overview table's row for it still calls it „Kaskadierender Kollaps der Kernwelt(en)" ^[monstergruppe-primzahlen-plot-blueprint.md:L451], the parenthesis carrying the same uncertainty.
No figure but Kael and AEGIS is named (`Lex`, `Nyx`, `Kiko`, `Selene` and every other alter 0), so no alter is placed in any world. On the counts, nothing beyond one Kernwelt with a hedge toward several; on the correspondence, nothing — there is no roster to place.
