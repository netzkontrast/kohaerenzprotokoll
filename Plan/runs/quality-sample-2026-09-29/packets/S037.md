### The claim as written on the page

A compendium of the Kael system that cites no source, and in which AEGIS is inside that system.
**A category error.** „The monitoring entity, AEGIS, operates under a catastrophic category error:" ^[the-architecture-of-fracture-a-compendium-of-the-kael-system.md:L21] „AEGIS believes it is the K1 Kernel (Coherence), but it is actually the K0 Kernel (Entropy/Erasure)." ^[the-architecture-of-fracture-a-compendium-of-the-kael-system.md:L21] In the kernel table, K0's actual identity is AEGIS, „The architect of systemic separation." ^[the-architecture-of-fracture-a-compendium-of-the-kael-system.md:L21]
**Expanded, and a machine in its axioms.** Under „AEGIS: The Tragic God of Internal Order" ^[the-architecture-of-fracture-a-compendium-of-the-kael-system.md:L45]: „AEGIS (Autonomous Entropic Gatekeeper for Integrity Systems) is an autopoietic system defined by the tautology:" ^[the-architecture-of-fracture-a-compendium-of-the-kael-system.md:L47] „"AEGIS is, what AEGIS prevents not-to-be."" ^[the-architecture-of-fracture-a-compendium-of-the-kael-system.md:L47] „It is not a villain, but a machine trapped in its own axioms." ^[the-architecture-of-fracture-a-compendium-of-the-kael-system.md:L47] (C1).
**The Fragmentation Night is its doing.** „AEGIS misinterpreted emotional fluctuation as fatal system entropy." ^[the-architecture-of-fracture-a-compendium-of-the-kael-system.md:L43] „AEGIS severed the system's ability to feel in order to preserve its ability to calculate." ^[the-architecture-of-fracture-a-compendium-of-the-kael-system.md:L43] — here the self-amputation is of the Kael system, not of AEGIS (C3).
**Three Primary Protocols** (Q2): „Active erasure of K1-Coherons (misidentified as chaos)." ^[the-architecture-of-fracture-a-compendium-of-the-kael-system.md:L56], „Enforcement of internal consistency, which paradoxically generates the" ^[the-architecture-of-fracture-a-compendium-of-the-kael-system.md:L57] Landauer Heat, and „Dynamic patching of reality rifts." ^[the-architecture-of-fracture-a-compendium-of-the-kael-system.md:L58] — Suppression, Coherence and Re-Containment, the worldbuilding concept's three by name.
Its mirror inside Kael is Oblivion, „Mirror of AEGIS; governs erasure." ^[the-architecture-of-fracture-a-compendium-of-the-kael-system.md:L34] (J74). It has no Vortex and no chapter (`Vortex` 0, `Chapter` 0) and gives AEGIS no person or voice (`person` 0, C14; `05-verify-readers.txt`).
