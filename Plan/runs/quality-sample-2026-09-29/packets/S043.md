Three classes name their voices: Class P, „Typical of the ANP Lex" ^[editorial-style-dossier-somatic-and-linguistic-implementatio.md:L27]; „NP-Hard: The mode of the EP-Protectors ( Nyx , Alex )." ^[editorial-style-dossier-somatic-and-linguistic-implementatio.md:L29]; „Generative: The poetic, choral mode of KW4 and Juna-influenced states." ^[editorial-style-dossier-somatic-and-linguistic-implementatio.md:L30] The paraconsistent class names no voice: „Loops of logic manifest in speech, reflecting the "Halting Problem" of KW2." ^[editorial-style-dossier-somatic-and-linguistic-implementatio.md:L28] No world is named by its name (`Konstrukt-Stadt`, `Construct`, `Archipel`, `Archipelago`, `Labyrinth`, `Garden` 0; `05-verify-readers.txt`). A dossier of mandates — recorded, not applied.
On the counts, thirteen, and four regimes by the range; on the correspondence, voices to classes — Lex to P, Nyx and [[alex|Alex]] to NP-Hard, Juna's influence to KW4's — several alters to one class, the rest unplaced.
