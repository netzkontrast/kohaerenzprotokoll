## Reading — `a-guide-to-the-society-of-self-understanding-system-kael`, 2025-10-15, the society guide — the Host and the face of the system

The society guide, an unsigned explanatory guide offered as „a compassionate tool“ ^[?], places Kael among the four Daily Life Managers and calls him the Host: „As the Host, Kael is the alter who spends the most time interacting with the external world.“ ^[?] It gives his core fear as „Being overwhelmed by the intense emotions held by the EPs, leading to a total loss of control and system collapse.“ ^[?] Of the whole it says „System Kael is not a singular mind with errors, but a society of survivors.“ ^[?], and it names the final state the `Rhizomatic Self`, „a dynamic, interconnected network where all alters cooperate consciously“ ^[?]. The guide describes; it claims no canon.
