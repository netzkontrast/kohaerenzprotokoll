## Reading — `a-guide-to-the-society-of-self-understanding-system-kael`, 2025-10-15, the society guide — eleven alters as an internal society

The society guide describes the alters as „different personalities, or“ ^[?] who coexist in Kael's mind, together an internal society. Its goal is not their elimination: „They are not symptoms to be cured, but survivors of trauma to be understood and integrated.“ ^[?] It sorts them into groups: the ANPs, who are „primarily responsible for helping Kael function in the world“ ^[?]; the EPs, „the alters who hold the raw, unprocessed experiences of trauma“ ^[?]; and a last group of which it says „These final alters have unique, overarching functions related to the system's self-awareness“ ^[?]. Its conclusion: „each alter is a necessary and valid part of a greater whole“ ^[?].
