## Reading — `tsdp-analyse-kohaerenz-protokoll-charaktere`, 2025-04-28, the TSDP tension analysis — Moros as the EP of freeze, submission and collapse

The TSDP tension analysis counts Moros among the EPs as „Moros (Erstarrung/Kollaps)“ ^[?]. Its profile (section 4.7) opens: „Moros verkörpert die extremste Form der Verteidigungsreaktion“ ^[?]: freeze, submission and collapse, with feelings of abandonment and hopelessness, and, it hedges, `wahrscheinlich` as a response to a life-threatening experience or extreme neglect. Relations: „Moros wird von den meisten anderen Anteilen, insbesondere den ANPs (Kael, Lex), zutiefst gefürchtet und vermieden“ ^[?]. For integration it writes: „Moros braucht vor allem“ ^[?] witnessing, Zeugenschaft, being seen and acknowledged in his despair.
