## Reading — `tsdp-analyse-kohaerenz-protokoll-charaktere`, 2025-04-28, the TSDP tension analysis — Kiko as a classical child EP of flight, freeze and attachment cry

The TSDP tension analysis counts Kiko among the five EPs it calls clearly defined, listing him as „Kiko (Flucht/Erstarrung/Bindungsruf)“ ^[?]. The profile (section 4.4) opens: „Kiko ist ein klassischer kindlicher EP, der die Kerngefühle früher Traumata trägt“ ^[?], with fear, abandonment and shame as the core feelings the line names. On relations: „Kiko lebt in ständiger Angst vor Kritik und Ablehnung“ ^[?], which makes him, it says, particularly vulnerable to Lex's suppressing stance (PP-INT-02, a brief's code). For integration it writes: „Kiko braucht vor allem Sicherheit, bedingungslose Annahme“ ^[?] and the experience of co-regulation from caring inner or outer figures, Rhys, Selene or Juna/V.
