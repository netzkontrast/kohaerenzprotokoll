## Reading — `tsdp-analyse-kohaerenz-protokoll-charaktere`, 2025-04-28, the TSDP tension analysis — Juna/V as external trigger and relationship figures

The TSDP tension analysis treats Juna/V as outside interaction partners: „Juna/V stellen als externe Interaktionspartner wichtige potenzielle Auslöser und Beziehungsfiguren für System Kael dar“ ^[?]. Their effect depends, it says, on which part is dominant or triggered. They may evoke safety and trust (Kiko, Lia, Rhys) or be perceived as a threat (Nyx, Isabelle, Lex), and may produce inner conflicts between the parts. About their potential the analysis writes: „Juna/V haben das Potenzial, korrigierende emotionale Erfahrungen zu ermöglichen“ ^[?], and in the same line also that they can reopen old wounds unintentionally.

In the plot recommendations (advice, not a finding): „Juna/V als Katalysator“ ^[?] is the heading of the line that begins „Lassen Sie Interaktionen mit Juna/V spezifische Anteile triggern“ ^[?].
