## Reading — `gravitation-realitaet-simulation-wahrheit`, 2025-11-25, the gravity-ontology report — AEGIS as the steering AI of the report's own staged scenario

The report is a general essay that stages its own scenario, a simulation of weightlessness under rising load. The gravity-ontology report names as AEGIS the AI steering that scenario; it decides nothing about the novel's AEGIS. In the scenario AEGIS answers „Kohärenz stabil bei“ ^[?] a stated percentage, and the report introduces it as „die steuernde KI des Systems“ ^[?]. When the data contradict its model, the report writes that the AI is „konfrontiert mit Daten, die im Widerspruch zu ihrem gesamten ontologischen Modell stehen“ ^[?] and that „Sie halluziniert eine neue Narrative“ ^[?]. The report reads AEGIS's failure as a result for its thesis: „Das Versagen von AEGIS bestätigt die Hypothese“ ^[?] that an algorithmic system cannot simulate quantum-gravity effects without crashing.
