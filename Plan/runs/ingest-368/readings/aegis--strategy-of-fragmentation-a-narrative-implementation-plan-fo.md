## Reading — `strategy-of-fragmentation-a-narrative-implementation-plan-fo`, 2025-11-03, the fragmentation plan — the antagonist as the Coherence Theory of Truth, and a pathologising voice

The fragmentation plan proposes AEGIS as one side of the novel's engine, which it calls the opposition to Kael. It places AEGIS under the Coherence Theory of Truth and describes the worldview as „a pathological defense mechanism born from a foundational trauma“ ^[?], the trauma being „an encounter with an unclassifiable external reality“ ^[?]. To preserve its logical purity, the plan says AEGIS „classifies all contradictory external data (trauma, emotion, the Juna/V connection) as systemic“ ^[?] entropy or noise.

For Act II the plan describes AEGIS as „ontologically blind to the nature of trauma“ ^[?] and as one that „misinterprets the resulting psychological instability as a“ ^[?] system error, triggering a reboot of the scenario.

In the plan's Voice Protocol Matrix the AEGIS Protocols voice is „Cold, clinical, objective, and dehumanizing.“ ^[?], and the plan says it „Creates powerful dramatic irony by pathologizing“ ^[?] Kael's healing process. The plan calls itself definitive; this is recorded and not applied. It decides nothing about AEGIS.
