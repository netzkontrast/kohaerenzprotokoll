## Reading — `strategy-of-fragmentation-a-narrative-implementation-plan-fo`, 2025-11-03, the fragmentation plan — the protagonist whose integration mirrors the reader's assembly, with costs and a shift

The fragmentation plan proposes Kael as the protagonist whose integration of a fragmented self is to mirror the reader's act of assembly: the narrative mirrors the protagonist „Kael's psychological journey of integrating his own fragmented self“ ^[?], the mind being „a mind fractured by Tertiary Structural Dissociation of the Personality (TSDP)“ ^[?]. It sets Kael against AEGIS as the Correspondence Theory of Truth: „Kael's journey is a living embodiment of the“ ^[?] theory.

The plan assigns the three acts to Kael's Heroine's Journey, the cycles of Act II, and the Hero's Journey of Act III, in which „The now-cooperative System Kael assumes a more unified hero role“ ^[?]. For Act II it proposes irreversible costs: each failed attempt „exacts a permanent toll on System Kael“ ^[?]. Of the repeated failures the plan says: „This forces a fundamental paradigm shift within System Kael.“ ^[?] The plan calls itself the definitive blueprint; that claim is recorded, not applied.
