## Reading — `kael-s-dissociative-architecture-analysis`, 2026-01-02, the dissociative architecture report — TSDP as the diagnostic lens on System Kael

The dissociative architecture report applies `TSDP` as one of two lenses on the novel's protagonist, with IFS as the other, and gives it a role: the Theory of Structural Dissociation of the Personality is, in its words, „as the diagnostic framework for the system's fragmentation“ ^[?]. It describes the division of labour as hardware against software: „TSDP provides the hardware specifications“ ^[?] of the trauma, and „It defines the system's starting state“ ^[?], which it names „a Tertiary Structural Dissociation characteristic of Dissociative Identity Disorder“ ^[?].

For the arc, the report says the movement from TSDP to IFS is the narrative's main argument: „the transition from the static, fear-based geometry of TSDP to the fluid, trust-based topology of IFS is the primary“ ^[?] one. In its three-act mapping, Act I is rigid: „Kael is in denial. The system is compartmentalized.“ ^[?] In its Table 3 the same act carries the label Fragmented TSDP, with the turning point „The denial fails. The system acknowledges its multiplicity.“ ^[?]
