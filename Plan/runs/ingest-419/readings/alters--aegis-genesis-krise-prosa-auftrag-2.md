## Reading — `aegis-genesis-krise-prosa-auftrag-2`, 2025-04-29, the second Genesis-Krise work order — the Ursprungs-Ich falling into several alters

The second Genesis-Krise work order proposes the dissociative identity structure as a model that „Bietet ein extremeres Modell für die Fragmentierung“ ^[?]. The Ursprungs-Ich, it says, „Das Ursprungs-Ich könnte in mehrere distinkte“ ^[?] alters fall apart, with Kael as one of them (`Kael` named in the same line), each with its own functions, memories and degrees of awareness of the others. The alters are here parts of the Ursprungs-Ich, not figures tied to a world.
