## Reading — `aegis-genesis-krise-prosa-auftrag-2`, 2025-04-29, the second Genesis-Krise work order — Kael as one alter of the Ursprungs-Ich, and the last step of the commission

The second Genesis-Krise work order proposes, in its theory field on the dissociative identity structure, that „Das Ursprungs-Ich könnte in mehrere distinkte“ ^[?] alters fall apart, and it adds in brackets „(Kael ist einer davon)“ ^[?]. Kael is here one of several parts of the Ursprungs-Ich, each with „jeder mit unterschiedlichen Funktionen, Erinnerungen und Bewusstseinsgraden füreinander“ ^[?]. The field is a proposal about what the model could offer the plot, not a statement about the world.

The commission's seventh step is named for Kael. In the prose plan it asks for „Enger Fokus auf das entstehende Kael-Fragment“ ^[?], and in the short list of the commission it repeats the step as „Fokus auf das naszierende Kael-Fragment“ ^[?]. The step before it describes the partitioning as „eine erzwungene Amnesie/Dissoziation“ ^[?], so the work order places Kael's beginning after AEGIS's act of splitting.
