## Reading — `operationalizing-the-coherence-protocol-a-narrative-stress-t`, 2025-11-03, the stress-test guide — the Risse as the objective to turn metaphor into a causal threat

The stress-test guide's first objective is to transform the Risse „from passive metaphor into an active, causal threat“ ^[?], and it describes a Riss as „the visible scar of the battle between our universe's two fundamental forces“ ^[?]. Its prompts ask for a Riss staged as „the direct, physical consequence of a specific K₀ intrusion (a decoherence event)“ ^[?], and as „a direct, one-to-one causal manifestation of a specific EP intrusion“ ^[?]. The guide sets an objective and asks; it does not report what the Risse are.
