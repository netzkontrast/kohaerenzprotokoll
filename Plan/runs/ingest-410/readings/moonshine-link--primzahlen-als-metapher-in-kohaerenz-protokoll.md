## Reading — `primzahlen-als-metapher-in-kohaerenz-protokoll`, 2025-04-25, the prime-number report — Moonshine as the bond of Kael and Juna, and a rarity analogy

The prime-number report is an evaluation of a proposed metaphor. It gives as background the connection between Kael and Juna „als emergentes Phänomen namens“ ^[?] Moonshine, which „das als nicht-lokal und sub-protokollarisch beschrieben wird“ ^[?] (L24).

It proposes mapping very rare events or entities onto Mersenne or Fermat primes, and adds „Moonshine könnte solche Eigenschaften haben“ ^[?] (L99). In another row, M-coherence/emergence (Moonshine) is potentially infinite but, it says, „tritt aber sporadisch, unvorhersehbar auf“ ^[?] (L92).
