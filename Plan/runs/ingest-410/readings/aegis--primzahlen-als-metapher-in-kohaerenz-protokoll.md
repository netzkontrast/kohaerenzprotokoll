## Reading — `primzahlen-als-metapher-in-kohaerenz-protokoll`, 2025-04-25, the prime-number report — AEGIS as the reductionist side of a proposed prime metaphor

The prime-number report is an evaluation of a proposed metaphor, not an account of the novel. It gives as background that AEGIS is „Eine fragile Künstliche Intelligenz, deren Existenz durch Negation definiert ist“ ^[?] and that „AEGIS repräsentiert einen reduktionistischen Ansatz zur Weltbeschreibung und -beherrschung“ ^[?]. It also restates the core conflict as „Der zentrale Antagonismus zwischen AEGIS“ ^[?] reductionist control and the holistic nature of M's coherence.

It proposes mapping AEGIS onto the metaphor in its table: AEGIS' „grundlegende Axiome oder Direktiven sind“ ^[?] 'prime', indivisible and the basis of its operations, yet possibly faulty or rigid (L86). Under unique factorisation, „AEGIS sieht Realität als deterministisch konstruierbar aus“ ^[?] 'prime' logical building blocks and tries to 'factorise' M and Kael (L89). As a failure case, „AEGIS scheitert, wenn es einzigartige“ ^[?] entities such as Kael treats them as replaceable instances of a type (L97).

In its weighing of potential the report says the contrast with M „kann den Kernkonflikt auf einer neuen konzeptuellen Ebene beleuchten“ ^[?]; its closing recommendation is conditional (L215).
