## Reading — `primzahlen-als-metapher-in-kohaerenz-protokoll`, 2025-04-25, the prime-number report — Potentialmeer in two proposed mappings

The prime-number report is an evaluation of a proposed metaphor; it proposes mapping the Potentialmeer twice in its table. Under indivisibility: „Bestimmte Regeln im Potentialmeer/Kernwelten sind absolut und unbrechbar“ ^[?], 'prime' (L88). Under distribution: „Fundamentale Wahrheiten“ ^[?] ('Primzahlen') are infinite but „dünn im chaotischen Potentialmeer verstreut“ ^[?] (L93), and „Ihre Entdeckung erfordert Suche“ ^[?] (L93).
