The causal-linkage report sets out Kael as the point where psychology meets the world's physics. It says the Risse „are not merely metaphors for his internal state“ ^[?], and that System Kael's mind is built on a clinical model: „The architectural blueprint for the protagonist's mind, System Kael“ ^[?] is TSDP. Through switches and amnesia the report places him as an unreliable narrator: „This dynamic positions Kael as a classic“ ^[?]. His body is given a testing role: „serves as an infallible marker of truth“ ^[?] against the simulated worlds. The report ends on integration: „The only viable path to stability is not external control but internal healing.“ ^[?] This is the report's own proposal for the character, not a canon statement.
