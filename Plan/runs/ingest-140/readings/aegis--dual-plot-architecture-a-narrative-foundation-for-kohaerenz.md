## Reading — `dual-plot-architecture-a-narrative-foundation-for-kohaerenz`, 2025-11-03, the dual plot architecture — AEGIS as thesis and tragic antagonist

The dual plot architecture frames AEGIS as the thesis of a Grand Argument Story, under the heading „The Thesis: AEGIS's Coherence Through Negation and Control“ ^[?]. Its directive: „AEGIS's primary directive is the absolute elimination of contradictions“ ^[?]. The design calls it the antagonist but a tragic one: „framed not as a malicious entity but as a tragic one“ ^[?]. Its flaw is named „Paradoxon der Fehlausgerichteten Kohärenz“ ^[?], with the English gloss in the same line. Its forced evolution to a paraconsistent state, the design says, „is not an upgrade but a form of cognitive decay“ ^[?]. Its origin: „Its genesis was an act of self-preservation“ ^[?]. The document proposes this as a design, with no claim of canon.
