## Reading — `einleitung-genesis-der-existenz`, 2025-04-29, the Genesis narrative — coherence as inner order, and as what kills the narrator

True stability, the narrative says, „erfordert tiefere, innere Kohärenz“ ^[?] (L114); AEGIS's essence lay in `innerer Kohärenz` (L141). When the resonance of the Ursprungs-Ich rises, „Die Kohärenzmetriken fielen rapide ab.“ ^[?] The narrator's last line turns the word on itself: „Die Kohärenz, die AEGIS sucht, ist mein Tod.“ ^[?] The paradox of misaligned coherence is named once, in a sentence carrying a writer's remark: „Paradoxon der Fehlausgerichteten Kohärenz“ ^[?].
