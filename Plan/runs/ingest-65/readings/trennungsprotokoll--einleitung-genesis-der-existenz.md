## Reading — `einleitung-genesis-der-existenz`, 2025-04-29, the Genesis narrative — a protocol told under the heading Trennungsprotokoll

The last part of `Die Krise` carries the heading „Das Trennungsprotokoll: Fragmentierung als Heilung“ ^[?], while the sentences under it, and before it, name the `Kohärenz Protokoll`; the narrative uses both names and does not say they are one thing. The number stands `Kohärenz Protokoll 1.0` ^[einleitung-genesis-der-existenz.md:#1] time (L161). AEGIS's decision is told as the cold consequence of a faulty premise; the protocol is „ein radikaler, invasiver Eingriff“ ^[?] (L161). Its activation was „kein Schalter, der umgelegt wurde“ ^[?], but a sequence of a forced reboot and partitioning (L189). The narrator then tells it from inside, in the first person (L193–L197), ending: „Die Kohärenz, die AEGIS sucht, ist mein Tod.“ ^[?]
