In the narrative the closure (L87–L95) comes first and then a part headed „Komponente 734: Funktion an der Grenze“ ^[?]. The narrator, in the narrative's own words, „ist nun Komponente 734, eine Funktionseinheit“ ^[?], and the `Komponente 734` surface stands six times in the document. Its being is the border: „Die Grenze ist der Fokus der Existenz dieser Komponente.“ ^[?]

What was the fragment's loneliness remains as an incompletely integrated remainder: „Es ist das Echo der Einsamkeit“ ^[?], and fear is turned into data, „Risiko-Assessment-Marker“ ^[?] (L108). The narrative makes the component at the closure; the protocol comes later (L161, L187). The narrative names the narrator 734 and says nothing of what the number is.
