## Reading — `romanplot-kohaerenz-protokoll-entwickeln`, 2025-04-23, the detailed plot blueprint — LogOS as a Wächter-subsystem for logical consistency, active in KW1, and a question about its limits

The detailed plot blueprint names LogOS among „spezialisierte Wächter-Subsysteme wie LogOS (zuständig für logische Konsistenz)“ ^[?] of AEGIS. In KW1, the Konstrukt-Stadt, „AEGIS und sein Wächter LogOS intervenieren hier am deutlichsten“ ^[?]. In the first phase of AEGIS's interventions it is charged, with Mnemosyne, to collect data and analyse (L70).

Its speculative section asks, as a question, whether LogOS might fail: „Könnte LogOS versuchen, Integration rein logisch zu simulieren, und dabei auf Gödel'sche Weise“ ^[?] fail, the line continues, with bizarre or dangerous malfunctions. The question is asked, not answered.
