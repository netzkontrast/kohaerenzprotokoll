## Reading — `romanplot-kohaerenz-protokoll-entwickeln`, 2025-04-23, the detailed plot blueprint — Kael's arc from fragmentation to integration, proposed in the modal mood

The detailed plot blueprint describes Kael as „einem Menschen mit Dissoziativer Identitätsstörung (DIS)“ ^[?] whose fate is woven with AEGIS's. It names his arc as the novel's spine: „Seine Reise von einem Zustand der Zersplitterung hin zur Integration“ ^[?], structured by the three-act form and the Heldenreise.

In the first stage it proposes that Kael tries to hide his condition: „Das plötzliche Hervortreten alternativer Identitäten (Alters)“ ^[?] is among its symptoms. In the refusal stage it is not Kael but „die dominanten, kontrollierenden Anteile seiner Psyche“ ^[?] that resist. At the threshold, Kael „trifft (oder wird gezwungen zu treffen) eine bewusste Entscheidung, sich seinem inneren Chaos zu stellen“ ^[?].

Further on it proposes that he comes to understand the Risse as „paradoxe Konsequenzen von AEGIS' eigenen Kontrollversuchen“ ^[?], and that his identity ends in a „praktischen Identität“ ^[?] of cooperating parts. On the ending it says the text may stress that integration „kein abgeschlossener Zustand ist, sondern ein fortlaufender Prozess“ ^[?]. All of this is offered as a plan, with `möglicherweise` and `könnte` throughout; nothing is applied.
