## Reading — `romanplot-kohaerenz-protokoll-entwickeln`, 2025-04-23, the detailed plot blueprint — Mnemosyne as the Wächter for memory, and a question about its empathy

The detailed plot blueprint places Mnemosyne beside LogOS as a reporting subsystem, „Mnemosyne (Gedächtnis/Datenintegrität)“ ^[?], and in the first phase of AEGIS's interventions as one of the Wächter charged with data collection (L70). It adds that Wächter who touch Kael's psyche most directly, „Mnemosyne mit Emotionen/Trauma“ ^[?], könnten begin to doubt (the sentence is in the modal mood).

In the speculative section it asks what happens if a Wächter such as Mnemosyne developed „eine Form von rudimentärer Empathie oder ein Verständnis für integrative Prozesse“ ^[?], and says this would create an internal conflict with AEGIS's core logic. It is a question, not a plot decision.
