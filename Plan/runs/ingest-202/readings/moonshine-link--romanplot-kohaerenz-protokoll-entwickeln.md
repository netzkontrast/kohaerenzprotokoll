## Reading — `romanplot-kohaerenz-protokoll-entwickeln`, 2025-04-23, the detailed plot blueprint — Moonshine as a possible symbol of the K-J connection, not a named link

The detailed plot blueprint does not write `Moonshine-Link`. It describes the Monstergruppe as a metaphor for the Kohärenz-Insel, with its `Happy Family` and `Monstrous Moonshine` as the group's deep connections to other areas of mathematics. Then it proposes, in the modal mood: „könnten die unerklärliche, sub-protokollarische Natur der K-J-Verbindung symbolisieren“ ^[?], which links „scheinbar disparate Realitätsebenen (Kaels Psyche, das Potentialmeer, AEGIS' System)“ ^[?]. It adds that Kael „könnte diese Metapher in KW4 entdecken oder intuitiv erfassen“ ^[?]. It states no boundary of the link.
