## Reading — `plot-blueprint-methodik-kohaerenz-protokoll`, 2025-04-20, the plot-blueprint method handbook — AEGIS as the domain of the Überwelt, as the fight against information entropy, and as a paradox

The plot-blueprint method handbook proposes that the Überwelt and the control level be shown so that they mirror AEGIS: „kalt, logisch, kontrollierend, vielleicht subtil fehlerhaft oder verfallend“ ^[?]. It asks for AEGIS' primary function to be framed as a fight against `Informationsentropie`, a striving for order that works by „Eliminierung von wahrgenommenem Rauschen/Unordnung“ ^[?]. It proposes presenting AEGIS' misreading of the K-Juna connection, and says AEGIS' attempts at control „erhöhen tatsächlich die systemische Instabilität“ ^[?]. Among the metaphors it lists „AEGIS als Maschine, Torwächter, rigide Struktur“ ^[?], and it names AEGIS itself a paradox: „für Ordnung konzipiert, verursacht aber durch seine Rigidität Chaos“ ^[?]. A method handbook; the proposals are recorded, not applied, and the z.B. examples are examples.
