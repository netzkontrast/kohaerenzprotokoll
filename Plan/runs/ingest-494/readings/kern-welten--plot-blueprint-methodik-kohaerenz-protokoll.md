## Reading — `plot-blueprint-methodik-kohaerenz-protokoll`, 2025-04-20, the plot-blueprint method handbook — the Kern-Welten as psychological landscapes

The plot-blueprint method handbook proposes that the environments mirror inner states, with examples „rigide Geometrie in KW1 für Logik“ ^[?] and „verfallende Strukturen in KW2 für Trauma“ ^[?]. It says the Kern-Welten are „psychologische Landschaften“ ^[?]. For atmosphere it asks that „KW2 (Mnemosyne-Archipel)“ ^[?] and its counterpart KW3 be infused: „sollten mit Elementen des psychologischen Horrors durchdrungen werden“ ^[?]. The environmental pairings are z.B. examples; recorded, not applied.
