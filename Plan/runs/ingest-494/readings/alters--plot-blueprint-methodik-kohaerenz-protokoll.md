## Reading — `plot-blueprint-methodik-kohaerenz-protokoll`, 2025-04-20, the plot-blueprint method handbook — alters as mirrors in the environment, as coping functions, and as sources of shifting perspective

The plot-blueprint method handbook proposes that the environments of the Kern-Welten mirror „die Denkweise des dominanten Alters“ ^[?]. It asks for alters shown „mit spezifischen Fähigkeiten, Perspektiven und Rollen innerhalb des inneren Systems“ ^[?] and for linking their functions to coping: „Verknüpfung der Alter-Funktionen (Beschützer, Trauma-Halter usw.) mit Bewältigungsmechanismen“ ^[?]. For narration it proposes a point of view that shifts „zwischen den Perspektiven verschiedener Alters wechselt“ ^[?], and it proposes depicting conflicts between alters, naming as an example „Praetor strebt Sicherheit an vs. Flicker strebt Erkundung an“ ^[?]. The names are given as z.B. examples; recorded, not applied.
