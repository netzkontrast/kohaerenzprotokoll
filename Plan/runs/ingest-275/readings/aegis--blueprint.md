## Reading — `blueprint`, 2025-07-29, the blueprint — the autopoietic, radically closed antagonist and its paradox

The blueprint, a plan in an adviser's voice, describes AEGIS among its core concepts as „Das antagonistische KI-System oder Kollektiv“ ^[?], „kein bloßer Computer, sondern ein autopoietisches System“ ^[?] that developed out of the `Potentialmeer`. Its operation is „radikal geschlossen“ ^[?]. The core problem it names is the `Paradoxon der Fehlausgerichteten Kohärenz`, AEGIS's attempt „absolute Kohärenz durch Kontrolle und Fragmentierung zu erzwingen“ ^[?]. The Kernwelten are, in its words, „geschaffenen, simulierten Realitäten“ ^[?] of AEGIS. Its thesis sets AEGIS's rigid reading of its own directives against a coherence that does not arise „durch aufgezwungene algorithmische Ordnung“ ^[?]. All of this is the blueprint's description, recorded and not applied.
