## Reading — `blueprint`, 2025-07-29, the blueprint — functional multiplicity as Kael's aim and the thesis's acceptance of multiplicity

The blueprint states its central thesis as true coherence arising through „durch die Akzeptanz und Integration von Paradox, Trauma und Multiplizität“ ^[?L23]. For Kael it names the aim „funktionalen Multiplizität“ ^[?L39], not fusion. In its section on technique it asks that this state be performed by the prose itself, with distinct syntax, rhythm and diction for each part (L82).
<!-- differ -->
- The blueprint takes the side that multiplicity is to be accepted, aiming at „funktionalen Multiplizität“ ^[?L39] and not at fusion.
