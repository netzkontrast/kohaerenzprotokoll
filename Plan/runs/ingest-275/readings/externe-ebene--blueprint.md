## Reading — `blueprint`, 2025-07-29, the blueprint — the mysterious Externe Ebene as contrast to AEGIS's domain, its nature still open

The blueprint counts the Externe Ebene among its six levels: „Die mysteriöse Externe Ebene“ ^[?] is one that „das Unbekannte und die Verbindung verkörpert“ ^[?] and serves „als Kontrast zu“ ^[?] AEGIS's domain of control. It does not say whether the level lies inside or outside the simulation.
