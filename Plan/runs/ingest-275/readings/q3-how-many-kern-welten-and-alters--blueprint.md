## 2026-10-12 — `blueprint`, 2025-07-29, the blueprint

**The blueprint counts six levels, four of them psychological Kernwelten, and names no alters by number.**

The record asks how many Kern-Welten there are and how many alters. The blueprint says the novel works on „sechs distinkten Realitätsebenen“ ^[?], of which „Die vier psychologischen“ ^[?] Kernwelten KW1-4 are four; the others are the Überwelt (L45) and the Externe Ebene (L46). Of the parts of Kael it names three in one parenthesis: „wie Lex der Analytiker, Nyx die Beschützerin, oder Selene die Integratorin“ ^[?]. It does not say whether one Kernwelt corresponds to one part.

Stands as one more document's count of four Kernwelten within six levels; the correspondence of worlds and alters stays open, recorded and not decided.
