## 2026-10-12 — `blueprint`, 2025-07-29, the blueprint

**The blueprint names the Externe Ebene as a contrast to AEGIS's domain and Juna/V as outside AEGIS's control, and does not say where the level lies.**

The blueprint lists „Die mysteriöse Externe Ebene“ ^[?] as one of six levels; it embodies „das Unbekannte und die Verbindung verkörpert“ ^[?] and serves „als Kontrast zu“ ^[?] AEGIS's domain of control. Juna/V is „Diese externe Entität oder Anomalie“ ^[?] outside AEGIS's direct control. The Fundament is postulated as the deepest level „Postuliert als die tiefste Realitätsebene“ ^[?] behind AEGIS's simulation. Nowhere does it say that the Externe Ebene lies beyond or inside the simulation; its nature is an open next step: „die Konkretisierung der Natur von“ ^[?] Juna/V and the Externe Ebene.

Stands as a contrast to AEGIS's domain with the placement unstated; neither side of the record's question is taken, recorded and not decided.
