## Reading — `blueprint`, 2025-07-29, the blueprint — a system of multiple consciousness aiming at functional multiplicity

The blueprint describes Kael as „ein System multiplen Bewusstseins“ ^[?], whose fragmented identity came from the original self through a traumatic intervention by AEGIS's `Kohärenz Protokoll 1.0`. His journey is aimed not at fusion of all parts but at „funktionalen Multiplizität“ ^[?L39], „einen Zustand des Mit-Bewusstseins und der Kooperation“ ^[?], in which parts such as Lex, Nyx and Selene act as an inner community. This, it says, makes him „dialetheischen Geist“ ^[?]. The thesis puts the point as his learning to live a healthier, integrating interpretation of the manifesto: „Die Reise des Protagonisten“ ^[?] mirrors the conflict with AEGIS. In the Dramatica frame he is the Main Character (L84). The origin of his trauma is listed among the open next steps (L94). All of this is the blueprint's plan, recorded and not applied.
