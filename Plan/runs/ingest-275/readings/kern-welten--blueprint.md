## Reading — `blueprint`, 2025-07-29, the blueprint — four psychological Kernwelten, each with a Guardian, among six levels

The blueprint describes the Kernwelten as „geschaffenen, simulierten Realitäten“ ^[?] of AEGIS that mirror Kael's psychic state and says the novel works on six levels. Four of them are „Die vier psychologischen“ ^[?] Kernwelten KW1-4, each with its own psychological function and aesthetic, each `Guardian`-watched („überwacht“ ^[?]); its one example is „KW1 (Konstrukt-Stadt) steril, logikbasiert“ ^[?]. The other two levels are the Überwelt and the Externe Ebene (L45, L46). The blueprint names no further Kernwelt.
