## Reading — `roman-konzept-und-philosophische-fragen`, 2025-07-29, the philosophical synthesis — TSDP as the blueprint of Kael's character

The philosophical synthesis reads TSDP as the model that defines Kael's starting point: „Kaels Ausgangszustand wird durch die Theorie der Strukturellen Dissoziation der Persönlichkeit“ ^[?] (L118, with the abbreviation `TSDP` following in brackets). It maps the ANPs and EPs onto Kael's parts, Lex and Nyx, and names the dissoziative Phobien between them as the driver of the inner conflict. The goal of the therapy it describes is not fusion but a functional multiplicity: „Kaels Heilungsprozess folgt einem etablierten, dreiphasigen Therapiemodell“ ^[?].
