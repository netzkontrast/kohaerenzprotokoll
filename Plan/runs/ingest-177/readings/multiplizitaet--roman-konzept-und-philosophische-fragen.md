## Reading — `roman-konzept-und-philosophische-fragen`, 2025-07-29, the philosophical synthesis — functional multiplicity as a higher-order complexity

The philosophical synthesis calls Kael's final state „funktionalen Multiplizität“ ^[?] and reads it as no return to a monolithic self but „die Etablierung einer Komplexität höherer Ordnung“ ^[?], one that acknowledges paradox as part of being. In its therapy section the goal is not fusion but this multiplicity, conceived as an inner democracy in which every part brings its strengths (L126). In its synthesis it reads the state as a new form of being that integrates unity and diversity (L50).
