## Reading — `roman-konzept-und-philosophische-fragen`, 2025-07-29, the philosophical synthesis — two definitions of coherence, and a redefinition

The philosophical synthesis builds its dialectic on two definitions of coherence: AEGIS's „Kohärenz durch Negation“ ^[?L26] as the thesis and Kael's „Kohärenz durch Integration“ ^[?L36] as the antithesis. It reads AEGIS's order as imposed, static and top-down, and Kael's as emergent and bottom-up.

Its synthesis argues that the title is ironic and that the true protocol is the narrative of Kael's journey: „Die ultimative These des Romans ist eine radikale Neudefinition von Kohärenz“ ^[?].
