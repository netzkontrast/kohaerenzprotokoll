## Reading — `roman-konzept-und-philosophische-fragen`, 2025-07-29, the philosophical synthesis — four Kernwelten as thematic puzzles on complexity theory

The philosophical synthesis argues that the simulated worlds are more than settings: they are „als interaktive, thematische Rätsel, die auf Prinzipien der Komplexitätstheorie basieren“ ^[?]. It adds that each world shows the limits of its Guardian system and of AEGIS: „die logischen Grenzen ihres jeweiligen Wächter-Systems und von AEGIS selbst aufzeigt“ ^[?].

Its matrix lists four, each with a Guardian: `KW1` Konstrukt-Stadt with LogOS, `KW2` Resonanz-Landschaft with Mnemosyne, `KW3` Grenzfeste with Cerberus, and `KW4` Möglichkeits-Garten with „Kairos/Sophia“ ^[?]. Their logics run „Rigide klassische Logik“ ^[?], „Parakonsistente Logik“ ^[?], „Relevanzlogik“ ^[?] and „Explorative/Dialetheische Logik“ ^[?].
