## Reading — `romanarchitektur-validierung-und-synthese`, 2026-04-28, the architecture validation — AEGIS placed as the Collapse Kernel

The architecture validation places AEGIS under Axis 1 as a system whose standing is inverted: it is „initially assumed by the protagonist to be the system of Coherence“ ^[?], but „is revealed through physical constraints to operate as the Collapse Kernel“ ^[?]. Appendix A repeats the claim in the novel's terms: AEGIS is „falsely asserting itself as the Coherence Kernel“ ^[?] and, because its deletions are irreversible, „AEGIS is the true source of entropy“ ^[?].

For the truth theories it states that „The canonical mapping aligns AEGIS with the Coherence Theory of Truth“ ^[?]. In Storyform B it assigns AEGIS the Influence Character's place: „AEGIS gaslights the protagonist“ ^[?]. At the climax it describes the reversal of that role: „Kael's Steadfast commitment to his trauma forces AEGIS into a Change Resolve“ ^[?]. The validation calls these placements „Inverted (Superior)“ ^[?L29] on Axis 1; the label is its own.
