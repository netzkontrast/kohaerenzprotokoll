## Reading — `emergenz-aegis-und-selbststrukturierung`, 2025-04-27, the emergent self-structuring research — emergent self-structuring defined, and emergence from nothing as a constitutive principle

The self-structuring research defines its own term, emergent self-structuring, as „Bezeichnet die spontane Entstehung komplexer Muster, Strukturen und Ordnungen“ ^[?] in systems, without external steering or a given plan. For AEGIS it postulates that the first cluster examines „das konstitutive Prinzip der Emergenz aus dem Nichts“ ^[?], and that AEGIS, as a self-generating system, „muss per Definition einen Mechanismus zur Emergenz“ ^[?] from a state of lower organisation or non-existence in the sense of missing coherent structure. AEGIS is a postulated model here.
