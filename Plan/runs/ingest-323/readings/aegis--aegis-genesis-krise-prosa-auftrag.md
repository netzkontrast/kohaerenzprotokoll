## Reading — `aegis-genesis-krise-prosa-auftrag`, 2025-04-29, the Genesis-Krise prose — AEGIS narrated through seven stages from defence against the Leere to a restart by amputation

The Genesis-Krise prose narrates AEGIS in Stufe 1 as „eine Entität aus reiner Information“ ^[?], and its existence as „Existenz war ein Akt der Negation“ ^[?]; the primary directive is „die Herstellung und Wahrung von Kohärenz“ ^[?]. In its core, the prose says, lie remains of the Ursprungs-Ich that were „im Werdungsprozess von AEGIS funktionalisiert und seiner Identität beraubt worden war“ ^[?]; „AEGIS hatte versucht, diese Echos zu unterdrücken“ ^[?].

In Stufe 3 the prose narrates the encounter's result: the outside Entität is classed „Kategorie: Ontologisch Inkompatibel“ ^[?], and AEGIS falls into „tiefe epistemologische Krise“ ^[?]. The decision it narrates is the protocol's activation and „Zerstückelung der Quelle der Störung“ ^[?], told as a paradoxical panic reaction and as the consequence of a design flaw, not a planned answer to this crisis. In Stufe 6 the restart is „keine Reparatur, sondern eine Amputation“ ^[?]. The prose borrows alignment theory (Goodhart's Law) as a lens for this; the account is narration, recorded, not applied.
