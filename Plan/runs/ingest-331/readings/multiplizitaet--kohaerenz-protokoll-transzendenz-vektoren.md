## Reading — `kohaerenz-protokoll-transzendenz-vektoren`, 2025-05-04, the Transzendenz-Vektoren analysis — multiplicity as a potential and as a catalyst, in the conditional

The report names the multiplicity as the ground of its three transcendent states: „Die funktionale Multiplizität von System Kael bietet ein einzigartiges Potenzial“ ^[?] for transformative states beyond simple cooperation. In its closing item it proposes: „Multiplizität als Katalysator“ ^[?], and that „Kaels Multiplizität könnte der“ ^[?] key to transcendence be. It grounds this in theories of consciousness, collective intelligence and network neuroscience, which are borrowed science; the claim stays a possibility.
