The objectivity guide reads the Kernwelten as „The Kernwelten are externalized psychological landscapes, each engineered to isolate and analyze a specific facet of Kael's mind.“ ^[?] It gives two examples: „KW1 (Logos-Prime) is a sterile world of formal logic that empowers the rational alter Lex“ ^[?] and „KW2 (Mnemosyne-Archipel) is a fluid trauma-landscape that contains the chaotic Emotional Parts (EPs)“ ^[?] It judges the arrangement a false balance „that gives equal weight to its own sterile logic and the messy reality of trauma, when in fact the trauma holds the deeper truth.“ ^[?] The guide names worlds here, not Guardians; the reading is its own.
