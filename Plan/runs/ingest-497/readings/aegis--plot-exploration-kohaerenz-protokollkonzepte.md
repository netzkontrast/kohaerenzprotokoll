## Reading — `plot-exploration-kohaerenz-protokollkonzepte`, 2025-04-23, the plot-exploration report — AEGIS as a logic-and-control system whose control attempts produce instability, and as the centre of concept 2

The plot-exploration report restates, as a premise tagged Query-II, AEGIS as „einem System, das Kohärenz durch Logik, Kontrolle und Abgrenzung (Autopoiesis) anstrebt“ ^[?] and AEGIS's perception of the Kael-Juna connection as a threat: „von AEGIS als Bedrohung wahrgenommen wird“ ^[?]. It restates that AEGIS's control attempts produce instability: „Kontrollversuche paradoxerweise Instabilität“ ^[?]. The report proposes concept 2, which asks whether „ein System, das auf scheinbar absoluter Logik, Kontrolle und Selbsterhaltung“ ^[?] can process the Kael-Juna connection, and names as its driving force „AEGIS' eskalierende Anstrengung, die K-J-Verbindung zu analysieren“ ^[?].
