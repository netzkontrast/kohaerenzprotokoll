## Reading — `kael-charakterarchitektur-und-konfliktdynamik-2`, 2025-04-28, the TSDP revision — Kael recast from host to the whole system

The TSDP revision of the report recasts Kael's profile, headed `Überarbeitet nach TSDP` (L28). Its name line now reads „Kael (Gesamtsystem“ ^[?], with the inner quotation marks cut off, where the host profile of the same report presents him as the host of a system of personas ^[kael-charakterarchitektur-und-konfliktdynamik.md:L31]. The revision states: „Kael repräsentiert das gesamte Persönlichkeitssystem“ ^[?], and calls the fragmentation itself „adaptive Reaktion“ ^[?] on overwhelming experiences.

The host analogy is kept only as one analogy among others: the revision says the „Host“ of DID describes the perspective of the part that most often holds consciousness and control of the body (L32), whereas Kael himself is the system. His basis is tertiary structural dissociation — „Sein System weist Merkmale der“ ^[?] `tertiären strukturellen Dissoziation` auf — with several ANPs and several EPs and the comparison with DID (L32).

On the Kernwelten, the revision says „Als Gesamtsystem interagiert Kael mit“ ^[?] all of them, and that his experience and capacity to act are set by the dominant ANP or EP, where the host profile has him filtered by the dominant persona ^[kael-charakterarchitektur-und-konfliktdynamik.md:L33]. His typical behaviour lists amnesia between the parts, sudden switches and „Depersonalisations- und Derealisationserleben“ ^[?] (L36).
