## Reading — `kael-charakterarchitektur-und-konfliktdynamik-2`, 2025-04-28, the TSDP revision — TSDP as the core model of Kael's profile

The TSDP revision of the report recasts Kael's profile on TSDP. It says that „Primär basiert Kaels Zustand auf der“ ^[?] Theorie der Strukturellen Dissoziation der Persönlichkeit (L32), and that his system shows features of tertiary structural dissociation, divided into several ANPs for daily functioning and several EPs fixed in traumatic memories (L32). The motivations are given „Aus TSDP-Sicht sind die Kernmotivationen des Systems“ ^[?] with survival, normality for the ANPs and expression and defence for the EPs (L34).

On the profile's themes it states: „TSDP liefert das Kernmodell“ ^[?] for explaining identity, reality, trauma and control on the psychological level (L39). The development path is „Der Entwicklungspfad im Sinne der TSDP zielt auf eine verbesserte“ ^[?] integration, and it „umfasst drei Phasen“ ^[?] — stabilisation, processing of traumatic memories, personality reintegration and rehabilitation (L40).
