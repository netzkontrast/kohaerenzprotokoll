## Reading — `kael-charakterarchitektur-und-konfliktdynamik-2`, 2025-04-28, the TSDP revision — functional multiplicity stated as a possible goal

The TSDP revision of the report recasts the integration path as a statement, not a hedge: „Das Ziel ist nicht notwendigerweise Fusion aller Teile zu einer Einheit“ ^[?], but can also be „funktionale Multiplizität“ ^[?], in which the parts work together cooperatively and the whole system functions more coherently (L40). The host profile of the same report words the goal as a possibility ^[kael-charakterarchitektur-und-konfliktdynamik.md:L40].
