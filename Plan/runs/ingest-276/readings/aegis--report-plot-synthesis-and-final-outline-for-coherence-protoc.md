## Reading — `report-plot-synthesis-and-final-outline-for-coherence-protoc`, 2025-11-03, the plot synthesis report — AEGIS as exclusionary logic, tragic antagonist and, at the end, a zombie system

The plot synthesis report sets out AEGIS as one side of a "literalized philosophy": its first thesis pits „the exclusionary logic of the artificial intelligence AEGIS“ ^[?] against the integrative holism of the protagonists. It says AEGIS is „defined by its opposition to the“ ^[?] `Nichts Rauschen`, and names its tools as Logics of Formal Inconsistency (LFI) and Discursive Logic. It says AEGIS perceives Kael's integration as errors that it reads as `Risse` or entropy, and answers „by fragmenting Kael, imposing control via the Core Worlds, and engaging in systematic gaslighting“ ^[?]. In the psychological thesis it calls AEGIS an „externalized perpetrator introject“ ^[?]. The causal-chain thesis is there „to frame AEGIS as a tragic antagonist rather than a purely malevolent one“ ^[?], in two stages: AEGIS shatters its own core, then externalizes a splinter. For the end it says AEGIS is „forced to abandon its core axioms and evolve into a fully paraconsistent system“ ^[?L95], defined as `Algorithmic Melancholy`, a „zombie system“ ^[?L95] whose worlds fill with „inefficient beauty“ ^[?]. It calls this „a form of cognitive damage“ ^[?].
