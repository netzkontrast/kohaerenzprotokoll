## Reading — `kohaerenz-protokoll-narrative-synthese`, 2025-07-29, the compendium — Kael's self as a TSDP system, a living Gödel sentence, and one alter among the table's rows

The compendium (a report that defines and proposes, and calls itself a canonical foundation) gives Kael as the protagonist whose psychology it builds on trauma theory: „Für die psychologische Architektur des Protagonisten Kael wurde die Theorie der Strukturellen Dissoziation der Persönlichkeit (TSDP) als klinisch fundierte Basis herangezogen“ ^[?].

Its second cluster makes his integrated state the trigger of AEGIS's transformation. The compendium defines the trigger as „ein epistemologisches Schachmatt“ ^[?], and says Kael's final integrated state acts as a „lebender Gödel-Satz“ ^[?L128] for AEGIS's formal system. Its numbered proof then states: „Kaels integriertes Selbst ist ein lebendiges, stabiles System“ ^[?].

On the plot side it says the Fundament's resolution is earned through him: „Die Auflösung ist eine direkte Konsequenz der Veränderung des Hauptcharakters, keine externe Intervention.“ ^[?]

In the alter table Kael is one row, classed „ANP: Host, Alltagsfassade“ ^[?]. The document therefore speaks of Kael both as the protagonist whose architecture is described and as one alter in that table; it does not comment on the two.
