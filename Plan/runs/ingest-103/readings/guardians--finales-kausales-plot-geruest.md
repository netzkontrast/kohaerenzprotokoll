## Reading — `finales-kausales-plot-geruest`, 2025-07-29, the causal beat sheet — the Guardians as AEGIS's analysts and Systemagenten, some of them doubting

The beat sheet places the Guardians in its second act and gives them, for the OS (AEGIS) throughline, the role of AEGIS' agents. In Kapitel 18-21 (Beat 2.2) it writes that they „setzen AEGIS' fehlerhafte Protokolle aktiv durch“ ^[?], acting as „Systemagenten“ ^[?]. Where Kael is analysed, it names them in one parenthesis: „wo er von AEGIS und seinen Guardians (LogOS, Mnemosyne, Cerberus, Kairos/Sophia)“ ^[?]. It gives each a limit, and these limits keep the Guardians from understanding Kael: „hindern sie daran, Kael wirklich zu verstehen, was zu kontraproduktiven Interventionen führt“ ^[?].

In Kapitel 22-24 (Beat 2.3) the beat sheet turns them: „Einige Guardians beginnen, ein“ ^[?] paradox in AEGIS' acts to observe, which makes them doubt their loyalty or the system's final aim. The causal link says the paradox „wird selbst für seine Agenten unbestreitbar und schafft internen Dissens im antagonistischen System“ ^[?]. For the OS line it reads this as AEGIS' brittleness shown „durch die dämmernde Erkenntnis seiner Guardians über die Fehler des Systems“ ^[?].

The beat sheet is a plan and writes it as one; the Guardians' relation to AEGIS is here that of agents who may come to doubt.
