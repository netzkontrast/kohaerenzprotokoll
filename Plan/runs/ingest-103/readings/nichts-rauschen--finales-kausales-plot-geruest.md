## Reading — `finales-kausales-plot-geruest`, 2025-07-29, the causal beat sheet — the Nichts Rauschens as the threat AEGIS answers

The beat sheet's introduction to Act I speaks of the threatening presence of the `Nichts Rauschens` ^[finales-kausales-plot-geruest.md:#2], to which it ties AEGIS' emergence. Beat 1.1 (Kapitel 1–2) has AEGIS initiate its reboot against a threat „nicht-lokale Nicht-Existenz“ ^[?] perceived, and gives AEGIS an existence „dessen Existenz durch die Negation des“ ^[?L32] Nichts Rauschens defined. The sheet writes the term in the genitive, `Nichts Rauschens`, both times.
