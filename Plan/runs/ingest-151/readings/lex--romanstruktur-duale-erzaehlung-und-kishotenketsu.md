## Reading — `romanstruktur-duale-erzaehlung-und-kishotenketsu`, 2025-08-15, the dual structure — Lex as the rational ANP that AEGIS strengthens, planned across four acts

The dual structure is an outline; Lex is a planned figure in it. In Kapitel 3 he warns: „Lex (der rationale ANP) vor den emotionalen Risiken warnt“ ^[?]. In Kapitel 10 AEGIS decides „den logischen ANP, Lex, zu stärken“ ^[?], and in Kapitel 11 the Ki pass has „Die Stimme von Lex ist in seinem Kopf stärker und überzeugender“ ^[?].

The table of the Anteile follows him: in Akt I he is „Dominanter interner Kritiker“ ^[?], in Akt II „Von AEGIS gestärkt“ ^[?] and the internal tyrant who locks up the EPs. In Akt IV, Kapitel 32, he „bietet seine logischen Fähigkeiten für die Planung an“ ^[?], no longer as a tyrant but, in the plan's words, tempered by an understanding of emotional needs.
