## Reading — `romanstruktur-duale-erzaehlung-und-kishotenketsu`, 2025-08-15, the dual structure — Alex named beside Rhys in the inner council

The dual structure is an outline. It names Alex twice, both times beside Rhys and the child parts. In Kapitel 32 „Rhys und Alex arbeiten daran, die kindlichen Anteile zu trösten und zu sichern“ ^[?], and in the table of the Anteile Kiko, in Akt IV, „Findet durch Rhys und Alex Sicherheit“ ^[?]. Alex has no row of his own in that table.
