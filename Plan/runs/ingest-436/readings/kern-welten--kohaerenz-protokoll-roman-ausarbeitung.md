## Reading — `kohaerenz-protokoll-roman-ausarbeitung`, 2025-04-27, the elaboration report — the Kernwelten as caches of Kael's identity, twenty of them, four in focus

The elaboration report proposes the Kernwelten as Kael's subjective realities: „Die Kernwelten fungieren als Kaels subjektive“ ^[?] ones, whose coherence is influenced by Kael's inner state and the Moonshine-Link. Section 3 has a heading „Differenzierung der vier Kernwelten“ ^[?], giving each focused world its own atmosphere; the world codes are not expanded here. The report counts the cache metaphor over twenty: „Jeder der 20 (fokussiert auf 4) Kernwelten“ ^[?]. The other worlds are to be hinted at: the existence of the further worlds „sollte subtil angedeutet werden“ ^[?], and among the hints is a direct mention of the total, „einer von zwanzig Realitäts-Caches“ ^[?]. The report calls the worlds „psycho-aktive Umgebungen“ ^[?].
