## Reading — `kohaerenz-protokoll-roman-ausarbeitung`, 2025-04-27, the elaboration report — a design for AEGIS as autopoietic, reductionist and limited

The elaboration report proposes a treatment of AEGIS; it is a report and framework, and its sections on the novel are proposals. Its analysis of Kael's identity says that „AEGIS' reduktionistische Analyse könnte die verschiedenen Kael-Instanzen“ ^[?] as qualitatively similar but numerically distinct entities. On motive the report proposes „AEGIS' primärer Antrieb ist nicht Bosheit, sondern Autopoiesis“ ^[?], and that the per-chapter `Einblick in AEGIS` sections, which it restates from the commission (`[Query Pt 4]`), should show „die Wahrnehmung von M als destabilisierenden Faktor“ ^[?]. For methods it restates the commission: „AEGIS' Werkzeuge sind Logik, Analyse und ein inhärent reduktionistischer Ansatz“ ^[?].

On limits, the report holds that AEGIS as a logic-based system meets formal limits (L161, whose line names a person) and proposes that this shows as „Kontrollverlust“ ^[?]: „Unerwartete, emergente Phänomene treten in den Kernwelten auf, die AEGIS nicht kontrollieren kann.“ ^[?] In its cache metaphor it sets „AEGIS = Kohärenzprotokoll/Bus“ ^[?], which tries to manage the relations between the cache-like Kernwelten and fails because of the complexity of M.
