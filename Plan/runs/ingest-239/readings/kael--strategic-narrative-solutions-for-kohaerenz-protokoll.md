## Reading — `strategic-narrative-solutions-for-kohaerenz-protokoll`, 2025-11-03, the strategic solutions report — Kael's state as the mechanism of the Risse, the Act 2 cycles and the resolution

The strategic solutions report proposes that the Risse „are a direct, causal manifestation of Kael's internal psychological state“ ^[?], and grounds this in a claim about his origin: „Since Kael is a sentient fragment of the system's original architect (AEGIS)“ ^[?]. For Act 2 it proposes that each cycle „represents a failed attempt by Kael's Apparently Normal Personality parts“ ^[?] to suppress trauma, with permanent costs: „a small but permanent cost is exacted on Kael's internal system“ ^[?]. It proposes that these costs force a turn to the Gardener role: „This transition is embodied in the adoption of the“ ^[?] the `Gardener` role.

For the resolution it proposes that „The primary causal mechanism of the resolution is Kael's development of a“ ^[?] dialetheic mind, and that „By integrating his alters, Kael transforms his mind into a high-Φ“ ^[?] system. It adds that the Juna/V connection is „a catalyst, not a magical solution“ ^[?], so that Kael keeps his agency. These are the report's proposals, not canon.
