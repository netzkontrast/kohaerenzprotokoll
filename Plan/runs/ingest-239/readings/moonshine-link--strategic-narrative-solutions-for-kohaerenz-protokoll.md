## Reading — `strategic-narrative-solutions-for-kohaerenz-protokoll`, 2025-11-03, the strategic solutions report — a damaged link, and a natural phenomenon AEGIS cannot see

The strategic solutions report proposes that the Moonshine-Link „to the external figures Juna/V is damaged with each cycle“ ^[?] of Act 2, growing fainter and noisier, which raises Kael's isolation. In its resolution it proposes defining the link as „non-local, sub-protocol“ ^[?], and as a natural phenomenon that „AEGIS, with its classical and local framework, is ontologically blind to“ ^[?]. Its function is to give Kael gnosis rather than episteme: „The link's primary function is to provide Kael with“ ^[?] it, and the connection „acts as a catalyst for Kael's own transformative work“ ^[?]. Proposals of the report, not canon.
