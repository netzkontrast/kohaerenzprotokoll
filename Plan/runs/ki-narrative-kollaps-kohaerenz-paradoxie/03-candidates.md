## open while reading

The document is one voice, an expository synthesis that restates two other documents of the
project and then sides with the second. It never names a speaker and never marks a passage
with a label; what marks a passage is its heading, the footnote number glued to its
sentences, and the word the sentence itself uses for a document's standing (`kanonische`,
`kritische`, `Prämisse`, `Beweis`).

The export lost every inline symbol. Where the running text names the two kernels or the
pressure, the threshold and the erasure cost, only an empty pair of parentheses or a hyphen
with nothing before it is left, so the symbols are not on the list and no count can find
them. The one place the kernels keep a name is the header row of the table, which writes
`K1` and `K0` with plain digits and an invisible character after each; the document never
writes a subscripted form.

Section numbering runs 2.3 and then 4, there is no section 3, and the title the manifest
gives the file is not the heading the file itself opens with.

One name carries two things. Kael is the whole system of parts and, in the table, one of
the parts listed under ANP; the document says how many parts the system has and names fewer
figures than that, and writes Lia in a sentence and Lyra in the table without saying whether
they are one figure. System 1 and System 2 number two pairings: the orchestrator and executor
of the table, and Semantic World Engine and Experiential Renderer in the bias list, which
puts the numbers the other way round or leaves open whether the two are one pair.

Kohärenz is used for the kernel AEGIS is assigned to, for the closed-system theory of truth
AEGIS is said to defend, for the emergent truth of the entropic column of the table, and for
the target coherence of the last section; the table sets Kohärenz-Kernel and Kohärenz-Wahrheit
in adjacent columns with the correspondence theory under the first. Fusion is the title of
Phase III and also the merging of the parts the Mosaik-Herz is said to reject.
