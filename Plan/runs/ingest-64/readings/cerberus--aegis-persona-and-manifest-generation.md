## Reading — `aegis-persona-and-manifest-generation`, 2026-04-27, the AEGIS Reboot Manifest — Guardian of the Boundary Fortress, security and the hostility of all novelty

The manifest says the administration of the Boundary Fortress, Core World 3, „is delegated to the Guardian subsystem designated as Cerberus“ ^[?]. His domain „covers security, defense, systemic boundaries, and integrity protocols“ ^[?], and he is the „ultimate firewall and immune system of the architecture“ ^[?]. His limitation is a „hardcoded algorithm that mandates the classification of all novelty, ambiguity, and deviation from the established baseline as an immediate hostile threat“ ^[?]. Facing the anomaly he „processes it strictly as an unidentifiable intrusion penetrating system boundaries“ ^[?]; the manifest adds that he „actively works against system resolution by isolating and neutralizing the anomaly's signals“ ^[?].
