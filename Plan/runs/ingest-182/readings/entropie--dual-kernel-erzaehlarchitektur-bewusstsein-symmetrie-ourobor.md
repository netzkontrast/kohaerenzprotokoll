## Reading — `dual-kernel-erzaehlarchitektur-bewusstsein-symmetrie-ourobor`, 2026-04-28, the Dual-Kernel analysis — entropy in the Dual Kernel Theory and in AEGIS' misreading

The Dual-Kernel analysis uses entropy twice. In its account of the Dual Kernel Theory it calls the Erasure-Kernel an active, structure-destroying pressure „der als computationales Äquivalent der Entropie fungiert“ ^[?] (the kernel symbol was lost in the export).

In its report of the Protokoll, AEGIS „interpretiert die steigende interne Kommunikation und Komplexität als einen katastrophalen Anstieg der Entropie“ ^[?]; Kael's healing is therefore read by AEGIS as a rise of entropy, not as progress.
