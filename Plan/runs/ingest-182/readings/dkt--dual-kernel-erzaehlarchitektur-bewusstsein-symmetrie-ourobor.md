## Reading — `dual-kernel-erzaehlarchitektur-bewusstsein-symmetrie-ourobor`, 2026-04-28, the Dual-Kernel analysis — the theory as the lens of the report

The Dual-Kernel analysis names the Dual-Kernel-Theorie (DKT) in its opening as one of the lenses through which it reads the Kohärenz-Protokoll, beside Dramatica. Its own section on the theory says: „Die Dual Kernel Theory (DKT) bietet einen radikalen Neuentwurf der Realität“ ^[?], a tension between the Kohärenz-Kernel and the Erasure-Kernel (the kernel symbols were lost in the export). The same line adds that the theory „verschiebt den Fokus von der statischen Materie“ ^[?] toward the dynamics of information kept and information lost.

The report presents the DKT as a theory it applies, not as part of the novel's canon.
