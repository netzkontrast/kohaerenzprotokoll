## Reading — `roman-outline-stilmittel-perspektiven-umsetzung`, 2026-02-23, the drafting compendium — Kael as Host, and the arc the compendium proposes for him

The drafting compendium is a guide to the author for writing out the outline; of Kael it partly reports the author's documents (reference numbers glued to the lines) and partly instructs. It reports him beginning as the „Apparently Normal Part (ANP) oder Host“ ^[?], whose function is „das Bewältigen des Alltags“ ^[?], with amnesia about his traumas and his multiplicity. It reports that he first takes his flashbacks and emotional intrusions as „externe Programmierfehler“ ^[?] of the Konstrukt-Stadt, and then instructs: the author must structure the story so that the reader first reads the Risse as cyberpunk elements.

The arc in dramatic phases (L161–L166) is the compendium's own layout. In the Exposition Kael works as an unknowing cog (Host amnesia). In the Rising Action he falls „durch die Risse in die Resonanz-Landschaft und die Grenzfeste“ ^[?] and meets his Alters and their Guardians. At the Climax he tries „die Logik von AEGIS mit dessen eigenen Mitteln zu schlagen“ ^[?] and meets Gödelian incompleteness (L164). In the Falling Action he applies the concept of „funktionalen Multiplizität“ ^[?], synchronising the Alters, and acts as a „Gärtner“ in the Möglichkeits-Garten (L165); the Denouement is the „Wir-Geflechts“ ^[?] (L166).

All of this is drafting advice, not a statement of what the novel's canon holds.
