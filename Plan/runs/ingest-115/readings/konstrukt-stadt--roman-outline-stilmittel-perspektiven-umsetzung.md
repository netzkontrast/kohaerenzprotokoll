## Reading — `roman-outline-stilmittel-perspektiven-umsetzung`, 2026-02-23, the drafting compendium — KW1 of the Manager parts, and the first chapter's Architectural Storytelling

The drafting compendium reports the Konstrukt-Stadt (KW1) as the Kern-Welt of the IFS Manager parts, with LogOS as its Guardian: „Eine Welt aus euklidischer Geometrie, kausalem Determinismus und absoluter Vorhersehbarkeit“ ^[?]. It instructs that K1-dominated scenes, „etwa in der Konstrukt-Stadt“ ^[?], be written in a strict, clinical, objective and minimalist style. For the first chapter it instructs: „Die Ausformulierung der Konstrukt-Stadt im ersten Kapitel erfordert den gezielten Einsatz“ ^[?] of Architectural Storytelling and Uncanny Valley effects, and in its phase layout it proposes that the Konstrukt-Stadt be established „als steriler, fehlerfreier Raum“ ^[?]. Its figures there, such as the Therapeut-Konstrukt, „dürfen nicht als echte Menschen geschrieben werden“ ^[?]. The compendium also reports that Kael first reads his flashbacks as the Konstrukt-Stadt's programming faults (L89). These are drafting instructions and reports, not canon.
