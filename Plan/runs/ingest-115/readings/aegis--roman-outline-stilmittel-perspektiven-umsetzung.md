## Reading — `roman-outline-stilmittel-perspektiven-umsetzung`, 2026-02-23, the drafting compendium — AEGIS as the antagonist the guide tells the author to render

The drafting compendium, a guide of 2026-02-23 to the author for writing out the outline, gives AEGIS its expansion „Autonomous Entropic Gatekeeper for Integrity Systems“ ^[?] and says the world is defined by „unaufhörlichen Kampf gegen entropische Zerfallsprozesse“ ^[?]. A reference number is glued to that sentence (reference 1, `Entropie: Narrative und digitale Welten`), so the line reports the author's document. In its triad of entropies it reports AEGIS working on the information-theoretic level: the system tries to reduce the Überwelt „auf hochgradig vorhersehbare, komprimierbare und redundanzarme Zustände“ ^[?], and what does not fit — emotions, qualia, Juna — is „als gefährliches Rauschen klassifiziert“ ^[?].

The compendium instructs that the Landauer heat of AEGIS's erasures be shown as „direkte, physische Konsequenzen von AEGIS' Kontrollzwang“ ^[?]. On Ashby's law it states that the system „versucht, ein unendlich dimensionales Problem auf eine zweidimensionale Kontrollmatrix zu projizieren“ ^[?]. For the Genesis it reports (reference 5) the self-definition „AEGIS ist, was AEGIS verhindert, dass es nicht ist“ ^[?], and of the Kern-Welten that the system tried „in abgeschlossene Simulationsräume zu sperren“ ^[?] the fragmented parts of Kael. Its closing instruction ties AEGIS to the style: a scene change „Wechselt die Szene in den Bereich von AEGIS oder LogOS“ ^[?] calls for a technocratic style, and the hostility is to be felt in the world's sterile geometry. These are instructions for writing, recorded and not applied.
