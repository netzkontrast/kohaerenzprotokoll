## Reading — `roman-outline-stilmittel-perspektiven-umsetzung`, 2026-02-23, the drafting compendium — Guardians as confrontations with IFS parts

The drafting compendium seats one Guardian per Kern-Welt in its IFS section — LogOS, Mnemosyne, Cerberus (L96, L104, L112) — and instructs that the confrontations with Guardians in each Kern-Welt not be mere obstacles: „dürfen nicht lediglich physische Hindernisse darstellen“ ^[?]. Each confrontation, with Cerberus in the Grenzfeste as its example, is one with an isolated part of Kael's own psyche (L176). In the phase layout Kael meets „die jeweiligen Guardians“ ^[?] in the fall through the Risse.
