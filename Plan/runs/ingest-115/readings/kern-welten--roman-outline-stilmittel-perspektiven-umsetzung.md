## Reading — `roman-outline-stilmittel-perspektiven-umsetzung`, 2026-02-23, the drafting compendium — the Kern-Welten as the externalisation of Kael's DID

The drafting compendium states, with a reference number glued to the line, that the construction principle of the simulated Kern-Welten is „die Externalisierung von Kaels Dissoziativer Identitätsstörung“ ^[?], and that AEGIS tried to lock the fragmented Alters into closed simulation spaces: „in abgeschlossene Simulationsräume zu sperren“ ^[?]. It names three worlds by IFS role — Konstrukt-Stadt (KW1), Resonanz-Landschaft (KW2), Grenzfeste (KW3) (L97, L105, L113) — and speaks of the Möglichkeits-Garten and the Ly-Welt only in the final phases (L164–L165), without a number. It instructs that the author understand every setting and side figure as the expression of a specific IFS part (L83).
