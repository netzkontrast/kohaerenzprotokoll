It corresponds to the“ ^[?] first world, the City. The Chaos kernel: „It is high-entropy, fluid, and dynamic. It corresponds to the“ ^[?] second world, the Archipelago. A third world, the Überwelt, appears in its plot: „Kael breaches the firewall of the“ ^[?] Überwelt, which the report numbers as the third world and calls AEGIS's command center.
