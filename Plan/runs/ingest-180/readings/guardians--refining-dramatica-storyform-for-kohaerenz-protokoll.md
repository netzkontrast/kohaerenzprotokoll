## Reading — `refining-dramatica-storyform-for-kohaerenz-protokoll`, 2026-01-02, the storyform exegesis — the Guardians as subsystems of AEGIS, with a schism

The storyform exegesis decides the Guardians' role as a design choice of its plot: the `Wächter` are, in its words, „are subsystems of AEGIS. To heighten the drama, we implement the“ ^[?] `Guardian's Dilemma`. It sets the mechanism: „As Kael integrates, the Guardians—designed to protect the system—begin to glitch.“ ^[?] A separate section of the report frames the same decision: „The Guardians are not monolithic. As AEGIS weakens, they split.“ ^[?]

In its plot the Dilemma appears when „A Guardian (Kairos) tracks them to“ ^[?] the Archipelago and hesitates, and the Schism as „The Guardians fight each other. Civil war in the OS.“ ^[?] This is the report's proposal for its own storyform, not a statement of what the sources it cites say of the Guardians.
