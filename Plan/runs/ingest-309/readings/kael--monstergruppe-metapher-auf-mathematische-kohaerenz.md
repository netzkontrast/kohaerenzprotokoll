What the Monster-metaphor validation report says of Kael comes from the request it answers. The report judges the request's presentation of Kael and Juna as representatives of the Baby-Monster-Gruppen „ist konzeptionell anspruchsvoll“ ^[?], and its claim about their connection and `M` „steht im Widerspruch zur mathematischen Beziehung“ ^[?].

On Kael's fragmentation, as the request states it, the report finds the reading „ist metaphorisch interessant, aber mathematisch heikel“ ^[?], because „Einfache Gruppen wie B sind per Definition unzerlegbar“ ^[?]. It guesses what the request means: „Möglicherweise ist gemeint“ ^[?] a fragmented manifestation within AEGIS's simulation. In its verdict section it calls the interpretation „ebenfalls mathematisch inkohärent“ ^[?]. In `Strategie B1` it proposes an instability instead of a fragmentation. All of this is the report's judgement of a metaphor, not applied.
