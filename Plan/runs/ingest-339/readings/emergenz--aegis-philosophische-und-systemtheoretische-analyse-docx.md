## Reading — `aegis-philosophische-und-systemtheoretische-analyse-docx`, 2025-08-05, the philosophical and systems-theory analysis — emergence as filtering and as the status of existence

Part I reports the systems-theory concept as borrowed: „Das Auftreten neuer, qualitativer Eigenschaften auf Systemebene“ ^[?] that cannot be derived from isolated parts. The essay's own application: the fourth axiom of its brief, emergence by negation, is read as „AEGIS' Negation ist vielmehr ein“ ^[?L143] active filter process, a delimitation from inconsistency and not a creation from nothing. The essay says „Indem Inkonsistenz aktiv ausgegrenzt wird, verstärkt sich die kohärente Struktur selbst“ ^[?], and in the systems-theory section „Existenz und Struktur sind emergente Eigenschaften“ ^[?] arising from local interactions and verification. The essay's own reading offers this as an interpretation, not a finding.
