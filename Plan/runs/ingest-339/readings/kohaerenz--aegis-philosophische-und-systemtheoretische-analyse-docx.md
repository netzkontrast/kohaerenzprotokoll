## Reading — `aegis-philosophische-und-systemtheoretische-analyse-docx`, 2025-08-05, the philosophical and systems-theory analysis — coherence instead of truth as operational closure

The essay lists „Kohärenz statt Wahrheit“ ^[?L182] among AEGIS's operative principles of its brief, and the phrase stands `Kohärenz statt Wahrheit` ^[aegis-philosophische-und-systemtheoretische-analyse-docx.md:#6] times. Part I reports the systems-theory definition as borrowed: „Kohärenz bezieht sich auf die interne Konsistenz und Stimmigkeit eines Systems“ ^[?]. The mapping table gives the principle's core as „Interne Konsistenz, nicht externe Korrespondenz“ ^[?], with coherentism, constructivism and operational closure as the theoretical concepts.

The essay's own reading: by establishing the principle, „Wahrheit als Übereinstimmung mit einer externen Realität wird durch interne, strukturelle Konsistenz ersetzt“ ^[?], so the external perspective is irrelevant by definition; the same passage connects this to operational closure. It adds that the system „Das System strebt nach Kohärenz“ ^[?L197] and so to a form of stability. For non-existence it asks whether it is a state of „fundamentaler Inkohärenz“ ^[?]. The essay weighs the price: its conclusion names the danger of solipsism and uncorrectable internal error.
