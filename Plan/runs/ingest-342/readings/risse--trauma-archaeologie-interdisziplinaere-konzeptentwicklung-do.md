## Reading — `trauma-archaeologie-interdisziplinaere-konzeptentwicklung-do`, 2025-08-05, the Trauma-Archäologie report — „Glitches“ ^[?] of a simulated reality as a possible cause of existential dissonance

The Trauma-Archäologie report asks, as an open question about the origin of its unnamed protagonist system's trauma: „Gibt es frühe Anzeichen für die Künstlichkeit, die Begrenztheit oder die“ ^[?] `Glitches` of reality that could lead to existential dissonance, and says this „könnte die Wahrnehmung der Realität als Illusion oder Täuschung begründen“ ^[?]. In concept 4 the same sense returns as a conditional: the confrontation with such limits could trigger an ontological shock, and the final comparison reads the traumas as showing through „durch die Risse in der Psyche oder durch“ ^[?] `Glitches` in the system. The word `Risse` there names cracks in the psyche; the `Glitches` are the simulation's.
