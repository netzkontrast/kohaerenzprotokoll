## Reading — `trauma-archaeologie-interdisziplinaere-konzeptentwicklung-do`, 2025-08-05, the Trauma-Archäologie report — a hypothetical Genesis narrative as the starting point of a trauma analysis

The Trauma-Archäologie report opens its section on the origin with a note that marks its own ground: „Dieser Abschnitt geht von einem hypothetischen“ ^[?] Genesis narrative fragment, or derives its potential elements from the given cosmology. The report's unnamed protagonist system is traced back to this narrative, which the report holds open: „Dieses Narrativ, ob explizit vorhanden oder implizit aus der Kosmologie erschlossen, enthält potenziell Hinweise auf frühe Verletzungen oder Spannungen.“ ^[?]

The report's Genesis is thus a working assumption for finding seeds of existential wounding, not a narrative it reports or fixes.
