## Reading — `briefing-document-the-kohaerenz-protokoll-narrative-framewor`, 2025-11-03, the framework briefing — Kael as a TSDP system whose integration is the resolution

The framework briefing synthesises the project and introduces System Kael as „a human consciousness fragmented by trauma“ ^[?], modeled on the clinical TSDP. It calls him the protagonist, „a single consciousness whose internal fragmentation is the direct cause of the world's instability“ ^[?]. In its table of parts Kael is typed „ANP (Host)“ ^[?], with the motivation „Maintain normalcy; Fear of losing control and being overwhelmed by EPs“ ^[?].

For the resolution it says Kael achieves „functional multiplicity“ ^[?], and that his integrated state becomes a living Gödel-Satz, a truth AEGIS's system cannot take in: it is valid in that system „but cannot be proven or resolved by its classical, binary logic“ ^[?]. The briefing writes this as the story's own account.
