## Reading — `roman-als-transformativer-prozess-alchemie-der-erzaehlung`, 2025-10-16, the alchemy-of-narration blueprint (an unsigned German strategy report) — the Nichts Rauschen as the drift of the unobserved

The alchemy-of-narration blueprint places the awakened Kael as „einer Insel stabiler Information im chaotischen Potenzial der ungelesenen Geschichte“ ^[?], and in Part II says that the natural tendency of the universe, when unobserved, is decay: „Die natürliche Tendenz des Universums ist es, in Bedeutungslosigkeit“ ^[?]; the line names it `Nichts Rauschen`.
