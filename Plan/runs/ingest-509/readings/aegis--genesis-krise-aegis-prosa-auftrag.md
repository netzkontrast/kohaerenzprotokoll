## Reading — `genesis-krise-aegis-prosa-auftrag`, 2025-04-29, the Genesis-crisis prose commission — AEGIS as a system whose misreading of inner suffering the commission plans to show

The Genesis-crisis commission is a plan and a writing order, not a narrative; its suggestions are mostly hedged. For the state before the crisis it plans an „Andeutung der latenten Vulnerabilität durch das Kernparadoxon“ ^[?] of AEGIS and the Ursprungs-Ich. The turn is placed in AEGIS's logic: „AEGIS interpretiert das interne Signal des subjektiven Leidens fälschlicherweise als gefährliches Rauschen oder Datenkorruption“ ^[?], which the commission names as AEGIS's Hamartia. Its core identity it gives as „AEGIS ist, was es verhindert“ ^[?].

The Kohärenz Protokoll is planned as a defence: „Es repräsentiert den Versuch des ANP (AEGIS-Kernlogik), den EP (Ursprungs-Ich) zu kontrollieren und zu eliminieren“ ^[?]. It adds that „Das Protokoll ist somit auch eine ontologische Gewaltanwendung“ ^[?], and that AEGIS actively produces ignorance about the Ich's state: „AEGIS produziert aktiv“ ^[?]. In the table of borrowed concepts, trauma bonding is offered as a possible metaphoric dynamic with AEGIS „als Kontrollinstanz“ ^[?].

For the voice, the commission directs the AEGIS scenes to be „Kühl, technisch, präzise.“ ^[?]

Recorded, not applied: the commission asks the writer to keep to established lore but does not itself define it.
