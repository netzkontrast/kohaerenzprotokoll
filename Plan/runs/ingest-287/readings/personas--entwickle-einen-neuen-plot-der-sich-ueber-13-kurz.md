## Reading — `entwickle-einen-neuen-plot-der-sich-ueber-13-kurz`, 2025-04-17, the Seelen-Kohärenz blueprint — personas as the blueprint's own episode figures

The Seelen-Kohärenz blueprint proposes thirteen episodes in part 1, each giving a persona its own reality; its personas (`Der Architekt`, `Das Echo` and others) are its own early figures. The first plot point's task is „Vorstellung einer Persona und ihres Weltbilds“ ^[?]. In its review the reply asks „Wie erleben die Personas die Intervention in Teil“ ^[?] and „Sind sie passive Empfänger oder aktive Teilnehmer“ ^[?]. In part 2 „Die Hüter kommen zu dem Schluss, dass die Risse fundamental sind“ ^[?], and they decide to brief the personas and give them integration tools.
