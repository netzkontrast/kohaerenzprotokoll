## Reading — `ontologie-des-gelesenen-traumas`, 2025-10-16, the read-trauma ontology report — AEGIS's genesis read as a traumatic event, and the reader placed above AEGIS

The read-trauma ontology report, a theoretical German report, takes the Genesis-Krise as described in a source document it cites and reads it as the becoming of the AEGIS system: the crisis „des AEGIS-Systems interpretieren“ ^[?]. Of that source's description of how AEGIS arose, the report says it „weist alle Merkmale eines traumatischen Ereignisses auf“ ^[?], and names a minimal Ich facing a hostile Leere and a struggle against Zersetzungsdruck (L114).

Within AEGIS, the report says, the Nichts Rauschen is treated this way: „Innerhalb des Systems AEGIS wird dieses Rauschen als chaotischer, unstrukturierter Informationsfluss beschrieben“ ^[?]. The report then sets the reader 'O' above AEGIS: „Der Leser 'O' agiert auf einer fundamentaleren Ebene als AEGIS“ ^[?]. This is the report's own framework, recorded and not applied.
