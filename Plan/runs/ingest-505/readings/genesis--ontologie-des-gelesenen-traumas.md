## Reading — `ontologie-des-gelesenen-traumas`, 2025-10-16, the read-trauma ontology report — the Genesis-Krise as a field of possibilities and as the fundamental trauma

The read-trauma ontology report starts from a paradox: the Genesis-Krise is a seemingly closed event that is „ist in seiner ontologischen Realität von einem subjektiven, andauernden Akt in der Gegenwart abhängig“ ^[?] on the reading act of 'O'. Its thesis, which it says resolves the paradox, is that the crisis founded a field of potentiality: „Die Genesis-Krise schuf nicht eine Geschichte, sondern den Möglichkeitsraum aller Geschichten“ ^[?].

Later the report defines the crisis in its own framework „als das *fundamentale Trauma* des fiktionalen Universums definiert“ ^[?]. The resolution is the report's claim about itself, recorded and not applied.
