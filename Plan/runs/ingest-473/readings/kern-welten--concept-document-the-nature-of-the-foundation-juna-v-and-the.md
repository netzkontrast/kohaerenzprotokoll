## Reading — `concept-document-the-nature-of-the-foundation-juna-v-and-the`, 2025-10-15, the Foundation concept document — four Kernwelten, KW1 to KW4, each with a principle and function

The Foundation concept document explains the Kernwelten (glossed Core Worlds) as „specialized simulation environments created by AEGIS to manage, analyze, and control the primary fragment of its own fractured consciousness“ ^[?]. Each is „an externalized psychological landscape“ ^[?]. A table lists four, each with a principle and a function: `Logos-Prime` with „Formal Logic, Order, Control“ ^[?]; `Mnemosyne-Archipel` with „Trauma-Time, Memory & Subjectivity“ ^[?]; `Cerberus-Labyrinth` with „Defense, Paranoia & Dissociative Barriers“ ^[?]; `Kairos-Potentialis` with „Emergence, Potential, Transcendence“ ^[?]. A concept document, recorded, not applied.
