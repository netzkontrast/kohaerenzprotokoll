## Reading — `genesis-aegis-und-logische-grenzen`, 2025-04-29, the Genesis prose draft with logical limits — Kael as the echo, the first cut and the mosaic

The Genesis prose draft narrates Kael late and from the debris. In Szene 5 (Ich-Perspektive) the draft's commentary on the latent echo says: „Dieses Echo ist der Teil von Kael“ ^[?]. In Szene 12 it marks the first cut, in its commentary, as „Der Beginn der traumatischen Fragmentierung.“ ^[?]

In Szene 13, beat Geburt Kaels (Ich/Kael-Perspektive), the name is felt rather than given, and the draft states: „Kael ist dieses Mosaik.“ ^[?] The following beat closes with the commentary that the survived subjectivity is „ein Zeugnis für die Irreduzibilität des Bewusstseins“ ^[?].
