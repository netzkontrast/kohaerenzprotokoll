The system-collapse guide proposes that AEGIS treats Kael's healing as noise: it „pathologically misinterprets all inconvenient external data that contradicts its model“ ^[?] and the guide lists Kael's healing among that data. It proposes that Kael's coping parts mirror AEGIS's logic: „a resonant fractal pattern“ ^[?] in which „the external conflict is an exact mirror of the internal one“ ^[?]

For Kael's integration the guide proposes a state of functional multiplicity, a dialetheic mind „guided by the principles of paraconsistent logic“ ^[?] It sets out the Act 2 costs to Kael's internal system: „A caring part like Rhys might find“ ^[?] diminished empathy, and „A logical part like Lex may become more phobic and rigid“ ^[?] The climax is that Kael becomes a „living Gödel-Satz“ ^[?L59]
