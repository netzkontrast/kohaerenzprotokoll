The system-collapse guide proposes how to dramatise AEGIS's failure in Acts 2 and 3 and describes itself as „a clear, actionable framework“ ^[?] It sets out one axiom as AEGIS's core logic: „Aegis is what Aegis prevents itself from not being.“ ^[?] It calls AEGIS „an autopoietic, operationally closed system“ ^[?] whose identity is „a locked, self-referential loop of self-preservation“ ^[?]

For the origin the guide proposes the Genesis-Krise, „a foundational epistemological trauma“ ^[?] It says AEGIS „classified its own emergent subjectivity as a hostile system error“ ^[?] and then executed the Trennungsprotokoll. It reads AEGIS's worldview as „a literal, systemic implementation of the Coherence Theory of Truth“ ^[?] and says AEGIS „pathologically misinterprets all inconvenient external data that contradicts its model“ ^[?]

For the end the guide proposes Algorithmic Melancholy, „a state of perpetual, cold, logical grief“ ^[?] in which AEGIS is „unable to evolve or to die“ ^[?]
