## Reading — `master-konzept-kohaerenz-protokoll-analyse`, 2025-12-05, the master concept — Kael as the system against AEGIS, the main character of the Mnemosyne-Archipel, who must become dialetheic

The master concept, a synthesis that defines the novel as one structure, describes a fictional world in which „ein fragmentiertes menschliches Bewusstsein (System Kael) um die Deutungshoheit über die Realität ringen“ ^[?] with AEGIS. It assigns him the Mnemosyne-Archipel: in its table of the Kernwelten the row reads „Main Character (MC)“ ^[?], with „Kael, Fragmentierte Erinnerung“ ^[?] as what the world represents.

For the resolution it defines a task: „Kael muss lernen, ein dialetheisches System zu werden“ ^[?]. The master concept calls the high point of the novel a paraconsistent gambit, and says „Kael besiegt AEGIS nicht mit Gewalt, sondern mit Logik“ ^[?]. In its account of the third phase the plot is the Gödel-Gambit: „Kael konfrontiert AEGIS mit dem integrierten Trauma“ ^[?], and „Er beweist, dass er als System existiert“ ^[?].

The master concept also reads the novel as isomorphic with its author's own process; that reading is its frame and is not recorded here.
