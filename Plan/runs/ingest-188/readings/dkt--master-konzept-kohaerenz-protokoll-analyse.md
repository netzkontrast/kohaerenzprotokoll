## Reading — `master-konzept-kohaerenz-protokoll-analyse`, 2025-12-05, the master concept — the DKT as the novel's world law and as the sorting of its twenty sources

The master concept calls the `DKT` the fundamental physics of the novel's world and says it is sketched in earlier documents and deepened by outside work. Its definition: „Sie besagt, dass die Realität aus dem Konflikt zweier fundamentaler Rechenkerne entsteht“ ^[?]. The two kernels are K1, the coherence kernel, and K0, the erasure kernel.

It says the novel postulates a process, not a state: „Der Roman postuliert, dass Existenz nicht statisch ist“ ^[?], and goes on to describe existence as energy-intensive protocol preservation, with consciousness and reality arising at the interface where K1 structures resist the pressure of K0.

The master concept also uses the DKT as a sorting rule for its own sources: „Um das Master-Konzept zu operationalisieren, werden die 20 Quelldokumente inventarisiert“ ^[?] and divided into two categories that correspond to the DKT. That inventory is the master concept's working method and is not read further here.
