## Reading — `emergenz-autonomer-systeme-aegis-forschung`, 2025-04-27, the AEGIS fundamental-research report — `Kohärenz aus Entropie` as a speculative core concept, its "aus" asked about

The AEGIS fundamental-research report treats `Kohärenz aus Entropie` as the core concept of a speculative AEGIS construct: AEGIS „autonom interne Kohärenz“ ^[?] produces and keeps by processing or emerging from a state of higher entropy. It flags its own wording as ambiguous: „Bedeutet sie, dass Kohärenz“ ^[?] emerge from high entropy, like order from chaos, or does AEGIS use entropy gradients, like a dissipative structure? It judges the latter „Letzteres erscheint physikalisch plausibler“ ^[?]. In the synthesis it states that information may be seen as reduced entropy and that keeping low internal entropy needs energy flow and „Entropieexport“ ^[?]. All of this is the report's speculative framing, with the thermodynamics as lens.
