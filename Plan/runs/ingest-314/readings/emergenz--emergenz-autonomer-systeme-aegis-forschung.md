## Reading — `emergenz-autonomer-systeme-aegis-forschung`, 2025-04-27, the AEGIS fundamental-research report — weak against strong emergence put to AEGIS as a question

The AEGIS fundamental-research report distinguishes weak emergence (novelty as unpredictability) from strong emergence (novelty as irreducibility) in its review of the borrowed concept, and says the distinction „ist für das AEGIS-Projekt von entscheidender Bedeutung“ ^[?]. It asks which AEGIS aims at: „Zielt AEGIS auf Systeme ab, die fundamental neue kausale Kräfte aufweisen“ ^[?] (strong), or at unpredictable behaviour within existing physical law (weak). It adds that the goal of „autonomen Realitätserzeugung“ ^[?] sounds more like strong emergence; the passage is a question and a hunch, not a decision.
