## Reading — `the-thematic-architecture-of-kohaerenz-protokoll-a-conceptua`, 2025-11-03, the lexicon of core dualities — Juna/V in the lexicon: a question of healing or paternalism, and a dual nature

The lexicon of core dualities asks whether forcing the integration of an exiled part is healing or paternalism: „Is forcing the integration of an exiled part like Juna/V“ ^[?] (L120) a benevolent act or a violation of her autonomy. Later it says Juna/V is a catalyst who is „simultaneously a transcendent entity from an external level beyond AEGIS's comprehension“ ^[?] (L144), and an exiled part of Kael's Original Self (L144). The lexicon holds both at once and resolves neither.
