## Reading — `kohaerenz-protokoll-plot-synthese`, 2025-04-25, the 39-beat plot synthesis — AEGIS as an entity of negation, its arc, and three open resolutions

The 39-beat plot synthesis, a blueprint that calls itself complete and does not choose between endings, describes AEGIS in its list of fixed elements as „Eine Entität im Potentialmeer, deren Existenz auf Negation und Abgrenzung basiert“ ^[?]. It plans, in Beat 2 (Akt 1), that „AEGIS postuliert M als Bedrohung seiner Stabilität“ ^[?]. In Beat 20 (Akt 2) it plans that „AEGIS erleidet einen signifikanten Systemfehler“ ^[?], with visible cracks in the system.

In the character vectors it writes „AEGIS' Arc ist die Tragödie einer Entität“ ^[?], and names collapse, transformation or synthesis as the resolution that decides the end of that arc.

The three resolutions are planned as variants, not as a sequence. Beat 35 (Variante A) has, as its concept, „Tragisches Ende für AEGIS“ ^[?]. Beat 36 (Variante B) plans that „AEGIS durchläuft eine radikale Transformation“ ^[?]. Beat 37 (Variante C) plans that „Aus der Interaktion von Kael/M und AEGIS entsteht etwas Neues“ ^[?]. The document says of the choice that it „hat erhebliche thematische Implikationen“ ^[?]; the choice is left open.
