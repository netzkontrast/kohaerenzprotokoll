## Reading — `kohaerenz-protokoll-narrative-architektur-2`, 2025-07-29, the system plan — KW1, a P world with a disguised NP-hard puzzle

The system plan designs KW1 as „Konstrukt-Stadt (P - Deterministische Logik)“ ^[?], with LogOS as Guardian. It proposes that „Die Welt funktioniert nach klaren, deterministischen Regeln“ ^[?] and that its central challenge is a puzzle disguised as P, a Boolean satisfiability problem. The scenario has Kael activate „ein altes Sicherheitsnetz“ ^[?L266]; LogOS tries a sequential solution and ends „in einer endlosen logischen Schleife oder einer kombinatorischen Explosion gefangen“ ^[?], a system crash it calls a „Riss“ ^[?].
