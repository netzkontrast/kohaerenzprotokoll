## Reading — `kohaerenz-protokoll-narrative-architektur-2`, 2025-07-29, the system plan — functional multiplicity as the therapeutic aim, and as narrative voice

The system plan proposes functional multiplicity as the aim, against fusion: the state is „durch verbesserte Kommunikation, Kooperation und Ko-Bewusstsein zwischen den Anteilen gekennzeichnet“ ^[?L110], and it says this fits the „Manifest der Handlungsfähigkeit“ ^[?]. For the narration it proposes that in the late phases „Funktionale Multiplizität“ ^[?] becomes a collective voice. It also says that the Fundament lets Kael build on principles of „Kooperation, Akzeptanz, Resonanz“ ^[?L232] and calls „Kaels funktionale Multiplizität“ ^[?] a more resilient form of structure. At L363 it argues that Kael creates meaning by taking „die Verantwortung für seine eigene Multiplizität“ ^[?].
