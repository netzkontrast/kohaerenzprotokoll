## Reading — `kohaerenz-protokoll-narrative-architektur-2`, 2025-07-29, the system plan — the Risse as AEGIS's cascade of failures, as puzzle consequences and as proofs of a simulation

The system plan places the Risse in three roles. In the Gödelian ending, AEGIS's analysis of the Juna connection leads to „Dies führt zu einer Kaskade von logischen Fehlern und Systemausfällen“ ^[?], and these are the Risse. In the Kernwelt entries a failed solution is a `Riss`: for the first world, LogOS being caught in a loop leads „zu einem Systemabsturz“ ^[?]; in the fourth world the `Riss` is „destruktives Chaos“ ^[?]. In the simulation section the plan proposes that they be designed as „sind die primären Beweise“ ^[?] of a simulated reality.

Of the Leere it writes of subtle „Glitches“ ^[?] in AEGIS's simulation, and says „Dies sind keine Fehler in der Simulation“ ^[?], but irruptions of entropy.
