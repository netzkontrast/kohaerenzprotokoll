## Reading — `kohaerenz-protokoll-narrative-architektur-2`, 2025-07-29, the system plan — the Kernwelten as P/NP puzzle levels, with a design matrix

The system plan proposes that each world is a game level: „Jede Kernwelt fungiert als“ ^[?L240] such a level, whose rules are the manifestation of its logical and computational physics. Section 5.2 is headed „Entwürfe für die Rätsel der Kernwelten“ ^[?] and gives KW1 as P, KW2 as beyond NP (qualia), KW3 as NP-complete and KW4 as NP search. Table 1 is offered as „Referenz-Blaupause“ ^[?] so that the foundations of each world are „konsistent in narrative Mechaniken übersetzt“ ^[?]. Its logic column reads „Rigide klassische Logik“ ^[?], „Parakonsistente Logik (FDE)“ ^[?], „Relevanzlogik (Bedrohungsfokus)“ ^[?] and „Explorative/Dialetheische Logik“ ^[?]. Among the five recommendations it proposes: „Die Kernwelten sollten nicht nur als Schauplätze dienen“ ^[?].
