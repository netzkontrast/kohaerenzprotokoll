## Reading — `dramatica-und-kohaerenz-protokoll-analyse`, 2026-04-27, the Dramatica loop analysis — AEGIS as the Universe class, its Understanding concern, and an alternative storyform in which its reality collapses

The Dramatica loop analysis, an unsigned German report, maps the Dramatica model onto the Kohärenz-Protokoll in four loops of question, hypothesis and verification, and then proposes four alternative storyforms. Its verdicts of confirmation rest on data the file does not contain; what follows records what it maps and proposes, and applies none of it.

It sets AEGIS against Kael in logic: „Während die antagonistische Kraft AEGIS auf Basis klassischer Logik operiert“ ^[?]. In the first loop its hypothesis says that AEGIS must take the class Universe: „zwingend die Klasse Universe okkupieren muss“ ^[?]. In the second loop it assigns AEGIS the Objective Story concern, „AEGIS' Anliegen in der Objective Story ist das“ ^[?] Understanding, which it explains as „Es versucht zwanghaft, die fehlerhafte Logik der Realität zu greifen und durch starre Protokolle zu bereinigen“ ^[?].

On the Risse the analysis reads them as AEGIS's own product, after the Landauer principle: „Die Risse sind also keine externen Angriffe“ ^[?], but „sondern das paradoxe Produkt von AEGIS' eigenem Versuch, Entropie zu reduzieren“ ^[?]. In the same passage AEGIS acts „durch seine autopoietische, operativ geschlossene Logik“ ^[?].

In the first alternative storyform it names the heat as the cause of the tearing: „Die Landauer-Abwärme übersteigt die Absorptionskapazität der Simulation“ ^[?]. In the fourth alternative storyform, where Kael refuses Order, it writes: „Die Realität von AEGIS kollabiert vollständig unter der nicht mehr zu bändigenden Entropie“ ^[?]. This is one proposal among four, not a decided course for AEGIS.
