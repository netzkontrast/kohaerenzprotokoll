## Reading — `holographisches-prinzip-fuer-kohaerenz-protokoll`, 2025-04-26, the holography report — the Kernwelten as different projections of one substrate

The holography report proposes that „Die verschiedenen Kernwelten innerhalb der Simulation könnten als unterschiedliche holographische Projektionen verstanden werden“ ^[?], all arising from the same M-based substrate; differences could rest on different boundary conditions, excited states of the boundary theory, or projections from different regions of the boundary. Its summary table says the Kernwelten are „Verschiedene Projektionen desselben Hologramms“ ^[?], and asks whether travel between worlds is a „Reisen zwischen Welten als Wechsel der Projektion/Boundary-Region“ ^[?] — put as a question, not a claim.
