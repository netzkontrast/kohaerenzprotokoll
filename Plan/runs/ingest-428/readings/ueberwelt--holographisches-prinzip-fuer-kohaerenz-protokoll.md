## Reading — `holographisches-prinzip-fuer-kohaerenz-protokoll`, 2025-04-26, the holography report — the simulation as hologram, projected from boundary information

The holography report names the simulation(s) AEGIS maintains „als Schutzbarriere“ ^[?] among the novel's central elements. In its summary table it proposes reading the simulation as „Hologramm/Projektion“ ^[?], a „Bulk-Realität, beschrieben durch Boundary (M)“ ^[?], emerging from boundary information. In its thematic section it asks „Ist AEGIS' Simulation nur eine weitere Schicht holographischer Projektion“ ^[?] and states that the boundaries between reality and simulation blur. Its answer on compatibility is conditional on how M is defined in the project, and its proposals do not claim canon.
