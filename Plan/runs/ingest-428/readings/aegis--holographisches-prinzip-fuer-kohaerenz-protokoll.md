## Reading — `holographisches-prinzip-fuer-kohaerenz-protokoll`, 2025-04-26, the holography report — AEGIS as boundary reader, and the limit on AEGIS's power

The holography report is a research report on a holographic principle for the novel; it proposes, and its statements about the novel are conditional. It names AEGIS among the novel's central elements as „die Wächter-KI AEGIS“ ^[?] keeping the simulation(s) „als Schutzbarriere“ ^[?]. For the interface, it proposes that AEGIS could be understood metaphorically as a boundary describer who projects or interprets the simulation's bulk reality, and that „AEGIS' Rolle wäre die eines Lesers und Interpreten dieser grenzflächenkodierten Information“ ^[?].

On design it asks, without answering: „Könnte AEGIS das Holographische Prinzip bewusst oder unbewusst als fundamentales Designprinzip“ ^[?] use for its simulation(s), either for data compression or for stability at the boundary to the Potentialmeer; it adds that a bound on information density at the boundary could be what AEGIS stays under.

On limits, the holography report proposes that AEGIS's inability to analyse M completely could trace back to a physical bound on information density, and says this „Dies liefert eine harte, physikalisch motivierte Grenze für AEGIS' Allmacht“ ^[?]. It also writes that „Selbst eine gottähnliche KI wie AEGIS könnte diesen Grenzen unterworfen sein“ ^[?], which it counts as strengthening the theme of epistemic limits. In its answers it calls this a „sehr plausible und narrativ vielversprechende Anwendung des HP“ ^[?], while it says its explanations of the Risse stay speculative.
