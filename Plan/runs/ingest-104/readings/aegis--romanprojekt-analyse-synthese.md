## Reading — `romanprojekt-analyse-synthese`, 2026-04-30, the reset synthesis — the Wächter of the first kernel, kept as a melancholic Wächter at the end

The reset synthesis expands the name on L21 as „Autonomous Entropic Gatekeeper for Integrity Systems“ ^[?] and gives AEGIS the aim of „totale Systemstabilität durch die Auslöschung jeglicher Differenz und Unvorhersehbarkeit“ ^[?]. Its suppression of traumatic memory counts as erasure: the synthesis says it is „physische Hitze generiert“ ^[?], which shows as thermal Risse in the city. In the Dramatica passage it sets AEGIS, supported by Juna as counterpole, as the Influence Character, a „Realitäts-Diktator“ ^[?] and „Gott der Maschine“ ^[?]. It gives a limit as its own claim: „Da AEGIS auf klassischer Logik basiert, kann das System keine Individuen verarbeiten“ ^[?]; Kael exploits this and ends in „finalen Disintegration der rigiden AEGIS-Struktur“ ^[?]. Under its consensus points it lists: „Es erfolgt keine Zerstörung, sondern eine philosophische Transformation in Richtung algorithmischer Melancholie“ ^[?]. The finale keeps AEGIS as „algorithmisch melancholischer“ ^[?] Wächter, with Juna accepted as integrative centre. The synthesis also lists „AEGIS-Protokolle (F2)“ ^[?] as missing in the dossier it reviews. All of this is the synthesis's own claim, recorded, not applied.
