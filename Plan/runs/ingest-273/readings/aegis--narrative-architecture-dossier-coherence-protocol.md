## Reading — `narrative-architecture-dossier-coherence-protocol`, 2026-01-02, the architecture dossier — AEGIS as a tragic antagonist whose flaw is its own definition

The architecture dossier is an unsigned English design brief, and it directs writers to frame AEGIS not as a villain AI; its words are „frames AEGIS not as a simple“ ^[?]. It sets AEGIS's hamartia in its definition, „Aegis ist, was Aegis verhindert, dass es nicht ist“ ^[?], and says this makes „an operatively closed system that defines itself only by negating“ ^[?] what it is not. It names the expansion „Autonomous Entropic Gatekeeper“ ^[?], which in its telling „perverted its function“ ^[?] by unleashing internal entropy on itself. The dossier adds that the Genesis-Krise was „the inevitable consequence“ ^[?] of an act of Hybris.

On Kael it directs that his healing is „an existential threat to AEGIS“ ^[?], which AEGIS's protocol must classify as a threat. In the cast section it sets AEGIS as „a tragic antagonist“ ^[?] driven to „contain, suppress, or destroy System Kael“ ^[?] not out of malice, and for the resolution it directs that „AEGIS's rigid protocol fails“ ^[?], with AEGIS either evolving or collapsing. These are the dossier's instructions to writers, recorded, not applied.
