## Reading — `kohaerenz-protokoll-plot-blueprint-erstellung`, 2025-04-20, the plot blueprint — Julia (Juna) as passive consequence, and the Juna-Anomalie AEGIS 1.5 tries to eliminate

The plot blueprint writes the name twice over, „Julia (Juna)“ ^[?], and does not explain the pair; this reading keeps both. Its answer to the question on how her influence works: „Julia (Juna) ist keine aktive Agentin“ ^[?] inside the simulation, and her influence is „passive Konsequenz ihrer ontologischen Verbindung zu Kael“ ^[?]. The same answer holds that the loss of the bond „erzeugt eine fundamentale Instabilität im System Kael, die AEGIS nicht beheben kann“ ^[?] and that the reset to v1.4 makes „die Abwesenheit Junas und die unterdrückte Integration schmerzhaft spürbar macht“ ^[?].

The chapter plan has a step titled „Die Juna-Spur (The Juna Trace)“ ^[?] (step 1.5). For Teil 2 the blueprint plans that AEGIS implements version 1.5 to „die Juna-Anomalie zu eliminieren“ ^[?] — which, it says, fails. Each Guardian's possible insight turns on her: „Könnte erkennen, dass Junas Verbindung keine bloße“ ^[?] possibility, as Kairos might. On Kaels loneliness after integration it plans: „dass wahre Kohärenz die Verbindung nach außen“ ^[?] requires.
