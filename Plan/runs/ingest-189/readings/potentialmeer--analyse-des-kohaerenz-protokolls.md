## Reading — `analyse-des-kohaerenz-protokolls`, 2025-11-28, the protocol analysis — the substrate as maximal Shannon entropy and a heat bath

The protocol analysis opens with the substrate and says its sources call it „alternierend als“ ^[?] `Potentialmeer`, `Die Leere` or `Nichts Rauschen`; it adds that this is not the philosophical nothing but „ein dynamisches Feld reiner, unstrukturierter informationeller Potentialität“ ^[?] (L44). In Shannon's terms it holds that the sea is a state of maximal entropy: „Potentialmeer einen Zustand maximaler Entropie“ ^[?] (L52).

For AEGIS this means „Das Potentialmeer enthält alle möglichen Informationen“ ^[?], in a superposed, uncorrelated form, white noise, so that no coherent structure can last (L60). A sentence opening `Die Dokumente` reports that the sources tie the information-theoretic entropy to the thermodynamic one, and says: „Das Potentialmeer fungiert als ein Wärmebad unendlicher Temperatur“ ^[?] (L68). AEGIS experiences the resulting pull as a permanent pressure (L68).
