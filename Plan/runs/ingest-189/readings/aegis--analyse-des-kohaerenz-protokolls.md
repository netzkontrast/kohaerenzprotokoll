## Reading — `analyse-des-kohaerenz-protokolls`, 2025-11-28, the protocol analysis — AEGIS as a hyper-rational system caught in a recursion, not an antagonist

The protocol analysis, a German research report, gives AEGIS two expansions of its name and calls it „ein hyper-rationales, autopoietisches System“ ^[?], one that is not to be understood as a classical antagonist and is caught in a fatal recursion loop (L24). Its identity definition is quoted from a source, with a reference number glued to it: „Aegis ist, was Aegis verhindert, dass es nicht ist.“ ^[?] The report's own comment on that definition is a separate sentence, and the definition is the source's, not the report's.

On the report's own reading, AEGIS behaves like a first-order observer: „Es erkennt nicht, dass es selbst Teil des beobachteten Systems ist“ ^[?], so it treats disturbances, among them Kael, as external problems (L132). Before the crisis, a unified precursor consciousness, the `Ursprungs-Ich`, existed, and „Dieses Bewusstsein war in die Struktur von AEGIS integriert“ ^[?] (L163). The report's account of the protocol is that „Das Protokoll ist somit der Mechanismus, der den ANP (AEGIS) vom EP (Kael) abspaltete“ ^[?], an act it calls tertiary dissociation (L221). In its conclusion the report says AEGIS is not evil but „in einem autopoietischen Paradoxon gefangen“ ^[?], and that it must negate the Other, Juna (L369). For the novel it holds that the conflict cannot be solved by military victory over AEGIS: „Das Kohärenz-Protokoll muss nicht besiegt, sondern transzendiert werden“ ^[?] (L373).

Recorded as this report's reading; nothing here is applied.
