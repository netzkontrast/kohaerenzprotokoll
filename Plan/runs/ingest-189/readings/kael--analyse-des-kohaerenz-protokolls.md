## Reading — `analyse-des-kohaerenz-protokolls`, 2025-11-28, the protocol analysis — Kael as the EP split from AEGIS, and the one who reaches functional multiplicity

The protocol analysis, a German research report, reads Kael through logic, trauma psychology and the plot. In its section on paraconsistent logic it names Kael as the example of a tolerated inconsistency: AEGIS allows inconsistencies „wie der Existenz von Kael, der zugleich Systemteil und Systemfeind ist“ ^[?], as long as they are isolated and encapsulated.

In its psychological reading it states: „Das Protokoll ist somit der Mechanismus, der den ANP (AEGIS) vom EP (Kael) abspaltete.“ ^[?] Kael is the Emotional Part (EP), AEGIS the Apparently Normal Part. In the table of the Kernwelten it lists him among the inhabitants of KW1 Logos-Prime: „Kael, Index“ ^[?], a world the table describes as „Eine sterile Welt perfekter Geometrie.“ ^[?]

On the ending, the report says: „Kael erreicht schließlich einen Zustand der Funktionalen Multiplizität (Integration ohne Verschmelzung).“ ^[?] The sentence stands in the paragraph on the Gödel-Gambit, whose preceding sentence opens the words `Die Dokumente deuten darauf hin`, so the report presents it as what its sources point to.

In the conclusion the analysis names Kael as the Ursprungs-Ich: AEGIS must negate the Other, which has already become part of its own Ursprungs-Ich — „Ursprungs-Ichs (Kael) geworden ist, muss AEGIS sich selbst negieren.“ ^[?] The same section calls the structural dissociation a „Fragmentierung in Kael und AEGIS“ ^[?]. These are the report's own synthesis, recorded here and not applied.
