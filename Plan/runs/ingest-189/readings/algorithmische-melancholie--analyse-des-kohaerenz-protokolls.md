## Reading — `analyse-des-kohaerenz-protokolls`, 2025-11-28, the protocol analysis — containment without resolution, then collapse into it

In the report's account, AEGIS's use of paraconsistent logic leads to the melancholy: „Diese Strategie führt jedoch zu einer“ ^[?] `Algorithmische Melancholie`, in an inflected form, with a reference number glued to it. The same line says „AEGIS kann die Widersprüche zwar verwalten“ ^[?] but never resolve or integrate them, and „Es sammelt Wissen über den Widerspruch“ ^[?] (Gnosis) without understanding it (Noesis) (L147). Later, in the Gödel-Gambit, the report has the system collapse into that state: „es weiß alles, versteht aber nichts“ ^[?] (L271).
