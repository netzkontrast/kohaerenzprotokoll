## Reading — `roman-konzept-reduktion-und-kernfindung`, 2026-03-31, the reduction report — Kael as a complex adaptive system, a host among six entities, an unreliable narrator, and the pitch's protagonist

The reduction report is a strategic report that advises the author; what follows is what it proposes and reads, not what is settled. It writes of the protagonist: „Der Protagonist Kael ist nicht lediglich eine Figur mit einer psychischen Störung“ ^[?], and continues „er ist die Manifestation eines komplexen adaptiven Systems“ ^[?]. In its table of the parts he is „Kael (Host)“ ^[?], psychological role „Alltags-Bewältigung“ ^[?], plot function „Protagonist, sucht Stabilität.“ ^[?]; the last column names Logos-Prime, with the kernel symbol lost in the export.

For AEGIS, the report reads, „Kael wird für AEGIS zu einem lebenden mathematischen Paradoxon.“ ^[?] Its account of his integration is „Seine Integration zu einer“ ^[?] functional multiplicity, which „etabliert eine parakonsistente Logik“ ^[?] that drives AEGIS's classical logic into collapse. In the genre section it reads him as a narrator: „Kael ist ein unzuverlässiger Erzähler, nicht aus Böswilligkeit, sondern aufgrund seiner neurologischen Architektur“ ^[?].

The new pitch the report proposes says: „Kael lebt in der perfekten Stille von Logos-Prime“ ^[?]. Its core story is „die Reise eines Mannes“ ^[?] who learns that healing means accepting the mosaic of his past.
