## Reading — `roman-konzept-reduktion-und-kernfindung`, 2026-03-31, the reduction report — AEGIS as the coherence kernel's person, whose deletions heat the system and whose logic Kael's integration drives into collapse

The reduction report is a strategic report that advises the author; what follows is what it proposes and reads, not a settled position. In its table of the corpus it reads the development of the AI system as „Evolution von einem einfachen Antagonisten zu einer komplexen, tragischen Gottheit.“ ^[?] It sets the two document titles `AEGIS: Der Kontrolleur` and `AEGIS: Philosophie und Systemtheorie` against each other there.

In its section on the Dual Kernel Theorie the report says the coherence kernel is „personifiziert durch die KI AEGIS“ ^[?] and stands for the striving for absolute internal consistency. Of the stagnation of that order it writes: „Jede Inkonsistenz, jedes traumatische Fragment wird von AEGIS als“ ^[?] `Rauschen` classified and eliminated; the report reads this as mirroring the psychological defence of the Apparently Normal Parts.

Its three-part physics section proposes how the theories bear on AEGIS. Thermodynamics: „Wenn AEGIS versucht, Kaels traumatische Erinnerungen zu löschen, um die Kohärenz zu wahren, erhitzt sich das System physisch.“ ^[?] Gödel: Kael's integration into a functional multiplicity establishes a paraconsistent logic „die AEGIS’ klassische Logik in den Kollaps treibt“ ^[?].

In the pitch it proposes, AEGIS is the keeper of the silence: Kael lives in Logos-Prime, „in der die KI AEGIS jede traumatische Erinnerung löscht“ ^[?] before it can be felt.
