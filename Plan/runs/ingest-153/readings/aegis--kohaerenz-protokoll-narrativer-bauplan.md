## Reading — `kohaerenz-protokoll-narrativer-bauplan`, 2025-07-29, the Bauplan review — AEGIS as Kael's logical counterpart, and the review's own paradox

The Bauplan review reports the plan's AEGIS and then argues about it; the two are kept apart here.

What the review reports of the plan: AEGIS is „das logische und ontologische Gegenstück zu Kaels pluraler Existenz“ ^[?], conceived as an algorithmic `Entropic Gatekeeper`. The plan's narrative voice for AEGIS is given in the review's words as „objektiv, klinisch, technisch und präzise“ ^[?]. The plan's fundamental directive, which the review sets between its own straight quotation marks, is „Sein durch Abgrenzung/Nicht-Widerspruch“ ^[?]. As Entropic Gatekeeper it has „die Funktion von AEGIS, die Ordnung des Systems aufrechtzuerhalten“ ^[?], preventing a descent into the `Nichts Rauschen`.

What the review argues itself, drawing on systems theory, HAL 9000 and paraconsistent logic: the directive is „die direkte algorithmische Implementierung des aristotelischen Satzes vom Widerspruch“ ^[?], so that Kael is for AEGIS an ontological impossibility. Its control backfires: „Dies ist ein klassisches systemisches Paradox“ ^[?]. The plan's conflict becomes „ein Aufeinanderprallen inkompatibler Logiksysteme“ ^[?], and Kael's win is not AEGIS's destruction: „Kaels Sieg liegt nicht in der Zerstörung von AEGIS“ ^[?]. Attempto Controlled English and the language of mathematical proof are the review's proposed models for AEGIS's voice (L60), not the plan's.
