## Reading — `kohaerenz-protokoll-narrativer-bauplan`, 2025-07-29, the Bauplan review — the four worlds as mirrors of Kael's states

The review reports four `Kernwelten` as sketched in the plan and argues what they are: more than settings, they act as „aktive narrative Agenten“ ^[?] and as „direkte Spiegelungen von Kaels psychologischen Zuständen“ ^[?]. The plan's table (L156–L160) assigns primary worlds to the three parts: part one KW1 and KW2, part two KW2 and KW3, part three KW4 and Das Fundament. The sensory and symbolic frames for each world (Brutalismus, swamp, bunker, garden) are the review's own proposals (L94–L141).
