## Reading — `kael-charakterarchitektur-und-konfliktdynamik`, 2025-04-28, the character architecture — TSDP as the origin model beside IFS and Jung

The character architecture names the theory of structural dissociation as one of three psychological models, and gives it the role of origin: it offers an explanation of fragmentation „als Folge von Trauma, das die normale Integration von Ich-Zuständen verhindert“ ^[?]. Integration is understood there as „Integration wird hier als verbesserte Kooperation und Harmonie zwischen den Teilen verstanden“ ^[?].

The profiles then carry the vocabulary into the personas as a secondary label: the Logiker as „Sekundär TSDP-ANP (Apparently Normal Part)“ ^[?], the Kind as „Sekundär TSDP-EP (Emotional Part)“ ^[?]. For the host the architecture says what TSDP would do: „TSDP würde ihn als das Gesamtsystem der Persönlichkeit betrachten“ ^[?]. IFS is named the primary model for function, so TSDP stands beside it, not above it.
