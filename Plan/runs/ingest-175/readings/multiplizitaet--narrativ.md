## Reading — `narrativ`, 2025-07-30, the architect's compendium — functional multiplicity as healing goal, and as a prose target

The compendium (L11–L113) sets the prose aim: Kael's functional multiplicity is to be made felt directly in the prose (L19). Its late phase is where this is written: sentences hold contradictory clauses in tension, and „Die Prosa vereint die unterschiedlichen stilistischen Signaturen der Anteile“ ^[?] into one voice (L36).

The dramaturg's blueprint (L115–L238) sets the healing goal: „Kaels Heilungsprozess zielt nicht auf“ ^[?] fusion, the extinction of the parts, but on functional multiplicity, which it defines as „Dies ist ein Zustand kooperativer und ko-bewusster Zusammenarbeit zwischen allen Anteilen“ ^[?] (L147).
