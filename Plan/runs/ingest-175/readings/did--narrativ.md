## Reading — `narrativ`, 2025-07-30, the architect's compendium — DID on the TSDP model shapes the voice, and portraying it is an ethical duty

The compendium (L11–L113) directs prose. It opens with Kael's condition: „Seine Dissoziative Identitätsstörung (DID), basierend auf dem TSDP-Modell“ ^[?] is not only a plot element but shapes the whole narrating voice (L19). Its ethics section demands care: „Die Darstellung der Dissoziativen Identitätsstörung (DID) und des Traumas erfordert besondere Sorgfalt“ ^[?], and calls this an ethical necessity (L70); cliché is to be avoided and switches between parts kept mostly subtle (L72).

The dramaturg's blueprint (L115–L238) repeats the demand among its themes: „Authentizität & Sensibilität“ ^[?], an ethical necessity for portraying DID, „psychologisch fundiert und nicht-sensationslüstern ist“ ^[?] (L227).
