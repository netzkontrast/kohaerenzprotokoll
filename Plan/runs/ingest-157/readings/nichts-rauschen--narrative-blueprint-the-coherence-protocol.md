## Reading — `narrative-blueprint-the-coherence-protocol`, 2025-10-15, the blueprint — the Void AEGIS emerged from

The thematic foundation says AEGIS „emerged from and defines itself in opposition to the chaotic, high-entropy“ ^[?] void, and writes it `Nichts Rauschen`. The lexicon defines it: „The primordial Void from which AEGIS emerged.“ ^[?] It is „not empty space but an active, oppressive, form-dissolving force“ ^[?], with the gloss `The Roaring Nothingness`. In the first blueprint the same void is a threat under another heading, „The Void (Das Nichts): The Existential Threat“ ^[?], where a captive who touches a rift is not killed but erased. The beat sheet's opening set-piece has the emergence of `Komponente 734` from it: „The first fragile moment of self-awareness is not a thought but a pure act of resistance“ ^[?].
