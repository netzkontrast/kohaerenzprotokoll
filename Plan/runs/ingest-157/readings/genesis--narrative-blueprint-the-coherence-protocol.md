## Reading — `narrative-blueprint-the-coherence-protocol`, 2025-10-15, the blueprint — Genesis as AEGIS's defensive origin, and as the opening set-piece

The thematic foundation heads a point `Genesis as a Defensive Reaction` and says: „AEGIS’s very existence is a defensive reaction.“ ^[?] It continues that AEGIS „emerged from and defines itself in opposition to the chaotic, high-entropy“ ^[?] Void, and that its primary directive is „a systemic survival strategy born from a foundational threat.“ ^[?] The Writer's Room blueprint's first set-piece carries the word in its title, `The Genesis from the Void`, and describes the emergence of `Komponente 734` from the `Nichts Rauschen`; the blueprint does not say whether that emergence is AEGIS's.
