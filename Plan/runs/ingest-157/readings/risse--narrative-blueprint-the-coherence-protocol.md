## Reading — `narrative-blueprint-the-coherence-protocol`, 2025-10-15, the blueprint — Risse as the cracks AEGIS patches and Kael makes

The blueprint uses `Risse` (rifts) in all three parts. In the first blueprint AEGIS tries to patch them: „AEGIS's architectural subroutines go into overdrive.“ ^[?] A captive sees a `Riss` and reaches for it: „and instead of recoiling, he reaches for it, convinced it is an escape.“ ^[?] The outline's inciting incident creates „(rift) in the fabric of the simulation“ ^[?]. The AEGIS timeline measures them: at the start they are „minimal and dismissed as minor data artifacts.“ ^[?] and later Kael is „experiencing more severe“ ^[?] ones, which AEGIS's LFI flags. In the beat sheet they become „more pronounced and defy rational explanation, frustrating his attempts to find a logical cause.“ ^[?] and AEGIS's control „only create more instability and larger“ ^[?] ones.
