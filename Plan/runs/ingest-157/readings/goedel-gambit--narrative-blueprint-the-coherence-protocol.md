## Reading — `narrative-blueprint-the-coherence-protocol`, 2025-10-15, the blueprint — one name for three moments

The blueprint uses `Gödel Gambit` for three moments in three of its parts. The first blueprint's three-act outline puts it at the inciting incident: „A subtle intrusion from the Juna/V-Link (the Moonshine-Link) acts as a catalyst.“ ^[?] The lexicon defines it as „The story's climactic weapon.“ ^[?] and an „ontological proof, not an attack.“ ^[?] The beat sheet then lists `The Gödel Gambit, Act 1` as a beat of its Part 1: „Kael consciously uses Nyx's rage to overload a Guardian's logic filter, an unpredictable and dangerous move.“ ^[?] Its set-pieces carry the climax under the same name: „Kael does not launch an attack.“ ^[?]

The AEGIS timeline writes it once as `Gödel-Gambit` and once as `Gödel-Satz`: AEGIS is „Confronted by a living Gödel-Satz“ ^[?]. So the blueprint uses one name for an early catalyst, an Act 1 beat and the climax; it does not say whether they are one device.
