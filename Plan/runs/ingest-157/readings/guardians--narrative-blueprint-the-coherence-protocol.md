## Reading — `narrative-blueprint-the-coherence-protocol`, 2025-10-15, the blueprint — Guardians as AEGIS's agents, and their fate

The first blueprint's scenes give the Guardians a menace and then a malfunction. One scene: „A Guardian, its form shifting and indistinct, corners Kael in the surreal, memory-soaked landscape of KW2“ ^[?]. The escalation plans `Unberechenbare Wächter (Guardians)`: „AEGIS's agents, the Guardians, begin to exhibit behaviors that are logically valid but practically useless.“ ^[?] The beat sheet's Part 2 has `The Schism of the Guardians`: „some Guardians begin to conflict with each other or question their directives.“ ^[?] Part 3 gives their fate: „The remaining Guardians are now aimless, executing bizarre, useless loops“ ^[?], and the set-piece list adds that they are „now aimless, engaged in acts of“ ^[?] `inefficient beauty`. Here they are agents of AEGIS; the blueprint does not name them as bearers of a world.
