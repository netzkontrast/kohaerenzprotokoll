## Reading — `narrative-blueprint-the-coherence-protocol`, 2025-10-15, the blueprint — Mnemosyne as the archipelago of KW2 and as a Guardian

The blueprint writes `Mnemosyne-Archipel` as the second Core World in three of its parts. The first blueprint's scene: „corners Kael in the surreal, memory-soaked landscape of KW2“ ^[?]. The beat sheet: „He is drawn into KW2 (Mnemosyne-Archipel)“ ^[?], and the set-piece list says it „is a chaotic sea of raw emotion where fragmented memories manifest as shimmering, unstable islands.“ ^[?] The beat sheet also makes it a figure: in beat 14 memories are „actively manipulated by the Guardian Mnemosyne.“ ^[?] and in beat 20 Kael fights „the Guardian of Memory, a battle fought within a psychic landscape.“ ^[?] The blueprint does not say whether the place and the Guardian are one name for one thing.
