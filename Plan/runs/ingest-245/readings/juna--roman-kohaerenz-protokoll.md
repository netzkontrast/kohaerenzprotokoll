## Reading — `roman-kohaerenz-protokoll`, 2025-04-17, the early prose draft — Juna as a formal voice that flickers

The narrator (German prose) shows Juna as an image on the wall of Kael's unit: „Auf der Wandfläche ihm gegenüber materialisierte sich ein Bild“ ^[?]. Her speech opens the exchange — „Statusbericht Kael“ ^[?] — and goes on, in speech, „Synchronisation mit LogOS erfolgt“ ^[?]. Then the narrator reports Kael's perception breaking: „Ihre Stimme klang plötzlich anders“ ^[?]. In the later check-in Kael wonders whether he projects his break onto her; the narrator writes there of the encounter that she seemed faulty, and the question stays open in the text.
