## Reading — `roman-kohaerenz-protokoll`, 2025-04-17, the early prose draft — `(Cerberus?)` as the narrator's guess at a voice in Kael

Twice the narrator (German prose) brackets a guess beside a doubt of Kael's, `(Praetor ? Cerberus?)`: once at the feeling of being watched, once at his scanning for flaws — „Zurück am Knotenpunkt war etwas anders.“ ^[?] The name is guessed as one of Kael's voices, not the Guardian. The surveillance itself is LogOS's, in the narrator's words.
