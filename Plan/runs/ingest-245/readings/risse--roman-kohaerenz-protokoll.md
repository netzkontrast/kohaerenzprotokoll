## Reading — `roman-kohaerenz-protokoll`, 2025-04-17, the early prose draft — Risse as entropy, as perception, as search

The narrator (German prose) first, in the reboot passage: the `Risse` in quotation marks are „Manifestationen digitaler Entropie“ ^[?]. In Kapitel 1 the word returns in Kael's perception: after the anomaly, he scans the data streams „auf weitere Risse, auf Unregelmäßigkeiten“ ^[?]. Alone in his unit, the silence is charged „mit den Rissen in der Wahrnehmung“ ^[?]. Both are the narrator reporting Kael's experience; the draft does not connect them to the entropy sentence beyond sharing the word.
