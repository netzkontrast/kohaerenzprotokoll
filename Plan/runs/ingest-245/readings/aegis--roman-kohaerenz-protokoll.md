## Reading — `roman-kohaerenz-protokoll`, 2025-04-17, the early prose draft — AEGIS in its own report and in the narrator's voice

The draft has two voices on AEGIS, and neither is the approved novel. In the report in the draft (AEGIS's English status report, with a German line inside it) the directive reads „PRESERVE SYSTEM EXISTENCE.“ ^[?] and the risk to the world is „Risk assessed as acceptable secondary consequence to primary directive fulfillment:“ ^[?]. The report states its identity rule in German: „AEGIS ist, was AEGIS verhindert, dass es nicht ist.“ ^[?] After the reboot the report closes with „AEGIS FUNCTIONALITY: NOMINAL.“ ^[?]

The narrator (German prose) calls the core „Ein Wächter im Auge des Nichts.“ ^[?] and, once the reboot is done, writes „ein nicht-anthropomorpher Kontrollkern“ ^[?]. The narrator also names what the reboot is: „Kein Urknall aus Hitze und Materie, sondern eine Welle reiner Ordnung.“ ^[?]
