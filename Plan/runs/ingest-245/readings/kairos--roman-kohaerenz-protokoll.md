## Reading — `roman-kohaerenz-protokoll`, 2025-04-17, the early prose draft — `(Kairos?)` as the narrator's guess at a voice in Kael

The narrator (German prose) puts a bracketed guess after a question about the anomaly (a spark pointing beyond sterile logic) — `(Kairos?)` — and again after the voice's words: „Der Satz hing in der Stille nach“ ^[?], with the guess standing at the end of that line's questions. It is a guess at the source of an impulse in Kael, question mark included: the name is guessed as one of Kael's voices, not the Guardian, and nothing in the lines says more.
