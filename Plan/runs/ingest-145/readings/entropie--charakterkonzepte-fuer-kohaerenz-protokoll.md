## Reading — `charakterkonzepte-fuer-kohaerenz-protokoll`, 2025-04-18, the character concepts — entropy as AEGIS's target and Juna's supposed source

The character concepts describe AEGIS as organised around entropy: its core programming is „die aktive Minimierung und Kontrolle von Entropie“ ^[?], understood as disorder, chaos, randomness and loss of information. Its binary worldview sets order against chaos in entropy terms (L127), and Juna is held to be „eine Quelle unkontrollierbarer Entropie und Instabilität“ ^[?] from AEGIS's side. As a theme, Kael embodies „den Konflikt zwischen Ordnung und Entropie“ ^[?].
