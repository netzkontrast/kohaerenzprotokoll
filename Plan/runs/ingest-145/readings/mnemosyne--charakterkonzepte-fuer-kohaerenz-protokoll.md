## Reading — `charakterkonzepte-fuer-kohaerenz-protokoll`, 2025-04-18, the character concepts — Mnemosyne as the Guardian of the Resonanz-Landschaft, memories as data points

The character concepts name `Mnemosyne` second among the five Guardians, „Mnemosyne (Zugeordnet: Resonanz-Landschaft)“ ^[?]. Its function is the administration and monitoring of memories, emotional resonances and historical data streams (L169). Its blind spot: „Sie behandelt Erinnerungen rein als Datenpunkte“ ^[?], and „Sie versteht nicht den Unterschied zwischen bloßer Speicherung und sinnhafter Verarbeitung von Erinnerung“ ^[?]; the paper writes that attempts to integrate traumatic memories could be misread by it as data corruption or dangerous emotional entropy (L170).
