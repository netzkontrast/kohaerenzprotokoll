## Reading — `charakterkonzepte-fuer-kohaerenz-protokoll`, 2025-04-18, the character concepts — Cerberus as the Guardian of the Grenzfeste, paranoia as blind spot

The character concepts name `Cerberus` third among the five Guardians, „Cerberus (Zugeordnet: Grenzfeste)“ ^[?]. Function: „Implementierung und Überwachung von Sicherheitsprotokollen, Grenzkontrolle“ ^[?], associated, the paper writes, with Kael's defence mechanisms and fears and „vielleicht persecutorischen Anteilen“ ^[?] (L179). Blind spot: a programming aimed at maximum safety, „was zu Paranoia und übermäßiger Aggressivität führt“ ^[?]; it reads vulnerability or strong emotion as a security breach (L180). A proposed minor figure, a Grenzwärter, is said to represent the function of Cerberus at a more specific level (L578).
