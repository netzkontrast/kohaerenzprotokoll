## 2026-10-07 — `romanarchitektur-kohaerenz-protokoll-finalisierung`, 2025-07-29, the final framework

**The final framework proposes a post-integration table of eight alters, four named and four unnamed, and four transformed Kernwelten.**

Its table has the header „Alter Name | TSDP-Klassifikation (Initial)“ ^[?], and names Kael, Nyx, Kiko and Selene; the other four rows are placeholders `[Alter 5]` to `[Alter 8]`, which stay unfilled here. Selene is „Das integrierte Selbst; bewusster Kern des Systems, erleichtert die interne Kommunikation und trifft finale Entscheidungen“ ^[?].

For the Kernwelten it writes „Kernwelt 1 (Logik)“ ^[?] through „Kernwelt 4 (Potential)“ ^[?], each transformed into an inner landscape; Kernwelt 4 becomes „kreativen Kern“ ^[?]. It writes no correspondence between a Kernwelt and an alter.

Stands as a document that gives eight alters in a table and four Kernwelten; it does not decide the record.
