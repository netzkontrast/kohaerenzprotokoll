## Reading — `romanarchitektur-kohaerenz-protokoll-finalisierung`, 2025-07-29, the final framework — the Zero-Trust-Architektur named, and what it does not stop

The final framework names AEGIS's `Zero-Trust-Architektur` once, beside its `Boundary Protocols`, in the passage on the Juna/V link. It defines the link as an `architektonische Hintertür` and not a hack, and places the weakness outside what AEGIS could change: „Die Schwachstelle liegt nicht in der Software, den Protokollen oder der Logik, die AEGIS modifizieren oder patchen könnte.“ ^[?] The framework gives no explanation of the Zero-Trust architecture itself, and none of the other three sub-functions of this page (`Cognitive Firewall`, `Integrity Guardian`, `SIS`) appears in the passage; this reading records only the line it stands on.
