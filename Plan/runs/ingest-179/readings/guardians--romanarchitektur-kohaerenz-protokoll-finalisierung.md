## Reading — `romanarchitektur-kohaerenz-protokoll-finalisierung`, 2025-07-29, the final framework — the Guardians as the visible symptom of AEGIS's paraconsistent logic

The final framework proposes the Guardians as the place where AEGIS's new logic shows its costs: „Die Guardians, die nun in der Lage sind, widersprüchliche Befehle gleichzeitig zu verarbeiten“ ^[?] could be caught in loops that are logically valid and practically useless, and the framework adds that the efficiency that was AEGIS's trademark is lost. In its narrative-technique part it returns to them: „Dies wird am besten durch das Verhalten der Guardians gezeigt.“ ^[?] A Guardian pursuing Kael stops and repeats a pointless action because its internal `Kohärenzmetrik` has dropped below a threshold.
