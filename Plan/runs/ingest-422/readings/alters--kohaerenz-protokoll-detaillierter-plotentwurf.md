## Reading — `kohaerenz-protokoll-detaillierter-plotentwurf`, 2025-04-25, the detailed plot draft — the Alters in Kap 1 and Kap 3

The detailed plot draft maps the Alters onto the parts of a therapy model: „Die verschiedenen Alters/Persönlichkeitszustände Kaels entsprechen den“ ^[?] (L27). Kap 1 plans „Gedächtnislücken und ein Gefühl der Depersonalisation/Derealisation“ ^[?] (L87) beside sudden switches between states, and Kap 3 plans that „Ein dominanter Alter Kaels“ ^[?] (L89) takes control as a rational, controlling manager part. It gives no count of alters.
