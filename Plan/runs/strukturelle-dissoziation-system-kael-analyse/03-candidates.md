# Candidates — strukturelle-dissoziation-system-kael-analyse

Written while reading, before any count. One `- term` per line.

## What this document is, noted before the list

**This is the first `theorie-psychologie` document and the first `T2-theory`
document** — every document read so far was `T3-work`. It shows up on the page
immediately: 27 numbered external references, all real psychology and trauma
sources (`did-research.org`, Van der Hart/Nijenhuis/Steele's TSDP, Psychology
Today, ResearchGate, a Reddit thread on ANP/EP), and none of them describe this
project — they describe the discipline the document imports wholesale. That is
different from the worldbuilding document's 72 craft citations, which named
films and novels as inspiration; here the citations are the theory itself,
applied directly and by name to eleven named characters.

**Four parts.** Teil 1 maps „Spannungspunkte" (tension points) between the
eleven Anteile, between Anteile and the four Kernwelten, between Anteile and
AEGIS, between Anteile and Juna/V, and around Integration/Selene. Teil 2 is a
straight synthesis of TSDP theory and adjacent trauma research — no project
content at all, purely the discipline being explained. Teil 3 is eleven
character profiles, each following one fixed template
(`capture.py`'s repeated-labels probe found the eight profile fields at 11×
each). Teil 4 is narrative recommendations for plot, characterisation and world
design.

**The tier difference is exactly what the task asked to watch for, and it is
visible, not subtle.** Teil 2 (177 lines) is the discipline importing itself
unchanged — `ANP`, `EP`, `Aktionssysteme`, `Phobien`, the three-tier SD spectrum,
`Ko-Bewusstheit`, `Switching`, `Depersonalisation`, `Derealisation`,
`Homöostase`, Internal Family Systems — every one of these is a term of trauma
psychology, cited to a real source, meant the way the discipline means it. Teil
1 and 3 are where the discipline is *applied*: the same vocabulary now names
this project's own eleven Anteile, four Kernwelten, AEGIS and Juna/V. So one
document holds both registers, and the boundary between them is a heading
(Teil 2 vs Teil 1/3) rather than a sentence-level marker — the same shape the
worldbuilding document had between its craft-theory half and its gazetteer half,
but here the imported half is much larger relative to the whole and the
hand-off is total: nothing in Teil 2 mentions Kael by name.

**Hedging here is not question marks.** The profile counted only 7 question
marks — low, like a confident document — but the prose is saturated with
`könnte`, `wahrscheinlich`, `möglicherweise`, `dürfte` in almost every sentence
of Teil 1 and Teil 3 (`Kael *mag* sich... sehnen`, `Nyx *könnte* fürchten...`).
This is speculative writing about the characters' likely psychology, not
assertion, and it is invisible to the question-mark count that caught document
5's hedging. Whether this document is a *reading* of the characters or a
*proposal* about them is the same question document 5 raised, answered by a
different grammatical device.

**Each Anteil carries a former name**, given once in the section heading —
`Kael (ehem. Michael)`, `Selene (ehem. Die Wächterin)`, `Nyx (ehem. Shadow)`,
`Kiko (ehem. Der Kleine)`, `Lia (ehem. Isabella — jüngere Form)`, `Isabelle
(ehem. Isabella — ältere Form / Sexual Alter)`, `Moros (ehem. The Lost One)`,
`Alex (ehem. Alexander)`, `Rhys (ehem. Stefan)`, `Lex (ehem. Data)`, `Argus
(ehem. Beobachter/Kritiker)`. `Kael (ehem. Michael)` ^[L246] is directly the
naming instability `Plan/learnings/extract-terms.md` special case 8 already
flagged from document 1 — this document confirms Michael as Kael's former name
rather than Kael and Michael being two different things.

**Each Anteil also carries a short English epithet, used three times each**
across §1.3/1.4/1.5 as a section label — `Kael (ANP Host)`, `Lex (ANP Analyst)`,
`Nyx (EP Fighter)`, `Kiko (EP Child)`, `Lia (EP Child Ambivalent)`, `Isabelle
(EP Sexualized)`, `Moros (EP Collapse)`, `Alex (ANP Protector)`, `Rhys (ANP
Carer)`. These match `profile.py`'s repeated-labels probe (`Argus (Observer)
x3`, `Selene (Integration Potential) x3`) and are a third surface for each
Anteil, in English, beside the German name and the former name — the same
`Entropic Gatekeeper`-travels-without-`AEGIS` shape from document 1, except here
it is systematic across all eleven rather than one role.

**Template field names are not counted as candidate terms.** `Beziehung zu
AEGIS`, `Beziehung zu Juna/V`, `Beziehungen zu anderen Anteilen`, `Beziehung zum
Trauma & Kernpsychodynamik`, `Potenzielle Entwicklung`, `Stance on Integration &
Selene`, and the `Resonanz` / `Konflikt/Trigger` sub-headings in §1.2 are this
document's own profile scaffold, already measured by `profile.py`'s
repeated-labels probe, and listing them again here would be the failure the
model run was scored against — headings instead of the things they hold. `TSDP
Klassifikation` is the exception kept below, because `TSDP` itself is the
imported theory and worth a row on its own.

## Candidates found only in questions or hedged past assertion

None of the seven question marks in this document sit on a candidate — every
`?` is inside a plot-recommendation example in Teil 4 ("Bildest du dir das nur
ein?", "Du überreagierst") rather than a question the document asks about its
own vocabulary. So `status: asked` does not apply to anything here; the
hedging in this document is grammatical (`könnte`, `wahrscheinlich`) rather than
interrogative, which is the finding recorded above rather than a candidate.

## Observations while reading, not candidates

- **Teil 2 is a clean discipline import with zero project content** — not one
  of the eleven Anteile, not AEGIS, not a Kernwelt appears in its 45 lines
  (§2.1–2.2 excluding the one bridging sentence that names System Kael in
  passing at L196). That is the strongest form of the tier-2 signal the task
  asked to watch for: an entire section is textbook TSDP, verbatim, before any
  application begins.
- **`Negentropie` does not appear in this document at all** — worth naming
  because the task briefing raised it as the standing example of a term that
  means different things in different documents. It is simply absent here,
  which is itself information for whoever reconciles across documents:
  this document adds no reading of it, imported or project.
- **`Kohärenz Protokoll` (the novel title) appears only once**, in the
  introduction (L15) and once more in the conclusion (L568/L522) as the target
  of the whole analysis — a bare title-drop, not a term this document defines.
- **Argus's TSDP classification is itself hedged**: „Entstehender ANP/EP-Mix
  oder Metakognitiver Anteil" ^[L501] — the document proposes two candidate
  classifications for the same Anteil and does not choose between them. That is
  a live ambiguity inside the document's own theory-application, not export
  damage and not a question mark.
- **Selene's classification is similarly double**: „Modifizierter ANP mit
  EP-Komponenten oder Repräsentation des Integrationspotenzials" ^[L274] —
  same shape as Argus, a second Anteil the document cannot cleanly place on its
  own ANP/EP axis.
- **The document's own root theory term, TSDP, is never defined inline** — it
  is expanded once as "Theorie der Strukturellen Dissoziation" in the
  introduction and never spelled out again, the same gap `Entropie` had in
  document 1: assumed known, used throughout, defined nowhere in this file
  itself (the definition lives in the cited external sources).
- **`Kael` the Anteil and `Kael` the system name are the same string used two
  ways** — "System Kael" (the whole eleven-part system) versus "Kael" (the
  single primary-host Anteil within it). A string match would treat every
  occurrence of `Kael` as one term; the document itself distinguishes system
  from part throughout, most clearly at L15 and again at L246–268.
- **`B` as a Kernwelt name is one character** and will not survive a
  word-boundary regex the way `Co₁`, `McL` and `Ly` might — the same one-symbol
  problem `Plan/learnings/extract-terms.md` recorded for `⊕`, `θ`, `α`, `Σ`,
  `Δ`, `Π` in document 2, on a much shorter symbol.
- **Quotation marks here mark AEGIS's own gaslighting lines, not invention or
  citation** — "Bildest du dir das nur ein?", "Du überreagierst" ^[L106] are
  quoted as things AEGIS might say, a third convention beside the invention-mark
  of document 1 and the citation-mark of document 2.
