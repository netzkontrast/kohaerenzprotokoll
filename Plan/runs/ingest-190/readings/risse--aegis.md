## Reading — `aegis`, 2025-07-29, the AEGIS concept file — the Risse as symptoms of misdirected control, and as a by-product of its entropy

The AEGIS concept file reads the Risse in the Kernwelten as consequences of AEGIS's own method. Its first analysis says the Risse are „die direkten visuellen und logischen Symptome“ ^[?L25] of the misguided control and of growing chaos. It gives three explanations. Gödel's theorems, „Metaphorisch oder wörtlich“ ^[?L35] a limit on AEGIS's logic, explain the inherent Risse (L35, a hedge it keeps). Feedback loops: AEGIS's attempts at stabilisation produce „positive Rückkopplungsschleifen, die die Instabilität“ ^[?L42] amplify (the Risse in brackets). And entropy: each ordering act makes waste entropy, so the Risse are not only a failure but a „unvermeidliches, paradoxes Nebenprodukt seiner eigenen, obsessiven Kontroll- und Ordnungsfunktion“ ^[?L44].

The manifesto passage carries the heading „III. Die Risse im Bauplan“ ^[?L107], where AEGIS's inherent fault is set out; the later passage repeats that the Risse are a „unvermeidliches Nebenprodukt seiner Existenz und Funktion“ ^[?L203].
