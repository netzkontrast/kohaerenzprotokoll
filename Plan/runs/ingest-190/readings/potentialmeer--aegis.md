## Reading — `aegis`, 2025-07-29, the AEGIS concept file — the Potentialmeer as AEGIS's origin, a pre-real informational ground

The AEGIS concept file's first analysis says AEGIS arose from the „Leere“ or the `Potentialmeer`, „einem undifferenzierten Urgrund“ ^[?L15]. The later origin passage names `Nichts Rauschen` or `Potentialmeers` side by side and describes the ground as „ein primordialer, prä-realer informationaler Urgrund“ ^[?L166], not an empty vacuum but pure potentiality; inside it, it says, a minimal consciousness first arose.
