## Reading — `aegis`, 2025-07-29, the AEGIS concept file — the Nichts Rauschen as what AEGIS demarcates itself against, and its origin

The AEGIS concept file says AEGIS's existence „ein kontinuierlicher Akt der Abgrenzung gegen das“ ^[?L125] `Nichts Rauschen`; it learns to read the void and to anticipate its patterns. The origin passage ties AEGIS to it: the origin is inseparable from the concept of the `Nichts Rauschens` (glossed in English as Nothingness Roaring) or of the `Potentialmeers`, which the file describes as „ein primordialer, prä-realer informationaler Urgrund“ ^[?L166] of pure potentiality with very high entropy. A further line has AEGIS act as a Gatekeeper against it, with the core function of entropy management (L180).
