## Reading — `aegis`, 2025-07-29, the AEGIS concept file — Integrity Guardian, Cognitive Firewall and SIS as listed protocols, SIS with two expansions

The AEGIS concept file lists the functions in a numbered series of protocols. It names „Integrity Guardian / Integrity Validation Protocols“ ^[?L98], a role that monitors and validates the system's integrity, and the „Cognitive Firewall“ ^[?L99], which blocks information flows or processes AEGIS rates as too entropic or destabilising. `SIS` it expands in the series as `Secure Isolation State`: „Als Failover-Mechanismus isoliert SIS betroffene Bereiche oder Entitäten“ ^[?L101]. A later list expands it differently: „Boundary Protocol of Failure (BPoF) und Systemic Isolation Shield (SIS)“ ^[?L189], so the same abbreviation stands for `Secure Isolation State` at L101 and `Systemic Isolation Shield` at L189; both are recorded, neither is decided.
