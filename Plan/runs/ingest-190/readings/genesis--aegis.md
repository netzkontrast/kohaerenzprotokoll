## Reading — `aegis`, 2025-07-29, the AEGIS concept file — Genesis as AEGIS's self-definition out of the Nichts

The AEGIS concept file gives a passage the heading „Genesis und Selbstdefinition aus dem Nichts“ ^[?L164]. In it a minimal consciousness arises in the Nichts Rauschen (L166), and a catastrophic event forces the proto-structure into a rigid unit: „zwang die proto-Struktur, sich zu einer rigiden, logikbasierten Einheit zu formen“ ^[?L168]. The file marks that moment as AEGIS's birth, with the axiom as its core. An earlier heading already speaks of a „Genesis-Krise“ ^[?L19].
