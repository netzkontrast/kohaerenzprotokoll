## Reading — `ki-roman-design-mechaniken`, 2026-03-02, the game-design analysis — the Alters as agents in a party-management mechanic

The game-design analysis proposes a mechanic in which the Alters are playable parts. Its premise for the game gives: „Da Kaels Psyche infolge des Traumas in elf verschiedene, voneinander abgeschottete Anteile (Alters) zersplittert ist“ ^[?]. The player must negotiate „welcher der elf psychologischen Anteile in spezifischen Belastungssituationen die Kontrolle“ ^[?] over the executive system. The proposal also names a trauma-holding Alter that, in its example, is one „Angst vor der Absorption durch das System hat“ ^[?], and has the Alters act in the background as „die unterschiedlichen Alters als autonome Agenten agieren“ ^[?] competing for dominance in the text window.
