## Reading — `ki-roman-design-mechaniken`, 2026-03-02, the game-design analysis — Kael as the premise of four game mechanics

The game-design analysis proposes four player mechanics for the Kohärenz Protokoll as an interactive game; what it says of Kael is the premise it states for those mechanics, not the novel as written. Its premise for the game gives: „Die narrative Prämisse verlangt, dass der Protagonist (Kael) unter Tertiärer Struktureller Dissoziation der Persönlichkeit (TSDP) leidet.“ ^[?] In its first mechanic, the proposal has Kael's consciousness taken over when the player's order value passes a threshold: „wird Kaels Bewusstsein von der AEGIS-Matrix absorbiert“ ^[?]. The second mechanic rests on the premise that „Da Kaels Psyche infolge des Traumas in elf verschiedene, voneinander abgeschottete Anteile (Alters) zersplittert ist“ ^[?]. The fourth mechanic asks the player to tell system errors from errors of the self, glossing the first as „AEGIS manipuliert die Matrix, um Kael zu täuschen“ ^[?] and the second as „Kaels Trauma bricht durch die dissoziative Barriere“ ^[?].
