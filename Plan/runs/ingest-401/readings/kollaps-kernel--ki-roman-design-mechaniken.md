## Reading — `ki-roman-design-mechaniken`, 2026-03-02, the game-design analysis — the Kollaps-Kernel as premise, metric and domain

The game-design analysis proposes mechanics for an interactive game. Its premise for the game names the „Kollaps-Kernel (K0)“ ^[?] and gives what it stands for: „für Entropie, emotionalen Schmerz, aber auch organische Wahrheit und die Entität Juna steht“ ^[?]. In its first mechanic, the opposite metric to coherence is described as „emotional, entropisch, traumabasierend, aufbrechend“ ^[?]. Its third mechanic has the player recover corrupted data from „dem Kollaps-Bereich“ ^[?], which the proposal calls the Void.
