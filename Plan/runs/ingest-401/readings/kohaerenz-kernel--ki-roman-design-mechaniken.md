## Reading — `ki-roman-design-mechaniken`, 2026-03-02, the game-design analysis — the Kohärenz-Kernel as premise and as a metric

The game-design analysis proposes mechanics for an interactive game. Its premise for the game names the „Kohärenz-Kernel (K1)“ ^[?] and gives what it stands for: „für unerbittliche Ordnung, Information und die absolute Kontrollstruktur der Entität AEGIS steht“ ^[?]. In its first mechanic, every dialogue choice accumulates a metric for coherence, described as „logisch, kalt, strukturerhaltend“ ^[?]; the proposal makes this the game's primary resource and condition system.
