## Reading — `romananalyse-kohaerenz-plot-kritik`, 2026-02-23, the publisher's report — the Host of ten Alters, then a passive protagonist the report would make active

The publisher's report summarises the manuscript's Kael as the Host of „eines stark fragmentierten psychischen Systems“ ^[?]. It reports that the Monster Group is „als perfekte Metapher für Kaels Psyche genutzt“ ^[?], AEGIS failing to factor him because his identity is „irreduzibel und von einer derart unvorstellbaren Komplexität“ ^[?]. In its table of Alters the row for Kael (Host) carries the IFS role `Manager (ANP)`: he „Leidet unter massiver Amnesie bezüglich des Systems und des Traumas.“ ^[?] and is „Der primäre POV-Charakter.“ ^[?]. Its plot summary of Teil 1 says „Kael scheitert hier fundamental“ ^[?] at LogOS's validation test, which the Gödel theorems make unsolvable, and of Teil 2 that „Kael verweigert den Befehl.“ ^[?] to transfer to `Beta-Rho-5`; he turns from `Architekten` to `Gärtner` of emergence (L99).

The report's own voice begins at its criticism. It criticises the opening chapters: „ist Kael extrem reaktiv und passiv“ ^[?], and it recommends that the novel start with him, „muss zwingend, physisch und unmittelbar bei Kael beginnen“ ^[?], and that „Kael muss wesentlich früher im Plot beginnen, aktiv gegen die Systemregeln zu verstoßen“ ^[?]. These are the report's recommendations, not the novel's facts.
