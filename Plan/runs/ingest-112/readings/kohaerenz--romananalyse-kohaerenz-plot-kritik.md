## Reading — `romananalyse-kohaerenz-plot-kritik`, 2026-02-23, the publisher's report — healing called Kohärenz, and a coherence that holds contradictions

The report summarises healing in the manuscript as `Kohärenz`: it comes „nicht durch die feindselige Auslöschung der Alters“ ^[?], which is AEGIS's approach, but through recognising a functional multiplicity (L71). On the narrative arc it reports that the `Mosaik-Herz` becomes the metaphor for „eine neue, residierende Kohärenz, die Widersprüche aushält, statt sie zu glätten“ ^[?], and that Kael must accept „Akzeptanz der Fragmentierung als Ausgangspunkt“ ^[?] rather than suppress entropy.

Both lines are the report's summary of the manuscript and its concept documents (its reference 9), not a definition of its own; its other uses of the word are the title of the work.
