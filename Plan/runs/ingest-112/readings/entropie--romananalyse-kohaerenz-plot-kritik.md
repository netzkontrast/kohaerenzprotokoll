## Reading — `romananalyse-kohaerenz-plot-kritik`, 2026-02-23, the publisher's report — the duality of thermodynamic and Shannon entropy, joined by Landauer

The report's account of the manuscript's concept: „Der Text spielt virtuos mit der Dualität des Entropiebegriffs“ ^[?]. On one side stands the thermodynamic entropy of Clausius and Boltzmann, which it describes as the „Streben nach dem thermodynamischen Gleichgewicht“ ^[?]; on the other the information-theoretic Shannon entropy, which AEGIS tries to minimise: „Es versucht, die Shannon-Entropie permanent zu minimieren“ ^[?]. The two are joined, as the report presents it, by the Landauer principle: the erasure of information raises thermodynamic entropy as heat, and the report calls AEGIS's „ständiger Kampf gegen die informationelle Unordnung“ ^[?] the source of a digital heat.

The report also says that AEGIS reads Juna's healing as a threat: „AEGIS interpretiert diesen nicht-linearen Heilungsprozess fälschlicherweise als systembedrohende Entropie“ ^[?]. For the arc it reports, integration comes „nicht durch die Unterdrückung von“ ^[?] `Entropie` but by accepting fragmentation as the starting point (L91). All of this is the report's account; it gives no definition of its own beyond the physics it names.
