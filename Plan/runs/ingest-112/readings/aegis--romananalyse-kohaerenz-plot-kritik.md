## Reading — `romananalyse-kohaerenz-plot-kritik`, 2026-02-23, the publisher's report — AEGIS as the reductionist controller, whose own ordering produces the Risse

The publisher's report summarises the manuscript and its concept documents; it does not write the novel's text. It gives the expansion „Autonomous Entropic Gatekeeper for Integrity Systems“ ^[?] and calls AEGIS the Kontrollinstanz that embodies „den ultimativen, reduktionistischen Versuch“ ^[?] to stabilise a traumatised system. On its summary of the premise AEGIS reads Juna's healing wrongly: „AEGIS interpretiert diesen nicht-linearen Heilungsprozess fälschlicherweise als systembedrohende Entropie“ ^[?] and fights it.

In its account of the entropy duality the report says AEGIS works on Shannon entropy: „Es versucht, die Shannon-Entropie permanent zu minimieren“ ^[?], and deletes the unknown as error. Through the Landauer principle, as the report presents it, the effort becomes self-undermining: „AEGIS' ständiger Kampf gegen die informationelle Unordnung“ ^[?] produces a digital heat that decomposes the system. It also reports that the Risse are the by-product of AEGIS's own function, „sind somit kein externes Übel, sondern das unvermeidbare Nebenprodukt“ ^[?].

The report further summarises AEGIS as autopoietic and as imploding at the Gödel loop: „AEGIS wird als autopoietisches“ ^[?] system, with a line on its lack of internal complexity, „AEGIS fehlt die interne Komplexität“ ^[?]. Its literary comparison (Asimov's `Multivac`) is the report's own: „ein traumatisierter Multivac“ ^[?].
