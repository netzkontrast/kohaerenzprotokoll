## Reading — `welten`, 2025-04-20, the world-concept reply — the Überwelt as AEGIS's operative domain

In its section III the world-concept reply describes the `Überwelt` as „AEGIS' operative Domäne“ ^[?], and as „rein informationsbasierte, abstrakte Realitätsebene“ ^[?] structured and regulated by AEGIS's protocols; the reply calls it the `Maschinerie` behind the simulated Kern-Welten. Its structure: „Besteht aus Datenströmen, logischen Knotenpunkten, Kontrollinstanzen“ ^[?]. Its inhabitants are „Primär die Guardians als funktionale Subsysteme von AEGIS“ ^[?], and potentially other information-based constructs or processes; for these it refers to the `Digitale Überwelt`, a document it names and does not explain. The Guardians operate from it, and the reply asks whether the `Nexus` might be an interface to it (as a possibility, „Möglicherweise ein Begriff für die Schnittstellen oder Übergangszonen“ ^[?]).
