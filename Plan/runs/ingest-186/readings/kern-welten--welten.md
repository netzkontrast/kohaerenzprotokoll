## Reading — `welten`, 2025-04-20, the world-concept reply — four proposed names and four worlds as externalisations of Kael's psyche

The reply opens by proposing names for the Kern-Welten, it says „Basierend auf den Eigenschaften der Kern-Welten“ ^[?], as the documents describe them, above all the `Guardians und Kern-Welten-Konzept`. Only the first is marked as taken from the novel: KW1 is `Konstrukt-Stadt`, „Bestätigt aus Roman“ ^[?]. The other three are its own proposals, each with a reason: `Resonanz-Nebel`, „Passt zur emotionalen Resonanz, der nebligen Atmosphäre“ ^[?]; `Schattenlabyrinth`, „Spiegelt den Fokus auf Grenzen, Abwehr“ ^[?]; and `Möglichkeitsstrom`, „Reflektiert Potential, Veränderung, Fluss, Verzweigung“ ^[?]. Each name is tied to one Guardian (Mnemosyne, Cerberus, Kairos).

In the concept proper it describes them as „Vier primäre simulierte Realitäten, die als Externalisierungen von Kaels Psyche“ ^[?] serve, the main stages of Kael's journey. Their laws are shaped by AEGIS and the Guardians, but, it says, they mainly reflect Kael's inner state. It then lists each world's properties and its Risse/Echos (L86–L114); for KW1 it hedges that the world „Repräsentiert vielleicht Kaels Versuch der Kontrolle“ ^[?].

Its dynamics section says „Kaels innerer Zustand und seine Handlungen beeinflussen die Kern-Welten direkt“ ^[?]. In its conclusion it states that the names and properties „bieten nun konkrete Ankerpunkte für die narrative Ausgestaltung“ ^[?]. Recorded as a proposal; nothing is applied.
