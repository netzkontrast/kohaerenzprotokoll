## Reading — `welten`, 2025-04-20, the world-concept reply — the protagonist whose psyche the Kern-Welten externalise

The reply bases its concept on the central conflict around the protagonist Kael, whose „fragmentierte Identität (DID)“ ^[?] and „seine unsichtbare Verbindung zu Julia“ ^[?] confront the system with an unsolvable paradox, in its words.

The Kern-Welten are described as „Externalisierungen von Kaels Psyche“ ^[?], of his identity structure, emotions, traumas and potentials, and as the main stages of his journey. In its dynamics section it says „Kaels innerer Zustand und seine Handlungen beeinflussen die Kern-Welten direkt“ ^[?], and names KW2 to KW4 in particular. His journey through them is, it says, „sowohl eine äußere Erkundung als auch eine innere Konfrontation und Integration“ ^[?].

On AEGIS's side the reply says it can recognise his DID as a local disturbance and (supposedly) manage it (L46).
