## 2026-10-07 — `welten`, 2025-04-20, the world-concept reply

**The world-concept reply has each of four Guardians watch one Kern-Welt and leaves Sophia without one.**

It writes it as a field of each Guardian's entry: LogOS watches primarily the Konstrukt-Stadt (KW1), Mnemosyne the Resonanz-Nebel (KW2), Cerberus the Schattenlabyrinth (KW3), Kairos the Möglichkeitsstrom (KW4) (`Überwacht primär` ^[welten.md:#4]). Of Sophia it says only that her task is „Zuständig für Wissen, Synthese, Übersicht“ ^[?] and „Ihre Rolle ist möglicherweise übergreifender als die der anderen vier“ ^[?]. The proposed names themselves are justified in part by the Guardian: the Schattenlabyrinth by „die Verbindung zu Cerberus“ ^[?].

Stands on the side of one Guardian per world for four worlds, with Sophia's role open; recorded, not applied, and the record is not decided by it.
