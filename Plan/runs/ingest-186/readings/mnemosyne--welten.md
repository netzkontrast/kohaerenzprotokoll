## Reading — `welten`, 2025-04-20, the world-concept reply — Mnemosyne for memory and emotion, fixed on the past

The world-concept reply gives `Mnemosyne` the domain „Zuständig für Erinnerung, Emotion, Vergangenheit“ ^[?], within the system. Its blind spot is being „Fixiert auf vergangene Daten und“ ^[?] internal emotional patterns; it cannot grasp that the K-J connection is external, a-temporal or ontologically other, and „interpretiert zugehörige Emotionen falsch“ ^[?]. It watches primarily the Resonanz-Nebel (KW2), a name the reply proposes in part because of the connection to Mnemosyne and memory (`Mnemosyne` ^[welten.md:#4] stands four times in the reply).
