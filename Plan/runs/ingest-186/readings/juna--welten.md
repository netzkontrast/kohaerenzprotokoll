## Reading — `welten`, 2025-04-20, the world-concept reply — Julia as Kael's invisible connection and as a possible resident of the Externe Ebene

The reply writes her name `Julia`, not `Juna`. It describes „seine unsichtbare Verbindung zu Julia“ ^[?], called `Partnerin`, as one of the two things that put the system before a paradox. AEGIS, it says, cannot recognise the nature of the connection (L46).

In the section on the connecting level it gives the Externe Ebene as „Potenzieller Ursprung oder Aufenthaltsort von Julia“ ^[?], or of the essence of the K-J connection, and its conclusion pairs it with her: „der transzendenten Verbindung (Externe Ebene/Julia)“ ^[?]. The `Partnerin-Echos` in the Kern-Welten are described as manifestations of Julia's presence or absence or of the K-J connection (L77).
