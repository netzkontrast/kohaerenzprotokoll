## 2026-10-07 — `welten`, 2025-04-20, the world-concept reply

**The world-concept reply names five Guardians, pairs four of them with a Kern-Welt each, and pairs Sophia with none.**

It lists the „Bekannte Guardians und ihre Domänen/Blinden Flecken“ ^[?]: `LogOS`, `Mnemosyne`, `Cerberus`, `Kairos`, `Sophia`. Each of the first four lines ends with `Überwacht primär` ^[welten.md:#4] and one world, the Konstrukt-Stadt (KW1), Resonanz-Nebel (KW2), Schattenlabyrinth (KW3) and Möglichkeitsstrom (KW4). The line on Sophia names no world, and says „Ihre Rolle ist möglicherweise übergreifender als die der anderen vier“ ^[?]. The pairing recurs in the section on the Kern-Welten, where each world's heading carries one Guardian in brackets, for example „KW1: Konstrukt-Stadt“ ^[?] with `LogOS` after it.

Stands on the side of five named Guardians with four pairs, Sophia unpaired and hedged; recorded, not applied, and the record's rows are not changed.
