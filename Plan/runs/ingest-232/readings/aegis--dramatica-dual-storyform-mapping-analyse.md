## Reading — `dramatica-dual-storyform-mapping-analyse`, 2026-04-30, the storyform mapping analysis — AEGIS as MC in two transposition hypotheses

The storyform mapping analysis tests AEGIS in several branches of its hypothesis tree and gives a verdict to each. In the Ontologische Transposition branch it holds that AEGIS „scheitert“ ^[?] on a dogmatic internal illusion in the Mind domain, and for this branch it argues „Dramatica erlaubt KI-Systeme in Fixed Attitude (Mind), sofern das Problem ein Dogma ist“ ^[?]. The report's verdict on that branch is „Zweig überlebt.“ ^[?]. In the first branch it describes the entity as „AEGIS (Antagonist-System) ist ein autopoietisches System, operativ geschlossen“ ^[?], and rejects that branch as an operative solution. In the Prozessuale Transposition branch the report argues „Dies rechtfertigt AEGIS als MC in Physics“ ^[?], from Landauer's principle. The fourth branch, a collective pronoun rotation, argues from AEGIS's architecture: „AEGIS darf aufgrund seiner dezentralen Architektur“ ^[?] never hold the singular pronoun `I`; its counter-evidence is „Die Verschiebung von AEGIS in die OS degradiert den entropischen Wärmetod“ ^[?], and the verdict is „Zweig verworfen.“ ^[?]. The report leaves the choice between its two surviving alternatives to the author: „ästhetisch und tonal durch den Autor zu treffen“ ^[?].
