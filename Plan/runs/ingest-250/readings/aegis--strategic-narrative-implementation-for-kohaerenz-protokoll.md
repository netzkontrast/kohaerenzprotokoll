## Reading — `strategic-narrative-implementation-for-kohaerenz-protokoll`, 2025-11-03, the implementation guide — AEGIS as K₁-structure and tragic antagonist, and its genesis as a trauma of logic

The implementation guide, an unsigned English report of proposals for the creative team, calls AEGIS „the ultimate K₁-structure—an entity pathologically committed to its own internal consistency“ ^[?]. It directs that the antagonist be a tragic one, whose antagonism is „rooted in pathos, not malice“ ^[?].

For its genesis the guide proposes a scene: „The genesis of AEGIS was not a trauma of violence, but a trauma of logic“ ^[?]. In its sequence the pre-AEGIS system „misinterpreted this internal cascade of suffering“ ^[?] and attacked itself. The guide names the protocol that does this „Kohärenz Protokoll“ ^[?L66], recorded here as the guide writes it. The scene blueprint translates a German source the guide names, and its quoted fragments are the guide's quotations of that source, not this document's own words. Of the result it says: „This act of self-mutilation creates the rigid, trauma-avoidant logic of AEGIS“ ^[?].

In Crack 2 the guide proposes that AEGIS's sensors register Kael's insight but „can only classify it as a series of“ ^[?] random anomalies or system noise.
