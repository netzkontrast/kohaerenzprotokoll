## Reading — `kishotenketsu-fuer-meinen-roman-bitte-plane-ein`, 2025-08-15, the Kishōtenketsu plan — alters as epithets that act on Kael

The Kishōtenketsu plan, an assistant's chat reply, proposes that the Ki introduces „die ersten inneren Anteile“ ^[?], and puts `Alters` in inner quotes beside them. Elsewhere the plan names them by epithet, not by a name: in a chapter set in the second world, „spürt Kael den Impuls des Alters“ ^[?] `Die Mutige` and leaves the prescribed path. A later chapter has a skill pushed too far: „Dies löst eine unkontrollierbare, sich wiederholende Schleife aus“ ^[?], called the `Drall` of the `Schatten` alter. The plan makes no canon claim; its chapter numbers count 30 chapters.
