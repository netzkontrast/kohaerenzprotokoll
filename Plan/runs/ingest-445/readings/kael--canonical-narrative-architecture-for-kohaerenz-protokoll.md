## Reading — `canonical-narrative-architecture-for-kohaerenz-protokoll`, 2025-11-03, the canonical-architecture report — Kael declared the male host of System Kael

It makes Kael's arc the plot's engine: „Kael's entire journey is defined as the path from fragmentation to“ ^[?] functional multiplicity, „a process that makes his internal state the primary source of his power“ ^[?]. Its Act III has Kael, „Leveraging his full internal integration, his meta-knowledge of the system, and the aid of rebel Guardians“ ^[?], stage the final confrontation; the report there describes Kael's final state, in a term that carries a name, as the irrefutable truth that confronts AEGIS.

The report calls itself canonical and definitive; that is its own claim, recorded, not applied.
