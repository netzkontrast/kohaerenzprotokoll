## Reading — `analyse-des-romanprojekts-kohaerenz-protokoll`, 2025-11-03, the project analysis — the Kollaps-Kernel as the domain of irreversible computation, matched to the Void

The project analysis (an unsigned essay; its `Kapitel` are its own sections) defines the kernel in Kapitel 1 under the label K₀, `Das Kollaps-Kernel`, as „die Domäne der irreversiblen Berechnung, der Entropie und der Informationslöschung“ ^[?].

In Kapitel 2 it matches the Void of the Genesis-Krise to it: the described emptiness „entspricht exakt der Definition des K₀-Kollaps-Kernels als Domäne der Entropie und Informationslöschung“ ^[?]. It reads the Genesis-Krise as the confrontation of a K₁ structure with the force of this kernel, and the kernel in physics as quantum decoherence.
