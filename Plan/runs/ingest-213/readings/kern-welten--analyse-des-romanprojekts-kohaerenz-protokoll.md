## Reading — `analyse-des-romanprojekts-kohaerenz-protokoll`, 2025-11-03, the project analysis — the Kernwelten as conditioning environments, each aimed at one part

The project analysis (an unsigned essay; its `Kapitel` are its own sections) reads the Kernwelten as instruments of AEGIS: they „fungieren als riesige, umgebungsbasierte“ ^[?L107] Skinner boxes in which operant conditioning shapes the parts, and, under models of coercive control, „werden die Kernwelten als totalitäre Umgebungen gestaltet“ ^[?]. 

Its second table, the conditioning protocol, lists per world the target part. `KW1 (Logos-Prime)` is aimed at Lex, `KW3 (Cerberus-Labyrinth)` at Nyx (L120, L121). For Rhys and Kiko the table gives worlds marked `Annahme` (L122, L123); no KW2 appears in it, and the essay names no Guardian.
