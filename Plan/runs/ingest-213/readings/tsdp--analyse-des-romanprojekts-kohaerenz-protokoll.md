## Reading — `analyse-des-romanprojekts-kohaerenz-protokoll`, 2025-11-03, the project analysis — the TSDP as the rule set of System Kael

The project analysis (an unsigned essay; its `Kapitel` are its own sections) gives the TSDP its own section, headed „Das TSDP-Framework – Die Physik eines traumatisierten Geistes“ ^[?]. It reports the theory from its reference 7 and applies it: „Die Theorie der Strukturellen Dissoziation der Persönlichkeit (TSDP) dient als das grundlegende Regelwerk für das Bewusstsein von System Kael.“ ^[?]. Dissociation is read „nicht als Pathologie verstanden“ ^[?], but as an adaptive survival mechanism.

It distinguishes ANPs, oriented to everyday life and avoiding trauma memories, from EPs, which carry the trauma and belong to the defence action systems; System Kael is said to show a tertiary structural dissociation: „Die TSDP unterscheidet zwischen“ ^[?] the two kinds.
