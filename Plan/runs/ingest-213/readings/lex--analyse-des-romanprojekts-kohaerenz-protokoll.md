## Reading — `analyse-des-romanprojekts-kohaerenz-protokoll`, 2025-11-03, the project analysis — Lex as the logic part, target of the first Kernwelt

The project analysis (an unsigned essay; its `Kapitel` are its own sections) reads Lex through the TSDP and operant conditioning. Its table of the system's parts types him „ANP (Rationalist)“ ^[?], with the motive „Aufrechterhaltung logischer Ordnung; Phobie vor Irrationalität/Chaos.“ ^[?] and the conflicts „Extreme EP-Phobie; Konflikt mit Nyx (Logik vs. Aggression) und Rhys (Logik vs. Empathie).“ ^[?]. In the duality table the essay sets him against two other parts: „Lex (Logik) vs. Nyx (Wut) und Kiko (Angst)“ ^[?].

In its table of Kernwelten the essay lists `KW1 (Logos-Prime)` with Lex as the target part, the TSDP action system „Analyse, Kognitive Kontrolle“ ^[?L120] and the cultivated behaviour „Emotionslose, rein logische Problemlösung“ ^[?].

The essay reads the conditioning as a trap for the self-image: „Lex' Stolz auf seine Logik und Nyx' Identifikation mit seiner Stärke sind Produkte der feindlichen Programmierung.“ ^[?]. It then makes Lex's quarrel with Rhys a miniature of the larger one: „Miniaturversion des größeren Kampfes“ ^[?] between AEGIS's rigid coherence and the correspondence Kael seeks.
