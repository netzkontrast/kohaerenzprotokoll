## Reading — `analyse-des-romanprojekts-kohaerenz-protokoll`, 2025-11-03, the project analysis — Kael as the host ANP of a system of parts

The project analysis (an unsigned essay; its `Kapitel` are its own sections) reads Kael both as a protagonist and as a system. As a system, it takes the TSDP as its rule set: „Die Theorie der Strukturellen Dissoziation der Persönlichkeit (TSDP) dient als das grundlegende Regelwerk für das Bewusstsein von System Kael.“ ^[?]. It counts the system twice: „Die elf identifizierten Anteile von System Kael“ ^[?] form „eine komplexe innere Gesellschaft“ ^[?], while another paragraph gives „mindestens vier ANPs und fünf klaren EPs“ ^[?] (see the record `q3-how-many-kern-welten-and-alters`).

Its table of parts types Kael „ANP (Host)“ ^[?] with the motive „Aufrechterhaltung der Normalität; Angst vor Kontrollverlust.“ ^[?]. The conflict list puts him on the functional side: „Kael, Lex“ ^[?L163] against the trauma-holding EPs.

The three-act plan sends him outward: „Kaels äußere Reise in die instabilen“ ^[?] Risse of the Überwelt „erzwingt eine innere Reise“ ^[?]. On Jung, the essay frames „Kaels Reise der Integration“ ^[?] as individuation.
