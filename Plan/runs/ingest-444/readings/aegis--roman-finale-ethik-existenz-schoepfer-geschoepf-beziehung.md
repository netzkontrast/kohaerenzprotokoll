## Reading — `roman-finale-ethik-existenz-schoepfer-geschoepf-beziehung`, 2025-10-16, the epilogue dossier — AEGIS as the defeated origin of the parts' competences and as ruins in the garden

The epilogue dossier proposes that the parts' competences came from AEGIS: „Sie waren das Ergebnis einer von AEGIS gezielt kultivierten und instrumentalisierten Isolation“ ^[?]. The dossier proposes that the conflict with AEGIS is over, and that Kael now works against narrative decay instead: „Er kämpft nicht mehr gegen AEGIS, sondern gegen die narrative Entropie“ ^[?].

Gardening is set against what AEGIS stood for: „Er lehnt die sterile Perfektion ab, die AEGIS anstrebte“ ^[?], and it tends things that contradict AEGIS's logic of efficiency, „ineffiziente Schönheit“ ^[?].

Two of the proposed scenes show what remains. Scene 1: „Kael besucht die nun inerten Überreste von AEGIS“ ^[?], where the agents run their old routines in „sinnlosen, repetitiven Schleifen“ ^[?]. Scene 3: „Kael meditiert in der Nähe des Kerns des kollabierten AEGIS“ ^[?], whose state the dossier calls „algorithmischer Melancholie“ ^[?]. Both scenes are building blocks, not decisions.
