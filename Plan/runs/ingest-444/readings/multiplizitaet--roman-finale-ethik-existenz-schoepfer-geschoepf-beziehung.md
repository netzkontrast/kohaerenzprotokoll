## Reading — `roman-finale-ethik-existenz-schoepfer-geschoepf-beziehung`, 2025-10-16, the epilogue dossier — functional multiplicity as the end of the healing journey

The epilogue dossier proposes that Kael's stability is the result of a healing journey whose end is named `funktionale Multiplizität` (L72, the dossier puts the term in straight quotes): „Diese Stabilität ist das Ergebnis seiner vollendeten Heilungsreise“ ^[?]. Scene 2 is proposed to show it in action, with the parts as „integrierte Aspekte einer einzigen, kreativen Persönlichkeit“ ^[?]. The dossier describes the healing as coexistence and cooperation of the parts, not their fusion or erasure.
