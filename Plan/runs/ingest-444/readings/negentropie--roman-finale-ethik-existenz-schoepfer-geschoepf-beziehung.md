## Reading — `roman-finale-ethik-existenz-schoepfer-geschoepf-beziehung`, 2025-10-16, the epilogue dossier — gardening as negentropy against narrative entropy

The epilogue dossier proposes that Kael's gardening is negentropic: it defines „Negentropie, in der Informationstheorie, bezeichnet die Zunahme von Komplexität, Ordnung und Information“ ^[?L118], and says „Indem Kael bewusst Schönheit, Komplexität und subtile Bedeutung schafft, wirkt er der natürlichen Tendenz zum narrativen Zerfall entgegen“ ^[?]. The pact with the creator-observer is stated as an exchange: „Der Pakt lautet also“ ^[?] that Kael delivers negentropy as meaningful complexity and beauty, and the creator-observer delivers attention. The term occurs on four lines in all (L26, L118, L120, L223), the list of frameworks among them.
