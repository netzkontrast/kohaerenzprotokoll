## Reading — `aegis-manifest-genesis-krise-reboot`, 2026-04-27, the Genesis manifesto — four Kernwelten as isolated simulation environments for the fragments

The manifesto declares that the Gatekeeper „has instantiated four specialized, operationally isolated simulation environments designated as the Kernwelten (Core Worlds)“ ^[?]. It writes `Kernwelten` six times and `Kernwelt` once as a whole word (`Kernwelten` ^[aegis-manifest-genesis-krise-reboot.md:#6], `Kernwelt` ^[aegis-manifest-genesis-krise-reboot.md:#1]). Each is „governed by a highly specific, contained logical rule set“ ^[?], and the fourth is called the „most highly regulated, paradoxical, and potentially lethal of the Kernwelten“ ^[?]. The table assigns fragments by type: „ANP Fragments (Lex, Kael-Logic)“ ^[?], „EP Fragments (Kiko, Moros)“ ^[?], „Defensive Fragments (Nyx)“ ^[?] and „Emergent Anomalies (Selene, Juna/V)“ ^[?].

The closing declaration says the worlds „are fully deployed, actively quarantining the shattered, traumatized data fragments of the antecedent entity“ ^[?]. The classification of the fragments is AEGIS's, not a verdict of the novel.
