## Reading — `the-true-coherence-protocol-architecting-kaels-journey-from`, 2025-11-03, the True-Coherence protocol — Kael's healing as the engine of the plot, mapped from TSDP fragmentation to functional multiplicity

The True-Coherence protocol, an unsigned design paper, calls itself „the definitive blueprint for architecting the protagonist Kael's internal healing journey“ ^[?]; the claim is recorded, not applied. It plans Kael's arc as „a psychological arc that must function as the primary engine of the plot's resolution“ ^[?].

Its roster lists Kael as Host: „The primary function is to manage daily life and maintain a façade of normalcy.“ ^[?] The journey runs through four Core Worlds, each „an externalized representation of a specific psychological state“ ^[?], mapped to the stages of the Heroine's Journey (L78–L104).

The resolution it plans is not fusion: „The culmination of Kael's arduous journey is not the erasure of his parts or a forced fusion into a single identity, but the achievement of“ ^[?] functional multiplicity (L112). It frames the healing as political: „Kael's healing is a profound philosophical and political rebellion.“ ^[?] and ends on the claim „The protocol guarantees that Kael's integration is the sole mechanism of victory.“ ^[?]
