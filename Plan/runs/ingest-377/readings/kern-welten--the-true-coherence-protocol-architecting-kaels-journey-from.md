## Reading — `the-true-coherence-protocol-architecting-kaels-journey-from`, 2025-11-03, the True-Coherence protocol — four Core Worlds as allegorical landscapes of four stages

The True-Coherence protocol maps four Core Worlds to the stages of the Heroine's Journey, each „an externalized representation of a specific psychological state“ ^[?]. Core World 1, `Logos-Prime`, is „a world of brutalist architecture, unnatural symmetry, and oppressive acoustics“ ^[?]; `Mnemosyne-Archipel` is „a fragmented, swamp-like landscape of memory and emotion“ ^[?]; `Cerberus-Labyrinth` is „a bunker-like fortress of paranoia and defense mechanisms“ ^[?]; and `Kairos-Potentialis` is, in its words, „an overgrown“ ^[?] ruin-garden (L101).
