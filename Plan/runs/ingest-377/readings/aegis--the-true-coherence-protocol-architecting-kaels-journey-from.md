## Reading — `the-true-coherence-protocol-architecting-kaels-journey-from`, 2025-11-03, the True-Coherence protocol — AEGIS as an externalized perpetrator introject that the protocol maps Kael's journey against

The True-Coherence protocol, an unsigned design paper, plans Kael's healing as the inverse of AEGIS's course: „The entire development of the antagonist, AEGIS, will mirror Kael's journey in a perfect inversion“ ^[?]. It describes AEGIS as an „autopoietic system whose core logic functions as a direct and precise obstacle to Kael's psychological integration“ ^[?], and gives it the philosophy of `coherence through negation` and the `Paradox of Misaligned Coherence` (both in L64).

The paper positions AEGIS as an `externalized perpetrator introject` (L66) and says „Its methods are a direct technological reflection of an abuser's tactics.“ ^[?] AEGIS also „actively reinforces the dissociative phobias between his Alters.“ ^[?]

It plans AEGIS to read Kael's recovery as error: „Crucially, AEGIS consistently misinterprets Kael's every step toward psychological health as a system error.“ ^[?] The sterile logic is staged in the first Core World: „Logos-Prime is a world of brutalist architecture, unnatural symmetry, and oppressive acoustics, symbolizing the sterile, rigid logic of AEGIS.“ ^[?]
