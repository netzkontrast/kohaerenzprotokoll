## Reading — `the-true-coherence-protocol-architecting-kaels-journey-from`, 2025-11-03, the True-Coherence protocol — the Alters as adaptive functions mapped to the Heroine's Journey

The True-Coherence protocol states its thesis as a mapping: „The core thesis of this protocol is that by meticulously mapping the adaptive functions of Kael's distinct personality parts (Alters) to the narrative milestones of the Heroine's Journey, Kael's agency remains central to his own salvation.“ ^[?] Its roster names six parts, Kael, Lex, Alex, Nyx, Kiko and Lia (L36–L41), and gives no total.

For the resolution it describes functional multiplicity by three principles, among them „Co-consciousness: The lowering of amnesiac barriers between Alters.“ ^[?] and „Cooperation: The active and willing collaboration between Alters.“ ^[?] and „System Responsibility: The collective acceptance by all Alters of shared responsibility for the actions, well-being, and history of the entire system.“ ^[?]
