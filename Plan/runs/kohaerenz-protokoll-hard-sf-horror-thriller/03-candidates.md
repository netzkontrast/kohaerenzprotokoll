argument, none of them defined, marked or set in bold): `Kohärenz`, `Integration`, `Exklusion`, `Subtraktion`,
`Addition`. The document sets two ways of reaching coherence against each other, „durch Subtraktion“ and
„durch Addition“ (L31, L167, L169), and heads two sections with `Integration` and `Exklusion`; the note quotes
these places. Left off for the same reason: the five Grenzsituationen named in a run of ordinary nouns (L61),
the word `Existenz` outside its one marked use (L63), `Hardware`, `Dateien` and `Strahlung` in scare quotes
(L79, L87, L53), and the nine titles of the reference list.

One word, several things. `Kael` is the protagonist (L25), the whole system (`System Kael`, L15, L71) and one
of the ANPs (`Kael (Host)`, L84, with `Der Host` at L87). `Kohärenz` is the project's title and a concept.

words the text does not connect. `Moonshine-Link` (L123, L125) and `Moonshine-Resonanz` (L146) may be one
thing under two names, and the text does not say. `K-J Connection` and `Moonshine-Link` are joined by „oder“
(L123), so those two are one thing by the document's own word. The letters K and J are not expanded; the next
paragraph speaks of „Die Verbindung zwischen Kael und Juna“ (L125) without writing `K-J`. `Cosmic Horror` (L19, L115, L147, L153) and `Kosmische Horror` (L103) are the English and the German
form of one heading term.
