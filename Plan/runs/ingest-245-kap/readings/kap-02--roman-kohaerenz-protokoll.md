## Reading — `roman-kohaerenz-protokoll`, 2025-04-17, the early prose draft — `Resonanz`

- The early prose draft's chapter 2, „Resonanz“ ^[?L417], is cut into 40 numbered Moments in a grey place that answers Kael's feelings: „Meine Gefühle... sie formen diesen Ort“ ^[?L537]. It ends at a fork, „(40. Moment: Der Scheideweg)“ ^[?L585], with „Er musste wählen.“ ^[?L587] A remark in another voice before Moment 31 calls the last ten Moments the arrival at the threshold to KW2 (L545). A draft of 2025, not the chapter as approved.
