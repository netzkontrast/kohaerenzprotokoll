## Reading — `the-architecture-of-being-a-philosophical-thesis-on-the-core`, 2025-11-03, the Architecture-of-Being paper — Coherons as the K₁ kernel's building blocks, and the repair cycle

The Architecture-of-Being paper places the Coheron inside its reading of the Dual-Kernel Theory. The K₁ kernel „represents the force of structure, information preservation, and internal consistency“ ^[?] and works through fundamental building blocks called Coherons, and in the paper's words is there „to create and maintain order against the universal tendency toward dissolution“ ^[?]

On the cycle of damage and repair, the paper writes that when K₀ intrudes, „K₁ structures respond by emitting self-generated“ ^[?] Corrective Wavelets, described as „localized bursts of oscillatory activity that repair their internal Coheron networks“ ^[?]

The paper interprets; it defines the Coheron no further.
