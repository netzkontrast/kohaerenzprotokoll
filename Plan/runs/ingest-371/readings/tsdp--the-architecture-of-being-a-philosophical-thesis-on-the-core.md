## Reading — `the-architecture-of-being-a-philosophical-thesis-on-the-core`, 2025-11-03, the Architecture-of-Being paper — TSDP as a mirror of the conflict of truth theories

The Architecture-of-Being paper bases the structure of System Kael's mind on the Theory of Structural Dissociation of the Personality (TSDP) and reads it as something that „is a precise mirror of the story's master conflict between two theories of truth“ ^[?]

It assigns the Apparently Normal Parts to coherence and the Emotional Parts to correspondence: the ANPs „strive to maintain a coherent“ ^[?] normalcy, while the EPs „are the carriers of the unprocessed, traumatic truth“ ^[?]

The paper interprets; it makes no clinical claim.
