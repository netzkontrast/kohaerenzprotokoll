## Reading — `narrative-plot-exploration-existenzielle-kohaerenz`, 2025-04-26, the existential-coherence plot exploration — Guardians as agents whose interventions worsen the instability

The existential-coherence exploration restates as premise that the system „Setzt Agenten“ ^[?] (`Guardians`) to repair by tighter control, that „Die Interventionen verschlimmern die Instabilität“ ^[?], and that „Einige Agenten beginnen möglicherweise, die Systemlogik in Frage zu stellen“ ^[?]. In its discussion of DID and IFS it adds that the Guardians „könnten unbeabsichtigt wie externalisierte“ ^[?] protectors act.

In Synopsis A it proposes that AEGIS sends them against Kael: Guardians, „Agenten von AEGIS“ ^[?], are sent to correct or neutralise Kael; that „Einige Guardians, konfrontiert mit der Ineffektivität ihrer Methoden“ ^[?] begin to question AEGIS; and that „Kael, unterstützt von abtrünnigen Guardians, dringt zum logischen Kern von AEGIS vor“ ^[?]. Here Kael is the exploration's analogue of the novel's figure. Synopsis B and C are not read here.
