## Reading — `protokoll-ontologie-roman-konzeptentwicklung`, 2025-11-03, the Protokoll-Ontologie exposé — cache-coherence protocols as the analogue of Overhead

The Protokoll-Ontologie exposé names the cache-coherence protocols of computer architecture as the technical analogue of its Overhead: „Als konkretes, technisches Analogon dienen die“ ^[?] Cache-Kohärenz-Protokolle, which keep the cores of a multiprocessor system at one coherent version of memory by communication and validation. In its glossary the source column of the entry `Protokoll` reads „Systemtheorie 5; Cache-Kohärenz“ ^[?].
