## Reading — `kohaerenz-protokoll-architecture-synthesis`, 2026-04-28, the architecture synthesis — Kael's reality as the true K₁, by its inversion, with his role in Storyform A and the climax

The architecture synthesis proposes an inversion of the kernels, and Kael is on its receiving end. It says that „The canonical architecture positioned the antagonist system, AEGIS, as the Coherence Kernel (K₁) and the protagonist Kael’s fragmented trauma as the Erasure Kernel (K₀)“ ^[?L23], and that the inversion renders his lived reality the true K₁; the German block puts it as „Kaels dissoziative Zyklen hingegen bewahren die wechselseitige Information über Risse hinweg“ ^[?L27]. The verdict on this axis is the brief's own: „Inversion Superior“ ^[?L23].

For Storyform A the synthesis places him as Main Character: „Kael is trapped in a specific situation—the construct—and holding an unintegrated history“ ^[?L40]. In the appendix on the truth theories it proposes that „Kael embodies Correspondence“ ^[?L157]. On L99 the synthesis has Kael become `The Gardener`: „Kael learns to align with The Foundation“ ^[?L99]. (The title stands there inside straight quotes, so it is named here, not quoted.)

At the climax the synthesis writes that Kael, „having integrated his 11 entities (including Lex, Nyx, Kiko, and Moros)“ ^[?L73], presents his functional multiplicity to AEGIS's LFI-Kern; in the Witness Function's second layer, „Kael acts as the Prover“ ^[?L178]. Beat 1 of the handoff has him „having fully aligned his internal entities“ ^[?L187], and the handoff's action has him counter AEGIS „not with physical combat“ ^[?L373].
