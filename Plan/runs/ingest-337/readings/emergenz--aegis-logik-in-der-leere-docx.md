## Reading — `aegis-logik-in-der-leere-docx`, 2025-08-05, the Aegis-Logik-in-der-Leere report — emergence as self-organisation and as Scenario B

The Aegis-Logik-in-der-Leere report restates the request's premise that `Aegis` was not designed, and analyses it: „Dies deutet auf Prinzipien der Selbstorganisation und Emergenz aus Komplexität hin.“ ^[?] In its own derivation of Scenario B it proposes a mechanism by analogy with Wolfram: „Die Emergenz folgt Prinzipien, die denen von Wolframs komputationalem Universum ähneln.“ ^[?] The scenario is one of three the report offers and none is chosen.
