## Reading — `plot-entwicklung-fuer-kohaerenz-protokoll`, 2025-04-23, the plot blueprint — Juna through the connection, a catalyst and no superpower

In this blueprint Juna appears through the connection with Kael, not as a character with scenes of her own; it names the connection `Kael-Juna-Verbindung` ^[plot-entwicklung-fuer-kohaerenz-protokoll.md:#27]. It proposes that the connection is „kein gewöhnlicher Kommunikationskanal“ ^[?], operating as sub-protokollarische Resonanz. Its effect is aimed at Kael's deepest layers, „die möglicherweise mit seiner Herkunft von der Kohärenz-Insel verbunden sind“ ^[?]; the hedge is the blueprint's own. It calls the connection „Die Kael-Juna-Verbindung ist der entscheidende Katalysator“ ^[?] for Kael's healing and integration, and denies it the role of a weapon: „Die Kael-Juna-Verbindung ist somit keine“ ^[?] superpower in the usual sense, but „sondern die Manifestation einer alternativen Seinsweise“ ^[?]. In the setup it is hinted at „vielleicht als unerklärliche Sehnsucht“ ^[?].

- The plot blueprint proposes the Juna link as catalyst and alternative way of being, never as a direct attack on AEGIS.
