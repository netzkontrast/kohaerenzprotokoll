## Reading — `plot-entwicklung-fuer-kohaerenz-protokoll`, 2025-04-23, the plot blueprint — the Potentialmeer as what AEGIS isolates itself from

The plot blueprint proposes the Potentialmeer as the chaotic outside against which AEGIS defines itself: AEGIS secures its existence „durch rigide Abgrenzung vom chaotischen Potentialmeer“ ^[?], and its principle of demarcation leads to „die Isolation des gesamten Systems vom unkontrollierbaren Potentialmeer“ ^[?]. Of the Kael-Juna connection it says it points to a deeper level, „das Potentialmeer als Substrat“ ^[?]. For the climax it names as one possible setting „sogar der Übergangsbereich zum Potentialmeer“ ^[?]. Recorded as a proposal, not applied.
