## Reading — `plot-entwicklung-fuer-kohaerenz-protokoll`, 2025-04-23, the plot blueprint — Mnemosyne, Guardian of KW2, who erases memories and strengthens them

The plot blueprint proposes `Mnemosyne` as the Guardian of the Resonanz-Nebel: „Mnemosyne (KW2) soll potenziell destabilisierende Erinnerungen und emotionale Resonanzen löschen oder neutralisieren“ ^[?]. Its attempts are proposed to turn against it: „Mnemosynes Versuche, emotionale Inhalte zu löschen, stärken paradoxerweise deren Bedeutung“ ^[?]. In KW2 the blueprint says she tries, `möglicherweise`, to delete or distort memories, which has the opposite effect. Recorded as a proposal, not applied.
