## Reading — `dramatica-storyform-kohaerenz-protokoll`, 2026-01-02, the January storyform (an unsigned English Dramatica storyform) — the Kernwelten as resonators of the four throughlines

The January storyform is an English Dramatica storyform; its values are its own and decide nothing. It names the Kernwelten worlds, not Guardians, and gives them a function: „externalized psychological landscapes that serve as thematic resonators for the Dramatica throughlines“ ^[?] It directs: „The final writer should use these mappings as a guide for environmental storytelling“ ^[?]

It pairs one world with each throughline. The first, Logos-Prime, goes with the Overall Story: „This world of rigid, sterile order and“ ^[?] The second, Mnemosyne-Archipel, goes with Kael: „This chaotic, surreal landscape of fragmented memory externalizes Kael's Main Character Throughline.“ ^[?] The third, Cerberus-Labyrinth, goes with the Subjective Story: „It visualizes their relational struggle“ ^[?] The fourth, Kairos-Potentialis, goes with the Impact Character: „This world of unpredictable possibility embodies the Impact Character Throughline.“ ^[?]
