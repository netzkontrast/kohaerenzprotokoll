## Reading — `operationalizing-the-triple-helix-structure-as-a-trauma-prot`, 2025-11-03, the Triple-Helix blueprint — Kael's external journey locked to a three-stage healing arc, with costs and a prescribed end

The Triple-Helix blueprint prescribes that Kael's external journey is „causally and thematically locked to his internal healing process“ ^[?], mapped to Stabilization, Confrontation and Integration. For Act I it mandates „Kael rediscovering within himself the fundamental aspects of reality that AEGIS rejects“ ^[?]; for Act III the cooperating System Kael „acts as a unified protagonist to directly challenge AEGIS“ ^[?]. Act I is to chart a fall from artificial order through an event the blueprint calls a „profound betrayal by AEGIS“ ^[?]. In Act II it demands a permanent loss each time Kael fails to break a loop, among them a key location in the Kernwelten that „is irrevocably corrupted or destroyed“ ^[?]. The blueprint sets the climax as Kael's „final act of achieving functional multiplicity“ ^[?], and, for the end, Kael's role as The Gardener (L113). Each of these is an instruction to a writer.
