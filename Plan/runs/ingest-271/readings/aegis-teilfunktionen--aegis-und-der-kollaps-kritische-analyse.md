The critical analysis names `Zero-Trust` as a security posture of AEGIS, not as one of a list of sub-functions. It argues that the classical logic AEGIS rests on forces it into this posture: „Aufgrund dieses drohenden Trivialismus ist AEGIS gezwungen“ ^[?] to keep it, and the line adds that it eliminates every small ambivalence with extreme algorithmic hardness. In the Player Dilemma passage it writes: „AEGIS operiert unter einem paranoiden Zero-Trust-Paradigma und scannt permanent alle algorithmischen Datenpakete auf Widersprüche“ ^[?].
