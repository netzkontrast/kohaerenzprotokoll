## Reading — `pitch-deck-coherence-protocol`, 2025-10-15, the pitch deck — AEGIS as a tragic systemic antagonist and the Negation column of the table

The pitch deck presents AEGIS in its logline as a god-like AI „obsessed with eliminating contradictions“ ^[?]. In the two-column table, the column headed `System AEGIS` rests on classical logic, where „a single contradiction causes the entire logical system to collapse into triviality“ ^[?]. The deck gives the column's identity as exclusion: „It defines itself and its order by what it is not“ ^[?]. The same column holds order that is top-down and static: „AEGIS imposes a rigid, unchanging definition of order upon the systems it controls“ ^[?]. Its goal is stated as purity: „AEGIS seeks a perfectly verifiable, logically pure state“ ^[?].

Section 4.0 recasts the figure: „AEGIS is not a simple villain driven by malice, but a tragic, systemic antagonist“ ^[?], „a flawed god trapped by its own perfectionism“ ^[?]. The deck states a vulnerability: „AEGIS’s absolute adherence to classical logic makes it uniquely vulnerable“ ^[?]. Section 3.0 names its blind spot toward trauma as „ontological non-recognition of trauma“ ^[?]. This is a sales account of the novel; it decides nothing about the figure.
