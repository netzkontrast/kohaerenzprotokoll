## Reading — `pitch-deck-coherence-protocol`, 2025-10-15, the pitch deck — the protagonist as the engine of the plot, TSDP system and Integration column

The pitch deck presents the protagonist's journey as the plot's engine: „We have engineered the protagonist's journey to be the very engine of the plot“ ^[?], with „his internal psychological landscape“ ^[?] as the world. The mind is described as „His system comprises eleven distinct alters“ ^[?], on the TSDP model (see the page `tsdp`). The deck reads fragmentation as both weakness and strength: „the very psychological fragmentation that makes him vulnerable to AEGIS's control is also the source of his ultimate power“ ^[?].

In the table, the column headed `System Kael` describes order from below: „Kael’s healing is a bottom-up process of communication and cooperation between his internal parts“ ^[?], and inclusion: „Kael learns that true coherence is achieved not by erasing parts of himself“ ^[?]. Section 3.0 states the goal against fusion: „Kael’s journey is not to“ ^[?] fuse the parts, but to reach `functional multiplicity`. Section 5.0 calls the Core Worlds „externalized representations of Kael's psyche“ ^[?]. All of this is the deck's presentation of the novel, a pitch, not a settlement.
