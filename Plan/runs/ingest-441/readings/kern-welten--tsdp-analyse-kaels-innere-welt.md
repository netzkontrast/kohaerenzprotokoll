## Reading — `tsdp-analyse-kaels-innere-welt`, 2025-04-28, the TSDP analysis of Kael's inner world — the four worlds as the setting of each part's tension points

The report's section III analyses every part against four areas, among them „für jeden einzelnen Anteil von Kael in Bezug auf die vier definierten Bereiche: Kernwelten“ ^[?L79]. Each part's list opens with the worlds by code, `Co₁`, `McL`, `B` and `Ly`, glossed as „Co₁ (Konstrukt-Stadt)“ ^[?L87], „B (Bibliothek/Archiv)“ ^[?] and „Ly (Labyrinth/Wildnis)“ ^[?]; the second world is glossed in L88. For the plot the advice reads: „Schärfen Sie das Design der Kernwelten“ ^[?] so that worlds offer specific challenges to specific parts. The text assigns, for example, „Co₁ testet Rhys und Nyx.“ ^[?]
