## Reading — `roman-outline-kohaerenz-protokoll-uberarbeitung`, 2025-05-03, the strategy report — the Guardians as AEGIS' means of intimidation and control

The strategy report places the `Guardians` in three theory contexts. Under psychological warfare it lists intimidation: „Einschüchterung (Guardians, KW3)“ ^[?]. It then reads the KWs and Guardians as internal control: „Diese KWs und Guardians fungieren als Werkzeuge der psychologischen Kontrolle“ ^[?]. Under alignment it says what they represent: „Die Guardians repräsentieren AEGIS' Kontrollmechanismen und defensive instrumentelle Ziele.“ ^[?] Under the hard problem of consciousness it asks whether they could be conscious: „Dies betrifft AEGIS, die Guardians und potenziell Kael selbst.“ ^[?]
