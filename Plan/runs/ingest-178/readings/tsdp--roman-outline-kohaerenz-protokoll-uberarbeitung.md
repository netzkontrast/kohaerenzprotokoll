## Reading — `roman-outline-kohaerenz-protokoll-uberarbeitung`, 2025-05-03, the strategy report — TSDP as the core theory applied to Kael, with a phobia table and a phase model

The strategy report names its core theory outright: „Theorie der Strukturellen Dissoziation der Persönlichkeit (TSDP)“ ^[?]. It states the theory's claim as the theory's: „Die TSDP postuliert eine grundlegende Spaltung der Persönlichkeit bei komplex traumatisierten Individuen“ ^[?], and adds that dissociation „wird als Mangel an Integration zwischen diesen Anteilen verstanden.“ ^[?]

The strategy report applies the theory to the outline in three ways. First, it takes the phobias as the part of the theory that keeps dissociation going: „Ein zentraler Aspekt der TSDP sind spezifische“ ^[?] phobias, and it maps five of them to chapters in its first table, among them „Phobie vor dissoziativen Anteilen“ ^[?]. Second, it places the theory's treatment model against the outline's three acts: „korrespondiert das in der TSDP beschriebene phasenorientierte Behandlungsmodell“ ^[?] with the proposed structure. Third, it reads AEGIS's manipulation through Kael's condition: „Die Wirksamkeit von AEGIS' psychologischer Manipulation (z.B. Gaslighting in Ch 8) wird durch Kaels bereits bestehende TSDP-Vulnerabilitäten verstärkt.“ ^[?]

The report's later summary lists as the first strength of its basis the anchoring of Kael's inner world in this theory (L286).
