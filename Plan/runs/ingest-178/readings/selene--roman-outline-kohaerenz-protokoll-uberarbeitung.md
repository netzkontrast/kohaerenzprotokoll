## Reading — `roman-outline-kohaerenz-protokoll-uberarbeitung`, 2025-05-03, the strategy report — Selene as wholeness, integration and coordinator in Murdock's stages

The strategy report places Selene in four of Murdock's stages, always as the name of wholeness or integration. Stage 1 maps Kael's origin as „Kaels fragmentierter Ursprung (Trennung von Ganzheit/Selene)“ ^[?]. Stage 7 maps the longing: „Streben nach Integration (Selene)“ ^[?]. Stage 9 maps the reconciliation of the ANPs under her: „Aussöhnung/Koordination der ANPs (Lex, Alex, Nyx) unter Selene“ ^[?]. Stage 10 maps „Emergenz von Selene als Koordinatorin“ ^[?].

In prose the strategy report applies the same figure twice more. Integration, it argues, means reintegrating the logical and protective parts „sie auf gesunde Weise in das Gesamtsystem (Selene) zu“ ^[?] and it ties the dissonance reading to „Das Erreichen funktionaler Multiplizität unter Selene“ ^[?].
