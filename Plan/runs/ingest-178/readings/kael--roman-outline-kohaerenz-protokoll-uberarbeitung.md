## Reading — `roman-outline-kohaerenz-protokoll-uberarbeitung`, 2025-05-03, the strategy report — Kael as the subject of TSDP and of Murdock's Heroine's Journey, written with female pronouns

The strategy report applies theory to an outline and writes Kael as a woman throughout. Its pronouns stay in the quotations below: it speaks of „ihre dissoziative Erfahrung“ ^[?] and of „Kaels fragmentierte Realität“ ^[?], and later of „Ihr fragmentiertes Selbstgefühl“ ^[?] and „Ihre existenzielle Krise“ ^[?]. The report gives the goal of its first section as letting the reader dive into this reality: „Den Leser in Kaels fragmentierte Realität eintauchen zu lassen“ ^[?].

The strategy report applies the theory of structural dissociation to Kael's origin: „Kaels gewaltsame Fragmentierung im Prolog durch AEGIS etabliert diesen traumatischen Ursprung direkt.“ ^[?] Its table of phobias places Kael's arc chapter by chapter; in the row for the fifth phobia it places her future after the confrontation: „Kaels finale Entscheidungen über ihre Zukunft nach der Konfrontation mit AEGIS“ ^[?]. It reads AEGIS's gaslighting as made effective by this structure: „Ihr fragmentiertes Selbstgefühl, ihre Amnesie und ihre inneren Phobien machen sie anfälliger für Manipulationen“ ^[?].

The strategy report frames the arc with Murdock, whom it describes as addressing „speziell die psycho-spirituelle Reise von Frauen“ ^[?]. Its table of Murdock's stages maps Kael as follows: stage 1, „Kaels fragmentierter Ursprung (Trennung von Ganzheit/Selene)“ ^[?]; stage 2, „Kael verlässt sich auf Logik (Lex) und Abwehr (Alex) zur Navigation in AEGIS' feindlicher Umgebung.“ ^[?]; stage 10, „Emergenz von Selene als Koordinatorin“ ^[?]. On the existential crisis it writes: „Ihre existenzielle Krise“ ^[?] in the chapter it cites is said to take up the questions of identity under extreme conditions.

In the table of AEGIS's alignment failures the report names the prologue violence with an inner name: „Gewaltsame Fragmentierung von“ ^[?] `Echo` (Kael), listed for the prologue and three further chapters. The name `Echo` there is the report's own inner quotation of the name, and it is here an occurrence naming Kael, not a reading of the residual echoes.
