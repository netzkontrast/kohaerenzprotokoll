## Reading — `dramatica-storyform-fuer-romananalyse`, 2026-01-02, the Dramatica exegesis — Kael as the Main Character in Mind, proposed by the report

The Dramatica exegesis, an unsigned research report building a Storyform, takes Kael as its Main Character. It reads his conflict as inward: „Sein Konflikt ist nicht primär physisch“ ^[?] Its frame for the Influence Character, AEGIS, sits against him: „Wenn der MC im Mind ist, muss der IC im Universe (Situation) sein.“ ^[?] It names the Emotional Parts as breaking into his consciousness: „Die EPs (Emotional Parts) wie Kiko (Angst) und Nyx (Wut) sind Manifestationen des Unterbewusstseins, die in das Bewusstsein einbrechen.“ ^[?]

The report gives Kael and AEGIS the same Problem, Control, and from that draws a change for Kael: „Da MC Problem und OS Problem identisch sind“ ^[?] It calls them mirrors: „Dass Kael dasselbe Problem wie AEGIS hat (Control), macht sie zu Spiegelbildern.“ ^[?] For the relationship it writes: „Kael übernimmt Verantwortung für AEGIS (als Teil von sich)“ ^[?] These are the report's own proposals for a Storyform, not statements of the novel's canon.
