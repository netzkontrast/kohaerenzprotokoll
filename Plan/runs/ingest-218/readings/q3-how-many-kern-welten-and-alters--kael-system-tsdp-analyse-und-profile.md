## 2026-10-07 — `kael-system-tsdp-analyse-und-profile`, 2025-04-28, the TSDP profile report

**The TSDP profile report counts eleven parts and four Kernwelten and proposes which parts resonate with which world, without assigning one world to one part.**

The count of parts stands in the introduction: „elf identifizierten Persönlichkeitsanteilen“ ^[?], listed there by name (Kael, Selene, Nyx, Kiko, Lia, Isabelle, Moros, Alex, Rhys, Lex, Argus). The four worlds carry their own labels: „Die fiktionalen Kernwelten (Co₁: Ordnung, McL: Netzwerk/Wissen, B: Chaos/Trauma, Ly: Potentialität/Emergenz) fungieren als externalisierte Repräsentationen der inneren Konflikte und Zustände“ ^[?]. World B is labelled Chaos/Trauma here, and the report calls it „Die direkteste Trigger-Welt“ ^[?].

The report maps parts onto worlds as triggers and resonances, several at once, hedged: B „Aktiviert wahrscheinlich mehrere EPs“ ^[?], and of Ly: „Könnte mit Lias Kreativität resonieren“ ^[?]. So one world touches many parts and one part many worlds in the report's proposal; the profile report does not say one world is one part.

Where it stands in the record's own terms: it gives eleven and four, and a resonance map offered as proposal, not assignment. It leaves the correspondence question open and does not say whether four is all of the worlds.
