## Reading — `kohaerenz-protokoll-konzeptionelle-ausarbeitung`, 2025-04-19, the conceptual report on the protocol — thermodynamic entropy as a borrowed frame

The conceptual report describes an abstract construct for any system and names no figure, place or event of the novel. The thermodynamic entropy entry (the first of its five forms of incoherence) is a frame borrowed from physics: „Die Entropie ist ein zentrales Konzept der Thermodynamik und statistischen Mechanik“ ^[?]. Its relevance for the protocol: „Entropie repräsentiert die fundamentale Tendenz physikalischer Systeme zum Zerfall“ ^[?], and a protocol for physical or biological systems would work against it.

The introduction lists thermodynamic entropy among the incoherent environments in which the protocol operates: „Es operiert insbesondere dann, wenn das System mit einer Umgebung oder einem Substrat konfrontiert ist“ ^[?]; the line names entropy in that list, not as the protocol's origin.
