## Reading — `narrative-kernentwicklung-aegis-und-system-kael`, 2025-11-03, the development dossier — Kael as AEGIS's trauma made living and as the host of a system of at least eleven parts

The development dossier, an unsigned planning dossier, frames the novel around System Kael. It calls the protagonist „Der Protagonist, System Kael, ist die lebendige, atmende Verkörperung des Traumas von AEGIS“ ^[?], and it describes Kael's inner world as set out in a TSDP analysis it cites: „Kaels innere Welt, wie sie in der TSDP-Analyse detailliert beschrieben wird“ ^[?].

On the size of the system it says „System Kael besteht aus mindestens elf identifizierten Persönlichkeitsanteilen“ ^[?]. Its roster of parts names Kael first among the four ANPs: „vier ANPs (Kael, Lex, Alex, Rhys)“ ^[?]. In the role table and in the act plan Kael is the host: „Kael (ANP-Host) verspürt den Drang, der Sache nachzugehen, angetrieben von Neugier“ ^[?]; the table row is headed „Kael (ANP-Host)“ ^[?].

The sentence the dossier labels its narrative core makes the protagonist a man: it is „die Reise eines fragmentierten Mannes, Kael“ ^[?], who must confront the digital god, AEGIS, that made him in the image of its own trauma.

For the midpoint of act II the dossier plans a revelation: Kael is not an error in the system but „eine lebendige Erinnerung an die Genesis-Krise“ ^[?]. This is a plan, set in the dossier's own account of the acts.
