## Reading — `narrative-kernentwicklung-aegis-und-system-kael`, 2025-11-03, the development dossier — the Risse as correspondence errors and fragmented memories

The development dossier, a planning dossier, reads the `Risse` as the places where the coherence theory meets its objection: „sind physische Manifestationen des“ ^[?] isolation objection, and „Störungen, Risse im Gewebe der digitalen Realität“ ^[?] where the system's internal model does not match a deeper external truth.
