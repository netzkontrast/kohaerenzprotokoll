## Reading — `aegis-paradoxon-konzeption-und-analyse`, 2025-04-29, the AEGIS paradox report — Four Kernwelten glossed by theme, with two glosses for KW1

In the setting the report restates (`\[Kontext\]`), AEGIS „erschafft und managt sie Simulationen, die“ ^[?] the Kernwelten KW1–KW4. The report glosses them by theme in its analysis: „KW1 (Logik/Ordnung)“ ^[?], „KW2 (Emotion/Erinnerung)“ ^[?] and „KW4 (Potentialität/Emergenz)“ ^[?]. In the passage on AEGIS' actions it writes „KW1 (Ordnung) und KW3 (Abwehr)“ ^[?], so the KW1 gloss differs between the two passages; both are recorded.

For the invented KW3 scenario the report writes that „KW3 ist thematisch auf Chaos, Abwehr, Paranoia und Grenzen fokussiert“ ^[?], marked as the restated setting.
