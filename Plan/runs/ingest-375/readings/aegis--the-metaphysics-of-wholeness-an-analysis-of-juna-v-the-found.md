## Reading — `the-metaphysics-of-wholeness-an-analysis-of-juna-v-the-found`, 2025-11-03, the wholeness report — AEGIS as a closed Coherentist system that exiles, and cannot see the link

The wholeness report argues that Juna/V is „the embodied principle of Correspondence that AEGIS’s closed, Coherentist system was forced to exile to maintain its own fragile integrity“ ^[?]. The exile is called „an act of logical self-harm“ ^[?], and what was exiled, loneliness and longing, AEGIS read as „a hostile system error“ ^[?]. The report lists „the core aspects of reality that AEGIS classifies as existential threats“ ^[?]. On the link it argues: „AEGIS cannot recognize it as a signal“ ^[?], because the link „has no origin point, no transmission path, and no data packets“ ^[?]. Reaching The Foundation is not a task for AEGIS's method: „fundamentally the wrong tool for the job“ ^[?]. The report proposes these as the plot's reconstruction after the second act; they are its argument, recorded and not applied.
