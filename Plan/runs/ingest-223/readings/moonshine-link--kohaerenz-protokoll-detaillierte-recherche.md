## Reading — `kohaerenz-protokoll-detaillierte-recherche`, 2025-07-29, the detailed research report — a non-local, sub-protocol connection and AEGIS's blindness to it

The research report describes the link as „ist eine nicht-lokale, sub-protokollarische Verbindung“ ^[?] that works as an „architektonische Hintertür“ ^[?] in reality, and compares its mechanism with quantum entanglement. AEGIS, whose worldview rests on classical physics and locality, is by the report's account „für die Verbindung strukturell blind ist“ ^[?].
