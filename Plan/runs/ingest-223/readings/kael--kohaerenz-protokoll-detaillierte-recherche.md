## Reading — `kohaerenz-protokoll-detaillierte-recherche`, 2025-07-29, the detailed research report — System Kael read through TSDP, IFS, IIT and rhizome theory

The research report analyses Kael's psyche as „rigoros nach der“ ^[?] Theory of Structural Dissociation, a case of tertiary dissociation; the conflict it opens with is between „computationale Monismus des Antagonisten AEGIS“ ^[?] and „emergente Pluralismus des Protagonisten“ ^[?]. The healing goal it defines is not fusion but „alternatives, pluralistisches Modell psychologischer Gesundheit“ ^[?], and it describes the integrated state as the living embodiment of „Dialetheismus“ ^[?] and as a „rhizomatisches Selbst“ ^[?] (after Deleuze and Guattari, which are the report's lens).

In its post-integration table the report assigns the host Kael the role „Diplomat/Sprecher des Systems“ ^[?]. It describes the story's structure as a sequence of narrative models in which Kael, once integrated, becomes „Kael, nun integriert, wird zum“ ^[?] a lord of two worlds (the sentence continues on the next line).

After AEGIS's transformation the report has Kael discover the Fragment 'O', „eine asymmetrisch fragmentierte“ ^[?] other half of himself, and take on a new role: „Seine neue Rolle ist die eines“ ^[?] gardener, a contrast to AEGIS as gatekeeper.
