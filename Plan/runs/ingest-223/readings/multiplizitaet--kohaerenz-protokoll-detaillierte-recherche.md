## Reading — `kohaerenz-protokoll-detaillierte-recherche`, 2025-07-29, the detailed research report — functional multiplicity as healing goal, not fusion

The research report defines the healing goal as „Das Heilungsziel für Kael ist nicht die“ ^[?] fusion, but „das Erreichen einer“ ^[?] functional multiplicity, a state of co-consciousness, cooperation and system responsibility. It calls this a pluralistic model of health that questions the pathologising of multiplicity.

It adds that „Kaels Entscheidung, funktionale Multiplizität anstelle von Fusion anzustreben“ ^[?] is a rejection of a standard imposed from outside: the report says „AEGIS erzwingt eine monolithische, singuläre Definition von“ ^[?] health.
