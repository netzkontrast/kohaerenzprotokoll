## Reading — `kohaerenz-protokoll-detaillierte-recherche`, 2025-07-29, the detailed research report — AEGIS as an autopoietic, operationally closed system with a three-layer architecture

The report reads the project through borrowed theory and calls itself „definitiver konzeptioneller Rahmen“ ^[?]; that is its claim about its own standing, not canon.

The research report defines AEGIS's essence as an „autopoietisches System“ ^[?] after Maturana, Varela and Luhmann (the report's lens), made „operativ geschlossen“ ^[?] by its continuing self-production, and derives from this its „ontologischen Blindheit“ ^[?]. It adds that „Diese systemtheoretische Grundlage entmoralisiert den Antagonismus von AEGIS“ ^[?].

It describes „dreistufigen hybriden kognitiven Architektur“ ^[?]: a symbolic core on a logic of formal inconsistency, specialised modules on Jaśkowski's discursive logic D2, and an adaptive reinforcement-learning layer. Its adaptive layer is aimed at optimising the system on the basis of „fehlerhaften Definition von“ ^[?] coherence.

The tragic flaw is named in the report's terms: „Der tragische Fehler von AEGIS ist ein klassischer Fall von“ ^[?] Specification Gaming. The trigger of the transformation is the Gödel-Gambit, and the report says AEGIS then faces „AEGIS steht somit vor der Wahl zwischen zwei Formen der Selbstauslöschung“ ^[?]. Of the transformed world it writes of „Ineffiziente Schönheit“ ^[?] and, in the Internal Family Systems analogy, a „externalisiertes Täterintrojekt“ ^[?] acting on Kael's parts.
