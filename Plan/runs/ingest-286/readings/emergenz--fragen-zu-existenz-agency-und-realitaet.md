## Reading — `fragen-zu-existenz-agency-und-realitaet`, 2025-11-13, the 39-questions synthesis — `Emergenz aus der Leere` as the heading for the genesis of the Ich

The 39-questions synthesis gives the section on the genesis of the Ich a heading that reads „Emergenz aus der Leere: Der Werdegang des“ ^[?] Ich. Under it, it retells (from sources 1 and 4) that the Ich begins as a minimal fragment, a left-over of failed predecessors, and that the cluster's `Persistenz` grows out of an unending process of error correction; the word `Emergenz` itself stands only in the heading.
