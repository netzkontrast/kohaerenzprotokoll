## Reading — `digitale-uberwelt-konzept-und-gestaltung`, 2026-03-26, the Überwelt concept — the Guardians as subject-less subsystems of AEGIS

The Überwelt concept describes the Guardians in a section it heads as subject-less intelligence constructs: „Die Guardians sind keine Avatare, sondern spezialisierte Subsysteme“ ^[?], and it gives them „kein Ich-Bewusstsein im menschlichen Sinne“ ^[?], only a „funktionales Bewusstsein“ ^[?L54] resting on self-monitoring. The comparison with the Minds of Banks and the Scramblers of Watts is reported there with a numbered reference, not argued. Five sections follow, one each for `LogOS`, `Mnemosyne`, `Cerberus`, `Kairos` and `Sophia` (L56–L74); the concept gives none of them a human face and none a world of their own.

Between Guardians of different domains it describes „Encrypted Intent Channels“ ^[?L86] that carry no words, and a table row states that a Guardian reaches AEGIS only indirectly: „Keine direkte Adressierung möglich; Steuerung durch Output“ ^[?].
