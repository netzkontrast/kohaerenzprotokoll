## Reading — `digitale-uberwelt-konzept-und-gestaltung`, 2026-03-26, the Überwelt concept — Alters as parts the system treats as data nodes

The Überwelt concept writes the plural once, in a parenthesis: „Die verschiedenen Persönlichkeitsanteile“ ^[?] are treated by the system as separate data nodes or speakers (L100). It gives no count and names no alter there. When parts send commands at once, the Risse arise: „Wenn verschiedene Persönlichkeitsanteile gleichzeitig gegensätzliche Befehle an das System senden“ ^[?] (L104). Three are named in the glitch list: Nyx, Kiko, Moros (L106 to L108).
