## 2026-10-06 — `digitale-uberwelt-konzept-und-gestaltung`, 2026-03-26, the Überwelt concept

**The Überwelt concept describes five Guardians, each a subsystem of AEGIS, and pairs none of them with a world.**

It gives each its own section, in order `LogOS` (L56), `Mnemosyne` (L60), `Cerberus` (L64), `Kairos` (L68) and `Sophia` (L72), and calls the group „spezialisierte Subsysteme“ ^[?L54]. Sophia „empfängt und verarbeitet die aggregierten Datenströme aller anderen Guardians“ ^[?]. The only contact with a world is the name: the administrative centre is „auch Logos-Prime oder Konstrukt-Stadt genannt“ ^[?], and the concept does not say there that LogOS belongs to it.

Stands on the five-Guardian side by the count of its sections, with no pairing stated; recorded, not applied, and the record's rows are not changed.
