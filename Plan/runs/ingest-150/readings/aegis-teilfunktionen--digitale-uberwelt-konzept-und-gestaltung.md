## Reading — `digitale-uberwelt-konzept-und-gestaltung`, 2026-03-26, the Überwelt concept — SIS and the Zero-Trust Environment Mandate as protocols of the Überwelt

The Überwelt concept describes `SIS` in a protocol table as „Systemische Isolation“ ^[?], with the effect „Vollständiges Verschwinden von Sektoren in der Schwärze“ ^[?]. In the Cerberus section it spells the name out: Cerberus „setzt das Systemic Isolation Shield (SIS) um“ ^[?] when data nodes are rated incurably corrupt (L66). Communication between the entities stands under „den strengen Vorgaben des Zero-Trust Environment Mandate“ ^[?]. Of a Cognitive Firewall as a sub-function of AEGIS the concept says only that Cerberus perceives „kognitive Firewalls“ ^[?] as walls (L66).
