## Reading — `digitale-uberwelt-konzept-und-gestaltung`, 2026-03-26, the Überwelt concept — LogOS as the system's constructed logic

The Überwelt concept opens its LogOS section: „LogOS ist die personifizierte (oder vielmehr konstruierte) Logik des Systems“ ^[?]. In LogOS's perception the Überwelt consists of mathematical constructs; it acts as the primary filter for incoming data streams, and a fault is for it „eine zu korrigierende Inkonsistenz in einer Gleichung“ ^[?]. Over the Encrypted Intent Channels it sends no verbal warning: „Wenn LogOS Cerberus anweist, einen Sektor zu isolieren“ ^[?], it sends a mathematical construct instead. The concept also gives the name `Logos-Prime` to the administrative centre (L50), without saying that LogOS resides there.
