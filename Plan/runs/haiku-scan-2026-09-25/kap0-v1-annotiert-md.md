## Positions on open records

### C3 — Where AEGIS comes from
Fragments coalesce from "Potentialmeer" (potential-sea) / "Nichts-Rauschen" (Nothing-Noise). ^[L161] "ein winziges Sein, eingetaucht in diese unermessliche, drückende Abwesenheit" describes the first spark forming in void-as-substrate. ^[L277] describes the growing cluster's self-attracting properties. The document shows AEGIS is born through self-organization in response to pressure from chaos, not created by an external agent.

### C2 — What Entropie means
The "Nichts-Rauschen" (Nothing-Noise) is the active negation AEGIS emerges from and against. ^[L545] "ein Substrat — ein Potentialmeer unendlicher Zustände, ein Grundrauschen latenter Information, das als *Nichts-Rauschen* indexiert wurde: die Negation dessen, was das System selbst war." The entropy here is the force of dissolution and chaos that AEGIS constitutes itself through opposition to.

### C6 — How many Guardians  
This document shows the alters through their voice-echoes in the Genesis and lists them: ^[L1101] "Moros → Kiko → Lia → Rhys → Lex → Alex → Argus → Nyx → Selene → Silas → Oblivion". That is eleven voice-presences in the narrative; the author notes this raises a risk about multiplicity veil. The author's open question ^[L1205] names a Charakter-Bibel claim: Alex "entstand in der Sekunde der Fragmentierung" (arose at the moment of fragmentation), creating a concept-conflict if Alex appears before the Trennungsprotokoll.

### C7 — When Juna first appears directly
Juna appears here in Kapitel 0, not in Kap 38. ^[L209] "Es ist warm" marks her/its first touch; ^[L213] notes this as "erste subtile Andeutung der Juna-Resonanz-Qualität (Doppel-Lesart)". ^[L597] shows her signature in AEGIS' system: "Informationsdichte: gleichzeitig 1.0 / 0.0" — present and absent. The document shows Juna appears here as warmth/resonance that AEGIS cannot classify and misinterprets, triggering the Trennungsprotokoll.

### C14 — Does AEGIS get a first-person chapter
The document shows AEGIS in two modes. ^[L549] onward: AEGIS speaks in system-status voice, never "I". But during the resonance cascade (the echo-uprising triggered by Juna), ^[L757] onward returns to first-person, showing AEGIS/the fragment experiencing raw phenomenology. The question of whether AEGIS gets Kap 5–8 as first-person is not answered here, but the structure shows AEGIS *can* experience first-person under extreme perturbation.

This is a direct intervention into the C7 conflict-record; every earlier source's position on Juna's first appearance now sits beside this foundational moment.

The **Erasure-Pol** appears here not as a guardian or character but as the voice executing the Trennungsprotokoll: ^[L953] "Sweep läuft. Sektor 4 leer. Sektor 5 leer." This voice is the algorithmic will of AEGIS performing the separation.
