## Reading — `analyse-und-uberarbeitung-des-gesamtplots-mit-subplots`, 2025-05-02, the subplot revision — a proposal to turn AEGIS from a generic control AI into a system with a specific flawed logic

The subplot revision is an unsigned German analysis of an earlier plot draft that proposes a rewrite of the plot as 39 one-line chapters; it marks nothing as canon. For AEGIS it plans a change of kind. In its analysis, the subplot Lex' Systemanalyse & AEGIS' Paradoxon is said to transform AEGIS „von einer generischen Kontroll-KI zu einem komplexen System mit einer spezifischen, fehlerhaften Logik“ ^[?]. That logic is named in the line, in the document's own inner quotes, which are left out here; the same line says it supplies concrete weaknesses.

The chapter lines then place AEGIS step by step, each tagged with subplot numbers that are left out here:

- Part 1, Kap 1, `Der Glitch im Spiegel`: the first glitches are tied to Subplot 4 and KW1 is called „als AEGIS-Domäne“ ^[?].
- Part 1, Kap 8, `Die Logik des Gaslichts`: „AEGIS (LogOS, Mnemosyne) nutzt Kaels Schwächen für Gaslighting“ ^[?].
- Part 1, Kap 9, `An Mauern der Grenzfeste`: the line adds „AEGIS' direkte Kontrolle“ ^[?].
- Part 2, Kap 14, `Das Echo im Code`: „Kael erkennt AEGIS als steuernde Instanz“ ^[?].
- Part 2, Kap 17, `Der Fehler in der Maschine`: „Kael (Lex/Argus) entdeckt AEGIS' Kernparadoxon“ ^[?].
- Part 2, Kap 23, `Preis des Wissens`: „AEGIS eskaliert Kontrolle (Gaslighting, Guardians)“ ^[?].
- Part 3, Kap 27, `Eintritt in die Höhle des Löwen`: Kael „dringt in AEGIS-Kern/Überwelt ein“ ^[?].
- Part 3, Kap 28, `Der Spießrutenlauf der Paradoxien`: Kael „navigiert AEGIS-Verteidigung (Paradoxien)“ ^[?].
- Part 3, Kap 29, `Die Versuchung der Ordnung`: „AEGIS bietet Handel an“ ^[?].
- Part 3, Kap 32, `Das System zerfällt`: „Kaels/Juna/Vs Aktionen lösen Kollaps AEGIS-Kontrolle aus“ ^[?].
- Part 3, Kap 36, `Der Fall des Wächters`: „AEGIS besiegt/verändert“ ^[?], with the paradox's resolution listed in the same line.

The Kap 36 line offers two verbs, defeated or changed, and the document does not choose between them. It states no further account of what AEGIS is after that chapter; this is a plan to be applied or not by the author, and it is recorded here, not applied.
