## Reading — `subplot-entwicklung-fuer-romanstruktur`, 2025-05-02, the subplot catalogue — an integration or Self part that may facilitate co-consciousness

The subplot catalogue places `Selene` as the integrating part: „Selene (Integration/Selbst)“ ^[?L308], in Kapitel 12 the facilitator of co-consciousness. For Kapitel 11 it says the prompt takes Selene as an analogy to the Self of IFS: „Sowohl TSDP als auch IFS“ ^[?L284]. Kapitel 5 names her potential among inner allies, „Auftauchen des Potenzials von Selene/Argus“ ^[?L133].

Kapitel 12, the Heilige Hochzeit, proposes the union of opposites, mediated, as the catalogue puts it, by the „Selbst-ähnlichen Selene-Anteil“ ^[?L309]. A subplot idea there begins „Ermöglicht durch Selene (Integration/Selbst)“ ^[?L323] an internal council of the parts.
