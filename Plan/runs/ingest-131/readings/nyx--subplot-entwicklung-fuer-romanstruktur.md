## Reading — `subplot-entwicklung-fuer-romanstruktur`, 2025-05-02, the subplot catalogue — a fighter EP in the subplot catalogue

The subplot catalogue names `Nyx` as an EP of the fighter type: „Nyx (Kämpfer EP) übernimmt gewaltsam die Kontrolle“ ^[?L123] when Alex's protective measures fail, in a subplot idea for Kapitel 4 (possibly in KW3, as the catalogue words it). In Kapitel 5 it considers that an EP such as Lia „oder sogar Nyx, der defensiv“ ^[?L149] acts in a way that helps Kael by accident.

In Kapitel 10 Nyx could take a contact attempt as a threat: „Nyx (Kämpfer EP)“ ^[?L273] lashes out inwardly. In Kapitel 13 the catalogue names him with the coordinated system: „Panik (Kiko), Wut (Nyx) oder logischer Blockade (Lex)“ ^[?L359] would earlier have been the reaction.
