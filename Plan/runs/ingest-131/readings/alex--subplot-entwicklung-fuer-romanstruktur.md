## Reading — `subplot-entwicklung-fuer-romanstruktur`, 2025-05-02, the subplot catalogue — a protector ANP, from the warning of Kapitel 1 to a safe inner space in Kapitel 11

In the subplot catalogue `Alex` is the protector among the ANPs. In Kapitel 1 the first subplot ideas have him warning: „Alex, der Protektor, warnt Lex davor, Anomalien zu ignorieren“ ^[?L49]. In Kapitel 2 he checks the perimeter: „Alex (Protektor ANP) wird hypervigilant und scannt aktiv die Umgebung“ ^[?L74], possibly in KW1 or KW3, and the catalogue adds that he could implement security protocols that isolate the EPs further.

Kapitel 10 gives him a position in the inner debate: „Alex (Protektor) argumentiert dagegen basierend auf Sicherheit“ ^[?L274]. In Kapitel 11 he turns from suppression to containment: „Alex erkennt, dass einfache Unterdrückung versagt hat“ ^[?L299]. All of this is proposed by the catalogue as possible subplots.
