## Reading — `subplot-entwicklung-fuer-romanstruktur`, 2025-05-02, the subplot catalogue — a collapse EP who may surface in despair

The subplot catalogue names `Moros` as the EP of collapse: „Moros (Kollaps EP) könnte an die Oberfläche treten.“ ^[?L199] The line stands in a subplot idea of the existential crisis, where a large glitch destroys the illusion of control. In Kapitel 8 the core trauma is held by the primary EPs, „wahrscheinlich Kiko, Lia, vielleicht Moros“ ^[?L208], and in Kapitel 9 the sight of an integrated individual „Riskiert die Auslösung von Verzweiflung/Moros EP“ ^[?L250], as the catalogue takes from the prompt for the EP.
