## Reading — `subplot-entwicklung-fuer-romanstruktur`, 2025-05-02, the subplot catalogue — four worlds KW1 to KW4, named and read as inner landscapes

The subplot catalogue writes the worlds as KW1 to KW4 and names three in parentheses and one in a guess: KW1 as „KW1 (Logos-Prime)“ ^[?], KW2 as „KW2 - Mnemosyne-Archipel“ ^[?], KW3 as „KW3 - Cerberus-Labyrinth“ ^[?] and KW4 as „KW4 - Kairos-Potentialis“ ^[?], the last marked with vielleicht. A Kapitel 13 idea gives the four by theme: „KW1 (Logik) wirkt vielleicht weniger steril“ ^[?], with KW2 Emotion/Erinnerung, KW3 Abwehr and KW4 Potenzial. The catalogue writes the hyphenated `Kern-Welten`, never `Kernwelt`.

In Kapitel 15, as the catalogue takes from the prompt: „Die Kern-Welten von AEGIS sind psychologische Landschaften, die Kaels Innenleben spiegeln“ ^[?]. A Kapitel 2 idea leaves Kael's location a guess: „wahrscheinlich KW1 oder KW3“ ^[?]. The catalogue pairs each world with a Guardian only as proposals (see `guardians`).
