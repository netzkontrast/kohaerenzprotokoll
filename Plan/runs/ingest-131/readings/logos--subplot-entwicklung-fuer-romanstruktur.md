## Reading — `subplot-entwicklung-fuer-romanstruktur`, 2025-05-02, the subplot catalogue — LogOS as KW1's Guardian

The subplot catalogue pairs LogOS with the first world, KW1 (Logos-Prime): Lex tries to solve a task there by logic alone, and „LogOS (Guardian) könnte dies unbeteiligt beobachten“ ^[?]. Later ideas use LogOS as an example Guardian: one has him deliver 'useful' data, another has him give a directive that contradicts an earlier one, and Kapitel 17 puts him beside Sophia as a possible Guardian: „Kael konfrontiert einen Guardian (vielleicht LogOS oder Sophia)“ ^[?].
