## Reading — `romanidee-als-interaktiver-prototyp`, 2025-08-05, the CAVE prototype proposal — four Kernwelten created by AEGIS to analyse Kael; a game stability variable

The prototype proposal reports, from its outline, the four Kernwelten: „die vier Kernwelten (KW1: Logos-Prime, KW2: Mnemosyne-Archipel, KW3: Cerberus-Labyrinth, KW4: Kairos-Potentialis)“ ^[?]. It reports: „AEGIS hat die Kernwelten explizit geschaffen, um Kael zu analysieren, zu verstehen und zu kontrollieren“ ^[?], and that they are functional environments, a kind of laboratory. The SIS protocol is said to seal off „ganze Kernwelten abschottet“ ^[?] on incoherence.

The prototype proposal plans, for the game, a variable `World.Stability.KW[1-4]` for „Die ontologische Stabilität und Integrität jeder der vier Kernwelten“ ^[?].
