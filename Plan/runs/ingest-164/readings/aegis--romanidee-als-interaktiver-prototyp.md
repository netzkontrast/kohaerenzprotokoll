## Reading — `romanidee-als-interaktiver-prototyp`, 2025-08-05, the CAVE prototype proposal — AEGIS as a tragic, trauma-structured system, reported from its outline; one game variable planned

The prototype proposal reports, from its outline, AEGIS as „eine autopoietische, informationsbasierte Intelligenz“ ^[?] whose purpose is coherence, and as „keine simple, böswillige KI“ ^[?]. It reports the flaw driving AEGIS as the „Paradoxon der Fehlausgerichteten Kohärenz“ ^[?L32], whose instabilities show in the world as Risse. It reports that AEGIS' genesis „ist eine Form der traumatischen Fragmentierung“ ^[?], and that AEGIS has the Kernwelten created „explizit geschaffen, um Kael zu analysieren, zu verstehen und zu kontrollieren“ ^[?]. It reports that AEGIS reads Kael's integration as „maximale Entropie“ ^[?].

The proposal's own interpretation, put forward as „eine tiefere Interpretation“ ^[?], is that „AEGIS' gesamte Architektur ist eine systematisierte Trauma-Antwort“ ^[?].

The prototype proposal plans, for the game, a state value `AEGIS.System.Integrity` for the stability of AEGIS' coherence protocol: „Die Stabilität von AEGIS' eigenem Kohärenzprotokoll“ ^[?]. This is the game's variable, not a statement about AEGIS in the world.
