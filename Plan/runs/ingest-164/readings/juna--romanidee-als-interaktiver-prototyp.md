## Reading — `romanidee-als-interaktiver-prototyp`, 2025-08-05, the CAVE prototype proposal — an external anomaly, and the game's connection variable

Reporting its outline, the proposal says: „Juna/V hingegen ist eine externe Anomalie“ ^[?], and that the link to Kael is „ist nicht-lokal und akausal, basierend auf Konzepten wie Quantenverschränkung und prozessphilosophischer Resonanz“ ^[?]. The potential must be earned: „Ihr Potenzial muss durch Kaels eigene psychologische Entwicklung“ ^[?] activated.

Its own reading is that the connection brings another physics into the game: „Die Juna/V-Verbindung führt jedoch eine fundamental andere, nicht-lokale und akausale“ ^[?] one. For the game it plans the variable `JunaV.Connection.Strength`, the strength of the link between Kael and the external anomaly — the prototype's state, not a reading of Juna.
