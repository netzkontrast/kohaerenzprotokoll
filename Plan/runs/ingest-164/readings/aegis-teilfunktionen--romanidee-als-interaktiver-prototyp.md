## Reading — `romanidee-als-interaktiver-prototyp`, 2025-08-05, the CAVE prototype proposal — ZTEM, RTSV and SIS named as AEGIS' protocols and read as trauma defences

The prototype proposal reports, from its outline, that AEGIS is steered by core protocols: „Zero Trust Environment Mandate (ZTEM)“ ^[?] and „Recursive Trust Signature Verification (RTSV)“ ^[?], which „Vertrauen als epistemische Schwachstelle eliminieren“ ^[?]. The proposal's own interpretation reads them as defences: „ist eine perfekte Manifestation von Hypervigilanz“ ^[?] for ZTEM, and for SIS the line says „Das SIS-Protokoll (Systemic Isolation Shield), das bei Inkohärenz ganze Kernwelten abschottet“ ^[?]. The proposal's own gloss is that this corresponds to dissociation.
