## Reading — `konzeptionelle-transzendenz-fuer-kohaerenz-protokoll`, 2025-05-04, the Konzeptionelle-Transzendenz report — functional multiplicity as a possibly limited endpoint

The directive, as the report puts it, asks for states that go „über konventionelle Integration oder funktionale Multiplizität hinausgehen“ ^[?]. The report acknowledges integration or functional multiplicity as standard goals of trauma therapy and treats them, in the project's context, „als potenziell limitierte Endpunkte betrachtet“ ^[?]; its own goal is transcendence. It offers models beyond both and chooses none.
