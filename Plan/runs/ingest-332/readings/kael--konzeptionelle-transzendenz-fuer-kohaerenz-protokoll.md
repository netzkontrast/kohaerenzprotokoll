## Reading — `konzeptionelle-transzendenz-fuer-kohaerenz-protokoll`, 2025-05-04, the Konzeptionelle-Transzendenz report — models and states beyond integration, offered not chosen

The report starts from „Die bisherige Charakterisierung von Kael als multiples Bewusstsein“ ^[?] and offers extrapolations. It links Kael's inner state to the Fundament: „Kaels interne Fragmentierung“ ^[?] mirrors a potential fragmentation within the Fundament itself (Konzept 4, a collective psyche, as a possibility).

It offers three models of consciousness, as alternatives: Modell 1, „Emergentes Meta-Bewusstsein“ ^[?]; Modell 2, „Verteiltes/Vernetztes Bewusstsein“ ^[?]; Modell 3, „Parallele Verarbeitung/Spezialisierte Kognition“ ^[?]. Of the transformative states it names first Zustand 1, „Direkte Fundament-Schnittstelle“ ^[?]. It also states a cost: „Eine extreme Machtzunahme kann die narrative Spannung“ ^[?] and the identification with the character reduce, so a chosen state needs built-in limits. The report chooses none.
