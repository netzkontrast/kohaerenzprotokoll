## Reading — `physik-fuer-simulierte-realitaet`, 2025-04-26, the simulated-physics report — AEGIS as the steering instance that physical clues might point to

The simulated-physics report proposes that the physics of the novel's simulation can be read as pointing to „einer übergeordneten steuernden Instanz (AEGIS)“ ^[?], which the inhabitants, Kael among them, might notice. Its glossary gives AEGIS as „Eine avancierte Wächter-KI, die die Simulation erschafft und erhält“ ^[?].

In the many-worlds paragraph it proposes: „Vielleicht führt AEGIS multiple Instanzen der Kernwelten parallel aus“ ^[?]. It proposes that decoherence could be the mechanism by which AEGIS enforces the classical rules, and it reads the fine-tuning as design: „die Parameter der Simulation absichtlich so gewählt hat, dass die Kernwelten stabil sind“ ^[?]. In its worldbuilding list it writes AEGIS as „Gärtner der Emergenz?“ ^[?]. These are the report's conditional proposals, recorded, not applied.
