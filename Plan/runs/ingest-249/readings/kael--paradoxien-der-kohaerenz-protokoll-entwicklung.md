## Reading — `paradoxien-der-kohaerenz-protokoll-entwicklung`, 2025-04-20, the paradox report — Kael as the protagonist with DID in a proposed double bind

The paradox report proposes that AEGIS could put Kael in a double bind: „Alternativ könnte AEGIS Kael in einen Double Bind versetzen“ ^[?], with the consequence „Kael kann keine der beiden Anforderungen erfüllen, ohne die andere zu verletzen.“ ^[?] This is a proposed application of a paradox to the novel, not a statement of the world.

The definition the report gives in its appendix, labelled Erweiterter Kontext 3, opens: „Der Protagonist mit Dissoziativer Identitätsstörung“ ^[?]. The report says his essence comes from a Kohärenz-Insel in the Potentialmeer (same line). Of his traits it writes „besitzt eine Verbindung zu Julia/Potentialmeer“ ^[?]; here the report calls the bond's other figure Julia.

On identity it asks, as a question about integration: „Wenn Kael seine Anteile integriert und sich mit Julia verbindet“ ^[?], is he still the same Kael? The line puts it as a question about the fluidity of identity against AEGIS's pursuit of static coherence.
