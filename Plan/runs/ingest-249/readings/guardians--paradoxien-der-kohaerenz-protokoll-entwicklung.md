## Reading — `paradoxien-der-kohaerenz-protokoll-entwicklung`, 2025-04-20, the paradox report — the Guardians as AEGIS's specialised agents with blind spots

The appendix of the paradox report gives, labelled Erweiterter Kontext 2, the definition „Spezialisierte Agenten/Subroutinen von AEGIS“ ^[?], which operate as local instances of the protocols in the Kernwelten; the label marks the report's account of that text. Their function is „Überwachung und Steuerung der Kernwelten“ ^[?].

Their limit is given as „Ihre interne Komplexität reicht nicht aus, um die Komplexität von Kael/Julia zu modellieren.“ ^[?] The report names Julia in this line. They also „melden Zustände an die Überwelt“ ^[?].

In the stage-5 entry on control the report proposes that every action of the Guardians to repair the Kernwelten leads to larger Risse; that is its application, not a world statement, and the line before it reads „Je mehr AEGIS versucht, Kontrolle zu erlangen, desto mehr entgleitet sie ihm“ ^[?]. In the table of a possible structure it lists „Guardian (Logik), Kern-Welt 1“ ^[?].
