## Reading — `paradoxien-der-kohaerenz-protokoll-entwicklung`, 2025-04-20, the paradox report — the Potentialmeer as origin of AEGIS and Kael

In its appendix the paradox report gives a definition, labelled Genesis 1.1, of the Potentialmeer as „Eine fundamentale Ebene der Realität jenseits bekannter Physik“ ^[?], and continues „Es ist die Quelle, aus der AEGIS emergierte“ ^[?]; the label marks the report's account of that text. Its function line reads „Ursprung von AEGIS und Kael“ ^[?] and adds „und die Quelle der K-J-Verbindung“ ^[?].

On interaction the report writes „AEGIS versucht, sich vom Chaos des Meeres abzugrenzen“ ^[?]. In the report's earlier entry on the information paradox of black holes, the Potentialmeer appears as one place the paradox might touch; the report there proposes it as metaphor, not as a world fact.
