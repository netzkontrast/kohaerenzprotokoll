## Reading — `paradoxien-der-kohaerenz-protokoll-entwicklung`, 2025-04-20, the paradox report — the Kernwelten as AEGIS's attempt to divide Kael's psyche

The appendix of the paradox report gives, labelled Erweiterter Kontext 2, the definition „AEGIS' Versuch, Kael's Psyche“ ^[?] to divide into seemingly separate subsystems (the line names Logik, Emotion, Abwehr, Potential), as the report's account of that text. On their rules it writes „sie sind jedoch nicht wirklich getrennt, sondern miteinander verbunden“ ^[?], which, the line adds, contradicts AEGIS's model; the same line calls them susceptible to Risse and instability.

Its interaction line has „Guardians überwachen sie“ ^[?]. The report's stage-5 entry on vagueness puts the Risse at the borders: the line has „zwischen den Kernwelten“ ^[?L150]. The table of a possible 39-step structure (introduced as a sketch) pairs a Guardian with the first Kern-Welt: „Guardian (Logik), Kern-Welt 1“ ^[?].
