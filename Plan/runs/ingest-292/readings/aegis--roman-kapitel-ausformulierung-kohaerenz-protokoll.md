## Reading — `roman-kapitel-ausformulierung-kohaerenz-protokoll`, 2025-04-18, the chapter-1 guide — AEGIS named with LogOS as a presence the city is to show

The chapter-1 guide prescribes that the presence of both systems show in the order of the city: „Die Präsenz von Systemen wie AEGIS und LogOS“ ^[?] is to manifest itself in ubiquitous order and efficiency, and the city is to read as a monitoring mechanism: „Die Stadt selbst wirkt wie ein Überwachungsmechanismus, der Konformität erzwingt und Individualität unterdrückt“ ^[?]. In the section on Juna the guide lists, among the possible causes of her distance, „eine systemische Vorgabe von AEGIS/LogOS“ ^[?], offered as one option in a question. A table row ties a feeling of being watched to the two systems only with a question mark: „Subtiles Gefühl, beobachtet zu werden (LogOS, AEGIS?)“ ^[?]. A guide, not the chapter; the bracketed pointers beside these lines report other texts and are not quoted here.
