## Reading — `genesis-ein-implementierungsleitfaden-prosa-version`, 2025-04-29, the Genesis implementation guide — dissociation as the trauma lens

The Genesis implementation guide offers dissociation as the lens for the Ich and for Kael: the Ich's later state is read through trauma theory, and dissociation is, in the guide's words, a coping strategy: „Dies ist keine Fehlfunktion, sondern ein adaptiver Überlebensmechanismus“ ^[?]. The same line lists the extreme case, fragmentation of the self into dissociated parts, and names DID among its examples. A borrowed lens in a direction book, recorded, not applied.
