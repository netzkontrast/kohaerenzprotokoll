## Reading — `kohaerenz-direktive-narratives-protokoll`, 2026-02-25, the narrative directive — AEGIS as the physical eraser and the pole of order

The narrative directive prescribes, under its Landauer mechanic, that AEGIS deleting Kael's traumatic memories is a physical act: „Wenn AEGIS versucht, Kaels traumatische Erinnerungen (Juna) zu löschen, ist dies ein physischer Akt.“ ^[?] The first narrative level is headed „Die Konstrukt-Stadt (Ordnung / AEGIS)“ ^[?], so the directive assigns order and AEGIS to that level. Its checklist asks of each scene „Wie reagiert AEGIS?“ ^[?] and names as options „(Fehlermeldung, Rekalibrierung, physische Blockade)“ ^[?]. These are instructions to the writer, recorded, not applied.
