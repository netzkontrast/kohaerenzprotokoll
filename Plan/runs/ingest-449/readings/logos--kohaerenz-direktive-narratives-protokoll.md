## Reading — `kohaerenz-direktive-narratives-protokoll`, 2026-02-25, the narrative directive — the rule that LogOS may never understand Juna

The narrative directive sets a rule for LogOS: „LogOS darf Juna niemals direkt verstehen.“ ^[?] LogOS can only measure the effects of Juna, and the directive names the horror for LogOS as meeting a truth LogOS cannot compute: „Der Horror für LogOS ist die Begegnung mit einer Wahrheit, die er nicht berechnen kann.“ ^[?] The rule is the directive's own and is recorded, not applied.
