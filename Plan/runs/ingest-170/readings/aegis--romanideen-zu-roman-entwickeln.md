## Reading — `romanideen-zu-roman-entwickeln`, 2025-10-15, the master blueprint — AEGIS as the exclusion logic and an externalised trauma logic, with its own voice and a perverse learning loop

The master blueprint directs that the novel's core conflict set AEGIS's „Kohärenz durch Ausschluss“ ^[?] against Kael's integration. It requires that every action of AEGIS be shown as a necessary consequence of that logic: „muss als direkte und unausweichliche Konsequenz seiner auf Ausschluss basierenden Logik dargestellt werden“ ^[?].

On AEGIS's nature the blueprint reports its sources: „Die Quelldokumente beschreiben AEGIS treffend als“ ^[?] „externalisierten Täterintrojekt“ ^[?]. In the blueprint's own words its logic is not to be read as plain malice but as „eine externalisierte Form einer Trauma-Logik“ ^[?].

For the prose it directs: „AEGIS' Stimme muss kalt, klinisch, technisch und distanziert sein.“ ^[?]

In Act I it proposes an interlude from AEGIS's perspective in which the glitch is misread as „unbedeutende Systeminterferenz“ ^[?] or „post-reboot fatigue“ ^[?], answered by a subtle act of gaslighting. In Act II it describes a loop in which each of Kael's steps toward integration is misinterpreted and attacked, and „Der Antagonist wird so, ohne es zu wollen, zum Katalysator für die Heilung des Protagonisten.“ ^[?]

After the Act III climax the blueprint proposes that AEGIS must give up its core axiom and switch „in einen parakonsistenten logischen Modus zu wechseln“ ^[?], a „systemischer Schlaganfall“ ^[?]; AEGIS remains a „Zombie-System“ ^[?L131] that processes Kael's truth without feeling it.
