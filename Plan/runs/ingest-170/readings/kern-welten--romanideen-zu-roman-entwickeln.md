## Reading — `romanideen-zu-roman-entwickeln`, 2025-10-15, the master blueprint — the Kernwelten as externalised therapy and as narrative mechanics

For Act II the master blueprint proposes: „Kaels Reise ist ein externalisierter therapeutischer Prozess.“ ^[?] It then lists KW2 `Mnemosyne-Archipel` and KW3 `Cerberus-Labyrinth`, where each „Sicherheitstür“ ^[?] demands the cooperation of two formerly hostile alters. Among its weaving techniques it directs: „Die Regeln der Kernwelten werden zu narrativen Mechaniken.“ ^[?] In the Cerberus-Labyrinth, it proposes, „könnten die Rätsel tatsächlich NP-schwere Probleme sein“ ^[?]. The blueprint names these worlds only in this way; it gives no list of four.
