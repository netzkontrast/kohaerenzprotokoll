## Reading — `romanideen-zu-roman-entwickeln`, 2025-10-15, the master blueprint — Kael's integration set against AEGIS's exclusion, his voice and his place in the three acts

The master blueprint proposes the conflict as a confrontation of „Kohärenz durch Integration“ ^[?] (Kael) with AEGIS's exclusion (L30). It directs that Kael's healed mind become a system „das in der Lage ist, wahre Widersprüche zu akzeptieren und zu halten“ ^[?], and that each inner achievement be staged as an act of integration (L32). It calls his healing „zu einem ontologischen Akt der Rebellion“ ^[?].

On voice, it directs: „Sie beginnt in einem fragmentierten, dissoziativen Zustand“ ^[?], and that in the course of his healing the style turn a polyphonic Wir-perspective of his alters (L48); it reads this as a performance of the Integrierte Informationstheorie (L50).

In the table it consolidates he is „Kael (Host)“ ^[?], classed „ANP (Host/Sucher) - Verantwortlich für das tägliche Leben“ ^[?]. For Act I it proposes: „Kael erwacht als Host, der sich für den einzigen“ ^[?] `Ich` hält, the host who takes himself for the only `Ich` (L96). For Act III it proposes a Kael „der nun als integriertes“ ^[?] `Wir` agiert, confronting AEGIS in the Überwelt (L129), and in the resolution „Kael übernimmt die Rolle des“ ^[?] Gardener (L132).
