# Candidates — angst-bei-komplexen-traumafolgen

Written by hand while reading, **before any counting**. One line per
candidate, nothing else. This is the only artifact of the run a program
cannot produce, and the baseline any model gets scored against.

Read in parts of about fifty file lines, each part listed before the next was opened. The profile said 25 headings, 15 table rows, 80 glued reference numbers and 64 backslash escapes, and no invisible characters; the document turned out to be a clinical review in German, with English technical terms in ASCII quotation marks, and a numbered reference list at its end. It names nothing of a fictional world; every candidate is a clinical or neuroscientific term the document defines, quotes, heads a section with, or abbreviates. Borrowed frameworks that the document applies to the anxiety picture stand under the lens heading at the bottom, and the persons the document names as authors of a framework stand in the main list. Phrases were asked of `read.py --find` before they went on the list, so `innerer Kritiker` (refused) is written `inneren Kritiker` and `innere Kritiker`, the two forms the document has.
