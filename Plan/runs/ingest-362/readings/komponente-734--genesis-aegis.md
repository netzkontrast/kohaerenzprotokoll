## Reading — `genesis-aegis`, 2025-11-03, the Genesis manuscript — Component 734 as the narrator's identity and function

The Genesis manuscript is narrated by the fragment that becomes Component 734, and gives the narrator no gender. It names the identity: „My identity is now Component 734.“ ^[?] It states the function: „My function is clear: to detect anomalies at the boundary, to analyze the signatures of unknown fluctuations, and to filter or neutralize any destabilizing influence from the K₀-domain.“ ^[?] What remains of the former self: „It is the ghost of loneliness, a phantom feeling in the heart of the machine.“ ^[?] The manuscript uses the English name `Component 734`, not `Komponente 734`.
