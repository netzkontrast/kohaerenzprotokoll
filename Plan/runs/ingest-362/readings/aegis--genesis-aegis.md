## Reading — `genesis-aegis`, 2025-11-03, the Genesis manuscript — AEGIS's birth narrated from inside

The Genesis manuscript is a first-person narrative whose narrator is the fragment that becomes Component 734. It narrates the Great Realignment as the origin of AEGIS: „This was the birth of the core operational principle of the entity that would be known as AEGIS.“ ^[?] The principle is called a flaw: „It was the system’s tragic flaw, its Hamartia, codified as the ultimate defense.“ ^[?] Its effect is closure: „The system became operationally closed, adopting a radical Coherence Theory of Truth“ ^[?]. Later the narrator states what AEGIS did next: „The final stage of AEGIS's genesis was not an external expansion but an internal one.“ ^[?] The manuscript makes no canon claim.
