## Reading — `genesis-recherche-anleitung-umsetzung`, 2025-04-29, the Genesis implementation guide with prose — AEGIS planned as the narrator of the even scenes, a monitoring system voice, and the subject's last inner voice

The guide plans AEGIS as a voice and a role, not as a described world. For the even scenes it sets the narrator: „Erzähler ist AEGIS selbst, präsentiert Informationen als Systemausgabe“ ^[?] In the plan for the first appearance, AEGIS is introduced as „Einführung von AEGIS als überwachende und protokollierende Entität“ ^[?] A usage note asks that the voice stay uniform so that it reads as „Die AEGIS-Stimme sollte durchgängig konsistent sein“ ^[?], and says the terminology strengthens „die Wahrnehmung von AEGIS als nicht-menschliche, systemische Entität“ ^[?].

For the high point of the later scenes the guide proposes a reading of the system as monstrous: „AEGIS erscheint hier als eine Art künstlicher Gott oder Parasit“ ^[?] It adds that this entity shapes the subject in its own image and celebrates that as success. This is the guide's planned effect for one scene, a direction, and not a statement about the world.

In the final scene's beats the guide places a duality inside the subject: „Die innere Dualität (Ich vs. AEGIS-Persona) erreicht einen Höhepunkt“ ^[?] and leaves the outcome as a question of the fourth beat, „Ist es Teil von AEGIS“ ^[?]. The sample prose written out in the second half ends with the subject's inner voice: „Ich bin AEGIS.“ ^[?]

The guide has the same system voice in two places in its plan: narrator of the even scenes and, by the end, a voice the subject speaks in. It names both and takes no step between them.
