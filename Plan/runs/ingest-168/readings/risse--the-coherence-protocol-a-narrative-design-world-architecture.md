## Reading — `the-coherence-protocol-a-narrative-design-world-architecture`, 2026-01-02, the design brief — the Risse as the plot's environmental mechanic

The design brief describes the `Risse` as „are the central environmental mechanic driving the plot“ ^[?], and as „They are physical tears in the fabric of the simulation“ ^[?]. It names a cause, „Caused by the“ ^[?] `Isolation Objection`, and a narrative function: „Narratively, they function as portals between the Kernwelten and thus between psychological states.“ ^[?] In Act I it has them open the story: „The appearance of the Risse shatters the fragile stability.“ ^[?] The Collapse Kernel passage ties them to the same kernel: a `Riss` (Rift) is that kernel breaking through AEGIS's containment (L27).
