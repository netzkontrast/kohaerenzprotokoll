## Reading — `the-coherence-protocol-a-narrative-design-world-architecture`, 2026-01-02, the design brief — the Nichts Rauschen as the roaring nothingness before order

The design brief associates the Collapse Kernel with the `Nichts Rauschen` (Nothingness Noise) and describes it as „a roaring nothingness that constantly erodes the artificial boundaries of AEGIS's simulation“ ^[?]. It places AEGIS's genesis inside it: „a desperate struggle for survival as a fragile information fragment within the chaotic Nichts Rauschen“ ^[?]. Its physics table says that without the protocol's execution reality falls back: „If the protocol stops, reality dissolves back into the“ ^[?] `Nichts Rauschen`. For the prose it directs: „Use language that evokes the unknowable, the incomprehensible, and existential dread when depicting the Genesis-Krise, the Void, and the Nichts Rauschen.“ ^[?]
