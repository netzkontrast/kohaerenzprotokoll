## Reading — `the-coherence-protocol-a-narrative-design-world-architecture`, 2026-01-02, the design brief — the four Kernwelten as diagnostic charts of Kael's mind

The design brief describes the settings as „are not geographical locations“ ^[?]: „They are externalized diagnostic charts of System Kael's mind“ ^[?]. It gives four worlds, each with a domain: „Logos-Prime (The Cage)“ ^[?], „Mnemosyne-Archipel (The Swamp)“ ^[?], „Cerberus-Labyrinth (The Bunker)“ ^[?] and „Kairos-Potentialis (The Garden)“ ^[?]. Of the third it writes „The domain of Nyx and Soren; the state of hypervigilance.“ ^[?], of the second „The domain of Lyra and the EPs; the swamp of the past.“ ^[?], and of the fourth „it is the only world where new things can be created“ ^[?]. The `Risse` are described as portals between them (L124).
