## Reading — `the-coherence-protocol-a-narrative-design-world-architecture`, 2026-01-02, the design brief — the Gödel-Gambit's delivery system and a blind spot in AEGIS's panopticon

The design brief describes the link as the delivery system of the climax: „The Gödel-Gambit is delivered via the Moonshine-Link“ ^[?], „a connection between Kael and an external entity, Juna/V“ ^[?]. Its name, the brief says, derives from Monstrous Moonshine mathematics: „The name comes from“ ^[?] it, linking infinite complexity (the Monster Group, K0) with predictable patterns (modular functions, K1). Its narrative role is „The link serves as a backdoor connection“ ^[?]. As a mechanism it carries „transmitting subjective data (feelings, resonance) rather than algorithmic data packets“ ^[?], and the brief directs that, because of AEGIS's ontological blindness to subjectivity, it is „a fundamental blind spot in AEGIS's panopticon, invisible to its sensors until it is too late“ ^[?]. In the physics table the brief says wavelets are „Used by the Moonshine-Link to transmit subjective information“ ^[?].
