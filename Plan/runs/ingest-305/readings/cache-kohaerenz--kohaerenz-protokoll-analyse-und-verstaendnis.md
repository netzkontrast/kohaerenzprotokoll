## Reading — `kohaerenz-protokoll-analyse-und-verstaendnis`, 2025-04-25, the cache-coherence report — the computer-architecture definition of the term

The cache-coherence report is an unsigned German technical report in the computer-architecture sense of the term. It names no figure, place, event or chapter of the novel, and this reading records its definition without applying it to the novel. The report explains that the phrase „wird im Deutschen primär im Kontext der Computerarchitektur verwendet“ ^[?] and stands for the `Cache Coherence Protocol` of multiprocessor systems. Such protocols are, in its words, mechanisms „die Konsistenz von Daten zu gewährleisten, die in mehreren lokalen Caches gespeichert sind“ ^[?]. Its section on the term in computer science gives the aim „dass alle Prozessoren zu jeder Zeit eine konsistente Sicht auf die Daten im Speicher haben“ ^[?].
