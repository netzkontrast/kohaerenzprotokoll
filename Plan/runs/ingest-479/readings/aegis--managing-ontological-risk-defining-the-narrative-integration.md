## Reading — `managing-ontological-risk-defining-the-narrative-integration`, 2025-11-03, the ontological-risk report — AEGIS as the classical-logic system, its Episteme, its blindness and the climax it must face

The ontological-risk report proposes AEGIS as a system „whose existence is predicated on classical logic where any contradiction necessitates systemic collapse“ ^[?], one that „defines order through the exclusion of paradox“ ^[?]. Its mode of knowing is called Episteme, which „represents AEGIS’s mode of knowing“ ^[?]. The report places the Moonshine-Link outside AEGIS's reach, as something „to which AEGIS is structurally blind“ ^[?]. The seeded artefacts of the proposal are, for AEGIS, noise: „For AEGIS, these events are unclassifiable noise, analogous to its own system failures“ ^[?]. For the climax the report prescribes that Kael presents an integrated self, „a stable, higher form of coherence that AEGIS's axioms cannot process or negate“ ^[?], and that this ends by „forcing its own foundational crisis“ ^[?]. A proposal of this report, recorded, not applied.
