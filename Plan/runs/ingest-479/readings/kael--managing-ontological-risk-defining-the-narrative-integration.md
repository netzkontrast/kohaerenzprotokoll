## Reading — `managing-ontological-risk-defining-the-narrative-integration`, 2025-11-03, the ontological-risk report — Kael's earned Gnosis as the resolution, and the prerequisites and stages the report prescribes

The ontological-risk report proposes that the resolution must not come from outside: an unearned one would „invalidate the themes of psychological integration and earned agency embodied in Kael's journey“ ^[?]. Its counter is to „reframe it as a passive, fundamental principle of reality that Kael must learn to perceive and embody through his own efforts“ ^[?]. Gnosis is named as Kael's mode of knowing: „represents Kael’s mode of knowing: an earned, transformative, and experiential understanding“ ^[?]. Among the prerequisites the report lists „achieving co-consciousness and deliberate cooperation among his internal“ ^[?] alters, and the ability „to hold conflicting yet valid self-states within a single, stable consciousness“ ^[?]. Of the resolution it says „Gnosis is not information“ ^[?] given to Kael but something Kael becomes able to perceive and act on. In its three-stage progression the first stage reads „In his early, fragmented state, Kael experiences the“ ^[?] artefacts as glitches, and the last is „Kael’s final, integrated state of functional multiplicity is the ultimate expression of his earned Gnosis“ ^[?]. All of this is the report's proposal, recorded, not applied.
