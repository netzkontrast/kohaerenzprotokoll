## Reading — `exploring-the-coherence-protocol`, 2025-11-03, the cross-domain coherence essay — the Dual Kernel Theory reported as a speculative theory, then judged metaphysical

The cross-domain coherence essay reports the theory in its section on speculative theories: the Dual Kernel Theory (DKT) „presents a compelling metaphysical synthesis.“ ^[?] It reports two kernels. K₁ is „A pre-spatial, pre-temporal domain of reversible computation“ ^[?], and K₀ is „The domain of irreversible computation, entropy, and information erasure.“ ^[?] Up to here the essay reports what the theory proposes and does not judge it.

Under the label `Critical Analysis` the essay judges. It says the theory's core concepts „are neologisms specific to this framework.“ ^[?] The theory should be understood as „a metaphysical and explanatory architecture rather than a conventional scientific theory.“ ^[?] This verdict is the essay's own, set after its report, and it does not apply the theory to any fiction.
