## Reading — `kapitel-1-generierungsprozess`, 2025-11-25, the chapter-1 report — Kael as ANP in the report's commentary, and as the figure of the chapter text it quotes

Two voices stand in this document, and this reading keeps them apart. The chapter-1 report reads Kael as a psychological counterpart of the physics it sets out: „Der Protagonist Kael ist das psychologische Äquivalent zu dieser physikalischen Spannung.“ ^[?] In the report's commentary he is the ANP of the TSDP: „Kael agiert als ANP.“ ^[?] The same bullet gives him a loss of memory: „Er leidet unter Amnesie bezüglich seiner traumatischen Vergangenheit“ ^[?].

In the chapter text the report quotes, the first sequence is headed by the report's own field line „Ort: Wohneinheit 4-Alpha, Sektor Gamma.“ ^[?] and the narration then opens: „Das Licht kam nicht.“ ^[?] The fourth sequence has Kael looking at his hands: „Die Geometrie seiner Finger löste sich auf.“ ^[?] Further down in the same sequence a voice speaks from the cracked mirror, in the chapter text: „Finde die Naht, Kael.“ ^[?] The narration before it says only that it is „Nicht die Stimme von Nyx.“ ^[?]; no speaker is written for it.

The forecast is the chapter-1 report's proposal for chapter 2, not chapter text: „Kael hat die Ergosphäre“ ^[?] left and crossed the event horizon, in the report's words.

The chapter-1 report is a draft and its analysis; it does not state a canon status for itself.
