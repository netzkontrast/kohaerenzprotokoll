## Reading — `kapitel-1-generierungsprozess`, 2025-11-25, the chapter-1 report — Negentropie as the energy cost of KW1's order

The chapter-1 report introduces Negentropie in its theory section on KW1: the elimination of friction and entropy, with „Es gibt keinen Verfall, keine Abnutzung, keine Geschichte.“ ^[?] It sets the term as the first item of its commentary on the first sequence: „Die Negentropie der sterilen Umgebung“ ^[?]. There it reads what the chapter text describes: „Die Abwesenheit von Staub und Geruch ist ein Beweis für aktive Negentropie.“ ^[?] The same line adds that AEGIS spends massive energy to keep the room in the Kohärenz-Kernel's state. The summary table lists `Sterile Stille` with the cost: „Negentropie / Energieaufwand“ ^[?]. All of this is the report's commentary; the chapter text it quotes does not use the term.
