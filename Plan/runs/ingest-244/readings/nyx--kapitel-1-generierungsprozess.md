## Reading — `kapitel-1-generierungsprozess`, 2025-11-25, the chapter-1 report — Nyx as wrath in the table and as an Emotional Part in the commentary

The chapter-1 report's table lists among the manifestations of the Kollaps-Kernel „Das Trauma / Die Risse:“ ^[?] a series of items that includes the wrath, `Nyx`, in brackets, next to `Leere` (`Moros`). In the chapter text the report quotes, the name arrives in the third sequence as a question: „Wer war Nyx?“ ^[?] The narration of the chapter text gives no origin there.

The report's commentary then reads the impulse of that scene: „Dies ist der Durchbruch eines Emotional Part (EP) gemäß TSDP.“ ^[?] and names it: „Nyx (Nacht/Wut) repräsentiert die“ ^[?] Fight-Response (the line goes on). In the fourth sequence of the chapter text a voice is placed against Nyx: „Nicht die Stimme von Nyx.“ ^[?] That voice has no speaker in the text.
