## Reading — `aegis-manifest-genesis-krise-reboot-2`, 2026-04-27, the AEGIS manifesto, reboot — four subsystems the Gatekeeper delegates to

Section 6.0 of the manifesto (speaking as AEGIS) declares that it acts through „highly specialized, non-anthropomorphic, operationally segregated subsystems designated as the Guardians“ ^[?]. It calls them „These Guardians are fundamental components of the System AEGIS“ ^[?]. They are said to suffer a dissonance named the Wächter-Zwiespalt: „these Guardians occasionally suffer from a mathematical dissonance known as the“ ^[?].

Four are named in section 6 (LogOS, Oblivion, Silas, Isabelle) and again in the final declaration (L287). A validation procedure is assigned to them jointly: „To eradicate this dissonance, the Guardians execute a tri-layered validation matrix against all data input“ ^[?].
