## Observations

Each chapter of Part 1 carries Inhalt, Fokus and (in some chapters) a third field with a different label per chapter, such as Narrative Ziele, Psychologischer Zustand, DID-Darstellung. In Part 3 the fields are Plot, Erzählperspektive, Thematische Kernfrage, Fokus, Subplot-Integration and Reisestufe, repeated per chapter; those are the template, not terms. Part 2 has no chapter list. The chapter numbers are written as „Kapitel 27“ and so on; the export has dropped digits glued to some words, which the find tool shows (KW1 appears as KW there), so KW1 to KW4 are listed as the file writes them. Several chapter titles carry alternatives joined by a slash. The document speaks of a Reisestufe numbering that it itself says deviates in one chapter.
