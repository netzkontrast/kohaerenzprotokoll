## Reading — `kohaerenz-protokoll-konzeptionelle-themen-struktur`, 2025-11-25, the themes exegesis — three Kernwelt themes, no Kernwelt 2

The themes exegesis writes the Kernwelt under three of its themes (themes, not chapters): theme 15 with the concept `Kernwelt 1` (`LogOS`), theme 19 with `Kernwelt 3` (`Cerberus`), and theme 23, on emergence and creative chaos in the Dionysian sense, with the bare concept „Konzept: Kernwelt“ ^[?L257] after the digit 4 glued to the word. A Kernwelt 2 is not written. Of the third it says: „Das Dionysische (Rausch/Chaos) als notwendiges Gegenstück zum Apollinischen“ ^[?]. It names no guardian for any of them.
