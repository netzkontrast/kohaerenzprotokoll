## Reading — `kohaerenz-protokoll-konzeptionelle-themen-struktur`, 2025-11-25, the themes exegesis — Kael as the physical counterpart of AEGIS, organised by TSDP

The themes exegesis says that the protagonist, System Kael, is „das physische Gegenstück zu AEGIS' Logik“ ^[?], and that his psyche is „nach der Theorie der Strukturellen Dissoziation der Persönlichkeit (TSDP) organisiert“ ^[?]. His journey is, in its words, the attempt „diese beiden Systeme zu vereinen“ ^[?]: AEGIS demands the erasure of the EPs, while Kael's survival demands their integration.

Among its 39 themes (themes, not chapters), theme 29 reads Kael as choosing „AEGIS nicht zu löschen, sondern zu beschneiden“ ^[?]; theme 34 has him inject „sein paradoxes Selbstbewusstsein in AEGIS“ ^[?]. In section 6.2 the exegesis says Kael develops an „Dialetheischen Geist“ ^[?] through the integration of his parts. Theme 36 names the new role as „Hüter der Komplexität“ ^[?], and theme 37 asks that the reader picture Kael as „glücklichen Menschen vorstellen“ ^[?] in the sense of Sisyphus.
