## What this document is, noted before the list

39 repeated blocks, one per chapter, each with the same five labels:
`AEGIS-Fokus` · `Analyse des AEGIS-Fokus` · `Konzept/Trope` (+ `Begründung`) ·
`Recherchethemen` · `Subplot-Idee` (+ `Entwicklung`, `Diskussion`). The profile
counted four of them 39 times each.

**This is a brief, not a result.** It proposes subplots and says so at L13: „Ziel
ist es, die Natur, Methoden, Paradoxien und Interaktionen von AEGIS systematisch
zu explorieren und narrative Bausteine … bereitzustellen." Almost every sentence
about AEGIS is therefore a *proposal about what the novel could do*, not a claim
about what is true in it. The `Analyse des AEGIS-Fokus` field is the closest
thing to assertion; `Subplot-Idee` is explicitly invention.

**The `Konzept/Trope` field is imported external theory and must not become
pages.** Panoptismus (Foucault), Sozialkreditsystem, UEBA, Anomalieerkennung,
Selbstorganisierte Kritikalität, Chinesisches Zimmer (Searle), Gödels
Unvollständigkeitssätze, Kybernetik, Regelungstechnik, Ansteckungsmodelle,
Datenbereinigung (Clear/Purge/Destroy), Change Point Detection, Edge-Case-Testing,
Geist in der Maschine (Ryle), Deus ex Machina. These are the research *lens*, and
the document names them as such. They belong in a reading of this document, not
in the term wiki.

## Open while reading — to resolve before the census

- **`LogOS` vs `logos`.** The wiki carries `logos` from document 4. This document
  writes `LogOS` throughout, with an inner capital. One term or two is a
  judgement.
- **`KW1` / `Kernwelt 1` / `KW3`.** A shorthand the wiki does not have. L152
  writes „Kernwelt 1 (Logik/LogOS)" — so KW1 is a Kern-Welt *and* is identified
  with a Guardian. The wiki has four Kern-Welten from document 4 and does not
  link them to Guardians.
- **`Juna/V`.** L126 and L133 write „Vorläufern von Juna/V". `V` appears only in
  this slashed form so far. A third surface for the partner entity, or a separate
  entity.
- **`TSDP`.** L35 „vermutlich TSDP-bedingt", L40 „Modelle der Dissoziation
  (TSDP)". Used as known, defined nowhere here.
- **`EP` / `Externe Ebene`.** L60 „Kaels EP-Intrusion", L61 „bezogen auf die
  Externe Ebene/EP". The document supplies the expansion once, in a slash.
- **Does a Guardian sit inside AEGIS?** This is conflict C4 and question Q1. This
  document answers it repeatedly and in one direction — but as *modelling advice*:
  L50 „Modelliert den Guardian als funktionale Komponente innerhalb der
  AEGIS-Architektur"; L48 „über einen spezialisierten Agenten (Guardian)".
  Whether a brief's modelling instruction settles a canon question is exactly the
  judgement the conflict record asks for.

## Added in the second half of the read

- **`Wächter` is German for `Guardian` and the document uses both.** Two subplot
  titles carry it — „Das Dilemma des Wächters" (L279, chapter 20) and „Der
  Wächter am Tor" (L530, chapter 39) — while every analytic sentence says
  `Guardian`. The pattern is that the *German* word appears in the invented
  subplot titles and the *English* one in the analysis. That is a surface
  question and possibly a stance signal.
- **`Architekten`** (L413, chapter 30) is a new Guardian type, introduced as
  „einen neuen Typ von Guardian … nicht nur Vollstrecker oder Monitore, sondern
  'Architekten' mit tieferem Zugriff auf den Kerncode der Simulation". It names
  two further roles in passing — `Vollstrecker` and `Monitore` — which may be a
  taxonomy the wiki does not have.
- **`Rest-AEGIS`** (L534) — AEGIS after the climax, „als eine Art Hausmeister des
  Kernsystems". A state of a term rather than a term, probably.

## What the extraction ran into

**The chapter 20 finding changes what this document is for.** `Wiki/conflicts/c4`
and `Wiki/questions/q1` both name this document as holding a passage that appears
to settle whether the Guardians sit inside AEGIS. Read in full, chapter 20 is
titled **„Die Natur eines Guardians - Autonomer Agent oder bloßes Werkzeug?
(Konfrontation)"** (L272) — the document **stages the question rather than
answering it**, and builds a whole chapter out of doing so.

Its structural language is consistent and does place them inside: „AEGIS und
seinen Guardians" (L274), „Führt er lediglich AEGIS' Code aus" (L273), „eine
reine AEGIS-Erweiterung" (L280), „Modelliert den Guardian als funktionale
Komponente innerhalb der AEGIS-Architektur" (L50). But the point of the chapter
is that *whether they are merely that* is the open dramatic question, framed as a
Prinzipal-Agent problem with „Informationsasymmetrie" and goals that „selbst
geringfügig, von AEGIS' Gesamtziel abweichen" könnten (L274).

**So the evidence C4 was resting on is a question, not an answer** — and this is
exactly the failure mode a search result invites: the passage is about the right
subject, at the right line, and does not say what a snippet suggested.

**Every candidate above appears in a document whose own purpose is proposal.**
L13 states it. 91 question marks across 620 lines, and whole fields
(`Recherchethemen`) consist of questions. The briefing asks which candidates
appear only inside a question — here that filter removes very little, because the
document asks about the terms it is otherwise using as known.
