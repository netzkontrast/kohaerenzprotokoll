## Reading — `plotanalyse-kohaerenz-protokoll-szenario`, 2025-04-23, the Plotanalyse — Kael's `Alters` as the parts to be integrated, unnumbered

In its reading of the Monster group's structure the Plotanalyse names Kael's parts `Alters`: the group's „Parias“ outside M stand for the need to integrate all of them, even the difficult ones. The report writes: „Dies kann metaphorisch für die Notwendigkeit stehen,“ ^[?] with the clause continuing on the same line to all aspects of Kael and to restoring M's coherence (L87). In its identity section it asks whether identity is kept when the parts change: „Bleibt die Identität erhalten, wenn die Teile (Alters) sich verändern oder zu einem neuen Ganzen zusammenfügen“ ^[?] The lines cited (L87, L130) name no number of parts.
