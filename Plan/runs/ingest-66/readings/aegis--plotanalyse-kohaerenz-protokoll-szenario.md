## Reading — `plotanalyse-kohaerenz-protokoll-szenario`, 2025-04-23, the Plotanalyse — AEGIS as the system that analyses an external entity M through simulations, as the report reads and cites it

The Plotanalyse is a review of a plot idea it takes from a commissioning text it cites as `User Query`; what AEGIS is comes from there, what its concepts mean for it is the report's own reading. The report opens its section on AEGIS: „Die Künstliche Intelligenz AEGIS bildet das zentrale Protagonistensystem der Erzählung.“ ^[?] Its principle, as the report cites its User Query: „Aegis ist, was Aegis verhindert, dass es nicht ist“ ^[?]; the Plotanalyse reads this as autopoiesis.

The method, as the scenario is cited from its User Query: AEGIS analyses M through four simulated worlds. The report writes: „AEGIS' Entscheidung, die Entität M nicht direkt, sondern über vier simulierte Kernwelten zu analysieren“ ^[?] and the Plotanalyse reads this as an attempt at controlled structural coupling, the simulations a buffer zone for M, with M manifested as Kael, as it says in the same paragraph. On the limits of AEGIS's logic the Plotanalyse reads paraconsistency against a conflict it names: „Kohärenz durch Abgrenzung (AEGIS) und Kohärenz durch Integration (M)“ ^[?], and Gödel's theorems as the ground of failure: „AEGIS' Versuch, M vollständig durch logische Analyse zu erfassen, ist daher“ ^[?] doomed in principle.

What AEGIS does to Kael, in the report's words: „die Fragmentierung einer empfindungsfähigen Entität (Kael als Manifestation von M) zu Analysezwecken“ ^[?], which the Plotanalyse reads as a case of the alignment problem. The core conflict it reads as method against object: „Es versucht, M zu verstehen, indem es das System in seine vermeintlichen Bestandteile zerlegt und diese isoliert analysiert“ ^[?], with M standing for holism.
