Open observations, after the whole read.

The document is a proposal about its own subject and says so. Its three
Setzungen (§VIII) each carry „These", „Konsequenz, falls angenommen" and
„Konsequenz, falls verworfen" — the three repeated labels the profile reports —
and it ends by asking for them to be confirmed (L616). Nothing in it is marked
as decided; the Vermittler-Stimme as Wir-AEGIS-plural is argued, not locked.

It counts the book three ways without flagging it: „39 Kapitel" lie between
Kap 0 and Kap 40 (L29, L588), K0 is where „die 39 Kapitel" play (L149), and the
whole is „die gesamte 41-Kapitel-Architektur" (L124). The arithmetic holds —
Kap 1 to Kap 39 between two frame chapters — but „39 Kapitel dazwischen" (L263)
calls the chapters between the frames 39.

The Genesis has four beats here, and Beat 4 is not in Kap 0 at all: „Beat 4 ist
Kap 39" (L173), „in Kap 39 vollzogen" (L161). Beats 2 and 3 are Kap 0 (L169),
Beat 1 is told nowhere (L165).

Kap 40 (L315, L319, L556) — warmth as the trace of love, before AEGIS' filter.
The document calls it „die Wärme-Spur, die in der Probe vom letzten Turn
vorgeschlagen wurde" (L315): the passage names its own origin as a chat turn.
