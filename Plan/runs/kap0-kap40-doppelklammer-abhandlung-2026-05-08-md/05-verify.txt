# What the count's zeros mean

Four candidates count 0 word 0 in because the reader wrote them in the
nominative, and the document writes them inflected only:
  großer Wandel            -> „großen Wandel" L112, „großen Wandels" L352 L572
  fallendes Glas           -> „fallenden Glas" L112, „fallende Glas" L352 L572
  Beat 2 / Beat 3          -> „Beats 2 und 3" L169 only
  theoretischer Beobachter -> „theoretischen Beobachter" L430 only
The list was committed before the count and is left as written; this is the
correction beside it. A term at 0 is written differently here, not absent.

`Issue Falsehood vs. Truth` and `Issue Fact vs. Fantasy` were read by capture.py
as prose (the period after `vs`); each stands once, L49 and L73.

grep -w treats a hyphen as a word boundary, so the `Wärme` line count above
includes the seven `Wärme-Spur` lines.

No `Guardian`, `Sophia`, `Landauer` or `Ozon` stands in the document.
