## Reading — `romanentwurf-kohaerenz-protokoll-teil-1`, 2025-04-18, the chapter-1 draft — Julia, the early name of Juna, asked about rather than stated

The draft calls her Julia, the early name of Juna (the page's alias); every line below is about her under that name. The research part asks questions about her; the prose sketch shows her at the counter.

In the research part, her distance is read as possibly not coldness: it „könnte nicht aus Gefühlskälte resultieren, sondern aus Zwang oder Zweckmäßigkeit im System“ ^[?]. The draft then asks outright „Ist sie unter AEGIS' Kontrolle?“ ^[?] and does not answer. It adds that „Ihre Formalität könnte eine Maske sein, die einer externen Regel oder einem verborgenen Zweck dient“ ^[?]. All of this is hedged with `könnte`.

In the blueprint she appears as an outer sign: „Julias perfekt beherrschter Ausdruck“ ^[?].

In the prose sketch the narration has her already there: „war Julia bereits da“ ^[?]. Her face is „eine Maske ruhiger Neutralität“ ^[?], and her voice is „klar, melodisch, aber ohne Wärme“ ^[?]. In the evening scene the narration says only „Julia war nicht da“ ^[?].
