## Reading — `romanentwurf-kohaerenz-protokoll-teil-1`, 2025-04-18, the chapter-1 draft — an unrecognised DID, proposed as a hint behind Michael's confusion

The draft's research part, on Michael (the early name of Kael), speaks of „Bei einer Person mit unerkannter DID manifestieren sich“ ^[?] intrusions of other parts as foreign thoughts and feelings. For the novel it proposes that hints at unexpected emotion „auf Michaels DID“ ^[?] point to it, while glitches point to the simulation. On his amnesia it asks whether the confusion is only loss of facts or also emotional, and says the latter „deutet stärker auf die zugrundeliegende DID und emotionale Fragmentierung hin“ ^[?].

The draft does not spell the term out in its own text, and the prose sketch never names it. The research part marks its proposals with `wahrscheinlich` and `könnte`; it is a plan, not a statement of the world.
