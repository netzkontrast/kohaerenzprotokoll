## Reading — `romanentwurf-kohaerenz-protokoll-teil-1`, 2025-04-18, the chapter-1 draft — LogOS named beside AEGIS as what controls the city

In the research part of the chapter-1 draft, an unsigned working draft that proposes and claims no canon, `LogOS` stands in three places and only there. The city's perfection is proposed as „eine direkte Manifestation der Kontrolle durch LogOS und des Ziels von Kern-Welt 1“ ^[?]. Its logic is proposed as „die Logik von AEGIS und LogOS“ ^[?], aimed at system stability and coherence. The summary table gives the first point again in short: „Perfektion als Zeichen der Kontrolle durch LogOS“ ^[?]. The draft says nothing more of what LogOS is; it is named as a controlling system next to AEGIS, and Michael (the draft's early name of Kael) is the figure the perfection is set against.
