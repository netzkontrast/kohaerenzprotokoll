## Reading — `romanentwurf-kohaerenz-protokoll-teil-1`, 2025-04-18, the chapter-1 draft — AEGIS as the logic the city runs on, and a neutral voice

The chapter-1 draft is an unsigned working draft; it proposes and sketches, and claims no canon. In its research part the implications paragraph on logic-dominated worlds proposes that the city's logic „ist nicht zwangsläufig menschliche Logik, sondern die Logik von AEGIS und LogOS, die primär auf Systemstabilität und Kohärenz ausgerichtet ist“ ^[?]. The summary table of the same part gives the short form: „Welt operiert nach AEGIS-Logik“ ^[?]. Michael (the draft's early name of Kael) is the figure this logic is set against in the same lines; the draft asks, and does not answer, whether Julia (the early name of Juna) stands under AEGIS's control. In the prose sketch AEGIS appears once, only as a comparison: the supervisor speaks „seine Stimme ebenso neutral wie die von AEGIS“ ^[?].
