## Reading — `romanentwurf-kohaerenz-protokoll-teil-1`, 2025-04-18, the chapter-1 draft — the city as the aim of Kern-Welt 1

The chapter-1 draft, an unsigned working draft, writes `Kern-Welt` once, in its research part: the city's perfection is proposed as „eine direkte Manifestation der Kontrolle durch LogOS und des Ziels von Kern-Welt 1“ ^[?], and the same sentence calls it a space of rationality and order that after the reboot suppresses chaos and entropy. The draft names no other Kern-Welt and says nothing of how many there are; the city is here the aim of Kern-Welt 1, and its prose sketch never uses the word.
