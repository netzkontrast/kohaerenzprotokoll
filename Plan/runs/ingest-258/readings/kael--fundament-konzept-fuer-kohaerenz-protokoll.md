## Reading — `fundament-konzept-fuer-kohaerenz-protokoll`, 2025-04-28, the Fundament analysis — Kael's integration as the proposal's human-scale mirror

The Fundament analysis states the TSDP model „dient als Grundlage für die Charakterarchitektur von Kael“ ^[?], and that as protagonist Kael „verkörpert Kael durch seine TSDP-basierte Struktur den Kampf um psychische Integration“ ^[?]. In its consistency check it judges that the TSDP basis „bietet eine konkrete psychologische Grundlage für das Integrationsthema“ ^[?].

Its central proposal for the new entity is a parallel: Kael's struggle for inner coherence „spiegelt somit die Struktur der ultimativen Realität wider“ ^[?], and „Sein Erfolg oder Misserfolg bei der Integration könnte daher direkt“ ^[?] correlate with his ability to understand the proposed ground. The analysis frames this as an option for anchoring the proposal in the character arc, not as something settled.

In the recommendation (conditional on the proposal being integrated) it asks that the conflicts be resolved through the characters, especially Kael, and that his agency be kept; the passage opens „Fokus auf Charakter-Interaktion und Agency“ ^[?].
