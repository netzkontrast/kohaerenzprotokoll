## Reading — `outline`, 2025-07-30, the outline — DID as a depiction labelled in Kap 3, Kap 6 and Kap 12

`DID` stands in labels and notes of Teil 1. Kap 3's psychological note is headed „DID-Darstellung“ ^[?] (L37), where the Riss is described as an intrusion overlaying Kael's thoughts and triggering depersonalisation. Kap 6's Fokus holds that the success or failure of the inner negotiation shows Kael's progress „im Umgang mit seiner DID“ ^[?] (L57). In Kap 12 co-consciousness is called „ein Schlüsselziel in der DID-Therapie“ ^[?] (L82), because it reduces amnesia and enables cooperation.
