## Reading — `outline`, 2025-07-30, the outline — Rhys as the caretaker ANP, mediator of co-presence

The outline plans Rhys as „der Pfleger-ANP“ ^[?] (L42, Kap 5), who steps forward to keep inner harmony. In Kap 10 the moments of rudimentary co-consciousness are „oft vermittelt durch Rhys“ ^[?] (L72), and in Kap 12 two Anteile, „z.B. Lex und Rhys“ ^[?] (L82), are named as an example for a state of co-consciousness. Rhys is one of the ANPs of L116 (list quoted under `alex` and the other pages of that list), and in Kap 30 Kael is supported by integrated Anteile, „insbesondere Echo, Rhys“ ^[?] (L187).
