## Reading — `outline`, 2025-07-30, the outline — the alters as Anteile, and the ANP/EP division

The outline speaks of Kael's inner parts as Anteile and uses `Alters` as a gloss: „Kael und seine inneren Anteile (Alters)“ ^[?] (L99). Its psychological note on Kap 3 allows that contradictory data fragments „könnten Echos anderer Alters oder vergangener Leben enthalten“ ^[?] (L37). Where it lists them, it divides them: „Kaels Anteile (ANPs wie Lex, Rhys, Alex und EPs wie Nyx, Kiko, Moros, Isabelle, Lia, Argus) entwickeln ihre Kooperation weiter“ ^[?] (L116). A first inner meeting names, as an example, „z.B. Lex, Echo, Nox“ ^[?] (L56), and the Fokus names the presences of „Limina (klar), Nox (dunkel), Echo (transparent), Chronos (präzise), Schattenkind (kalt)“ ^[?] (L57).
