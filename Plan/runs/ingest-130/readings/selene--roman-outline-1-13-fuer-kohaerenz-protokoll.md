## Reading — `roman-outline-1-13-fuer-kohaerenz-protokoll`, 2025-05-03, the final outline — Selene as the later integrated self

The final outline plans Selene as a later emergence. In the Prologue the Echo is „möglicherweise den Keim des zukünftigen integrierten Selbst (Selene) enthaltend“ ^[?L23]. In Chapter 10 the plan names a „Selene-Potenzial“ ^[?L701]: the ability to coordinate and communicate consciously is called the ground of Selene's later emergence.
