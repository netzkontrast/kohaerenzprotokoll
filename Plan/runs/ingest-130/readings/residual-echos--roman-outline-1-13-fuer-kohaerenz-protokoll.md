## Reading — `roman-outline-1-13-fuer-kohaerenz-protokoll`, 2025-05-03, the final outline — the Echo and the origin of the trauma

Among the Prologue's key symbols the final outline writes „Echo: Repräsentiert verlorene Ganzheit, unterdrücktes Potenzial, den Ursprung des Traumas“ ^[?L73]. The Echo is named the origin of the trauma, beside lost wholeness and suppressed potential.
