## Reading — `report-reorienting-the-central-conflict-as-a-call-to-wholene`, 2025-11-03, the Call-to-Wholeness report — Juna/V redefined from a separate entity to Kael's exiled core

The Call-to-Wholeness report sets two conceptions of Juna/V side by side in a table. The previous conception holds that Juna/V „was conceived as a separate, external entity“ ^[?]. The new directive defines Juna/V as „the primary exiled part of Kael's original self“ ^[?], exiled in the Genesis-Krise to carry the traumatic burden. The report directs that the call to adventure be the leakage of Juna/V's pain, showing as „disruptive emotional intrusions, fragmented memory flashes“ ^[?]. It positions Juna/V through the link as „a catalyst who enables Kael's heroism“ ^[?], and ends the integration with the „homecoming“ ^[?] of Juna/V. This is a directive revising an earlier conception, recorded here and applied nowhere.
