## Reading — `report-reorienting-the-central-conflict-as-a-call-to-wholene`, 2025-11-03, the Call-to-Wholeness report — the plot reframed as Kael's internal reintegration

The Call-to-Wholeness report calls itself „a global directive“ ^[?] and directs that „The entire plot must now be framed as Kael's internal“ ^[?] Call to Wholeness, in the report's term. The goal is the finding and reintegration of the lost part, Juna/V. The report directs that Kael's Manager parts be the „authors of his unreliable narrative“ ^[?] and that they „actively create memory gaps“ ^[?], so that Kael's amnesia becomes an inner conflict. Disruptive intrusions are what the report calls the leakage of Juna/V's pain: „disruptive emotional intrusions, fragmented memory flashes“ ^[?]. The quest is described as one of „finding the exiled Juna/V, befriending her“ ^[?]. In the climax Kael reaches „functional multiplicity“ ^[?] and the „homecoming“ ^[?] of Juna/V, and the report claims victory on „all four of the narrative's primary thematic axes“ ^[?]. The report is a directive revising an earlier conception, recorded and not applied.
