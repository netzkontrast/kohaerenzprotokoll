## Reading — `kohaerenz-protokoll-the-official-master-story-guide-2`, 2025-10-15, the Master Story Guide — a table of eleven alters and the arc of functional multiplicity

The Master Story Guide, an unsigned English design guide, gives a section titled „The Canonical Society of Alters“ ^[?] and introduces its table as „The following table synthesizes all source documents into the single, canonical reference for the 11-alter model of System Kael.“ ^[?] The table has eleven rows (Kael, Lex, Nyx, Kiko, Rhys, Selene, Alex, Lia, Isabelle, Moros, Argus), each with a TSDP classification; these are the guide's claims about its own table.

For the arc it prescribes „a state of co-consciousness, communication, cooperation, and shared responsibility among all parts of the internal system“ ^[?] and says integration becomes „offensive weapon“ ^[?] against AEGIS, as alters develop capabilities its logic cannot model.
