## 2026-10-11 — `kohaerenz-protokoll-the-official-master-story-guide-2`, 2025-10-15, the Master Story Guide

**The Master Story Guide tabulates eleven alters and four Kernwelten, and calls its alter table the ground truth.**

The Master Story Guide, an unsigned English design guide, introduces its alter table as „The following table synthesizes all source documents into the single, canonical reference for the 11-alter model of System Kael.“ ^[?] The table has eleven rows, Kael to Argus. The guide also calls it „This is the ground truth for all narrative and character development.“ ^[?] That is the guide's claim about itself; it is recorded here and decides nothing.

For the worlds it writes „The Kernwelten (Core Worlds) are specialized simulated realities created by AEGIS as laboratories to analyze and control Kael.“ ^[?] and tabulates four: „Logos-Prime“ ^[?], „Mnemosyne-Archipel“ ^[?], „Cerberus-Labyrinth“ ^[?] and „Kairos-Potentialis“ ^[?], numbered KW1 to KW4. The guide does not say that one world corresponds to one alter.

Stands: one more position on the record, eleven alters and four Kernwelten as the guide tabulates them; the question stays open.
