## Reading — `kohaerenz-protokoll-the-official-master-story-guide-2`, 2025-10-15, the Master Story Guide — System Kael as a system, healing as the plot's mechanism

The Master Story Guide, an unsigned English design guide, prescribes that Kael „must not be treated as a singular character but as a complex system“ ^[?] called System Kael and modelled on TSDP. In its alter table Kael is the host: „ANP (Host) - Manages daily life, maintains façade of normalcy, coordinates external contact.“ ^[?]

On the guide's two levels, „Kael's personal healing is the very mechanism that rewrites the flawed physics of his prison.“ ^[?]

The arc it prescribes ends in functional multiplicity, „a state of co-consciousness, communication, cooperation, and shared responsibility among all parts of the internal system“ ^[?] The guide also names Kael's final integrated state as the „living refutation“ ^[?] of AEGIS's philosophy.

For his voice it requires, in the fragmented state, „Employ short, aborted sentences, abrupt shifts in tense or person, and non-sequiturs“ ^[?] and, in the model passage for the integrated state, „Each part a voice, not in conflict, but as a chord.“ ^[?]
