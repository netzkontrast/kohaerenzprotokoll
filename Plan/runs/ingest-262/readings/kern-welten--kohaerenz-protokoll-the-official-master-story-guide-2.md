## Reading — `kohaerenz-protokoll-the-official-master-story-guide-2`, 2025-10-15, the Master Story Guide — the Kernwelten as simulated laboratories, a table of four

The Master Story Guide, an unsigned English design guide, defines „The Kernwelten (Core Worlds) are specialized simulated realities created by AEGIS as laboratories to analyze and control Kael.“ ^[?] Its table lists four, by name „Logos-Prime“ ^[?], „Mnemosyne-Archipel“ ^[?], „Cerberus-Labyrinth“ ^[?] and „Kairos-Potentialis“ ^[?], each numbered KW1 to KW4 in the document. Of the first it says „Embodies the rigid, sterile order of AEGIS.“ ^[?] The guide does not tie the worlds to alters in that table.
