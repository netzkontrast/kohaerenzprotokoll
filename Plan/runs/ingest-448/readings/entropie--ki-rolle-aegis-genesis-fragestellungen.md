## Reading — `ki-rolle-aegis-genesis-fragestellungen`, 2025-11-28, the AEGIS-genesis research report — entropy as the ontological threat of the substrate

The AEGIS-genesis research report reads the novel's reality as one that emerges „aus einem Zustand maximaler Entropie und informationeller Fluktuation emergiert“ ^[?]. Against the pull of entropy, which it names `Sog der Entropie`, it sets the pull of order as counterforce; existence becomes a continuous process of error correction. The report ties this to the information-theoretic sense of entropy; recorded, not applied.
