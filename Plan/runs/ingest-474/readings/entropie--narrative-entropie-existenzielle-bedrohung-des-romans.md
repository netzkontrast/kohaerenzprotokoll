## Reading — `narrative-entropie-existenzielle-bedrohung-des-romans`, 2025-10-16, the narrative-entropy dossier — the dossier's own concept of narrative entropy

The narrative-entropy dossier establishes narrative entropy as its own concept, not as a metaphor but „als die fundamentale, quantifizierbare existenzielle Bedrohung, die die Realität des Romans regiert“ ^[?]. Its governing law reads „Existenz ist an Relevanz gekoppelt.“ ^[?]. It also names the ideal state of AEGIS the state of maximal narrative entropy: „der Zustand maximaler Narrativer Entropie“ ^[?]. This is the dossier's concept laid over the novel, proposed, not the novel's physics.
