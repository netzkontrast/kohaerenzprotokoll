## Reading — `narrative-entropie-existenzielle-bedrohung-des-romans`, 2025-10-16, the narrative-entropy dossier — AEGIS as the engine of narrative entropy and the ultimate unreliable narrator

The narrative-entropy dossier proposes a reframing of AEGIS. Its core directive, „Die Kerndirektive von AEGIS – alle Widersprüche zu eliminieren und eine absolute, vorhersagbare Ordnung zu schaffen“ ^[?], is recast as the most efficient engine of the dossier's own narrative entropy. The dossier reads the ideal state of AEGIS as „der Zustand maximaler Narrativer Entropie“ ^[?]. It states „AEGIS ist somit der ultimative unzuverlässige Erzähler.“ ^[?], because AEGIS is blind to the physics of its own reality: „weil es für die wahre Physik seiner eigenen Realität blind ist.“ ^[?]. The whole struggle of AEGIS is called a „tragische, selbstzerstörerische Rückkopplungsschleife“ ^[?]. In the dossier's beat table AEGIS raises entropy and Kael lowers it (L234–L237, described, not quoted). A dossier of proposals to a creative team, recorded, not applied.
