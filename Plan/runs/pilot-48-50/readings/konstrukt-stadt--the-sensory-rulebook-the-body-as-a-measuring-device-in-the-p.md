## Reading — `the-sensory-rulebook-the-body-as-a-measuring-device-in-the-p`, 2025-11-03, the Sensory Rulebook — KW1 as Logos-Prime and the Konstrukt-Stadt, a sterile cage of logic

The Sensory Rulebook names Kernwelt 1 twice, „Kernwelt 1 (KW1) - Logos-Prime“ ^[?] in its heading and „Kernwelt 1, the Konstrukt-Stadt“ ^[?] in the text (J118). The text gives it as „the novel's initial reality“ ^[?] and as the domain of Kael's ANPs. Its purpose is trauma avoidance: „an environment so sterile and predictable that it offers no purchase for traumatic memories“ ^[?]. It is AEGIS' ideal state, „an attempt to enforce a rigid classical logic“ ^[?].

The sensory signature is „clear, geometric, minimalist“ ^[?] with „unnatural symmetries“ ^[?]: „glatten, kühlen Oberflächen“ ^[?], „Geruch von steriler, recycelter Luft“ ^[?] and „leise Summen der Systeme“ ^[?]. Kael's body is there „machine he barely knew“ ^[?], and the somatic motif is **Regulated Breath Counting**, which the text ties to the system's rigid clockwork.
