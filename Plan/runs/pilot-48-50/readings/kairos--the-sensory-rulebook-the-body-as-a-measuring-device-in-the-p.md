## Reading — `the-sensory-rulebook-the-body-as-a-measuring-device-in-the-p`, 2025-11-03, the Sensory Rulebook — KW4's Guardian, one of two, named with Sophia

The Sensory Rulebook: „The Guardians of this world are Kairos and Sophia, complementary forces representing intuition, opportunity, and integrative wisdom.“ ^[?]. The sentence does not say which of them stands for which of the three. Their purpose is stated for both: „Their narrative purpose is to facilitate the generative and sometimes chaotic forces that Juna represents.“ ^[?]. The world's own names, Kairos-Potentialis and the Möglichkeits-Garten, are read on the world (J49, J118).
