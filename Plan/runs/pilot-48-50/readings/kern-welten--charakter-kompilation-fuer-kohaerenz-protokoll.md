## Reading — `charakter-kompilation-fuer-kohaerenz-protokoll`, 2026-03-31, the Charakter-Kompilation — four worlds by number and Guardian-built name

The Charakter-Kompilation writes the worlds by number and by a name built from a Guardian, in the list of the Guardians inside AEGIS' entry: „LogOS (KW1 - Logos-Prime)“ ^[?], „Mnemosyne (KW2 - Mnemosyne-Archipel)“ ^[?], „Cerberus (KW3 - Cerberus-Labyrinth)“ ^[?] and „Kairos & Sophia (KW4 - Kairos-Potentialis)“ ^[?]. Its one other mention counts them: „Allesamt Legacy-Namen, die mit den 4 Kernwelten verbunden waren“ ^[?], `Kernwelten` ^[charakter-kompilation-fuer-kohaerenz-protokoll.md:#1]. It says nothing about what a world is made of or where it lies.
