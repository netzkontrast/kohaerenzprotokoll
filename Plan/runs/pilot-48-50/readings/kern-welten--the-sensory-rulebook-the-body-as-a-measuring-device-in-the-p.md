## Reading — `the-sensory-rulebook-the-body-as-a-measuring-device-in-the-p`, 2025-11-03, the Sensory Rulebook — four worlds as psycho-architectures, each named twice

The Sensory Rulebook is an English essay that asserts and does not hedge. It opens on the worlds: „This conflict is externalized into four Core Worlds (Kernwelten)“ ^[?], which „manifest Kael's inner psychological landscape under AEGIS's flawed control“ ^[?]. It names each twice: Kernwelt 1 by its heading „Kernwelt 1 (KW1) - Logos-Prime“ ^[?] and in the text as „Kernwelt 1, the Konstrukt-Stadt“ ^[?]; Kernwelt 2 as „Kernwelt 2 (KW2) - Mnemosyne-Archipel“ ^[?] and „Kernwelt 2, the Resonanz-Landschaft“ ^[?]; Kernwelt 3 as „Kernwelt 3 (KW3) - Cerberus-Labyrinth“ ^[?] and „Kernwelt 3, the Grenzfeste“ ^[?]; Kernwelt 4 as „Kernwelt 4 (KW4) - Kairos-Potentialis“ ^[?] and „Kernwelt 4, the Möglichkeits-Garten“ ^[?] (J118).

Each world is a part of Kael's psychology and has a Guardian. The conclusion lists them as parts of one whole: „It is the integration of logic (KW1), paradoxical emotion (KW2), hypervigilant defense (KW3), and creative potential (KW4)“ ^[?].
