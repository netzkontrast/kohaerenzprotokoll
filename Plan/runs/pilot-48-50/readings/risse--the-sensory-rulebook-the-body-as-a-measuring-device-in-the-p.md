## Reading — `the-sensory-rulebook-the-body-as-a-measuring-device-in-the-p`, 2025-11-03, the Sensory Rulebook — a break in the breath as an intrusion from Kael's deeper parts

The Sensory Rulebook has no `rift` ^[the-sensory-rulebook-the-body-as-a-measuring-device-in-the-p.md:#0] and no `crack` ^[the-sensory-rulebook-the-body-as-a-measuring-device-in-the-p.md:#0]. It has one `glitch` ^[the-sensory-rulebook-the-body-as-a-measuring-device-in-the-p.md:#1], in Kernwelt 1, for the body's break in a rhythm: „Any disruption to this pattern—a sudden gasp of fear, a held breath of anxiety, a sigh of inexplicable sadness—becomes an undeniable somatic truth.“ ^[?]. The text calls it „an intrusion of a more paradoxical reality from his deeper, suppressed parts“ ^[?]. The word names an event in Kael's body, not a rift in the world.
