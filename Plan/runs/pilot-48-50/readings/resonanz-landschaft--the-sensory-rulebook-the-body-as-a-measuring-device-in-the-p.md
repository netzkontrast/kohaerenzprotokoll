The Sensory Rulebook names Kernwelt 2 twice, „Kernwelt 2 (KW2) - Mnemosyne-Archipel“ ^[?] in its heading and „Kernwelt 2, the Resonanz-Landschaft“ ^[?] in the text (J118). It „represents the submerged world of emotional memory and unprocessed trauma“ ^[?], the domain of Kael's EPs, and it works on a logic that „permits true contradictions (A and not-A)“ ^[?].

Its signature is „fluid, dream-like, and reactive“ ^[?], with „Echos von Stimmen oder Musik aus der Vergangenheit“ ^[?] and „plötzliche Kälte oder Wärme in Traumabereichen“ ^[?]. Kael's body there is a conduit: „A sudden, inexplicable chill runs down his spine in a place he remembers as warm“ ^[?]. The somatic motif is **Visceral Gut Reactions**.
