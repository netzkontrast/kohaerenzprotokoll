## Reading — `charakter-kompilation-fuer-kohaerenz-protokoll`, 2026-03-31, the Charakter-Kompilation — a bare list under AEGIS' entry, a world after each name

The Charakter-Kompilation is a model's compilation, made from Drive documents it says it ordered „nach ihrer inhaltlichen Reife und konzeptionellen Tiefe“ ^[?]; the entries carry bracket numbers of its own list, not this corpus's, and its status report is one it calls „Hard Canon Definition“ ^[?]. Recorded, not applied.

It has no section for the Guardians. They stand inside the entry of AEGIS, under „Die Guardians:“ ^[?], as four lines with a world in parentheses: „LogOS (KW1 - Logos-Prime)“ ^[?], „Mnemosyne (KW2 - Mnemosyne-Archipel)“ ^[?], „Cerberus (KW3 - Cerberus-Labyrinth)“ ^[?] and „Kairos & Sophia (KW4 - Kairos-Potentialis)“ ^[?]. The word is written `Guardians` ^[charakter-kompilation-fuer-kohaerenz-protokoll.md:#1] once in the document. The parenthesis names the world the Guardian is set beside; it is not the Guardian's name (J49).

Two of the alters carry the word Wächter as a role, not as the Guardians: Alex „Aktiver Schutz des Systems, Krisenmanagement, Wächter an der Grenze.“ ^[?] and Selene „Wandelt sich von einer starren Wächterin (Blockade) zur Architektin innerer Harmonie (Mediation).“ ^[?] (J20, Q4).

The compilation also names four legacy figures: „Allesamt Legacy-Namen, die mit den 4 Kernwelten verbunden waren“ ^[?]. It does not call them Guardians.
