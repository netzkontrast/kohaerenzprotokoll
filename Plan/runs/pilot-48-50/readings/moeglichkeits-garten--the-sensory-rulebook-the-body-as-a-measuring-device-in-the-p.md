## Reading — `the-sensory-rulebook-the-body-as-a-measuring-device-in-the-p`, 2025-11-03, the Sensory Rulebook — KW4 as Kairos-Potentialis and the Möglichkeits-Garten, Juna's world, at ease

The Sensory Rulebook names Kernwelt 4 twice, „Kernwelt 4 (KW4) - Kairos-Potentialis“ ^[?] in its heading and „Kernwelt 4, the Möglichkeits-Garten“ ^[?] in the text (J118). It is „the psycho-architectural space of Juna/V's influence“ ^[?], a whole world, not a place inside one, whose landscape is described as an „überwucherter 'Ruinen-Garten'“ ^[?].

The signature is „changeable, symbolic, and chaotically generative“ ^[?]: „Inspirierende Klänge (Musik, Naturgeräusche)“ ^[?], „Geruch von Ozon oder frischer Erde“ ^[?] and „warmes, dynamisches Licht“ ^[?]. Kael's body is at ease, and the somatic motif is **The Sensation of Unclenching Hands and an Easing of Breath**. The Guardians are Kairos and Sophia.
