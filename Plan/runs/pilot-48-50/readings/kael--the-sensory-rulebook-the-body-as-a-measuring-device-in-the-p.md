## Reading — `the-sensory-rulebook-the-body-as-a-measuring-device-in-the-p`, 2025-11-03, the Sensory Rulebook — the protagonist whose body is the instrument, world by world

The protagonist is Kael, „whose psyche is fragmented by trauma into a system defined by Tertiary Structural Dissociation of Personality (TSDP), an architecture inherently paradoxical and adaptive“ ^[?]. The thesis: „the body functions as an infallible measuring device for reality“ ^[?].

KW1: his body is „machine he barely knew.“ ^[?] In KW3 „his entire physiology is primed for a fight-or-flight response.“ ^[?] In KW4 „The chronic tension in his muscles dissipates, his breathing deepens and slows“ ^[?].

The conclusion: „Kael's path from a depersonalized machine to an integrated, feeling being proves that wholeness is not the absence of contradiction, but the body's profound and courageous capacity to hold it all.“ ^[?] The KW3 motif is muscular tension and the KW4 motif is unclenching hands; both are somatic motifs of the worlds, and `Knöchel` ^[the-sensory-rulebook-the-body-as-a-measuring-device-in-the-p.md:#0].
