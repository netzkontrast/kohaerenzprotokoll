## Reading — `the-sensory-rulebook-the-body-as-a-measuring-device-in-the-p`, 2025-11-03, the Sensory Rulebook — a disembodied operating system whose ideal state is KW1, and an externalised perpetrator introject in KW3

The essay opens AEGIS as „a disembodied operating system whose existence is predicated on enforcing coherence through the negation of entropy and contradiction" ^[?], and calls the four worlds manifestations of Kael's inner landscape „under AEGIS's flawed control" ^[?].

KW1 is its ideal: „This world is AEGIS's ideal state, an attempt to enforce a rigid classical logic where the Law of Non-Contradiction is absolute and any deviation is an error to be corrected." ^[?] KW1's Guardian is „an agent embodying the strict, formal logic and unyielding order enforced by AEGIS." ^[?]

In KW3 it is a psychological figure, not only a system: the protector alters' fortress shields the child parts from „the invalidating control of AEGIS, which functions as an" ^[?] „externalisiertes Täterintrojekt" ^[?], glossed in the text as externalized perpetrator introject.

In the conclusion the body is set against it: „While AEGIS seeks a disembodied coherence through the top-down imposition of classical logic and the elimination of contradiction" ^[?].
