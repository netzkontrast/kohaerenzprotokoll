## Reading — `kohaerenz-protokoll-plot-entwicklung-und-wahrheitsdualitaet`, 2025-11-18, the truth-duality paper — AEGIS as the coherence-theory antagonist, its tragic fault and its fate chosen

The truth-duality paper, a strategy report that sets Plot A as its base (L112), reports AEGIS as one pole of two truth theories: „Das System ist autopoietisch (selbsterhaltend) und operativ geschlossen“ ^[?] (L82, passed on from another document it cites by digit). Its tragic fault is, in the paper's account of the Protokoll-Ontologie, the „Paradoxon der Fehlausgerichteten Kohärenz“ ^[?], or Paradoxon X: AEGIS is unable to tell the coherence kernel from the collapse kernel (L94), and „greift AEGIS die Struktur an, die es eigentlich schützen sollte“ ^[?] (L95, also reported).

For its fate the paper reports two hypotheses from a cited document: „AEGIS entwickelt eine parakonsistente Logik“ ^[?] (L122) and „AEGIS' binäre Logik zerbricht am Paradoxon“ ^[?] (L123). Its own proposed plot then chooses: „Wir wählen die thematisch stärkere Option“ ^[?] (L226). In its Denouement „AEGIS überlebt, aber in einem tragischen, gebrochenen Zustand“ ^[?] and „Es ist in einer ewigen, sinnlosen Berechnung gefangen“ ^[?] (L227). Act 1 places AEGIS's coherence theory in the city of KW1, described as „physische Manifestation von AEGIS' Kohärenztheorie“ ^[?] (L193). These are the paper's proposals and choices, not canon.
