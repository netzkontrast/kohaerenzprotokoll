## Reading — `genesis-und-krise-ausfuehrliche-darstellung`, 2025-04-29, the Genesis narrative — the Überwelt as AEGIS's simulation space and its limit

The Genesis narrative tells the Überwelt in Scene 7 as: „Kein physischer Ort, sondern ein Zustand erhöhter, fokussierter interner Aktivität“ ^[?] which AEGIS has created as a tool of optimisation. It tells what AEGIS does there: „Hier, im sicheren Raum der Simulation, analysiert AEGIS externe Signaturen, ohne das Gesamtsystem zu gefährden.“ ^[?] Abstract concepts such as space, time and causality are told as born there as internal tools, not discovered as external realities (L59).

Scene 8 tells the simulation reaching its limit: „Doch an einem bestimmten Punkt stößt die Simulation an eine harte Grenze.“ ^[?] In Scene 10 Komponente 734 is drawn into the Überwelt with other analysis units to simulate the external entity, and the narrative tells the simulation failing and AEGIS concluding that the Überwelt fails (L87).
