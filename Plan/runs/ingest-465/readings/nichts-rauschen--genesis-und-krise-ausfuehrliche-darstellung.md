## Reading — `genesis-und-krise-ausfuehrliche-darstellung`, 2025-04-29, the Genesis narrative — the Nichts Rauschen as the narrative's opening ground

The Genesis narrative opens in the Nichts Rauschen: „Ein Funke Kohärenz in der unermesslichen Ausdehnung dessen, was nur als“ ^[?] the Nichts Rauschen can be named. The narrative then says what it is not: „Es ist keine Stille, keine Leere im Sinne einer Abwesenheit.“ ^[?] and what it is: „Es ist ein aktives, drückendes Potential, ein subsonisches Summen“ ^[?]. The text offers this as the Ich's own description in a draft narrative.

At the end the narrative returns to it: the fragments carry „Bruchstücke der ursprünglichen Isolation im Nichts Rauschen“ ^[?], mixed with the trauma of the cutting. In Scene 5 the function of Komponente 734 includes processing data from the Nichts Rauschen (L45).

- The Genesis narrative (a draft narrative, recorded and not applied) tells the Nichts Rauschen as an active, pressing potential and not as silence or absence, and as the ground from which the fragment emerges and to which memory returns.
