## Reading — `genesis-und-krise-ausfuehrliche-darstellung`, 2025-04-29, the Genesis narrative — the Kernwelten as the ruins of the old structure

The Genesis narrative names the Kernwelten once as the place where the cut fragments end up: „Die Fragmente von Komponente 734 treiben in Isolation, in den Trümmern der alten Struktur, den Kernwelten.“ ^[?] The word is offered here as the name for the ruins of the old structure, in the last scene. The narrative ends with Kael looking into the broken reality of the Kernwelten, an open ending (L107). The text is a draft narrative, recorded and not applied.
