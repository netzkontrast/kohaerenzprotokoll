## Reading — `narrative-context-protocol-ncp-spezifikation`, 2025-07-03, the NCP white paper — AEGIS as the Obstacle Character subsystem in a proposed Dramatica table

The NCP white paper, an unsigned German specification proposal, lists AEGIS as one of „Die vier definierten Story-Subsysteme (Kael, AEGIS, Juna/V, Das Fundament)“ ^[?].

Its Table 1 heads the column „AEGIS (Logisch-Systemische Ebene)“ ^[?] and gives the role „Obstacle Character (IC)“ ^[?]. The white paper proposes, attributing each value to that table and not to the storyforms of decision 025:

- Domain „Physics (Aktivität)“ ^[?];
- Concern „Obtaining (Erlangen/Erreichen)“ ^[?];
- Issue „Self-Interest vs. Morality“ ^[?];
- Problem „Control (Kontrolle)“ ^[?];
- Solution „Uncontrolled (Unkontrolliertheit)“ ^[?];
- Goal „Learning (Lernen)“ ^[?];
- Consequence „Doing (Sinnloses Tun)“ ^[?].
