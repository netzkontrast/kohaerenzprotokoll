## Reading — `narrative-context-protocol-ncp-spezifikation`, 2025-07-03, the NCP white paper — Kael as the Main Character subsystem in a proposed Dramatica table

The NCP white paper, an unsigned German specification proposal, names Kael among „Die vier definierten Story-Subsysteme (Kael, AEGIS, Juna/V, Das Fundament)“ ^[?], which it calls instantiations of the four Dramatica perspectives at the macro level and, each for itself, „eine vollständige Dramatica-Storyform“ ^[?].

The white paper proposes a value set for Kael in its Table 1, introduced as „einen Vorschlag für die hochrangigen Dramatica-Spezifikationen“ ^[?]. The column is headed „Kael (Psychologische Ebene)“ ^[?], and the table gives Kael the role „Main Character (MC)“ ^[?]. Every value below is the table's, attributed to Table 1, and none is a storyform of decision 025:

- Issue „Appraisal vs. Re-appraisal“ ^[?];
- Problem „Doubt (Zweifel)“ ^[?];
- Solution „Certainty (Gewissheit)“ ^[?];
- Goal „Understanding (Verständnis)“ ^[?];
- Consequence „Memories (In Erinnerungen gefangen)“ ^[?].

The white paper calls the table „die entscheidende kreative und logische Grundlage“ ^[?]. In its interference section Kael appears once more, as an example of a subsystem driven by Faith while AEGIS is driven by Disbelief, whose interaction the white paper calls „ist ihre Interaktion von Natur aus unvereinbar und hochgradig konfliktgeladen“ ^[?].
