## Reading — `narrative-context-protocol-ncp-spezifikation`, 2025-07-03, the NCP white paper — Juna/V as the Objective Story subsystem in a proposed Dramatica table

The NCP white paper, an unsigned German specification proposal, names „Juna/V“ ^[?] as the third of its four story subsystems, in „Die vier definierten Story-Subsysteme (Kael, AEGIS, Juna/V, Das Fundament)“ ^[?].

Table 1 heads the column „Juna/V (Ontologische Ebene)“ ^[?] and gives the role „Objective Story (OS)“ ^[?]. The white paper proposes these values, attributed to Table 1 and not to the storyforms of decision 025:

- Domain „Universe (Situation)“ ^[?];
- Concern „Future (Zukunft)“ ^[?];
- Issue „Faith vs. Disbelief“ ^[?];
- Problem „Inequity (Ungleichgewicht)“ ^[?];
- Solution „Equity (Gleichgewicht)“ ^[?];
- Goal „The Past (Die Vergangenheit ändern)“ ^[?];
- Consequence „Progress (Unkontrollierter Fortschritt)“ ^[?].

The table says nothing about Juna beyond the subsystem's column; the white paper's text uses the name `Juna/V` only in the list of the four subsystems.
