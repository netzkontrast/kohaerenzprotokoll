## Reading — `umfassendes-lokalitaeten-konzept-fuer-roman`, 2025-04-18, the place profiles — the worlds as externalisations of a dissociative identity structure

The concept names DID twice in reasons for its design. The worlds mirror „seiner dissoziativen Identitätsstruktur (DID)“ ^[?L31]. The therapy place of profile 7 is a „Wichtiger Plotpunkt für Kaels Konfrontation mit seiner DID“ ^[?L203] and its plot field adds „Wichtiger Ort für die Entdeckung/Diskussion seiner DID“ ^[?L209]. The document proposes both; no clinical claim of its own is made in these lines.
