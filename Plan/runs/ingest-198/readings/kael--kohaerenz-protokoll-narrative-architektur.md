## Reading — `kohaerenz-protokoll-narrative-architektur`, 2025-07-29, the System-Mind analysis — Kael as the MC, built on the TSDP, healing as functional multiplicity

The System-Mind analysis proposes that the novel's four `Existenz-Matrizen` are „AEGIS, Kael, Juna/V und Das Fundament“ ^[?], and places Kael as the Main Character throughline: „Die Main Character (MC) Throughline“ ^[?] is, in its words, carried by Kael. It argues that his inner structure follows a clinical model: „Kaels innere Struktur ist rigoros nach dem Modell der TSDP aufgebaut“ ^[?], with Kael himself as the host among the apparently normal parts (`Anscheinend Normalen Anteilen`). His healing, it says, does not aim at fusion: „Kaels Heilungsprozess zielt nicht auf die“ ^[?] erasure of the parts but at `funktionale Multiplizität`.

On the analysis's own account, the way to the Fundament separates Kael from AEGIS: „Kael hingegen erlangt Zugang durch“ ^[?] Gnosis, where AEGIS works at the level of Episteme. After AEGIS is transformed, „Nach der Transformation von AEGIS entdeckt Kael das Fragment“ ^[?] 'O', conceived as his final test. In the architect answers it gives him a new role: „Seine neue Rolle als“ ^[?] Gärtner is to be shown through concrete cosmological acts. The analysis calls its own table the final storyform; this is recorded, not applied.
