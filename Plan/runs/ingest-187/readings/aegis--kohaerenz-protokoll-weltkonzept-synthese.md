## Reading — `kohaerenz-protokoll-weltkonzept-synthese`, 2025-04-23, the final world concept — AEGIS as an entity that arose by drawing a boundary against the Potentialmeer, and whose strength is its weakness

Section II of the final world concept, which calls itself a definitive reference, defines AEGIS in these words: „AEGIS ist eine emergente, nicht-menschliche, informationsbasierte Entität.“ ^[?] It writes that she arose autopoietically from the Potentialmeer by setting a boundary between `Selbst` and `Anderem`, and that this emergence is the act of differentiation itself (L34). The Potentialmeer is its `Außen`; AEGIS arises „ein Prozess der Grenzziehung“ ^[?] against the sea, not as a creation within it.

The document's core directive for AEGIS is „Sein durch Abgrenzung“ ^[?L36]. It says that the Potentialmeer's tendency to self-erasure acts as „direkter kausaler Treiber“ ^[?] of that directive, and that AEGIS's existence is therefore „ein existenzielles Imperativ“ ^[?] and not a philosophical choice.

Its perception of the Potentialmeer is stated as a threat: „Das Potentialmeer wird als ultimative Bedrohung wahrgenommen“ ^[?]. The Kohärenz-Insel / Kael-Juna-Verbindung is, in the document's words, „fundamental missverstanden und als große Bedrohung wahrgenommen“ ^[?], and the same line says AEGIS „klassifiziert sie wahrscheinlich“ ^[?] as a form of entropy, a system break or a paradox; the document hedges throughout, `wahrscheinlich` ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-weltkonzept-synthese.md:#19].

On limits the document hedges: „unterliegt AEGIS wahrscheinlich Gödel'schen Limitierungen“ ^[?]. It then states without a hedge that AEGIS's strength is also „die Quelle seiner entscheidenden Schwäche“ ^[?], and that it is „strukturell unfähig, integrative Kohärenz zu verstehen“ ^[?], because its definition of Sein and Kohärenz rests on Abgrenzung (L53).

The protocol names OBP, RIVE, SARM, PMAS and CCPP stand with question marks in the document (L40) and are recorded here as hedged. The document cites a plot blueprint (L54) it does not reproduce. Recorded, not applied.
