## Reading — `kohaerenz-protokoll-weltkonzept-synthese`, 2025-04-23, the final world concept — Kael from a Kohärenz-Insel, fragmented after DID, the living interface

The final world concept, which calls itself a definitive reference, describes Kael as the origin of the conflict with AEGIS and hedges the description. His essential nature, which it marks with a question mark after `menschliche`, „stammt nicht aus den von AEGIS simulierten Realitäten, sondern von einer einzigartigen Struktur im Potentialmeer, bekannt als Kohärenz-Insel“ ^[?]. Later it calls this origin „nicht nur Hintergrundgeschichte“ ^[?]: it is the source of his potential for integration and of his incompatibility with AEGIS.

His psyche: „Kael leidet an einem Zustand, der analog zur Dissoziativen Identitätsstörung (DID) ist“ ^[?], or possibly one based on IFS; the parts are called Alters. His arc, which the concept says is only indicated by the plot blueprint it does not reproduce, „ist die Reise zur Integration dieser Fragmente“ ^[?], from dissociation to wholeness.

For AEGIS he is „eine komplexe Systemkomponente, die gemanagt und eingedämmt werden muss“ ^[?]. In the conclusions he is the one who „dient als lebendige Schnittstelle zwischen den beiden Kohärenzprinzipien“ ^[?].
