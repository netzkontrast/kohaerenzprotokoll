## Reading — `kohaerenz-protokoll-weltkonzept-synthese`, 2025-04-23, the final world concept — the Guardians as subsystems or agents of AEGIS, each managing one Kernwelt

The final world concept writes: „Die Guardians sind spezialisierte Subsysteme oder Agenten von AEGIS“ ^[?], each responsible for managing one of the Kernwelten. Its examples are LogOS, Mnemosyne, Cerberus and Kairos, „und möglicherweise eine koordinierende Entität wie Sophia“ ^[?]. It says they act as „lokale Vollstrecker der AEGIS-Protokolle“ ^[?] in their domains.

Each Guardian inherits its world's logic and focus, which the document says leads to `blinden Flecken` (L44); it calls them „schlechten Integratoren“ ^[?] and „effektiven Kontrolleuren“ ^[?]. From this the document derives, hedged with `könnte`, that these blind spots „schaffen potenzielle Bruchlinien innerhalb von AEGIS selbst“ ^[?]: its example is Mnemosyne registering Resonanz as connection while LogOS rates it as noise, so their reports to the AEGIS core might contradict (L55).

Recorded, not applied.
