## Reading — `kohaerenz-protokoll-finale-pfeiler`, 2025-07-29, the final pillars report — access by Gnosis, a psyche built on TSDP

The final pillars report proposes that Kael reaches the Fundament by another route than AEGIS: „Kael hingegen erlangt Zugang durch“ ^[?] Gnosis, a personal, transformative knowing won by direct experience. His psyche, it says, is built on a clinical model: „Kaels Psyche wird rigoros nach dem Modell“ ^[?] of the Tertiäre Strukturelle Dissoziation (TSDP).

The report names co-fronting as the outward form of his integration: „Durch Co-Fronting können verschiedene Anteile gleichzeitig die Kontrolle teilen“ ^[?]. His inner world is staged as „Kaels innere Welt wird narrativ als ein“ ^[?] polyphonic novel in Bachtin's sense. These are the report's lenses (Bachtin, Deleuze/Guattari), applied to Kael.
