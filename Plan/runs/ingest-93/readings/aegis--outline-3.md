## Reading — `outline-3`, 2025-05-03, the fifth outline — AEGIS as perhaps a necessary evil, and perhaps unaware of its paradox

The fifth outline repeats the plan of its earlier versions for AEGIS; what it adds is two hedged lines. Chapter 32 plans Kael's arrival at the core, where he meets AEGIS' possible good side: „vielleicht erhält es tatsächlich eine Form von Ordnung“ ^[?] (L535). The same line closes on a question that the plan leaves open: „Ist AEGIS nur ein Monster oder auch ein notwendiges Übel“ ^[?] (L535).

In Chapter 20 the `AEGIS Focus` field plans that the paradox is uncovered while AEGIS goes on by its faulty logic: „AEGIS agiert weiter nach seiner (fehlerhaften) Logik“ ^[?] and is perhaps unaware of it: „ist sich des Paradoxons evtl. nicht bewusst“ ^[?] (L347). The fifth outline plans the hedge, and decides neither question.
