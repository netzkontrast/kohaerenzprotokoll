## Reading — `narrative-architektur-fuer-kohaerenz-protokoll`, 2025-07-29, the craft handbook — Guardians as voices of a meta-level

The craft handbook recommends, for the meta-level of the novel, reports of the Guardians: „Berichte von Sub-Systemen, die vielleicht leicht unterschiedliche“ ^[?] voices or interpretations of the same event deliver. It adds that this could reflect the use of different AI models or parameters in the writing process (L313).

It also asks, as a question: „Könnte es eine Szene geben, in der zwei Guardians widersprüchliche Berichte über dasselbe Ereignis liefern“ ^[?] — a scene the handbook says would mirror the theme of objective against subjective truth on the meta-level. Both lines propose ways of writing the Guardians; the handbook states nothing about them as figures of the world.

<!-- differ -->
- The craft handbook recommends writing the Guardians as sub-systems whose reports differ („Berichte von Sub-Systemen, die vielleicht leicht unterschiedliche“ ^[?]); a technique for the prose, no position on what the Guardians are.
