## Reading — `narrative-architektur-fuer-kohaerenz-protokoll`, 2025-07-29, the craft handbook — the Risse shown typographically

The craft handbook has a subsection on the visualisation of the `Risse` and system errors. Its recommendation: the Risse in reality, as the novel describes them (footnote 1), „sollten nicht nur beschrieben, sondern visuell“ ^[?] shown — the line continues that they should be *dargestellt*, which the handbook says can be done through targeted typographic disturbances. This is advice on how to write the Risse; it adds nothing about what they are.
