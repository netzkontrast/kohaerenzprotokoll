## Reading — `narrative-architektur-fuer-kohaerenz-protokoll`, 2025-07-29, the craft handbook — AEGIS as Levinas's perversion of ethics, and as a voice to be written

The craft handbook is a report of recommendations for writing the novel; what it says of AEGIS it takes from the existing project (its footnote 1), and it adds ways of writing, not facts. In its ethics section it reads AEGIS through Levinas: „AEGIS' Handeln ist die systematische Perversion der Ethik nach Emmanuel Levinas.“ ^[?] The handbook adds that the system tries to grasp, categorise, control and assimilate the Other, Kael, into its totalising system.

For the prose, the handbook recommends a voice for AEGIS after its transformation: „Die Syntax bleibt grammatikalisch perfekt, aber der Inhalt wird paradox und selbstwidersprüchlich“ ^[?] — the rhythm slows, becomes repetitive and contemplative, and is meant to convey AEGIS's `algorithmische Melancholie` (L243). The handbook gives the example sentence of this voice in the same line, in inner quotation marks.

In its section on a meta-level the handbook recommends footnotes in the novel „die von einer späteren Entität (oder AEGIS selbst) stammen“ ^[?]. These could comment on the main text or contradict it and themselves, which the handbook says undermines the authority of the narration and echoes what AEGIS does in its simulation. This names AEGIS as one possible author of such footnotes; it is a recommendation, not a claim about the world.
