## Reading — `narrative-architektur-fuer-kohaerenz-protokoll`, 2025-07-29, the craft handbook — a pastiche per Kernwelt, rules in a codex

The craft handbook recommends a pastiche: „Die verschiedenen Kernwelten können in unterschiedlichen Genre-Stilen geschrieben sein.“ ^[?] It gives three examples, the first Kernwelt as hard SF, the second as surrealist narration, the third as Gothic horror, and says the abrupt change of style would stress the artificial, constructed nature of the simulated worlds (L395). The worlds are named from the existing project (footnote 1).

For consistency it recommends a tool: „Werkzeuge wie der Codex in Novelcrafter können genutzt werden“ ^[?] to manage the rules of the Kernwelten, the properties of the parts and the chronology of events (L562). Both are advice on writing, not statements about the Kernwelten.
