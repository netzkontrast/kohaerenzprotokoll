## Reading — `narrative-architektur-fuer-kohaerenz-protokoll`, 2025-07-29, the craft handbook — the Resonanz-Landschaft as surrealist prose

In its pastiche recommendation the craft handbook assigns the Resonanz-Landschaft a genre: the Kernwelt named `Resonanz-Landschaft` could be written „im Stil einer surrealistischen Erzählung“ ^[?], beside a hard-SF first world and a Gothic-horror third one (L395). The handbook gives the reason for the whole device: the abrupt style change would stress the constructed nature of the simulated worlds. It says nothing else of this world; the name comes from the existing project.
