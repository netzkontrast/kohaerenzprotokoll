The three-voice chapter structure plans Juna in the Kael passages and, once, in a log. Its Kap 1 AEGIS log lists „Conforming to social interaction protocols“ ^[?] with Juna-Interface in the parenthesis. Its Kael passage for Kap 1 has Kael see: „Junas Bild erschien. Makellos, neutral.“ ^[?]; the image flickers and the voice seems to plead before it is normal again.

For Kap 2 the Kael passage has Kael name the betrayal as the system's: „Nicht von Juna. Von dem System, dem ich dienen sollte.“ ^[?] The Kap 7 passage has a presence that is „Nicht ihr Bild, nicht ihre protokollarische Stimme.“ ^[?] The Kap 8 passage leaves it open who the presence is: „oder ist es Juna, oder beide?“ ^[?] A plan with draft passages, not canon.
