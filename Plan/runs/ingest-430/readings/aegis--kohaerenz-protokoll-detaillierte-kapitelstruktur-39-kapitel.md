The three-voice chapter structure plans a log headed `Einblick in AEGIS` under each of sixteen chapters; it is a plan with draft passages, none of it canon. In its Kap 1 log AEGIS reports on the Kael-Unit and closes: „Monitoring Kael-Unit for optimal data acquisition“ ^[?].
