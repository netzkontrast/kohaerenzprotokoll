## Reading — `kohaerenz-protokoll-detaillierte-kapitelstruktur-39-kapitel`, 2025-04-26, the three-voice chapter structure — the Mosaik-Herz as an accepted self-image in Kap 11

The three-voice chapter structure uses the Mosaik-Herz only in Kap 11, `Das Mosaik des Selbst`, and as a way of seeing the self. The narrator passage says that accepting the `Mosaik-Herzens` „befreit von dem Zwang, ein unmögliches Ideal monolithischer Identität zu verfolgen“ ^[?]. Its AEGIS log records that the Kael-Unit „conceptualizes self as a cooperative system of diverse but integrated functions“ ^[?], and the same log notes the metaphor as logged. Kael's passage gives the image without the name: „Jede Scherbe hat ihren Platz, ihre Farbe, ihre Textur.“ ^[?] In this plan it is Kael's self-image, with no place and no chapter of its own. A plan with draft passages, not canon.
