## Reading — `aegis-genesis-krise-konzeptioneller-rahmen`, 2025-04-29, the Genesis-Krise framework — Kael emerging at the end of a six-stage arc

The Genesis-Krise framework is an unsigned concept and prose plan, and what it says of Kael is a proposal. In the sixth and last stage of its narrative arc it proposes that, once the Ursprungs-Ich of AEGIS is gone or changed, „Kael (und potenziell andere Fragmente) tritt als distinkte Entität hervor“ ^[?], perhaps with traumatic imprints or fragmented directives. The commission to a writer states the same as its goal: the scene is to lead to the fragmentation of the Ursprungs-Ich and to „der Emergenz des Kael-Fragments“ ^[?]. For the writer's treatment of stage six, the framework proposes „Kael einführen, vielleicht definiert durch die traumatische Prägung“ ^[?] or by lost functions and memories.

On point of view, the framework proposes that Kael's own perspective is optional: if shown, the voice should be „vielleicht einfacher, stärker auf unmittelbare Empfindung/Trauma fokussiert“ ^[?]. In the perspective strategy the closing phase is left open between Kael's emerging view and a more distant, external one.
