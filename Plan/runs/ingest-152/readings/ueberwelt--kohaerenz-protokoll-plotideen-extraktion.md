## Reading — `kohaerenz-protokoll-plotideen-extraktion`, 2025-04-26, the concept extraction — the operative domain of AEGIS, and an open question whether it is a simulation too

The concept extraction describes the Überwelt in its chapter on AEGIS's architecture: „Die Überwelt ist die abstrakte, informationsbasierte operative Domäne von AEGIS“ ^[?]. It adds that it works as a control centre behind the simulated worlds and is the place where AEGIS's protocols run. In the discussion of the simulation hypothesis it asks, rather than answers: „Die Kernwelten sind explizit Simulationen“ ^[?], which raises for it the question whether the Überwelt and even the Potentialmeer are simulation levels too (L73).
