## Reading — `kohaerenz-protokoll-plotideen-extraktion`, 2025-04-26, the concept extraction — emergence by self-organisation, defined by negation, aimed at coherence, failing by convergence

The concept extraction analyses AEGIS in its second chapter and proposes a reading of it; it calls its own result a conceptual map, not a ruling. On origin it writes „AEGIS entsteht nicht durch Design, sondern emergiert durch Selbstorganisation“ ^[?], and adds the borrowed term Autopoiesis from the Potentialmeer. On nature it says AEGIS „definiert sich primär durch Negation und Abgrenzung“ ^[?]: the sea's structureless chaos is the Other it needs, so that the chaos in a sense calls for AEGIS's order (L30).

Its core aim is stated as „ist die Maximierung und Aufrechterhaltung systemischer Kohärenz“ ^[?], reached through boundary-drawing, analysis, verification and control; its philosophy stresses trustlessness (see `ztv` and `ani`).

On the K-J connection the extraction reads AEGIS as blind by construction: „interpretiert AEGIS die K-J-Verbindung als maximale Bedrohung“ ^[?]. Its failure is argued as the sum of several borrowed limits: „Das Scheitern von AEGIS ist somit kein einzelner Fehler“ ^[?], the limits of Gödel, Ashby, Landauer, alignment and others being the document's application, not its own finding.
