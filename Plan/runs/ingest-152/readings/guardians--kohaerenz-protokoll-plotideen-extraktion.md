## Reading — `kohaerenz-protokoll-plotideen-extraktion`, 2025-04-26, the concept extraction — five Guardians as subsystems of AEGIS, each a blind spot, together embodied limits

The concept extraction names five Guardians and gives them a function: they „sind spezialisierte Subsysteme oder Agenten von AEGIS“ ^[?], and „Jeder Guardian ist für die Überwachung und Steuerung einer spezifischen Kernwelt zuständig“ ^[?]. Its list of worlds has four names (L241) against five Guardians (L257); it does not remark on the difference.

Each has a blind spot: „Jeder Guardian besitzt eine spezifische funktionale Einschränkung“ ^[?], and the blind spots together keep AEGIS from recognising the K-J connection (L259). It also allows doubt: the Guardians are not necessarily loyal and could become unwilling helpers or opponents (L261). Finally it proposes „Guardians als verkörperte Limitationen“ ^[?] — personifications of AEGIS's limits, offered hedged, with `könnten` and `möglicherweise`.
