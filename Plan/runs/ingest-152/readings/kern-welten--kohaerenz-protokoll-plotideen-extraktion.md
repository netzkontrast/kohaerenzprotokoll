## Reading — `kohaerenz-protokoll-plotideen-extraktion`, 2025-04-26, the concept extraction — simulation environments of AEGIS with a double purpose

The concept extraction proposes: „Die Kernwelten (Konstrukt-Stadt, Resonanz-Nebel, Schattenlabyrinth, Möglichkeitsstrom) sind von AEGIS geschaffene Simulationsumgebungen“ ^[?]. It gives them „Sie dienen einem doppelten Zweck“ ^[?], the first named being the externalisation of Kael's psyche, each world standing for a psychic function (L243). In the chapter on the simulation hypothesis it states: „Die Kernwelten sind explizit Simulationen“ ^[?], and asks whether the Überwelt and the Potentialmeer are simulations as well.
