## Reading — `kohaerenz-protokoll-duale-dramatica-storyform-synthese`, 2026-04-28, the dual storyform synthesis — Functional multiplicity as the result the report builds toward

The dual storyform synthesis (storyform letters its own) states functional multiplicity first as a prior: accepting true contradictions and integrating trauma leads „zu einer resilienten, funktionalen Multiplizität führen“ ^[?] (the prior's own wording), which it calls the epistemological centre of its analysis. It reaches it through the Solution element of Storyform B: „Das Erreichen dieses Elements ermöglicht den Übergang in die funktionale Multiplizität.“ ^[?]

In the synthesis the report names the result „5-dimensionale Architektur (Functional Multiplicity)“ ^[?], and its section heading is „Die Etablierung der Funktionalen Multiplizität“ ^[?]. In its own audit it says the architecture „priorisiert funktionale Multiplizität und die Kishōtenketsu-Struktur über klassische Modelle“ ^[?], a verdict the report itself passes.
