## Reading — `kohaerenz-protokoll-duale-dramatica-storyform-synthese`, 2026-04-28, the dual storyform synthesis — Four worlds KW1–KW4 as the epistemological landscape of the six alters

The alter table of the dual storyform synthesis (storyform letters its own) has a column of „Epistemologische Landschaft“ ^[?L106], filled with four worlds numbered KW1 to KW4 (the numbers are dropped by the export in quotations): Logos-Prime for Kael and Lex (L107, L108), Mnemosyne-Archipel for Kiko (L110), Cerberus-Labyrinth for Nyx and Moros (L109, L112), Kairos-Potentialis for Selene (L111).

Elsewhere the report uses the worlds in the plot: Kael's fight is „Kael ringt mit dem Grabenkampf zwischen seiner komfortablen Scheinrealität in“ ^[?L89] Logos-Prime and the Mnemosyne-Archipel; in Ketsu „Kael betritt“ ^[?] Kairos-Potentialis; and the Aufhebung of System Kael takes place „Parallel vollzieht sich die Aufhebung von System Kael in der Welt“ ^[?] Kairos-Potentialis.
