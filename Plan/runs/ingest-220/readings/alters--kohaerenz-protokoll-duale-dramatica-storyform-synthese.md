## Reading — `kohaerenz-protokoll-duale-dramatica-storyform-synthese`, 2026-04-28, the dual storyform synthesis — Alters in the order row and in the TSDP-Alter table

The dual storyform synthesis (storyform letters its own) mentions the alters twice in a structural way. In the isomorphy table the order row lists, under the psychological column, „Zwangskontrolle durch funktionale Alters“ ^[?], beside hypervigilance and the ANP-EP phobia. In Step 3 it sets out a table headed „TSDP-Alter“ ^[?L106] with six alters, each given a function, a Dramatica character dimension and a world (L107–L112).

The report says of the TSDP concept that it „fordert eine Aufsplittung der Dramatica-Funktionen innerhalb der Main Character Throughline“ ^[?]. In its synthesis the alters are not fused: the 5D step lets them exist „als eigenständige, orthogonale Vektoren im Raum“ ^[?L184], forming a polyphonic swarm. Of forced fusion it says that Kael „zerschlägt das Konzept der Fusion.“ ^[?]
