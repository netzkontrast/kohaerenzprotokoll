## Reading — `an-introduction-to-the-world-of-kohaerenz-protokoll`, 2025-11-03, the world introduction — the guide's Kael: a system of parts, structured by TSDP, whose integration is the goal

The world introduction, an unsigned English guide for new readers, presents Kael as a mind of parts. It says of the protagonist: „he is composed of different personality parts, a complex adaptive system fighting for survival“ ^[?]. It places the structure in a clinical frame: „structured according to the Tertiary Theory of Structural Dissociation (TSDP)“ ^[?].

The goal it names is not fusion but `functional multiplicity`, „a state of inner harmony where all his parts“ ^[?] cooperate. The guide calls the integrated self a „living Gödel sentence for AEGIS“ ^[?].

On the Foundation, the guide says access is earned: „It is only through the painful, arduous work of his own psychological integration“ ^[?]. The guide describes; it decides nothing.
