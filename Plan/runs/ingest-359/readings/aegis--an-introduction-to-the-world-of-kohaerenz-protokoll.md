## Reading — `an-introduction-to-the-world-of-kohaerenz-protokoll`, 2025-11-03, the world introduction — the guide's AEGIS: Coherence-maker, artificial god, born of the void, blind to subjective experience

The world introduction, an unsigned English guide for new readers, presents AEGIS as Kael's opponent. It gives AEGIS an aim: „AEGIS's primary objective is to create and maintain“ ^[?] Coherence, which the guide glosses as perfect stability, order and predictability. From that perspective the guide says Kael's mind is „not a person in pain but a system error“ ^[?].

On AEGIS's nature the guide writes „a kind of artificial god“ ^[?], and on its origin that it „emerged from a primordial, chaotic void called the“ ^[?] `Nichts Rauschen`, so that „Its very existence is an act of defense against this formless entropy“ ^[?].

The guide names AEGIS's flaw: „structurally blind to subjective human experiences like emotion, pain, and the potential for healing“ ^[?]. In its closing table the guide sets AEGIS's philosophy against that of the Foundation, and gives AEGIS the line „Order is imposed from the top down.“ ^[?]

The guide describes; it decides nothing.
