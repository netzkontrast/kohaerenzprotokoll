## Reading — `ki-roman-architektur-kritische-analyse-methoden`, 2026-03-01, the critical-methods framework — the Moonshine-Link as the Player Dilemma's answer and an out-of-band channel

The critical-methods framework, a methods handbook reporting the novel from its reference 1, says the system resolves the player dilemma „löst dieses Dilemma durch die Implementierung des“ ^[?] Moonshine-Link (the name follows in quotation marks). The reason it gives: AEGIS scans and blocks all semantic data packets, so the player, or the entity Juna, must inject external guidance without being deleted by the particle filters (L144).

The handbook describes the mechanism as „operiert als asymmetrischer Out-of-Band (OOB) Datenkanal“ ^[?], conceptually based on Monstrous-Moonshine mathematics and quantum non-locality. It carries „ausschließlich non-verbale Resonanzen“ ^[?] and affective variables to the receptive EPs, named as Kiko or Lia (L146). Because AEGIS classes subjectivity as irrelevant noise, the filters do not trigger; the architecture table says: „Daten passieren die Firewall unbemerkt“ ^[?].
