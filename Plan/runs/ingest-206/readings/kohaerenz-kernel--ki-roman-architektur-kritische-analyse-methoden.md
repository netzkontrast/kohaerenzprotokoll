## Reading — `ki-roman-architektur-kritische-analyse-methoden`, 2026-03-01, the critical-methods framework — the kernel of reversibility, as a methods handbook reports it

The critical-methods framework, a methods handbook reporting the architecture from its reference 1, heads its section „Der Kohärenz-Kernel“ ^[?L38] and ties it to reversibility (the kernel symbol was lost in the export, so the name stands without it). It says the kernel „operiert nach den Prinzipien der klassischen Logik und der absoluten Reversibilität“ ^[?], and in the table header the kernel is paired with AEGIS (L54).

For testing, the handbook asks that the kernel's architecture be kept apart from text generation, and names its quality criterion: „die absolute Abwesenheit von semantischem Drift über extrem lange Kontextfenster“ ^[?].
