## Reading — `ki-roman-architektur-kritische-analyse-methoden`, 2026-03-01, the critical-methods framework — Kael as a multi-agent system of eleven subsystems, as a methods handbook reports it

The critical-methods framework (an unsigned methods handbook) reports the novel's architecture from its reference 1; the agent-system vocabulary is the handbook's own. It says its method is to be applied whenever „die psychologische Isomorphie des Protagonisten Kael gefährdet scheint“ ^[?].

Citing that source, it describes Kael as „Der Protagonist Kael ist kein homogenes“ ^[?] construct but one that was „in elf hochspezialisierte Subsysteme fragmentiert wurde“ ^[?]. Among the ANPs it lists „Zu dieser Gruppe gehören der Host Kael (Alltagsmanager, maskiert die Normalität)“ ^[?], and its table puts him first among the ANP sub-entities: „Kael, Lex, Argus, Alex, Rhys“ ^[?].

In its Trace 1 the handbook has the system, steered by the integrator Selene, bring Kael past the phobic barriers: „Kael erreicht den Zustand der funktionalen Multiplizität“ ^[?]. In Trace 3 it writes „Kael erreicht das Cerberus-Labyrinth (KW3)“ ^[?], and after the logic has failed, „Kael integriert den Anteil Moros (den totalen Kollaps)“ ^[?].
