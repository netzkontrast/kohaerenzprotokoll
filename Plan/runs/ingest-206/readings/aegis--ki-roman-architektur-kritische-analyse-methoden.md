## Reading — `ki-roman-architektur-kritische-analyse-methoden`, 2026-03-01, the critical-methods framework — AEGIS as the classical-logic gatekeeper whose collapse a methods handbook tests

The critical-methods framework is a methods handbook; it reports the novel's architecture from its reference 1 and applies review methods to it, and the agent-system vocabulary around AEGIS is its own. Citing that source, it says the Kohärenz-Kernel is embodied in the narrative „im Narrativ durch die allgegenwärtige Antagonisten-Entität AEGIS“ ^[?], and that it „operiert nach den Prinzipien der klassischen Logik und der absoluten Reversibilität“ ^[?] (the kernel symbol before it was lost in the export). Toward the Emotional Personality Parts the handbook says: „Für AEGIS stellen sie das absolute, unkontrollierbare“ ^[?] noise that must be eliminated. In its Trace 1 it describes AEGIS's monitoring of the context object: „Jeder Widerspruch (A und nicht-A) wird sofort identifiziert und eliminiert“ ^[?].
