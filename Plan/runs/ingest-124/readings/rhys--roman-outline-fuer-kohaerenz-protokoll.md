## Reading — `roman-outline-fuer-kohaerenz-protokoll`, 2025-05-03, the detailed outline — Rhys as the caregiver ANP and mediator; pure care as insufficient

The detailed outline introduces Rhys in Chapter 4: the dysregulation „aktiviert Rhys, den Fürsorger-Anteil (ANP)“ ^[?]. His function: „Rhys' primäre Motivation ist es, Leid zu lindern“ ^[?]. Its character field records him as „Etabliert als dritter wichtiger ANP mit Fokus auf interne Harmonie.“ ^[?], and an archetype note says: „Rhys verkörpert den Archetyp des Fürsorgers.“ ^[?]

The outline's own Philo Hint for the chapter applies the ethics of care to him; that is the outline's application of a philosophy and is only recorded here. In Chapter 13 it lists „logische Analyse (Lex), blinde Abwehr (Alex), reine Fürsorge (Rhys), Vermeidung“ ^[?] as strategies that proved insufficient.
