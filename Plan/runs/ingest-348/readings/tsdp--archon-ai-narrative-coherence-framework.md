## Reading — `archon-ai-narrative-coherence-framework`, 2025-10-15, the ARCHON report — the ARCHON report proposes TSDP as the test of psychological coherence

The ARCHON report proposes, as a case study, that the framework be tested on the Theory of Structural Dissociation of the Personality; this is a proposal for a test, not an account of the novel. The section is headed „Testing Psychological Coherence: The Theory of Structural Dissociation (TSDP)“ ^[?]. The report states that „TSDP posits that severe trauma can prevent the integration of personality“ ^[?], and that the protagonist of the case study is „modeled on the Theory of Structural Dissociation of the Personality“ ^[?]. It states the outcome only conditionally: „Successfully modeling TSDP would demonstrate an unparalleled capability“ ^[?].
