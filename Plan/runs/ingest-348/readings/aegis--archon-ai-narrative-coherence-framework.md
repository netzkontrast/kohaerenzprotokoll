## Reading — `archon-ai-narrative-coherence-framework`, 2025-10-15, the ARCHON report — the ARCHON report proposes AEGIS as a test of logical coherence

The ARCHON report proposes, as a case study, that its framework be tested against a novel; this is a proposal for a test, not an account of the novel. In that proposed case study „The antagonist of the case study is an AI, AEGIS“ ^[?], whose „motivations and tragic failure are rooted in formal logic“ ^[?]. The report proposes that the Narrative Context Protocol encode the motivation as a formal process: „The NCP must encode AEGIS's core motivation not as a simple goal“ ^[?].
