## Reading — `kohaerenz-protokoll-kapitel-outline-erstellung`, 2026-04-30, the dual-storyform outline — an emotion-driven archipelago in Kap 7, K1 hardware in Kap 35, one of two Wächter kept

The outline plans Mnemosyne twice and the two chapters do not describe the same thing. In Kap 7 the Archipel is an environment: Kael lands in it, „einer Umgebung, deren fluide Geometrie und Wetterbedingungen sich direkt an seine emotionalen Ausbrüche koppeln“ ^[?] and the entity appears „Die Entität Mnemosyne tritt auf – nicht als Feind, sondern als manipulative Kuratorin“ ^[?] In Kap 35 the plan gives the same name another nature: „als K1-Cache-Bänke und physische Hardware von AEGIS“ ^[?] The concept line of Kap 35 writes `Mnemosyne-Archipel (Hardware)`.

Kap 22 sets it against LogOS: „LogOS (der Lösch-Pol) prallt operativ auf Mnemosyne (den Speicher-Pol).“ ^[?] Anhang C reports the count of Wächter and the choice: „Drift zwischen 5 Wächtern (Legacy) und 2 Wächtern (LogOS, Mnemosyne).“ ^[?] It also couples the pivot to the place: „Mechanischer Pivot an die Hardware (Mnemosyne-Archipel) gekoppelt“ ^[?]

- Kap 7 plans the Mnemosyne-Archipel as an environment coupled to Kael's emotions, Kap 35 as K1 cache banks and physical hardware; the outline keeps Mnemosyne as one of two Wächter and reports the other three as discarded.
