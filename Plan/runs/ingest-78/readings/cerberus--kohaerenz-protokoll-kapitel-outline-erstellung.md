## Reading — `kohaerenz-protokoll-kapitel-outline-erstellung`, 2026-04-30, the dual-storyform outline — named only as discarded

`Cerberus` stands in the outline only in the appendices. Anhang B lists it as discarded together with two others: „Kairos / Cerberus / Sophia (Wächter)“ ^[?] and gives the reason it reports from its canon source: „nur LogOS und Mnemosyne den Wächter-Dualismus bilden“ ^[?] Anhang F repeats the removal: „Nox zu Silas umgewandelt; Kairos/Cerberus gestrichen.“ ^[?] The removal is the outline's decision, recorded and not applied.
