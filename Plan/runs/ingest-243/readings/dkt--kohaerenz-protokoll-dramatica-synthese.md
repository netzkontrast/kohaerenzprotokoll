## Reading — `kohaerenz-protokoll-dramatica-synthese`, 2025-12-05, the Dramatica synthesis report — the DKT as bridge and as a source of inconsistency

The Dramatica synthesis report writes: „Die Dual Kernel Theory (DKT) dient als Brücke zwischen der“ ^[?] Objective Story (Universe) and the Subjective Story (Desire) (L158). It uses the DKT as a figure for the relation of Kael and Juna: „Die Anziehung zwischen Kael und Juna fungiert wie die Gravitation in der DKT“ ^[?]. Its `Implikation:` line (L94) is an instruction to the author: „Die physikalischen Gesetze der DKT müssen Inkonsistenzen zulassen, um realistisch zu sein“ ^[?]. Step 1 of its Resonanz-Kodex asks the author to accept the DKT as law (L205).
