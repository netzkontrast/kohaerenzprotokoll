## Reading — `kohaerenz-protokoll-aktuelle-gesamtkonzept-synthese`, 2025-04-26, the concept synthesis — AEGIS as a boundary-making entity, limited by its own protocols

The synthesis calls itself „den aktuellen, kohärenten Stand“ ^[?]; recorded, not applied.
