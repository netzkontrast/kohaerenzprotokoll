## Reading — `kohaerenz-protokoll-aktuelle-gesamtkonzept-synthese`, 2025-04-26, the concept synthesis — Kael as M-avatar, DID as AEGIS's doing, his journey as integration of his parts

The concept synthesis, a German abstract, lists Kael in its section IV, `Der Protagonist: Kael, Fragmentierung und Integration`. It defines him as „Kael als M-Avatar/Essenz“ ^[?]: he „verkörpert oder ist direkt verbunden mit der M-inspirierten Struktur“ ^[?], and „Seine Natur ist inhärent integrativ und kohärent“ ^[?]. The abstract puts the `Kohärenz-Insel` in brackets after the M-structure; elsewhere it names the Potentialmeer as potentially containing such islands „als Ursprung von Kael/Julia“ ^[?].

His dissociation is placed as an effect: Kaels DID „ist keine inhärente Eigenschaft“ ^[?] but a consequence of AEGIS's analytic trauma, „dem Versuch, seine irreduzible Kernstruktur gewaltsam zu zerlegen“ ^[?]. His path is described as „Kommunikation, des Verständnisses und der Integration“ ^[?] of his parts under the Selbst, and his experience of fragmented reality is said to be described by phenomenological principles (L39). The statement on his origin (L36) and on his DID (L37) closes with a `Quellen:` list naming other documents; the abstract records them, and this reading does not treat them as its own evidence.
