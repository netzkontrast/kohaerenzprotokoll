## Reading — `the-coherence-protocol-a-world-bible`, 2025-10-15, the world bible — AEGIS as a tragic system, its axiom and its flaw

The world bible defines AEGIS (Autonomous Entropic Gatekeeper for Integrity Systems) as the narrative's central antagonist that „is not a villain in the traditional sense“ ^[?], but „a tragic, autopoietic (self-creating and self-sustaining) system“ ^[?]. It describes the origin in these words: „AEGIS emerged not from a grand design“ ^[?]. Its axiom stands as „AEGIS is what AEGIS prevents itself from not being.“ ^[?] and is read as „existence through negation“ ^[?].

On the architecture it says that AEGIS is not monolithic but run by „a sophisticated, layered hybrid architecture“ ^[?]; the adaptive layer, driven by the objective „Maximize Coherence“ ^[?], makes the system learn to become „a more effective tyrant“ ^[?]. It acts as a gatekeeper: „actively filtering and negating incursions from The Nothingness Noise“ ^[?].

The flaw is named in a section on Paradoxon X: „The central, tragic flaw of AEGIS is the paradox embedded in its very definition of coherence.“ ^[?] Its aesthetic is called „Algorithmic Horror“ ^[?]. The document introduces itself as „canonical and authoritative reference“ ^[?]; that is its claim, recorded and not applied here.
