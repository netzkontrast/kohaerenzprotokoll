## Reading — `juna`, 2025-07-30, the Fundament and Juna/V concept — the Gambit and the Satz at the same place in the two passes

The Fundament and Juna/V concept has Juna/V help Kael in the finale; the second pass writes the presented state as „Gödel-Gambit“ ^[?L95], the first pass at the same place as „Gödel-Satz“ ^[?L43]. Both passes state that this means presenting the integrated state of Kael directly to the core processor of AEGIS: „direkt dem Kernprozessor von AEGIS zu präsentieren, was eine systemische Krise erzwingt“ ^[?L95]. The second pass describes the act as „ein epistemologischer Sieg, der einen selbstreferenziellen Widerspruch im Herzen des Systems erzwingt“ ^[?L95]. The two names are recorded as the document writes them; the document does not say they are one thing.
