## Reading — `juna`, 2025-07-30, the Fundament and Juna/V concept — the Moonshine-Link as non-local, sub-systemic connection

The Fundament and Juna/V concept names the connection between Kael and Juna/V the Moonshine-Link and states that it „wird als nicht-lokal und sub-systemisch definiert“ ^[?L39]. The mechanism is explained by a synthesis of quantum entanglement and a concept of prehension, giving „eine nicht-lokale, akausale, sub-protokollarische Resonanz“ ^[?L41]. The second pass adds that Kael and Juna/V are conceived as „zwei Teile eines einzigen, untrennbaren Quantensystems konzipiert“ ^[?L89]. At L91 the second pass reads the Kael-Juna connection as a manifestation of the phenomenon Monstrous Moonshine, which joins the Monster Group embodied by Kael to modular functions; the line has inner quotation marks, so it is described here and not quoted. The first pass states the link at L39 and L41, the second at L89 and L91.
