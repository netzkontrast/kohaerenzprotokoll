## Reading — `juna`, 2025-07-30, the Fundament and Juna/V concept — Juna/V as a source of entropy for AEGIS

The Fundament and Juna/V concept states that AEGIS perceives Juna/V as a threat to its integrity: „eine kritische Quelle von Entropie und Instabilität wahr, die die Systemintegrität bedroht“ ^[?L97]. The sentence reports how AEGIS takes Juna/V; it is the concept's statement of AEGIS's perspective, not a definition of entropy.
