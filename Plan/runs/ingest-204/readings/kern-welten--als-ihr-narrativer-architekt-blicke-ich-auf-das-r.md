## Reading — `als-ihr-narrativer-architekt-blicke-ich-auf-das-r`, 2025-07-30, the final causal blueprint — four worlds named by their labels KW1 to KW4 across the chapters

The final causal blueprint never lists the Kernwelten in one place; it names them where a chapter enters them, by the labels KW1 to KW4. KW1 is the first world: Kael wakes „gefolgt von Kaels Erwachen in der sterilen Konstrukt-Stadt“ ^[?] (chapter 1). KW2 holds the lake of tears (chapter 4, L36), and chapter 7 has Kael enter „Kael betritt unwillentlich die Resonanz-Landschaft“ ^[?] there; the same line sends him back into an inner bunker, KW3 with the Cerberus labyrinth (chapter 7), and chapter 18 describes the labyrinth as „eine Externalisierung seiner tiefsten Ängste.“ ^[?] KW4 is the Möglichkeiten-Garten, glimpsed in chapter 9: „Er erhascht einen Blick auf“ ^[?] it. In chapter 21 Kael reaches it: „und lernt, dass Kreativität“ ^[?] arises from uncertainty.

KW2 and KW3 also appear in chapter 8 as zones to avoid, from the perspective of Lex (L56). Chapter 15 sets a Guardian in KW2: „Konfrontation mit Guardian Mnemosyne in“ ^[?] it. The outline pairs the worlds with no Guardians beyond this chapter and calls no Kernwelt an act marker.
