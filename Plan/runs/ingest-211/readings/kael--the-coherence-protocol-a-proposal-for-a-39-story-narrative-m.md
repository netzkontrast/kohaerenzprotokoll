## Reading — `the-coherence-protocol-a-proposal-for-a-39-story-narrative-m`, 2025-11-03, the 39-story mosaic — Kael as System Kael, a TSDP-modelled host, and a settled name and gender

On the psychological axis, his consciousness „is modeled on the clinical“ ^[?] Theory of Structural Dissociation of the Personality (TSDP).
