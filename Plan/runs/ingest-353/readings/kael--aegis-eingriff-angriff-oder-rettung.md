## Reading — `aegis-eingriff-angriff-oder-rettung`, 2025-10-16, the Angriff-oder-Rettung dossier — System Kael as a fragmented, highly vulnerable subject, and what each thesis makes of the intervention

In the dossier's own framing (Abschnitt I) the system is placed in „Tertiäre Strukturelle Dissoziation“ ^[?L53]. Its inner landscape is „ein Ökosystem des Konflikts“ ^[?], and its conflicts and barriers make up „ein detailliertes Verwundbarkeitsprofil“ ^[?]. Of the relation to AEGIS it says „System Kael ist ebenfalls ein System, dessen Fragmentierung eine adaptive Überlebensstrategie ist“ ^[?].

The intervention is triggered, the dossier says, by „kritischen Anomalie innerhalb von System Kael“ ^[?].

Under These A the dossier argues AEGIS forces a repetition: „sein Kerntrauma in einer von AEGIS kontrollierten Umgebung wiederzuerleben“ ^[?]. Under These B it argues the rescue is not for the system's sake: „Er rettet Kael nicht um Kaels willen“ ^[?].
<!-- differ -->
- These A reads the intervention as deliberate retraumatisation of System Kael; These B as a self-interested reset that is not done for Kael's sake. The dossier chooses neither.
