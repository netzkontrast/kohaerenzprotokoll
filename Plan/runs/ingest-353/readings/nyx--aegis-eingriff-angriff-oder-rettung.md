## Reading — `aegis-eingriff-angriff-oder-rettung`, 2025-10-16, the Angriff-oder-Rettung dossier — Nyx as a fighting part, and the intervention driving Nyx toward Paradoxon X

In the dossier's own account of System Kael, Nyx stands for fight against Kiko's flight: „Nyx' Kampf vs. Kikos Flucht“ ^[?]. Of the intervention's effects (Abschnitt II, the evidence both theses read) it says the intervention „Er kann den Kampf-Anteil Nyx in den kritischen Zustand des“ ^[?] `Paradoxon X` (the dossier: a catastrophic system collapse). In Abschnitt V, for These A, it names Nyx among the parts through which the attack could be told: „Die Erzählperspektive sollte durch Kaels traumatisierte EPs (wie Kiko oder Nyx) fokussiert werden“ ^[?].
