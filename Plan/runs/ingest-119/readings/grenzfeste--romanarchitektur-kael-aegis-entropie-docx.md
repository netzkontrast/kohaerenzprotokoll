## Reading — `romanarchitektur-kael-aegis-entropie-docx`, 2025-08-05, the architecture plan — KW3 as defence, protection and borders

Kapitel 9 of the architecture plan is titled „Die Mauern der Grenzfeste“ ^[?]. It proposes „Erkundung der dritten Kernwelt (KW3), der Grenzfeste“ ^[?], and says that „Diese Welt repräsentiert Abwehr, Schutzmechanismen, Grenzen“ ^[?], möglicherweise also avoidance strategies. It names it the domain of Cerberus, the Guardian responsible for security and defence.
