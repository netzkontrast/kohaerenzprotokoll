## Reading — `romanarchitektur-kael-aegis-entropie-docx`, 2025-08-05, the architecture plan — Sophia, alongside or alternative to Kairos, in the fourth Kernwelt

The architecture plan proposes `Sophia` for Kapitel 17, with Kairos. Its section says Kael meets Kairos „und/oder Sophia (Weisheit, Wissen)“ ^[?L295], and that they möglicherweise try to steer or prune his potential. Kapitel 17 warns of „die Gefahr der subtilen Manipulation durch Kairos/Sophia“ ^[?], and Kapitel 16 asks whether one Guardian, „vielleicht einer der weniger rigiden wie Kairos/Sophia?“ ^[?] shows doubt of AEGIS. In Kapitel 15 she is listed as „evtl. Kairos & Sophia“ ^[?L267] among the Guardians in the Überwelt.
