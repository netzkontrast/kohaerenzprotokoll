## Reading — `romanarchitektur-kael-aegis-entropie-docx`, 2025-08-05, the architecture plan — TSDP as the source of the narration and of the phobias between parts

The architecture plan builds the first part of the novel on TSDP. Its Gesamtfokus for Teil 1 says that „Die Darstellung von TSDP-Symptomen“ ^[?] such as amnesia and dissociative switches shapes the narrative perspective. Kapitel 1 sets the unreliability of the narrator as „eine direkte Manifestation der TSDP-Symptomatik“ ^[?]. Kapitel 7 (Phobien im System) has as its theme „Interne Barrieren und die Angst voreinander“ ^[?], named „ein Kernmerkmal von TSDP“ ^[?], and Kapitel 3 and 4 give psychological notes on protector parts („Die Funktion von Beschützer-Anteilen in TSDP-Systemen“ ^[?]) and on the ANPs. Kapitel 6 introduces the Emotional Parts (EPs), which hold the traumata (L137).
