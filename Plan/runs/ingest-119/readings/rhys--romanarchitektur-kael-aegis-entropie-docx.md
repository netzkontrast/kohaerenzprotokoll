## Reading — `romanarchitektur-kael-aegis-entropie-docx`, 2025-08-05, the architecture plan — the care part and internal mediation

In Kapitel 4 (Stimmen der Fürsorge) the architecture plan sets: „Einführung des Fürsorge-Anteils Rhys“ ^[?], whose main function is inner harmony and emotional regulation. It proposes that this part „versucht, zwischen den zunehmenden Spannungen“ ^[?] to mediate, and, as a subplot, „beginnt, heimlich Kommunikationsversuche“ ^[?] between hostile parts. It also casts the part as an ANP, calling it „Die Rolle von ANPs“ ^[?] in daily life (Kapitel 4, L109), and in Kapitel 18 as one who might guide trauma work with the Selene/Selbst figure (L310).
