## Reading — `romanarchitektur-kael-aegis-entropie-docx`, 2025-08-05, the architecture plan — an integration figure that emerges over Teil 2 and 3

The architecture plan (`Thematische & Narrative Architektur: System Kael vs. AEGIS`) writes in the conditional and names the figure only as it emerges. In Kapitel 17 (Die Gärten des Potenzials) it proposes that Rhys and vielleicht „die entstehende Integrationsfigur Selene/Selbst“ ^[?] could unfold in the fourth world. In Kapitel 18 it proposes that Rhys and „die aufkommende Selene/Selbst-Figur“ ^[?] could guide the trauma work. In Kapitel 27 the figure „tritt möglicherweise stärker hervor“ ^[?] to coordinate the system. In Kapitel 31 it sets that the figure „etabliert sich als koordinierendes Zentrum“ ^[?], and proposes „inneren Rat“ ^[?] at which all important parts contribute. The plan writes both `Selene/Selbst` and, alone, `Selene` (Kapitel 22, L366).
