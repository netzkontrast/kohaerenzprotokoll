## Reading — `romanarchitektur-kael-aegis-entropie-docx`, 2025-08-05, the architecture plan — KW2 as the opposite of KW1

Kapitel 5 of the architecture plan is titled „Der Ruf der Resonanz-Landschaft“ ^[?]. It proposes of the second world: „Diese Welt ist das Gegenteil von KW1“ ^[?], chaotic and fluid, shaped by emotions and memories. The plan sets that it represents „die unterdrückten affektiven Zustände von System Kael“ ^[?] and möglicherweise a domain that AEGIS can hardly control.
