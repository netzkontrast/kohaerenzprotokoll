## Reading — `romanarchitektur-kael-aegis-entropie-docx`, 2025-08-05, the architecture plan — Risse as local manifestations of Entropie and signs of AEGIS's limited control

The architecture plan proposes the first Riss for Kapitel 11, `Der erste Riss`. Its Vertiefende Konzepte say this Riss is „eine lokale Manifestation von Entropie“ ^[?], an error in AEGIS's control. Its trope entry for the Glitch in the Matrix (the plan's own `Innovation` line, a claim about the borrowed trope) calls the glitch „sondern ein ontologischer Bruch“ ^[?] and says „Die Risse sind Symptome des grundlegenden Konflikts“ ^[?] between control system and consciousness. The chapter's closing field introduces the Risse „als sichtbares Zeichen der Systeminstabilität“ ^[?], and its subplot has „Ein Anteil wird kurzzeitig durch den“ ^[?] Riss pulled, returning with a fragmentary memory.

Kapitel 16 says AEGIS's methods lead to „Instabilität, Widerstand und den“ ^[?L281] Rissen im System.
