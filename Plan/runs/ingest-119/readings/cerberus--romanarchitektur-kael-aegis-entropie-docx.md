## Reading — `romanarchitektur-kael-aegis-entropie-docx`, 2025-08-05, the architecture plan — Cerberus as the AEGIS-Guardian of the third Kernwelt

The architecture plan proposes `Cerberus` as the Guardian of the third Kernwelt, the Grenzfeste. Kapitel 9 sets him as „dem AEGIS-Guardian, der für Sicherheit, Abgrenzung und die Abwehr“ ^[?] of external, vielleicht also internal, threats. Its Charakter-/Systemdynamik field says the confrontation shows AEGIS's definition of Sicherheit and Bedrohung, „wahrscheinlich alles, was die etablierte Ordnung“ ^[?] puts in question. The trope line for Guardian at the Threshold (the plan's own `Innovation` claim) says his function is ambiguous: „Er schützt AEGIS' System“ ^[?] and, by repelling Kael, vielleicht also shields Kael. The Fortress trope is placed between „AEGIS/Cerberus' Kontrollmechanismen“ ^[?] and Kael's inner walls. Kapitel 14 returns to him for AEGIS's defence protocols, with „Interaktion mit Cerberus“ ^[?L253].
