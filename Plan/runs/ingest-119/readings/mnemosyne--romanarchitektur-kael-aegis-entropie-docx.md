## Reading — `romanarchitektur-kael-aegis-entropie-docx`, 2025-08-05, the architecture plan — Mnemosyne as a question mark for the second Kernwelt

The architecture plan does not set `Mnemosyne` as the Guardian of the Resonanz-Landschaft but asks. In Kapitel 5 it says the second Kernwelt is möglicherweise a domain AEGIS can hardly control, then adds in brackets „Wächterin der Erinnerung“ ^[?] with a question mark after the name. Its trope line for the World of Symbolism (Kapitel 5) says that AEGIS's attempts to order this world, „durch Mnemosyne?“ ^[?], appear as suppression of feeling. The table pairs her with the EPs for Kapitel 6: „Erste Begegnung mit traumatischen Echos (EPs & Mnemosyne)“ ^[?L22]. In Kapitel 15 she stands in „Begegnung mit den Guardians (LogOS, Mnemosyne, Cerberus, evtl. Kairos & Sophia)“ ^[?L267] in the Überwelt.
