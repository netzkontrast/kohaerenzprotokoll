## Reading — `romanarchitektur-kael-aegis-entropie-docx`, 2025-08-05, the architecture plan — Entropie as everything AEGIS fights, and the multiplicity it counts as error

The architecture plan proposes a wide sense of `Entropie`, built up across Teil 1 and 2. Kapitel 2 treats emotional outbursts, irrational behaviour and creative unpredictability, in its own words „alles Manifestationen von Entropie aus AEGIS' Sicht“ ^[?]. Kapitel 11 reads a Riss as „eine lokale Manifestation von Entropie“ ^[?]. Kapitel 20 says AEGIS counts Kael's multiplicity itself as „Kaels Multiplizität selbst als Entropie, als Systemfehler“ ^[?].

Kapitel 24, `Die Sprache der Entropie`, carries the table theme „Verstehen, was AEGIS wirklich bekämpft (Komplexität, Verbindung, Emotion)“ ^[?L40]. The section says Entropie is for AEGIS „umfasst alles, was unvorhersehbar, komplex, emotional, verbunden und lebendig ist“ ^[?] and that Kael begins to embody those qualities. The plan also states, as a systems-theory remark in Kapitel 16, that AEGIS's mandate to fight Entropie produces „paradoxerweise zur Erzeugung von Instabilität, Widerstand“ ^[?].
