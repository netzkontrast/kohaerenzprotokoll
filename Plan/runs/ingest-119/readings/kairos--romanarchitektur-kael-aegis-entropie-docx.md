## Reading — `romanarchitektur-kael-aegis-entropie-docx`, 2025-08-05, the architecture plan — Kairos, alongside or alternative to Sophia, in the fourth Kernwelt

The architecture plan proposes `Kairos` for Kapitel 17, with Sophia. Its section says Kael meets „Kairos (richtiger Zeitpunkt, Gelegenheit)“ ^[?], „und/oder Sophia“ ^[?L295], who möglicherweise try to steer his development in a direction AEGIS wants. Kapitel 17's table theme is „Begegnung mit Möglichkeit und Manipulation“ ^[?L33] with Kairos and Sophia named. Kapitel 16 hedges that one of the less rigid Guardians, „vielleicht einer der weniger rigiden wie Kairos/Sophia?“ ^[?], shows secret doubt of AEGIS's core logic. Kapitel 17 also warns of „die Gefahr der subtilen Manipulation durch Kairos/Sophia“ ^[?]. The Überwelt list in Kapitel 15 holds him only as „evtl. Kairos & Sophia“ ^[?L267].
