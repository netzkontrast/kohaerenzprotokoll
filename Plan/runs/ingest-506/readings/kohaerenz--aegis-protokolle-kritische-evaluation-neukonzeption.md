## Reading — `aegis-protokolle-kritische-evaluation-neukonzeption`, 2025-04-19, the AEGIS protocol critique — coherence as condition of existence, and its undefined operationalisation

The AEGIS protocol critique states: „AEGIS postuliert Kohärenz als Existenzbedingung“ ^[?]. It then asks what this means operationally and offers several candidate readings (logic, structural compatibility, resonance, prediction-error minimisation, fixed point, type system) without choosing one. Its conjecture is that „Die hypothetischen AEGIS-Protokolle haben wahrscheinlich unterschiedliche, inkompatible Facetten von Kohärenz operationalisiert“ ^[?].

Earlier it anticipates the same inconsistency: „Solche Abweichungen führen unweigerlich zu logischen Widersprüchen und Integrationsfehlern“ ^[?], where the deviations are the hypothetical sketches defining coherence structurally, functionally or relationally (L31). All of this is hypothetical by the critique's own account.
