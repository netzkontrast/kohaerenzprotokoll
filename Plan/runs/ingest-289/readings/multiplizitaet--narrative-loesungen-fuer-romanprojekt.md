## Reading — `narrative-loesungen-fuer-romanprojekt`, 2025-04-17, the narrative-solutions report — functional multiplicity as integration, shown as process

The narrative-solutions report states as the project's ambition to show a state of „funktionalen Multiplizität“ ^[?L15] as a form of integration. Its section on this question proposes that integration be shown „nicht als simplifizierte Heilung oder statischen Endpunkt darzustellen“ ^[?], but as a dynamic state of coexistence and cooperation among the personas. As its conceptual frame it names Dialogical Self Theory and says of it: „Integration bedeutet hier nicht Stille, sondern produktiven Dialog“ ^[?]. Among the points left to clarify it asks how the transition is to be shown, in the words „zur funktionalen Multiplizität konkret dargestellt“ ^[?L277]; the report leaves this as a question for stylistic elaboration.
