## Reading — `the-coherence-protocol-a-definitive-guide-to-the-narrative-a`, 2025-11-03, the Coherence Protocol guide — the guide sets `Overhead` as a protocol's cost, drawn from cache coherence protocols

The guide defines `Overhead` in a glossary entry as „the continuous energy expenditure a protocol requires to maintain its coherence“ ^[?L46], and says it is „Drawing a direct parallel to cache coherence protocols in computer architecture“ ^[?L46]. It names the cost „the active, ongoing cost of being“ ^[?L46] and sets that a protocol fails once the cost outruns what it can acquire: „A protocol collapses when the energy cost of its Overhead exceeds the resources it can acquire.“ ^[?L46] The lexicon table repeats the term, giving its definition as „The continuous energy expenditure a protocol requires to maintain its coherence against the pressure of K₀“ ^[?L60] and its source concept as `Cache Coherence Overhead`. Here the metaphor is a physical cost in the story's world (Persistence is „a finite resource, not a given“ ^[?L60]), not a memory fault in Kael's psyche. The guide speaks as a binding specification; this is recorded, not applied.
