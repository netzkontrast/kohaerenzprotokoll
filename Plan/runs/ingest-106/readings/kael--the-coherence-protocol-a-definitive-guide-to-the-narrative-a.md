## Reading — `the-coherence-protocol-a-definitive-guide-to-the-narrative-a`, 2025-11-03, the Coherence Protocol guide — Act I as Kael's internal journey, Act III as his external confrontation

The guide sets Act I, „5.1 Act I: The Heroine's Journey (Chapters 1-13)“ ^[?L151], as an internal journey: „The focus is on self-discovery, confronting the reality of fragmentation“ ^[?L153]. Here `Heroine's Journey` names the act's internal journey; it says nothing of Kael's gender. Act III, „5.3 Act III: The Hero's Journey (Chapters 27-39)“ ^[?L163], is the external one: „Kael is equipped to take the fight to AEGIS“ ^[?L165], „turning his integrated self into a weapon against the system that fragmented him“ ^[?L165]. The guide sets this as specification; it is recorded, not applied.
