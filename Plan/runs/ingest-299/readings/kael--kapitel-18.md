## Reading — `kapitel-18`, 2025-04-20, the Kapitel-18 plan — Kael as the one LogOS cannot model, and the perspective problem

The Kapitel-18 plan proposes that `LogOS` receives the data on „Kaels Überlebenstaktiken“ ^[?], and that its models fail because „Kaels Resilienz und die Juna-Anomalie passen nicht“ ^[?]. It proposes that modelling the Kael–Juna link ends in an „unauflösbaren Widerspruch“ ^[?], and that his internal order raises his external unpredictability, „durch Kommunikation der Alters“ ^[?]. On perspective the plan notes „Da der Roman primär aus Kaels subjektiver Sicht erzählt wird“ ^[?] and offers as one option „kurze, quasi-objektive Vignetten“ ^[?] for `LogOS`. For the end it asks, with a question mark, about an „Indirekte Auswirkung auf Kael?“ ^[?]. A plan of options, not the chapter.
