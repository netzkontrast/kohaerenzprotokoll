## Reading — `system-kael-konzeptentwicklung-und-analyse`, 2025-06-24, the concept synthesis — the safe, co-regulating bond, and the central conflict as a struggle for bonding

The synthesis sets Juna against AEGIS as the second polarising force: „Die Juna/V-Verbindung hingegen repräsentiert das genaue Gegenteil“ ^[?], the possibility of a safe, co-regulating bond (reference 1, glued `1`); „Juna/V steht für bedingungslose Akzeptanz, Mitgefühl“ ^[?] It names the novel's central conflict in its own words as a „Kampf um Bindung“ ^[?]: „Das System Kael ist zerrissen zwischen der Anziehungskraft der sicheren Bindung“ ^[?] (Juna) and dependence on AEGIS. The questions of Part IV about what Juna is are questions, not claims.
