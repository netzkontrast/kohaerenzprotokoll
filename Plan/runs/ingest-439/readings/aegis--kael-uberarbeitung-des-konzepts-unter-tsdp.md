## Reading — `kael-uberarbeitung-des-konzepts-unter-tsdp`, 2025-04-28, the TSDP revision of Kael — AEGIS read as cause or sustainer of Kael's dissociation

This unsigned character concept is fiction about Kael, and the TSDP revision proposes that AEGIS's acts be read through structural dissociation. It states the central assumption that Kael's personality „nicht integriert wurde“ ^[?] after overwhelming trauma, possibly under AEGIS, and the text says „AEGIS' Handeln wird in diesem Licht nicht nur als Manipulation“ ^[?] read, but as potential active causing or maintaining of the dissociative structure. In the profile it hedges: „AEGIS könnte hierbei eine direkte Rolle spielen“ ^[?]. Among the conflict dynamics, the trigger mechanism is hedged the same way: „AEGIS könnte diese Trigger gezielt nutzen“ ^[?]. The Pressure Point section proposes that AEGIS's control methods (surveillance, manipulation, punishment, forced state switches, the last with a question mark in the text) can be interpreted „als fortlaufende Traumatisierung interpretiert werden“ ^[?] that maintains or deepens the dissociation. Section 6 states that AEGIS „Wird noch deutlicher als Antagonist positioniert“ ^[?].
