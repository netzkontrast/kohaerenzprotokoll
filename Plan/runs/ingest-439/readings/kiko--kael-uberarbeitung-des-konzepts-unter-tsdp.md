## Reading — `kael-uberarbeitung-des-konzepts-unter-tsdp`, 2025-04-28, the TSDP revision of Kael — Kiko as an emotional part carrying the childlike trauma

The TSDP revision proposes Kiko, labelled `Das Kind` (L48), as an emotional part: „Verkörpert die kindlichen Affekte und Erinnerungen“ ^[?] from the time of the trauma. The stated function is „Trägt die Kernemotionen des Traumas“ ^[?]. In the conflict dynamics Lex, as an ANP, tries „unterdrücken oder zu kontrollieren“ ^[?] Kiko and Nyx, and triggers can activate Kiko (L95). Kiko is also named as the part out of place in a strongly structured ANP world (L97).
