## Reading — `kael-uberarbeitung-des-konzepts-unter-tsdp`, 2025-04-28, the TSDP revision of Kael — triggers in the Kernwelten

The TSDP revision proposes under EP intrusions that triggers in the Kernwelten (places, sounds, emotions) can activate emotional parts of Kael unbidden: „Trigger in den Kernwelten (Orte, Geräusche, Emotionen) können EPs (Kiko, Nyx) ungewollt aktivieren“ ^[?], leading in the same sentence to sudden state changes and flashbacks.
