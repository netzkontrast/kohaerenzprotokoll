## Reading — `kael-uberarbeitung-des-konzepts-unter-tsdp`, 2025-04-28, the TSDP revision of Kael — Kael as a system of structural dissociation

The TSDP revision of Kael is an unsigned German character concept, and what follows is what it proposes for the character. Its first section is headed „Einleitung: Kael als System Struktureller Dissoziation“ ^[?], and it positions TSDP as „das primäre Erklärungsmodell“ ^[?] for Kael's fragmented state. The central assumption is that Kael's personality „nicht integriert wurde“ ^[?] after overwhelming trauma. The profile gives the core problem as structural dissociation, hedged with „vermutlich Tertiäre Dissoziation, gekennzeichnet durch multiple ANPs und multiple EPs, was die Komplexität seiner“ ^[?] Personas. The conclusion calls the frame „klinisch fundierte und strukturell klare Basis“ ^[?] for the characterisation of Kael.
