## Reading — `kael-uberarbeitung-des-konzepts-unter-tsdp`, 2025-04-28, the TSDP revision of Kael — Nyx as an emotional part fixed on fight or flight

The TSDP revision proposes Nyx, labelled `Der Schatten` (L57), as „Primär ein EP, wahrscheinlich“ ^[?] fixed on fight or flight defence reactions; the text continues „Verkörpert die Wut, Aggression oder panische Angst“ ^[?]. The function is „Schutz des Systems (insbesondere anderer EPs wie Kiko)“ ^[?] by aggressive defence or flight. The text hedges that Nyx might also have traits of an ANP (L61). Lex tries to suppress Nyx (L94), and triggers can activate Nyx (L95).
