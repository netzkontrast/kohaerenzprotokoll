## Reading — `kael-uberarbeitung-des-konzepts-unter-tsdp`, 2025-04-28, the TSDP revision of Kael — Rhys as a probable ANP of social interaction

The TSDP revision proposes Rhys, labelled `Der Relationale Anteil` (L66), as „Wahrscheinlich ein ANP“ ^[?], specialised in social interaction and attachment. The text says Rhys „Versucht, durch Empathie und Harmoniestreben“ ^[?] to secure functioning in social contexts, and the function is „Aufrechterhaltung sozialer Verbindungen“ ^[?]. In the conflict dynamics Rhys is the example of an ANP that may be dysfunctional where EP triggers exist, as in the Grenzfeste (L97), and of conflict with Lex (L96).
