## Reading — `kael-uberarbeitung-des-konzepts-unter-tsdp`, 2025-04-28, the TSDP revision of Kael — the Grenzfeste as an environment holding EP triggers

The TSDP revision proposes that a part can be maladapted to its environment: an ANP (Rhys is the example) „kann in einer Umgebung“ ^[?] be dysfunctional when the environment contains EP triggers, named `Grenzfeste` in parentheses, because its strategies are dangerous there; the document's word for the strategy is „(Offenheit)“ ^[?]. The example is given as an illustration of a dynamic, not as a description of the place.
