## Reading — `strategic-narrative-analysis-forging-a-compelling-story-from`, 2025-11-03, the strategy memo — the memo maps the four Kernwelten to alters and uses them as its tool

The strategy memo plans the four Kernwelten as a diagnostic chart: „each Kernwelt (Core World) mapped to specific psychological functions“ ^[?]. KW1 is the domain of Lex, „Kael's rational part“ ^[?]; KW2 that of the Emotional Parts; KW3 „The domain of the protectors“ ^[?], Alex and Nyx; KW4 „The domain of the healer and relational parts“ ^[?], with Lia, Rhys and Selene.

Among its strategies the memo proposes that the Kernwelten „are more than settings; they are our primary tool“ ^[?] for making abstract ideas sensory.
