## Reading — `strategic-narrative-analysis-forging-a-compelling-story-from`, 2025-11-03, the strategy memo — a plan for the unified protagonist and his goal

The strategy memo proposes a design in which Kael's inner state shows in his world: his internal psychological state is „causally and directly manifested in his external, simulated reality“ ^[?]. It plans to map each Kernwelt to his mind: „each Kernwelt (Core World) mapped to specific psychological functions“ ^[?].

It stages the conflict as „two opposing models of order“ ^[?], AEGIS against Kael. For Kael's ending it proposes a goal: „a state of cooperation and co-consciousness“ ^[?], the memo's gloss of functional multiplicity.

For the final act it gives him a role: „Kael's ultimate purpose is not as a destroyer of the old order, but as a cultivator of a new one“ ^[?] – the Gardener, a proposal and not a statement about the novel.
