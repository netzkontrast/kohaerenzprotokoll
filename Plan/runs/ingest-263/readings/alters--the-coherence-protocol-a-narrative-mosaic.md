## Reading — `the-coherence-protocol-a-narrative-mosaic`, 2025-11-03, the narrative mosaic brief — the internal parts as a roster, to be written in close third person

The narrative mosaic brief groups the alters in its roster as „The Internal Parts (Alters)“ ^[?] and gives the instruction „To be written in close third-person“ ^[?]. The lines that follow list the parts one per line, each marked as an ANP or an EP by its class, for example „The rationalist ANP, obsessed with logical order“ ^[?] and „The child EP, embodying“ ^[?]. Each is to be assigned to exactly one of its stories. It is a brief for stories not written; the roster characterises points of view.
