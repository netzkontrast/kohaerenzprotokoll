## Reading — `the-coherence-protocol-a-narrative-mosaic`, 2025-11-03, the narrative mosaic brief — the primary host as the first-person point of view, and the collection's model

The narrative mosaic brief names its protagonist in the introduction: stories meant to „the fragmented consciousness of the protagonist, System Kael“ ^[?]. In its roster Kael is the host, „The primary host personality, focused on maintaining a facade of normalcy“ ^[?], „grappling with amnesia, depersonalization, and incoherence“ ^[?]. The first story's instruction asks to „Write a story that establishes Kael's precarious status quo“ ^[?]. The roster also describes Juna/V as the exiled part of his Ursprungs-Ich („The exiled part of Kael's“ ^[?]). The conclusion draws the model for the whole collection from him: „Like System Kael itself, the collection's ultimate meaning is not contained within any single part“ ^[?]. It is a brief for stories not written; the roster characterises points of view.
