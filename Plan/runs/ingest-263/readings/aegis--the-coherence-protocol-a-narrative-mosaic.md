## Reading — `the-coherence-protocol-a-narrative-mosaic`, 2025-11-03, the narrative mosaic brief — the antagonist as a roster of points of view, with a core logic called Component 734

The narrative mosaic brief frames the collection around „the tragic, logic-bound reality of his antagonist, AEGIS“ ^[?]. Its roster lists AEGIS's side as points of view to be written „in a cool, analytical log-format or an objectified third-person perspective“ ^[?]. The first entry is the AEGIS Core Logic, described as „The perspective of the traumatized“ ^[?] and as „after its fragmentation, now a functional component defined by the prime directive“ ^[?], with the directive „Aegis is what Aegis prevents itself from not being“ ^[?]. The second is „An overarching, impersonal log detailing system operations, resource allocation, anomalies“ ^[?], and the Guardians follow: „An unthinking enforcer of AEGIS's will, viewing Kael as a system anomaly to be corrected“ ^[?], against a rebel guardian who „begins to question AEGIS's logic and becomes a secret ally to Kael“ ^[?]. In the instruction for its Component 734 entry the brief asks for „the cold, functional perspective of the AEGIS component that was once the“ ^[?] Ursprungs-Ich, whose task is maintaining the simulation. the brief's darkest-hour entry has a Guardian at the boundary of „the formless, mindless chaos that AEGIS was born to hold back“ ^[?]. It is a brief for stories not written; the roster characterises points of view.
