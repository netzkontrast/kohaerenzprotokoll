## Reading — `project-coherence-protocol-narrative-blueprint-decisions`, 2025-10-15, the locked blueprint — a table of four Core Worlds, each with a governing logic

The locked blueprint offers a table as „a definitive reference for the creative team“ ^[?]. It names four Core Worlds with their governing principle (L101–L104): `Logos-Prime` with „Classical Logic“ ^[?L101], `Mnemosyne-Archipel` with „Paraconsistent Logic“ ^[?L102], `Cerberus-Labyrinth` with „Relevance Logic“ ^[?], and `Kairos-Potentialis` with „Dialetheic Logic“ ^[?].

Beyond the table it ties them to AEGIS's decay: „AEGIS's internal logical decay will directly and visibly alter the physics of the Kernwelten“ ^[?]. For Logos-Prime this means that „the sterile, Euclidean geometry of Logos-Prime will become unstable and hostile“ ^[?].
