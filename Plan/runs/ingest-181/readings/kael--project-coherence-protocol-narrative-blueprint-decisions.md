It describes scenes in which a singular mind would be paralysed, while Kael, co-conscious, uses his alters at once: „The analytical alter Lex can process the formal structure“ ^[?] and „the aggressive protector alter Nyx can act on the raw, contradictory impulse“ ^[?].

For the conclusion it names „Kael's transformation into“ ^[?] the Gärtner and gives it rules of non-intervention. The ethical test is built on „The Ethical Test (Popper“ ^[?] Paradox of Tolerance, and the second fragment is mandated, in the report's words, as the `Differently Fragmented Other` scenario (L185). The report's table lists Kael as „ANP (Host) - Manages daily life.“ ^[?]

These are the report's own mandates, recorded, not applied.
