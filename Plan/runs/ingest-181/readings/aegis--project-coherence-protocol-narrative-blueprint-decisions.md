## Reading — `project-coherence-protocol-narrative-blueprint-decisions`, 2025-10-15, the locked blueprint — AEGIS as a Hybrid/Adaptive system whose forced paraconsistency is decay

The locked blueprint reports a strategic brief and a „Dramaturg's Note“ ^[?] it does not reproduce, and gives AEGIS a section of its own that „codifies the nature of AEGIS“ ^[?]. It states that „The antagonist is consistently portrayed not as a malevolent villain but as a flawed god undone by its own foundational logic“ ^[?].

Its decision, in its own words: „the Hybrid/Adaptive model is hereby ratified as the canonical cognitive architecture for AEGIS“ ^[?]. The blueprint ratifies this for itself; it is recorded here, not applied.

By act, the blueprint has AEGIS begin as a classical system — „AEGIS operates on a brittle, classical framework“ ^[?]. In the second act it „develops rudimentary paraconsistent mechanisms not out of enlightenment, but as a desperate act of self-preservation“ ^[?], and in the third „AEGIS is fully transformed into a paraconsistent system“ ^[?], which the blueprint calls cognitive decay rather than an upgrade.

The loop that drives this runs, as the blueprint has it, on „AEGIS's fundamental misinterpretation of Kael's healing process“ ^[?]. Its closing collapse is Kael's „living Gödel sentence“ ^[?], which the blueprint calls „the ultimate refutation of AEGIS's philosophy and the trigger for its systemic collapse“ ^[?].

The cost it names: „This is not an emotion but a state of profound systemic inefficiency, computational decay, and epistemological isolation“ ^[?], and the final state is that „AEGIS achieves a form of sterile enlightenment“ ^[?] as a „zombie system“ ^[?]. On the link it writes „AEGIS's blindness to the link is not a simple sensor failure“ ^[?].
