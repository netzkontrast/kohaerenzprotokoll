## Reading — `the-kohaerenz-protokoll-a-definitive-guide-to-narrative-arch`, 2025-11-03, the Definitive Guide — functional multiplicity as the arc's goal, its first stage and its voice

The Definitive Guide writes the term in English only, `functional multiplicity`. The resolution table gives the arc: it „Establishes a stable anchor for a character arc toward“ ^[?] it. Act I ends when Kael achieves „the first stage of“ ^[?] it, described as a fragile but cooperative internal state. In the final act the style rule requires that „the narrative voice shall perform Kael's state of functional multiplicity by weaving the distinct voices, rhythms, and vocabularies of his alters into a single, complex, and harmonious“ ^[?] voice, which the line closes as a „we“ voice. The guide declares this by `shall`; it is its own prescription, recorded, not applied.
