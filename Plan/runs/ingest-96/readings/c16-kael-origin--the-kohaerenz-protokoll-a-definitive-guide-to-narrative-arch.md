## 2026-10-05 — `the-kohaerenz-protokoll-a-definitive-guide-to-narrative-arch`, 2025-11-03, the Definitive Guide

AEGIS performs the `Trennungsprotokoll`, which „violently fragmented its own“ ^[?] `Ursprungs-Ich` (L112). Of Juna/V it says she is „exiled part of Kael's own“ ^[?] `Ursprungs-Ich.` (L138). No line makes the two the same self or Kael a remainder of AEGIS's, and no entity M occurs (`Monster` ^[the-kohaerenz-protokoll-a-definitive-guide-to-narrative-arch.md:#0]).
