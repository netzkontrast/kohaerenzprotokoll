## Reading — `report-narrative-methodology-for-kohaerenz-protokoll`, 2025-10-15, the methodology report — directives for the evolution of Kael's voice

The report directs that Kael's voice chart a journey: the prose must chart „his transformation from a state of Tertiäre Strukturelle Dissoziation (TSDP)“ ^[?] to „functional, co-conscious multiplicity“ ^[?]. For the fragmented phase it directs „Employ short, aborted sentences“ ^[?], and an internal monologue of contradictory thoughts between the Apparently Normal Parts (ANPs) and the Emotional Parts (EPs) (L42).

For the integrated phase it proposes that Kael's voice become polyphonic or choric (L46), and that this prose is the literary performance of high integrated information: „Perform High-Φ Consciousness“ ^[?]. Its sample sentence for this weaving (L48) illustrates a style and is not the novel's text.
