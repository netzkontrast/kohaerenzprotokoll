## Reading — `report-narrative-methodology-for-kohaerenz-protokoll`, 2025-10-15, the methodology report — the Guardians as functional manifestations of AEGIS's logic

The report directs that the supporting characters „do not function as independent actors with their own motivations and desires“ ^[?]. Their role is „functional manifestations of AEGIS's core logic and its pathological, trauma-induced defense mechanisms“ ^[?]. On their relation to AEGIS it directs that they operate within the system of laws AEGIS represents: „Their existence is one of adherence to fundamental law, not obedience to a master“ ^[?]. Every interaction with Kael is to be framed as a test: „Every interaction between Kael and a Guardian must be framed as a specific thematic or psychological test“ ^[?].
