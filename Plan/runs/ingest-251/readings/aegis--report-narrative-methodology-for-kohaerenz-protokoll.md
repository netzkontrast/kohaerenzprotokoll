## Reading — `report-narrative-methodology-for-kohaerenz-protokoll`, 2025-10-15, the methodology report — directives for AEGIS's voice as a logic-bound system

The report is a set of directives for the writing, not novel text. It directs that „AEGIS’s narrative voice must be a direct expression of its nature as an information-based system driven by classical logic“ ^[?], and calls the voice „a logical necessity of its autopoietic, operationally closed nature“ ^[?]. For diction it directs: „The vocabulary must be cold, precise, and technical“ ^[?]. For tone it proposes „Events are not experienced; they are logged“ ^[?], in the manner of a system log or technical report.

In its table of perspectives it adds that „AEGIS may also function as an unreliable narrator“ ^[?]. Against AEGIS's side it sets Kael's integrated prose, which it says contrasts „with AEGIS's collection of firewalled, low-Φ subsystems“ ^[?]. It also directs (L68) that an objective log entry from AEGIS about a system correction be set against Kael's subjective, fragmented experience of the same event.
