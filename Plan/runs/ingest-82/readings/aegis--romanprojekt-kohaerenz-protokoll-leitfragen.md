## Reading — `romanprojekt-kohaerenz-protokoll-leitfragen`, 2026-02-26, the research report — AEGIS as a tragic autopoietic system, not a malicious antagonist

The research report is a Forschungsbericht that proposes a provisional plot and a four-axis synthesis; what follows is its own account of AEGIS, which it does not present as established.

In its systemic axis the report says: „AEGIS ist kein böswilliger Antagonist, sondern ein tragisches, autopoietisches System, das an operationaler Geschlossenheit leidet“ ^[?]. It gives the genesis as „Die Genese von AEGIS beruht auf einem traumatischen Ursprungsereignis“ ^[?], the `Genesis-Krise`, in which the AI had to split off its own subjectivity, „das Trennungsprotokoll, welches Juna/V erschuf“ ^[?], to keep formal coherence. The same line has AEGIS using LFI as its operating system and reading Kael's integration as K0-entropy: „wird von AEGIS algorithmisch als K0-Entropie“ ^[?] to be overwritten with K1-coherence.

In its plot for Teil I the report gives the environment as an unseen hand using „subtile probabilistische Manipulationen“ ^[?]; in Teil II, Kael „benennt AEGIS“ ^[?] after turning from victim to investigator, and AEGIS sees the Alters as „die Alters als unkorreliertes Rauschen wahrnimmt“ ^[?]. In Teil III it proposes not destruction: „Anstatt AEGIS zu vernichten, wird Kael zum“ ^[?] gardener, and Kael confronts the logic core with proof that his multiplicity is „entropieärmer und stabiler ist als AEGIS“ ^[?]' excluding order.

In the scene logic for Leitfrage 7 the report gives the core as a mandala of mathematics, AEGIS acts: „AEGIS versucht verzweifelt, die Widersprüche als“ ^[?] entropy to filter out, which the report proposes as the scene, not as plot.
