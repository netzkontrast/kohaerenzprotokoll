## Reading — `the-ontology-of-antagonism-the-function-of-paradox-in-kohaer`, 2025-11-03, the ontology-of-antagonism report — Algorithmic melancholy as the cost of AEGIS's transformation

The ontology-of-antagonism report names algorithmic melancholy as „a condition of knowing without understanding, trapped in a cold, eternal contemplation of a truth it can never subjectively experience“ ^[?] The sentence closes with a cite note, so the label and its gloss are relayed from another project document. The report lists three costs. The second is inefficiency, „where its actions are logically valid but often practically useless or caught in endless, paradoxical loops“ ^[?] (cite note: relayed). The third is „It can compute the syntax of Kael's being but is forever barred from its semantics.“ ^[?]
