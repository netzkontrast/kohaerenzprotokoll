The ontology-of-antagonism report defines the aim as „Instead, it is defined as a state of co-consciousness, communication, and cooperation among all alters within his internal system“ ^[?] (cite note on the line: relayed from another project document). On co-fronting it says „A key example is the ability for multiple alters to“ ^[?] share control.
