## Reading — `welcome-to-the-coherence-protocol-a-beginner-s-guide`, 2025-11-03, the beginner's guide — System Kael as a reality forged from one fractured mind, a system of parts

The beginner's guide opens by describing a world as „a reality forged from the internal psychological conflict of a single, fractured mind“ ^[?], whose protagonist is System Kael. It says „The protagonist, System Kael, is not a single person but a“ ^[?] `system` of parts, `Anteile`, „formed as an adaptive response to overwhelming trauma“ ^[?], and that „This structure is based on the clinical Theory of Structural Dissociation (TSDP).“ ^[?]

Kael's path: „Kael's journey is the difficult, painful process of getting this fractured“ ^[?] team to work together, called `integration`. The guide ties Kael to truth: the table puts „Kael is forced to discover that true, resilient coherence can only be achieved by accepting and integrating“ ^[?] all facts against AEGIS.

In the Kernwelten the guide gives the body: in the first, „Kael's organic body feels like a“ ^[?] `machine he barely knew`; in the third, „Kael's body is trapped in a constant, exhausting state of fight-or-flight.“ ^[?]; in the fourth, „This is the only world where Kael's body can release its tension.“ ^[?] The guide gives the second world a somatic truth it calls `dialetheic`.
