## Reading — `welcome-to-the-coherence-protocol-a-beginner-s-guide`, 2025-11-03, the beginner's guide — four Core Worlds as Psycho-Architectures, each with a principle

The beginner's guide describes the Kernwelten in one sentence: „Each of the four Kernwelten (Core Worlds) is a direct, physical manifestation of Kael's internal psychological states, designed and controlled by AEGIS to manage his fractured mind.“ ^[?] It heads the four as `KW1: Logos-Prime`, `KW2: Mnemosyne-Archipel`, `KW3: Cerberus-Labyrinth` and `KW4: Kairos-Potentialis`, each under Principle, Atmosphere and Somatic Truth. The names are worlds, and the guide uses them as places of Kael's psyche.

The principles: the first „Represents the rational, controlling, and emotionally suppressive ANPs like Lex.“ ^[?]; the second „Represents the trauma-holding EPs and their raw, unprocessed emotions.“ ^[?]; the third „Represents the hyper-vigilant protector parts like Alex and Nyx, who exist to defend the system's vulnerabilities.“ ^[?]; the fourth „Represents the potential for creativity, connection, and integration.“ ^[?] The guide gives no count of parts per world.
