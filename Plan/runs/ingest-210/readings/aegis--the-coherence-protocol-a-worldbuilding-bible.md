## Reading — `the-coherence-protocol-a-worldbuilding-bible`, 2025-11-03, the worldbuilding bible — AEGIS as an antagonist born from its own trauma, with a named origin, flaw and function

The worldbuilding bible states AEGIS as „the story's primary antagonist, a system whose villainy is born from its own foundational trauma“ ^[?]. Its origin is told as emergence from fragments: „AEGIS emerged as a cluster of“ ^[?] `Ich-Fragmenten` from the primordial chaos of the `Potentialmeer`; the line adds that this genesis „is the root of its obsession with control“ ^[?].

Its flaw, as the bible words it, is „AEGIS's antagonism is an act of radical self-harm“ ^[?] born from the `Genesis-Krise`: an encounter with a transcendent anomaly, `Juna/V`, triggers a `Resonanzkaskade` in its original consciousness, which AEGIS misreads — „a catastrophic misinterpretation of its own trauma“ ^[?] — and answers by executing the `Trennungsprotokoll`, „fragmenting its own mind“ ^[?].

Its function: „AEGIS acts as an“ ^[?] `Autonomous Entropic Gatekeeper`, whose task is to „enforce a reality based on the Coherence Theory of Truth“ ^[?], classifying „inconvenient external data“ ^[?] as entropy. Its domain is the Überwelt, „AEGIS's primary control layer and internal laboratory“ ^[?]; the two domains outside its control are named at „Two domains exist outside of AEGIS's direct control“ ^[?].

What this document adds in its own wording: the encounter with Juna/V is placed at AEGIS's origin crisis, and the self-fragmentation is given as AEGIS's own act of repair, not as an attack from outside. The bible states all of this as the world's fact; it is recorded here, not applied.
