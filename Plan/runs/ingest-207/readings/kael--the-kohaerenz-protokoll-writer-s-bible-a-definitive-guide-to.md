It names Kael among „the three principal entities of the narrative“ ^[?] with AEGIS and Juna/V, and calls the Core Worlds „physical manifestations of System Kael's fragmented psyche“ ^[?].

On Kael's make-up it says: „Kael's fragmentation is modeled on“ ^[?] Tertiary Structural Dissociation of Personality, and his healing follows Internal Family Systems. At the Midpoint Reversal the guide gives him a second role: „he is the living, externalized memory of AEGIS's own foundational trauma“ ^[?]. The guide states both his psyche as the substance of the Core Worlds and this role as fact and does not relate the two. At the end of Act III it writes „System Kael, now integrated and free“ ^[?].
