## Reading — `kapitel-17`, 2025-04-20, the Kapitel-17 plan — only in compounds

`Juna` never stands alone in the Kapitel-17 plan; it occurs in two compounds. The first is the reason of the threat rating, „seine Juna-Fokussierung als erhöhte Bedrohung“ ^[?]. The second is a place the plan proposes Cerberus may cut Kael off from, „der Juna-Spur-Ort, falls erreichbar“ ^[?]. A plan, not the chapter.
