## Reading — `optimierter-prompt-fuer-kohaerenz-protokoll`, 2025-07-29, the Architektur-des-Kollapses report — Kael as the exiled part and the original self in the IFS mapping

The report's Kapitel 7 reports the Internal Family Systems model of Richard Schwartz (L209) and then applies it to AEGIS; the roles self, exile, manager and firefighter are borrowed, their assignment is the report's. Its table gives Kael the role of the exile, together with the experience of the anomaly: „zum exilierten Teil des AEGIS-Systems“ ^[?]. The text names the same figure the original self: „ist das ursprüngliche, integrierte Selbst“ ^[?]. Through the resonance with the anomaly, the report writes, Kael „wird untrennbar mit dieser exilierten Last verbunden“ ^[?]. The report calls the decision of AEGIS's managers a reversal of the healthy dynamic: „treffen die Manager-Teile von AEGIS die katastrophale Entscheidung, das Selbst (Kael) zu exilieren“ ^[?].

In the report's Kapitel 8 the relation to the anomaly is modelled as a feedback loop: „Die Resonanz zwischen Kael und der Anomalie ist eine positive, destabilisierende Schleife“ ^[?]. In its systems-archetype section the exile is the unintended consequence of the repair: „Die Schaffung einer fundamentalen Spaltung innerhalb des Systems“ ^[?].

The report gives Kael several roles in different vocabularies (original self, exile, the part exiled by the managers) and does not say which of them the novel keeps.
