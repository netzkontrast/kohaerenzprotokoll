## Reading — `narrative-techniken-fuer-kohaerenz-protokoll`, 2025-04-27, the craft report — techniques for the subtle K-J connection

The craft report lists the connection in its metaphor section as „Moonshine/K-J Verbindung (Subtile intuitive Verbindungen)“ ^[?] and gives it a subsection, „Die K-J Verbindung (Moonshine)“ ^[?]. Its advice is to avoid exposition: „Die Verbindung sollte nicht durch Charaktere erklärt werden“ ^[?].

It proposes a role for the connection: „Die K-J Verbindung fungiert als narrativer Kontrapunkt zu AEGIS“ ^[?] logic and to Kael's fragmentation, and concludes „Daher wirkt Moonshine als narrative Kraft, die holistische, intuitive Verbindung repräsentiert“ ^[?]. It bases the description of the connection as intuitive and defying logic on the blueprint, which it reports and does not contain.
