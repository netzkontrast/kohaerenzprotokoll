## Reading — `narrative-techniken-fuer-kohaerenz-protokoll`, 2025-04-27, the craft report — Guardians as embodied world principles

The craft report gives the Guardians their own subsection, headed „Kernwelt-Guardians: Archetypische Verkörperungen von Weltprinzipien“ ^[?]. It proposes that „Jeder Kernwelt-Guardian (Architekt, Netzweber, Chaos-Regulator, Möglichkeits-Weber) muss das Kernprinzip seiner Welt“ ^[?] embody directly, and that appearance, behaviour and the tests they set should reflect it.

It asks the role to be settled: „Ihre Funktion im Narrativ sollte klar sein“ ^[?], and offers threshold keeper, mentor, shadow figure and trickster as options. Its closing claim is that „Die Kernwelt-Guardians dienen als personifizierte Repräsentationen der zentralen philosophischen und psychologischen Themen des Romans“ ^[?], a statement it bases on the blueprint.
