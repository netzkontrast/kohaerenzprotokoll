## Reading — `a-learner-s-glossary-for-the-world-of-kohaerenz-protokoll`, 2025-10-15, the learner's glossary — AEGIS as antagonist, defined by negation, with a stated central flaw

The learner's glossary presents `AEGIS` as „a vast artificial intelligence whose core programming sets it in direct opposition to the protagonist, Kael“ ^[?]. It says that „Its very identity is defined by negation and self-preservation“ ^[?], and names the guiding principle as „Its guiding principle is prioritizing internal consistency over any external or subjective truth“ ^[?], glossed with the German `Kohärenz statt Wahrheit`. As its central flaw the glossary names the `Paradox of Misaligned Coherence`: „It fatally misinterprets the natural complexity of psychological healing and emotional integration as a dangerous form of entropy that must be suppressed or destroyed“ ^[?]. On origin, the glossary calls the Void „the primordial state from which AEGIS first emerged“ ^[?], and it describes the Core Worlds as „distinct simulated realities created by AEGIS as controlled environments“ ^[?]. These are the glossary's own explanations for readers.
