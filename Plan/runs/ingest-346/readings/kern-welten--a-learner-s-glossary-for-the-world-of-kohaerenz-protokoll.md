## Reading — `a-learner-s-glossary-for-the-world-of-kohaerenz-protokoll`, 2025-10-15, the learner's glossary — the Core Worlds as AEGIS's controlled environments with a table of purposes

The learner's glossary defines `Die Kernwelten` as „distinct simulated realities created by AEGIS as controlled environments“ ^[?] and gives their purpose as to analyze, contain and manage fragments of Kael's psyche. Its table lists four worlds (KW1 to KW4) with a purpose and metaphor each; for the first it writes „Represents logic, order, and control“ ^[?]. The names in the table are worlds, not Guardians.
