## Reading — `kohaerenz-analyse-kapitel-2`, 2025-12-28, the analysis report — Konstrukt-Stadt as LogOS's world, sterile after the reboot

The analysis report names Konstrukt-Stadt as LogOS's world, KW1 (L65). Of Kapitel 1 it says the city is a place of „verstörender Perfektion“ ^[?], where the absence of dirt, history and irregularity signals sterility and death (L139). Its recommendation 3 asks the author for „Die Konstrukt-Stadt muss als Antagonist agieren“ ^[?] (L208) — a recommendation, not a statement about the world.
