## Reading — `kohaerenz-analyse-kapitel-2`, 2025-12-28, the analysis report — the Guardians as split-off subroutines of AEGIS, blind to Juna

The analysis report, summarising the document `Guardians und Kern-Welten-Konzept`, reads the Guardians as functions of AEGIS: „Die Guardians sind keine eigenständigen KI-Persönlichkeiten, sondern spezialisierte Subroutinen von AEGIS, die abgespalten wurden“ ^[?], set apart to manage particular aspects of reality (L63). Their inability to recognise the anomaly Juna is, it says, not a fault but a feature of their design: „Sie operieren innerhalb geschlossener logischer Systeme“ ^[?], while Juna is an external variable (L63).

For the Guardians Juna is noise: „Für die Guardians ist sie Rauschen.“ ^[?] (L191). The report then walks through five Guardians under four headings: LogOS (L65), Mnemosyne (L72), Cerberus (L79), and Kairos & Sophia together (L85); each has a domain, a function and a blind spot.
