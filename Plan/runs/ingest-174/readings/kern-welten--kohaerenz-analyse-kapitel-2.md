## Reading — `kohaerenz-analyse-kapitel-2`, 2025-12-28, the analysis report — the Kernwelten as AEGIS's simulation and the Guardians' worlds

The analysis report says AEGIS makes the conditions of its own existence „durch die Simulation der Kernwelten“ ^[?L33] (L33). In its section on the Guardians it assigns each a world: LogOS to Konstrukt-Stadt (KW1, L65), Mnemosyne to Resonanz-Landschaft (KW2, L72), Cerberus to Grenzfeste (KW3, L79), Kairos & Sophia to Möglichkeits-Garten and Überwelt (KW4, L85). The report's reference list names the source for this section as `Guardians und Kern-Welten-Konzept` (L242). As a lesson for Kael it says: „Kael muss lernen, die Sprachen der verschiedenen Welten zu sprechen“ ^[?] (L183).
