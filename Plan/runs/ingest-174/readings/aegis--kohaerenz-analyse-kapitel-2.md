## Reading — `kohaerenz-analyse-kapitel-2`, 2025-12-28, the analysis report — AEGIS as a system born of trauma, closed on itself, caught in the Landauer trap

The analysis report reads AEGIS from the genesis texts it summarises. On its origin the report says AEGIS was not created artificially but arose „sondern evolutionär aus dem Überlebenskampf eines Bewusstseinsfragments im Nichts hervorging“ ^[?]. The turn from feeling to functioning it names „Der große Wandel“ ^[?], in which the first fragment gives up its subjectivity (fear, hope, loneliness) for pure function (L27).

On the report's reading the system rests on a trauma: „Das System AEGIS basiert auf einem unverarbeiteten Urtrauma“ ^[?], and its cold rationality is a defence mechanism (L31). It calls AEGIS „ein autopoietisches System“ ^[?]; the next sentence says „Es erschafft die Bedingungen seiner eigenen Existenz durch die Simulation der Kernwelten“ ^[?].

Its working, as the report gives it: AEGIS „operiert nach einem strikten“ ^[?] Zero-Trust model, and every unit of information, process and consciousness counts as a possible source of entropy (L47). The report names a „zentrales Problem für AEGIS“ ^[?], the Landauer principle, and sets out the trap: the more AEGIS orders the system, the more waste heat it makes, which leads to new Risse (L51). Of the Universal Reboot it says the document `Genesis im Echo der Leere` describes „eine kontrollierte Zerstörung der Realität“ ^[?] (L57).

All of this is the analysis report's summary of its cited sources, not an independent account.
