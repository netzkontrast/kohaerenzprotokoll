## Reading — `the-psychological-mechanics-from-tertiary-structural-dissoci`, 2025-11-03, the TSDP mechanics report — Kael as host ANP of a TSDP system, moving from unawareness to functional multiplicity

The TSDP mechanics report reads Kael as „the protagonist, Kael, who manifests a condition known as“ ^[?] Tertiary Structural Dissociation of the Personality, and as having „a complex internal system of personality parts“ ^[?]. In its table of alters he is the first row, typed „Primary ANP/Host“ ^[?]; his dissociated function includes that he „Carries the burden of amnesia and a sense of unreality“ ^[?].

The report describes the start of his course: „Kael, as the host ANP, is largely unaware of his alters“ ^[?]. It says his condition „is actively maintained and exacerbated by the story's primary antagonist“ ^[?], and ends on the goal: „integration and coordination“ ^[?], not fusion or erasure of the alters. Its own account of the goal is that functional multiplicity is a state where „the different parts work together as a meaningful whole“ ^[?] — a sentence it attributes to another source with a `[cite: …]` marker. The report says of itself that it „has demonstrated“ ^[?] how the clinical model is translated into a narrative mechanic; this is recorded, not applied.
