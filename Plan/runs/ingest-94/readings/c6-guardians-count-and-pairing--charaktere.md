## 2026-10-05 — `charaktere`, 2025-07-29, the character concept

**The character concept pairs one Guardian with each Kernwelt and gives KW4 two.**

„Jeder Guardian ist einer spezifischen Kernwelt zugeordnet“ ^[?] (L363). `LogOS` stands for KW1, `Mnemosyne` for KW2, `Cerberus` for KW3 (L365–L367), and for KW4: „Die Wächter der KW4 (Kairos-Potentialis), einer kreativitäts- und potenzialbasierten Welt“ ^[?], `Kairos & Sophia` (L368).

Stands as four worlds with five named Guardians, two of them in KW4; recorded, not applied.
