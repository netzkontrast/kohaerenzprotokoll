## Reading — `kontext-outline`, 2025-05-03, the outline commission — Juna/V as the briefing's external entity and the planned contact chapters

The commission's glossary has „Externe Entität/Anomalie/Kontaktquelle außerhalb von AEGIS' direkter Kontrolle“ ^[?] for `Juna/V`. The prologue plan names it as trigger, but with a question mark in its plot line (`Externe Resonanz (Juna/V?)`) and without one under the subplot: „Juna/V als Auslöser“ ^[?].

First contact is planned for Chapter 19, and its nature is held open: „Natur unklar (Andere Simulation? Höhere Realität?)“ ^[?]. The deepening in Chapter 25 keeps a mystery: „Natur Juna/V klarer, aber Mysterium bleibt“ ^[?]. For the climax the outline plans „Juna/V spielt entscheidende Rolle“ ^[?], and the field line for it hedges: „Finale Rolle klar (oder rätselhaft)“ ^[?].
