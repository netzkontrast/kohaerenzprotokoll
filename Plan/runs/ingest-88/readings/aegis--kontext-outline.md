## Reading — `kontext-outline`, 2025-05-03, the outline commission — the briefing's gloss, its origin and paradox lines, and the planned end

The commission briefs AEGIS in its glossary as „Antagonistisches KI-System/Kollektiv; entstanden aus Komponente 734“ ^[?], and the project paragraph says it is driven „von einem inhärenten Widerspruch angetrieben“ ^[?]. The glossary entry `Paradox (AEGIS)` gives the paradox as „AEGIS' Kernproblem“ ^[?]; its label `Fehlausgerichtete Kohärenz` is the entry's own and is read here, not on a page of its own (J9, J12). The glossary is written `zur Orientierung` for the commissioned author, so these are the briefing's glosses.

The outline plans an origin for the prologue and hedges it: „AEGIS (oder Vorläufer) entsteht aus Chaos/Angst, sucht Ganzheit“ ^[?]. The briefing does not connect this with the glossary's component.

The outline plans the paradox as understood in the middle of the book: „Paradoxon benannt, Implikationen verstanden“ ^[?], and as shown at work later: „Kernparadoxon in Aktion demonstriert“ ^[?]. For the last act the `AEGIS Focus` fields plan „Paradoxon führt zu Selbstzerstörung/Transformation“ ^[?] and, for the climax, „Endgültiger Kollaps/Zerstörung/Transformation“ ^[?]. The chapter after it leaves the state open as a question: „Zustand nach Klimax (Zerstört? Transformiert? Irrelevant?)“ ^[?]. Before that, Chapter 32 asks whether the adversary is only an enemy: „Ist AEGIS nur Monster?“ ^[?].

The plan is a commission's plan; it records open points as question marks and decides none of them.
