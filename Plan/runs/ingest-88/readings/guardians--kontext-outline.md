## Reading — `kontext-outline`, 2025-05-03, the outline commission — the glossary's five Guardians over four worlds

The commission's glossary has the entry „AEGIS-Entitäten, die die KWs verwalten/kontrollieren“ ^[?], and names five in brackets: `LogOS`, `Mnemosyne`, `Cerberus`, `Kairos`, `Sophia`. The world entries pair them: the first world is „Sterile, logikbasierte Welt“ ^[?], the third „Verteidigungs-/angstbasierte Welt“ ^[?], and the fourth has two, „Kreativitäts-/potenzialbasierte Welt. Guardians: Kairos & Sophia“ ^[?] — read as the briefing writes it: four worlds, five names.

The outline then uses them as `Subplot Focus` fields, for example Chapter 9's `AEGIS Focus` plans „Cerberus als Verkörperung Angst/Kontrolle“ ^[?], and Chapter 27 plans for the assault „Einsatz Guardians“ ^[?]. Chapter 17 plans for the fourth world „Kairos/Sophia als ambivalente Wächter“ ^[?].
