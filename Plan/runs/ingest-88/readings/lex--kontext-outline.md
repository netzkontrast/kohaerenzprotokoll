## Reading — `kontext-outline`, 2025-05-03, the outline commission — the glossary gloss and Lex in the chapter plans

The commission's glossary, which its note says is a basis the commissioned author may refine, glosses the figure briefly: „Logischer/Analytischer Anteil.“ ^[?] The outline plans Lex as latent in Chapter 1 („Lex latent?“ ^[?L79]) and then as more active in Chapter 2: „Lex aktiver (rationaler Kontrollversuch)“ ^[?]. Chapter 3 plans the first conflict between two of the figures: „Erster ANP-Konflikt (Lex vs. Alex)“ ^[?]. In the analytic chapters the outline pairs Lex with Argus: „Lex/Argus dominant“ ^[?].
