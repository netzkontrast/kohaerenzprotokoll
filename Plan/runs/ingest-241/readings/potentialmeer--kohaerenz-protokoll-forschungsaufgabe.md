## Reading — `kohaerenz-protokoll-forschungsaufgabe`, 2025-07-29, the ontological analysis — the Potentialmeer as what AEGIS excludes, world 4 and ground of the Fundament

The ontological analysis reads the Potentialmeer as what AEGIS is not: the line calls it „das entropische“ ^[?] Potentialmeer. The encounter with the void „(dem Potentialmeer)“ ^[?] is framed as AEGIS meeting its Gödel statement. World 4 is proposed as the Potentialmeer itself, „einen Raum reiner Potentialität“ ^[?]. The Kohärenz-Insel is proposed as a strange attractor in its phase space, and the Fundament lets life arise from it: „Es erschafft nicht Leben oder Bewusstsein“ ^[?].
