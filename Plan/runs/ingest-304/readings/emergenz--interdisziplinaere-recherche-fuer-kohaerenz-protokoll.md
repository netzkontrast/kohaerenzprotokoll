## Reading — `interdisziplinaere-recherche-fuer-kohaerenz-protokoll`, 2025-04-25, the interdisciplinary research protocol — emergence as a lens on the instability of an abstract core

The research protocol scans outside concepts for an abstracted core of entity, space, protagonist, connection and core tension; its story elements are roles of that core, never named figures, and its accounts of emergence are lens, not statements about the novel. Emergence enters its phase-1 scan twice as a list item: „Emergenz von Raumzeit / Quantengravitation“ ^[?] and „Komplexe Adaptive Systeme (CAS) / Emergenz“ ^[?].

When it selects concepts for deep scan it gives emergence and complex adaptive systems a role: „Zentral für die Kernspannung und als potenzielle Quelle der“ ^[?] cracks and unpredictability. Its synthesis proposes that the principles of CAS and emergence „erklären, warum diese Aufrechterhaltung inhärent schwierig ist und zu unvorhersehbaren Instabilitäten“ ^[?] could lead.

In the application phase it proposes the entity could be modelled as an autopoietic system that holds its organisation „an der Grenze zum Chaos aufrechterhält“ ^[?], with failure modes that could be cascading errors if the space is regarded as a CAS.
