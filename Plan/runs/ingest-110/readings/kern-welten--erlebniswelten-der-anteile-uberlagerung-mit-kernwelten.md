## Reading — `erlebniswelten-der-anteile-uberlagerung-mit-kernwelten`, 2025-04-29, the Erlebniswelten concept — the four Kernwelten set beside group-theoretical names, two marked as interpretation

The Erlebniswelten concept describes how the eleven Anteile of Kael meet the Kernwelten; the introduction says it is analysed „mit den vier simulierten Kernwelten“ ^[?] that these inner worlds interact. Before the eleven entries it gives a list headed „Zuordnung der Kernwelten zu Gruppentheoretischen Entsprechungen“ ^[?], one line per world, numbered KW1 to KW4.

The concept pairs KW1, `Konstrukt-Stadt` with `LogOS` in brackets, with `Co₁`, and gives its focus as „Fokus auf rigide Ordnung, Struktur, Logik, Fehlerkorrektur“ ^[?]. It pairs KW2, `Resonanz-Landschaft` with `Mnemosyne`, with `McL`, focus „Fokus auf Netzwerke, Verbindungen, emotionale Resonanz, Erinnerung“ ^[?]. KW3, `Grenzfeste` with `Cerberus`, goes with `B`, focus „Fokus auf Chaos, Abwehr, Grenzen, Paranoia, das Ungezügelte/Bedrohliche“ ^[?]. KW4, `Möglichkeits-Garten` with `Kairos/Sophia`, goes with `Ly`, focus „Fokus auf Potentialität, Emergenz, komplexe Möglichkeiten, Kreativität, Wachstum“ ^[?].

The concept marks the second and third pairings as its own reading: each line ends „Interpretation im Romankontext“ ^[?L18], and the third again „Interpretation im Romankontext“ ^[?L19]. The first and fourth lines carry no such mark.

The body repeats the four worlds as short forms `KW1 (Co₁)` to `KW4 (Ly)` in each of the eleven entries; the entries say how each Anteil feels in each world, for Kael, for instance, KW3 as „Bedrohlich, löst Angst vor Kontrollverlust aus“ ^[?]. The document frames itself by three albums, with „Together We Confide“ ^[?] named in the introduction; the albums are its frame, not a reading of the worlds.
