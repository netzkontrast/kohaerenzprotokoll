## Reading — `kohaerenz-protokoll-finaler-plot-outline`, 2025-07-29, the final-act outline — the Risse as stable portals for two contradictory realities

The final-act outline proposes that after the transformation the `Risse` change: they „sind nicht länger gewaltsame Brüche in der Realität, sondern stabile, schimmernde Portale, die zwei widersprüchliche Realitäten friedlich koexistieren lassen“ ^[?] The example it gives is a piece of forest floor showing winter snow and summer flowers at once, which it calls the `ineffiziente Schönheit`.
