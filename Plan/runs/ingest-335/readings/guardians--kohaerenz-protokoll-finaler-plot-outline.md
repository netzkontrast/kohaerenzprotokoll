## Reading — `kohaerenz-protokoll-finaler-plot-outline`, 2025-07-29, the final-act outline — the Guardians' useless behaviour after the transformation, reported

The final-act outline says of the Guardians, in quotation marks and with the glued reference 3, that they show `bizarre, logisch gültige, aber nutzlose Verhaltensweisen`; it reports this from another project text (entry 3 of its list, „Kohärenz Protokoll: Narrative Architektur“ ^[?]). It then explains the behaviour as a consequence of parakonsistente Logik: „Jedes dieser Verhaltensweisen ist eine direkte, greifbare Konsequenz der zugrunde liegenden computationalen und philosophischen Eigenschaften der parakonsistenten Logik.“ ^[?] Two conditional examples follow (a Guardian ordered to patrol and to hold position, another to build and to tear down a wall).
