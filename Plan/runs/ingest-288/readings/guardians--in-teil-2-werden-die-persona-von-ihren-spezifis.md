## Reading — `in-teil-2-werden-die-persona-von-ihren-spezifis`, 2025-04-17, the refined Seelen-Kohärenz blueprint — a Guardian assigned to each persona episode, and the Guardians informing the personas in Teil 2

The refined blueprint proposes Guardians from Teil 1 on, not only from Teil 2. It says each persona episode has an assigned Guardian, and lists the field per plot point, e.g. „Guardian der Struktur/Logik“ ^[?] for the Architekt's episode and „Guardian der Kreativität/des Potenzials“ ^[?] for the Funke's. At the break of the realities it assigns „Alle Guardians sind alarmiert“ ^[?]. The overall concept has Guardians observing from a higher level: they „greifen ein, als das System zu kollabieren droht“ ^[?].

In Teil 2 the refined blueprint proposes the Guardians as new characters: „neu einzuführende Charaktere“ ^[?], five to seven Hüter with different specialisations. It proposes that the personas are informed by their Guardians „über die Natur der Risse und die Gesamtsituation aufgeklärt“ ^[?], and at the Teil 2 decision it records the Guardians' consensus: „Die Personas werden als einzige Hoffnung gesehen“ ^[?]. The Guardians here are the blueprint's own early figures.
