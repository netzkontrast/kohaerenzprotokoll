## Reading — `in-teil-2-werden-die-persona-von-ihren-spezifis`, 2025-04-17, the refined Seelen-Kohärenz blueprint — cracks opened by persona intrusion, read as disturbances in the information fabric, worsened by the hunt for the Außen

The refined blueprint proposes the Risse as growing through Teil 1: the later episodes are said to be marked by „Die Risse in der Realität werden häufiger und offensichtlicher“ ^[?]. In its fifth plot point the Funke opens one: it is „Erste deutliche Manifestation der Realitätsrisse durch Persona-Interaktion“ ^[?]. In Teil 2 the Guardians are informed about, and in turn explain to the personas, the nature of the cracks (L14).

For Teil 3 it proposes a revelation that souls are information units and „Die Risse sind Störungen in diesem Informationsgewebe“ ^[?]. A false conclusion, that repair must lie outside the simulation (L234), starts the search for the Außen, which „verursacht massive neue Risse und Instabilität im gesamten System“ ^[?].
