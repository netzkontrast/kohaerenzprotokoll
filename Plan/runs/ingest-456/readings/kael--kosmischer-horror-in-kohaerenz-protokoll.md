## Reading — `kosmischer-horror-in-kohaerenz-protokoll`, 2025-04-25, the cosmic-horror analysis — Kael as ground for body and mind horror, with a guideline that Kael keeps agency

The cosmic-horror analysis takes Kael as the place where its horror principles meet a person. Its `Analyse` says: „Kaels Situation (fragmentierte Psyche, Avatar-Status, Integrationsprozess) ist fruchtbarer Boden für Körper-/Geist-Horror“ ^[?], and the table row (headed „Kael/Juna“ ^[?]) summarises the horror as psychological fragmentation and the terror of integration or dissolution into the alien (M).

The `Verstärkung` for Kael is an instruction to the writer: to focus on the subjective experience of Kael's fragmentation (sensory distortion, loss of control over thoughts and body, feelings of being watched) and to frame the integration process with visceral fear. It also instructs: „Nutze die K-J Verbindung, um verstörende, unerklärliche Phänomene einzuführen“ ^[?]. Strategies 4 and 6 repeat this for Kael as internalised existential dread and the horror of integration; strategy 4 names derealisation and depersonalisation.

Against that, the guideline on agency and hope asks for a counterweight: „Stelle sicher, dass Kael bedeutungsvolle Entscheidungen trifft, auch wenn diese eingeschränkt sind.“ ^[?] It adds that „Die K-J Verbindung kann als Anker oder Quelle der Resilienz inmitten der kosmischen Angst dienen.“ ^[?]
