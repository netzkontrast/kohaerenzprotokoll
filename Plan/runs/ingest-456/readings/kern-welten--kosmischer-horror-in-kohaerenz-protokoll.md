## Reading — `kosmischer-horror-in-kohaerenz-protokoll`, 2025-04-25, the cosmic-horror analysis — the Kernwelten read as fragile simulations open to intrusion

In its table the cosmic-horror analysis gives the Kernwelten a row of their own and reads them as „Simulationen als inhärent fragile Realitäten“ ^[?], with the potential for disturbances or invasions from the Potentialmeer or M, and the uncanny nature of constructed worlds.

Among its plot strategies it instructs the writer to design plot points where the underlying horror surfaces: a Kernwelt that suffers a catastrophic reality failure through influence from the Potentialmeer, and „eine direkte, aber unbegreifliche Manifestation“ ^[?] of M's nature. A guideline later names the fragility of simulated realities (Kernwelten) as one source of horror particular to the novel.
