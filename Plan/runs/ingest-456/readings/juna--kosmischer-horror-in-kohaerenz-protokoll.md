## Reading — `kosmischer-horror-in-kohaerenz-protokoll`, 2025-04-25, the cosmic-horror analysis — Juna joined to Kael, the K-J connection as both disturbance and anchor

The cosmic-horror analysis gives Juna no separate row; Juna appears in a joint row and heading. The table row is headed „Kael/Juna“ ^[?], and the detailed resonance point is headed „4. Kael/Juna:“ ^[?]. Its `Analyse` there gives Juna one sentence: „Junas Verbindung fügt das Unheimliche hinzu.“ ^[?]

The analysis uses the connection in two directions and does not reconcile them. As disturbance, the `Verstärkung` instructs the writer: „Nutze die K-J Verbindung, um verstörende, unerklärliche Phänomene einzuführen“ ^[?]. As resilience, the guideline on agency says: „Die K-J Verbindung kann als Anker oder Quelle der Resilienz inmitten der kosmischen Angst dienen.“ ^[?]
