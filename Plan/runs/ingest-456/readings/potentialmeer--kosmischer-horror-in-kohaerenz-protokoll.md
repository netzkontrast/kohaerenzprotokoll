## Reading — `kosmischer-horror-in-kohaerenz-protokoll`, 2025-04-25, the cosmic-horror analysis — the Potentialmeer as the unknown itself, to be left in part undefined

The cosmic-horror analysis lists the Potentialmeer first among its resonance points. The table row calls it „Verkörperung der unbegreiflichen Prä-Realität“ ^[?], a source of existential dread and awe and the ultimate unknown that threatens the order of AEGIS. The detailed `Analyse` begins: „Als prä-realer Raum unstrukturierter Potentialität“ ^[?] it embodies the genre principles of the unknown, the immeasurable and the indifferent; the same passage adds that the Potentialmeer is filled with unformed possibilities, some of which could be monstrous or reality-distorting.

The `Verstärkung` is an instruction to the writer: „Beschreibe es nicht nur als Potential, sondern als brodelnde, paradoxe Leere, in der Proto-Gesetze kollidieren“ ^[?]. Another strategy asks for non-Euclidean, paradoxical environments in describing aspects of the Potentialmeer.

On knowledge, the table row for limits of knowledge reads: „Unfähigkeit, M/Potentialmeer zu verstehen, als direkte Quelle existenzieller Angst“ ^[?]. The guideline on subtlety instructs: „Lasse Schlüsselaspekte von M und dem Potentialmeer bewusst undefiniert.“ ^[?] The same guideline holds that over-explanation weakens cosmic dread.
