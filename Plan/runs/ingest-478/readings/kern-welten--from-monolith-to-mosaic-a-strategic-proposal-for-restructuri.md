## Reading — `from-monolith-to-mosaic-a-strategic-proposal-for-restructuri`, 2025-11-03, the mosaic proposal — four Kernwelten as environments for stories

The mosaic proposal proposes stories that focus on the Kernwelten, glossed as „the simulated realities that are direct externalizations“ ^[?] of Kael's inner landscape, with the environment itself as the protagonist (L61). A table (L66–L70) pairs each of four Kernwelten with a story concept: KW1 Logos-Prime, an inhabitant's descent into madness over a tiny imperfection; KW2 Mnemosyne-Archipel, an EP in a time-loop inside one memory-space; KW3 Cerberus-Labyrinth, a bunker-fortress Guardian's logs; KW4 Kairos-Potentialis, a ruin-garden that generates a new lifeform. The KW3 cell begins „From the paranoid POV of a Guardian of the bunker-fortress“ ^[?], and the KW4 cell says the garden „spontaneously generates a new, hybrid lifeform“ ^[?]. Recorded as story ideas, not applied.
