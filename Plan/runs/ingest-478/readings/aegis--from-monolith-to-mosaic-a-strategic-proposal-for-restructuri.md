## Reading — `from-monolith-to-mosaic-a-strategic-proposal-for-restructuri`, 2025-11-03, the mosaic proposal — AEGIS as a tragic entity in a proposed first-person log

The mosaic proposal proposes giving AEGIS its own story. It starts from the worry that „AEGIS risks becoming an abstract threat“ ^[?] and proposes to transform it from a villain into „a tragic entity born from its own foundational trauma“ ^[?]. The means would be the Genesis-Krise told in the first person by the Ursprungs-Ich, in a fragmented log format (L57). Elsewhere it sets AEGIS's demand for coherence instead of truth against the protagonist's coherence through integration (L15). Recorded as a proposal, not applied.
