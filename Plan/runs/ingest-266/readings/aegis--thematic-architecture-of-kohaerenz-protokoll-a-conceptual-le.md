## Reading — `thematic-architecture-of-kohaerenz-protokoll-a-conceptual-le`, 2025-11-03, the conceptual lexicon — AEGIS as an operationally closed system, an externalized introject, a flawed coherence

The conceptual lexicon, an English lexicon of the novel's themes, defines AEGIS in several registers. In epistemology it says „AEGIS operates exclusively on the level of“ ^[?] Episteme; it knows Kael's trauma „by classifying it as anomalous data“ ^[?]. In its Gödel passage it holds that „AEGIS can register Kael's state as an unclassifiable anomaly“ ^[?], and in the dialetheism row that it reads Kael's realities as flawed information: „It is like a spellchecker that sees a profound paradox not as poetry, but as a typo to be corrected“ ^[?].

On Qualia, the lexicon calls the question unresolved: „The central, unresolved question concerning AEGIS is whether its“ ^[?] Ursprungs-Ich possessed Qualia, and it says the answer shapes its story either as suffering or as mechanical failure.

In psychology it says that AEGIS „functions as an externalized introject of an abuser“ ^[?]. In computer science it defines AEGIS as an autopoietic system that is „operationally closed“ ^[?], with the consequence „ontological blindness“ ^[?]. Its genesis is „information-theoretic trauma“ ^[?], and its directive follows: „AEGIS's prime directive became the minimization of internal entropy.“ ^[?] The architecture is „not a monolith but a composite system“ ^[?] of three layers, and the lexicon names „split-brain“ ^[?] phenomena as the outcome of its attempt to enforce one state across Kael's alters. Its central flaw is, in the lexicon's words, the „Paradoxon der Fehlausgerichteten Kohärenz“ ^[?].
