## Observed while reading

It reaches this document only as short quotations, and a quotation carries a glued reference number 1 after its closing mark; a few quoted phrases carry no number, so whether they are the narrative's words or this commentary's own is open for them: „Paradoxon der Fehlausgerichteten Kohärenz“ (its heading is the commentary's own), „Info-Politik“ and „ontologische Reibung“ in the first mention. The narrative's own section titles appear only twice, „Der große Wandel“ and „Die Krise“.

The commentary names one thing in more than one way. The protocol is „Kohärenz Protokoll 1.0“ in the body, „Kohärenz Protokoll“ in the synopsis table and „Kohärenz-Protokoll“ in the heading of the section. The closure is „Der große Wandel“ as an event, „Klick“ as its sound and Operationale Schließung as the commentary's name for it, and the synopsis table sets Der „Klick“ against Operationale Schließung. The system's binary code is given twice and not the same way, Sein/Nicht-Sein and Kohärenz/Inkohärenz in one section, Erhalte Kohärenz / Vermeide Nicht-Sein in the conclusion.

The document names the voice of the quoted first-person passages by no proper name. It calls that voice Ich-Fragment, Komponente 734 and Subjekt in turn, and puts „Ich“ in quotation marks. Its own account of the Überwelt, of the Entität and of Komponente 734 rests on short quotations, each a sentence or less, and the rest of what it says about them is its reading through Luhmann, Lacan and Baudrillard.
