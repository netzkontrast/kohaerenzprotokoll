## Reading — `projektplanung-fuer-kohaerenz-protokoll`, 2025-12-05, the planning report — System Kael as a Systemkarte, in a proposed workflow

The planning report proposes a workflow; what it says of Kael is a plan for the author's database, and for the system's make-up it cites the Writer's Bible (its reference 5). It calls Kael a system: „System Kael“ ^[?], who is „ein komplexes System aus Apparently Normal Parts (ANP) und Emotional Parts (EP)“ ^[?], with TSDP named as its basis. The report proposes that the `Maximal-Plotter` workflow replace the character sheet: „ersetzt das statische Charakterblatt durch eine dynamische“ ^[?] Systemkarte kept in the database.

In its profile of the alters, as the report renders the Writer's Bible, Kael is the host: „Kael versucht ständig, die Existenz der anderen zu leugnen oder zu minimieren.“ ^[?] The same line adds that his failure drives the first act. The report also uses Kael as an example in the scene properties, for Fronter and for Co-Conscious („z.B. Argus, Kael“ ^[?]). Its „Ch N“ are Hamilton's model chapters and are not mapped here.
