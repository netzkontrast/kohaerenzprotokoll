## Reading — `projektplanung-fuer-kohaerenz-protokoll`, 2025-12-05, the planning report — AEGIS as an interviewed agent, a log-entry slot and a blind spot, all proposed

The planning report proposes a workflow; what it says of AEGIS is a plan for the author's work, not a statement of the world. Its author is „System-Dirigent“ ^[?L21] of agents that include AEGIS, and the report proposes to question that agent: „Wie interpretierst du Kaels letzte Handlung basierend auf deiner Logik der“ ^[?] Kohärenz durch Negation. It justifies the method by a claim about AEGIS: the procedure prevents logical breaks, „da AEGIS immer streng gemäß seiner Programmierung handelt“ ^[?], even where that leads to tragic wrong decisions (the report names it Hamartia).

In the ontology table the K₁ row gives as an example „Die zentrale AEGIS-Kernarchitektur, Logos-Prime“ ^[?], and the Riss-Zone row adds „AEGIS sendet Enforcer, um diese Zonen zu bereinigen“ ^[?]. Of Lex the report says „Lex ist der natürliche Verbündete von AEGIS“ ^[?], a conflict it wants to escalate in Phase II.

For the Hamilton mapping (the report's own „Ch“ numbers are chapters of Hamilton's model, not of the novel), it proposes at its first slot „Einführung von AEGIS“ ^[?] and at the midpoint slot „AEGIS wird als tragisch, nicht böse erkannt“ ^[?]. Phase III ends in „Die finale Konfrontation mit AEGIS“ ^[?] by logical overload, not by combat.

In the table of open questions the report gives the antagonist a slot: „Ein dedizierter Slot für AEGIS' Perspektive“ ^[?] as log entries, and the same cell says „AEGIS wird tragisch“ ^[?]. The catalyst row requires showing „dass AEGIS diesen Link“ ^[?] (the Moonshine-Link) cannot see; the resolution row asks that „Der Workflow muss sicherstellen, dass AEGIS' logische Axiome vorher etabliert wurden“ ^[?].

Everything here is a proposal for the plotting workflow; the report does not say whether the log-entry slot is in the first or third person.
