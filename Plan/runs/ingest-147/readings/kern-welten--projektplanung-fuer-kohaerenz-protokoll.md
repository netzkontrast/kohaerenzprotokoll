## Reading — `projektplanung-fuer-kohaerenz-protokoll`, 2025-12-05, the planning report — the four Kernwelten as writing-rule containers

The planning report proposes that the four Kernwelten are „externalisierte psychologische Zustände des Protagonisten Systems“ ^[?], a claim it attributes by reference number to another document, and that its workflow uses them as containers for the 39 mosaic stories. For each it proposes a writing rule that the workflow activates when a scene is placed there.

For Logos-Prime (KW1) the rule begins „Keine Metaphern. Reine Deskription.“ ^[?], and its psychological counterpart is the rational defence of the ANPs. For the Mnemosyne-Archipel (KW2): „Nicht-lineare Zeitstruktur.“ ^[?] with olfactory dominance, and the world as „Der Speicherort der EPs“ ^[?] (Kiko, Lia). For the Cerberus-Labyrinth (KW3): „Klaustrophobische Räume.“ ^[?]. For Kairos-Potentialis (KW4): „Fließende Formen.“ ^[?], labelled a container of possibility and emergence.

The report says the method „Diese Methode löst das Problem der“ ^[?] mood fragmentation by tying the atmosphere to the place.

All of this is a proposed writing method; the rules are the report's, not stated as the novel's.
