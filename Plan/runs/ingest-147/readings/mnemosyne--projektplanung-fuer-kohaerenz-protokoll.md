## Reading — `projektplanung-fuer-kohaerenz-protokoll`, 2025-12-05, the planning report — the Mnemosyne-Archipel as a container and as a cost

In the planning report's proposal the Mnemosyne-Archipel is KW2, the place of memory and grief, with a non-linear time rule (L67, L72). Among the three categories of irreversible cost the report proposes for Phase II, the world category reads „Ein Teil des Mnemosyne-Archipels stürzt ins Nichts-Rauschen“ ^[?], and the map then becomes smaller. In its table mapping stories to Hamilton's model, the slot at the lowest point has „Totaler Zusammenbruch der Juna-Verbindung“ ^[?] and the destruction of an important place (for example Mnemosyne), as an irreversible cost. These are proposals for the plot, not statements of what happens.
