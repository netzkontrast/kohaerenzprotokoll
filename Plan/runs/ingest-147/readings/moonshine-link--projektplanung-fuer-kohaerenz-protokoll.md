## Reading — `projektplanung-fuer-kohaerenz-protokoll`, 2025-12-05, the planning report — the Moonshine-Link as a concept to define, and AEGIS's blind spot

In its table of open questions the planning report asks how the Moonshine-Link works technically, and gives the slot „Konzept-Definition in DB“ ^[?]: it is to be defined in the database under Quantenverschränkung and Prehension. For Phase II the report adds that it must be shown „dass AEGIS diesen Link“ ^[?] cannot see (Blinder Fleck). The roadmap lists writing the short concept for the Moonshine-Link physics among the tasks (L379). The report proposes; what the link carries it does not say.
