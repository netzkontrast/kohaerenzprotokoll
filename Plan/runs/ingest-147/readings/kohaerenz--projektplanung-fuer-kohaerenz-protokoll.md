## Reading — `projektplanung-fuer-kohaerenz-protokoll`, 2025-12-05, the planning report — Kohärenz (`K₁`) against Kollaps (`K₀`) as the classifying logic of a database

The planning report proposes to make the duality the metadata schema of the plotting tool. It states the world as governed by „fundamentalen und ewigen Konflikt zwischen zwei Prinzipien“ ^[?], Kohärenz (`K₁`) and Kollaps (`K₀`), a framing it cites to a numbered reference; it adds that the theory is „der physikalische Motor der Welt“ ^[?] and, in the workflow, the classifying logic of every database entry.

Its ontology table gives `K₁` „Orte oder Zustände maximaler Ordnung, Information und Kontrolle“ ^[?], `K₀` „Orte des Zerfalls, irreversible Informationslöschung“ ^[?], and a fourth row, the Integraler Status, „Gleichzeitigkeit von“ ^[?] `K₁` and `K₀`, the target state of the third act, where „Hier gelten paradoxe Wahrheiten“ ^[?].

As a pacing rule for the author: „Ein Plot-Strang, der zu lange in“ ^[?] `K₁` states stays, becomes static; too much `K₀` leads to chaos, so the workflow asks for a rhythmic balance. The report also quotes AEGIS's logic as „Kohärenz durch Negation“ ^[?L166] inside a sample prompt, without defining it.

The table is the report's schema for a database, not its statement of canon.
