## Reading — `romanplot-kohaerenz-protokoll-teil-1`, 2025-04-18, the Teil-1 plot — Juna as an irrational warmth, an anchor, contraband and an absence

The Teil-1 plot gives Juna no character profile of her own; she appears as a connection Kael feels. In Kap 1 it proposes a „Plötzliches, unlogisches Gefühl von Wärme“ ^[?], which Kael cannot analyse and suppresses. In KW2 she is „Juna als Anker“ ^[?], and in KW3, under Cerberus, „Juna als Schmuggelware“ ^[?]: there Kael learns to suppress every sign of the connection, which for the plot shows Cerberus's flaw, „Verbindung/Verletzlichkeit als Bedrohung zu sehen“ ^[?]. At the lowest point, „Junas Abwesenheit“ ^[?] is a connection that feels fully cut. In Kap 11, „Er versteht, dass die Juna-Verbindung nicht nur Erinnerung oder Emotion ist“ ^[?], and the same line adds that it is possibly tied to an `Externe Ebene`. The matrix has a column `Manifestation Juna-Echo`, and its KW1 row gives the blind spot „Ignoriert Nicht-Logisches (Emotion, Juna)“ ^[?].
