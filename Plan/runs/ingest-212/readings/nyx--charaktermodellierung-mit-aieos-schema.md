## Reading — `charaktermodellierung-mit-aieos-schema`, 2026-02-28, the AIEOS evaluation — Nyx as the fighter and a case study of unbindable triggers

The AIEOS evaluation, citing its sources, places Nyx with Alex in a protection front that exists in relation to the vulnerable Kiko and Lia (L69). Its third case study is `Fallbeispiel: Nyx (Der Kämpfer EP)` (L126): „Nyx verkörpert die Trauma-Wut und die aggressive Verteidigung“ ^[?]. The line adds that he arose from experiences of powerlessness and that his aggression is, it says, a misdirected protective function for the child parts Kiko and Lia.

The Bewertung says the schema models him well through `triggers` and `dominance_score`, but: „Es fehlt jedoch die Möglichkeit, im Schema die direkte Abhängigkeit zu definieren“ ^[?], for instance binding `triggers.anger` to the state vector of the object Kiko (L141).
