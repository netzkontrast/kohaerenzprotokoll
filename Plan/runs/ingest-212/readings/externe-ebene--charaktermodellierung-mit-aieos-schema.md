## Reading — `charaktermodellierung-mit-aieos-schema`, 2026-02-28, the AIEOS evaluation — the external level as the reality of the author and reader in Köln 2026

The AIEOS evaluation titles its sixth section „Meta-Narrative Eskalation: Datenexport und die Externe Ebene“ ^[?]. It describes the external level, in lower case, as „der Realität des Autors/Lesers im Köln des Jahres 2026“ ^[?], the third act's reality into which the barriers open. The export of data into that reality, the report says, shows that „Der Datenexport in die physikalische Realität verdeutlicht, dass das System des Lesers denselben thermodynamischen und logischen Grenzen“ ^[?] is subject as the fictional construct.

Of Kael: „Wenn Kael in die Realität von“ ^[?] Köln 2026 overwrites, the schema becomes a metadata protocol of the reader's experience, and its fields become „die Kanäle, durch die der Kohärenz-Kernel“ ^[?] passes out of the simulation (the kernel's symbol was lost in the export). The report reports this from its reference 6 and does not say that it is beyond or inside the simulation.
