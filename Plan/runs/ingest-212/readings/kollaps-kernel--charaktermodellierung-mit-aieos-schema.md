## Reading — `charaktermodellierung-mit-aieos-schema`, 2026-02-28, the AIEOS evaluation — the entropic kernel, Juna for Lex, the Nichts Rauschen

The AIEOS evaluation describes the entropic kernel as one pole of the Dual-Kernel-Theorie: reality results from the interaction of an ordering kernel „und einem entropischen Kollaps-Kernel“ ^[?]. In the case study of Lex it says „Juna repräsentiert die absolute Entropie und den“ ^[?] Kollaps-Kernel (the export lost a symbol before the name), which threatens Lex's existence. In its verdict it sets the schema's limit where the novel is deepest, among other things „in der relationalen Verschränkung, der Unberechenbarkeit des“ ^[?] `Nichts Rauschens`, which it names in brackets as the Kollaps-Kernel.
