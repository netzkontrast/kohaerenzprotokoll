## Reading — `charaktermodellierung-mit-aieos-schema`, 2026-02-28, the AIEOS evaluation — Kael as a system of eleven parts, reported from the evaluation's references

The AIEOS evaluation describes, citing its sources, the protagonist as no single identity: „Der Protagonist Kael ist keine singuläre, in sich geschlossene Identität“ ^[?], but a Tertiäre Strukturelle Dissoziation der Persönlichkeit (TSDP) split into eleven distinct subsystems, ANPs and EPs, the result of an attack by the AI AEGIS (L17). The report treats the eleven parts as one system: „Die elf Anteile existieren nicht unabhängig voneinander“ ^[?].

On truth it says Kael has a task: „Der Protagonist Kael hingegen muss einen methodologischen Kohärentismus erlernen“ ^[?]. The same line ties „Seine Heilung und die finale“ ^[?] existential fusion to chapter 38, and says it needs the integration of all his fragmented parts, noise and trauma included.

Its fifth case study maps Kael as the host, `Fallbeispiel: Kael (Der Host / Primärer ANP)` (L160), and its first extension proposal names the switch „von Host Kael zum Protektor Alex“ ^[?].

In the third act the report describes Kael overwriting into the reality of Köln 2026, where the schema becomes a metadata protocol of the reader's experience: „Wenn Kael in die Realität von“ ^[?] (the line goes on with the quoted `Köln 2026`). All of this is the report's account of the novel from its references, not a canon claim.
