## Reading — `charaktermodellierung-mit-aieos-schema`, 2026-02-28, the AIEOS evaluation — entropy as a thermodynamic limit, and as Lex's fear

The AIEOS evaluation describes Moros physically: „Physikalisch entspricht Moros der Annäherung an den thermodynamischen Nullpunkt“ ^[?], and glosses it as maximal entropy and standstill. In the case study of Lex it lists `Entropie` among his irrational fears and justifies the row: „Juna repräsentiert die absolute Entropie und den“ ^[?] Kollaps-Kernel. The report writes of the third act that the reader's system is subject to the same limits as the fictional construct, among them entropy and Gödelian incompleteness: „Der Datenexport in die physikalische Realität verdeutlicht, dass das System des Lesers denselben thermodynamischen und logischen Grenzen“ ^[?].
