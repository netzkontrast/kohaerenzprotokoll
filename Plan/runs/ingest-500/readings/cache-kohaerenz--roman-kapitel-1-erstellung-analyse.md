## Reading — `roman-kapitel-1-erstellung-analyse`, 2025-12-28, the chapter-1 analysis — the mirror scene as failed Cache-Kohärenz

The chapter-1 analysis drafts the mirror scene with the failure of the `Cache-Kohärenz` of „seiner fragmentierten Psyche versagte unter dem Druck des Systems“ ^[?]. In the analytic insert it explains the term as borrowed from computer architecture and applied to the psyche: „Indem wir diesen Begriff auf die Psyche anwenden“ ^[?]. It reads the failure as a symptom, the brittleness of the barriers between Alters, and calls the scene „ein klassisches Tropus der DID-Literatur“ ^[?]. A draft with commentary, recorded, not canon.
