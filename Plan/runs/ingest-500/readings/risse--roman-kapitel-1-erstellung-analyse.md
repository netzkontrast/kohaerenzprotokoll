## Reading — `roman-kapitel-1-erstellung-analyse`, 2025-12-28, the chapter-1 analysis — the Risse in the fall and at the close

The chapter-1 analysis drafts the fall: „Er fiel durch die“ ^[?] Risse, the gaps in the Kohärenz-Protokoll, because the simulation could no longer hold Kael's fragmenting soul. Earlier it drafts Juna's `Glitch` and, in the insert, reads it as „ein Durchbruch der Externen Ebene“ ^[?]. It closes: „Das Fundament ist gelegt. Die Risse weiten sich. Das Protokoll läuft.“ ^[?] A draft with commentary, recorded, not canon.
