## Reading — `roman-kapitel-1-erstellung-analyse`, 2025-12-28, the chapter-1 analysis — Mnemosyne as the guardian of memory and grief

In an insert the chapter-1 analysis reads Kael's heartbeat as the first sign of the „Resonanz“ ^[?] that Mnemosyne will later identify as a threat. In section 3.2 it assigns Mnemosyne the wave of grief at Juna's call: „Die Welle der Trauer, die Kael bei Junas Anruf spürt, ist das Territorium von Mnemosyne“ ^[?]. A draft with commentary, recorded, not canon.
