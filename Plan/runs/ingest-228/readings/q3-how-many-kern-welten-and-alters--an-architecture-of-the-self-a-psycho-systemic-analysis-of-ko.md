## 2026-10-11 — `an-architecture-of-the-self-a-psycho-systemic-analysis-of-ko`, 2025-10-15, the psycho-systemic analysis

**The psycho-systemic analysis lists eight alters in three groups and three Kernwelten with worlds for the ANPs, the EPs and the phobias.**

The essay's table sets out the TSDP groups: „Apparently Normal Parts (ANPs)“ ^[?L35] with Kael (Host), Lex, Rhys and Praetor, „Emotional Parts (EPs)“ ^[?] with Nyx, Kiko and Oblivion, and „Inner Self Helper (ISH)“ ^[?L35] with Selene (L35–L39). It says of the ensemble: „this analysis adopts the canonical ensemble from“ ^[?], noting that other sources „offer variant lists“ ^[?] (L41) — so the count is adopted, not found by the essay. For the worlds it writes KW1 Logos-Prime as „the architectural manifestation of the ANPs' worldview“ ^[?], the Mnemosyne-Archipel as „the chaotic, unprocessed realm of the EPs“ ^[?], and the Cerberus-Labyrinth as „the physical manifestation of the deep-seated phobias between alters“ ^[?] (L100–L107). The essay does not say that one world corresponds to one alter; its worlds correspond to groups and to the phobias between them.

Stands: one more position in the record, an analysis rather than canon, with eight alters and three Kernwelten as it adopts them; it decides neither count nor the correspondence.
