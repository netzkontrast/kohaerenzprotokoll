## Reading — `an-architecture-of-the-self-a-psycho-systemic-analysis-of-ko`, 2025-10-15, the psycho-systemic analysis — System Kael as a psycho-architectural battleground and its fate

The psycho-systemic analysis, an essay that reads the novel as if written, calls System Kael „a living psycho-architectural battleground“ ^[?L21] and elevates him to a *vereinigter Protagonist* (united protagonist): „vereinigter Protagonist“ ^[?]. Its table sets Kael as „Kael (Host)“ ^[?L36] among the ANPs, an ensemble it says it adopts from another text (L41). Among the phobias it names „Phobia of Integration“ ^[?], and it reads the dyad Kael vs. Selene as the Host's phobia clashing with the system's drive toward wholeness: Selene is „internal therapist“ ^[?] and Kael sees her work as dangerous. At the climax it reads his integrated mind as a „living Gödel-sentence“ ^[?]. His fate is headed „Coherence through Integration“ ^[?], which the table describes as „Kael achieves a state of stable, functional multiplicity“ ^[?].
