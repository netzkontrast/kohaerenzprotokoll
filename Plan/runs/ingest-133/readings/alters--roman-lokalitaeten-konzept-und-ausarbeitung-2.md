## Reading — `roman-lokalitaeten-konzept-und-ausarbeitung-2`, 2025-04-18, the second locations concept — associated alters per world, and the seat of Limina

Each of the four world sections lists the alters it associates with the world, in the same words: „Assoziierte Alters sind“ ^[?L128] for KW1 (`Index`, the `Architekt` aspect of Kael, with `Praetor` and `Nox` showing through), „Assoziierte Alters sind“ ^[?L139] for KW2 (`Echo`, `Flicker`, `Silas`, `Oblivion`), „Assoziierte Alters sind“ ^[?L150] for KW3 (`Limina`, `Praetor`, `Oblivion`, `Nox`) and „Assoziierte Alters sind“ ^[?L161] for KW4 (`Eos`, `Index`, `Silas`, `Nox`). The names stand in the document's own straight quotation marks and are not quoted here.

For KW3 it adds that an inner bunker could serve „als Kontrollzentrum von Cerberus oder Sitz des“ ^[?] `Limina` alter; the sentence is conditional. In KW2's Risse it names „Bereiche des Vergessens oder der emotionalen Leere (Einfluss von“ ^[?] `Oblivion`.
