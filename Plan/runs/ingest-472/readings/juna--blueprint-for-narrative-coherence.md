## Reading — `blueprint-for-narrative-coherence`, 2025-10-15, the English Chapter 1 draft — Juna as a resonance across the Moonshine-Link

The English Chapter 1 draft narrates a wave of emotion that Kael meets in a corridor with the smell of rain. Its explaining voice says the wave was „a direct resonance from an entity named Juna“ ^[?] and that it came as „a shared quale transmitted across the non-local, acausal bond of the Moonshine-Link“ ^[?]. Juna does not appear in the scene; the draft names Juna only in this gloss. A draft, recorded here, not canon.
