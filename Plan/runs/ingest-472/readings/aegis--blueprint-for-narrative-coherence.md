## Reading — `blueprint-for-narrative-coherence`, 2025-10-15, the English Chapter 1 draft — AEGIS's world as brittle artifice, and a frequency its logic cannot perceive

The English Chapter 1 draft, an unsigned draft chapter and not canon, narrates Kael's glitches and then glosses them in an explaining voice. That voice calls the organic patterns „the first fingerprints of The Foundation, a deeper, more fundamental order trying to press through the brittle artifice of AEGIS’s world.“ ^[?] Later, when a wave of feeling reaches Kael in the corridor, the same voice says it was „activated by a frequency of empathy that AEGIS’s classical logic could not perceive.“ ^[?] The draft thus gives AEGIS a world of artifice and a classical logic, and records these as the draft's own gloss.
