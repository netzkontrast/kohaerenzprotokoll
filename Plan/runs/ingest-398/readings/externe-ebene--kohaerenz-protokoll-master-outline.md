## Reading — `kohaerenz-protokoll-master-outline`, 2026-02-25, the Master-Outline — the external level as the reader's real world, visited briefly, glitched

The Master-Outline plans in Part III that the borders flicker also towards the external level, which the line gives as „der realen Welt des Autors/Lesers“ ^[?]. Kael visits it briefly: „Er sieht die Welt des Lesers“ ^[?], and finds „dieselbe Entropie und dieselbe Unvollständigkeit herrschen“ ^[?]. The annotation instructs: „Der Besuch der externen Ebene muss so subtil“ ^[?] glitched that the reader doubts, „ob seine eigene Welt nicht auch nur eine Ebene des Konstrukts ist“ ^[?]. A plan, recorded and not applied.
