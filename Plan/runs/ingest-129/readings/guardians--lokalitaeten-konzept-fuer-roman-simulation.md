## Reading — `lokalitaeten-konzept-fuer-roman-simulation`, 2025-04-18, the locations concept — one Guardian per Kern-Welt in a table, five named in the Überwelt

The concept's comparison table has a column for the associated Guardian and the alters. It names LogOS for the first world, in the cell „LogOS / Index, Architekt, (Praetor, Nox)“ ^[?], and for the fourth world two names in one cell: „Kairos/Sophia / Eos, Index, Silas, (Nox)“ ^[?]. The headings give Mnemosyne for the second world, Cerberus for the third: „Resonanz-Landschaft (Mnemosyne)“ ^[?] and „Grenzfeste (Cerberus)“ ^[?], and „Möglichkeits-Garten (Kairos/Sophia)“ ^[?] for the fourth. Cerberus is described as „Cerberus ist der Guardian, der Grenzen und Sicherheitsprotokolle durchsetzt“ ^[?]. Of the fourth pair: „Kairos und Sophia versuchen, dieses Potenzial zu kanalisieren oder zu interpretieren“ ^[?]. The count and pairing are in the records C6 and Q5.
