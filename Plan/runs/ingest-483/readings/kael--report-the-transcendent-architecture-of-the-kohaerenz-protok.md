## Reading — `report-the-transcendent-architecture-of-the-kohaerenz-protok`, 2025-11-03, the transcendent-architecture report — Kael's evolution and earned gnosis, not a gift

The transcendent-architecture report reads the Juna/V connection as a device that acts „as a catalyst for Kael’s evolution“ ^[?] and not as a solution. Among the three origins it lists for the connection, one is „A transcendent aspect of Kael himself“ ^[?]. A Gnostic Injection gives Kael „direct, intuitive“ ^[?] understanding. The report places the resolution in Kael, not in Das Fundament: „Kael's earned“ ^[?] gnosis lets an integrated consciousness „finally resonate with and embody the fundamental principles of reality“ ^[?]. The report reads the foreshadowing in „sudden, intuitive flashes of understanding“ ^[?] that prefigure the final state. Its verdict that this avoids a Deus ex Machina is the report's own, recorded here and not applied.
