## Reading — `openai-math-contents`, 2026-10-06, the manuscript map — two families, the restricted and the quantum geometric Langlands equivalences

Family 014 is headed „Restricted geometric Langlands, global Arthur enhancements, and generic Ramanujan.“ ^[?] Its description reports the equivalence proved under conditions: it „Proves the restricted geometric Langlands equivalence for connected reductive groups on smooth projective connected curves“ ^[?] over finite fields, „under the four stated Lie-theoretic characteristic hypotheses“ ^[?].

Family 069 is headed „Global quantum geometric Langlands at irrational level.“ ^[?] Its description: it „Proves the unramified de Rham quantum geometric Langlands equivalence for every connected simple complex algebraic group on every smooth projective connected complex curve“ ^[?], and it „identifies the full derived categories of twisted D-modules for the group and its Langlands dual“ ^[?].

Both are geometric forms of the Langlands correspondence, each claimed in a stated range; the catalogue claims no form of the arithmetic programme in general.
