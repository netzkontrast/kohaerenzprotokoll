## Stance, read per passage

The document is an implementation guide in five numbered sections plus a source list, and it speaks as a plan for writing, not as a report of events in the novel. It labels its own parts and the labels say how to read a passage.

**Section 1 (L13 to L81), a synthesis of theory applied to the novel.** The heading is „Synthese der Fundamentalen Forschung“ ^[L13]. Its subsections apply concepts from philosophy of mind, existentialism, systems theory, trauma theory and narrative craft to the novel's two voices. It reports theories as theories (the hard-problem reasoning at L19 is a reported philosophical position) and then applies them: AEGIS is „als rein funktionales System konzipiert“ ^[L21], and the guide reads AEGIS' later elimination of Komponente 734 as following from the system's self-preservation logic ^[L31]. Scene numbers appear in parentheses as pointers to the outline.

**Section 2 (L83 to L157), a plan.** The heading is „Relevanz und Tiefenrecherche-Strategie“ ^[L83]. It groups findings into four clusters, each with the repeated labels `Relevanz`, `Nutzen für Outline` and `Potenzielle Fallstricke` (four times each, see the profile). A cluster entry reads, for example, „Potenzielle Fallstricke: Gefahr der Übertreibung“ ^[L93]. Subsection 2.3 holds search tasks written as quoted imperatives, for instance „Suche nach spezifischen literarischen Beispielen“ ^[L130]; these are questions to put to a research step and carry no reading of their own. The section closes by proposing two resources, a `Sensorisches Lexikon` and a „Technisches Glossar & Stil-Leitfaden“ ^[L157].

**Section 3 (L159 to L297), sample material marked as an example.** The note under the heading says „Da die Tiefenrecherche selbst nicht durchgeführt wurde“ ^[L161], so the section is filled „exemplarisch mit plausiblen“ content on the document's own account ^[L161]. Its quoted lines are sample prose in the novel's first-person voice ^[L169] and sample report lines in AEGIS' register ^[L437]; the `Ich` there is the novel's narrative voice. Word lists (glossary, metaphor lists, Wortfelder) are diction, not statements about the world.

**Section 4 (L299 to L673), the instructions.** Titled with „Regiebuch“ ^[L299]. A table named „Stylistic Contrast Summary“ ^[L307] contrasts the two perspectives in cells such as „Primärer POV“ ^[L312]. Thirteen scene entries follow under „Szenenbasierte Anweisungen“ ^[L321]; each repeats the labels `Stylistische Direktiven` ^[genesis-mehrstufige-recherche-und-ausformulierung.md:#13], `Spezifische Techniken/Beispiele` ^[genesis-mehrstufige-recherche-und-ausformulierung.md:#13] and `Dominante Stimmung/Atmosphäre` ^[genesis-mehrstufige-recherche-und-ausformulierung.md:#13]. Three scene headings carry the flag `NEUE Szene` ^[genesis-mehrstufige-recherche-und-ausformulierung.md:#3] (L468, L519, L570). Each beat quotes sample prose, either marked as the Ich's voice or as AEGIS' third-person report (for example „Systemzustand: Kritisch. Transformation eingeleitet.“ ^[L437]).

**Section 5 and the source list (L675 to L685).** The closing remarks state the guide's purpose and expected effect ^[L681]. The „Quellenangaben“ heading ^[L683] introduces a single flattened line of numbered web sources.

The document's own claim about its standing is a directive: „Die strikte Einhaltung dieser Vorgaben ist entscheidend für das Gelingen des Projekts.“ ^[L303] This is recorded as the document's claim and applied to nothing.
