## Stance, read per passage

The document speaks as an analyst addressed to the novel's maker: it reports on a concept, criticises it and proposes a plan. It labels most passages with the same words.

**Section I (L13 to L145), critique.** Each item is built from a `Konzept` ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-analyse-und-loglines.md:#26] part that restates the concept, an `Analyse` ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-analyse-und-loglines.md:#17] part and a `Potenzielle Schwachstelle` ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-analyse-und-loglines.md:#11] part. The concept lines report the novel's world, for example „Vier zentrale Welten“ ^[L38] and „plus eine abstrakte Überwelt“ ^[L38]. The analysis lines apply outside theory (autopoiesis, cybernetics, a parts-of-the-mind model) to AEGIS and Kael, and those theories are the sources' own words, reported by the document. Weaknesses are put as questions: „Hat Juna Handlungsmacht oder ist sie eine Kraft“ ^[L50] and „Ist Paradoxon X eine genuine emergente Eigenschaft“ ^[L30].

**Section II (L147 to L204), plan.** A pacing strategy per part, each with `Emotionaler Bogen` ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-analyse-und-loglines.md:#4], `Pacing-Ziel` ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-analyse-und-loglines.md:#4] and `Schlüssel-Wendepunkte` ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-analyse-und-loglines.md:#4], and a table of six rows (L187 to L192). Its stated purpose is „Diese Tabelle liefert eine klare, übergeordnete Roadmap“ ^[L194]. The three parts are named `Innere Reise`, `Zyklischer Kampf` and `Äußere Befreiung` at L131.

**Section III (L206 to L268), proposed loglines.** The document describes it as „Dieser Abschnitt liefert das Kern-Deliverable“ ^[L208]. The loglines are proposals for 39 chapters; they hedge with question marks and the word `vielleicht`, and some are tagged `Pacing: Langsam` ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-analyse-und-loglines.md:#6] or `Pacing: Mittel` ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-analyse-und-loglines.md:#8]. They are not reports of written chapters.

**Section IV (L270 to L287), synthesis and recommendation.** A self-check headed `Kohärenzprüfung` ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-analyse-und-loglines.md:#1] opens „Eine abschließende Überprüfung bestätigt“ ^[L276], and the closing paragraph says „Dieses Dokument bietet eine kritisch reflektierte Grundlage“ ^[L287]. The References (L289 to L314) are web sources for the outside theory and writing advice.

The document names a bracket tag `Allgemeines Erzählprinzip` ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-analyse-und-loglines.md:#2] and `[Konzept]` for the premises it reasons from; these tag the document's own reasoning chains, not novel terms.
