## Stance, read per passage

The file holds four passages under four headings, and each speaks differently. None carries a status label such as plan or lock; the document marks itself only by headings, by capitals, and by one remark outside the story.

- **Vorwort (the heading at L11).** A second-person address to the reader, with an exercise: the voice proposes „Versuchen wir es gemeinsam.“ ^[L34] and then turns to questions about an existence that guards the border against nothing: „Was, wenn es eine Form von Existenz gäbe“ ^[L94]. It breaks off mid-word: „Eine nicht-lokale Nicht-“ ^[L110]. It speaks of the philosophers' and physicists' nothing in the voice of an essay, naming `Parmenides` ^[roman-kohaerenz-protokoll.md:#1] and `Śūnyatā` ^[roman-kohaerenz-protokoll.md:#1].
- **Genesis im Echo der Leere (L114).** English status report in bold capitals, apparently the system's own output; it opens „ALERT: INTEGRITY VIOLATION DETECTED.“ ^[L118] and sets „STATUS: HIGH ALERT.“ ^[L132]. Modules report in turn (L156 to L218). Inside the report a German motto stands between ASCII quote marks (L250); it is the report's own words, not narration. From L268 the heading continues in German narration, a prose account of a reboot, interrupted by report lines such as „UNIVERSAL REBOOT COMPLETE.“ ^[L304]. Its last sentences are statements, not questions: „Die Grenze wurde gehalten.“ ^[L336]
- **Kapitel 1: Instrumente der Ordnung (L353).** Close third person on `Kael` ^[roman-kohaerenz-protokoll.md:#15], in the past tense, with direct speech in „…“ marks. Digits are glued to sentence ends as footnote numbers. Several sentences end with a name and a question mark in round brackets, the narrator guessing whose voice an impulse is; the same device stands on L373, L377, L379, L387, L389, L391, L395, L397 and L399 (observed).
- **Kapitel 2: Resonanz (L417).** The chapter is cut into 40 headings of the form `Moment:` ^[roman-kohaerenz-protokoll.md:#40], each numbered, from „(1. Moment: Sensorischer Angriff)“ ^[L419] to „(40. Moment: Der Scheideweg)“ ^[L585]. The prose under each is narration of Kael's perception. The text before Moment 31 is a remark in another voice, outside the story: „Okay, hier ist die Ausformulierung der letzten zehn Momente“ ^[L545]. It says these ten Momente conclude „die Ankunft an der Schwelle zu KW2“ ^[L545].
