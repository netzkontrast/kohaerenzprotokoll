## Stance, read per passage

The document is an explanatory guide in English that reads a fictional cast through two psychological frameworks; it reports nothing as a plan or lock and asks no questions of its own beyond the label of its first section.

- L15 (introduction): the document claims the characters' behavior is „not a product of creative chaos“ ^[L15] and calls the struggles, functions and collective journey „a profound allegory for the human mind's capacity to endure and heal“ ^[L15]. Register: claim about the story, in the document's own voice.
- L19 (section 1): defines „System“ as not a computer: „does not refer to a computer or a network“ ^[L19]. Register: definition.
- L23 (section 2): reports a theory (TSDP) and then maps it onto the story's cosmology: the daily-life parts mirror „the drive for order and control embodied by AEGIS“ ^[L23]. Register: the theory's claims, then the document's own mapping.
- L27 (2.1): a two-column table labelled `Function:`, `Relationship to Trauma:` and `Core Drive:`; each appears twice, once per column. Register: the theory's division, set out as a template.
- L31 (2.2): a roster table of six alters, columns `Alter`, `TSDP Role`, `Function in the System`. Register: the document's assignment of roles to the cast.
- L37 to L44 (section 3): reports a therapeutic model (IFS) and applies it to two alters; the principle is given as the model's: „has a positive intention and is trying to protect the system“ ^[L37]. Register: the model's claim, then the document's application.
- L48 to L52 (section 4): the document's synthesis: „The goal is not to merge all alters into a single personality“ ^[L48]; a three-item numbered list of principles, two of them given with German names, and a closing claim about AEGIS and a logic it cannot compute. Register: the document's own thesis.
- L56 (conclusion): restates the two frameworks as the „what“ and the „how“. Register: summary.

The italic sentences at L19, L33, L44 and L48 are bridges between sections, not terms.
