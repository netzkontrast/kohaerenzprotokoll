## Stance, read per passage

The document is a chapter-by-chapter outline of a novel, in the future tense of a plan and hedged throughout. Observed: the body runs from a prologue, headed `Chapter Prologue:` ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-detailliertes-roman-outline-leserzentrie.md:#1] there, through 39 numbered chapters (`Chapter` stands 40 times in all), and each chapter carries a bracketed title, a bracketed `Handlung` label with one paragraph of plot, and a fixed block of eight field labels (the repeated labels of the profile).

- Acts. Three bold act lines divide the chapters: „Innere Reise (Heroine's Journey Fokus)“ ^[L30], „Meta-Ebene & Zyklen (Meta-Exploration)“ ^[L253] and „Äußere Konfrontation & Rückkehr (Hero's Journey Fokus)“ ^[L476]; the prologue stands under its own line `Act Prologue:` (four `Act` in all).

They describe what Kael does and meets and mostly hedge with `vielleicht` and `evtl.` (`vielleicht` ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-detailliertes-roman-outline-leserzentrie.md:#16], `evtl` ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-detailliertes-roman-outline-leserzentrie.md:#10]); names are often followed by a question mark, as in „Kiko?“ in the field lines.
- Field labels. `Core Theme`, `Kael Internal`, `AEGIS Focus`, `Setting`, `Subplots`, `Philosophy`, `Genre/Trope` and `Reader Psychology Note` are the template of every chapter; they name fields and are not world terms. Near the end the template gains `Juna/V Focus` (L625, L659) and `Fundament Focus` (L660), and three chapters, 37 to 39, carry no `AEGIS Focus` line (it stands 37 times, `AEGIS Focus` ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-detailliertes-roman-outline-leserzentrie.md:#37]).

- Reader voice. The last field of every chapter, `Reader Psychology Note`, speaks about the effect on the reader, not about the world: it opens the series with „Etabliert die kosmische Dimension und den Antagonisten vor dem Protagonisten“ ^[L28].
- Outline against itself. Later chapters point back by number ("Kap. 12", "Kap. 25", `Kap` 14 times) so the outline is also a dependency map between chapters, but it never states its own status as canon or draft.
