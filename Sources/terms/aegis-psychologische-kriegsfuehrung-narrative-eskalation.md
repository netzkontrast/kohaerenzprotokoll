Third, Table 1 writes the world labels for Rhys and Kiko with escaped underscores and extra backslashes, so they were not listed. Fourth, the tables are markdown with escaped bold markers, and the table titles are bold lines inside a table cell; `Tabelle 1`, `Tabelle 2` and `Tabelle 3` are listed as written in those cells. Fifth, the phrase `The following table:` is an English line the export left before each of the three tables.
