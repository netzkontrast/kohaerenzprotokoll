## Stance, read per passage

The document is a plan written as an analysis: four Teile, each opened by a sentence saying what the part does. Part I: „Dieser Teil dekonstruiert den tragischen Bogen von AEGIS“ ^[L23]. Part II: „Dieser Teil zeichnet Kaels innere Reise“ ^[L100]. Part III: „liefert eine definitive, logisch konsistente Erklärung“ ^[L157]. Part IV: „Dieser letzte Teil konzentriert sich auf die meta-narrative Architektur“ ^[L205]. The openers of the first three Teile begin with the same words (`Dieser Teil` ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-finaler-bauplan.md:#3]).

Passages 1.1 to 1.4 (L27 to L83) speak in the present tense as description of the story's machine, with proposals marked as such: „wird eine hybride kognitive Architektur vorgeschlagen“ ^[L43]. Passage 1.4 describes the state after the transformation and frames one symptom as depiction: „Es wird dargestellt, wie es den Großteil“ ^[L81] of its computing power goes to contemplation.

Passage 2.3 reports the plot as settled: „Wie im finalen Plot dargelegt“ ^[L134] and „Die Erzählung folgt definitiv dem“ ^[L136] one scenario. The document's title line calls itself „Ein Definitives Narratives und Ontologisches Framework“ ^[L13]; this is a claim about its own standing and is recorded, not applied.

Part IV speaks in the future and the conditional about technique, a plan for the prose: „Die Erzählung wird nicht-lineare Techniken verwenden“ ^[L223]. Three sentences of Parts IV end in a bracketed `User Query` ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-finaler-bauplan.md:#3] mark, which the document uses to attribute a phrase to the commissioning request. The closing passage leaves a question to the reader: „Die Geschichte endet daher nicht“ ^[L237] with a final answer.

The two tables (L87 to L92 and L142 to L149) are cells in a template, one row per logic or per part, not running prose; their column heads are the labels (`Alter Name`, `Trauma-basierte Funktion (Vorher)`, `Integrierte Rolle (Nachher)`).
