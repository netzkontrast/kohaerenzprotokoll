## Stance, read per passage

The document has no labels of its own such as plan, lock or report. It speaks as one voice, the `Narrativer Architekt` ^[charaktere.md:#3], and addresses a reader as the one who commissioned it. Its passages differ by what they do, and the grammar and the headings are the only markers. The reading below is by passage.

- **Opening, L10 to L13.** A first-person assignment frame: „Als Narrativer Architekt ist es meine Aufgabe“ ^[L11] begins the document, and the same line names the commission as „Ihr Forschungsauftrag für“ ^[L11]. The frame states what the text is for and asserts no world fact.
- **First answer, L15 to L108.** Headed „Die Architektur von System Kael“ ^[L15]. It is a causal account (trauma, eleven Anteile, inner dynamics, a Fazit). Where it reports what its inputs say it names them only as „Die Quellen belegen explizit“ ^[L21]; the inner quotation on that line is those sources' wording, and the document names no source. The roster in L29 to L83 repeats one field set under each Anteil: `Funktion & Ursprung` ^[charaktere.md:#22], `Motivation/Angst` ^[charaktere.md:#11], and `TSDP-Typ` ^[charaktere.md:#11].
- **Second answer, L110 to L248.** A second opening of the same commission (L110 repeats the Narrativer Architekt frame). It announces a mapping: „Hier ist eine detaillierte Kartierung jedes Anteils“ ^[L129]. Its field labels differ (`Trauma-Fokus` ^[charaktere.md:#22], `Kern-Phobien` ^[charaktere.md:#11], `Integrierte Rolle` ^[charaktere.md:#11], `Beziehung zu AEGIS` ^[charaktere.md:#11]). It marks gaps in its own profiles with a parenthesis: „Nicht explizit in allen Quellen“ ^[L170] for Lia, and „Nicht explizit, aber würde auf gesunde Sexualität“ ^[L178] for Isabelle, and „Nicht explizit, aber impliziert als fortgesetzter“ ^[L194] for Alex. Those three profiles therefore carry an inferred, not a reported, role.
- **Healing path, L232 to L246.** A plan in the present tense: „Kaels Reise zielt nicht auf die vollständige Fusion“ ^[L234] states the goal as a stated direction. It also describes the novel's ending as settled: „Der Roman schließt mit Kael in einem Zustand stabiler funktionaler Multiplizität“ ^[L242].
- **Third answer, L248 to L397.** Headed „Kohärenz Protokoll: Umfassendes Charakter-Konzept-Dokument“ ^[L250], with Roman-numeral sections. Its stated purpose is „Das vorliegende Dokument dient als umfassende“ ^[L254]. It reports its own method as „Unsere Methodik umfasste eine detaillierte Analyse der vorliegenden Quellen“ ^[L256], and says of its profiles „Die folgenden Profile wurden basierend auf DID-Forschung kritisch überprüft und verfeinert“ ^[L263].
