## What the document is

A commentary on one narrative text, written as an exegesis. It quotes and retells the narrative stage by stage and reads each stage through a borrowed theory, chiefly Luhmann's systems theory, with Parmenides, Śūnyatā, Sartre, Heidegger, Lacan, Baudrillard, Foucault and Hegel beside it. It is one headline, eight numbered sections with 26 headings in all, a synopsis table of eight rows and a list of 39 references, the first of which is the analysed text. The narrative is present only as short quotations and paraphrases; the document never reproduces it whole. Its prose is German, with English glosses in parentheses and English titles in the list of references. It gives its subject as „Genesis der Existenz“ ^[L22], and the Drive title, `Textanalyse: Existenz, System und Leid`, is not the headline it carries.

## What the extraction ran into

**The analysed text's title is not on the list.** `Genesis der Existenz` ^[textanalyse-existenz-system-und-leid.md:#5] stands in the headline, at lines 22, 34 and 304, and once in reference 1 as `Einleitung: Genesis der Existenz` ^[textanalyse-existenz-system-und-leid.md:#1]. It is the title of the text the document analyses, so by the rule for titles of cited works it is left off the list. Its nouns for what it reads include `Text` ^[textanalyse-existenz-system-und-leid.md:#25] (as a word, whatever each use refers to), `Erzählung` ^[textanalyse-existenz-system-und-leid.md:#2] and `Textkorpus` ^[textanalyse-existenz-system-und-leid.md:#1], and once, only to deny it, „bloße narrative Skizze“ ^[L22].

**The zeros.** Two of the 239 candidates count zero, `Abwehr des Nichts` and `Integration der Leere`, and both are export damage, not terms the document lacks. The file carries emphasis marks inside each phrase, `*Abwehr* des Nichts` and `*Integration* der Leere` at line 310, so the marked line does not hold the phrase. Read unmarked, both stand there, and their first words stand twice each, `Abwehr` ^[textanalyse-existenz-system-und-leid.md:#2] and `Integration` ^[textanalyse-existenz-system-und-leid.md:#2]. They are the document's antithesis, defence against the Nichts and integration of the Leere. No other candidate is written differently from the list.

**The counts include the list of references.** Where a candidate is also a word in the title or the address of a cited work, the count takes it in. Split between the prose (lines 10 to 311) and the references (lines 312 to 353), checked by line range in `05-verify.txt`: `AEGIS` ^[textanalyse-existenz-system-und-leid.md:#33] 30 in the prose and 3 in the references; `Aegis` ^[textanalyse-existenz-system-und-leid.md:#15] 6 and 9; `Luhmann` ^[textanalyse-existenz-system-und-leid.md:#19] 12 and 7; `Autopoiesis` ^[textanalyse-existenz-system-und-leid.md:#8] 4 and 4; `Systemtheorie` ^[textanalyse-existenz-system-und-leid.md:#5] 4 and 1; `Kommunikation` ^[textanalyse-existenz-system-und-leid.md:#4] 2 and 2; `Parmenides` ^[textanalyse-existenz-system-und-leid.md:#9] 8 and 1; `Nagarjuna` ^[textanalyse-existenz-system-und-leid.md:#2] 1 and 1; `Ontological Friction` ^[textanalyse-existenz-system-und-leid.md:#2] 1 and 1; `Sartre` ^[textanalyse-existenz-system-und-leid.md:#3] none in the prose and 3 in the references; `Lacan` ^[textanalyse-existenz-system-und-leid.md:#4] 2 in the prose, both in the synopsis table, and 2 in the references.
