## Stance, read per passage

**Observed:** the document is an album concept, not a text about the novel's world: it is a research report that prepares a song cycle and says so itself. Its stance changes with the section; each passage is read here in its own terms.

- L11 to L25, introduction: a purpose statement. It states the aim as „Ziel dieses Berichts ist es, eine fundierte psychologische und narrative Basis für das musikalische Prelude zu schaffen“ ^[L25], and gives a roadmap of the sections. The passage also holds a statement of the situation the paper starts from, which is personal in kind and is not read here.
- L27 to L224, sections 2 to 4: a literature-style report on trauma, attachment, dissociation, co-dependence, trust repair and differentiation, written as description of psychological concepts. Three tables carry the summaries, each introduced by a bold label, for example „Auswirkungen unsicherer Bindungsstile in Post-Trauma-Beziehungen“ ^[L55], „Phasen der Vertrauenswiederherstellung und Heilung von Bindungsverletzungen“ ^[L107] and „Kontrastierung von geringer vs. hoher Differenzierung in Beziehungen“ ^[L175]. Several of these sentences report the models of other authors and schools, so their terms are borrowed concepts, not the document's own inventions.
- L224, the bridge paragraph: the report ties its concepts back to the cycle with „Die Entwicklung von Differenzierung kann als die Brücke betrachtet werden“ ^[L224]; this is the document's own proposal, phrased as a reading.
- L226 to L271, section 5, „Metaphern und Archetypen für die Reise“ ^[L226]: a list of inspirations (a tightrope walk, a labyrinth, veiled figures, snow, art shaping silence) and of archetypal journeys, each as a suggestion for the lyric and music design.
- L273 to L302, section 6: a synthesis headed „Kernthemen für die musikalische Erkundung“ ^[L277] and „Potenzielle Konflikte und Wendepunkte“ ^[L290]. The bullets are labelled `Konflikt` (four times) and `Wendepunkt (Positiv)` (three times), plus one `Wendepunkt (Positiv/Negativ)` and one `Wendepunkt (Intern)`; the labels name a slot in a scheme and carry the sentence after them.
