**Proof passages (L40 to L67).** Physics and cosmology are applied as lenses. The document labels them proofs: „Der Beweis für die funktionale Zugehörigkeit von AEGIS“ ^[L42] and „liefert den endgültigen kosmologischen Beweis“ ^[L56]. These are the document's claims about its own argument.

**Literary frame and synthesis (L133 to L147).** The trope of order against chaos is described, then the three-phase plot is retold under the heading „Synthese: Das Mosaik-Herz als wahre Kohärenz“ ^[L141].

**Fazit (L149 to L160).** The document says its analysis „führt zu einer definitiven Bestätigung“ ^[L151] of the thesis stated at the start. It records its own standing as a claim only; nothing here is applied. The numbered list at L155 to L158 is its four-part summary of the inversion.
