## Stance, read per passage

The text is a plan, not a report. It opens with a table under „Master Narrative & Thematische Arc Übersicht“ ^[L13] listing 39 chapters with a part, a phase and a core theme, and then gives each of three parts a paragraph headed `Gesamtfokus` ^[romanarchitektur-kael-aegis-entropie-docx.md:#3], at L59, L245 and L431. Each chapter section is a fixed template: `Vertiefende Konzepte` ^[romanarchitektur-kael-aegis-entropie-docx.md:#38], `Charakter-/Systemdynamik` ^[romanarchitektur-kael-aegis-entropie-docx.md:#37], `Genre-Tropes & Innovative Interpretation` ^[romanarchitektur-kael-aegis-entropie-docx.md:#37], `Subplot-Andeutungen` ^[romanarchitektur-kael-aegis-entropie-docx.md:#37] and `Narrative Funktion & Innovative Vermittlung` ^[romanarchitektur-kael-aegis-entropie-docx.md:#37]. These labels name fields of the template; they are not terms of the novel's world.

The voice is conditional throughout. `möglicherweise` ^[romanarchitektur-kael-aegis-entropie-docx.md:#36] and `vielleicht` ^[romanarchitektur-kael-aegis-entropie-docx.md:#31] stand on many lines, and the chapter content is offered as what a chapter could do: „Der Anteil Argus, der bisher eher als passiver“ ^[L407] is followed by a plan of a change of role. Questions stand inside the plan, for example „Wird AEGIS zerstört?“ ^[L565]; terms that appear only inside such a question have no reading. One passage shows the question and the guess together: „Ein überraschender Verbündeter“ ^[L556] is followed in the same sentence by a bracket that asks whether a guardian defected or whether Juna/V intervenes.

Inside the template the document speaks in two voices. The trope line names a borrowed genre pattern, and the word `Innovation` ^[romanarchitektur-kael-aegis-entropie-docx.md:#74] then states how the document's own world turns it, for instance in the sentence about the sterile city at „KW1 wird als perfekt geordnete, logische Stadt dargestellt“ ^[L71]. The trope names are the lens; the sentence after the word `Innovation` is the document's own claim. Chapter content names psychology as a source of its material with the label `Psychologisch` ^[romanarchitektur-kael-aegis-entropie-docx.md:#6], for instance „Die Rolle von ANPs“ ^[L109]. A genre mix is stated per part: „Der Genre-Mix verschiebt sich stärker in Richtung Hard Sci-Fi“ ^[L245] and „Der Genre-Mix integriert stärker Elemente des Cosmic Horror“ ^[L431].
