## Stance, read per passage

The document is a design analysis, not a chapter and not a plan of the novel; its opening states its aim as „Die vorliegende Analyse untersucht die modernsten methodischen und technologischen Ansätze“ ^[L17]. Its voice changes at L152, and the passages read as follows.

**L11 to L150, a survey of methods.** The text reports architectures and mechanics of AI-driven interactive fiction and numbers its sources with digits glued to the sentence end; the headings carry the terms (`Drama Manager`, `Lorebook`, `Storylets`). It speaks in the descriptive present, for example „Ein Drama Manager (DM) ist ein allwissender Hintergrundagent“ ^[L35] and „Ein Lorebook fungiert als das externe Langzeitgedächtnis des KI-Systems“ ^[L89]. Two tables (L75 to L81, L141 to L148) set a mechanic beside its effect.

**L152 to L184, an application to the novel.** The heading is „Konzeptionierung von Spielmechaniken“ ^[L152] and the lead-in says what is proposed: „ein hochgradig strategisches, psychologisches System“ ^[L154]. Under „Was der Spieler aktiv tun kann“ ^[L158] four numbered mechanics follow, and each holds two repeated labels, `Die Mechanik:` and `Spielerinteraktion:`, which are template labels and not terms. The mechanics are proposals for a game; they are not reports of the novel's text. Where they name figures and places of the novel they cite reference 54 (L165, L172, L181, L183, L184), whose entry reads „Roman: Kohärenz Protokoll“ ^[L249], so those details are the cited source's, reported here.

**L186 to L192, the conclusion.** It restates the design and closes on the claim that writing and playing become „isomorph“ ^[L192].

**L194 to L265, the reference list.** Seventy numbered entries with links; three of them are entries without a web address that point to Drive documents (L208, L249, L264).

The text asks no questions about the novel and sets no lock.
