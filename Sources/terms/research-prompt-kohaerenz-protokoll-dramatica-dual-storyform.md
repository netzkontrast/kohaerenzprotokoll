It closes with „Schlusswort an die ausführende KI“ ^[L2109], and a checklist headed „Selbst-Verifikations-Checkliste für die ausführende KI (13 Punkte)“ ^[L2081].

Inside that lock the document calls its own tendencies of the quad values „Hypothesen, nicht Vorgaben“ ^[L533]. **Standing of the work corpus.** The prompt says of the corpus it points at: „Der Werkkorpus ist Eingangsmaterial, nicht Wahrheitsquelle.“ ^[L489]. A few cells of this table hold personal material about real people and are not harvested; the census lists only the novel's terms from it.

Methods are defined under eight headings in a fixed template: `Was sie ist`, `Warum sie hier ist`, `Anwendungs-Schritte`, `Stop-Kriterium` (L889 to L1084; counted below).

**Template and slots.** The output schema (L1637 to L2077) and the storyform format (L545 to L665) are templates whose slots are written in square brackets, for example „STORYFORM A (= K1, Coherence Kernel)“ ^[L549]. Everything inside them is a slot, not a statement about the work.
