## Stance, read per passage

The text is one report in numbered sections, and it speaks throughout in the conditional of proposal; it reports mathematics and then proposes how that mathematics could serve a narrative. Its own title names the purpose: „Kohärenz, Struktur und Metapher für Aegis, Kael und Juna“ ^[L11].

- Introduction (L13 to L19): states the aim as a report that reads the Monstergruppe as a source of ideas for a narrative world, naming Aegis, Kael and Juna, and announces the four Kernwelten „(Logik, Emotion/Paralogik, Angst/Sicherheit, Potentialität)“ ^[L19]. Aegis is given a defining sentence in the report's own voice, „Aegis ist, was aegis verhindert, das es nicht ist“ ^[L17].
- Mathematical exposition (L23 to L65, L96 to L113, L126 to L134): description of group theory in the indicative, with a source number glued to many sentences (the reference list at L251 to L317 is numbered).
- Narrative proposals (L37 to L43, L57 to L61, L117 to L124, L138 to L145): each exposition is followed by a list of what it could mean for Aegis, Kael or the Potentialitätsraum, in the conditional with „könnte“ ^[L41] and „könnten“ ^[L86].
- The four worlds (L147 to L209): a section per world with a **Fokus** line and a **Manifestation der Monster-Eigenschaften** line, then a summary table (L200 to L209); the section opens by calling the worlds a method: „kann als Methode interpretiert werden, spezifische mathematische Facetten der Monstergruppe zu isolieren und zu verstärken“ ^[L149].
- Synthesis (L211 to L239): questions put to the reader, „Sucht Aegis danach, die Eigenschaften des Monsters“ ^[L217], followed by plot approaches headed „Konkrete Handlungsansätze und Thematische Erkundungen“ ^[L221] and introduced with „Die mathematischen Eigenschaften des Monsters bieten mehr als nur Metaphern“ ^[L223].
- Conclusion (L241 to L249) and a numbered reference list of web pages and papers (L251 to L317), each with an access date.
