## Stance, read per passage

The document is a research report in German that argues for a construction of the novel's ending; it names its own function in its title: „Ein ontologischer und dramaturgischer Synthesebericht zur Finalisierung des narrativen Bauplans“ ^[L13]. It is organised in five numbered parts (I to V, with sub-sections 1.1 to 5.3) and three tables, each introduced by the English line `The following table` ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-finale-pfeiler.md:#3], and it ends with a list headed „Referenzen“ ^[L292].

- **Plan / prescription (L22 to L62).** The foundation is something the document says must be conceived in a certain way: „muss das Fundament nicht als eine finale Antwort, sondern als eine tiefere, komplexere Frage konzipiert werden“ ^[L22]. The modal voice is that of a design brief.
- **Option space (L98 to L128, L265 to L268).** Two endings for AEGIS are laid out as „Pfad A“ ^[L122] and `Pfad B`, and two endings for Kael as „Szenario A“ ^[L267] and `Szenario B`. The choice is not made; of the second path the text says its transformation „bliebe für den Leser zutiefst ambivalent“ ^[L112].
- **Conditional and open (L102, L227).** Narrative manifestations are proposed with „könnte“: „Narrativ könnte dies als eine unaufhaltsame Kaskade“ ^[L102]; and the nature of Juna/V is explicitly left open: „Die Erzählung kann die genaue Natur von Juna/V bewusst in der Schwebe lassen“ ^[L227].
- **Declarative about the characters (L144 to L152, L183 to L187).** The sections on Kael's psyche and the character table state classifications as fact of the story: „Kaels Psyche wird rigoros nach dem Modell“ ^[L144]; the table gives Selene as „ISH/Torwächter: Vermittlerin, ethischer Kompass“ ^[L187].
- **Declaration about the title (L276).** The report states what the title will mean: „Das wahre Kohärenz Protokoll ist Kaels eigene Reise“ ^[L276]. This is the document's own proposal for the novel; it is recorded here and not applied.
- **Sources (L294 to L312).** Reference 1 is „Kohärenz Protokoll: Forschungsaufgabe“ ^[L294]; the others are web pages with „Zugriff am Juli“ dates ^[L295], and the glued numbers in the body point into this list. The body therefore reports other texts in many places; a name inside a reported theory (Whitehead, Deleuze, Bachtin) belongs to that theory, not to the novel.
