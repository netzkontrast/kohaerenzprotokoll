**Teil I to Teil IV (L38 to L248).** One part per throughline: Objective Story (AEGIS), Main Character (Kael), Impact Character (Juna/V), Subjective Story (Kael and Juna/V). Each part reasons from borrowed theories (autopoiesis, paraconsistent logic, structural dissociation, integrated information theory, rhizome, strange attractor, gnosis) and applies them to the novel's entities. One speculative passage is flagged as a proposal by its own verb: „wird eine hybride kognitive Architektur vorgeschlagen“ ^[L66]. The AEGIS part presents two alternative endings, „Pfad A“ and „Pfad B“ ^[L90], the second as „Transformation“ ^[L92].
