## This is the first `theorie-psychologie` document, and the first `T2-theory`

Every document read so far has been `T3-work`. The difference is visible, not
subtle, and it is structural rather than stylistic: **27 numbered external
references, all real trauma-psychology sources** — `did-research.org`, Van der
Hart/Nijenhuis/Steele's Theory of Structural Dissociation, Psychology Today,
ResearchGate, a Reddit thread on ANP/EP terminology. None of them describe this
project; they are the discipline this document imports and then applies.

**Teil 2 (§2.1–2.2, L173–L222, 50 lines) is a clean discipline import with
essentially zero project content.** `ANP`, `EP`, `Aktionssysteme`, `Phobien`,
the Primäre/Sekundäre/Tertiäre SD spectrum, `Ko-Bewusstheit`, `Switching`,
`Depersonalisation`, `Derealisation`, `Homöostase`, Internal Family Systems —
every one of these is textbook trauma theory, cited to a real source, meant the
way the discipline means it, with System Kael named only once in passing
^[L196] as an instance of the category being explained. Teil 1 and Teil 3 are
where the same vocabulary is *applied* — now naming this project's own eleven
Anteile, four Kernwelten, AEGIS and Juna/V. The boundary between the two
registers is a heading, not a sentence, exactly as it was in the worldbuilding
document's craft-theory/gazetteer split — but here the imported half explains
itself completely on its own terms before any application starts, rather than
interleaving citation and invention row by row.

**Whether an imported term has become a project term is exactly the judgement
the task briefing named, and this document supplies test cases rather than an
answer.** `Aktionssysteme` (Panksepp-derived action-systems theory) is
introduced generically in Teil 2 and then used to classify each of the eleven
Anteile individually in Teil 3 — imported vocabulary doing project-specific
work. `Homöostase` ^[L221, L530] is used exactly the way `Negentropie` was used
information-theoretically in one prior document: a systems-theory term
describing System Kael's dysfunctional equilibrium, never redefined for this
project, imported and applied but not re-coined. Neither becomes a
project-specific *name* the way `AEGIS` or `Kernwelten` are; both remain
recognisably the discipline's own words, doing descriptive work here. This
document does not itself decide which imported terms will need a project
reading and which will not — that stays reconciliation's and the author's call.

## Stance — declarative sentences, not questions, carry the hedging

Seven question marks, all of them inside Teil 4's plot-example dialogue
(`"Bildest du dir das nur ein?"` ^[L106]) — none inside a candidate term or a
theoretical claim. **That makes this document look confident by the measure
that caught document 5's hedging.** It is not: `könnte` occurs 99 times,
`könnten` 13, `wahrscheinlich` 43, `möglicherweise` 29, `vielleicht` 26,
`dürfte` 2 — **220 modal-hedge words in 12,762, one every 58** — denser than the
worldbuilding document's one-in-114, and it never surfaces as a question mark.
Teil 1 and Teil 3 are near-continuously conditional — Kael „mag sich nach
Klarheit und innerem Frieden sehnen" ^[L150] even as he fears the process; Nyx
„könnte fürchten, seine Identität als" der Starke — the phrase the document
itself sets off in quotes — „zu verlieren" ^[L161]. Teil 2, the imported-theory
half, is not conditional in the same way — it states TSDP's claims as TSDP's
claims. **So the hedging marks exactly the boundary between import and
application**: the discipline is asserted, its application to these eleven
specific characters is proposed.

**Two Anteile get a doubled classification the document does not resolve.**
Argus: „Entstehender ANP/EP-Mix **oder** Metakognitiver Anteil" ^[L501]. Selene:
„Modifizierter ANP mit EP-Komponenten **oder** Repräsentation des
Integrationspotenzials" ^[L274]. Both are live ambiguities inside the
document's own theory-application, stated as an „oder" rather than a question
mark — a second hedging device this document uses beside the modal verbs.

## What the extraction ran into

**A document that spends half its length explaining a discipline before naming
a single character.** Reading order matters here in a way it has not before:
Teil 2's uncontaminated theory statement is the baseline against which Teil 1
and Teil 3's application can be checked, and reading Teil 3 before Teil 2 would
make it hard to tell which classifications are the discipline's own claims and
which are this document's specific proposal about one of eleven fictional
people.

**Quotation marks mark a third convention here, beside invention (document 1)
and citation (document 2): a hypothetical line of dialogue.** „Bildest du dir
das nur ein?", „Du überreagierst" ^[L106] are quoted as things AEGIS might say
to Kael — neither a coined project term nor an external citation, but a
speculative script example. A citation checker built only on the first two
conventions would misread this one.

**`B` as a one-character Kernwelt name is the shortest formal symbol found so
far**, shorter even than the Greek letters `⊕`, `θ`, `α`, `Σ`, `Δ`, `Π` document
2's math flagged — those at least survive `grep -w`. `B` does not, which is why
its `incl. compounds` count is reported here as a substring count rather than a
real number.
