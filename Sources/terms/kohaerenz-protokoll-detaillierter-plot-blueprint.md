## Stance, read per passage

The document is a plan, not a report: it lays out a book in three parts and says what each part will do. Its own labels are `Konzept`, `Handlungsbogen`, `Dokument-Integration` and `Annotation \[Pacing\]` / `Annotation \[Meta-Twist\]`, each repeated under every part. Every sentence is in the present tense and describes what Kael experiences in the story.

- **Overview (L13 to L17).** The heading „Struktur-Übersicht“ ^[L13] opens a list of three parts with chapter ranges and a theme each, for example „Thema: Hard Sci-Fi Horror“ ^[L15].
- **Konzept lines (L21, L38, L63).** Each gives the part's idea in one sentence, e.g. „Der Durchbruch zur Externen Ebene“ ^[L63].
- **Handlungsbogen (L23 to L25, L40 to L42, L65 to L67).** A plot summary in the present tense, e.g. „Kael verlässt die Simulationsebene“ ^[L42].
- **Dokument-Integration (L27 to L32, L44 to L57, L69 to L74).** Each bullet names another research document in quotation marks and says how the book will use it, e.g. „Der Prolog nutzt die dort beschriebene“ ^[L29]. The quoted document titles are the document's references to other texts; the plot is this document's proposal.
- **Annotations (L34, L59, L76).** One line each on pacing or the twist, e.g. „Langsam, fast meditativ“ ^[L34] and „Hier greift das Foreshadowing“ ^[L76].
- **Closing questions (L78 to L82).** Three questions with answers under „Zusammenführung der Kernfragen“ ^[L78]; the document asks them and answers them itself. The question marks (3) are these three.
