## Stance, read per passage

The document is an analytic essay that names its own structure as six stages and walks through them in order. It speaks mostly in the register of a report with evaluation, and in one passage in the voice of the system it describes.

Observed: the body has one markdown heading (the title, ^[L11] „Tiefenanalyse von AEGIS als Ordnungsprinzip“); the sections are bold-only lines (20 in the profile) with roman numerals, and the stage labels stand inside them: „Synthese Stufe 1“ ^[L25], „Elaboration Stufe 2“ ^[L44], „Integration Stufe 4“ ^[L215], „Ausführung Stufe 5“ ^[L288], „Ausführung Stufe 6“ ^[L310]. Stage 3 and stage 4 are announced in the introduction ^[L23] and the critical questions of stage 3 are answered later in section V ^[L257]; no section carries a stage 3 label.

- Comparative analysis (III, L44 to L142). Headed „Detaillierte Analysen (Beispiele)“ ^[L48], then „Vergleichende Diskussion“ ^[L114], then a table whose last row is marked as provisional (`AEGIS (vorläufig)`, ^[L136]) and a „Vorläufige Einordnung von AEGIS“ ^[L138].

Subsection A lists the core philosophy, subsection B the protocols, with a statement that they come „basierend auf dem fiktiven“ ^[L162] protocol document; subsection C proposes a formal frame in the conditional: „Ein möglicher Ansatz wäre eine hybride Formalisierung“ ^[L203].

The grammar is the only label; the manifesto is addressed to no one. §8 labels it itself: „Das Manifest ist deskriptiv, nicht präskriptiv“ ^[L306]. The concluding paragraph ^[L379] opens „Weitere Forschung könnte sich“ and is a plan.
- Closing synthesis (VIII, L364 to L379). Metaphorical readings of AEGIS beyond the novel, in the conditional.
