## What the extraction ran into

**A research report that is a whole-novel plan.** „Das Kohärenz Protokoll: Architektonik eines
multidimensionalen Romans" ^[L11]: premise, the world's physics in four set-pieces, three tables, a
pacing section, „Das Plot-Outline (Kapitel 1 – 39)" ^[L88] — one bold-titled paragraph per chapter —
and a checklist of thirteen „Fixpunkte" ^[L208] with where each is embedded. No canon label, no date
in the body; the manifest dates it 2026-04-08. It ends in a numbered reference list (L229–266),
whose titles stay off the list.

**Chapters written in full, never abbreviated.** `Kapitel` stands on 54 lines, `Kap` once, in the
checklist's „Klimax Kap. 35-36" ^[L219]. Kapitel 1–39 each have a heading; fourteen of them — 4, 6, 7, 8,
12, 13, 16, 20, 27, 31, 33, 36, 37 and 38 — are a bold line with the paragraph below, the rest a bold title glued to
the paragraph. `40`, `Vortex` 0.

**Glued reference numbers.** 92 of them — „entstand.4" ^[L64], „Ozon.7" ^[L25] — and „() .5" where the
export dropped a formula: the Landauer relation at L25 and the Bekenstein bound at L174 stand as
empty parentheses.

**Heat, cold and warmth each have a place.** Akt I „kalt/steril" ^[L223], Akt II „heiß/fragmentiert" ^[L223];
heat and ozone are AEGIS' erasure from Kapitel 1 (L94) to the „Landauer-Hitze im Fundament" ^[L188];
KW4 has „warmes Licht" ^[L172]; in Kapitel 37 „Die glühende Hitze kühlt ab zu einer sanften,
lebenspendenden Wärme." ^[L200]

**Knuckles at both ends.** „blutig aufgerissenen Knöchel" ^[L94] in Kapitel 1 and „Die Narben auf
seinen Knöcheln sind real, alt und verheilt." ^[L206] in Kapitel 39.

**A Guardian for each world.** The Kernwelten table has a column „Zugeordneter Guardian" ^[L39]:
LogOS, Mnemosyne, Cerberus, „Kairos / Sophia" ^[L43]. `Guardian` stands on 3 lines, `Guardians` on 1.

**Names it does not write.** No `Konstrukt-Stadt`, `734`, `Komponente`, `Trennungsprotokoll`,
`Flight`, `Truth-Rotation`, `Wir`, `Telefon`, `Dramatica`, `Storyform`, `Überwelt` or `Nexus`; the
kernels are `K1` and `K0` with plain digits (`05-verify.txt`).

**Two zeros, both compounds.** `Deus-ex-Machina` stands only in „Deus-ex-Machina-Magie" ^[L31] and
`Fixpunkt` only in „Fixpunkte" ^[L210] and „Fixpunkt-Anforderung" ^[L214]; both stay on the list.

Lovecraft, *Use of Weapons*, *Gone Girl*, *Permutation City*, Maureen Murdock and Campbell are models
