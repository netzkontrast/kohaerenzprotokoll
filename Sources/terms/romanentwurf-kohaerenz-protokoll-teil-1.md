## Stance, read per passage

Three parts, three registers. **I. Rechercheergebnisse** (L13 to L65) is a research report on narrative techniques: each of six topics has a `Kernkonzept` (a general description) and `Narrative Techniken` (advice), then an „Implikationen für Konstrukt-Stadt & Michael“ paragraph that applies the advice to the novel ^[L21]. The advice is phrased as what should be done and as what is probable, e.g. „wahrscheinlich ein Schutzmechanismus“ ^[L39]; it is a plan and a hypothesis, not a report of settled world facts. The document's own table sums the six topics up ^[L53].

**II. Szenen-Struktur** (L67 to L219) is headed „Detaillierte Szenen-Struktur für Kapitel 1 (Blaupause)“ ^[L67], a blueprint of 15 scenes under repeated field labels. Its hedges are question marks set after a guess, e.g. „Möglicher Mnemosyne/Cerberus-Flicker?“ ^[L97], and the named Alters stand there only as a suggestion for which inner voice a scene could carry.

**III. Ausformulierter Kapitelentwurf** (L221 to L329) is prose, headed „Ausformulierter Kapitelentwurf“ ^[L221] and introduced as beginning „Beginnt exakt nach dem letzten Satz“ ^[L223] of an introductory text the file does not hold. Each scene is headed by its blueprint title in parentheses (`(Szene 1: Erwachen zur Ordnung)`, `(Szene 2: …`); the narration is close third person on Michael with dialogue in straight quotes, and questions inside the narration (e.g. „Zu makellos?“ ^[L229]) are his. **Referenzen** (L331 to L375) is a numbered list of 43 cited web pages, each closed by the same access-date phrase (`Zugriff am April 17, 2025` ^[romanentwurf-kohaerenz-protokoll-teil-1.md:#43]).
