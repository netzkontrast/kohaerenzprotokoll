## Stance, read per passage

The document speaks in the plan voice throughout. It is a handbook for working out plot points and it reports no scene, locks no fact and states no event of the novel.

**L13 to L17, the frame.** Three labelled paragraphs: „Zweckbestimmung“ ^[L15], „Kernzielsetzung“ ^[L16] and „Methodischer Überblick“ ^[L17]. The document calls itself a „Gebrauchsanweisung“ ^[L16]: a plan and a statement of purpose.

**L19 to L21, the opening of section II.** The document says it offers „kuratierte Auswahl narrativer Techniken“ ^[L21]. This is a plan voice about craft, not about the world.

**L22 to L266, the twelve technique blocks A to L.** Each block carries the same three labels. `Grundprinzipien` opens with general statements about a craft technique (a report of craft knowledge, not of the novel). „Strategische Anwendung in“ ^[L27] opens the proposals for the novel, written as what should or can be done (`sollte`, `können`); the world is named only here, with bracketed „Kontext“ numbers (47 whole-word occurrences, `Kontext` ^[plot-blueprint-methodik-kohaerenz-protokoll.md:#47]) that point to material not in this file. `Vertiefte Betrachtung` closes each block with an argument that applies the technique to the novel; its verbs are of recommendation, for example the handbook should guide the writer.

**L268 to L284, the matrix.** A table of twelve techniques against six columns; its own marker of weight is `Primär` (`Primär` ^[plot-blueprint-methodik-kohaerenz-protokoll.md:#16]). The cells are claims of the plan about fit, not of the novel.

**L286 to L393, the template.** Section III gives seven fields (a) to (g). Each field has an `Original:` line ^[L293] and „Angereicherte Leitfragen“ ^[L294], a set of questions. The questions are prompts to the writer, and a term that stands only inside such a question has no reading; `PMAS` and `CCPP` are of this kind (both only at L345).

**L395 to L399, section IV.** Three labelled paragraphs, „Anwendungshinweise“ ^[L397], an iterative-process paragraph and „Zielbekräftigung“ ^[L399], in the plan voice. The document says the prompts are for deeper thought „nicht zu rigider Befolgung“ ^[L398].

**L401 to L403, a numbered reference list** (headed „Quellenangaben“ ^[L401]), one very long line of web sources; it is the document's own list of what it drew on and is not read here.

No passage is a lock or a sample text in a voice.
