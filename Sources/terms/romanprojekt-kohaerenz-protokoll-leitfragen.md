## Stance, read per passage

**Ten Leitfragen (L62 to L104).** Each of ten headings carries the same four fields, which are template labels and not terms of the world: „Kontext“ ^[L68], „Wertvollste Quellen“ ^[L68], „Szenen-Logik & Somatisches Erleben“ ^[L68] and „Ziel für nächste Session“ ^[L68]. The first field is a question (it opens „Wie etablieren wir“ ^[L68]), the third is a scene proposal in present tense, the last is a task for a later session. The `Wertvollste Quellen` field holds only a footnote number glued to a colon (`Quellen:**.1`), the source names themselves are lost to the export.

**Check, with proposals (L106 to L130).** „Kohärenz-Check“ ^[L106] lists three problems, each followed by a „Korrektur-Vorschlag“ ^[L114], which is a proposal to the author and not a decision. Sentences there that open with `Die Quelle` or `Die Quellen` (L110, L118) report other texts.

**References (L138 to L146).** A list of seven numbered titles; the seventh is not a title but a long pasted passage that runs from L146 to L154.
