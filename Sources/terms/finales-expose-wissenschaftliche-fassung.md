## Stance, read per passage

Section I, a summary of the novel in four paragraphs (L15 to L21), reports the plot as a plan of the whole: „vollzieht sich in drei Akten von der Desorientierung zur Souveränität“ ^[L17]. Section II (L23 to L32) sets out two opposed positions and a resolution as three list items, each headed with its own label (L27, L29, L31).
