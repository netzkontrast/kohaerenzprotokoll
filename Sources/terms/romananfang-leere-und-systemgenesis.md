## Stance, read per passage

Observed: the document has two parts under its own headings, „Rechercheergebnisse und Strukturanalyse“ ^[L13] (L13 to L150) and „Entwurf: Einleitungskapitel“ ^[L152] (L152 to L220). Part I is analysis and proposal; part II is a chapter draft with a title line, „Genesis im Echo der Leere“ ^[L156].

Part I, sections A and C (L17 to L75): research report on how to write emptiness and an algorithmic perspective. It names the narrative parts by number (Teil 1 to Teil 4) and speaks of what the narrator "can" do, so it is a plan for the draft. The research on philosophy, physics and literature is reported as concepts of others; for example „Śūnyatā ist somit nicht reines Nichts“ ^[L23] and „repräsentiert ein Minimum an Energie und Aktivität“ ^[L29] report a concept and a physical theory.

Part I, section B (L41 to L51): a proposal. It labels its sample „Textvorschlag (ca. 200 Worte)“ ^[L45] and then gives the guided thought journey in italics; the same words return in the chapter draft (L164). This is sample text in a voice that addresses the reader.

Part I, section D (L77 to L119): a derivation of AEGIS's reaction, with a table (L99 to L115) headed „AEGIS Protokoll-Mapping zu Narrativen Aktionen“ ^[L99], whose second column the document labels as examples. The table states what each protocol term would do in the narration, mostly in hedged form (`möglicherweise`, `könnte`).

Part I, section E (L121 to L150): evaluation of the four-part structure, with options A and B, and a recommendation: „Es wird empfohlen, an der ursprünglichen 4-Teil-Struktur festzuhalten“ ^[L137].

Part II (L152 to L220): the draft, announced by a note in brackets: „Der folgende Text ist ein Entwurf und folgt der empfohlenen 4-Teil-Struktur“ ^[L154]. It changes voice twice without a label: second-person address to the reader (L158 to L166), then a bold English-capitals protocol log (L168 to L200) opening „ALERT: INTEGRITY VIOLATION DETECTED“ ^[L168], then narration in German (L202 to L208), a plain-capitals closing log (L212 to L218) opening „UNIVERSAL REBOOT COMPLETE“ ^[L212], and a last paragraph (L220). The protocol log speaks as the system (AEGIS's own status report); the document names no speaker.
