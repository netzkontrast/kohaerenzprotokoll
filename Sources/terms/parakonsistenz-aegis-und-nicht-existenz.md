## Stance, read per passage

The document calls itself a report and its subject hypothetical. Passage by passage:

The introduction (L13 to L55) is a plan. It announces its aim — „Das Hauptziel dieses Berichts ist eine eingehende Analyse des Aegis-Systems“ ^[L45] — and a numbered sequence of steps, and closes with „Der Bericht ist entsprechend strukturiert“ ^[L55]. Its problem statement (L29 to L41) is a framing: „Dieser Bericht widmet sich der Analyse eines hypothetischen Systems namens“ ^[L31], whose definition is set in bold as its own line, „aegis ist, was aegis verbindet, das es nicht ist“ ^[L33]. The definition has commas and so cannot stand on the candidate list; it is quoted here instead. Its next sentence reads the definition as a function: it places Aegis at a boundary, „Dies verortet Aegis an einer fundamentalen ontologischen Grenze“ ^[L39].

The foundations and variants (L57 to L212) are a survey of a logical framework, much of it reporting other literature (the passages carry glued reference digits and end in a table, L201 to L210). The table compares four approaches; the passage after it says the choice of system matters, „was die Wahl des Systems für die Analyse von Aegis zu einer nicht-trivialen Entscheidung macht“ ^[L212]. This part is exposition, not a claim about Aegis, except where it turns to Aegis.

The analysis of the definition (L214 to L244) is argument: it formalises the definition in two attempts (L228, L229), names its self-reference and asserts „Der Widerspruch ist Teil der Definition und damit des Wesens von Aegis selbst“ ^[L234].

The modelling section (L246 to L304) is a proposal in three variants, each introduced as an approach (L254, L273, L290). Its voice is conditional (`könnte`, `wäre`): „Aegis könnte als eine Entität existieren, deren definierender Widerspruch eine genuine Dialetheia ist.“ ^[L268]

The guardian section (L306 to L331) is interpretation, stated with more confidence: „Aegis ist also die Verbindung und somit die Grenze selbst“ ^[L314] and „ist die Funktion als Wächter keine externe Zuschreibung“ ^[L322].

The synthesis (L333 to L356) is a summary and then an open question: „Offene Fragen bleiben bestehen“ ^[L356], and it calls the system „imaginierten“ ^[L354], the report itself closing with „Aegis, obwohl hypothetisch, erweist sich somit als fruchtbarer Katalysator“ ^[L356].

The last block, headed „Referenzen“ ^[L358], is a list of sixty-three web and paper references, each ending in an access date: `Zugriff am` ^[parakonsistenz-aegis-und-nicht-existenz.md:#63]. It is the document's report of its sources, not its own claim.
