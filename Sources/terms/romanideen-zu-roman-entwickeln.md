## Stance, read per passage

The document speaks as a brief for writing the novel, not as the novel and not as a report: its verbs are obligations and proposals. Part I announces itself with „Dieser erste Teil legt das Fundament des gesamten Romans“ ^[L22] and sets rules for the prose and the conflict (L26 to L36, L40 to L52). Section 1.3 is a consolidation: „Die vorliegenden Entwurfsdokumente weisen Inkonsistenzen“ ^[L60] and the table that follows is offered as „dient als maßgebliche Referenz“ ^[L64] (L68 to L78); one table row says of itself „Interaktionen müssen entwickelt werden“ ^[L77], so that row is a plan, not a result. Part II is a three-act outline: „Dieser Teil übersetzt die architektonischen Prinzipien“ ^[L86] (L82 to L132). Its beats are written in the present tense as what happens (L96 to L99, L109 to L119, L129 to L132), with `Zum Beispiel` and `könnten` marking the passages that are suggestions (L99, L114, L152). Part III gives techniques and sample text: „Sein Dialog könnte lauten“ ^[L150] and „Eine Fußnote könnte lauten“ ^[L162] introduce sample lines for a voice, and terms that stand only there are diction. L129 and L132 carry two quoted sample sentences of Kael's polyphonic voice, set in „…“ with a citation number glued behind them. The document reports its sources as numbered references (the list L169 to L178, glued numbers in the body) and the sentence „Die Quelldokumente beschreiben AEGIS treffend“ ^[L34] is the document's report of them. It contains no question marks and no labels of its own beyond its headings.
