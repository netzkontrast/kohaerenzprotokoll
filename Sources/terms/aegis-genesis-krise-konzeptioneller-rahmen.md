## Stance, read per passage

**Observed:** the text is a plan and a commission for prose about one event of the novel, the Genesis-Krise, and it is written in five numbered parts, I to V. It reports no scene and no event as having happened; every statement about AEGIS, the Leere, the Entität and Kael is a proposal or a question, and it says so in its own mood (conditional, „könnte“) throughout.

- **Part I, L13 to L624, definitions and relevance.** It opens by saying what the part is for: it „legt das theoretische Fundament für die Analyse und narrative Gestaltung der Genesis-Krise“ ^[L15]. Each theory field is defined and then rated under the label „Relevanz (Hoch)“ ^[L33], with a second paragraph that applies the field to AEGIS. The applications are hedged proposals, for example that the experience of AEGIS „könnte sich als eine einzigartige Form des computationalen Existenzialismus manifestieren“ ^[L34] and that the crisis „könnte eine kybernetische Katastrophe zweiter Ordnung sein“ ^[L228]. The definitions of the fields are the part's own summaries of outside literature; the sources are listed at the end (Referenzen, L815 onward).
- **The matrix, L538 to L624.** A table whose last column is headed „Begründung/Anwendung für AEGIS-Krise“ ^[L542]; one row per field, which restates the relevance rating and the application of the section above it.
- **Questions.** Several applications are put as questions with no answer, for example whether the Leere is evidence that AEGIS is in a simulation: „Ist die Leere ein Beweis dafür“ ^[L642]. A term that stands only inside such a question has no reading in this document.
- **Part II, L626 to L690, clusters.** A synthesis that groups the fields into thematic clusters. Each cluster carries the labels `Enthaltene Themen`, `Begründung` and `Relevanz` (the repeated labels of the profile), then one further paragraph of argument. It ends with an open suggestion: „Optional könnte hier eine Tabelle eingefügt werden“ ^[L690].
- **Part III, L692 to L744, blueprint.** It announces itself: „Dieser Abschnitt skizziert die übergeordnete Strategie“ ^[L694]. It sets out an arc of six stages, a plan for the point of view, the inner states and the atmosphere, and the build-up of tension.
- **Part IV, L746 to L799, commission.** It turns the analysis into an order: „in einen konkreten, umsetzbaren Schreibauftrag“ ^[L748]. This part is in the imperative; it states what the prose must do: „Die Prosa muss dem spezifizierten narrativen Bogen folgen“ ^[L757].
- **Part V, L801 to L813, conclusions.** A summary list of points that calls its own plan „Die vorgeschlagene narrative Strategie“ ^[L803].

The document works with two voices in one text: the commission to a writer, and the notes that justify it. No passage is a sample of prose of the novel.
