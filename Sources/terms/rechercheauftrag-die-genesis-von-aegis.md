## Stance, read per passage

The document is an order for research, written in the imperative of a plan and in questions. It reports nothing and locks nothing; it asks, and it states what the research is to deliver.

Paragraph L13 to L15 states the aim: „Ziel ist die Erstellung einer fundierten Recherchegrundlage“ ^[L15] for a short story. The same line sets the story's premise as a plan: the process is to run „ohne externen Eingriff“ ^[L15].

L17 to L45 (area 1, the Potentialmeer) and L47 to L75 (area 2, the genesis of AEGIS) and L77 to L100 (area 3) each carry the labels `Ziel` and `Recherchefragen & Konzepte` ^[rechercheauftrag-die-genesis-von-aegis.md:#3]. Under them nearly every item is a question: „Wie kann ein Zustand reiner Potentialität“ ^[L24] be described, „Wie formt sich AEGIS aus diesen Resten“ ^[L64]. Items of the form „Gibt es einen Widerstand gegen die Aktualisierung“ ^[L41] ask whether something exists; they are not statements about the world.

One passage sets the research focus on the `negative Selbstdefinition`, which it calls „als entscheidende Innovation gegenüber den Vorgängern“ ^[L70]; the sentence is a research instruction and quotes its own formula in ASCII quotes.

L102 to L110 (area 5) lists requirements for the story in imperative and "soll" sentences, for example the role of the `Reste` is called central ^[L107].

L112 to L114 gives the expected result of the research as one sentence.
