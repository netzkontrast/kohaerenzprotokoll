## Stance, read per passage

The document is a plot concept in two movements: a premise block (L13 to L25) and a chapter-theme outline. It marks the second as a draft in its own heading, „Kapitel-Themen-Entwurf“ ^[L27], and gives the whole as „Angereichertes Plotkonzept“ ^[L11].

The premise block is written as a statement of the story, in the present tense, under labels set in bold: „Prämisse“ ^[L13], „Konflikt“ ^[L19], „Setting“ ^[L21], „Genre-Mix“ ^[L23] and „Kernthemen“ ^[L25]. It calls the story's own analysis attempt „fundamental fehlerhafter, reduktionistischer Analyseversuch“ ^[L15], and describes the worlds as „fehlerhafte“ ^[L17] caches in the sense of a cache coherence protocol.

The outline is three numbered lists under three part headings: „Fragmentierung und erste Echos“ ^[L29], „Netzwerke, Schatten und wachsende Risse“ ^[L42] and „Emergenz, Konfrontation und Integration“ ^[L60]. Each entry names a theme in bold, often with a bare number in brackets after it, and then gives a one-sentence plan of what happens in the chapter.

Several entries are posed as open questions, not as settled events: „ist er Kael? M? Beides?“ ^[L74], „Holismus triumphiert?“ ^[L75], and „Kollaps, Transformation oder Rückzug?“ ^[L73]. One entry hedges its own plan: „AEGIS erkennt (oder auch nicht)“ ^[L56].
