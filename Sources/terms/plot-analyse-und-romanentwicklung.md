## Stance, read per passage

- L13 to L19, the greeting and the task. It announces an analysis of gaps and weak points and says it will give „konkrete, umsetzbare Ratschläge“ ^[L17]. It reports having studied uploaded documents, among them „Genesis der Existenz“ ^[L15]; those are that text's reports of other documents, not their wording.
- L21 to L33, the thermodynamic foundation. The passage restates a definition of AEGIS and then, under the label „Narrative Umsetzungsempfehlung:“ ^[L31], proposes how the cracks should be written.
- L35 to L53, pacing in the first act. It names a „signifikantes Pacing-Problem“ ^[L41] and offers „Konkrete Handlungsempfehlungen zur Plot-Optimierung:“ ^[L45] as a numbered list of three.
- L55 to L75, the model of parts and the table of four worlds (L67 to L73). The table is a proposal in the assistant's voice; under „Inkonsistenz-Warnung:“ ^[L75] it advises how two figures could reinforce each other.
- L77 to L97, the Heroine's Journey in seven numbered phases (L83 to L95), mapped onto the plot. L93 flags an inconsistency in the earlier planning.
- L99 to L135, mathematical metaphors, with „Narrative Integration:“ ^[L117] and „Handlungsanweisung für das Finale:“ ^[L133] as labels.
- L137 to L150, four numbered suggestions for the next chapters.
