## Stance, read per passage

The document is an essay in report voice about narrative systems in general; it is not about the novel's world, and it names no figure, place or object of it. It opens with a definition, „Narrative Kohärenz bezeichnet den Zusammenhalt“ ^[L17], and ends under the heading „Fazit“ ^[L712] (`Fazit` ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-als-algorithmische-grundlage-fuer-narrat.md:#1]). The passages between are descriptions of what authors and published systems do: causes of incoherence (L59 to L224), authors' knowledge models (L226 to L404) and algorithmic approaches (L406 to L710).

Where it states a purpose for the protocol it speaks as a goal: „Das Ziel eines Kohärenz-Protokolls ist es folglich“ ^[L222]. It describes the protocol as a concept, not as a defined object of its own: „ein Bündel von Regeln, Modellen und Verfahren“ ^[L716].

Systems and theories are reported as other sources'. The three components of one framework are numbered in the prose („Erstens kommt dynamisches Zustandstracking zum Einsatz“ ^[L430]), and the closing section sums up its sources as pointer references: „Quellen: Die im Text angegebenen Ziffernverweise“ ^[L762] (`Quellen` ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-als-algorithmische-grundlage-fuer-narrat.md:#1]). The reference list repeats `Ebenda` ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-als-algorithmische-grundlage-fuer-narrat.md:#6] for a work already cited.
