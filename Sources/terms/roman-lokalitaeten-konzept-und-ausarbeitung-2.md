## Stance, read per passage

The document speaks as a concept and planning text of a research kind, in the conditional throughout; it does not report events of the novel. Observed: the modal verbs „könnte" and „könnten" carry most of Part 2, so a location is proposed, not stated, and the reading of a term from this document is a proposal.

The document says what they are for: „Sie dienen als Referenz und Werkzeugkasten" ^[L21]. They mix general craft statements with proposals for the six levels, and the document attributes its own wording to no outside speaker.

**Part 2 A, the six reality levels (L108–L210).** Table 1 and then six blocks that repeat the same field labels (counted in the profile), each with `Konzeptueller Kern`, `Visuelle Ästhetik`, `Sensorische Details` and so on. Observed: these labels are the template of the document, not terms of the world. The block for the Externe Ebene is the one that is openly undecided: „Ihre genaue Natur ist unbekannt" ^[L183], and Table 1 carries question marks in its cells (L124).

**Part 2 B, the key locations (L212–L272).** Seven numbered locations, each with `Zweck/Funktion`, `Design`, `Sensorik/Atmosphäre`, `Charakter/Plot`. The section is introduced as development of concepts: „Hier werden nun detaillierte Konzepte für" ^[L214]. All are phrased as possibilities.

**Part 3, narrative research (L274–L343).** A checklist (L282–L289), a list of six comparison works with their lessons (L305–L310) and a list of further research tasks (L312–L343), the last of which is an open to-do: „weitere Recherchen in spezifischen Bereichen empfehlenswert" ^[L314].

The conclusions (L363–L384) list five numbered findings and six recommendations in the imperative. L386 to the end is a numbered source list headed `Quellenangaben`.

**Sample text and quoted voices.** The document reports one imagined message of AEGIS, in quotation marks: „Chaos ist schlecht, diese Ordnung ist gut." ^[L106]. It is the document's own sentence about what AEGIS might communicate, not a line spoken in the novel.
