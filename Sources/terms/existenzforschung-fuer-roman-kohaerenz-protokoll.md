Stance: suggestion in the Konjunktiv, not a statement about the novel.

**Synthesis (L343 to L381).** Here the document states a definition of the protocol in the present tense: „kann daher als ein mehrschichtiges Regelwerk verstanden werden“ ^[L349], with four levels listed (L351 to L354).
