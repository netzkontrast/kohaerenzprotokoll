## Stance, read per passage

The document is an essay in three parts with a conclusion and a numbered reference list. It labels its own parts „Teil I: Die Natur der Wahrheit: Zwei grundlegende Paradigmen“ ^[L18], and later „Teil III: Synthese – Wahrheitsparadigmen als Deutungsrahmen für die Realität der Identität“ ^[L263]. It marks nothing as a plan, a lock or a question; it speaks as an analysis that reports theories and then applies them.

Report voice: Teil I presents two theories of truth and their objections, for example „Der Isolations-Einwand: Der schlagkräftigste Kritikpunkt besagt“ ^[L110]. The theories and the objections are reported from other texts, which the numbered footnote digits glued to the words point to.

Application voice: Teil III transfers the two theories to identity, in the document's words „Das Korrespondenzprinzip lässt sich auf das Identitätsverständnis übertragen“ ^[L275]. Its two case studies are labelled `Fallstudie` ^[wahrheitstheorien-und-identitaet-ein-konflikt.md:#2], and its advice is headed „Empfehlung: Ein hybrides Modell für eine hybride Welt“ ^[L356].

Closing voice: the conclusion states „Die Analyse hat gezeigt“ ^[L372]; this is the document's own claim about its result and is recorded, not applied.

The reference list from L376 on holds web addresses and titles; no term is taken from it.
