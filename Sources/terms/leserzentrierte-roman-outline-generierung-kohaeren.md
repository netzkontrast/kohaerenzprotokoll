## Stance, read per passage

The document is a plan, not a report: an outline of a prologue and 39 chapters, in which every chapter is written as a proposal in the conditional register. Its title names it „Leserzentriertes Roman-Outline“ ^[L11]. It cuts itself into acts with its own headings: „Innere Reise (Heroine's Journey Fokus)“ ^[L26] for Act 1, „Meta-Ebene & Zyklen (Meta-Exploration)“ ^[L191] for Act 2 and „Äußere Konfrontation & Rückkehr (Hero's Journey Fokus)“ ^[L354] for Act 3. Each chapter has two voices, a prose paragraph after the label `[Handlung]` and a block of eight labelled lines under it (Core Theme, Kael Internal, AEGIS Focus, Setting, Subplots, Philosophy, Genre/Trope, Reader Psychology Note), where the second voice is the outline commenting on what the chapter is for; the labels repeat in every block and are the template, not terms.

The prologue (L15 to L24) is written as a past-to-present account of an origin: „Ein nicht näher spezifiziertes katastrophales Ereignis“ ^[L15] stands in it, and the document marks one identification as implicit with „implizit Juna/V“ ^[L15]. The chapter paragraphs hedge with the words möglicherweise and vielleicht (40 each, `möglicherweise` ^[leserzentrierte-roman-outline-generierung-kohaeren.md:#40], `vielleicht` ^[leserzentrierte-roman-outline-generierung-kohaeren.md:#40]) and with question marks inside parentheses, so a named part, place or event in a chapter is usually offered, not fixed: for Nyx the paragraph writes „der noch latente Anteil Nyx“ ^[L128] followed by a question mark. In a few places the text turns to the plain statement, as where Chapter 20 says „Das Paradoxon wird klar benannt“ ^[L267], and at the end it says openly what it leaves open: „Das Ende kann bewusst einige Mysterien offenlassen“ ^[L511]. The file closes under the heading „Quellenangaben“ ^[L521] with a numbered list of 39 web pages on craft; those are references of the outline's method and no term of the novel's world.
