## Stance, read per passage

The document is one voice, the report's own („bedient sich dieser Bericht eines triangulierten Verfahrens“ ^[L27]), and it does not label passages with tags such as `[K]`; its labels are headings, a repeated `Implikation:` lead-in (`Implikation` ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-dramatica-synthese.md:#4]), `Mapping auf …` sub-headings (`Mapping auf` ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-dramatica-synthese.md:#8]) and bullet leads (`These`, `Antithese`, `Synthese`, `Problem`, `Effekt`, `Diagnose`, `Therapie`, `Protokoll`). Passages by stance:

Report on a project (L17 to L23). The opening says what the dossier is: „stellt das Ergebnis einer umfassenden, transdisziplinären Analyse“ ^[L17] and that the project „ist kein Romanprojekt im herkömmlichen Sinne“ ^[L19]. This section also speaks about matters outside the novel's world; the census records only that it does and neither lists nor quotes it.

Report on sources (L33 to L59). Section 2 gives the report's own reading of other, numbered documents, not those documents' words; it names three phases, headed „Die Diagnostische Starre“ ^[L49], „Die Strategische Öffnung“ ^[L53] and „Die Integrative Synthese“ ^[L57]. The section ends in an instruction to itself: „Aus diesem Vergleich ergibt sich eine klare Handlungsanweisung für den vorliegenden Bericht“ ^[L63].

Mapping, stated as necessary (L65 to L150). Section 3 puts four Dramatica throughlines onto four domains and dimensions and calls the result „folgende zwingende Zuordnung“ ^[L69]. Each of 3.1 to 3.4 has a heading of the form „Objective Story (OS) – Domäne: Universe / Dimension: Knowledge“ ^[L78], two `Mapping auf` sub-headings, an argument, and a closing `Implikation:` that says what the author must or must not do, for example „Die Welt muss Risse haben.“ ^[L94]. One sample of speech is given to Juna: „Du denkst, du kannst nicht, weil du denkst, du bist kaputt.“ ^[L128]; it is an invented line of dialogue offered in the report's argument, not a quotation of a source.

Synthesis in thesis form (L152 to L178). Section 4 is built from bullets whose leads are `These`, `Antithese`, `Synthese`, `Problem`, `Effekt`, `Begründung`, `Struktur`, `Funktion`; it states, as a plan, that „Kael transformiert sich im Finale in einen lebenden Gödel-Satz.“ ^[L169].

Instruction (L180 to L210). Section 5 turns to the author's working process („Der Autor darf nicht mehr versuchen, den perfekten Plot zu konstruieren“ ^[L196]) and fixes a four-row table, introduced by „Als finale Synthese wird der folgende operative Algorithmus“ ^[L200]. Its steps are imperatives, for instance „Wähle einen Slot basierend auf dem aktuellen mentalen Zustand (Resonanz).“ ^[L206].

Closure and appendix (L210 to L221). The report closes with a statement of its own standing: „Das System ist vollständig.“ ^[L210] and „Es gibt keine theoretischen Lücken mehr“ ^[L210]; earlier it calls the elements „ist nun funktional notwendig“ ^[L182]. This is the document's claim about itself and is recorded, not applied. An appendix table under „Tabellarische Übersicht der System-Isomorphie“ ^[L212] restates the mapping with a physical and a psychological column; the psychological column concerns matters outside the novel's world and is not recorded. The reference list (L225 to L230) names six documents by title, left out of the candidates.
