## Stance, read per passage

The document speaks throughout as an analytic proposal for an epilogue, not as a report of events in the novel. Each passage below is read with its lines.

It announces its own structure in „Der Bericht ist in drei Hauptteile gegliedert“ ^[L28]. This is a plan, in its own words.

**Part I, L32 to L88.** An argument about Kael's inner stability, with a summary table (L80 to L88, header cells flattened by the export, each cell wrapped in escaped asterisks). The passage describes what Kael does and is, in the present tense, as a proposed model. The phrase „Kael wählt die Revolte“ ^[L46] is stated as fact of the proposed ending; the document gives no sign that this is decided canon.

**Part II, L92 to L134.** The relation between Kael and the creator-observer written 'O'. It speaks in the modal: „Kael kann 'O' enttäuschen“ ^[L130] and „Kael kann 'O' herausfordern“ ^[L132]. These are options the document offers, not events.

**Part III, L138 to L180.** Scenes and options, explicitly offered as building blocks: „Die folgenden Szenarien dienen als Bausteine“ ^[L164] and „Es gibt mehrere strategische Optionen“ ^[L176]. The three scenes (L166 to L168) are numbered and titled in the list; the three options for the final image (L178 to L180) are lettered A to C. None is chosen.

**Closing, L184 to L190.** A report on itself: „Das hier entwickelte Framework beantwortet die drei Kernfragen“ ^[L188].

**Reference list, L192 to L229.** Numbered; most entries are web addresses with an access date, and a few are bare internal titles such as „Lebensphilosophie: Licht, Schatten, Synthese“ ^[L209] and „Heilung: Der Verlust von Superkräften“ ^[L212].
