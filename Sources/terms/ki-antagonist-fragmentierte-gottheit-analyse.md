## Stance, read per passage

The document speaks throughout as an analysis addressed to a team that builds the story, not as a narrative. Its four sections are headed `Sektion` ^[ki-antagonist-fragmentierte-gottheit-analyse.md:#5] and the two tables `Tabelle` ^[ki-antagonist-fragmentierte-gottheit-analyse.md:#2], each labelled by number in its own heading.

**Section I (L18 to L58), analysis of the origin.** It presents itself as reading the origin conflict as „die aktive, fortwährende traumatische Architektur“ ^[L22] and not as a past event. Its sub-sections state claims about AEGIS in the indicative: AEGIS is a system whose essence is the „aktive Verhinderung des Nicht-Seins“ ^[L54], and the Kohärenz Protokoll is „keine kühl kalkulierte Strategie, sondern eine tief verwurzelte Zwangshandlung“ ^[L58]. Numbers glued to the sentence ends in this section point to the numbered reference list (L216 onward), which names the novel-side sources by title.

**Section II (L62 to L120), model.** It announces itself as transfer: „überträgt die psychologischen Modelle der Strukturellen Dissoziation der Persönlichkeit“ ^[L66]. The model assigns roles: the Kohärenz Protokoll and operating system become „Anscheinend Normaler Persönlichkeitsanteil (ANP)“ ^[L74], and the Wächter become „werden als die Emotionalen Persönlichkeitsanteile (EPs) von AEGIS modelliert“ ^[L84]. Table 1 (L107 to L120) restates the mapping in three columns, headed `Tabelle` as „Tabelle: Psycho-systemische Kartierung der AEGIS-Architektur“ ^[L107].

**Section III (L124 to L153), phenomena.** Framed as a translation of the model into things the story can show: „konkrete technologische Phänomene, die innerhalb der Erzählung beschrieben und visualisiert werden können“ ^[L128]. The conditional register appears for the system flashback, which is „ein unfreiwilliger, partieller Systemzustands-Rollback“ ^[L149] described as conceptualised, not observed.

**Section IV (L157 to L214), recommendations.** The section addresses its audience directly: „liefert dem Kreativteam ein konkretes Instrumentarium“ ^[L161]. Its three revelation scenarios are offered as options: „bieten konkrete Möglichkeiten für diese Offenbarung“ ^[L182]. The ending is a proposal in the conditional: „Das Endspiel könnte Kaels Versuch sein“ ^[L196], and the last option opens „Vielleicht ist der einzige Weg, AEGIS zu heilen“ ^[L199]. Table 2 (L203 to L214) sets System Kael against System AEGIS in three columns.

**Reference list (L216 to L247).** Numbered entries; some are web pages with an access date, some are titles of other texts, for example „Genesis-Konflikt: Ich, AEGIS und Leere“ ^[L218] and „Juna/V: Exiliertes Ursprungs-Ich“ ^[L221].
