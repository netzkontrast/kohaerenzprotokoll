## Stance, read per passage

The document speaks as a research report in six stages and labels its own parts. The opening block names its fields „Zweckbestimmung“ ^[L15], „Methodologie“ ^[L17] and „Signifikanz“ ^[L18]; the method is announced as „einem sechsstufigen Prozess“ ^[L17], and Teil I to Teil VI carry the matching stage labels, `Synthese Stufe` ^[aegis-paradoxon-neukonzeption-und-analyse-docx.md:#6].

Teil I (L20 to L47) is a numbered list of borrowed concepts, each explained and then applied to AEGIS in the conditional (for example „AEGIS kann als tragischer Held interpretiert werden“ ^[L38]); it is a survey, not a decision. Teil II (L50 to L88) is a plan and a proposal: it groups the concepts under „Thematische Cluster“ ^[L54] and under „Vorgeschlagene Kernparadoxien“ ^[L59], each with the template fields „Formulierung“ ^[L61], „Bezug zu Clustern“ ^[L62] and „Narrative Eignung“ ^[L63], which name fields of a template and repeat under every paradox. Teil III (L90 to L104) analyses the five paradoxes again and bundles them into three areas. Teil IV (L106 to L126) is speculative: it sets out what AEGIS „könnte“ do under four sub-labels (a) to (d), the last of which is „Plot Driver“ ^[L114].

Teil V (L128 to L151) is the recommendation: „Empfehlung und Definition des Zentralen Paradoxons“ ^[L132], followed by „Ableitbarkeit anderer Paradoxien und Konflikte“ ^[L136]. Teil VI (L153 to L168) is a sample scenario that begins with „Ausgangslage“ ^[L158] and closes with „Zwangsläufigkeit“ ^[L167]. The Schlussfolgerung (L170 to L174) uses „Zusammenfassung“ ^[L172], „Wertbeitrag“ ^[L173] and „Abschließende Bemerkung“ ^[L174]. Lines L176 to L217 are a numbered reference list of web pages. The text names no speaker and gives no date inside the body; the grammar of the report is the only label.
