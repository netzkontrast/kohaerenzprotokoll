## Stance, read per passage

The document is a plan, not a report: it sets out inner stories (its word, `Binnenerzählungen`) for a novel of 39 chapters, in three parts. It labels itself as the title of an outline, „Binnenerzählungen für“ ^[L11], and gives each part a heading with a subtitle and a chapter range.

Part 1 is headed „Die Innere Reise zur Ganzheit“ ^[L13], its range is written „Heldinnenreise - Kapitel 1-13“ ^[L13], and a line below it states the part's focus, „Fokus: Trennung vom Weiblichen, Identifikation mit dem Männlichen, Prüfungen, Wiederverbindung, Integration“ ^[L15]. The steps are numbered 1 to 13 and nine of them carry the label `Stufe`; steps 10 to 12 are labelled `Erweiterung` (L26 to L28) and step 13 has neither label (L29).

Part 2 is headed „Zyklen der Realität und des Widerstands“ ^[L31], with „Zyklischer Erzählstil - Kapitel 14-26“ ^[L31]. A parenthesis under the heading says what the part is relative to an earlier version: „Die Binnenerzählungen hier bleiben konzeptionell gleich wie im vorigen Entwurf“ ^[L33]. The document therefore reports its own relation to an earlier draft; that draft is not in front of this reading. Its twelve steps are labelled `Zyklus 1`, `Zyklus 2` and `Zyklus 3`, or carry a plain title.

Part 3 is headed „Die Äußere Reise der Befreiung“ ^[L48], with „Heldenreise - Kapitel 27-39“ ^[L48], and carries the same kind of parenthesis: „Die Binnenerzählungen hier bleiben ebenfalls konzeptionell gleich“ ^[L50]. Its fourteen steps (L52 to L65) are titled with the stages of the borrowed journey scheme, for example „Der Tod des Alten Selbst (Verlust der Illusion)“ ^[L55] and „Rückkehr mit dem Elixier (Integrierte Identität)“ ^[L60].

Every step ends in two template fields, `Ort` and `Charakter` (39 each), which name the place and the figures of that step; they are the template, not terms. The steps use the hedge `vielleicht` in „Juna erscheint Kael vielleicht als Vision oder klares Gefühl“ ^[L23]: the document proposes, and nothing in it is marked as decided.
