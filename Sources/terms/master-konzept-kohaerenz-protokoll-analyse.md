## Stance, read per passage

It carries no labels such as plan, lock or question, and no sample text; its stance is declarative and evaluative, and it names no other speaker except the sources it cites by number.

**Report on its own origin (L15, L25, L56).** The document says it is the result of an analysis of twenty source documents: it „stellt das Ergebnis einer umfassenden forensischen Analyse“ ^[L15] and says of the sources that the analysis „erzwingt den Schluss“ ^[L25] that the author operates as a system.
