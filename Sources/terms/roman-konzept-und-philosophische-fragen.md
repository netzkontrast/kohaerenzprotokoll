## Stance, read per passage

The document is an essay about the novel in the analytic present tense, with no plan, lock or status label; it reports its own design reading and, in many sentences, a theory it borrows. Observed: the first three sections (L18 to L56) are headed as a dialectic, „Die These“ ^[L26], „Die Antithese“ ^[L36] and „Die Synthese“ ^[L46], and they claim the novel is built as an argument. The opening sentence calls the conflict one of „zwei fundamental entgegengesetzten Definitionen“ ^[L22] of coherence.

L60 to L102 describe AEGIS as a tragic figure that is designed (`konzipiert`, L64) and L106 to L140 describe Kael's development; the theories used there are reported as borrowed (autopoiesis, a logic of formal inconsistency, structural dissociation, Gödel), and a sentence such as „bekannte Fehlermodi in der KI-Sicherheitsforschung“ ^[L84] reports research outside the novel. The section on the rules of reality (L144 to L196) treats the Fundament, the Leere, the Juna link, Juna's role and the Kernwelten, and the table at L190 to L196 holds the Kernwelten, which the text calls `Blaupause`; its text reads „Die folgende Matrix dient als Blaupause“ ^[L188]. The summary table at L216 to L223 is called „thematischer Kompass“ ^[L214]. The last section is a list of four groups of questions (L233 to L265), introduced by „Die Erzählung ist darauf ausgelegt“ ^[L231]; they are questions of the document, with no answers beside them. L267 to L286 is a reference list.
