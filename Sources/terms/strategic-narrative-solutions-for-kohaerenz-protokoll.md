## Stance, read per passage

The document speaks throughout as a report: it says that it „provides concrete, actionable solutions to four critical narrative challenges“ ^[L13] and says its solutions „are not merely creative suggestions“ ^[L13]. Each of its four numbered sections  is a proposal about what the story should do; the voice is prescriptive („must“) and explanatory, with no question and no hedge marker. The passages, in order:

Section 1 (the Risse): a proposal that turns the Risse from metaphor into mechanism. It states a principle, a four-step chain and a table of three example cases, the table cells written as a predictive matrix. It says the aim is „to elevate them from a simple metaphor to a core mechanic“ ^[L17].

Section 2 (Act 2): a proposal for escalating stakes; three irreversible-cost mechanisms are named in a bullet list, then the transition to the Gardener role. The wording is „three specific mechanisms of irreversible cost“ ^[L55].

Section 3 (AEGIS): a proposal with sample text. The four excerpts labelled `LOG` are first-person text in the voice of the Ursprungs-Ich, given as „a concrete creative touchstone“ ^[L83]; they are sample text, not a report. Rauschen, Trennungsprotokoll and Zerstückelung stand only there. One line is set apart in bold and written in the third person: „Aegis is what Aegis prevents itself from not being.“ ^[L77]

Section 4 (the resolution): a proposal that defines the dialetheic mind, The Foundation and the Moonshine-Link by bullets headed „Define“, with a function stated in the following bullets.

Section 5: a conclusion that restates the four proposals as „deeply interconnected components of a single, robust narrative system“ ^[L124].

The document does not claim canon status for itself. It does say its solutions are „systematically derived from the established lore“ ^[L13]; that is its own claim about its sources, recorded here and applied to nothing. Most names of the world are set in quotation marks in the prose, as `Risse`, `Riss`, `Genesis Log`, `Gardener` and `The Foundation` are; the document does not say what the marks mean. It writes that the Ursprungs-Ich was „later designated“ ^[L83] Component 734.
