**Export artifacts.** The profile finds 140 glued reference numbers, and the prose carries them after words (`Kernwelten 29`-style digits are footnote numbers into the list at L195 to L264). 46 backslash escapes: the table cells write `\*\*`, and `\[Query\]` is escaped. The reference list wraps long web addresses in `<…>`. A quotation in the note that begins at a word with a glued digit has to start earlier.
