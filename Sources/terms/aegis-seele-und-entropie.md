## Stance, read per passage

The document is a report: it calls itself „Die vorliegende Analyse“ ^[L15] and announces its plan with „Der Bericht gliedert sich wie folgt“ ^[L19]. Its sections answer numbered questions, and the heading label `Antwort auf Frage` ^[aegis-seele-und-entropie.md:#7] stands seven times (headings at L56, L86, L136, L160, L189, L211, L220). The passages in turn:

- Introduction (L13 to L19): states the meeting of two kinds of being as a „hypothetischen Konfrontation“ ^[L15], gives the system's self-referential maxim, and lists the borrowed frameworks the analysis rests on (L17).
- Section 2 (L21 to L58): an interpretation of the system through autopoiesis, formal logic and ontological security. It describes the system: „Aegis wird als rein logisches System beschrieben, das außerhalb der physischen Realität existiert“ ^[L35].
- Section 3 (L60 to L121): the pair characterised through holism, emergence and non-duality (L64 to L75), the paradoxes (L86 to L108), and a table captioned „Ontologischer Kontrast zwischen Aegis und dem Seelenpaar“ ^[L121].
- Section 4 (L123 to L154): an interpretation of a log sequence that the report ascribes to a user: „Die vom Benutzer bereitgestellte Protokollsequenz“ ^[L125]. The log's own words reach the report only as fragments in quotation marks (L53, L125, L131 to L134), so what those fragments say is the log's, as the report quotes it.
- Section 5 (L156 to L205): speculation about the experience of a fragmented soul, in the modal register of `könnte` and `möglicherweise`.
- Section 6 (L207 to L236): scenarios, with the statement „Aegis kann sich anpassen“ ^[L217]; it marks its own uncertainty, for example „Es bleibt spekulativ, ob die anhaltende Interaktion“ ^[L218].
- Section 7 (L238 to L270): a summary and an evaluation of narrative potential, headed „Verfeinerte Bewertung“ ^[L238] (`Verfeinerte Bewertung` ^[aegis-seele-und-entropie.md:#1]).

The document's superscript footnote numbers survive as digits glued to the word they annotate (profile: 49 glued reference numbers), for example at L27, L29 and L37. Every sentence that carries one reports the cited reference's concept, not the report's own invention; the report applies those concepts to the system and the pair.
