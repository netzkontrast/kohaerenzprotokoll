## What the extraction ran into

**Zero.** `K1` stands alone 0 times and inside a compound 2 times: the document writes only „K1-Dominanz“ ^[L36] (and in L52); see the row of `K1-Dominanz`. Whether the document means a numbered kernel by `K1` it does not say; the figure `K0-Einbruch` ^[L38] is its only other K-numbered name. The document writes the plain digit, never a subscript. This zero is a compound, not an absence.

**Standing alone less often than with compounds, one reason each.** `MAP` is inside `MAP-Synthese`. `Kael` has the genitive `Kaels`; `Juna` has `Junas`. `ANP` and `EP` stand inside longer words such as `Host-ANP` and the table label. `Landauer-Limit` has the genitive `Landauer-Limits`; `Landauer` is the lens stem of four compounds. `Mosaik-Herz` is inside `Mosaik-Herzens`. `Teil I` is the start of `Teil II` and `Teil III` (substring). `Kapitel 3` is the start of `Kapitel 36` and `Kapitel 38-39` (substring); `Ki` is the start of `Kishōtenketsu`, so its 2 standing alone are the table cells of L66 and L77 only. `TSDP`, `Dramatica`, `Kishōtenketsu`, `Throughline`, `Halteproblem`, `Turing`, `Metzinger` and `Luhmann` each stand in a compound or an inflected form. The census lists `Mosaik-Herz` and `Mosaik-Herzens` both because both are written.

**Export damage.** The export wrote the table markers as `\*\*` (60 backslash escapes in all). Two formulas lost their symbol: the line „agiert als der Wächter des Kohärenz-Kernels“ ^[L42] is followed by empty parentheses, and the Landauer energy at L46 is also empty parentheses; what the two gaps held is not recoverable from the export. 47 reference numbers are glued to the preceding word („AEGIS.1“), from dropped superscripts.
