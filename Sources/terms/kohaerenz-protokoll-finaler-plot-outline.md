## Stance, read per passage

The document is a plan, and it calls itself one: its title promises „Eine definitive narrative Architektur für den finalen Akt“ ^[L13]. Each of the four parts opens with a sentence that says what the part does. Part 1 „legt einen definitiven Rahmen für die Integration“ ^[L22] fest; part 2 „übersetzt das abstrakte Konzept“ ^[L83] von Kael als Gärtner; part 3 „liefert ein reichhaltiges“ ^[L129] Portfolio; the last part „liefert einen konkreten, umsetzbaren Plan“ ^[L170]. Within part 1 the passage on the encounter says it „beschreibt das“ ^[L48] „Wie“ und „Wann“. The table that follows is announced by „Die folgende Tabelle systematisiert die narrativen Szenarien“ ^[L68].

The voice inside the parts is conditional. The scenarios, tools and endings are proposals: „Kael könnte kosmologische Konstanten“ ^[L107] anpassen, and „Der Roman könnte mit zwei oder drei kurzen“ ^[L178] Epilogen enden. The count of the whole word: `könnte` ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-finaler-plot-outline.md:#16]. Some sentences are flat statements about the planned story, for instance „Sie findet nach der Transformation von AEGIS statt“ ^[L50]. The document labels three scenarios `Szenario` ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-finaler-plot-outline.md:#4] in all, in the list and in the table header, and its list of two guardian examples is labelled `Beispiel` ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-finaler-plot-outline.md:#2] in the export, so the numerals of the headings (1.1 to 4.3) are the only numbering.

Passages that report another text: the numbers glued to words (30 glued reference numbers in the profile) point to a reference list, and entry 3 of that list reads „Kohärenz Protokoll: Narrative Architektur“ ^[L208]. A sentence such as „Die Guardians zeigen“ ^[L153] a quoted phrase is therefore the document reporting that other text, and the quoted words inside it are that text's, not this document's. The list also holds web pages on soulmates, otherness, deus ex machina, non-intervention and gardening, each with an access date; none of them is a term of the novel.

The document claims a standing for its own proposal: the title and part 1 call it „definitiven“ ^[L22]. That is recorded as the document's word and applied to nothing.
