## Stance, read per passage

The document is a report addressed to an album project, not a text of the novel's world; it says so in its opening lines. It speaks as an analysis that proposes: „Dieser Bericht dient als tiefgreifender analytischer Rahmen“ ^[L27] and states a thesis in „Die Kernthese dieses Berichts postuliert“ ^[L39]. The goal it gives is „nicht über Trauma zu singen, sondern aus den synaptischen Rissen selbst zu sprechen“ ^[L39].

Part I (L43 to L123) reads the figures as parts of a system through a dissociation theory (`TSDP` ^[neurochemische-lyrik-transzendenz-durch-klang.md:#2]); the passages are descriptive report of song lyrics, one bullet per figure, with the roles ANP and EP as the document's own labels (L55). Part II (L127 to L216) is a lexicon of neurochemical states, each with the repeated labels „Der Zustand“ ^[L154] and „Lyrische Gesten“ ^[L155]. Part III (L220 to L268) maps these onto sound. Part IV (L272 to L310) is a plan: a track table (L288 to L298) and a closing aim. It labels its own sections: „Dieser Abschnitt operationalisiert direkt das Kernmandat des Auftrags“ ^[L131], „Die folgende Tabelle operationalisiert die komplexe Analyse“ ^[L284], „Dieser abschließende Teil synthetisiert alle vorangegangenen Analysen“ ^[L276]. One passage names a three-step arc of the albums: „Dieser dialektische Aufbau“ ^[L123].

No passage is a question in the document's voice; the nine question marks sit in lyric lines, table cells and reference links (L80, L108, L293, L295, L318 to L357). There is no sample text of the novel and no lock or sync date.
