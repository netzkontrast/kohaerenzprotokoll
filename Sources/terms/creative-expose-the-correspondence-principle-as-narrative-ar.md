**L80 to L88, worldbuilding.** A bulleted list of the Überwelt and the Kernwelten, with a nested list KW1 to KW4, each with a code in parentheses and a function in the document's own labels. The document calls the simulated reality „a diagnostic chart of a fragmented mind“ ^[L80].

**L94 to L98, plot.** The plot as three acts, numbered 1 to 3, modelled on a trauma-therapy sequence: the document says the structure is „consciously modeled on the three phases of trauma therapy“ ^[L94].

**L102, conclusion.** A summary of the thesis; no new term.
