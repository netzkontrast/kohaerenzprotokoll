## Stance, read per passage

The document is a report that names itself a „philosophisch-narrative Analyse“ ^[L11] and states its aim as an investigation of dialetheism and its logics for the novel ^[L23]. It speaks in six registers, each under its own heading.

Passages I to II (L13 to L84) set out the philosophical position in the report's own voice, with reference numbers glued to the sentences; the lines report cited literature, not the world. Passage II defines the central notion: „Eine Dialetheia ist eine Aussage (A), für die gilt, dass sowohl (A) als auch ihre Negation“ ^[L36]. Passage III (L86 to L171) is a logic exposition; it contains a table comparing logics (L156 to L167) and an open self-correction inside a bullet ^[L140].

Passage IV (L173 to L209) is a question put to the world: two hypotheses, introduced as „Hypothese A: AEGIS operiert dialetheisch/parakonsistent“ ^[L177] and „Hypothese B: AEGIS als anti-dialetheisches (klassisches) System unter Stress“ ^[L187]. The document attributes the framing to a brief: „wie sie im Forschungsauftrag formuliert wird“ ^[L175]. Each hypothesis carries a bullet „Narrative Implikationen“, a template label that repeats ^[L185]. It also reads the core directive ^[L197] and a version upgrade ^[L204].

Passage V (L211 to L259) proposes a differentiation of four worlds, with four repeated field labels (Logik, Atmosphäre, Umgang mit Widerspruch, Manifestation) under each world heading, e.g. ^[L215], and a table ^[L247]. It frames this as a proposal: „Ein zentraler Vorschlag des Forschungsauftrags“ ^[L213].

Passage VI (L261 to L313) is a recommendation: it weighs the two hypotheses and says „Eine Empfehlung hängt von den primären narrativen Zielen ab“ ^[L270]. It leaves the choice open; it does not lock anything. Passage VII (L315 to L323) concludes, and L325 to L407 is a numbered reference list of web pages and articles.

The document does not claim canon status for any of its proposals.
