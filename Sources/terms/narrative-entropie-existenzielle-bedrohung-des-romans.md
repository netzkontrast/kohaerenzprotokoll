## Stance, read per passage

The document speaks as a design brief throughout, as an impersonal working paper addressed to a creative team, and never as a scene. It opens with „Dieses Dossier operationalisiert“ ^[L22] and says what it works on: „die zentrale meta-fiktionale Prämisse“ ^[L22]; its own aim is „Blueprints für existenzielle Dringlichkeit“ ^[L24]. The body is cut into three parts with a conclusion: Teil I, the laws (L28 to L117); Teil II, the symptoms (L121 to L166, with the subsection on "Risse" at L170 to L180); Teil III, the conflict and the scenarios (L184 to L237); then „Schlussfolgerung: Eine selbstbewusste Welt“ ^[L241].

Its coined names and also a quoted question and a borrowed theory name stand in double quotation marks in the prose (the `Nichts Rauschen`, the `Grauen Verfall`, the `Todsünden`), so the marks do not by themselves say whose word it is. The first paragraph of 1.1 (L40) says a synthesis named after two theorists governs the novel's world, which is the document's statement about the world; the physics terms „Summe über die Historien“ ^[L42] and „Kollaps der Wellenfunktion“ ^[L42] are called a „Quanten-Metapher“ ^[L42], so the document marks them as an analogy it applies.

Teil III ends in a scenario list that the document introduces as „konkrete, skriptfähige Momente“ ^[L224]; each scenario is a proposal for a scene, and one carries a line of invented dialogue, marked „Dialogfragment“ ^[L229], spoken by an Alter named Argus. A table at L231 to L237 sets four beats against the actions of AEGIS and of Kael; its cells are proposals in the present tense, not report. The numbered references (L253 to L278) are titles of sources, and the document's claims lean on them by glued numbers (38 glued ref numbers in the profile); what those sources say is theirs.

Where it reports research (the passages on suspense and on narrative transportation at L60 to L84), what it states is that research's account as the document relays it, with a reference number glued to the sentence. The document ends by calling the novel's world „eine selbstbewusste Erzählung“ ^[L249]; that is the document's own claim, recorded and never applied.
