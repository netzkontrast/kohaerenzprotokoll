## What the extraction ran into

**The document opens with a chat reply, not with its subject.** Its first two paragraphs are a cover note in the voice of an assistant speaking to the person it was made for: „Hier ist ein auf dein Projekt zugeschnittenes SKILL.md-Dokument.“ ^[L11] and „von dir vorgeschlagenen“ ^[L13]. They name the ecosystem the adapted frameworks come from, `LobeHub` ^[ontologische-inversion-von-aegis-kritisches-framework.md:#1], and they are the only place that says whose proposal the inversion of AEGIS is. The census lists what they name; the note records what they say of the document's standing.

**The skill's header was flattened into one heading.** The delimiter line of a YAML header became `\----------` inside a level-two heading, and `name:` and `description:` run on in one bold line: „name: narrative-critical-thinking description: Wende systematische Forschungsmethoden und kritisches Denken auf die Dual-Kernel-Narrativ-Engine an.“ ^[L15]. The skill's name is a candidate written with its hyphens, and the description carries one term in a surface the body does not use: `Bias-Kontrolle` here, where the body has `Verzerrungskontrolle` in L52 and `Risk of Bias` in L147.

**The last lines are escaped.** L162 is `\#\# Best Practices`, so the probe does not see it as a heading, and each of the three rules is an escaped list item whose bold label sits between `\*\*`: „Umarme die Paradoxie“ ^[L166]. The count matches the words and not the escapes, so the three labels count. L127 ends `Validierungen:markdown`. This and the escaped tail look like what a flattened code fence leaves, but the document does not say so. Three other places look like the trace of a dropped citation marker: a space before the colon at the end of L36, and a full stop after the question mark at the end of L57 and of L74. The document names no source near any of them.

**Three candidates count zero, and each is a joined form with an emphasis mark inside it.** `InstructionDeputies (ANPs)` and `ChatCompanions (EPs)` (L165, where each name is wrapped in `\*`) and `Collapse Susceptibility Index / CSI` (L28, where the English name is wrapped in `*`). **Two candidates the count reads as prose.** `Korrespondenz vs. Kohärenz` has a full stop after „vs“ and stands in the description header (L15); `Bewerte Prozesse, nicht nur Outcomes` has a comma and is the first rule (L164). Each was counted by hand with the same function and stands once.
