## Stance, read per passage

The document is a concept revision in numbered sections, written as a proposal about the figure Kael, and it marks its own certainty with modal words rather than labels. It treats a trauma-dissociation theory as a story lens for the novel's figure, never as anyone's real condition.

- **Framing, L15 and L17.** The revision declares a theory its lead frame: it positions the theory „als das primäre Erklärungsmodell“ ^[L15], with other models only as supplements. The origin is hedged: „möglicherweise im Zusammenhang mit seiner Erschaffung“ ^[L17].
- **Profile, L21 to L33.** A field list (`Name/Bezeichnung`, `Kernproblem`, `Ursprung`, `Systemstruktur`, `Ziel`) states a diagnosis-like label with a hedge: „vermutlich Tertiäre Dissoziation“ ^[L22].
- **Re-mapping, L35 to L88.** Each part of Kael carries two labelled fields, `TSDP-Rolle` and `Funktion`. The roles are mostly tentative: „Könnte aber auch Züge eines“ ^[L79] another role; the last figure is no part at all: „Repräsentiert nicht einen spezifischen Teil im TSDP-Sinne“ ^[L88].
- **Conflict dynamics, L90 to L99.** The older conflict labels are explained afresh: „Die „Pressure Points“ werden nun durch TSDP-Dynamiken erklärt“ ^[L92]. Each item closes with an `Entspricht` pointer to codes the document does not define. One item ends on a question mark: „erzwungene Zustandswechsel?“ ^[L99].
- **Goal, L101 to L112.** A list of aims, stated as what the aim is and is not: „nicht die Verschmelzung aller Teile“ ^[L103].
- **Implications and conclusion, L114 to L121.** The revision records its own standing: „eine klinisch fundierte und strukturell klare Basis“ ^[L121]. That is a claim of the document about itself, recorded here and applied to nothing.
