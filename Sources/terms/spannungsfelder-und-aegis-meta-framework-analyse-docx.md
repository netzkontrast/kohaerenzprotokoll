## Stance, read per passage

The document is an analysis written about another text: it restates a proposed framework and judges it. It marks no passage as a lock, a plan or a question of its own, and it uses no status labels. What it marks is structure, by the same five bold field labels under each numbered field: `Prinzip:` ^[spannungsfelder-und-aegis-meta-framework-analyse-docx.md:#13], `Modell:` ^[spannungsfelder-und-aegis-meta-framework-analyse-docx.md:#13], `Operation:` ^[spannungsfelder-und-aegis-meta-framework-analyse-docx.md:#13], `Theoretische Konzepte:` ^[spannungsfelder-und-aegis-meta-framework-analyse-docx.md:#13] and `Diskussion:` ^[spannungsfelder-und-aegis-meta-framework-analyse-docx.md:#13]. The first three restate the framework's own entry for the field; the last two are the analysis's voice. These labels are the template, not terms.

- Opening, L13 to L19: a statement of purpose in three steps. The analysis „widmet sich der Untersuchung von dreizehn fundamentalen Spannungsfeldern“ ^[L15], then examines the framework, whose „Selbstbestätigung“ claim it evaluates ^[L17], and finally evaluates its suitability for narrative projects ^[L19].
- Parts I to III, L21 to L236: one block per field, each opening with the framework's `Prinzip:`, `Modell:` and `Operation:` as reported, then a plain-language gloss, then numbered theoretical concepts, then `Diskussion:`. The concepts are sources the analysis reports and compares; where it says the framework fits one, it hedges with „scheint“, for instance „Das AEGIS-Modell scheint eine Form starker Emergenz zu implizieren“ ^[L36].
- Part IV, L237 to L288: a synthesis with a table, headed „Tabelle 1: Übersicht der analysierten Spannungsfelder und theoretischen Konzepte“ ^[L270], and a closing summary.
- Part V, L290 to L333: the evaluation of the framework, headed „Teil V: Analyse des AEGIS Meta-Frameworks“ ^[L290]. The framework's components are described in the document's words (Ziel, Metadimensionen, Achsen, Regeln, Architekturmodell, mathematische Formalisierung), each followed by a verdict. The verdicts are the analysis's own, for example „Die Formalisierung ist eher eine konzeptionelle Skizze als ein arbeitsfähiges mathematisches Modell“ ^[L316].
- Part VI, L335 to L379: the narrative evaluation, headed „Teil VI: Bewertung von AEGIS für narrative Projekte“ ^[L335], with a second table, „Tabelle 2: AEGIS Meta-Framework für narrative Projekte: Stärken und Schwächen“ ^[L370].
- Conclusion and references, L381 to L520: a closing verdict that AEGIS is „ein faszinierendes, wenn auch spekulatives, Meta-Framework“ ^[L389], then 128 numbered references.

The document has a second voice inside the framework's entries: the text of `Prinzip:`, `Modell:` and `Operation:` is the framework's, reported, and the analysis's reading follows it.
