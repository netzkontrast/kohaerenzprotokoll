## Stance, read per passage

The document is a report in seven numbered sections with a numbered reference list at the end (L284 to L286). It states its own purpose and structure at the start, defines and surveys in the middle, and ends with advice to creators. It carries no lock, status label or plan of the novel; none of its labels is a canon marking.

- **Opening, a plan of the report (L11 to L21).** The document announces its aim as „Das Ziel dieses Berichts ist es, eine detaillierte komparative Analyse von Monstergruppen und Paradoxien bereitzustellen“ ^[L21] and then lists what each of its sections will do. This is the document's description of itself, written in the future-facing register of a plan.
- **Section 2, definition and survey (L23 to L71).** Each paragraph opens on a bold label (`Etymologie und Kernkonzept`, `Grenzgänger`, `Reflexion von Normen`) and then reports what monsters are and do in myth, folklore, literature and film. The register is descriptive, and the paragraph closing the section generalises: „Monster sind somit kulturelle Konstrukte“ ^[L71].
- **Section 3, definition and typology (L73 to L108).** The same pattern for paradoxes: a definition (L79), bold-labelled core elements (L83 to L86), four named types (L92 to L95), and functions (L101 to L106). The definition of the Paradoxon is partly reported from a cited thinker on L79 and is not the document's own wording there.
- **Section 4, comparison (L110 to L136).** Four differences stated as bold labels (L116 to L119), then a table titled „Tabelle 1: Komparative Zusammenfassung von Monstergruppen und Paradoxien“ ^[L125] at L125 to L134, then a paragraph that qualifies the table: „Die Grenze verschwimmt dort, wo das Monströse konzeptuell unbegreiflich wird oder das Paradox eine greifbare, bedrohliche Form annimmt“ ^[L136].
- **Section 5, overlaps (L138 to L194).** The section heading states the document's two directions, „Das Monster als Paradoxon / Das Paradoxon definiert das Monster“ ^[L138]. Its bullets list monsters that embody paradoxes (L146 to L172) and paradoxes that define monstrousness (L178 to L181). L194 is its own summary paragraph.
- **Section 6, synergies (L196 to L259).** Each worked example is split under the labels `Monster-Element`, `Paradoxon-Element` and `Zusammenspiel` (L219 to L241), then „Tabelle 2: Beispiele für das Zusammenspiel von Monstern und Paradoxien“ ^[L247]. The labels name fields of a template that repeats per work; the label text under each is the analysis.
- **Section 7, advice (L261 to L282).** The register turns to imperative and conditional address of creators. It opens with „Für Kreative, die diese Elemente in ihren eigenen Projekten nutzen möchten, ergeben sich aus dieser Analyse mehrere handlungsleitende Einsichten“ ^[L273], and the bullets are labelled as instructions, such as „Monster vertiefen“ ^[L276] and „Kontraste nutzen“ ^[L275].
- **Reference list (L284 to L286).** Marked by the heading `Quellenangaben` ^[monstergruppen-und-paradoxien-im-narrativ.md:#1], a numbered reference list of web and print sources in one long line. The text body cites them by glued numbers (17 glued reference numbers in the profile), which the export left without their superscript form.

The document does not mention the novel, any of its figures or any of its places; its title addresses „narrative Projekte“ ^[L11] in general. Of the profile's 32 question marks, 19 stand in the single reference-list line (L286) and 13 in the body (L84, L93, L202, L230, L254, L276, L278): examples of paradoxes, questions the document says a work poses, and advice to creators.
