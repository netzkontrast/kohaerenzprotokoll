## Stance, read per passage

The document is one plan, a chapter outline, and it speaks in the descriptive present about a story that is not yet written; it names no author and no date of its own. It opens by saying what it is: „Dieses Dokument fusioniert die strukturellen und inhaltlichen Outlines“ ^[L13], titled „Finaler Kausaler Bauplan“ ^[L11]. That is the document's own claim about its standing and is recorded, not applied.

Chapters 1 to 13 (Akt I, L15 to L82) are written out, one bold title per chapter, each with two labelled bullets. The labels are `Inhalt` and `Fokus`: „Inhalt: Metaphysischer Prolog“ ^[L21] reports what happens, and „Fokus: Etablierung der sterilen Ästhetik“ ^[L22] says what the chapter is for. So each chapter has two voices, plot and function, and the label names which one speaks. Chapters 14 to 40 (Akt II to Akt IV, L88 to L148) carry no labels: one line each, title, colon, a sentence of plot and function together („14\. Resonanz und Dissonanz“ ^[L88]).

Each act opens with an italic `Fokus` line that gives the act its function, for example „Fokus: Vertiefung der etablierten Situation.“ ^[L86]. The acts are headed with the Kishōtenketsu names: „Akt I: Ki (起)“ ^[L15], „Akt II: Shō (承)“ ^[L84], „Akt III: Ten (転)“ ^[L114] and „Akt IV: Ketsu (結)“ ^[L134]; the document calls its structure „Kishōtenketsu-Struktur“ ^[L13].

Quotation marks are used for terms of the story and for borrowed or reported phrases without saying which: „Subjekt Kael“ ^[L26], „Risse“ ^[L27], „Waffenstillstand“ ^[L94], „Gödel-Gambit“ ^[L128], „Grounding-Artefakte“ ^[L126]. Chapter 26 carries the marker „(Midpoint)“ ^[L112]. The text names no speaker, and passages that take AEGIS's point of view are marked only by the sentence, for example „Aus der kalten, analytischen Perspektive von AEGIS“ ^[L26].
