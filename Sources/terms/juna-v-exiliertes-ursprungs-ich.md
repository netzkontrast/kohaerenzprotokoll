## Stance, read per passage

The document is a research dossier in one voice, and it labels its own parts. It opens with the heading „Executive Dossier“ ^[L18] and says what it is for: „Dieses Dossier legt die theoretische und narrative Grundlage für die Neukonzeption von Juna/V als exilierten Teil von Kaels“ ^[L22] — a proposal for a reconception, not a report of an existing story. It calls its result a „Ideen-Repository“ ^[L22].

Teil I (L26 to L98) is a plan and a model: it „verankert die Charakterdynamik in etablierten psychologischen Modellen von Trauma und Identität“ ^[L30]. Its bullets are labelled with bold lead words (`Kaels Kern-Selbst`, `Juna/V als die Exilantin`, `Der Manager`, `Der Feuerwehrmann`, `Innere Polarisierung`) and each ends in a footnote number glued to the sentence. Its closing table is introduced as „Psychologische und mythologische Rahmenwerke für ein gebrochenes Bewusstsein“ ^[L88].

Teil II (L103 to L143) is a question: it asks „Wie ist das möglich?“ ^[L107] and offers two alternative models, set out as `Modell A: Immanentes Exil` ^[L128] and `Modell B: Transzendentes Exil` ^[L129]. Section 2.3 puts the whole goal of reintegration in doubt: it „stellt das angenommene narrative Ziel der einfachen Reintegration oder Verschmelzung in Frage“ ^[L137] and closes on open questions (L143).

Teil III (L147 to L187) is a mechanism section. Its labels are `Mechanismus` and `Narrative Implikationen` ^[L162]; it promises „harte Wissenschaft“ ^[L151] and treats the physics, in its closing paragraph, as „kraftvolle, erdende Metaphern“ ^[L187].

The final section is a list of four scenarios, introduced as „umsetzbaren narrativen Szenarien“ ^[L195]; each bullet is labelled `Szenario` with a title (L197 to L200). The references (L204 to L261) are a numbered list of web pages, each with an access date.
