## Stance, read per passage

The document is one German dossier, headed „Ein psychologisches und systemtheoretisches Dossier zum Ersten AEGIS-Eingriff“ ^[L11]. It speaks in argued prose with numbered section headings, two tables and a closing list of references; it labels its own passages by section, not by tags.

- **Einleitung (L13 to L19)** is a question and a statement of method. The question is whether the first intervention was „ein Angriff oder eine Rettung“ ^[L15], and the premise is that the answer is „keine bereits existierende Tatsache“ ^[L15].
- **Abschnitt I (L21 to L76)** defines AEGIS, System Kael and the ontological rules in the document's own voice, as a description of the world, with a summary table at L69 to L76. It is a definition passage, not a plan.
- **Abschnitt II (L78 to L94)** is a reconstruction of the event in prose, headed „Ereignischronologie und Systemzustandsanalyse“ ^[L82].
- **Abschnitt III (L96 to L112)** and **Abschnitt IV (L114 to L128)** are two arguments, each framed as a perspective: the first opens „Aus dieser Perspektive“ ^[L102], the second „Hier wird der Eingriff als ein verzweifelter Akt der Selbsterhaltung von AEGIS gerahmt“ ^[L120]. Both are labelled `These A` ^[aegis-eingriff-angriff-oder-rettung.md:#4] and `These B` ^[aegis-eingriff-angriff-oder-rettung.md:#4] in the table at L134 to L141.
- **Abschnitt V (L130 to L157)** states that it „wird keinen Sieger küren“ ^[L132], then draws a synthesis and gives recommendations for the narrative, one per thesis (L155 to L157).
- **Quellenangaben (L159 to L161)** is a reference list of ten numbered web sources, flattened into a single line.
