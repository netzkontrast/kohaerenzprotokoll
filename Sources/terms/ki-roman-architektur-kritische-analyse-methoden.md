## Stance, read per passage

The document is a methods handbook, not a story and not a lock. It calls itself „ein derartiges exhaustives *Skill-Framework*“ ^[L13] and „methodisches Handbuch und operationelles Werkzeug“ ^[L15]; its prose is imperative and evaluative (`muss`, `zwingend`), and it describes the novel's architecture to say how to test it.

- Framing, L13 to L15: a framework for critical thinking applied to the Dual-Kernel narrative engine. Its scope sentence reads „nicht für die Beurteilung simplen Storytellings konzipiert“ ^[L19].
- Applications and the PRISMA/PICO table, L17 to L32: a plan for when to apply the method and a table of method correspondences; its header cells are the document's labels, and the cells are written with escaped asterisks.
- Kernel analytics, L34 to L59: a description of the two kernels (Kohärenz-Kernel with AEGIS, Kollaps-Kernel) plus a table of attributes against test approaches; the table header names the second kernel `Erasure-Kernel`, the heading `Kollaps-Kernel`.
- Epistemology, L61 to L83: a report on truth theories and the Gödel climax, ending in a numbered list of three stress-test steps.
- Physical isomorphy, L85 to L107: a report on thermodynamics and the Collapse Susceptibility Index, with its three parameters in a numbered list whose symbols were lost in export.
- Psychological topology, L109 to L130: a classification of ANPs and EPs with a summary table.
- NCP orchestration, L132 to L160: a report on the Narrative Context Protocol, the Moonshine-Link and the Player Dilemma, with a decision catalogue table.
- Traces, L162 to L182: two traces written as numbered steps, headed „Trace“ with a number for the first and third; the document has no second trace (see below).
- Operative guide, L184 to L206: four phases of a research design, each an instruction („muss zwingend eine präzise, überprüfbare Hypothese formuliert werden“ ^[L190]).
- Synthesis, L208 to L214: a closing claim about its own standing, recorded below.
- References, L216 to L227: a list whose first entry is the title of another document, „KI-Roman-Architektur: Kohärenz und Kollaps“ ^[L218]. The glued numbers after sentences are footnote numbers pointing into this list; most of them point to that entry, so the document's statements about the novel are reported from that text.
