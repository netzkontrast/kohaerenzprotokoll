## Stance, read per passage

The document is a plan for one chapter, in five lettered groups of numbered moments. It opens with a framing paragraph that places the chapter in the outline: „Abschluss und Klimax von Teil 1“ ^[L11]. A goal paragraph follows, headed „Ziel von Kapitel“ ^[L13], and then a list headed „Liste von 20-40 Momenten für Kapitel“ ^[L15] (the numbers are lost to the quotation check, see below). Groups A to C follow Kael; group D is AEGIS's side, marked as happening in the background: „Passiert im Hintergrund, parallel zu Kaels Aktion“ ^[L44]. Group E is the dissolution of the world. The moments are proposals, not report: they are hedged throughout, for instance „real oder symbolisch“ ^[L19] and „vielleicht nur mental“ ^[L40]. A closing paragraph sums the list up ^[L60]. Moments carry their own bold labels, one per moment. The 4 ASCII quotation marks enclose a sample of an inner voice ^[L22], which is diction, not a statement about the thing.
