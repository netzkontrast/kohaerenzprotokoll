## Stance, read per passage

The text moves through four registers, and only the headings and a few inline labels mark the change.

**Report and diagnostics, L11 to L17.** The title calls the whole text a „Konsultationsbericht und Manuskriptentwurf“ ^[L11], and the first section is headed „Initialisierung und Systemdiagnostik“ ^[L13]. It speaks of itself in the passive and names its maker: „Der vorliegende Bericht wird durch den Novel Writing Assistenten erstellt“ ^[L15]. It ends with a request for further material, which is a question to the reader and carries no statement about the novel's world.

**Advice, L19 to L55.** The sections on pacing, on the analytical part and on thermodynamics advise in the recommending mode: „wird empfohlen, kognitive Dissonanz“ ^[L27]; „Die Analyse legt nahe, diese Sabotage“ ^[L33]; „Hier muss das Landauer-Prinzip narrativ verankert werden“ ^[L39].
