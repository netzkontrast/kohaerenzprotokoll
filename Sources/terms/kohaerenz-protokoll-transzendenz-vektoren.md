These labels name the template's fields, not terms of the novel's world. The Fundierung items are comma lists of scientific concepts and carry no sentence of their own.

The speculative register is the document's own. On the Fundament as M-Theorie bulk it says the glitches „könnten somit Manifestationen von Interaktionen mit dem externen, höherdimensionalen Fundament sein“ ^[L36]. The scientific theories named are the report's sources, not claims about the novel's world.

Section II.A closes with a comparison table, headed „Vergleichende Analyse der Juna/V-Hypothesen“ ^[L183]. Observed: the export has an empty header row (L185 to L186), the bold markers are escaped `\*\*`, and one cell is wrapped inside a word (L191).

Section VI summarises the report in its own voice and says of itself: „Sie basieren auf rigoroser Recherche und plausibler Extrapolation“ ^[L355]. That sentence is the document's claim about its own basis and is recorded, not applied.

The reference list at the end (L357 onward) is a numbered run of web titles and addresses flattened into one line, and is not read for terms.
