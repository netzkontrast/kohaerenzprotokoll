Each label stands seven times in the body, one per part (count marks in the note). The theory sub-sections lean on outside sources: their sentences carry reference numbers glued to the last word, and the numbers point into the list from L574 on. The `Narrative Anleitung` sub-sections are the document's own recommendation.
