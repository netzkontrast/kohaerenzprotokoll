## Stance, read per passage

The document speaks as a report to the novel, in English, and says so in its first sentence: it „constitutes a comprehensive theoretical excavation designed to support the narrative architecture“ ^[L22]. It claims the anomalies of three fields are „structurally isomorphic“ ^[L22]. It writes no scene and names no figure of the novel; it speaks of the protagonist and of the Subject.

Two voices alternate. The physics, systems-theory and psychology passages (Parts I to IV) explain a borrowed concept in the register of a survey. After most of them a paragraph labelled `Narrative Insight` ^[gravity-as-narrative-metaphor-for-reality.md:#9] applies the concept to the story. Some of these paragraphs state what the story is (L228: „is an autopoietic defense mechanism“ ^[L228]), others hedge what it might be (L378: „might be the protagonist's attempt to artificially“ ^[L378]).

Part V is the synthesis. It opens with a definition, „can be defined as a metaphysical technology (or psychological regimen)“ ^[L422], and runs as a plan under three headings: „The Problem: Entropic Leakage“ ^[L426], „The Protocol (The False Solution)“ ^[L434] and „The Resolution (The True Solution)“ ^[L448]. The Conclusion states it flatly: „is a story of a system fighting its own physics“ ^[L464].

The appendix, headed „Structured Data Analysis“ ^[L468], restates the same concepts in four tables (hierarchy of forces against hierarchy of self, models of reality verification, geometry of trauma, and an act-by-act arc). The reference list is headed `Referenzen` ^[gravity-as-narrative-metaphor-for-reality.md:#1], a German heading over English entries.

Where the document reports a theory's standing in physics it says so in its own words, as at L106: „This remains a controversial and minority view“ ^[L106].
