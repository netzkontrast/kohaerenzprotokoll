## Stance, read per passage

The document is one text in a single register: a plan. It calls itself a „39-Kapitel-Outline (Dual-Storyform-Encoding)“ ^[L11] and does not narrate the novel's events as a story but lists, per chapter, what is to happen. Its passages differ by function, not by speaker.

**Blurb (L13 to L17).** An in-world sample text, a Klappentext, under the heading „Klappentext“ ^[L13]. It ends in a question: „Ist Liebe lediglich Information“ ^[L17]. Terms standing only here (the temperature, the Cologne setting, „Juna“ in quotation marks) are the blurb's, not the plan's.

**Analytical base (L19 to L47).** A framing report with two tables. It says the analysis rests on „das etablierte Kanon-Material“ ^[L21], which is a claim about its sources and is recorded, not applied. The first table assigns Dramatica parameters to two storyforms; the second assigns each Alter a function and an arc. Both tables are the document's assignments.

**Act introductions (L49, L535, L1013).** One framing paragraph per act, each naming the act's aesthetic in quotation marks, such as „Ästhetik der Ohnmacht“ ^[L51], „Anatomie der Spaltung“ ^[L537] and „Existenzielle Fusion“ ^[L1015].

**The chapter template (39 times).** Every chapter holds the same labelled blocks: `Worum geht es` ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-kapitel-outline-generierung.md:#39], `Eingeführte Konzepte` ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-kapitel-outline-generierung.md:#39], `Was passiert` ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-kapitel-outline-generierung.md:#39], a POV line, `Foreshadowing / Pacing / Reader-Substrate` ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-kapitel-outline-generierung.md:#39], two Encoding blocks, `Quellen pro Befund` ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-kapitel-outline-generierung.md:#39], `Widersprüche aufgetaucht` ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-kapitel-outline-generierung.md:#39], `Such-Parameter-Historie (Expansionen)` ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-kapitel-outline-generierung.md:#39] and `Confidence` ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-kapitel-outline-generierung.md:#39]. These labels name fields of a template and are not terms of the novel's world. The two Encoding blocks repeat Throughline, Signpost / Beat, Concern, Issue and Driver-Trigger lines (the word `Throughline` ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-kapitel-outline-generierung.md:#82] stands in the document that often). The Encoding blocks are themselves plan: they assign a Dramatica value to each chapter.

**Plan marks.** A POV line marked „[Vorschlag]“ ^[L207] is the document's own mark for a proposed point of view; the mark stands 11 times (`Vorschlag` ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-kapitel-outline-generierung.md:#11]). Near the end the document says that working titles carry the same mark: „Sämtliche [Arbeitstitel]-Vorschläge“ ^[L1621]. The Pivot-Marker blocks (`Pivot-Marker` ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-kapitel-outline-generierung.md:#12]) mark the act turns and give a Driver-Status, a Limit-Marker and an Outcome-Marker.

**Self-review.** Under each chapter the block `Confidence` is HIGH every time, and the block `Widersprüche aufgetaucht` says „Keine“ except where one chapter writes „Konflikt gelöst durch Kanon“ ^[L869]. The closing „Pre-Synthesis Integrity Check“ ^[L1511] is the document reporting on its own procedure; appendices A to H report on sources, rejected material, drift, history, search parameters, contradictions, cross-pollination and verification criteria.

**Rejections and drift (Anhang B, C, F).** Anhang B lists material as `Verworfen` ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-kapitel-outline-generierung.md:#4] or `Dekanonisiert` ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-kapitel-outline-generierung.md:#1]; Anhang C and F say which version was used and how conflicts were resolved. These are the document's decisions about what it kept, and a reading says so.

**Sample text.** The document gives no prose sample of the novel's chapters. It describes the prose techniques each chapter should use (the Reader-Substrate line).
