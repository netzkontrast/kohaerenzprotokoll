## Stance, read per passage

The document is one evaluation with a verdict, and it labels its own passages. It opens with a summary line marked „TL;DR: Eine perfekte, wenn auch anspruchsvolle Symbiose“ ^[L13] and a verdict sentence that calls the structure „ideale Erzählstruktur“ ^[L15]. Lines 17 to 55 are a mapping: the plot of the novel is laid onto four acts („Lassen Sie uns die Handlung von“ ^[L21] opens it), each act with a `Struktur` line describing the borrowed form and an Anwendung line applying it to the plot, introduced by the same label four times (the profile lists it as a repeated label). Two of those application lines close with a verdict in bold: „Dies passt perfekt.“ ^[L28] and „Dies passt ebenfalls perfekt.“ ^[L37]. Lines 57 to 79 list strengths and challenges, the challenges headed as demands on execution; the Anwendung lines report what the plot does, the challenge list states what has to be done. Lines 81 to 85 give the recommendation in the first person: „Meine definitive Empfehlung lautet“ ^[L85]. Nothing in it is marked as a lock, a decision or a question; it is a proposal for a structure, and its claim that applying the structure is a „strukturelle Notwendigkeit“ ^[L83] is the document's own judgement, recorded and not applied. The document names no speaker. It closes with an image, „Wir bauen hier keine Hütte, sondern eine Kathedrale des Geistes“ ^[L85].
