## What the extraction ran into

**1. One hundred zero-width spaces sit inside the formulas.** `A0` is stored as
`A0` + `U+200B`; the export flattened every subscript this way. A formal term
**cannot be matched, quoted or cited reliably** without normalising it away — and
a quote typed by hand will never equal the same quote read from the file.

**2. 116 reference numbers are glued to the words they annotate.**
„Fixpunkttheoremen 6" ^[L44], „Lawvere 6" ^[L45], „Autopoiesis 43" ^[L72], „Information 24" ^[L58]. The
export dropped superscript formatting, so a bare integer now follows the term. A
quoted fragment either includes the number, and then does not read as the term,
or excludes it, and then does not match the file.

**3. Five of eight protocol names are abbreviated differently in the summary
table than in the body.** One document, two surfaces per term, and the table is
where the document's own summary lives.

**4. `E` means three different things** (above). An index keyed by symbol would
merge a coherence function, an edge set and entropy.

**5. Quotation marks here mark citation of the criticised postulate.** They are
not this document's vocabulary; they are what it is arguing against.

**6. The document never mentions the novel.** Kael, Michael, Juna, Julia,
Überwelt, Risse, Guardians, Kern-Welten, DID, Roman — **zero occurrences of any
of them.** This is a purely theoretical document about a postulate.
