## Stance, read per passage

The document speaks as one analytic essay in five numbered parts (I to V) under a title that calls itself „Eine definitive Synthese der narrativen und ontologischen Architektur“ ^[L13]. Almost every sentence of the body ends with a glued footnote digit `1`, which points to the single first entry of the reference list, „Kohärenz Protokoll: Finaler Plot-Outline“ ^[L342]; the body therefore reports that outline, and the further entries are web titles with access dates. The voice is declarative-descriptive about a novel that is still planned: the future tense stands in „Der Roman wird Techniken der“ ^[L326], and the ending is called „bewusst so gestaltet“ ^[L324] in the present tense of a design.

- **Part I (L18 to L50), the frame.** A report on the novel's design as a „Großes Argument“ ^[L22]. It says the conclusion of the story should feel „logisch unausweichlich anfühlt“ ^[L50]. It maps four Dramatica throughlines onto characters in a table (L42 to L48).
- **Part II (L54 to L160), AEGIS.** An analysis that says of itself „Die folgende Analyse dekonstruiert AEGIS“ ^[L58]. Its architecture is put forward as a proposal: „wird eine hybride kognitive Architektur vorgeschlagen“ ^[L82]. A scenario voice follows in the present tense for the Gödel-Gambit (L128 to L143) with two named paths, `Pfad A` and `Pfad B`, offered as the two alternatives of one decision.
- **Part III (L164 to L236), Kael.** A report and a plan mixed: the TSDP model is presented as the structure of Kael's inner parts, then a heading names a post-apotheosis ethical test. The table at L231 to L236 sets three scenarios for the fragment 'O' side by side; it is a menu of options, not a decision.
- **Part IV (L240 to L284), the physics.** Opens by saying what it is for: „Dieser Abschnitt demystifiziert die metaphysischen Elemente“ ^[L244]. Concepts from outside the novel (quantum non-locality, a strange attractor, IIT) are applied to its world; they are lens terms.
- **Part V (L288 to L338), form.** Opens „Dieser letzte Abschnitt synthetisiert die strukturellen“ ^[L292] choices. It describes narrative structure, prose style and the reader's part, ending in a table of three-act against Kishōtenketsu (L332 to L338).
- **References (L340 to L421).** 80 numbered web entries with access dates; they are titles of cited works and carry no reading.

Observed: no passage carries a bracketed status label such as `[K]`, `[V]` or `[DEPRECATED]` (grep of the raw file finds none). The document's own standing claim is the title word „definitive“ ^[L13]; it is recorded here and applied nowhere. Of the 9 question marks, one sits in a table cell (L236) and the other eight in reference titles (L345 to L420).
