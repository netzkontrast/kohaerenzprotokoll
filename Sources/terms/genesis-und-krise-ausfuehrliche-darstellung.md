## Stance, read per passage

The document is narrated in the first person throughout its first part: „Ich bin. Kaum mehr.“ ^[L17] The „Ich“ is a narrative voice of the novel. The text is organised by its own labels: two headings, „Genesis“ and „Die Krise“ ^[L69], three bold part titles in capitals (`TEIL` ^[genesis-und-krise-ausfuehrliche-darstellung.md:#3]) and thirteen scene labels (`Szene` ^[genesis-und-krise-ausfuehrliche-darstellung.md:#13]), three of which carry the mark `NEUE` ^[genesis-und-krise-ausfuehrliche-darstellung.md:#3] before them. The labels are headings of the narrative, not fields of a template.

Passages L15 to L31 (Genesis, parts of TEIL 1) are narration from inside the awakening fragment. The voice also floats possibilities in the conditional: „Eine erste, einfache, organische Terminologie könnte hier entstehen“ ^[L31]. The three words that follow, „Bindung“, „Fluss“ and „Kern“, are offered as what such a terminology could contain, not as established terms.

Passages L37 to L67 (TEIL 2) change the register: the narration describes AEGIS and Komponente 734 in the third person, with system classifications set in quotation marks, for instance „Irrelevante strukturelle Signatur, keine Aktion erforderlich.“ ^[L53] — a classification the text attributes to the diagnosis of AEGIS. Speculation is marked with „Vielleicht“ ^[L63] and „könnte“; one passage reads „Hier könnte sich auch ein erstes Foreshadowing zeigen“ ^[L59].

Passages L75 to L107 (TEIL 3) narrate the crisis. Two perspectives are labelled in the text itself: „Aus der Perspektive des Systems“ ^[L99] and „Aus der Perspektive des erwachten Ichs“ ^[L101]. The last scene closes open: „ein offenes Ende, ein Versprechen zukünftiger Konflikte und unerwarteter Entwicklungen“ ^[L107].

The one question mark of the profile stands in the narration at L17, „Kälte?“ ^[L17], inside the voice of the „Ich“. The document states nothing about its own standing as a source.
