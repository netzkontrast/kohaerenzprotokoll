## Stance, read per passage

The document speaks as one design throughout and labels itself a design: „Dieses Dokument entwirft die architektonische Gesamtstruktur des Romans“ ^[L22]. It is an outline, not a report on written chapters, and it names no lock, no date and no status of its own.

Passage by passage. The opening (L18 to L27) states the thesis and two plots, each introduced by a bold label: „AEGIS' Mission (Externer Plot)“ ^[L26] and „Kaels Reise (Interner Plot)“ ^[L27]. Table 1 (L31 to L39) sets act, phase, chapter range and the two plots side by side and is introduced as „Strukturelle Gesamtkarte des Romans“ ^[L31]. Each of the four act sections opens with a bold „Aktübersicht:“ ^[L49] paragraph that states what the act is for AEGIS and for Kael. The chapter blocks (L53 to L293) repeat one template of labelled bullets, `Perspektive`, `AEGIS-Plot`, `Kaels Reise` and `Kapitel-Kishōtenketsu`, the last holding four sub-bullets `Ki`, `Shō`, `Ten` and `Ketsu`; these labels are the template's field names and name no term of their own. The ranges (`Kapitel 4-9`, `Kapitel 13-21`, `Kapitel 24-31`, `Kapitel 33-39`) are written as prose summaries without the template, and several of their sentences are conditional or tentative: „AEGIS könnte versuchen, Kaels andere Kernwelten zu manipulieren“ ^[L185] and, in Chapter 40, „vielleicht bei einem ruhigen Morgen mit Juna“ ^[L290]. The Anhang (L297 to L311) holds Table 2, „Entwicklungs-Tracker für System Kael“ ^[L301], one row per Anteil and one column per act, in a plan voice.

Where the document reports another text. The reference list gives one item, „AEGIS Paradoxon Neukonzeption und Analyse“ ^[L315], and glued footnote numbers in the body point to it (21 glued numbers in the profile). Statements that carry such a number rest on that other text, which this extraction does not see; the document does not say which parts of its own prose are not from it.

The voice changes without a label from the outline voice (present tense, „AEGIS initiiert sein finales Protokoll“ ^[L204]) to the rule-like voice of the act overviews, which map each act to a stage of two borrowed schemes. The document names no speaker for either.

## What the extraction ran into

The eleven terms that stand alone less often than with compounds are explained by what the export and the German inflection do. `AEGIS` and `Kael` take compounds and inflections: `AEGIS-Plot` is the repeated template label (10 times in the profile) and `Kaels` is the genitive; `Juna` takes `Junas`, `Kiko` takes `Kikos`, `Mnemosyne` takes `Mnemosynes`, `Kernwelt` takes the plural `Kernwelten` (the plural is also its own row), `Ko-Bewusstsein` takes `Ko-Bewusstseins`. `ANP` and `EP` stand alone 6 times and once, and their `in` counts (18 and 14) include the plurals `ANPs` (4) and `EPs` (9) and the hyphenated role labels of Table 2 such as `ANP-Host` and `EP-Kampf`. `Ki` is a substring of `Kiko`, `Kishōtenketsu` and other words, so its 35 `in` is not a count of the phase. `Kishōtenketsu` stands alone once; the other 12 are compounds, `Kapitel-Kishōtenketsu` (the template label, listed as its own row), `Kishōtenketsu-Struktur` and `Kishōtenketsu-Phase`. `Ki`, `Shō`, `Ten` and `Ketsu` are the four phase names, written both as bold table cells and as the template's sub-bullet labels, which is why they repeat 16 or 17 times; the Japanese characters beside them (`起`, `承`, `転`, `結`) are not listed.

The document spells `Co₁` with the subscript, so the plain `Co1` is not its form. The export glues the footnote number 1 to the end of sentences (21 glued numbers in the profile) and escapes the asterisks in the table cells (72 backslash escapes in the profile), so Table 1 and Table 2 quote their bold cells with the escapes. The `<!-- end list -->` comments between the chapter bullets and their `Kapitel-Kishōtenketsu` sub-bullets are export artifacts, and a few sentences are broken across lines at a bold word (L111 and L113, L185 and L187, L239 and L241), so a quotation of a bold phrase there stands on the second line only.

Two joined forms stand in the document with part of their punctuation as written: the „Wir“ perspective has the quotation marks around `Wir` alone, so `Wir-Perspektive` would count 0 and is not listed; `Mittelpunkt/Punkt ohne Wiederkehr` is listed as one name. `ZTEM` stands only inside the parenthesis of its full form, so all three surfaces count 1.

Claims about the document's own standing: it calls itself a design (L22); it makes no canon claim and the extraction applies none. The tragic-hero and Kishōtenketsu schemes, the Heldenreise stages and the TSDP role labels are listed under lens because the document applies them to its characters; it does not define them.
