## Stance, read per passage

The document is one report in a single register, a validation report that describes and judges; it plans nothing and locks nothing. It names its own kind in the title, „Validierungsbericht: Mathematische Kohärenz der Monstergruppen-Metapher“ ^[L11], and states its method as „Der Bericht folgt einem dreistufigen Ansatz“ ^[L17].

Passage by passage, by the document's own roman-numeral headings: I. Einleitung (L13 to L18) gives purpose, scope, method and structure. II. Mathematische Grundlagen (L20 to L80) is reference exposition of group theory and Moonshine, with a summary table headed „Zusammenfassung Schlüsselkonzepte und Narrative Interpretationen“ ^[L69]. III. Analyse der Metaphorischen Anwendung (L82 to L115) takes the novel project's metaphor point by point and weighs each against II. IV. Validierungsurteil (L117 to L121) is the verdict. V. Konsequenzen und Empfehlungen (L123 to L151) holds the flaws and a proposed alternative plot. VI. Schlussfolgerung (L153 to L157) summarises and recommends. The Referenzen (L159 to L193) are a numbered list of web pages with access dates.

The verdict is a closed answer: „Da das Validierungsurteil „NEIN“ lautet“ is quoted only as far as „folgt die Analyse gemäß Fall B“ ^[L125]. The alternative is offered as a proposal under „Alternative Plot-Idee (Synopsis)“ ^[L138], and the closing line is a recommendation: „Es wird dringend empfohlen, die aktuelle metaphorische Anwendung zu überarbeiten“ ^[L156].

Voices: the report speaks as an evaluator throughout. What it says of the novel project (the metaphor, the figures, AEGIS) it takes from a request it calls `Nutzeranfrage`, and it quotes that request's phrases inside its own sentences, for example „Idealbild integrierter Kohärenz, Stabilität und holistischer Komplexität“ ^[L84]; those phrases are the request's, not the report's.
