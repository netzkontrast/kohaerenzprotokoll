## Stance, read per passage

The document is a plan throughout: it states of itself that it „strukturiert den Roman in drei Akte“ ^[L15]. Each of the three parts has the same four slots, in order: a heading with a chapter range, a „Genre-Fokus“ ^[L19], a „Thema“ ^[L21], a list under „Inhaltliche Schwerpunkte“ ^[L23] and a list under „Annotationen“ ^[L33]. The slot labels repeat in all three parts (the profile lists `Annotationen` ×3, `Genre-Fokus` ×3, `Inhaltliche Schwerpunkte` ×3, `Thema` ×3), so they are the template and not terms of the story world.

Content points (L25 to L31, L47 to L57, L73 to L76) are written in the present tense as events of the plot, for example „Kael erwacht in der Konstrukt-Stadt“ ^[L25]. They are plan statements about what happens in the novel.

The annotations are a second voice about the same part. They carry three bracketed labels: `Pacing` ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-master-outline.md:#4], `Kohärenz-Check` ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-master-outline.md:#3] and `Limit-Integration` ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-master-outline.md:#3]. They give instructions for the writing of the part, for instance „dürfen aber im Text nicht zitiert werden“ ^[L36], and are not story content.

The closing block, headed „Epilog: Statusbericht PMAS“ ^[L84], is a plan for a report inside the story: „Ein finaler Systembericht von AEGIS.“ ^[L86]. The last section, „Strategischer Hinweis für das Pacing“ ^[L88], is a set of imperatives („Sorge in Teil I dafür“ ^[L90]).
