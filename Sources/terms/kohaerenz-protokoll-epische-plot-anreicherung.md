## Stance, read per passage

The document is a research brief with its answer: it heads itself „Rechercheauftrag: Epische Plot-Anreicherung“ ^[L11] and describes its own work as an analysis that „dekonstruiert und erweitert“ ^[L13] the narrative infrastructure of the novel. It never labels a passage `[K]`, `[V]` or as a lock; its own labels are the recommendation line and the heading marks listed here. Passage by passage:

- **Opening (L13):** a report of the premise in the document's voice — the `Dual Kernel Theory` and its two poles are stated as facts of the novel's world, not as proposals: „Kohärenz (K₁) agiert als erhaltende Kraft“ ^[L13].
- **Survey table and popular SF (sections 1.1 and 1.2, L19 to L47):** report of other works with footnote numbers glued to the sentences, and a last column that states what the novel does differently, for example „Kael benötigt keine externen Server“ ^[L26]. These last-column sentences are the brief's proposals about the novel, in the present tense.
- **Acts (section 2, L49 to L69):** a plan told as a description in the present tense, headed with the journey model of each act: „Die Heldinnenreise (Stabilisierung)“ ^[L53], „Zyklische Dekonstruktion (Konfrontation)“ ^[L59], „Die Heldenreise (Integration)“ ^[L65].
- **Journey structures (section 3, L71 to L83):** the brief applies borrowed models (Murdock, Campbell, Dramatica) and assigns Dramatica values to the novel, e.g. „Der Driver der Geschichte ist zwingend“ ^[L81].
- **Bekenstein fork (section 4, L85 to L115):** two options, marked „Variante A: Die harte Grenze“ ^[L89] and „Variante B: Das Brechen der Grenze“ ^[L102], then a decision line: „Empfehlung der Architekturanalyse“ ^[L115].
- **Beyond existing narratives (section 5, L117 to L125):** assertions about the novel's positioning and its characters in the present tense.
- **Matrices (section 6, L127 to L163):** three tables of options A, B and C against criteria; the third prefers „Option C: Funktionale Multiplizität als Ziel“ ^[L160].
- **Construction kit (section 7, L165 to L205):** five signature sequences, each titled in bold with its position in the acts, and three large plots; the third is marked „Empfohlener Rahmen“ ^[L198].
- **References (L207 to L266):** a numbered list of 58 web sources; it is the document's claim about where its survey comes from.

The document says nothing about its own canon standing: it presents itself as a proposal, not as a lock. Where it speaks of a real person it names only authors of cited works.
