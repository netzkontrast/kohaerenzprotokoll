## Stance, read per passage

The document is a report on Kael's parts, written as an analysis with recommendations. It names its own aim and frame in the opening: „Dieser Bericht verfolgt das Ziel einer detaillierten Analyse des inneren Systems“ ^[L17], and it limits its scope: „beschränkt sich auf die Anwendung der TSDP“ ^[L19]. Its voice throughout is the voice of a theory applied to the novel, hedged in nearly every sentence.

Sections I and II.A to II.B (L13 to L62) set out the theory and sort the five parts. The sorting is marked as a guess by the document itself: „lassen sich erste Hypothesen zu den Rollen“ ^[L56], and the section heading calls it „Vorläufige TSDP-Klassifikation“ ^[L54]. Single assignments carry the same hedge: „mutmaßlich Lex, Rhys, Kai“ ^[L37], „mutmaßlich Kiko, Nyx“ ^[L38], and „Wahrscheinlich EP“ ^[L59] for Kiko. Statements about the theory (L25 to L52) are reports of that theory, not claims about the novel; the footnote digits glued to them mark the sources the report draws on.

The table (L64 to L75) is the compressed form of the same hypotheses: its column head reads „Primäre TSDP-Rolle (Hypothese)“ ^[L70], and the lead sentence says the table „fasst die initialen Hypothesen zusammen“ ^[L66].

Section III (L77 to L284) is speculation per part, in the conditional and with the word `wahrscheinlich`: for example „Lex wird die Verbindung wahrscheinlich als irrational, potenziell gefährlich oder als Datenanomalie betrachten“ ^[L119] and „Kais Reaktion ist ungewiss“ ^[L283]. It is organised by four areas, named once: „Kernwelten, Selbst/Gesamtsystem, andere Anteile und äußere Umstände“ ^[L79]. Nothing in it is narrated scene or sample text.

Section IV (L286 to L388) gives eleven numbered profile fields per part. The fields read as statements, but the hedges continue where the part is unclear: „Funktion unklar, aber potenziell“ ^[L372] for Kai. Beliefs of the parts stand in straight quotation marks (L295, L315, L335, L355, L375); they are beliefs the report attributes to the parts, not statements of the report.

Section V (L390 to L444) is the synthesis, now with a firmer tone („höchstwahrscheinlich einer Tertiären Strukturellen Dissoziation“ ^[L394]) and then advice to the author of the narrative in the imperative: „Nutzen Sie die Profile“ ^[L410]. The report calls its result „eine fundierte Grundlage“ ^[L404]; that is the document's claim about itself and is recorded, not applied. The last block (L446 to L470) is a list headed „Referenzen“ ^[L446] with web pages and papers; they are not terms of the novel.
