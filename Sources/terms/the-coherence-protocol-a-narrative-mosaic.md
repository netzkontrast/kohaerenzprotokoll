## Stance, read per passage

The whole document is a plan, not a narrative: a brief for writing 39 stories, each assigned one point of view, and the stories themselves are not in it. It speaks in the imperative of an editor (the assignments are instructions to a writer) and describes the stories in the future tense.

- **Opening plan.** The collection is announced as a set of stories that are to form a mosaic: „Following the overarching three-act structure of the core narrative“ ^[L15]. The perspectives are a roster with one rule: „Each of the following perspectives must be assigned to exactly one story“ ^[L19].
- **Voice instructions per roster group.** The internal parts: „To be written in close third-person“ ^[L24]. The AEGIS group: „To be written in a cool, analytical log-format“ ^[L36]. The roster is extended: „Additional, unique Guardian and System POVs will be generated as needed“ ^[L46].
- **Three parts, three acts.** Part I is „structured as an analogue to the“ ^[L56] heroine’s-journey pattern; Part II „This part functions as a“ system analysis ^[L140]; Part III is „This final act, analogous to a“ hero’s-journey pattern ^[L224].
- **The 39 assignments.** Each has the three labels Core Concept, Assigned POV and Dramatize the Concept (39 times each, per the profile). The Core Concept line names a borrowed theory, the Assigned POV a figure of the roster, and the paragraph tells the writer what to dramatize. Some paragraphs prescribe an in-world document, for example „Write an impersonal log entry from AEGIS's perspective“ ^[L92]; these are instructions for a log, and the log’s own sentences are quoted inside them.
- **Question.** Three question marks stand in the file (L182, L216, L302); they sit in a quoted in-world log query, in the title of a Core Concept and in a rhetorical question of the last assignment.
- **Self-assessment.** The conclusion claims the finished collection will be „ultimate proof that the architectural foundation“ of the novel is sound ^[L306]. That is the document’s claim about its own use, recorded and not applied.
