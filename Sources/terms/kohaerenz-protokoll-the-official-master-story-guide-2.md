## Stance, read per passage

The document is a design guide written as instruction to whoever writes the novel: it speaks in the imperative and in the register of a specification, and it describes the story's world as a set of requirements. It gives itself a title that says „The Official Master Story Guide“ ^[L11]; that is the document's own claim about its standing, recorded here and not applied. Its stance words are `must` ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-the-official-master-story-guide-2.md:#17], `mandatory` ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-the-official-master-story-guide-2.md:#1], `mandates` ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-the-official-master-story-guide-2.md:#2] and `canonical` ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-the-official-master-story-guide-2.md:#3].

- **Opening (L13 to L33), a claim about itself and a plan of the argument.** The guide says it „serves as the canonical blueprint“ ^[L15] for the project. A one-sentence premise follows (L19) and a two-column table sets two kinds of order against each other, `Coherence via Negation` ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-the-official-master-story-guide-2.md:#1] and `Coherence via Integration` ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-the-official-master-story-guide-2.md:#1], one row per property (L29 to L33).
- **Genres (L35 to L46), description.** Four genres are named with a function each; this passage ends with a transition line, and every numbered section ends with one (L46, L71, L104, L144, L166).
- **Laws of the universe (L48 to L71), exposition as design.** The laws are said to be „engineered with strategic intent“ ^[L50]. The two forces of the Primordial Duality are set in a two-column table (L56 to L60), each cell opening with a bold field label.
- **The antagonist (L73 to L102), prescription.** „Under this protocol, AEGIS is to be framed“ ^[L75] as a tragic system, not a villain. L83 sets one sentence apart as the system's core axiom, in bold. The passages on the logic and the transformation (L87 to L102) describe what AEGIS does and what must be evoked.
- **The protagonist (L106 to L142), description and a reference table.** L121 calls the table of eleven alters the „single, canonical reference“ ^[L121] and goes on „This is the ground truth for all narrative and character development“ ^[L121]. That is the document's own authority claim and is recorded, never applied. The table carries a German header word, `Kanonisch` ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-the-official-master-story-guide-2.md:#1].
- **Settings (L146 to L166), description.** The Überwelt (L152) and four Kernwelten in a table (L158 to L164), one row each.
- **Execution (L168 to L204), mandate.** „This section mandates the specific techniques required“ ^[L170]; the two metafictional techniques are called mandatory: „The implementation of the following two techniques is mandatory“ ^[L199].
- **Sample text (L195).** One paragraph of first-person prose is offered as a model, introduced by „The following is the canonical model for this style“ ^[L193]. It is the one place the document writes the novel's voice instead of describing it; „Each part a voice, not in conflict, but as a chord“ ^[L195] stands there. Names that occur only there in the plain narration (the alters Kiko, Lex and Nyx) also occur in the table, so the sample adds no candidate.
- **Diction samples (L181).** German words are given as examples of AEGIS's vocabulary: „partitionieren“ ^[L181] and „autopoietische Selbstorganisation“ ^[L181]. They are diction, not terms the document defines, and are not on the list.

The document names no speaker for any passage and carries no label such as [K] or [V]. Quotation marks in it mark both invented names and quoted phrases of the world (for example the name of the link at L64) and metaphors; nothing announces which.
