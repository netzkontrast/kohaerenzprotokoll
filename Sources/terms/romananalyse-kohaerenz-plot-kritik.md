## Stance, read per passage

The document is a publisher's report written about a manuscript, and its own heading says so: „Analytischer Verlagsbericht“ ^[L11]. It speaks as an evaluator, not as the novel. Its opening passage reports a reading of the work and claims certainty for it: the core can be distilled „mit absoluter Sicherheit destillieren“ ^[L15]. Every world term below is therefore the report's account of the manuscript, not the manuscript's own text, and the report's reference list names its inputs by title ("Kohärenz Protokoll", "Roman: Kohärenz Protokoll", "Charakterkonzepte für Kohärenz Protokoll").

L15 to L19 (Zusammenfassung der Kernprämisse) is a summary: it names Kael, Juna, AEGIS, the Alters, the Kern-Welten and the Guardians, and says of Juna that she stands for the way to integration ^[L19]. L25 to L43 (Philosophische und Physikalische Fundamente) is a report of the science the manuscript is said to use, in the report's voice (Entropie, Landauer, Monster Group, Gödel). L45 to L61 and L63 to L91 are two flattened tables, each introduced by a sentence that says what the table does: „Die folgende Tabelle analysiert die Struktur der Kernwelten“ ^[L49] and „Die nachfolgende Tabelle schlüsselt die komplexe innere Systematik“ ^[L73]. The cells carry glued reference numbers 2 and 3, which point to reference-list entries 2 and 3 (L177, L178), so the cell contents are reported from those texts.

L93 to L99 (Plot-Analyse und Narrativer Bogen) is a retelling in two parts, labelled „Teil 1“ and „Teil 2“ in the heading ^[L93]; its sentences say what the novel does („Der Roman beginnt in der sterilen Konstrukt-Stadt“ ^[L97]). L103 to L113 compares the manuscript with five authors and is the report's market analysis, with no world terms of the manuscript. L117 to L143 is criticism, headed „Strenge Verlags-Kritik und Fehleranalyse“ ^[L117], in four numbered faults (Pacing, emotional distance, didactic reduction, passive protagonist). L145 to L162 is a plan of recommendations: the report says revisions are „werden folgende drastische Überarbeitungen dringend empfohlen“ ^[L147]; these are proposals for the manuscript (cut the prologue, begin with Kael, give Juna her own goals), not statements about it. L164 to L170 is three questions the report puts to the concept, which it says it „wirft abschließend folgende systemische Fragen auf“ ^[L166]; `Externe Ebene` and `Daten-Parasit` stand inside those questions only (L168, L169) and carry no reading.
