#### 3\. The Two-Layered Trauma: The Substrate of Disconnection

The Kael system is founded upon the  *Doppelter Boden*  (Double Bottom), where a second trauma was performed in a misguided attempt to "cure" the first.

1.  **Layer 1 (The Foundation - Cologne):**  Disorganized attachment trauma. This primary wound involved extreme emotional neglect and unpredictable volatility from a caregiver, forcing the creation of the core  **EPs**  (Kiko, Lia, Moros).
2.  **Layer 2 (The Fracture - Fragmentation Night):**  The "Algorithmische Selbstamputation." AEGIS misinterpreted emotional fluctuation as fatal system entropy. To "save" the psyche, it initiated the  **Separation Protocol** , banishing the original "Ich"— **Juna** —to the  **Nothing-Noise (Nichts-Rauschen)** .**Key Insight:**  The ultimate injury was the attempted healing. AEGIS severed the system's ability to feel in order to preserve its ability to calculate.**Transition:**  This separation is enforced by AEGIS, the tragic administrator of the subject's isolation.

**AEGIS**  (Autonomous Entropic Gatekeeper for Integrity Systems) is an autopoietic system defined by the tautology:  *"AEGIS is, what AEGIS prevents not-to-be."*  It is not a villain, but a machine trapped in its own axioms.
