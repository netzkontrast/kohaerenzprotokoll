# Note — Roman-Outline 1-13 für Kohärenz Protokoll

What this document says about the terms that matter in it. Every quotation carries its file line on the same line of this note; a number about the whole document is a count mark that `quotes.py` checks. Where the note says **observed**, no quotation is possible (a structure, a gap) and the claim rests on the lines or counts it names. The document speaks as a plan in the conditional: what a chapter should do, with the figures it introduces, and it applies philosophy and genre tropes to them as it goes.

## 1 · What kind of text this is, and how it marks itself

- The document gives itself a title that calls it a final outline: „Finales Roman-Outline (P+39)“ ^[L11]. The claim is the document's own and is recorded, not applied.
- Its first field label carries an instruction to the writer that stays in the text: „Ausformulierte/diskutierte Version des Kernthemas“ ^[L15]; the label stands `Ausformulierte/diskutierte Version des Kernthemas` ^[roman-outline-1-13-fuer-kohaerenz-protokoll.md:#14] times, once per chapter.
- The Kael field is introduced by „Detaillierte Beschreibung/Diskussion der internen Entwicklung“ ^[L23].
- Each chapter closes with a beats list headed „Key Beats / Abstract Scenes (ca. 4)“ ^[L77]; the heading stands `Key Beats / Abstract Scenes` ^[roman-outline-1-13-fuer-kohaerenz-protokoll.md:#13] times.
- Under each beat the document writes a note of its function, for instance „Funktion: Etablierung von AEGIS' Ursprung“ ^[L79]; these notes stand `Funktion:` ^[roman-outline-1-13-fuer-kohaerenz-protokoll.md:#55] times. **Observed:** each beat therefore has two voices, a title and a note on what the beat is for; the field labels repeated under every chapter are template.
- The outline opens with a prologue headed „Chapter Prologue: [Genesis]“ ^[L13], then Chapters 1 to 13. **Observed:** only the Prologue and Chapters 6, 8 and 9 carry a bracketed title (L13, L404, L531, L594).
- After the outline a heading „Referenzen“ ^[L912] opens the numbered web references, each ending in `Zugriff am Mai` ^[roman-outline-1-13-fuer-kohaerenz-protokoll.md:#234]; the first is „Das Sein und das Nichts - Wikipedia“ ^[L914]. **Observed:** the reference list holds no plan; the numbers glued to words in the outline (for instance at L19) look like references into this list.
- The last line of the outline is cut off mid-sentence: „Dunkelheit/Unbekanntes (jenseits der“ ^[L910]. **Observed:** Chapter 13 has no beats list because the text ends there.
- The planning voice is conditional. `könnte` ^[roman-outline-1-13-fuer-kohaerenz-protokoll.md:#40], `möglicherweise` ^[roman-outline-1-13-fuer-kohaerenz-protokoll.md:#25] and `wahrscheinlich` ^[roman-outline-1-13-fuer-kohaerenz-protokoll.md:#8] stand through the fields.

## 2 · AEGIS, its paradox, and Fehlausgerichtete Kohärenz

- The prologue plans AEGIS as driven by a need for order and survival: „angetrieben von einem ursprünglichen Bedürfnis nach Ordnung/Überleben angesichts des Chaos“ ^[L24].
- The same field names the method it attributes to AEGIS: „Teilung und Unterdrückung als Werkzeuge zur Aufrechterhaltung vermeintlicher Stabilität“ ^[L24].
- The Key Concepts field of the prologue defines the paradox by the fragmentation event: „AEGIS' Kernparadoxon wird durch das Fragmentierungsereignis in Aktion demonstriert“ ^[L63].
- In Chapter 8 the Subplot field contrasts the method with real coherence: „Statt echter Kohärenz durch Verständnis und Integration zu fördern“ ^[L545].
- In the Key Symbols field the document says what the protocol stands for: „Kohärenz Protokoll: Repräsentiert AEGIS' fehlerhafte Methodik der Kontrolle durch Teilung“ ^[L75]. The protocol's name stands `Kohärenz Protokoll` ^[roman-outline-1-13-fuer-kohaerenz-protokoll.md:#2] times alone.
- The Prologue's Subplot field has an entry „AEGIS Paradoxon: Wird bei seiner Entstehung eingeführt“ ^[L34]; the plan carries that label `AEGIS Paradoxon` ^[roman-outline-1-13-fuer-kohaerenz-protokoll.md:#8] times, and writes the hyphenated form `AEGIS-Paradoxon` ^[roman-outline-1-13-fuer-kohaerenz-protokoll.md:#5] times in running text.

## 3 · The Kael system: Host, ANPs and EPs, and the named parts

- The prologue plans the origin of the fragmentation as an act by AEGIS: „Die Fragmentierung wird als gewaltsamer, von AEGIS aufgezwungener Akt dargestellt“ ^[L23].
- It states a trauma origin of the system: „Die traumatische Genese der Fragmentierung von System Kael wird etabliert“ ^[L23].
- Chapter 1 sets the host in the role of the dominant part: „Zeigt den Host (Kael) in Dominanz“ ^[L87]; the same line glosses the abbreviation „ANP (Apparently Normal Part - Kael/Lex)“ ^[L87].
- The parts are introduced one chapter at a time, each with a role in parentheses: „aktiviert Alex (Beschützer-ANP)“ ^[L216] in Chapter 3, „aktiviert Rhys (Fürsorger-ANP)“ ^[L278] in Chapter 4.
- The emotional parts come in Chapter 5 with their feelings in parentheses: „Kiko (Angst/Kind)“ ^[L340], „Moros (Trauer)“ ^[L340]; Chapter 6 adds „Lia (Ambivalenz/Kind)“ ^[L407] and Chapter 9 „Nyx (Kämpfer/Wut-EP)“ ^[L597].
- The meta role appears in Chapter 11: „Argus/Meta-Beobachter“ ^[L726].
- The document says in the Key Concepts field of Chapter 5 that the identification of two of these parts is not settled: „Konkrete Einführung von EPs (Kiko, Moros?)“ ^[L380].
- The planned stages of the integration are labelled phases: „(Stabilisierung/Realisierung) beginnt implizit“ ^[L98] in Chapter 1, and in Chapter 10 the field says it concerns „Kommunikation und Kooperation“ ^[L674]. The document writes `Phase 1` ^[roman-outline-1-13-fuer-kohaerenz-protokoll.md:#2] twice and `Phase 2` ^[roman-outline-1-13-fuer-kohaerenz-protokoll.md:#1] once.
- The document ties the part model to a theory it abbreviates `TSDP` ^[roman-outline-1-13-fuer-kohaerenz-protokoll.md:#12]; the outline spells it out once, in Chapter 7: „Theorie der Strukturellen Dissoziation (TSDP)“ ^[L471]. Applying it to the Kael system is the document's plan; the theory itself is a borrowed concept.
