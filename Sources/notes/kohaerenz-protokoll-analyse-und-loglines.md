# Note — Kohärenz Protokoll: Analyse und Loglines

What this document says about the terms that matter in it. Every quotation carries its file line on the same line of this note; a number about the whole document is a count mark that `quotes.py` checks. Where the note says **observed**, no quotation is possible and the claim rests on the lines or counts it names. The document speaks as an analyst: it restates the concept, criticises it and proposes; the loglines are proposals, not written chapters.

## 2 · AEGIS

- The concept line gives AEGIS as a system: „Die KI AEGIS (S) existiert als autopoietisches System“ ^[L25].
- The analysis qualifies this: „AEGIS könnte eher einem hochkomplexen kybernetischen Kontrollsystem ähneln“ ^[L29].
- The document suggests a shortfall: „Komplexität erreicht möglicherweise nicht die notwendige“ ^[L28] variety, and ties it to „Diese Lücke könnte die Quelle des Paradoxon X sein“ ^[L28].
