- It names itself an outline with a two-storyform encoding: „Dual-Storyform-Encoding“ ^[L11].
- It opens with a blurb addressed to the reader: „Stell dir vor, du erwachst in einer makellos geometrischen Metropole“ ^[L15].
- It divides the 39 chapters into three acts; the first is introduced as „Akt I — Kapitel 1–13“ ^[L19].
- Every chapter has the same fields: `Worum geht es` ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-kapitel-outline-erstellung.md:#39], `Was passiert` ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-kapitel-outline-erstellung.md:#39] and `Foreshadowing / Pacing / Reader-Substrate` ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-kapitel-outline-erstellung.md:#39].

- Two encodings follow in every chapter, `Encoding Storyform A` ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-kapitel-outline-erstellung.md:#39] titled `Heuristics of Integration` ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-kapitel-outline-erstellung.md:#39] and `Encoding Storyform B` ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-kapitel-outline-erstellung.md:#39] titled `Phoenix Collapse` ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-kapitel-outline-erstellung.md:#39].
