## 4 · The protocols (section IV.B)

- „Zero-Trust Execution Model (ZTEM)“ ^[L164]: every entity is treated as not trustworthy by default.
- „Real-time Self-Verification (RTSV)“ ^[L165]: the recursive self-verification, called „der Kern der Kohärenz-Wahrung“ ^[L165].
- „Behavioral Proof-of-Function (BPoF)“ ^[L166]: access rights follow the demonstrated function of an entity.
- „Encrypted Intent Channels (EIC)“ ^[L167]: communication between entities such as the Guardians, where communication is itself under coherence checks.
- „Secure Isolation State (SIS) Protocol“ ^[L171]: a failover that isolates inconsistent areas or entities.
- „Controlled Interface Protocols“ ^[L172]: regulate interaction between the Überwelt and other levels such as the Kern-Welten.
- Three more are listed as bullets without an acronym: „Integrity Validation Protocols“ ^[L168], „Role-Based Access & Function Protocols“ ^[L169] and „Entropic Management Protocols“ ^[L170].
- The interplay is stated in one sentence: „RTSV liefert die Daten für BPoF-Entscheidungen“ ^[L174].
