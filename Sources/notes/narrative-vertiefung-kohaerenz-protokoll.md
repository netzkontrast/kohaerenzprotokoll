## 2 · AEGIS

The line closes with the label `User Query`, so it restates the plot concept. The word `Kohärenz-Controller` ^[narrative-vertiefung-kohaerenz-protokoll.md:#2] stands twice (L83 and L348).
- The report's own application of the self-observation idea: „AEGIS ist somit der Beobachter“ ^[L71]; the sentence goes on to say the observer stands inside its own analytical system.
