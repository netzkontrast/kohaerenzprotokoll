## 2 · AEGIS

- The dossier expands the name: „Autonomous Entropic Gatekeeper for Integrity Systems“ ^[L25] (`Autonomous Entropic Gatekeeper for Integrity Systems` ^[character-dossier-the-opposing-logics-of-kael-and-aegis.md:#1]).
- Its core principle is given in German, in the system's voice: „AEGIS ist, was AEGIS verhindert, dass es nicht ist.“ ^[L27]
- The dossier says of the system: „its ultimate and overriding goal is its own preservation“ ^[L29].
- It calls the system operationally closed: „its functions refer only to its own internal states“ ^[L29] (`operationally closed` ^[character-dossier-the-opposing-logics-of-kael-and-aegis.md:#1]).
- Of the world it says: „it only registers external data as“ ^[L29] perturbations (`perturbations` ^[character-dossier-the-opposing-logics-of-kael-and-aegis.md:#1]).

- The system's worldview is a binary code: it translates phenomena into `coherent` ^[character-dossier-the-opposing-logics-of-kael-and-aegis.md:#2] or `incoherent` ^[character-dossier-the-opposing-logics-of-kael-and-aegis.md:#2], based on „a pathologically narrow set of relevance criteria“ ^[L33].

- The flawed reading of the goal is given as „eliminate all unpredictable data“ ^[L45].
- Faced with Kael, the dossier says AEGIS „calculates that the most logical path“ ^[L46] is to fragment and destroy him.

## 3 · Kael

- Section 3 says Kael's arc moves „from a state of internal fragmentation toward a resilient, integrated wholeness“ ^[L54].
- The dossier reads his initial state through a named theory: `Theory of Structural Dissociation of the Personality` ^[character-dossier-the-opposing-logics-of-kael-and-aegis.md:#1], and says „his personality has split into different, semi-autonomous parts“ ^[L58].
- The table sets two part types, `ANPs` ^[character-dossier-the-opposing-logics-of-kael-and-aegis.md:#3] (`Apparently Normal Parts` ^[character-dossier-the-opposing-logics-of-kael-and-aegis.md:#1]) and `EPs` ^[character-dossier-the-opposing-logics-of-kael-and-aegis.md:#3] (`Emotional Parts` ^[character-dossier-the-opposing-logics-of-kael-and-aegis.md:#1]); the EP row says they „are stuck in“ ^[L64] trauma time.
- His goal is „functional multiplicity,“ ^[L70] defined by three labelled marks, among them „Co-consciousness: The different parts become aware“ ^[L72] and „Cooperation: The parts learn to communicate“ ^[L73].
- The healing path ends in a `rhizomatic self` ^[character-dossier-the-opposing-logics-of-kael-and-aegis.md:#1], which the dossier likens to a concept „from Deleuze and Guattari“ ^[L76] and sets against a hierarchical „tree“ model.

## 4 · The comparison and the Gödel Gambit
