## 2 · The Protocol Ontology and the two kernels

- The paper says of the Protocol Ontology: „This ontology serves as the fundamental "physics" of the story's universe“ ^[L19].
- It says the conflict at the heart of the universe is „defined by the Dual Kernel Theory (DKT)“ ^[L23].
- It describes `Coherence Kernel (K₁)` as „the principle of order, information preservation, and structure“ ^[L25] and `Collapse Kernel (K₀)` as „the principle of entropy, information erasure, and irreversible change“ ^[L26].
- It says K₀ „is not a malevolent force but a necessary and generative one“ ^[L26], and that its function is „directly analogous to quantum decoherence“ ^[L26] — the analogy is the paper's own.
- Its central axiom is that there are no static things: „The central axiom of the Protocol Ontology is that there are no static "things," only dynamic, self-maintaining "Protocols."“ ^[L30].
- It treats persistence as work: „persistence is an active, energy-intensive process“ ^[L36].
- `Overhead` is defined as „the continuous energy cost required to maintain internal coherence“ ^[L38], and the paper says the concept is drawn from „cache coherence protocols“ ^[L38].
- `Corrective Wavelets` are „the physical mechanism of self-repair“ ^[L39].
- `Das Fundament` „is not an entity, a place, or a deus ex machina“ ^[L43]; the paper says its laws „inherently favor the emergence of“ ^[L43] high-Φ states, and that AEGIS „remains a low-Φ“ ^[L43] zombie system.

## 3 · The two models of truth, and the Validation War

- The paper conceives the plot as „a dramatized philosophical argument“ ^[L51].
- AEGIS operates on the Coherence Theory of Truth; the paper says what does not conform to its model „is pathologically misidentified as“ ^[L57] entropy, noise or hostile error.
- Kael's healing is „a forced and painful evolution toward the“ ^[L61] Correspondence Theory of Truth.
- The plot „is therefore framed as a“ ^[L67] `Validation War`.
- Of the `Risse` the paper says: „These are not random glitches“ ^[L69]; they are manifestations of correspondence errors.
