- The document says the term does not name a machine: „does not refer to a computer or a network“ ^[L19].

- It describes the internal landscape as „a complex society trying to govern itself“ ^[L19]; `Society of Self` ^[the-minds-behind-the-machine-a-psychological-guide-to-system.md:#2] is its name for that society.
