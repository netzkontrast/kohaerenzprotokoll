## 2 · The two theories and the two sides

- In its table AEGIS is given the column heading „AEGIS: Coherence“ ^[L25] and its cell reads „Embodies the principle that truth is“ ^[L26] internal logical consistency, set in bold in the cell.
- AEGIS is said to reject contradictory data by „classifying it as hostile“ ^[L27] entropy, and the table gives it the name „Autonomous Entropic Gatekeeper“ ^[L27].
- The table says the system is vulnerable to the „isolation objection“ ^[L28], and that in the narrative these are the „Risse“ ^[L28] (rifts) where reality breaks through.

## 5 · Four meanings of correspondence

- The document says it draws on four fields: „four distinct intellectual domains“ ^[L58].
- Philosophical: Kael makes his inner understanding match the facts of his trauma; the text says the theory is „the primary driver of the plot“ ^[L62].
- Journalistic: `Juna/V` is said to function as „a journalistic correspondent“ ^[L66], also „an exiled part of Kael's own“ ^[L66] origin-self, reaching him through the „Moonshine-Link“ ^[L66], which gives „gnosis“ ^[L66] and not `episteme`.
- Physical: Kael's arc is „a narrative metaphor for Niels Bohr's“ ^[L70] `Correspondence Principle`. The document says his final self is a „dialetheic mind“ ^[L70].
- Mathematical: the healed identity is modelled on a correspondence, and the document writes that a correspondence maps „a single input to a“ ^[L74] set of outputs, which it calls „functional multiplicity“ ^[L74].

Each part carries a code: `KW1`, `Co₁`, `KW2`, `McL`, `KW3`, `KW4`, `Ly` (L85 to L88).
