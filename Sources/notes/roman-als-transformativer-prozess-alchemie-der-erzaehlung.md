## 2 · Kael

- The document sets Kael's starting state as a condition named in its own quotation marks: „tertiären strukturellen Dissoziation“ ^[L32].
- It lists Kael's parts: „fragmentierten Persönlichkeitsanteile (Lex, Nyx, Kiko usw.)“ ^[L32].
- It says the healed Kael „wird zu einem lebendigen Archetyp“ ^[L38] of what it calls the Selbst.
