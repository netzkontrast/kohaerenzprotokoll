# Note — Report: Narrative Methodology for "Kohärenz Protokoll"

What this document says about the terms that matter in it. Every quotation carries its file line on the same line of this note; a number about the whole document is a count mark that `quotes.py` checks. Where the note says **observed**, no quotation is possible and the claim rests on the lines or counts it names. The document speaks in directives to the writer ("must"), not as a story; the two labels `Function:` ^[report-narrative-methodology-for-kohaerenz-protokoll.md:#4] and `Voice:` ^[report-narrative-methodology-for-kohaerenz-protokoll.md:#3] fill the same template fields in the perspective table, and together they are the 7 stance markers counted above.

## 1 · What kind of text this is, and how it marks itself

- The document says what it is for: „a set of practical, actionable instructions“ ^[L17].
- It names its own standing: „This document serves as the foundational blueprint for that architecture“ ^[L17]. This is recorded as the document's claim about itself and decides nothing.
- Its method is that the form performs the theme: „the novel's form must not merely describe its themes but actively perform them“ ^[L17].
- The second section opens the same idea with „the prose is not merely descriptive but performative“ ^[L21].

## 2 · The voice of AEGIS

- The document derives the voice from the nature it gives AEGIS: „AEGIS’s narrative voice must be a direct expression of its nature as an information-based system driven by classical logic“ ^[L25].
- It also states the nature as „autopoietic, operationally closed“ ^[L25]; the German `autopoietische Selbstorganisation` ^[report-narrative-methodology-for-kohaerenz-protokoll.md:#1] is one of the words the voice should use.
- The directives are bold-labelled: „Impersonal and Technical Diction“ ^[L27], and the tone is „strictly objective, analytical, and distantiated“ ^[L28].
- The intended effect carries a name in quotation marks, `Algorithmic Horror` ^[report-narrative-methodology-for-kohaerenz-protokoll.md:#3], which the document defines in one sentence: „the uncanny and oppressive feeling of a world governed by an invisible, inscrutable, and fundamentally inhuman logic“ ^[L30].

## 3 · The voice of Kael: Dissoziation and Polyphony

- The document describes an arc: „a state of Tertiäre Strukturelle Dissoziation (TSDP)“ ^[L34] to a „functional, co-conscious multiplicity“ ^[L34]. The abbreviation `TSDP` ^[report-narrative-methodology-for-kohaerenz-protokoll.md:#1] is given once and not explained further.
- The fragmented state is headed „The Fragmented Voice (Dissoziation)“ ^[L36] and the integrated one „The Integrated Voice (Polyphony)“ ^[L44]; the latter is to become a „"polyphonic" or "choric" style“ ^[L46].
- The fragmented voice is explained through parts: „the Apparently Normal Parts (ANPs)“ ^[L42] and „the Emotional Parts (EPs)“ ^[L42], the latter holding „unprocessed trauma“ ^[L42].
- Named parts appear in the sample sentence and the rhythm directive: „Kiko’s dread“ ^[L48] and „The analytical, hypotactic rhythm of Lex“ ^[L49], with Nyx given „visceral, staccato phrases“ ^[L49]. The document gives no further account of who Lex, Nyx or Kiko are, beyond calling Lex „Kael's analytical part“ ^[L80].
- The integrated voice is tied to a measure: „high integrated information (high Φ)“ ^[L51]; it is contrasted with „AEGIS's collection of firewalled, low-Φ subsystems“ ^[L51]. Observed: the export keeps the glyph Φ as a character; the document does not define Φ.

## 4 · Perspectives and the epistemology-shift

- The shifting of perspective is the move „from AEGIS's externalized, systemic view of reality“ ^[L55], which the document calls an `epistemology-shift` ^[report-narrative-methodology-for-kohaerenz-protokoll.md:#1].
- The table lists three perspectives; the third is „Meta-Narrator (Unreliable Omniscient)“ ^[L64], whose voice makes „second-order cybernetics (Kybernetik zweiter Ordnung)“ ^[L64] part of the reading. Both `Kybernetik zweiter Ordnung` ^[report-narrative-methodology-for-kohaerenz-protokoll.md:#1] and the English form stand once.
- AEGIS in the table may be unreliable: „AEGIS may also function as an unreliable narrator“ ^[L63].
- A "system correction" is the example of an event told twice: „an objective log entry from AEGIS detailing a "system correction"“ ^[L68] against Kael's fragmented experience of it.

## 5 · The Guardians

- Their role is stated as: „do not function as independent actors“ ^[L72], and they are „functional manifestations of AEGIS's core logic“ ^[L72].
- The first principle is „Embodiment of AEGIS's Logic“ ^[L78]; the second says they „operate within the immutable system of laws that AEGIS represents“ ^[L79]. The word `Guardians` ^[report-narrative-methodology-for-kohaerenz-protokoll.md:#5] stands in the body.
- The one named example: „a confrontation with the Guardian of KW1 (Logik)“ ^[L80] is „a targeted assault on the logical frameworks of Kael's analytical part, Lex“ ^[L80]. `KW1` ^[report-narrative-methodology-for-kohaerenz-protokoll.md:#1] is the only Kernwelt label the document writes.
