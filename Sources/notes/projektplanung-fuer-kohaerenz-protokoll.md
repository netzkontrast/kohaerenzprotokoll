- The document says the world is governed by „fundamentalen und ewigen Konflikt zwischen zwei Prinzipien“ ^[L29], named `Kohärenz` and `Kollaps` with the symbols `K₁` ^[projektplanung-fuer-kohaerenz-protokoll.md:#2] and `K₀` ^[projektplanung-fuer-kohaerenz-protokoll.md:#4], and calls it the `Dual Kernel Theory` ^[projektplanung-fuer-kohaerenz-protokoll.md:#2].
