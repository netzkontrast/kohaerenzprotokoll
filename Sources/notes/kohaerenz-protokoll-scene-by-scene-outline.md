## 2 · AEGIS

- The lexicon expands the name: „An acronym for“ ^[L563] the phrase `Autonomous Entropic Gatekeeper for Integrity Systems` ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-scene-by-scene-outline.md:#1].
- The analysis says „AEGIS is not a malevolent villain“ ^[L506].
- Its principles are listed as four labelled items: „Prioritizes internal consistency over external truth“ ^[L510] under `Kohärenz statt Wahrheit`, „Defines its existence by what it is“ ^[L511] under `Emergenz durch Negation`, „Relentlessly verifies its own internal structure“ ^[L512] under `No-Trust & Rekursive Selbstverifikation`, and „Strives for complete self-definition“ ^[L513] under `Ontologische Autarkie`.
- Its architecture, as the analysis writes it: its core runs on `Logics of Formal Inconsistency (LFI)` ^[L515], and a `Discursive Logic (D2)` module handles Kael's psyche by „treating his alters as separate speakers in a contained debate“ ^[L515].
- In the outline, AEGIS's prime directive is quoted as „Its prime directive, “Maximize Coherence,” is translated by its flawed logic“ ^[L239]; the quoted directive names stand in the outline's own quotation marks.
- AEGIS appears as a speaking position in the plan: the scene's POV field reads „AEGIS (Log Format)“ ^[L194], and at the climax it holds the axiom „Coherence arises from the elimination of contradiction“ ^[L354], which the outline has the scene break.
- Its end state is written twice, as „an “algorithmic melancholy”“ ^[L371] in the outline and as `algorithmischer Melancholie` ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-scene-by-scene-outline.md:#1] in the analysis, where it is „forever excluded from its meaning or experience“ ^[L517].
