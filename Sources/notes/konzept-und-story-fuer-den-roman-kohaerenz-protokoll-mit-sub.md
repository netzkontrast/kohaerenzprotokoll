# Note — Konzept und Story für den Roman Kohärenz Protokoll (mit Subplots)

What this document says about the terms that matter in it. Every quotation carries its file line on the same line of this note; a number about the whole document is a count mark that `quotes.py` checks. Where the note says **observed**, no quotation is possible (a structure, a gap) and the claim rests on the lines or counts it names. The document is a plan in one voice and marks nothing as report, lock or question; chapter summaries are the document's proposals, and the later ones are hedged.

## 1 · What kind of text this is, and how it marks itself

- The document states its own purpose: „legt das detaillierte Konzept und die Storyline für den Roman“ ^[L13].
- It gives its basis as the theory of structural dissociation, named in the same sentence: „Basierend auf der Theorie der Strukturellen Dissoziation (TSDP)“ ^[L13].
- Its repeated label is the bold tag `Subplot` ^[konzept-und-story-fuer-den-roman-kohaerenz-protokoll-mit-sub.md:#80] after a chapter's summary, naming which of the five arcs the chapter serves. **Observed:** the tag stands on the chapter entries L27 to L75 and is how the document says what a passage is for.
- The chapter entries hedge more as they go: `könnte` ^[konzept-und-story-fuer-den-roman-kohaerenz-protokoll-mit-sub.md:#6], `möglicherweise` ^[konzept-und-story-fuer-den-roman-kohaerenz-protokoll-mit-sub.md:#4] and `vielleicht` ^[konzept-und-story-fuer-den-roman-kohaerenz-protokoll-mit-sub.md:#2]. One example is a plan offered as an option: „könnten hier als Foreshadowing dienen“ ^[L37].
- The closing line restates the scope: „Dieses Konzept bietet einen detaillierten Rahmen“ ^[L88]. **Observed:** the document makes no claim of canon, lock or sync and names no other document.

## 2 · AEGIS and Kohärenz

- The document spells the name out once: `Autonomous Entropic Gatekeeper for Integrity Systems` ^[konzept-und-story-fuer-den-roman-kohaerenz-protokoll-mit-sub.md:#1], and uses `AEGIS` ^[konzept-und-story-fuer-den-roman-kohaerenz-protokoll-mit-sub.md:#52] for the rest.
- In the introduction AEGIS' aim is given as „Aufrechterhaltung von“ ^[L17] Kohärenz, and Kohärenz as „verstanden als Stabilität, Ordnung und Vorhersagbarkeit“ ^[L17].
- The document says AEGIS reads Kael's psyche and the external connection as danger: „als Quellen gefährlicher Entropie“ ^[L17].
- In the themes list AEGIS is „der primäre Antagonist“ ^[L80], moved by its `Kernparadoxon` ^[konzept-und-story-fuer-den-roman-kohaerenz-protokoll-mit-sub.md:#1].
- The paradox has a name in quotation marks at L48 and again, in the genitive, at L72: `Paradoxon der Fehlausgerichteten Kohärenz` ^[konzept-und-story-fuer-den-roman-kohaerenz-protokoll-mit-sub.md:#1] and `Paradoxons der Fehlausgerichteten Kohärenz` ^[konzept-und-story-fuer-den-roman-kohaerenz-protokoll-mit-sub.md:#1].
