## 1 · What kind of text this is, and how it marks itself

- The document gives itself a standing: „the canonical source of truth from which all narrative execution must proceed“ ^[L15]. This is its own claim, recorded and not applied. `canonical source of truth` ^[the-coherence-protocol-a-definitive-guide-to-the-narrative-a.md:#1]

- **Observed:** the sections are numbered 1.0 to 5.4 and each opens with a short statement of purpose; the voice is that of a guide addressed to a writer, with no date and no label that marks a passage as a question or a report (L13 to L174).

## 2 · The two kernels and the cycle of existence

- The Coherence Kernel (K₁) is described as „the principle of order, structure, and information preservation“ ^[L31].
- The Collapse Kernel (K₀) is described as „the principle of entropy, information erasure, and irreversible change“ ^[L32]. The same line says: „Its function is explicitly analogous to the physical process of quantum decoherence.“ ^[L32]
- The pair is traced to a theory the document names: „as described by the speculative Dual Kernel Theory (DKT)“ ^[L29].
- The cycle is written as „Intrusion (K₀) → Korrektur (K₁) → Rekonfiguration (Emergence)“ ^[L34], which the document introduces with „Existence is defined by a perpetual cycle“ ^[L34].
- The document never writes the subscripted forms with plain digits; it keeps `K₁` ^[the-coherence-protocol-a-definitive-guide-to-the-narrative-a.md:#8] and `K₀` ^[the-coherence-protocol-a-definitive-guide-to-the-narrative-a.md:#9] as subscripts.

## 3 · Protocols, Overhead and the lexicon

- Identity is defined by the protocol: „the identity of the ship lies not in its material substance“ ^[L40], offered as a solution of the Ship of Theseus paradox.
- The cost of persistence is `Overhead` ^[the-coherence-protocol-a-definitive-guide-to-the-narrative-a.md:#5]; the line says: „A protocol collapses when the energy cost of its Overhead exceeds the resources it can acquire.“ ^[L46]
- The lexicon table is announced as „This table serves as a definitive glossary for the core metaphysical terms of the universe.“ ^[L51] **Observed:** its seven rows (L56 to L62) are Protocol, Coheron, Coherence (K₁), Collapse (K₀), Overhead, Corrective Wavelet and Correspondence-Check.
