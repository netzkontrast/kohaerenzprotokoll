# Note — Kohärenz Protokoll: Plot-Entwicklung und Wahrheitsdualität

What this document says about the terms that matter in it. Every quotation carries its file line on the same line of this note; a number about the whole document is a count mark that `quotes.py` checks. ## 1 · What kind of text this is, and how it marks itself

- The report names itself „strategisches Entwicklungspapier“ ^[L19].
- It gives its method as „dialektische Analyse der vorliegenden Entwicklungsdokumente“ ^[L21].
- Its labels repeat under every recommendation: `Empfehlung` ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-plot-entwicklung-und-wahrheitsdualitaet.md:#13], `Analyse` ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-plot-entwicklung-und-wahrheitsdualitaet.md:#13], `Begründung` ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-plot-entwicklung-und-wahrheitsdualitaet.md:#5] and, under the four questions of Part 3.2, `Antwort des Romans` ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-plot-entwicklung-und-wahrheitsdualitaet.md:#4]. These are the report's own template, which together make the stance marker count of 35 (13 plus 13 plus 5 plus 4).
- **Observed:** the report is in three parts (L27, L149, L246) and ends with thirteen numbered references (L336 to L350). The body cites them as bare digits glued to words, so a claim that carries one is the report's reading of that referenced document.

## 2 · The thesis, and the commission

- The report names its commission „Mandat“ ^[L19] and its thesis „Die Kernthese dieses Berichts ist wie folgt“ ^[L23].
- The thesis says the project should release itself from the „Glitch“-Plot and join two axes. The line gives the psychological axis as „psychologischen Achse“ ^[L23] and the philosophical one as „philosophischen Achse“ ^[L23].
- The phrase `Dualität der Wahrheit` ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-plot-entwicklung-und-wahrheitsdualitaet.md:#9] is the commission's own, quoted by the report.

## 3 · System Kael and the parts

- The report grounds Kael in a clinical theory: „Theorie der Strukturellen Dissoziation der Persönlichkeit“ ^[L49].
- It divides Kael's parts into two types. The Apparently Normal Parts it describes as „auf die Bewältigung des Alltagslebens fokussiert“ ^[L51].
- The Emotional Parts, in the report's words, „halten die rohen, unverarbeiteten und unbestreitbaren Fakten des Traumas“ ^[L52].
- Table 1 lists nine parts beside the host: the five ANPs are Lex, Alex, Rhys, Argus and Selene; the four EPs are Kiko, Lia, Moros and Nyx. **Observed** in L63 to L72. The cell for Selene reads „ANP (Integrator?)“ ^[L72], so the document marks her type as uncertain.
- The report adds a column that it says it adds itself: „Philosophische Entsprechung“ ^[L58].

## 4 · The two truth theories and AEGIS

- The report sets AEGIS against Kael as two truth theories: „Der Plot ist ein physischer Krieg zwischen zwei Wahrheitstheorien“ ^[L80].
- AEGIS is placed on the coherence side: „AEGIS (Kohärenztheorie)“ ^[L82].
- AEGIS' tragic fault is named as „Paradoxon der Fehlausgerichteten Kohärenz“ ^[L94], also written „Paradoxon X“ ^[L94].
- The „Risse“ ^[L83] are described as the physical form of the isolation objection. That claim is made by the report with a reference number to another document.
- The Protokoll-Ontologie is introduced as „definiert die Physik der Romanwelt“ ^[L91].
- The kernels `K\_1` ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-plot-entwicklung-und-wahrheitsdualitaet.md:#3] and `K\_0` ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-plot-entwicklung-und-wahrheitsdualitaet.md:#4] are written with an escaped underscore; the document never writes a subscript.

## 5 · The plot split, and the verdict

- The report says its sources describe two novels: „Die Dokumente beschreiben zwei fundamental unterschiedliche Romane“ ^[L107].
- Plot A is the psychological plot with the protagonist System Kael. Plot B is the physical plot with the protagonist Dr. Aris Thorne and the antagonist Silus. **Observed** in L109 and L110.
- The report decides between them on its own authority: „Daher wird Plot A als das kanonische Fundament für alle folgenden Entwicklungen dieses Berichts festgelegt.“ ^[L112] This is the report's claim about its own decision, recorded and not applied.

## 6 · The new plot, "Das Parakonsistente Gambit"

- The plot is headed „Der neue, integrierte Plot-Entwurf“ ^[L181].
- The three acts carry the labels „Die Kohärente Lüge“ ^[L189], „Die Chaotische Wahrheit“ ^[L200] and „Die Integrierte Wahrheit“ ^[L211], each paired with a phase of the treatment.
- The report defines Kael's final state as a synthesis: „Er ist die lebende Synthese von Kohärenz“ ^[L216].
- In the first beat of the climax Kael's bare existence is called „lebender Gödel-Satz“ ^[L221].
- The resolution is chosen, not found: „Wir wählen die thematisch stärkere Option“ ^[L226].
- The ending of AEGIS is the `Algorithmische Melancholie` ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-plot-entwicklung-und-wahrheitsdualitaet.md:#3], and of AEGIS in that state the line says „Es ist in einer ewigen, sinnlosen Berechnung gefangen“ ^[L227].

## 7 · The core worlds and the guardians

- The four core worlds are introduced as „Die vier Kernwelten“ ^[L235] and given as KW1 to KW4 in L237 to L240.
- Each world has a keeper named in the list: LogOS for `KW1` ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-plot-entwicklung-und-wahrheitsdualitaet.md:#5], Mnemosyne for `KW2` ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-plot-entwicklung-und-wahrheitsdualitaet.md:#4], Cerberus for `KW3` ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-plot-entwicklung-und-wahrheitsdualitaet.md:#3] and Kairos/Sophia for `KW4` ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-plot-entwicklung-und-wahrheitsdualitaet.md:#2]. The text calls them „Wächter“ ^[L237].

## 8 · Recommendations and questions

- Recommendation 1 proposes a hybrid structure: „Ein hybrides "Mosaik-Monolith"-Modell anwenden.“ ^[L260]
- Recommendation 3 makes Juna/V an inner figure: „als exilierter Teil von Kaels eigenem“ ^[L285].
- Recommendation 2 chooses the melancholy ending: „ist die thematisch bei weitem überlegene Auflösung“ ^[L279]
- Part 3.2 answers the central question: „Reine Kohärenz ist eine sterile, leblose Lüge“ ^[L319].

## 9 · Said two ways, or left open — recorded, not resolved

- The report sets out, with a reference number to another document glued to each, two hypotheses for AEGIS' fate: „AEGIS entwickelt eine parakonsistente Logik“ ^[L122] under Hypothese A and „AEGIS' binäre Logik zerbricht am Paradoxon“ ^[L123] under Hypothese B. The report itself closes the choice for A, but it is the report's decision.
- It also gives two climax approaches, A and B (L131 and L132); B is the one with „Kael verlässt die AEGIS-Realität“ ^[L132].
- The document writes „Kohärenzprotokoll“ ^[L206] once as one word and `Kohärenz Protokoll` ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-plot-entwicklung-und-wahrheitsdualitaet.md:#10] otherwise.
