## 2 · Monstergruppe, Happy Family, Pariahs

- The document reports the object as „ein zentrales Objekt der modernen Gruppentheorie“ ^[L19], also known as „Fischer-Griess-Monster“ ^[L19].
- It reports the sporadic groups split into a family inside the object and outsiders: the line says the remaining sporadic groups „keine Subquotienten von M sind“ ^[L40].
- It reads that split as a metaphor: the group could represent „integrierte versus dissoziierte Teile des Selbst“ ^[L106].
- It turns the split into a world-building model: „Man könnte verschachtelte Realitäten entwerfen“ ^[L164].
- Its question list asks how the distinction „zwischen der „Happy Family“ und den „Pariahs“ ^[L247] might be translated into social, political, psychological or cosmological terms.

## 3 · Mondschein

- It reports the phenomenon as „die überraschende Beziehung zwischen den Dimensionen der irreduziblen Darstellungen“ ^[L68] of the group and a function's coefficients, and says the name `Moonshine` was coined to express a „verrückte“ ^[L68] connection.
- It reports a proof of the conjecture by a mathematician in 1992 and lists the structures used ^[L88].
- It reads the phenomenon as theme: it demonstrates „eine tiefe, unerwartete Kohärenz“ ^[L94].
- In section IV it calls the phenomenon a metaphor for „die psychologische Suche nach Integration, Sinn und Kohärenz“ ^[L118].
- It poses an open question about discovery versus construction: „Erklärt die VOA die Verbindung“ ^[L120] or is it the connection.
- It proposes a character device: „Ein Charakter könnte eine plötzliche, weltverändernde Einsicht erleben“ ^[L171], a personal `monströser Mondschein` in its words.
- A later question asks what triggers the moment: „Was löst den „Mondschein-Moment“ der Erkenntnis“ ^[L288].

## 4 · Leere außerhalb des Seins

- The introduction frames the project „im Kontext der philosophischen Idee einer“ void outside being ^[L15].
- Section V opens by calling the idea „tief in philosophischen Traditionen verwurzelt“ ^[L128].
- It reports two readings of the void, as source and as annihilation: „Quelle der Potenzialität“ ^[L151] and „Reine Negation/Annihilation“ ^[L152]; both are reports of traditions.
- It sets the group against the void: „Struktur gegen Formlosigkeit“ ^[L144], and says the placement creates „eine inhärente ontologische Spannung“ ^[L144].
- It proposes a narrative image: the group could be shown as „eine komplexe „Kristallisation““ ^[L154] out of the void's potentiality, or as a bulwark against annihilation.
- It asks where the void lives in the narrative: the void can be shown as a place, a mental state or a concept ^[L165], and the questions repeat this ^[L248].

## 5 · Psychological readings

- The document maps the group's size to the psyche: it can serve as a metaphor for „die immense Weite und die vielschichtige Komplexität“ ^[L104].
- It reads simplicity as a core: „ein grundlegendes, irreduzibles Kern-Selbst oder eine Kernidentität“ ^[L110].
- It reads the difficulty of representing the group as the unconscious ^[L114].
- Its section on the name „Monster“ treats inner monsters as trauma, shadow aspects and fear: the name „Monster“ ^[L124] invites confrontation with them.

## 6 · Lessons and questions for the project

- It draws the lesson that abstract concepts must be grounded: „Abstrakte Konzepte, egal wie faszinierend“ ^[L233] need character experience.
- It states the creative task: „Die kreative Herausforderung besteht darin“ ^[L294] to turn the abstract ideas into a human story.
- Its question groups name the central conflict between human characters and „der perfekten, unermesslichen, zeitlosen Logik der Monstergruppe“ ^[L268].

## 7 · Said two ways, or left open — recorded, not resolved
