# Note — Kael System: TSDP-Analyse und Profile

What this document says about the terms that matter in it. Every quotation carries its file line on the same line of this note; a number about the whole document is a count mark that `quotes.py` checks. Where the note says **observed**, no quotation is possible (a structure, a gap) and the claim rests on the lines or counts it names. The document speaks as an analyst of the novel's material, not as the novel: it hedges its readings of the parts and cites the user's request and a research context as its sources for the world.

## 1 · What kind of text this is, and how it marks itself

- The document names its own kind in the first sentence of its introduction: „Dieser Bericht legt eine tiefgehende Analyse“ ^[L15] of the parts and the novel project. The word `Bericht` ^[kael-system-tsdp-analyse-und-profile.md:#2] stands twice in the document.
- It states its own four-part plan: „Teil 3 präsentiert umfassend ausgearbeitete“ ^[L19] profiles, one of the four parts it lists.
- Its inference marker is the hedge, not a label: `wahrscheinlich` ^[kael-system-tsdp-analyse-und-profile.md:#46], `möglicherweise` ^[kael-system-tsdp-analyse-und-profile.md:#19] and `könnte` ^[kael-system-tsdp-analyse-und-profile.md:#60]. **Observed:** the readings of what a part fears or does are mostly stated in these forms, for example „Konkret dürfte er Angst vor“ ^[L34] Nyx and others at Kael.
- It points to two sources of facts it does not reproduce: `User Query` ^[kael-system-tsdp-analyse-und-profile.md:#4] and `Forschungskontext` ^[kael-system-tsdp-analyse-und-profile.md:#12], each in parentheses after a claim.
- It reports its own handling of a disputed point: „obwohl die Benutzeranfrage die Gültigkeit von TSDP annimmt“ ^[L159] — the document says the request assumes the theory is valid and recommends following the trauma model.
- It makes no claim to be canon; it presents its Part 4 as recommendations for plot development, which opens at „Die identifizierten Spannungspunkte bieten reiches Material“ ^[L381].

## 2 · The theory the document applies

- The document calls the theory its foundation and names its developers: „wie sie von Van der Hart, Nijenhuis und Steele entwickelt wurde“ ^[L17]. The theory is reported there as theirs, not the document's.
- The theory as the document reports it sorts the parts into two kinds: „Die ANPs sind primär mit Handlungssystemen des täglichen Lebens assoziiert“ ^[L17], while the emotional parts are bound to defence systems.
- The document gives its own name to the classification of System Kael: „System Kael zeigt eindeutig eine Tertiäre Dissoziation“ ^[L137], and again as `Tertiäre Strukturelle Dissoziation` ^[kael-system-tsdp-analyse-und-profile.md:#2].
- The `Phobie` between the parts is the document's central mechanism: `Phobien` ^[kael-system-tsdp-analyse-und-profile.md:#44] stands alone 44 times and `Phobie` ^[kael-system-tsdp-analyse-und-profile.md:#20] 20 times.
- It borrows two further concepts, `Autopoiesis` ^[kael-system-tsdp-analyse-und-profile.md:#4] and `Strukturelle Kopplung` ^[kael-system-tsdp-analyse-und-profile.md:#2], and applies them: „System Kael ist autopoietisch“ ^[L141].
- Another borrowed frame is the `IFS-Modell` ^[kael-system-tsdp-analyse-und-profile.md:#2], used to describe the `Selbst-Energie` ^[kael-system-tsdp-analyse-und-profile.md:#4] of one part.

## 3 · The eleven parts

- The introduction counts them: „den elf identifizierten Persönlichkeitsanteilen“ ^[L15], and lists Kael, Selene, Nyx, Kiko, Lia, Isabelle, Moros, Alex, Rhys, Lex and Argus.
- Kael is typed by the document as the first kind: „Kael ist der primäre Anscheinend Normale Persönlichkeitsanteil“ ^[L192], and as host.
- Selene is set apart from an ordinary part: „Selene ist nicht nur ein Teil“ ^[L91]. Her role is called central in the same passage: „Ihre Rolle als „Erinnerungsschutz“ ist zentral“ ^[L91].
- Moros is placed at the extreme of one defence: „Moros ist ein EP, der den extremsten Pol der Erstarrungs-/Kollapsreaktion verkörpert“ ^[L288].
- Isabelle is the part the document says does not fit one defence: „Isabelle repräsentiert eine komplexe Mischung“ ^[L148].
- In its summary of the background research the document reads the parts as functions, not as separate people: „Die Anteile sind nicht als separate Personen zu verstehen“ ^[L142], and again in the conclusion „sind keine zufälligen Persönlichkeitsfacetten“ ^[L413].
- **Observed:** Table 3.1 and the eleven profiles (L169 to L360) give every part the same nine field labels, and the table types them as `Primärer ANP` ^[kael-system-tsdp-analyse-und-profile.md:#6], `Sekundärer ANP` ^[kael-system-tsdp-analyse-und-profile.md:#6], `Entstehender ANP` ^[kael-system-tsdp-analyse-und-profile.md:#2] and `Modifizierter ANP` ^[kael-system-tsdp-analyse-und-profile.md:#1] among others.
- Lex is analysed by the document as phobic toward the emotional parts: „Lex' Phobie richtet sich gegen die Irrationalität“ ^[L35].
