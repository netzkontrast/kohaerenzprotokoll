# Note — Kohärenz Protokoll: The Official Project Handbook

What this document says about the terms that matter in it. Every quotation carries its file line on the same line of this note; a number about the whole document is a count mark that `quotes.py` checks. Where the note says **observed**, no quotation is possible and the claim rests on the lines or counts it names. The document speaks in the voice of a ruling: it declares and mandates, and gives no scene.

The document also holds a framework taken from real clinical practice (TSDP and IFS) as the model of one figure; only its use as the figure's architecture is harvested here, and nothing about real people.

## 1 · What kind of text this is, and how it marks itself

- The document names itself: „This handbook serves as the canonical, single source of truth“ ^[L17]. This is its claim about its own standing, recorded and not applied.
- It calls one subsection „the final, unchallengeable ruling“ ^[L33].
- Its style section is written as an obligation: „its form must perform its content“ ^[L105].
- Each major section closes on a transition sentence, observed at L47, L63, L75, L87 and L99.

## 2 · The central conflict of AEGIS and Kael

- The thesis the document gives for the story: „true, resilient coherence emerges not from the forceful elimination of paradox, but from its courageous and compassionate integration“ ^[L17].
- The two principals carry paired slogans: „AEGIS: 'Coherence instead of Truth',Kael: 'Coherence through Integration'“ ^[L21].
- The document says of the AEGIS column that „Its prime directive is to eliminate contradiction, paradox, and unquantifiable subjective experiences“ ^[L23].
- For Kael it says „Kael's journey is toward a form of order that emerges from the acceptance of contradictory internal parts“ ^[L23].
- It states the conflict is physics, not figure of speech: „This philosophical war is not a metaphor; it is the fundamental law of the universe's physics.“ ^[L29]
- On the integrative side it names a philosophical position: „the capacity to hold true contradictions“ ^[L25].
