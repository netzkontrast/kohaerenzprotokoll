# Reading — Strukturierter Outline aller 41 Bewegungen, 2026-05-18

## How to read it

„Zweck: Vollständiger Outline-Stand für Kap 0–40" ^[L13], with four levels read
together per chapter: Kishōtenketsu, the dual storyform, three narrative modes,
plot and character. It states the dates of its sources on the same line, and at
the end which of them wins: „Bei Konflikt zwischen Quellen gewinnt das
Konzept-Dokument 2026-05-08." ^[L1393] **Recorded, not applied** (decision 006).

No passage labels and no questions in prose; the open questions are a table
(OQ-A to OQ-G, L1364–L1375). Each chapter entry names its POV, storyform status,
bridge, plot beats, characters, sensory motif, Genesis echo and tonal axis, so a
chapter's placement of a thing is the document's plan for it, not an aside.

  „Oblivion (übernimmt AEGIS' Funktion in Kaels Innensystem" ^[L1124].
