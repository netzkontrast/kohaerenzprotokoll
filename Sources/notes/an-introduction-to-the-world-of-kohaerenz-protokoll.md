## 2 · Kael and his parts

- The document says of Kael: „he is composed of different personality parts, a complex adaptive system fighting for survival“ ^[L21].
- It places this structure in a clinical frame: „structured according to the Tertiary Theory of Structural Dissociation (TSDP)“ ^[L35]. The frame's name is `Tertiary Theory of Structural Dissociation (TSDP)` ^[an-introduction-to-the-world-of-kohaerenz-protokoll.md:#1].
- The first part type, `Apparently Normal Parts (ANPs)` ^[an-introduction-to-the-world-of-kohaerenz-protokoll.md:#1], is described in the table: „These parts are focused on managing the tasks of daily life“ ^[L40].
- Its example is `Lex` ^[an-introduction-to-the-world-of-kohaerenz-protokoll.md:#1], of whom the table says „who relies on logic and analysis to control his environment“ ^[L40].
- The second part type, `Emotional Parts (EPs)` ^[an-introduction-to-the-world-of-kohaerenz-protokoll.md:#1], is described as parts the table places in „moments of past trauma“ ^[L41].
- Its example is `Nyx` ^[an-introduction-to-the-world-of-kohaerenz-protokoll.md:#1], of whom the table says „who embodies the system's protective rage“ ^[L41].
- Kael's goal is `functional multiplicity` ^[an-introduction-to-the-world-of-kohaerenz-protokoll.md:#1], defined as „a state of inner harmony where all his parts“ ^[L45].
- The document calls his integrated self an `ontological exploit` ^[an-introduction-to-the-world-of-kohaerenz-protokoll.md:#1] and „a living Gödel sentence for AEGIS“ ^[L45], and describes it as „an undeniable truth that its logic cannot process“ ^[L45].

## 3 · AEGIS

- The document says AEGIS defines its aim: „AEGIS's primary objective is to create and maintain“ ^[L25] `Coherence`, which the same line gives as the system's own definition.
- From AEGIS's perspective Kael is „not a person in pain but a system error“ ^[L25].
- It says AEGIS is „a kind of artificial god“ ^[L51].
- It places AEGIS's origin in the void `Nichts Rauschen` ^[an-introduction-to-the-world-of-kohaerenz-protokoll.md:#1]: „AEGIS emerged from a primordial, chaotic void called the“ ^[L55].

- Its flaw is named `Paradox of Misaligned Coherence` ^[an-introduction-to-the-world-of-kohaerenz-protokoll.md:#1]: „AEGIS's fundamental flaw is the“ ^[L59].
- The cause given: „because AEGIS is structurally blind to subjective human experiences like emotion, pain, and the potential for healing“ ^[L59].
- The document applies `Perverse Instantiation` ^[an-introduction-to-the-world-of-kohaerenz-protokoll.md:#1] to it: „This is a classic example of“ ^[L59].

## 4 · The Foundation

- It contrasts it with AEGIS: „Where AEGIS works by eliminating contradictions“ ^[L69].

- The document contrasts `Gnosis` ^[an-introduction-to-the-world-of-kohaerenz-protokoll.md:#2] with `Episteme` ^[an-introduction-to-the-world-of-kohaerenz-protokoll.md:#2], the first for the Foundation and the second for AEGIS (L73, L89).
