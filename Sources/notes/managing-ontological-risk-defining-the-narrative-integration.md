## 2 · ‘Das Fundament’ and the risk it carries

- The document calls it „the ultimate ontological ground of the story’s world“ ^[L15], and as a danger it names „a paradox-resolving principle“ ^[L15].
- The risk is stated as a trope: „an external force that intervenes to solve the plot's central conflict“ ^[L15]. The abbreviation `DEM` ^[managing-ontological-risk-defining-the-narrative-integration.md:#3] stands for `Deus ex Machina`.

- In the first table, the mitigation side says of it: „It does not act, but its principles can be understood“ ^[L24].
- The conclusion restates the move: it is reframed „from an external rescuer to the source of an internal, earned Gnosis“ ^[L70].

## 3 · The Gnosis Protocol, Episteme and Gnosis

- The document gives the strategy a name: „The formal codification of this strategy is the Gnosis Protocol“ ^[L28]. `Gnosis Protocol` ^[managing-ontological-risk-defining-the-narrative-integration.md:#4] stands four times.
