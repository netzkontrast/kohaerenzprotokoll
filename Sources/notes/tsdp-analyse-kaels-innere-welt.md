- The document reads Kael's inner figures as dissociated parts: „Die Analyse interpretiert Kaels innere“ ^[L19] (the line continues with the word Personas in quotation marks).
- The document counts five named parts and from that infers a tertiary form: „Angesichts der Existenz von fünf benannten, distinkten Anteilen“ ^[L23]
