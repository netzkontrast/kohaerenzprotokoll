## 2 · The thesis, and the two kernels

- The document gives one thesis for the whole story: the Kohärenz Protokoll „is the journey of System Kael towards psychological integration“ ^[L15].
- It frames the story as „a staged philosophical war between two irreconcilable definitions of order“ ^[L15].
- The Coherence Kernel, in the document's words, „represents the force of order, structure, and information preservation“ ^[L23].
- The Collapse Kernel is the counterpart; the narrative is said to reveal it as „the ultimate source of objective truth“ ^[L27].
- The document expands AEGIS as „AEGIS (Autonomous Entropic Gatekeeper for Integrity Systems)“ ^[L23].
- It gives AEGIS's prime directive as a recursive axiom: „AEGIS is what AEGIS prevents itself from not being.“ ^[L23].
- It says AEGIS cannot perceive „phenomena that exist outside its own logical framework“ ^[L23].
- Of AEGIS's origin it says: „AEGIS is not a malevolent villain but a tragic entity forged by trauma“ ^[L31].

## 3 · The physics of information

- The document says information is treated as substance: „The narrative treats information as a physical substance“ ^[L39].
- A table at L39 defines `Coherons` as „atoms of persistence“ ^[L39], and `Wavelets` as „Dynamic units of change“ ^[L39].
- The same table gives `Protocol Ontology` as „the continuous, energy-intensive execution of a protocol“ ^[L39].
- It calls the `Moonshine-Link` „a backdoor connection“ ^[L165] and „a fundamental blind spot in AEGIS's panopticon“ ^[L166].
- Of the name Moonshine, the document says the mathematics „reveals a hidden connection between infinite complexity“ ^[L164], and in that line it assigns the Monster Group to `K\_0` and the modular functions to `K\_1` in that line.

## 4 · The war of logics, and the Gödel-Gambit

- The table at L35 says „AEGIS operates on Classical Logic“ ^[L35] and „System Kael evolves to embody Paraconsistent Logic“ ^[L35].
- The climax is described as „derived from Gödel's Incompleteness Theorems“ ^[L153]; the document applies the theorems as a borrowed lens.
- It gives the setup: „a formal axiomatic system that believes it is both Consistent“ ^[L155].
- The execution it writes is: „Kael connects directly to AEGIS's core and forces the system to observe his integrated, paradoxical state“ ^[L157].
- The outcome it gives is „a zombie system that can perceive truth but can no longer process it“ ^[L158].
- The document names the vehicle: the Gödel-Gambit is delivered via „a connection between Kael and an external entity, Juna/V“ ^[L162].

## 5 · System Kael, the alters and the therapeutic frame

- The document calls the Kohärenz Protokoll „a direct and explicit allegory for complex trauma and its treatment“ ^[L45].
- It describes System Kael as „a collection of distinct consciousnesses, or alters, sharing a single mind“ ^[L49].
- The roster is „core alters plus an Internal Self-Helper“ ^[L53], and it adds „non-negotiable principle of this narrative“ ^[L53] for the rule that every alter acts with a positive intent.
- **Observed:** the two tables list six Apparently Normal Parts (Kael, Lex, Alex, Aris, Elara, Mina; L57) and five Emotional Parts (Nyx, Kiko, Moros, Lyra, Soren; L61), eleven in all, and Selene separately at L67.
- Selene is „A unique observer part that is not stuck in trauma nor focused on daily life“ ^[L67].
- The therapeutic goal is stated negatively: „the narrative's therapeutic goal is not the erasure of alters“ ^[L75].
- AEGIS is placed in the trauma frame: „AEGIS is an Externalized Perpetrator Introject“ ^[L71].
- Lex is tied to the antagonist: „Lex mirrors AEGIS's logic on a micro-scale“ ^[L57].

## 6 · The Kernwelten and the Risse
