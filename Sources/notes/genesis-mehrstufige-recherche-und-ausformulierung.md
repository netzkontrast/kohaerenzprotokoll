# Note — Genesis: Mehrstufige Recherche und Ausformulierung

What this document says about the terms that matter in it. Every quotation carries its file line on the same line of this note; a number about the whole document is a count mark that `quotes.py` checks. Where the note says **observed**, no quotation is possible and the claim rests on the lines or counts it names. The document speaks as a plan and an instruction for writing; its sample lines are prose and report text written for the two voices, and the `Ich` in them is the novel's narrative voice.

## 1 · What kind of text this is, and how it marks itself

- The guide calls itself a „detaillierte Regieanweisung“ for the prose version of a 13-scene outline ^[L303].
- The guide says that the strict observance of its specifications is decisive: „Die strikte Einhaltung dieser Vorgaben ist entscheidend für das Gelingen des Projekts.“ ^[L303] A claim about its own standing, recorded and not applied.
- It names its section 4 with „Regiebuch“ ^[L299].
- Each of thirteen scene entries repeats `Stylistische Direktiven` ^[genesis-mehrstufige-recherche-und-ausformulierung.md:#13], `Spezifische Techniken/Beispiele` ^[genesis-mehrstufige-recherche-und-ausformulierung.md:#13] and `Dominante Stimmung/Atmosphäre` ^[genesis-mehrstufige-recherche-und-ausformulierung.md:#13]; the labels name fields of the scene template, not terms of the world.
- Three scene headings are flagged `NEUE Szene` ^[genesis-mehrstufige-recherche-und-ausformulierung.md:#3], for example „NEUE Szene: Systemdiagnose: Stille im Kern“ ^[L468].
- **Observed:** the scene entries sit under three part headings in capitals, „DAS ERWACHEN IM RAUSCHEN“ ^[L323], „AEGIS - DIE FESTUNG DER LOGIK“ ^[L411] and „DIE KRISE UND DIE SCHERBEN“ ^[L544].
- The guide describes two resources it proposes, a `Sensorisches Lexikon` for the Ich and a „Technisches Glossar & Stil-Leitfaden“ for AEGIS ^[L157].

## 2 · Ich, Kael and the Ich's voice

- The guide reasons that the Ich „ist seine subjektive Erfahrung“, and reads this as the opposite of AEGIS' functional operation ^[L21].
- For the first-person voice it names techniques including „Deep POV“ ^[L59] and an „Unzuverlässigkeit“ of the narrator as a possibility, in a form that asks whether Kael in scene 13 is unreliable ^[L59].
- Scene 13 is described by the guide as the „Geburt von Kael als fragmentiertes Mosaik“ ^[L649]. In the sample lines of that scene the narrative voice says „Das ist Kael.“ ^[L671].
- The same scene has a sample beat on the double loss, with „Zweimal verloren“ ^[L670] and „Nur die Leere bleibt. Und die Sehnsucht.“ ^[L670].
- The guide gives the Ich's sample opening as „Es zerreißt mich.“ ^[L346].

## 3 · AEGIS, Direktive 0 and Komponente 734

- The guide treats AEGIS as functional: „AEGIS, als rein funktionales System konzipiert“ ^[L21].
- The sample report line for scene 4 gives the core protocol: „Kernprotokoll aktiviert“ ^[L438], defined in the sample as „AEGIS ist die Abwehr des Nicht-Seins. Existenz = Funktion.“ ^[L438] The guide's own prose places that wording under `Direktive 0` ^[genesis-mehrstufige-recherche-und-ausformulierung.md:#8].
- The sample report for scene 5 gives the new designation: „Neue Designierung: Komponente 734.“ ^[L461].
- The guide reads AEGIS' action against Komponente 734 as logical consequence and not malice: „keine Akte der Bosheit“ ^[L31].
- The guide also puts `Hybris` in quotation marks and says it lies not in malice but in an inability to recognise the limits of its own logic ^[L37].

## 4 · Echo, Resonanz and the Überwelt

- The sample line of the Ich in scene 5 describes the Echo: „Ein Echo. Immer da. Stört nicht. Aber es ist da.“ ^[L464].
- A sample report line reclassifies the „Subjektive Residuen“ ^[L440] (Echos, emotionale Marker) as latent data points in the sample report.
- The guide's scene entry for scene 7 has the sample report say „Überwelt dient der Modellierung von Systemzuständen“ ^[L515].
- Scene 10 has the sample report conclude that the external entity is „Externe Entität fundamental inkompatibel mit AEGIS-Logik“ ^[L594] in the sample report line.
- The guide gives `Resonanz` ^[genesis-mehrstufige-recherche-und-ausformulierung.md:#39] as a central concept for which it collects metaphors (section 3.3) and `Dissonanz` ^[genesis-mehrstufige-recherche-und-ausformulierung.md:#23] as its counterpart.

The longer form with `Kohärenz` stands once, `Kohärenz Protokoll Gamma-Exekution` ^[genesis-mehrstufige-recherche-und-ausformulierung.md:#1], the shorter `Protokoll Gamma-Exekution` ^[genesis-mehrstufige-recherche-und-ausformulierung.md:#4] four times.
