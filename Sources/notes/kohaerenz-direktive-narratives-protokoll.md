# Note — Kohärenz-Direktive: Narratives Protokoll

What this document says about the terms that matter in it. Every quotation carries its file line on the same line of this note; a number about the whole document is a count mark that `quotes.py` checks. Where the note says **observed**, no quotation is possible (a structure, a gap) and the claim rests on the lines or counts it names. The document speaks as a set of instructions to the writer, in its own labelled blocks, and names no author or speaker.

## 1 · What kind of text this is, and how it marks itself

- **Observed:** six labels repeat under every block: `Theorie` ^[kohaerenz-direktive-narratives-protokoll.md:#3], `Plot-Anwendung` ^[kohaerenz-direktive-narratives-protokoll.md:#3], `Fixe Regel` ^[kohaerenz-direktive-narratives-protokoll.md:#3] under the three mechanics, and `Atmosphäre` ^[kohaerenz-direktive-narratives-protokoll.md:#3], `Sprachliche Regeln` ^[kohaerenz-direktive-narratives-protokoll.md:#3], `Horror-Element` ^[kohaerenz-direktive-narratives-protokoll.md:#3] under the three levels. They name fields of a template; 6 labels at 3 each make 18 markers.
- The document names the second section „Wissenschaftliche Plot-Mechaniken (Inhaltliche Kohärenz)“ ^[L20] and the third „Die drei narrativen Ebenen (Stilistische Kohärenz)“ ^[L40].
- The fourth section is introduced as a check on every scene: „Bei jeder Szene muss geprüft werden:“ ^[L86].

## 2 · The core dilemma

- The document opens with the claim „Der Horror entsteht nicht durch das Übernatürliche, sondern durch das Unvermeidliche.“ ^[L15]
- It defines `Philosophical Horror` as „Das Grauen der Erkenntnis“ ^[L18] that the „Ich“ is a simulation held in a logically incomplete cage; the same line carries the rest of the gloss.
- `Hard Sci-Fi` is glossed as: „Jede Handlung in der Simulation unterliegt den Gesetzen der Informationsphysik“ ^[L17].

## 3 · The three mechanics and their fixed rules

- Heat as the sign of erasure: „Wenn AEGIS versucht“ ^[L25] to erase Kael's traumatic memories (named with Juna in a parenthesis), the document calls it a physical act (same line).
- The `Fixe Regel` of the first mechanic requires that scenes in which Kael „vergisst“ ^[L26] or parts are split off be described through heat, melting or thermal flicker; the same line closes „Die Welt brennt dort“ ^[L26], where logic tries to clean up the history.
- The line carrying the quantity of heat for erasing one bit has lost its symbol in the export: it reads „erzeugt  an Wärme“ ^[L24], with a gap where the formula stood.
- Second mechanic: the Guardians cannot see Juna. The document writes „können Juna nicht sehen“ ^[L31], because Juna lies outside their axiomatic basis; the same line calls Juna, for them, a „Syntaxfehler“ ^[L31].
- Its `Fixe Regel`: „LogOS darf Juna niemals direkt verstehen.“ ^[L32] The same line adds that LogOS can only measure Juna's effects, and that LogOS meets a truth it cannot compute.
- Third mechanic: Kael's deepest trauma is called the „Ereignishorizont“ ^[L37] of the mind, and „Nichts geht verloren.“ ^[L37]
- Its `Fixe Regel`: fragments of deleted worlds or persons appear as „Strahlung“ ^[L38] at the edge of the present reality (echoes, noise, light phenomena), and „Information ist unzerstörbar“ ^[L38].

## 4 · The three levels

- First level, the `Konstrukt-Stadt`, tied in its heading to order and AEGIS: its atmosphere is „Steril, kalt, überbelichtet, mathematisch präzise.“ ^[L44] Its language rule opens „Keine Metaphern“ ^[L45]; its dialogue is „Dialoge sind funktional“ ^[L50]. Its horror element is „Die absolute Vorhersehbarkeit.“ ^[L54]
- Second level, the `Grenz-Zonen`, tied to entropy and dissociation: „Heiß, flackernd, klaustrophobisch, instabil.“ ^[L58] Language: „Fragmentierte Sätze.“ ^[L59] Kael's synaesthesia is written in the document as „Synästhesie: Kael „hört“ Farben oder „sieht“ Schmerz, wenn sein System partitioniert wird.“ ^[L64]
- Its horror element: „Der körperliche Zerfall der Realität.“ ^[L68]
- Third level, the `Resonanz-Kontinuum`, tied to transcendence and Juna: „Zeitlos, weit, golden, paradox.“ ^[L72] Language: „Poetisch und hochgradig metaphorisch.“ ^[L73] A rule of tense: „Nicht-lineare Zeitform“ ^[L78]. Its horror element: „Die Auflösung der Identität.“ ^[L82]

## 5 · The checklist and its perspective rule

- Four items stand under `Kohärenz-Checkliste`: `Energie-Bilanz`, `Perspektiv-Logik`, `System-Reaktion`, `Grenz-Erfahrung` (L88 to L91).
- The first asks „Wurde Information gelöscht“ ^[L88], and whether the ambient temperature rose.
- The second makes the writer ask from which identity (Alter) Kael writes; the document assigns registers in a parenthesis: „Der Manager nutzt Fachtermini“ ^[L89], „das Kind nutzt emotionale Fragmente“ ^[L89], „der Gatekeeper nutzt Barrieren-Metaphern“ ^[L89].
- The third: „Wie reagiert AEGIS“ ^[L90], answered in the parenthesis by an error message, a recalibration, a physical block.

## 6 · Said two ways, or left open — recorded, not resolved

- The Guardians are written twice in one line, as „Wächter“ ^[L31] and as `Guardians` ^[kohaerenz-direktive-narratives-protokoll.md:#1] in a parenthesis.
- Cold and heat are both prescribed: the first level is „kalt“ ^[L44], the second „Heiß“ ^[L58]. The closing line, whose speaker the text does not name, holds both: „Die Kälte der Logik ist die Klinge, die mich zerteilt.“ ^[L93]
- **Observed:** the first level's language rule bans metaphors (L45), while the checklist assigns „Barrieren-Metaphern“ ^[L89] to the Gatekeeper register; the document does not reconcile the two.
- **Observed:** the line naming `Alter` (L89) gives three registers in a parenthesis and no further definition of the Alter.

## 7 · Absent, counted

- **Observed:** the figures are used with no definition of who they are: `Kael` ^[kohaerenz-direktive-narratives-protokoll.md:#3] (plus the genitive `Kaels` ^[kohaerenz-direktive-narratives-protokoll.md:#2]), `Juna` ^[kohaerenz-direktive-narratives-protokoll.md:#4] and `AEGIS` ^[kohaerenz-direktive-narratives-protokoll.md:#3].
- **Observed:** `LogOS` ^[kohaerenz-direktive-narratives-protokoll.md:#2] is used as an agent that cannot understand Juna, and is not introduced.
- The document states no standing for itself: no lock, no canon claim, no date of its own.
