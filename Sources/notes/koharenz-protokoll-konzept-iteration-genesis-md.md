# Reading — Genesis integration and plural apotheosis

## How this document speaks

The source calls itself „ein lebendes Dokument seiner eigenen Verwendung“ ^[L849]
and says it is „nicht die endgültige Storyform“ ^[L849]. Part I states an
ontology, II numbers the Genesis beats, III–IV propose chapter placements,
and VI labels unresolved issues OQ-A through OQ-G. Its own asserted precedence
has no authority over other sources (decision 006).

## Genesis and the two halves

The first layer is „Nichts-Rauschen / K1-Reinform“ ^[L44], the structured
second is „K0-Existenz“ ^[L48], and Juna is „zwischen“ them ^[L52]. The
resonance is read as „die Geburt der Welt“ ^[L56], which AEGIS reads as a
threat. The source says Kael is „das, was nach der Trennung subjektiv übrig
blieb“ ^[L72] and AEGIS „das, was nach der Trennung funktional übrig blieb“
^[L80]. It never names either half Ursprungs-Ich. Its division makes the
identity question more precise but cannot answer the glossary's J68 or the
outline's J75 by lexical evidence.

## C12 — four beats with 734 before separation

The source explicitly numbers four beats ^[L200]: cluster and
functionalisation including Komp 734 ^[L205], separation ^[L206], and the
plural fourth beat in Kap 39 ^[L207]. Its Kap-0 synopsis places Komponente
734 before the crisis ^[L295] ^[L297]. Kap 21 then recalls the separation
^[L588], and Kap 22 has a flashback of Komp 734 ^[L592]. These are memory
order and event order respectively. OQ-C says the flashback chapter assignment
is still a proposal ^[L790]. This document is another position in C12, not an
author decision on the three-beat alternative.

## Other open records

„Junas erste direkte Erscheinung“ ^[L656]. That distinction informs C7,
while Kap 33's presence still needs the author. Storyform B is Do-er ^[L152]
(C8). Kap 6 has „Landauer-Wärme“ ^[L528] and Kap 36 has „Landauer-Wärme“
^[L648] (C11), alongside ozone in Kap 1 ^[L508].

## Limit of this reading

A diagram of cosmic layers in this draft does not settle the whole corpus's
ontology. The source's final naming of Wir-AEGIS remains OQ-A ^[L770], the
Moonshine boundary is OQ-F ^[L802], and the status of monolithic AEGIS after
Vortex 1 is OQ-G ^[L805]. No claim from these open questions is promoted as
settled vocabulary.
