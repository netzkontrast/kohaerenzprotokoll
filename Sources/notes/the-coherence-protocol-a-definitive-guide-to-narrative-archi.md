## 1 · What kind of text this is, and how it marks itself

- The document calls itself „the definitive architectural specification“ ^[L15] for the narrative.
- It claims for itself the role of „canonical, single source of truth“ ^[L15]. This is the document's claim about its own standing, recorded and not applied.
- It says that „Adherence to this protocol is mandatory“ ^[L15].
- **Observed:** it names no author, no date and no other document; the body runs L11 to L155 in numbered sections 1.0 to 6.0.

## 2 · The premise: Coherence and Correspondence

- The document says the novel „must be understood as a dramatized philosophical argument“ ^[L19].
- It frames the conflict as „a war between two competing theories of truth“ ^[L19], named Coherence and Correspondence in the same sentence.
- The table at L27 gives AEGIS the slogan „Coherence instead of Truth“ ^[L27] and Kael „Coherence through Integration“ ^[L27].
- AEGIS's rule is „Exclusionary Order“ ^[L28], in which contradiction and unquantifiable subjective experience are classed as „noise“ ^[L28].

## 3 · The Protocol Ontology and the two kernels

- The document writes that the „Protocol Ontology“ ^[L36] is to be understood as the fundamental physics of the narrative universe.
- The Dual Kernel Theory (DKT) is the document's name for the conflict of two computational domains: „Dual Kernel Theory (DKT)“ ^[L40].
- The document says „The Coherence Kernel (K₁) is the principle of order, structure, and information preservation“ ^[L42].
- It says the Collapse Kernel „is the principle of entropy, information erasure, and irreversible change“ ^[L43].
- The Coherons are „minimal, self-correcting units of mutual information“ ^[L42].
- The central tenet is that „there are no static“ ^[L47] things, only protocols.
- The document says this ontology gives „a definitive solution to the Ship of Theseus paradox“ ^[L54]; the paradox is borrowed, and the claim of a solution is the document's.

## 4 · The two unknowable domains

- The Sea of Potentiality is given with its German names in one parenthesis: „Das Potentialmeer / Nichts Rauschen“ ^[L60].
- Its signature is described as „informational void“ ^[L60] and „auditory pressure without sound“ ^[L60].
- The Foundation, „Das Fundament“ ^[L61], is described as „the fundamental process of reality itself“ ^[L61], compared to a „strange attractor“ ^[L61], and the document says it „does not actively intervene“ ^[L61].

## 5 · Kernwelten, Risse and the Core Worlds

- The document says the „Kernwelten“ ^[L67] (Core Worlds) are „externalized manifestations“ ^[L67] of Kael's inner landscape, and it calls them „Psycho-Architectures“ ^[L67].
- It says that in them Kael's „somatic (bodily) experience serves as an infallible measure of truth“ ^[L67].
- The „Risse“ ^[L71] are glitches that follow „a precise four-step causal chain“ ^[L71]; the chain runs from an external trigger through an Emotional Part and an ANP-EP Phobia to an external Riss.
- The four Core Worlds are listed in a table at L85 to L88 as Logos-Prime (LogOS), Mnemosyne-Archipel (Mnemosyne), Cerberus-Labyrinth (Cerberus) and Kairos-Potentialis (Kairos/Sophia); each row names a psychological principle, a sensory signature and a somatic motif. **Observed:** the Kairos-Potentialis row gives its domain to the Impact Character Juna/V (L88).

- AEGIS's origin is given with two German terms: the „Genesis-Krise“ ^[L99] and the „Trennungsprotokoll“ ^[L99], glossed „Separation Protocol“ ^[L99].

- The link between Kael and Juna/V is the „Moonshine-Link“ ^[L126], which the document says AEGIS cannot perceive as anything but noise.
- The document says Juna/V is „a catalyst, not a savior“ ^[L127]; the link gives „gnosis“ ^[L127].

## 7 · The three acts

- The plot can be executed as a linear novel or as a „39-Story Narrative Mosaic“ ^[L133].
- Act I ends with „functional multiplicity“ ^[L137] and a „Sacred Marriage“ ^[L137] of the parts.
- In Act II „each failed cycle must exact“ ^[L141] Irreversible Costs, and the Moonshine-Link is damaged in the third of them (L145).
- In Act III, „Kael's goal is not to destroy AEGIS“ ^[L151].
- The document describes the ending as a „living Gödel-Satz“ ^[L155] in which the antagonist is forced into „algorithmic melancholy“ ^[L155].
