## 2 · The structure of the worlds

- The overview names the levels: „Die narrative Welt besteht aus mehreren Ebenen“ ^[L17]. Per the same line there are four Kern-Welten (`KW1` to `KW4`), a digital Überwelt and an external level.
- The Kern-Welten are said to represent „Aspekte von Kaels Psyche“ ^[L17].
- The Überwelt is the „Domäne von AEGIS“ ^[L17].
- The external level is the one „verbunden mit Juna/V“ ^[L17].
- A „universal reboot“ ^[L17] is named as an event after which the Kern-Welten were put into a state the sentence calls „künstlicher Ordnung“ ^[L17].
- The same line says that the Risse „Störungen dieser Ordnung und potenzielle Überlappungen“ ^[L17] represent, and the document uses `Risse` ^[weltenkonzept-fuer-kohaerenz-protokoll-tsdp-basiert.md:#8] eight times, also as a field label.
