# Note — Kohärenz Protokoll: Plot-Synthese Ω

What this document says about the terms that matter in it. Every quotation carries its file line on the same line of this note; a number about the whole document is a count mark that `quotes.py` checks. Where the note says **observed**, no quotation is possible (a structure, a gap) and the claim rests on the lines or counts it names. This text is one version of a plot synthesis; the other version is not read here, and nothing below compares them. The document speaks as a plan: what happens in a chapter is proposed and often hedged, not told.

## 1 · What kind of text this is, and how it marks itself

- The document names itself a report, „Synthese-Bericht: Plot-Blueprint“ ^[L11], and says it follows an assignment: „Dieser Bericht folgt der Direktive“ ^[L15].
- It calls the frame it builds on fixed: „den verbindlich definierten konzeptuellen Rahmen als Grundlage“ ^[L15]. In the analysis section it says the same with „gemäß dem definitiven konzeptuellen Rahmen“ ^[L19]. This is the document's claim about its own footing; it is recorded, not applied.
- Its labels are the thesis titles and the section called `Rationale` ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-plot-synthese-2.md:#2]; the word `Thesis` ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-plot-synthese-2.md:#6] stands in the four thesis titles, the choice and the sentence that builds the blueprint on the chosen one.
- **Observed:** every chapter block repeats four field labels (`Genre-Fokus` ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-plot-synthese-2.md:#41], `Nebencharaktere` ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-plot-synthese-2.md:#39], and the two others the census profile lists); they are template fields, not terms.
- Its hedges mark what is proposed: `vielleicht` ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-plot-synthese-2.md:#12] and `möglicherweise` ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-plot-synthese-2.md:#6]. A side-character field reads „Eventuell ein Arzt oder Therapeut“ ^[L134].
- On its own standing at the close, the report says it „stellt eine kohärente und detaillierte narrative Struktur dar“ ^[L535] and „Dieser Blueprint ist somit bereit“ ^[L537] to serve as a roadmap. It also names „die im NZT-Ideal geforderte“ ^[L537] clarity as its aim, and does not explain that ideal.

## 2 · AEGIS, the Potentialmeer and the Simulation

- The `Potentialmeer` ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-plot-synthese-2.md:#16] is described by the document as hostile to order: „Seine inhärente Instabilität und potentielle Feindseligkeit“ ^[L21].
- `AEGIS` ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-plot-synthese-2.md:#127] is described as defining itself by what it is not: „AEGIS definiert sich primär durch das, was es“ ^[L22]. The same line draws a consequence the document calls a limit: „Dies impliziert, dass es Wahrheiten über das Potentialmeer“ ^[L22].
- The Simulation is called a space that is not closed: „diese Simulation kein hermetisch abgeschlossener Raum ist“ ^[L23]. The document offers options for the Kernwelten, put as possibilities: „Die Kernwelten könnten parallele Simulationsinstanzen“ ^[L23].
- The Risse are described as symptoms: „Die Risse sind nicht bloße Systemfehler“ ^[L23].
- In chapter 15, AEGIS reacts to Kael: „initiiert gezielte Maßnahmen“ ^[L265]. In chapter 35 the Simulation falls apart: „Die Simulation beginnt rapide zu zerfallen“ ^[L485].
- In chapter 28 the block puts AEGIS before a choice: „AEGIS steht vor einem Dilemma“ ^[L408].
