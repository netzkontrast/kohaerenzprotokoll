# Note — Romanstruktur: Duale Erzählung und Kishōtenketsu

What this document says about the terms that matter in it. Every quotation carries its file line on the same line of this note; a number about the whole document is a count mark that `quotes.py` checks. The document speaks as a design for the novel, and it names no status of its own.

## 1 · What kind of text this is, and how it marks itself

- The document says what it does: „Dieses Dokument entwirft die architektonische Gesamtstruktur des Romans“ ^[L22]
- Each act section opens with a bold `Aktübersicht` ^[romanstruktur-duale-erzaehlung-und-kishotenketsu.md:#4] paragraph, and each full chapter block carries a `Kapitel-Kishōtenketsu` ^[romanstruktur-duale-erzaehlung-und-kishotenketsu.md:#10] label with four sub-bullets. **Observed:** these labels are the template's field names (L49, L123, L195, L249 and L60 and following), not terms the document defines.
- Footnote number 1 is glued to the end of many sentences and the reference list names one item, „AEGIS Paradoxon Neukonzeption und Analyse“ ^[L315]. **Observed:** the document does not mark which sentences rest on that text, so a sentence carrying the number is reported, not established by this document.

## 2 · The two plots and the principle of the form

- The document gives the form a purpose in the world of the novel: „AEGIS' Streben nach Kontrolle folgt einer westlichen Konfliktdramaturgie“ ^[L22]
- Kael's line is set against it: „Kaels Heilungsreise auf der transformativen Kishōtenketsu-Struktur basiert“ ^[L22]
- AEGIS' plot is described as „Die Auferlegung einer rigiden, logischen und extern verifizierbaren Kohärenz“ ^[L26] and as „dem tragischen Versuch eines fehlerhaften Schöpfers“ ^[L26]
- Kael's plot is described as „Das Erreichen einer authentischen, innerlich verhandelten und dynamischen Kohärenz“ ^[L27] and the Persönlichkeitsanteile are counted „zwischen seinen elf Persönlichkeitsanteilen“ ^[L27]
- The form is claimed to comment on the conflict: „Die Form des Romans wird so zu einem Meta-Kommentar“ ^[L41]

## 3 · The four acts, as the document maps them

**Observed:** Table 1 (L33 to L39) assigns Akt I to Kishōtenketsu phase Ki, chapters 1-9, Akt II to Shō, 10-21, Akt III to Ten, 22-31, and Akt IV to Ketsu, 32-40; the act headings at L45, L119, L191 and L245 repeat the ranges.
