# Note — Kohärenz Protokoll: Detailliertes Roman-Outline (Leserzentriert)

What this document says about the terms that matter in it. Every quotation carries its file line on the same line of this note; a number about the whole document is a count mark that `quotes.py` checks. Where the note says **observed**, no quotation is possible (a structure, a gap) and the claim rests on the lines or counts it names. The document speaks as a plan for chapters, not as a report of events, and it does not call itself canon or draft.

## 1 · What kind of text this is, and how it marks itself

- **Observed:** `Chapter` stands 40 times (`Chapter` ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-detailliertes-roman-outline-leserzentrie.md:#40]), once for the prologue and once for each of Chapters 1 to 39; every chapter has a bracketed title, a `Handlung` paragraph and eight fields.
- The last field of each chapter is `Reader Psychology Note` (`Reader Psychology Note` ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-detailliertes-roman-outline-leserzentrie.md:#40]), which says what the chapter does to the reader: for the prologue „Etabliert die kosmische Dimension und den Antagonisten vor dem Protagonisten“ ^[L28].
- The acts carry their own frame labels: „Innere Reise (Heroine's Journey Fokus)“ ^[L30] and „Äußere Konfrontation & Rückkehr (Hero's Journey Fokus)“ ^[L476].
- Plot paragraphs hedge: `vielleicht` stands 16 times (`vielleicht` ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-detailliertes-roman-outline-leserzentrie.md:#16]) and `evtl` 10 times (`evtl` ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-detailliertes-roman-outline-leserzentrie.md:#10]). Names are often marked with a question mark in the fields, e.g. „Kiko?“ in a `Kael Internal` line is not asserted as settled.

## 2 · The prologue: AEGIS and the origin of Kael

- The prologue paragraph tells that „Das ursprüngliche Ich wird zu einer nummerierten Komponente (734)“ ^[L19].
- AEGIS is described there as „ein größeres, logikbasiertes Kollektiv“ ^[L19].
- The same paragraph says of the `Kohärenz Protokoll` that it is „ein Akt gewaltsamer Zerschmetterung der Komponente 734“ ^[L19], and `Kohärenz Protokoll` stands once in the whole document (`Kohärenz Protokoll` ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-detailliertes-roman-outline-leserzentrie.md:#1]).
- The field line for the prologue calls AEGIS' paradox „Kernparadoxon ("Fehlausgerichtete Kohärenz")“ ^[L23].

## 3 · The core paradox of AEGIS

- Chapter 20 is titled „Der entlarvte Mechanismus: Das AEGIS-Paradoxon verstehen“ ^[L359]; its paragraph says „Das Kernparadoxon von AEGIS“ ^[L363] is understood and named, and `Fehlausgerichtete Kohärenz` stands 4 times (`Fehlausgerichtete Kohärenz` ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-detailliertes-roman-outline-leserzentrie.md:#4]).
- The reasoning given in the plan: AEGIS' attempt to control complex, emergent systems „systembedingt scheitern muss“ ^[L363].
- The field `AEGIS Paradoxon` is a subplot label, 32 times (`AEGIS Paradoxon` ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-detailliertes-roman-outline-leserzentrie.md:#32]), and the plan marks chapter 20 there as „Gelöst/Verstanden“ ^[L369].
- Chapter 24 shows the paradox in action: „Kernparadoxon in Aktion“ ^[L435].
