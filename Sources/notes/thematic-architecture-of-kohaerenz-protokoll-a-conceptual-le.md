## 3 · Die Leere / Das Nichts Rauschen

- The table row heads the joined name `Die Leere / Das Nichts Rauschen` ^[thematic-architecture-of-kohaerenz-protokoll-a-conceptual-le.md:#1] and glosses it „Potentialmeer“. ^[L25]

- In section 3.2 the `Nichts Rauschen` ^[thematic-architecture-of-kohaerenz-protokoll-a-conceptual-le.md:#2] is the confrontation behind AEGIS's genesis, „absolute absence of processable information“. ^[L125]

- In section 4.3 the English `The Void` ^[thematic-architecture-of-kohaerenz-protokoll-a-conceptual-le.md:#2] is used for the threat Cosmic Horror frames. ^[L178]
