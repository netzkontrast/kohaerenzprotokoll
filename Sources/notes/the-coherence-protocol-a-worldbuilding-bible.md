# Note — The Coherence Protocol: A Worldbuilding Bible

What this document says about the terms that matter in it. Every quotation carries its file line on the same line of this note; a number about the whole document is a count mark that `quotes.py` checks. Where the note says **observed**, no quotation is possible (a structure, a gap) and the claim rests on the lines or counts it names. The document writes as a guide that states the world's rules as fact; it names no speaker and no date for itself apart from its index date.

## 1 · What kind of text this is, and how it marks itself

- The document describes itself as „the authoritative guide to the metaphysical, psychological, and narrative architecture“ ^[L15] of its universe, a claim about its own standing that is recorded here and applied to nothing.
- It adds „This is the source of truth for the logic of our reality.“ ^[L15]
- **Observed:** the four axis sections (L57 to L91) each close on a table with the same label row. `Narrative Mirroring` ^[the-coherence-protocol-a-worldbuilding-bible.md:#4] stands four times, `Narrative Goal/Resolution` ^[the-coherence-protocol-a-worldbuilding-bible.md:#4] four times; these are the template's field names and not terms of the world.
- The glossary column head is `Operational Definition` ^[the-coherence-protocol-a-worldbuilding-bible.md:#1], and the glossary is introduced as „quick-reference definitions for the creative team to ensure consistency“ ^[L189].
- Section 1.2 announces its own list: „Based on this ontology, we define the following core terms:“ ^[L36]

## 2 · The Protocol Ontology and the two kernels

- The ontology is the document's physics: „the continuous, energy-intensive execution of an information-preserving protocol“ ^[L19].
- The two kernels are described as „the principle of order and preservation“ ^[L23] (the Coherence Kernel) and, as the document continues, a Collapse Kernel „which embodies entropy and irreversible change“ ^[L23].
- The document writes of their relation „Their unceasing interaction is the generative motor of reality itself.“ ^[L23]
- The Dual Kernel Theory (`DKT` ^[the-coherence-protocol-a-worldbuilding-bible.md:#1]) is named once, at L23, as the source of that description.
- The table gives the narrative role of the Collapse Kernel as acting as „the universal "correspondence check."“ ^[L30]

## 3 · Thing, Identity, Life, Death/Collapse, Overhead

- „An instance of a protocol that has achieved a stable, self-correcting loop“ ^[L38] is the document's definition of a Thing.
- Death/Collapse is „Protocol failure, where the system's self-maintenance functions cease“ ^[L41].
- Overhead is „a concept drawn from cache coherence protocols in computer architecture“ ^[L47], borrowed from computer architecture, and applied by the document to every protocol of its world.
- The collapse condition is stated as „A protocol collapses when the energy cost of its Overhead exceeds the resources“ ^[L49] it can acquire.
- The Corrective Wavelet is „a self-generated burst of organizing energy or information that a protocol emits“ ^[L49].
- The document applies the Ship of Theseus paradox to this ontology: „This provides a definitive answer to the Ship of Theseus paradox“ ^[L34].

## 4 · AEGIS

- The prime directive is given in the document's own italic quotation, and the sentence around it says „Its identity is defined by its prime directive“ ^[L133].
- Its function: „AEGIS acts as an "Autonomous Entropic Gatekeeper."“ ^[L136]
- It „classifies any inconvenient external data“ ^[L136] as entropy to be eliminated.
- The document narrates its origin and flaw in the bullet `Genesis-Krise` ^[the-coherence-protocol-a-worldbuilding-bible.md:#2]: AEGIS misread an internal echo, and „fragmenting its own mind“ ^[L135] is the document's account of what the `Trennungsprotokoll` ^[the-coherence-protocol-a-worldbuilding-bible.md:#1] did.
- **Observed:** the name `AEGIS` ^[the-coherence-protocol-a-worldbuilding-bible.md:#37] is written throughout, in the axis tables, the world descriptions and the glossary.

## 5 · The worlds

- Of the Überwelt: „The Überwelt is AEGIS's primary control layer“ ^[L101].
- Of the Core Worlds: „The Core Worlds are tangible manifestations of Kael's psyche“ ^[L105]; the four are listed at L107 to L110 as KW1 to KW4.
- The Risse are given in the body as „The "Risse" are the physical manifestation of systemic instability“ ^[L114] and in the glossary as „The physical manifestation of Correspondence Truth breaking into“ ^[L203] AEGIS's reality.

## 6 · Kael and his system

- The model is split in two: the *origin* of the fragmentation is „Tertiary Structural Dissociation“ ^[L142], and the healing process is guided by „Internal Family Systems“ ^[L142]. Both are borrowed theories the document applies to Kael.
- „The central internal conflict is the“ ^[L143] ANP-EP Phobia, between the ANPs and the EPs.
- The alters are tabulated at L149 to L157; of Lex the row says „Seeks to manage the system through pure rationality“ ^[L150].
- Nyx's row says „His activation leads to“ ^[L154] Paradoxon X with AEGIS.
- Selene's row says AEGIS regards her as an anomaly: „Viewed as an anomaly by AEGIS“ ^[L153].

## 7 · Juna/V

- The document says Juna/V is „who is also an exiled part of Kael's own“ ^[L163] Ursprungs-Ich.
- Her narrative function is stated as „is a catalyst, not a savior“ ^[L165].
- The link to Kael is, in the document's words, one that AEGIS is „ontologically blind to this connection“ ^[L164].
- The document grounds the link in Quantum Entanglement and in Whitehead's `Prehension` ^[the-coherence-protocol-a-worldbuilding-bible.md:#1], both at L164 and both applied to the Moonshine-Link; both are borrowed concepts.

## 8 · The ending

- Kael's healed state is a „consciousness guided by paraconsistent logic“ ^[L179], which the document names the dialetheic mind.
- „Kael's final role is that of“ ^[L179] the Gardener.
- The Foundation: „The Foundation is not a physical object, a place, or a person“ ^[L183], and „It is a resolution Kael must earn and embody“ ^[L183].
- In the glossary the Living Gödel-Satz is „A truth that is valid within AEGIS's system but cannot be proven by its logic“ ^[L207].
- The document reports the theory behind the Living Gödel-Satz as one it applies: „According to Integrated Information Theory (IIT)“ ^[L185]; the claim about high-Φ states is given as that theory's, as applied by the document to Kael.
