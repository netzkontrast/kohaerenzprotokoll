# Note — Plot-Rekonstruktion: Kohärenz Protokoll

What this document says about the terms that matter in it. Every quotation carries its file line on the same line of this note; a number about the whole document is a count mark that `quotes.py` checks. Where the note says **observed**, no quotation is possible (a structure, a gap) and the claim rests on the lines or counts it names. The document speaks as a plan and a rule set for the novel, and where it reports a validation or a user query, those are another text's words.

## 1 · What kind of text this is, and how it marks itself

- The title names the plan and its base: „Blueprint basierend auf validierter M-Metapher“ ^[L11].
- The document gives its own reason as a rebuild of a plot after a validation: „Die Notwendigkeit dieser Rekonstruktion ergibt sich aus der kritischen Validierungsanalyse“ ^[L17]. The validation analysis is another text that the document reports; this note does not have it.
- The document sets its size as a three-act plan: „Drei-Akt-Struktur mit ca. 39 Kapiteln/Beats“ ^[L27].
- The document calls its guiding principle by a name it puts in quotation marks: „NZT-Ideal“ ^[L33].
- Section II is a restatement of rules that the document takes from a user query, which it names `Nutzer-Query` ^[plot-rekonstruktion-kohaerenz-protokoll.md:#15]. It calls them „unumstößliche Regeln für die gesamte Plot-Rekonstruktion“ ^[L37]. This is a claim about its own standing and is recorded, not applied.
- **Observed:** each of the fifteen sketched beats repeats nine field labels, among them `Inhalt` ^[plot-rekonstruktion-kohaerenz-protokoll.md:#15]; the concept section uses `Kernkonzept` ^[plot-rekonstruktion-kohaerenz-protokoll.md:#5] and the glossary uses `Definition` ^[plot-rekonstruktion-kohaerenz-protokoll.md:#7] and `Narrative Anwendung` ^[plot-rekonstruktion-kohaerenz-protokoll.md:#6]. They are template labels.
- The sketches are a sample: „Beat-by-Beat Skizze (Ausgewählte Beispiele)“ ^[L125].

## 2 · AEGIS

- The document places AEGIS in the sea of potential as a guard: „AEGIS ist eine Wächter-KI, positioniert am Rand der Nicht-Existenz“ ^[L41].
- Its aim and way are given as „Ihr oberstes Ziel ist Stabilität“ ^[L41] and, for its definition, „Ihre Definition erfolgt primär durch Negation und Abgrenzung“ ^[L41].
- The document gives the first purpose of AEGIS's simulations as „Zur Verteidigung gegen das Potentialmeer“ ^[L41]. `Potentialmeer` ^[plot-rekonstruktion-kohaerenz-protokoll.md:#4] is used as already known, with no definition beyond the phrase in L41.
- AEGIS is not framed as malicious: „AEGIS' Handeln ist nicht primär von Bosheit getrieben“ ^[L42].
- Cosmic horror is derived in part from it: „Der kosmische Horror entspringt teilweise dieser radikalen Andersartigkeit von AEGIS“ ^[L42].
- In the plan, AEGIS's responses range from passive observation to alarm: „Alarmstufe Rot“ ^[L200] in Beat 12, and the document names the outcome options of Beat 34 as „Selbst-Partitionierung: AEGIS zieht sich zurück“ ^[L358].

## 3 · The Monstergruppe and the M-Paradoxon

- The document names the paradox of the group as the engine of the horror: „Das Paradox als Motor des Horrors“ ^[L54].
- It says AEGIS must fail at it: „zerlegender Ansatz muss an dieser fundamentalen algebraischen Unteilbarkeit scheitern“ ^[L46].
- The document writes `M-Paradoxon` ^[plot-rekonstruktion-kohaerenz-protokoll.md:#22] and the shorter `M-Paradox` ^[plot-rekonstruktion-kohaerenz-protokoll.md:#7] standing alone, the same idea in two surfaces (the shorter form appears in table and beat labels, for example L119).
- The numbers and group facts around it are the document's own report of mathematical statements, carried by glued reference digits in the export; this note does not use them.

## 4 · Kael and Juna

- The document says the two are not constituents of the group: „Kael und Juna sind nicht als direkte Repräsentanten von Baby-Monster-Gruppen (B) zu verstehen“ ^[L59].
- It gives the reason: „Diese Vorstellung ist mathematisch inkohärent“ ^[L59].
- It places Kael's disorder as a reaction caused by AEGIS: „AEGIS ist unmittelbar für Kaels Zustand verantwortlich“ ^[L66].
- `Juna` ^[plot-rekonstruktion-kohaerenz-protokoll.md:#25] is counted as a word. In the sketches Juna is a connection partner and carries a role in two beats: „Juna könnte hier eine Schlüsselrolle spielen“ ^[L344] and „Juna spielt eine entscheidende Rolle“ ^[L394].
- `Alters` ^[plot-rekonstruktion-kohaerenz-protokoll.md:#15] is used for Kael's personality states and also as the name of an analyst avatar (L217 and L225): „Analysten-Avatar“ ^[L225] is followed in the line by the name Alters in quotation marks. The document uses one word for two things.

## 5 · The K-J Signatur and the Moonshine-Signatur

- The document writes the connection as specific, not generic: „Die Verbindung zwischen Kael und Juna ist keine generische Emergenz oder Resonanz“ ^[L71].
- It gives the effect as „Ihre Wirkung ist sub-protokollarisch“ ^[L71].
- Of the mechanisms it offers for the signature, the document says: „Sie ist ein Hinweis auf die wahre Physik der Simulation“ ^[L86].
- The document writes `K-J Signatur` ^[plot-rekonstruktion-kohaerenz-protokoll.md:#49], `K-J Verbindung` ^[plot-rekonstruktion-kohaerenz-protokoll.md:#11] and `Moonshine-Signatur` ^[plot-rekonstruktion-kohaerenz-protokoll.md:#8]. - In the plan the signature is used by Kael in the final confrontation: „Kael nutzt die K-J Signatur“ ^[L381].

## 6 · The core conflict and the limits of AEGIS's logic

- The document sums the conflict as AEGIS fighting the results of its own method: „AEGIS kämpft somit gegen die Konsequenzen seiner eigenen Methodik“ ^[L94].
- It also reads the conflict as a philosophical one: „Die Handlung wird zu einer Untersuchung“ ^[L104].
- It names a weakness of AEGIS's logic, as a possibility: „könnte sich paradoxerweise als Nachteil erweisen“ ^[L105].
