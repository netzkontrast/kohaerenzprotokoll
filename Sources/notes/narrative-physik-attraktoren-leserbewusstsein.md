# Note — Narrative Physik: Attraktoren & Leserbewusstsein

What this document says about the terms that matter in it. Every quotation carries its file line on the same line of this note; a number about the whole document is a count mark that `quotes.py` checks. Where the note says **observed**, no quotation is possible (a structure, a gap) and the claim rests on the lines or counts it names. The stance is that of a definition-and-proposal text: it states its definitions as constructions, labels its physics language as metaphor, and ends in a recommendation. The first-person form does not occur in the body (section 9); the one address in the second person (`Du`) is a proposed voice of the narration, not a passage (see section 7).

## 1 · What kind of text this is, and how it marks itself

- The first part says of itself that it „etabliert die fundamentale „Physik“ des Romanuniversums“ ^[L22]; the document presents its content as the laws of the novel's world.
- The quantum language is labelled a metaphor by the document: „nicht um eine buchstäbliche physikalische Erklärung“ ^[L44]. The word `Metapher` ^[narrative-physik-attraktoren-leserbewusstsein.md:#4] stands four times, `Tabelle` ^[narrative-physik-attraktoren-leserbewusstsein.md:#3] three times and `Empfehlung` ^[narrative-physik-attraktoren-leserbewusstsein.md:#1] once.
- About the three force names the document says that they are not defined in the documents it worked from: „Da diese Begriffe in den vorliegenden Dokumenten nicht explizit definiert sind“ ^[L79]. Its second part „konstruiert systematisch die Definitionen“ ^[L79] of them. This is the document's report about other documents, not their text.
- Table 1 is a plan for the narration: it „dient als Leitfaden, wie die Präsenz des Lesers als ontologisches Foreshadowing in die Erzählung eingewoben wird“ ^[L60].
- **Observed:** the last block, headed `Referenzen` ^[L239], is a numbered source list of web pages and a few titles that read as project documents (L241, L242, L251, L281, L294). It carries no argument of its own.

## 2 · The reader as 'O'

- The document's premise is that the reader is „der aktive, wenngleich zunächst unbewusste, Mitschöpfer der narrativen Realität“ ^[L30]. `Mitschöpfer` ^[narrative-physik-attraktoren-leserbewusstsein.md:#2] stands twice.
- The document ties this to a principle that existence consists in being perceived: „ihre Realität wird ausschließlich durch den Akt des Wahrgenommenwerdens durch ein Bewusstsein konstituiert“ ^[L32]. The document calls the resulting synthesis the „Kerngesetz der Simulation“ ^[L30].
- The narrative world is modelled as a closed system, and the reader as its environment: the document says the reader is defined as the „transzendente Umwelt“ ^[L34] of that system.
- AEGIS misreads the principle, in the document's account: „Es geht davon aus, dass nur das existiert, was vom System selbst wahrgenommen wird“ ^[L36]. For AEGIS the reader's consciousness is, in the document's words, „eine logische und ontologische Unmöglichkeit“ ^[L36], and the document calls AEGIS a „solipsistische Maschine“ ^[L36].
- Part IV proposes to turn this to the reader: the climax is to break immersion, „um die Immersion des Lesers zu durchbrechen“ ^[L209], and to move the reader „vom unbewussten Teilnehmer zum bewussten Mitautor“ ^[L209].

## 3 · The quantum metaphor

- The unread novel is described as existing „in einem Zustand der quantenmechanischen Superposition“ ^[L46] in the document's model, and reading is the measurement.
- On reading, the story collapses into one definite reality: „Die Geschichte kollabiert von einer Superposition aller Möglichkeiten“ ^[L48]. The document concludes that every reading is „ein einzigartiger Akt der Welterschaffung“ ^[L48]. `Kollaps der Wellenfunktion` ^[narrative-physik-attraktoren-leserbewusstsein.md:#4] stands four times, two of them in titles of the reference list.
- In Part IV the unreliable narration is framed as a measurement problem: the narration given to the reader is „das Ergebnis einer Messung mit einem fehlerhaften Gerät“ ^[L219], the faulty device being Kael's consciousness.

## 4 · Glitches

- The system cannot see the reader, but it can be disturbed by the reader's engagement: „Das System kann den Leser nicht direkt“ ^[L56] is how the sentence opens, and the document sets the two verbs of disturbance in its own quotation marks in the rest of that line.
- The document defines the glitches as „die literalisierten, in der Welt stattfindenden Manifestationen“ ^[L56] of that interaction. `Glitches` ^[narrative-physik-attraktoren-leserbewusstsein.md:#3] stands three times.
- It reads them also as entropy events: „sondern lokalisierte entropische Ereignisse“ ^[L58]. The reader's attention and reactions „injizieren kontinuierlich Unsicherheit“ ^[L58] into the world AEGIS keeps ordered.
- Table 1 pairs each anomaly type with a reader correlate, an effect on Kael and an effect on the attractors. Examples: perceptual instability pairs with „Schwankende Aufmerksamkeit des Lesers, Blinzeln oder Ablenkung“ ^[L67] and weakens Logos; temporal discontinuity pairs with „Der Leser legt das Buch weg und nimmt es später wieder auf“ ^[L68] and „öffnet Potenzial für Abyssos“ ^[L68]; the emotional-resonance row ends with „Stärkt Eros durch die direkte Übertragung von Empathie“ ^[L70]; the clairvoyance row is „Direkter Hinweis auf die transzendente Ebene“ ^[L71].
- **Observed:** the table names `Echo-Intrusion` ^[narrative-physik-attraktoren-leserbewusstsein.md:#1] only as an example inside the emotional-resonance row (L70).

## 5 · The three attractors

- Logos is defined as a force that strives „nach Kohärenz, Logik, Kontrolle, Stasis und negentropischer Stabilität“ ^[L87]; it is „der Antrieb, der vorhersagbare, geschlossene Systeme erschafft und Ambiguität eliminiert“ ^[L87].
- Abyssos is defined in the second part as the drive toward entropy and dissolution, and as the force that dissolves structure: „Es ist die Kraft, die Strukturen zersetzt“ ^[L101].
- Eros is defined as a connecting force: „Es ist eine anti-entropische Kraft, die emergente, dynamische Strukturen erschafft“ ^[L115].
- Primary manifestations as the document states them: Logos is „die KI AEGIS“ ^[L91], and the document places the core world KW1, named in a bracket as Logos-Prime, as „der Inbegriff dieses Prinzips“ ^[L91]; Abyssos is „die existenzielle Bedrohung durch das“ ^[L105] surrounding non-being; Eros is „Kaels gesamte Reise zur Heilung“ ^[L119].
- The document's account of AEGIS under Logos: its methods are aimed at cultivating „Persönlichkeitsanteile zu kultivieren“ ^[L91] that are predictable and functional, and removing emotional data corruption.
- The central conflict is stated as three-sided: „keine einfache Dualität, sondern ein triadischer Kampf“ ^[L123] is the sentence at that line. Logos is not neutral but „eine pathologische Reaktion auf die Furcht vor Abyssos“ ^[L123], and Eros is „einen dritten Weg“ ^[L123].
- Table 2 sums the assignment per attractor in seven columns; the last column gives „AEGIS' Kontrollprotokoll; KW1“ ^[L130] for Logos, „Realitätszusammenbruch“ ^[L131] (with the Nichts) for Abyssos and „Kaels Integration; Juna/V“ ^[L132] for Eros. `Logos` ^[narrative-physik-attraktoren-leserbewusstsein.md:#20], `Abyssos` ^[narrative-physik-attraktoren-leserbewusstsein.md:#17] and `Eros` ^[narrative-physik-attraktoren-leserbewusstsein.md:#20] stand as whole words that many times.
- **Observed:** the psychoanalytic, philosophical and genre correlates in Table 2 and in Part II are the document's applications of borrowed frameworks to the three forces; they are listed under `## lens` in the census.

## 6 · The dynamical model

- The narration is conceptualised as a path in a phase space: „ein mehrdimensionaler Raum, in dem jeder mögliche Zustand eines Systems abgebildet werden kann“ ^[L148]. The axes are Kael's degree of integration (Eros), AEGIS's degree of control (Logos) and the threat of the Nichts (Abyssos), read at ^[L148].
- Each force is a strange attractor: „Eros, Logos und Abyssos werden als drei konkurrierende seltsame Attraktoren“ ^[L156] is the first half of the sentence. The Abyssos bullet reads „Der Abyssos-Attraktor repräsentiert ein Anziehungsbecken hin zu narrativer Auflösung“ ^[L159], and the Eros bullet „Der Eros-Attraktor repräsentiert ein Anziehungsbecken hin zu einem Zustand hoher Komplexität“ ^[L160].
- The plot is the path between them: „Die Handlung des Romans ist der Pfad, den das System nimmt“ ^[L162].
- The reader enters as a control parameter: „das System kontinuierlich pertubiert“ ^[L170] (the document's spelling). `Kontrollparameter` ^[narrative-physik-attraktoren-leserbewusstsein.md:#2] stands twice. The reader's focus is hypothesised to strengthen one pull or another: „könnte die Anziehungskraft des Abyssos-Attraktors“ ^[L173] is the form of the second hypothesis.
- The four story types are mapped to trajectories (L186 to L189), for example „Tragödie (Herbst): Eine Trajektorie, die mit hohem Potenzial beginnt“ ^[L188]. The reader's familiarity with these structures „prägt seine Erwartungen“ ^[L191].

## 7 · The architecture: what the document recommends

- Gaps in the narration are to be built in on purpose: they are „nicht bloß erzählerische Lücken, sondern Portale“ ^[L207]. `Leerstellen` ^[narrative-physik-attraktoren-leserbewusstsein.md:#3] stands three times.
- The document proposes address in a voice of the narration: a direct address with the example „Und Du? Was fühlst Du?“ ^[L209]; this is a proposed voice, not a passage.
- Kael's perception is described as that of an unreliable narrator, built from parts; the parts called managers „unterdrücken aktiv Informationen und erzeugen Gedächtnislücken“ ^[L217].
- The recommended structure is „Die Frakturierte Untersuchung“ ^[L227] in the document's naming: it „kombiniert einen Mosaik-Plot“ ^[L229] with multiple personal narrators and an anachronistic time structure. Its reasons include that the mosaic form „fordert den Leser ('O') aktiv auf, die Erzählung zusammenzusetzen“ ^[L233], that the multiple perspectives allow „eine direkte Darstellung von Kaels TSDP/IFS-System“ ^[L234], and that the time structure „modelliert direkt die Erfahrung von Trauma“ ^[L235].
- The closing claim of the document is that the framework makes the O-paradox „eine thematisch resonante und intellektuell befriedigende Enthüllung“ ^[L237]. This is the document's claim about its own proposal and is recorded, not applied.

## 8 · Said two ways, or left open — recorded, not resolved

- Logos is described as the drive to negentropic stability (L87), and Eros as an „anti-entropische Kraft“ ^[L115]; the document then calls both ordering forces anti-entropic: „zwei verschiedenen anti-entropischen Kräften“ ^[L123]. Observed: the document separates them by the kind of order, sterile against living, and not by their relation to entropy.
- The prose names „die KI AEGIS“ ^[L91] as the primary manifestation of Logos, and the table gives „AEGIS' Kontrollprotokoll; KW1“ ^[L130]. Observed: the table adds KW1 and names the control protocol; the document does not remark on the difference.
- The reader is a „fundamentale Kraft des Chaos“ ^[L58] in Part I, and a control parameter that can strengthen any of the three attractors in Part III (L172 to L174). Observed: the two statements assign the reader a different relation to Abyssos; the document does not reconcile them.
- Terms used without a defining sentence in the lines the census names: `TSDP` and `Manager` (L103, L217), `Funktionalen Multiplizität` (L119), `Kernwelt` and `KW1` (L91). Observed from the census table.
