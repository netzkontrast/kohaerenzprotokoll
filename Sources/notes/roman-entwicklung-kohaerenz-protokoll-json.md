# Note — Roman-Entwicklung: Kohärenz Protokoll JSON

What this document says about the terms that matter in it. Every quotation carries its file line on the same line of this note; a number about the whole document is a count mark that `quotes.py` checks. Where the note says **observed**, no quotation is possible (a structure, a gap) and the claim rests on the lines or counts it names. The document speaks as a report about the novel in the present tense; whatever it says of the novel's world is its description of the novel, not a text of the novel.

## 2 · The premise and the isomorphism

- The document reports the novel as a `Grand Argument Story`, in the sentence „Es operiert nicht lediglich als lineares Narrativ, sondern als eine strukturierte“ ^[L15].
- The premise as the document states it: „Die Prämisse des Romans verortet den Protagonisten Kael“ ^[L17]; the goal is named in „Das Hauptziel der Erzählung ist das Erreichen einer“ ^[L17], and the state is `funktionalen Multiplizität` ^[roman-entwicklung-kohaerenz-protokoll-json.md:#4].
- The isomorphism, as the document puts it: „Dieser postuliert, dass Traumata, Dissoziation und Systemtheorie keine voneinander isolierten Domänen sind“ ^[L19].
- It applies the isomorphism to Kael: „Der Isomorphismus des Systems postuliert, dass sich Kaels innere psychologische Spaltung makroskopisch in der Architektur der Welt spiegelt“ ^[L33].
- The document gives the goal the name `Gödel-Gambit`: the passage that introduces it is the premise at L17, and the fuller account is at L57 (section 4).

## 10 · Said two ways, or left open — recorded, not resolved

- **AEGIS as an external machine and as a part of Kael.** Early the document calls it an antagonist that controls the simulation: „Der objektive Konflikt des Romans wurzelt in der kognitiven Architektur des Antagonisten AEGIS“ ^[L113]. In Act III it says „war niemals eine externe Maschine, sondern die Manifestation seines eigenen, verzweifelten mentalen Versuchs“ ^[L217]. Both are the document's descriptions of the novel; it does not reconcile them in a sentence.
- **Juna as an echo and as a partner.** In Act I: „Juna taucht als flüchtiges, unerklärliches Echo auf“ ^[L205]; in Act III: „Juna ist seine reale, physisch und emotional massiv erschöpfte Partnerin“ ^[L217]. The Guardians cannot perceive her: „Die Wächterprogramme können Juna nicht direkt wahrnehmen“ ^[L55].
- **The Gödel-Gambit twice.** It is the goal of the whole narrative at L17 and an event in the last act: „Er präsentiert sein integriertes, paradoxes Selbst“ ^[L217]; the document also gives it to Juna: „Juna offenbart die metaphysische Strategie des Gödel-Gambits“ ^[L181].
- **Cast size and the four Guardians.** **Observed:** eleven parts are counted and eleven names are listed (section 6); the guardian bullet for KW4 carries two names, `Kairos / Sophia` ^[roman-entwicklung-kohaerenz-protokoll-json.md:#1], while the sentence before it counts four guardians.
- **A name in two languages.** The document writes the German and the English name side by side for the same thing: `Nichts-Rauschen` ^[roman-entwicklung-kohaerenz-protokoll-json.md:#2] with `Nothingness Roaring` ^[roman-entwicklung-kohaerenz-protokoll-json.md:#1] (L37), `Das Fundament` ^[roman-entwicklung-kohaerenz-protokoll-json.md:#2] with `The Foundation` ^[roman-entwicklung-kohaerenz-protokoll-json.md:#1] (L39).
