# Note — Outline

What this document says about the terms that matter in it. Every quotation carries its file line on the same line of this note; a number about the whole document is a count mark that `quotes.py` checks. Where the note says **observed**, no quotation is possible (a structure, a gap) and the claim rests on the lines or counts it names. The document is a plan: its paragraphs state intended events and mark their own uncertainty with question marks.

## 1 · What kind of text this is, and how it marks itself

- The document names its own kind: „Zusammenstellung der Outline-Informationen (Neues Format)“ ^[L15]. `Neues Format` ^[outline-2.md:#1] stands once.
- It divides the outline into acts whose headings give a focus: „Innere Reise (Heroine's Journey Fokus)“ ^[L33], then „Meta-Ebene & Zyklen (Meta-Exploration)“ ^[L217], then „Äußere Konfrontation & Rückkehr (Hero's Journey Fokus)“ ^[L401].
- **Observed:** the prologue and 39 chapters each open with the label `Chapter` ^[outline-2.md:#40], a bracketed title, one paragraph, and seven field lines: `Core Theme` ^[outline-2.md:#40], `Kael Internal` ^[outline-2.md:#40], `AEGIS Focus` ^[outline-2.md:#37], `Setting` ^[outline-2.md:#40], `Subplots` ^[outline-2.md:#40], `Philosophy` ^[outline-2.md:#40], `Genre/Trope` ^[outline-2.md:#40]. The labels are the template; `AEGIS Focus` is missing from the last three chapters (L544 to L583).
- The paragraphs hedge by question mark inside the plan itself, for example „Kael (Lex/Argus?) erkennt dies als Bedrohung (Kollaps?) und Chance (Flucht? Schwachstelle?).“ ^[L179].

## 2 · The prologue and the origin of the system

- The prologue paragraph places AEGIS and its precursor at the centre: „Die Erzählung fokussiert auf die Entität AEGIS oder ihr Vorläufer-Ich (Komponente 734).“ ^[L23].
- It states a catastrophe-and-resonance sequence: „Eine spätere Resonanz des Echos mit einer externen Anomalie (Juna/V?) löst eine Systemkrise aus.“ ^[L23]. The identity of `Juna/V` is marked with a question mark in this same sentence.
- It ends the sequence with a violent split: „was zur gewaltsamen Fragmentierung von Komponente 734 führt“ ^[L23], and calls this „der Geburtsstunde von System Kael“ ^[L23].
- The prologue's own field line gives the origin as trauma: „Traumatischer Ursprung der Fragmentierung etabliert“ ^[L26]. `Genesis` ^[outline-2.md:#2] is the bracketed title of the prologue at L21 and is named again at L449 as the possible core trauma.
- `Kohärenz Protokoll` ^[outline-2.md:#1] is written once, in the prologue paragraph, as the thing AEGIS activates as a reaction.

- Chapter 1 describes Kael as host who experiences loss of orientation: „Kael (Host) erlebt Desorientierung, Zeitverlust und Amnesie“ ^[L39]; it adds that she has „Keine bewusste Wahrnehmung anderer Anteile.“ ^[L39].
- Chapter 3 names the protector part: „Der Beschützer-Anteil Alex wird aktiviert“ ^[L67]. Chapter 4 names the caregiver part: „Der Fürsorger-Anteil Rhys tritt hervor“ ^[L81]. `Lex` ^[outline-2.md:#24], `Alex` ^[outline-2.md:#17] and `Rhys` ^[outline-2.md:#13] are the parts the field lines name most.
- The outline uses the abbreviations `ANPs` ^[outline-2.md:#8] and `EPs` ^[outline-2.md:#12] without expanding them. Chapter 6 lists the parts it expects among the EPs, with a question mark: „Ein oder mehrere EPs (Kiko, Lia, Moros?) werden stärker präsent“ ^[L109].
- `Selene` ^[outline-2.md:#10] is mostly offered as a guess in the field lines, for example „Selene als Koordinatorin?“ ^[L394] and „Kael (integriertes System, Selene?) wählt“ ^[L548].
- `Argus` ^[outline-2.md:#12] is named in a field line as „Argus (Meta-Beobachter) aktiv?.“ ^[L196] and in chapter 14 as working with Lex: „Kael (primär Lex/Argus) beginnt systematisch, AEGIS' Meta-Ebene (Überwelt) zu untersuchen“ ^[L223].
- The end state is named in the field line of chapter 38: „Leben in/mit funktionaler Multiplizität; Neue Realität erfahren“ ^[L565]. The word `Multiplizität` ^[outline-2.md:#7] is used in the inflected forms `funktionale` and `funktionaler` and the outline never defines it; chapter 39 says the end „kann offen bleiben bzgl. Mysterien“ ^[L576] while the epilog is the finale: „Epilog/Finale Szene.“ ^[L576].
