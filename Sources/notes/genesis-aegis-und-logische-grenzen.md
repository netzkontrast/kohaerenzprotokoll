## 2 · The noise: `Rauschen`, `Nichts Rauschen`

- The preface describes the state before the world as noisy, not silent: „ein Chaos, das tobte“ ^[L15].
- It calls this state one of „Ein Zustand maximaler Entropie“ ^[L15] and a „eine entropische Flut“ ^[L17] that threatens islands of coherence.
- The preface lists other names for it as what „Manche nennen es den Abgrund“ ^[L19] (the passage goes on to `Wuji` and `Quantenvakuum`, L19).
- The noise is written as a compound-like name, `Nichts Rauschen` ^[genesis-aegis-und-logische-grenzen.md:#2], and in the genitive `Nichts Rauschens` ^[genesis-aegis-und-logische-grenzen.md:#2]; `Rauschen` stands alone `Rauschen` ^[genesis-aegis-und-logische-grenzen.md:#20] times.
