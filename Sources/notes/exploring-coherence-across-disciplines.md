## 3 · Philosophy: coherence and correspondence

- The coherence theory of truth, as the report presents it, holds that „the truth of any given proposition consists not in its relationship to an external, objective world“ ^[L40].
- The report says „The coherence theory is defined primarily in opposition to its principal competitor“ ^[L48].
- On justification, the report notes that „Many modern philosophers adopt a coherence theory of justification while simultaneously holding a correspondence theory of truth“ ^[L64].
- The report names two objections. The first: „coherence, being a purely internal property of a belief system, provides no guarantee of a connection to external reality“ ^[L88].
