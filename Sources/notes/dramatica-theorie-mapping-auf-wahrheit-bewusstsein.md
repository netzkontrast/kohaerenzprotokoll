## 2 · The narrative theory's vocabulary, as the document reports it

- The `Four Throughlines` ^[dramatica-theorie-mapping-auf-wahrheit-bewusstsein.md:#1] are described as „Die vier zwingend erforderlichen Perspektiven zur Betrachtung des Kernkonflikts“ ^[L39]. This is the document's description of the theory; the throughlines are Main Character, Influence Character, Subjective Story and Objective Story, written as I, You, We and They.
- The `Story Mind` ^[dramatica-theorie-mapping-auf-wahrheit-bewusstsein.md:#3] is reported as one mind of a story: „einen einzigen makroskopischen Verstand“ ^[L38].
- The theory's rule for the `Four Domains` ^[dramatica-theorie-mapping-auf-wahrheit-bewusstsein.md:#1], as reported: „jede der vier Throughlines exakt einer dieser Domänen zugeordnet wird“ ^[L40]. The domains are named `Universe`, `Physics`, `Mind` and `Psychology` there.
- `Story Limit` ^[dramatica-theorie-mapping-auf-wahrheit-bewusstsein.md:#1] is given as „Das Story Limit (Optionlock vs. Timelock) setzt die thermodynamische Endbedingung“ ^[L43]. The document attaches a thermodynamic reading to the theory's term.
- Outcome and judgment are reported as separate: „Das Outcome (Success/Failure) bewertet, ob das objektive Ziel in der OS-Throughline physikalisch erreicht wurde“ ^[L44].

## 4 · The mapping and the Grand Argument

- The central thesis is the document's own: „isomorphe Ausprägungen der Witness Function“ ^[L106] are consciousness, truth and narration, and the throughlines give „die exakte logisch-ontologische Topologie“ ^[L106] for it.
- The conclusion names a consequence of leaving one throughline out: a mind or story that omits one of the four „scheitert unweigerlich an der Topologie der Realität“ ^[L118].
- The document marks where its own mapping is weak. It warns against mapping the Influence Character as plain destruction: „Dies widerspricht Dramatica, da der IC oft moralisch recht hat“ ^[L333]. The line is one of the document's self-reflections.
- It calls its Objective Story image „eine gigantische, eiskalte thermodynamische Registrierkasse“ ^[L100] and then, in the break note, reports the theory's emphasis on free decisions.
- It lists falsification conditions; one is that story models without a throughline could still be judged complete by „bei einer signifikanten Mehrheit menschlicher Rezipienten“ ^[L127].
