- It admits the real AEGIS combat system and cache-coherence protocols only as a metaphorical hint: „metaphorischer Anhaltspunkt“ ^[L25].
- **Observed:** the brief the analysis answers is not shown; it is cited as `User Query` ^[tsdp-analyse-kohaerenz-protokoll-charaktere.md:#40] after claims, so some statements about the novel (for example the item codes PP-INT-01 to PP-INT-04, PP-SYS-02, PP-SYS-03, PP-CCH-01) come from that brief and are not defined in the document.
