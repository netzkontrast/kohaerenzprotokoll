## 2 · System Kael and functional multiplicity

- The document introduces the personalities as „alters“ ^[L15] who coexist in Kael's mind, and calls the whole `System Kael` ^[a-guide-to-the-society-of-self-understanding-system-kael.md:#4] this „internal society“ ^[L15].
- The goal of the journey is „functional multiplicity“ ^[L17], which the document describes as „a state of harmonious collaboration where every alter is heard, respected, and works together as a cohesive whole“ ^[L17].
- Of the parts it says: „They are not symptoms to be cured, but survivors of trauma to be understood and integrated.“ ^[L17]
- Its thesis on coherence: „True, resilient coherence emerges not from the forceful elimination of paradox and complexity“ ^[L19].
- The final state is named `Rhizomatic Self` ^[a-guide-to-the-society-of-self-understanding-system-kael.md:#1]: „The final, integrated state they strive for is described as the“ ^[L141].
