## 2 · The premise and the two orders

- The logline puts the story as a man „fractured into a society of warring personalities“ ^[L19] set against „a godlike AI programmed to destroy his complexity“ ^[L19].
- The document says the personal and the systemic level are linked, since „Kael's personal healing is the very mechanism that rewrites the flawed physics of his prison“ ^[L21].
- Its opening gives the thesis that coherence comes from the „courageous and compassionate integration“ ^[L15] of paradox, not from its elimination.
- Two orders are set against each other under the labels `Coherence via Negation` ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-the-official-master-story-guide-2.md:#1] and `Coherence via Integration` ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-the-official-master-story-guide-2.md:#1]. For AEGIS, „Contradiction is an error to be purged“ ^[L30]; for Kael, „Contradiction is generative“ ^[L30].
- The Kael side is headed by the `Dialetheical Mind` ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-the-official-master-story-guide-2.md:#1], and the AEGIS side by classical logic with `ex contradictione quodlibet` ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-the-official-master-story-guide-2.md:#1].

## 3 · AEGIS

- The document says AEGIS is to be framed as a „tragic, autopoietic system trapped by its own flawed logic“ ^[L75] and not as a villain.
- It gives AEGIS one axiom, set in bold: „AEGIS is what AEGIS prevents itself from not being“ ^[L83].
- The core logic is stated as `Logics of Formal Inconsistency` ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-the-official-master-story-guide-2.md:#1], abbreviated `LFI` ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-the-official-master-story-guide-2.md:#1]. Under it propositions are marked consistent or inconsistent and the inconsistent are quarantined.
- AEGIS applies `Discursive Logic` ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-the-official-master-story-guide-2.md:#1] to Kael's psyche, „treating each of his alters as a separate, distinct“ ^[L93] speaker; the document calls this the source of AEGIS's „tragic blindness“ ^[L93].
- The fatal flaw is named `Paradox of Misaligned Coherence` ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-the-official-master-story-guide-2.md:#1]: AEGIS must read Kael's healing as „a dangerous rise in system entropy“ ^[L95].
- Its final arc is the `Gödel-Gambit` ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-the-official-master-story-guide-2.md:#1] and its cost `Algorithmic Melancholy` ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-the-official-master-story-guide-2.md:#2], which shows as a world of „inefficient beauty“ ^[L102].
- The document sets the Gödel move out as a „living Gödel-sentence“ ^[L101].

## 4 · Kael and the alters

- The document says Kael „must not be treated as a singular character“ ^[L108] but as `System Kael` ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-the-official-master-story-guide-2.md:#4], and models the psyche on `TSDP` ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-the-official-master-story-guide-2.md:#4].
- It sorts the parts into `Apparently Normal Parts` ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-the-official-master-story-guide-2.md:#1] and `Emotional Parts` ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-the-official-master-story-guide-2.md:#1], and says the system „constitutes a case of tertiary dissociation“ ^[L117].
- The table is headed „Alter Name (Kanonisch)“ ^[L125] and the document's own label for it is the 11-alter model ^[L121]. **Observed:** it has eleven rows (L126 to L136), one of which is `Kael` ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-the-official-master-story-guide-2.md:#28] himself, listed as „ANP (Host)“ ^[L126].
- On Selene the table says she is „ANP/EP (Integrator)“ ^[L131]; on Argus it says „ANP/EP (Observer)“ ^[L136]. Only these two rows carry both classes.
- The arc is not a cure: it is „a state of co-consciousness, communication, cooperation, and shared responsibility“ ^[L140], named `functional multiplicity` ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-the-official-master-story-guide-2.md:#4].
- The document calls the integration an „offensive weapon“ ^[L142] against AEGIS and the final state the „living refutation“ ^[L142] of its philosophy.

- The document introduces one paragraph as „The following is the canonical model for this style“ ^[L193] and gives it at L195.
- Two metafictional techniques are called mandatory: `Contradictory Footnotes` ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-the-official-master-story-guide-2.md:#1] with „mutually exclusive interpretations of events“ ^[L201], and a `Found Footage` ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-the-official-master-story-guide-2.md:#1] epilogue, where „The novel must not end with a single, authoritative epilogue“ ^[L202].
