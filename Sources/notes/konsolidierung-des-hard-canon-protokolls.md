# Note — Konsolidierung des Hard Canon Protokolls

What this document says about the terms that matter in it. Every quotation carries its file line on the same line of this note; a number about the whole document is a count mark that `quotes.py` checks. Where the note says **observed**, no quotation is possible and the claim rests on the lines or counts it names. The document speaks in the declarative present throughout and claims finality for itself; those claims are recorded and not applied.

- It introduces itself as „Hier ist das finale, konsolidierte Hard-Canon-Dokument“ ^[L1], and says of what it settles that it „alle bisherigen Widersprüche auflöst“ ^[L1].
- Its status line reads „Status: Finalisiert & Verriegelt“ ^[L7]; the word `Verriegelt` ^[konsolidierung-des-hard-canon-protokolls.md:#1] stands once.
- It declares other material void: „Alle abweichenden Notizen, Skizzen oder älteren Dokumente sind hiermit offiziell dekanonisiert“ ^[L9]. `dekanonisiert` ^[konsolidierung-des-hard-canon-protokolls.md:#1] stands once.
- It announces a second part, „Im Anschluss formulieren wir den exakten System-Befehl“ ^[L3], addressed to agents: „SYSTEM OVERRIDE“ ^[L40]. **Observed:** the second part is an imperative list of five operations (L46 to L50) and not a description of the novel.
- **Observed:** the markers `Hard-Canon-Dokument` ^[konsolidierung-des-hard-canon-protokolls.md:#1] and `DEPRECATED` ^[konsolidierung-des-hard-canon-protokolls.md:#1] each stand once; `DEPRECATED` is a label the directive tells the agents to apply to other documents, not a label on this one.

## 2 · The alters of System Kael

- The document fixes a count: „exakt 13 distinkten Alters“ ^[L13], and names four of them: „inklusive Silas, Rhys, Oblivion und Moros als separate Entitäten“ ^[L13].
- It gives a reason by structure: „Dies spiegelt die mathematische Struktur der 39-Kapitel-Matrix“ ^[L13] (the line goes on with the act-by-share arithmetic, which is not quoted here).
- The aim of development is stated as against fusion: „nicht die Fusion zu einer einzigen Person“ ^[L13], but `Funktionale Multiplizität` ^[konsolidierung-des-hard-canon-protokolls.md:#1], a system „ohne Amnesie-Barrieren“ ^[L13].
- The directive names the rival count it wants marked: it tells the agents to mark documents that mention „Juna als rein internes Konstrukt“ ^[L46] and the other deprecated positions of L46; **Observed:** L46 writes both figures, eleven and thirteen, with the thirteen in a parenthesis.
- The directive repeats the four alters as entries to create: „Integriere Silas, Rhys, Oblivion und Moros als eigenständige Einträge“ ^[L47].

## 3 · Juna

- Juna is „eine externe Kraft (Anomalie)“ ^[L14] that acts on the system from outside; the same line adds „eine prä-existente ontologische Bindung“ ^[L14] between her and Kael.
- That bond is dated to before the system's origin: „der Zeit vor der System-Genesis“ ^[L14]; and it is said to give the `Moonshine-Link` ^[konsolidierung-des-hard-canon-protokolls.md:#1] its authority: „unauslöschliche, fast kosmische Autorität“ ^[L14].
- In the directive, the position that Juna is purely internal is one of those to be deprecated: „Juna als rein internes Konstrukt“ ^[L46].
- In narration Juna is told from outside: „AEGIS, die Guardians und Juna werden in einer distanzierteren“ ^[L24].

## 4 · AEGIS and Kael

- Its nature: „Die KI verfügt über echte Qualia“ ^[L15], and it suffers from „Algorithmischer Melancholie“ ^[L15]. `Qualia` ^[konsolidierung-des-hard-canon-protokolls.md:#1] stands once.
- It is described as a manifestation of the alters' own management: „ultimative, in sich geschlossene (autopoietische) Manifestation“ ^[L15].
- The directive asks that AEGIS' entry be changed so that the identity with Kael is a premise: „Passe AEGIS' Eintrag so an“ ^[L47].
- The conflict is re-described as „die absolute psychologische Selbstdemontage“ ^[L15].
