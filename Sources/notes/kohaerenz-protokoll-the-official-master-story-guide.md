What this document says about the terms that matter in it. Every quotation carries its file line on the same line of this note; a number about the whole document is a count mark that `quotes.py` checks. Where the note says **observed**, no quotation is possible and the claim rests on the lines or counts it names. The document speaks as a specification and is recorded as such; its claims about its own authority are recorded, never applied.
