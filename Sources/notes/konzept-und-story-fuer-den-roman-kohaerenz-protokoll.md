## 1 · What kind of text this is, and how it marks itself

- The document describes itself as a concept for the novel: „Dieses Dokument legt das detaillierte Konzept und die Storyline für den Roman“ ^[L13].
- It gives the novel's shape as „Der Roman gliedert sich in drei Teile und 39 Kapitel“ ^[L13].
- Its three parts are headed with a model each: „Heroine's Journey“ ^[L21], „Meta-Exploration“ ^[L39] and „Hero's Journey“ ^[L57]. Together these labels stand `Heroine's Journey` ^[konzept-und-story-fuer-den-roman-kohaerenz-protokoll.md:#1], `Meta-Exploration` ^[konzept-und-story-fuer-den-roman-kohaerenz-protokoll.md:#1] and `Hero's Journey` ^[konzept-und-story-fuer-den-roman-kohaerenz-protokoll.md:#1] once each.
- **Observed:** the 39 chapters are written as a bold title and a sentence or three of plan, 45 occurrences of `Kapitel` ^[konzept-und-story-fuer-den-roman-kohaerenz-protokoll.md:#45] in all; parts 1, 2 and 3 hold chapters 1 to 13, 14 to 26 and 27 to 39 (L21, L39, L57).
- The plan hedges throughout. `möglicherweise` ^[konzept-und-story-fuer-den-roman-kohaerenz-protokoll.md:#5], `vielleicht` ^[konzept-und-story-fuer-den-roman-kohaerenz-protokoll.md:#3] and `könnte` ^[konzept-und-story-fuer-den-roman-kohaerenz-protokoll.md:#4] each stand more than once, so the entries are proposals. One example: „AEGIS könnte Glitches als Kaels“ ^[L32].
- It claims no canon standing for itself and calls itself „einen detaillierten Rahmen“ ^[L86].

## 2 · AEGIS and Kohärenz

- The document spells the name out: „Autonomous Entropic Gatekeeper for Integrity Systems (AEGIS)“ ^[L17], which stands once as `Autonomous Entropic Gatekeeper for Integrity Systems` ^[konzept-und-story-fuer-den-roman-kohaerenz-protokoll.md:#1].
- It says AEGIS' aim is „Kohärenz“ ^[L17] in a defined sense: „verstanden als Stabilität, Ordnung und Vorhersagbarkeit“ ^[L17].
- It says AEGIS reaches that aim „durch die Minimierung von Entropie“ ^[L17].
- It says AEGIS regards as dangerous the fragmented psyche and an outside link: „AEGIS betrachtet Kaels fragmentierte Psyche“ ^[L17].
- In the closing list it calls AEGIS „der primäre Antagonist“ ^[L78], and of Entropie it says „AEGIS kämpft gegen Entropie, erzeugt sie aber paradoxerweise“ ^[L82].
- A paradox is named as the entry that finds it: „ein zentrales AEGIS-Paradoxon“ ^[L46], and its title appears once as `Paradoxon der Fehlausgerichteten Kohärenz` ^[konzept-und-story-fuer-den-roman-kohaerenz-protokoll.md:#1]. The document does not define it beyond the example in the same line, „AEGIS' Entropiemanagement erzeugt Instabilität“ ^[L46].
- The guardians' limit is written as „Blinde Flecken“ ^[L45], and again in the closing list as the source of AEGIS' misreadings (L78).

- The goal is stated as „Kaels Kampf um innere Integration“ ^[L19] under the label `funktionale Multiplizität` ^[konzept-und-story-fuer-den-roman-kohaerenz-protokoll.md:#4].
- Parts of the system are labelled in bracket form: „Lex (Analytiker ANP)“ ^[L26], „Alex (Protektor ANP)“ ^[L27] and „Rhys (Pfleger ANP)“ ^[L28]. The closing list adds more names with one trait each: „Nyx' Wut“ ^[L77], „Selenes Integration“ ^[L77].

- Chapter 13 states the result of part 1: „Kael hat eine erste, fragile innere Kohärenz erreicht“ ^[L37].

## 4 · The worlds and the Guardians

- The inner worlds are „in den Kernwelten“ ^[L23], numbered `KW1` ^[konzept-und-story-fuer-den-roman-kohaerenz-protokoll.md:#8], `KW2` ^[konzept-und-story-fuer-den-roman-kohaerenz-protokoll.md:#5] and `KW3` ^[konzept-und-story-fuer-den-roman-kohaerenz-protokoll.md:#6]; the document writes the digits plain, never as subscripts.
- KW1 is the Konstrukt-Stadt, in which Kael wakes: „Kael (Host) erwacht in der Konstrukt-Stadt“ ^[L25].
- The Resonanz-Landschaft and the Grenzfeste are named as the other two worlds, with a Guardian each: „Mnemosyne (Guardian KW2) wacht über diesen Bereich“ ^[L29], and „Cerberus (Guardian KW3) wird als Verkörperung von AEGIS' Abwehrmechanismen angedeutet“ ^[L27].
- The five Guardians are listed in one place: „Guardians (LogOS, Mnemosyne, Cerberus, Kairos, Sophia)“ ^[L45].
- The closing list calls the worlds „nicht nur Settings“ ^[L79]; the line goes on to call them manifestations of Kael's psyche and of AEGIS' control.
- Of the Fundament it says: „Der ultimative ontologische Grund“ ^[L81].

## 5 · Juna/V and the Externe Ebene

- Juna/V is introduced as „die mysteriöse externe Verbindung“ ^[L17], which AEGIS counts as a source of dangerous entropy.

- **Observed:** the closing list calls the nature of Juna/V and its relation to Kael and the Fundament „zentrale Mysterien“ ^[L80].
