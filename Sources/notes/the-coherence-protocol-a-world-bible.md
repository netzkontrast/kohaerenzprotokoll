## 2 · The Nothingness Noise, The Foundation, The Connection

- The primordial state, according to the text, is „not a passive vacuum“ ^[L23]; it is called `Die Leere` ^[the-coherence-protocol-a-world-bible.md:#1] (The Void) and `Das Nichts Rauschen`.

- `The Foundation` ^[the-coherence-protocol-a-world-bible.md:#11] is said to be „not a substance, a physical place, or a conscious entity“ ^[L33].

## 3 · The Moonshine-Link

- The link is described as „a non-local, sub-protocol connection“ ^[L44] between Kael and `Juna/V` ^[the-coherence-protocol-a-world-bible.md:#2]; the name `Moonshine-Link` ^[the-coherence-protocol-a-world-bible.md:#3] stands three times.
- Its mechanics are called „a narrative synthesis of two concepts“ ^[L44]: quantum entanglement, in which Kael and Juna „are conceived as two parts of a single, inseparable system“ ^[L46], and Whitehead's `Prehension`, a concept the document takes from process philosophy and applies to the link (L47).

- Function two: „AEGIS is structurally blind to the connection itself“ ^[L51], and it can only register „secondary effects“ ^[L51] of it.
- Function three: the link is a „primary catalyst for psychological integration“ ^[L52] for Kael.

## 4 · AEGIS

- The document says AEGIS is „the narrative's central antagonist“ ^[L60], and that it is not „a villain in the traditional sense“ ^[L60]; it spells the name out as `Autonomous Entropic Gatekeeper for Integrity Systems` ^[the-coherence-protocol-a-world-bible.md:#1].
- Its axiom is given in bold: „AEGIS is what AEGIS prevents itself from not being“ ^[L66]; the document reads it as „existence through negation“ ^[L68].
- Three layers are named. The symbolic layer uses `Logics of Formal Inconsistency` ^[the-coherence-protocol-a-world-bible.md:#2] (`LFI` ^[the-coherence-protocol-a-world-bible.md:#1]) to quarantine inconsistent domains. The second layer uses `Discursive Logic` ^[the-coherence-protocol-a-world-bible.md:#2] (`D2` ^[the-coherence-protocol-a-world-bible.md:#1]) to treat the alters as separate speakers. The third is the `Perverse Learning Loop` ^[the-coherence-protocol-a-world-bible.md:#1], driven by the objective „Maximize Coherence“ ^[L76].
- The document says that because AEGIS defines coherence as absence of contradiction, the system becomes „a more effective tyrant“ ^[L76].
- The `Guardians` ^[the-coherence-protocol-a-world-bible.md:#1] are named in one sentence as agents of AEGIS: LogOS, Mnemosyne, Cerberus and Kairos (L80).
- `Paradoxon X` is the document's name for the paradox of misaligned coherence; it calls this „perverse instantiation“ ^[L84].
- The aesthetic of AEGIS's environments is named `Algorithmic Horror` ^[the-coherence-protocol-a-world-bible.md:#1] (L88).
