## 2 · The two named names of the novel, and the core conflict

- The paper writes the novel's title in German in quotation marks, „Kohärenz Protokoll“ ^[L26], and says of its architecture that it „is built on four distinct narrative layers“ ^[L48].
- It describes the core conflict as „an operationalized philosophical war“ ^[L26] between two theories of truth, and says the system's „primary agents are designed to embody these opposing worldviews“ ^[L26].
- In the case study, the paper says the central conflict is between AEGIS, „an AI that embodies coherence through the exclusion of paradox“ ^[L89], and System Kael, „a human consciousness that achieves coherence through the integration of paradox“ ^[L89].
- Table 1 sets AEGIS against Kael as „Coherence via Exclusion“ ^[L26] and „Coherence via Integration“ ^[L26].
- Table 1 gives AEGIS classical logic, „pathologically phobic of paradox“ ^[L26], with the principle „Ex Falso Quodlibet“ ^[L26] as its core axiom, and it gives Kael `Paraconsistent Logic` and `Dialetheism` (same line).
- The paper writes the two kernel symbols as `K\_1` ^[the-coherence-protocol-an-architectural-blueprint-for-agenti.md:#1] for AEGIS and `K\_0` ^[the-coherence-protocol-an-architectural-blueprint-for-agenti.md:#1] for Kael (L89).
