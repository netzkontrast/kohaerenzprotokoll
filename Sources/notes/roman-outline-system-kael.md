What this document says about the terms that matter in it. The document speaks as a plan for the writing of Teil 1, in the present tense, and cites one source for its content through glued reference numbers.

## 1 · What kind of text this is, and how it marks itself

- It names itself: „Plot-Blueprint entfaltet die narrative Struktur“ ^[L22] for the first part of the novel `System Kael`.
- Its own labels recur in each of the four parts: `Set the Scene` ^[roman-outline-system-kael.md:#4], `Plot Progression & Systemic Reaction` ^[roman-outline-system-kael.md:#4] and `Thematische Resonanz` ^[roman-outline-system-kael.md:#4], twelve in all, which is the `stance_marker_count`. **Observed:** each part also has a sub-heading on Kael's psychological state (L43, L86, L131, L176).
- The appendix is headed „Zu integrierende Referenzmatrizen“ ^[L203]. It recommends tables „im Forschungsmaterial enthaltenen Tabellen“ ^[L207] as reference tools for the scene work. The four tables are Tabelle 1 to Tabelle 4, introduced at L209, L225, L239 and L262.
- The reference list names one source: „Scifi-Roman mit KI schreiben“ ^[L287]. **Observed:** 30 glued reference numbers in the body (profile) stand for it; the sentences they end are therefore this outline's restatement of that source, and the outline does not say which statement is its own addition.
- It hedges the bearers of some Anteile: `möglicherweise` ^[roman-outline-system-kael.md:#2] and `könnte` ^[roman-outline-system-kael.md:#1], for example „könnte hier seinen Einfluss ausüben“ ^[L137], said of Isabelle.

## 2 · The driving conflict

- The outline reads the story as an inner process: „psychologischer Integrationsprozess“ ^[L22] following „archetypischen Mustern einer Heldinnenreise“ ^[L22].
- It names the engine of the plot the K-Juna-Paradoxon: „Kaels Versuch, durch die Verbindung mit Juna psychologische Kohärenz zu finden“ ^[L24] is read by AEGIS „als katastrophale Zunahme an System-Entropie fehlinterpretiert“ ^[L24]. The term `K-Juna-Paradoxon` stands once (L24).
- AEGIS's attempts to correct this „sabotieren nicht nur Kaels Heilung“ ^[L24].
- The part labels give the themes: „Kontrolle vs. Akzeptanz“ ^[L67] in Teil I.

## 3 · The four Kern-Welten and their Wächter

- Each world is introduced as the domain of a Wächter and the externalisation of a part of Kael's psyche. KW1 is „Domäne des Wächters“ ^[L37] LogOS; the same sentence calls it a „Externalisierung von Kaels“ ^[L37] ANPs, the `Anscheinend Normalen Persönlichkeitsanteilen` of L37.
- KW4 has two Wächter: „Domäne der Wächter“ ^[L170] Kairos (Potenzial) and Sophia (Synthese), the other three worlds one each (Mnemosyne in KW2, Cerberus in KW3, both at L80 and L125).
- Tabelle 2 assigns to each world a `Guardian Blind Spot` ^[roman-outline-system-kael.md:#1] and a manifestation of the Risse and of the Juna-Echo; the title of Tabelle 2 writes „Guardians“ ^[L225] while the body text says `Wächter` (7 times standing alone).
- `Überwelt` stands once, in the Kapitel 13 bullet, for the breaking space the group crosses on the way to the AEGIS hub (L191).

## 4 · The Anteile and the figures

- The host: „Der Host-Anteil Kael agiert als pragmatischer Manager“ ^[L47].
- Lex is described in the state sub-heading as an Anteil of rigid system logic (L47). In Kapitel 2 the text meets him differently: „Dort trifft er auf den Archivar“ ^[L57] Lex. See section 6.
- Kiko: „Der kindliche Angst-Anteil“ ^[L90] manifests as the figure Echo.
- Argus: „Argus, eine Manifestation seines metakognitiven“ ^[L102] perfectionist Anteil.
- Thorne: Kael is sent to a therapy session with Dr. Thorne, „einer KI-Entität“ ^[L59]. Thorne classes the Juna connection as „gefährliche emotionale Inkohärenz“ ^[L59].
- Nyx and Alex are named with roles, „Nyx (Kämpfer)“ ^[L135] and „Alex (Beschützer)“ ^[L135], and the text says they gather in Silas only possibly (see `möglicherweise` above).
- Selene is the regulating Anteil whose influence opens KW4 (L180). The outcome of the cooperation is „Wir-Geflecht“ ^[L190], a functional, cooperative Multiplizität, formed in Kapitel 12 at the `Inneren Rat`.
- Kael comes to see his multiplicity as a „Mosaik-Herz“ ^[L180].
