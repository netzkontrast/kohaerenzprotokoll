## 2 · AEGIS

- The document calls AEGIS „the story's primary antagonist“ ^[L19]
- It gives AEGIS an identity by negation, in quotation marks of its own: „AEGIS is what AEGIS prevents from not being“ ^[L21]
- Its guiding principle is given in German inside parentheses: „Kohärenz statt Wahrheit“ ^[L22]
- Its central flaw is named: „Paradox of Misaligned Coherence“ ^[L23]

## 3 · Kael and Functional Multiplicity

- Kael is the protagonist, whose goal is „a state of co-awareness, communication, and cooperation between all his identities“ ^[L30]
- The document grounds his structure in a model it calls „the real-world psychological model“ ^[L103] — the Theory of Structural Dissociation of the Personality.
- Lex is placed among the Apparently Normal Parts: „the analytical and logical Alter“ ^[L105]
- Nyx and Kiko are placed among the Emotional Parts: „the protector/fighter“ ^[L106] and „the frightened child“ ^[L106]

## 4 · The world: Foundation, Void, Core Worlds

- The Foundation is described as what it is not: „is not a physical place, a deity, or an object to be found“ ^[L39]
- The Void is named with a second term: „Nothingness Noise“ ^[L43]; it is „the primordial state from which AEGIS first emerged“ ^[L43]
- The Core Worlds are „distinct simulated realities created by AEGIS as controlled environments“ ^[L47]
- Each Core World gets a purpose in the table; the second one „Represents trauma, memory, and subjectivity“ ^[L53]

## 5 · Juna/V and the link

- Juna/V is not presented as an ordinary figure: „is not a traditional character“ ^[L121]
- She is called an „ontological exploit“ ^[L124]
- The link is introduced as „the name given to the mysterious, non-local connection between Kael and Juna/V“ ^[L128]
- The document says the entanglement concept „is used as a metaphor“ ^[L130]
