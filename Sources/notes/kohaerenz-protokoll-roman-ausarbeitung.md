# Note — Kohärenz Protokoll: Roman-Ausarbeitung

What this document says about the terms that matter in it. Every quotation carries its file line on the same line of this note; a number about the whole document is a count mark that `quotes.py` checks. Where the note says **observed**, no quotation is possible (a structure, a gap) and the claim rests on the lines or counts it names. The document is a plan and a set of recommendations: its sentences about the novel say what the novel is to do, and the bracket `Query Pt` ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-roman-ausarbeitung.md:#32] marks statements it takes from the commissioning request.

## 1 · What kind of text this is, and how it marks itself

- The document gives its purpose as a foundation for the novel project: „Das vorliegende Dokument dient der analytischen Fundierung und strategischen Ausarbeitung des Romanprojekts“ ^[L15].
- It names itself a report that offers a framework: „Dieser Bericht liefert einen strukturierten analytischen Rahmen“ ^[L19].
- It answers a request of eight points: „acht zentralen Punkte der Auftragsanfrage“ ^[L19].
- **Observed:** the body alternates between a definition part, whose sentences end in glued reference numbers (308 by the profile; the list at L491 to L726 has 234 entries, each ending `Zugriff am April` ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-roman-ausarbeitung.md:#234]), and an application part that opens with `Relevanz für` ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-roman-ausarbeitung.md:#13]. The definition parts report the references; the application parts are the document's own proposals for the novel.
- Statements taken from the request carry the bracket: „Diese Dichotomie ist explizit als zentraler Konflikt benannt“ ^[L53]. The words in such a sentence are the request's report, which the document passes on.
- The worked example of the chapter concept is marked as supposed by its heading, „Beispielhafte Anwendung (Hypothetisch)“ ^[L412].

## 2 · Kael, AEGIS and the pair of them

- The document puts Kael's task as the restoration of a whole experience: „Kaels Reise zielt auf die Wiederherstellung eines integrierten“ ^[L33].
- It says of AEGIS in the same line: „AEGIS repräsentiert eine materialistische, computationalistische Sichtweise“ ^[L33].
- It describes the identity question for Kael as: „Kael kämpft um die Wiederherstellung seiner numerischen Identität“ ^[L49].
- AEGIS is given a motive drawn from the lens of autopoiesis: „AEGIS' primärer Antrieb ist nicht Bosheit, sondern Autopoiesis“ ^[L147].
- The document, citing the request, ties AEGIS's strength to its weakness: „Gerade die Stärke von AEGIS“ ^[L159]. It names the failures of AEGIS the „Risse“ in a heading ^[L157] and lists how they are to show.
- It reads AEGIS as a figure of a systemic tragedy: „ist das Ergebnis einer systemischen Tragödie“ ^[L181].
- For the chapters it plans a view from AEGIS: „Die pro Kapitel vorgesehenen“ ^[L149] sections called `Einblicke in AEGIS` ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-roman-ausarbeitung.md:#1], which the sentence reports from the request.

- It gives the four their headings `Co₁` ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-roman-ausarbeitung.md:#7] with `Ordnung/Order` ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-roman-ausarbeitung.md:#1] (L191), `McL` ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-roman-ausarbeitung.md:#5] with `Netzwerk/Network` ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-roman-ausarbeitung.md:#1] (L201), B with `Chaos/Schatten` ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-roman-ausarbeitung.md:#3] (L211) and `Ly` ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-roman-ausarbeitung.md:#6] with `Potential/Potentialität` ^[kohaerenz-protokoll-roman-ausarbeitung.md:#1] (L221).

- It states that the worlds are active: „Die Kernwelten sind somit keine passiven Hintergründe, sondern psycho-aktive Umgebungen“ ^[L254].
- Citing the request, it sets further worlds behind the four: „Die Existenz der weiteren Welten sollte subtil angedeutet werden“ ^[L247]. The sentence carries a digit before „Welten“ that the quotation check drops. As examples of how AEGIS's logs could name them the document writes „Cache C₁₅ zeigt erhöhte Entropie“ ^[L249] and „Synchronisationsfehler zwischen C₃ und C₁₁“ ^[L249], and as a direct mention „einer von zwanzig Realitäts-Caches“ ^[L252].
