# Note — Narrative Techniken für Kohärenz Protokoll

What this document says about the terms that matter in it. Every quotation carries its file line on the same line of this note; a number about the whole document is a count mark that `quotes.py` checks. Where the note says **observed**, no quotation is possible (a structure, a gap) and the claim rests on the lines or counts it names. The document advises and proposes; it does not narrate, and almost every statement about the novel is a recommendation or a mapping it offers, not a fact of the story.

## 1 · What kind of text this is, and how it marks itself

- The document gives its own purpose: „Dieser Bericht dient als umfassende Ressource“ ^[L17].
- **Observed:** each subsection reports concepts from outside texts under a „Kernkonzepte“ label (for example ^[L31]) and then applies them to the novel; the working verbs of the application are `sollte` ^[narrative-techniken-fuer-kohaerenz-protokoll.md:#46] and `könnte` ^[narrative-techniken-fuer-kohaerenz-protokoll.md:#38], both whole-word, case-sensitive counts.
- The document reports a blueprint it does not contain: „Die Blaupause sieht die Möglichkeit vor“ ^[L41]. Everything it says about what the `Blaupause` requires is its report of another text; whole-word counts: `Blaupause` ^[narrative-techniken-fuer-kohaerenz-protokoll.md:#25] and the compound `Kapitel-Blaupause` ^[narrative-techniken-fuer-kohaerenz-protokoll.md:#4].
- The document recommends use alongside the blueprint: „werden folgende strategische Empfehlungen gegeben“ ^[L497].

## 2 · Perspective and voice: Kael, AEGIS, the narrator

- The document says the blueprint allows several perspectives: „Die Blaupause sieht die Möglichkeit vor“ ^[L41]. The document's own proposal for AEGIS' voice: „Die Stimme sollte hier konsistent analytisch, kühl und präzise sein“ ^[L45].
- The document reports that the blueprint mentions a log format: „Das in der Blaupause erwähnte Log-Format“ ^[L45].
- The document proposes that fragmented voice and Kael's perspective together make the story embody the incoherence: „verkörpert die Erzählung selbst die Inkohärenz“ ^[L44].
- The document says the planned language switch must enter the voice for every perspective: „muss in die Gestaltung der narrativen Stimme für jede Perspektive integriert werden“ ^[L49].

- **Observed:** the guardian list at L381 writes `McL` among the world principles, in „Beziehung/Netzwerk für McL“ ^[L381]; the three worlds above are the ones the bullets L61 to L63 label as `Kernwelt`.

## 5 · M, Monstergruppe/M and the integrated state

- The document heads a bullet „Monstergruppe/M (Implizite Komplexität/Holismus)“ ^[L89], and says of M: „Die Natur von M“ ^[L89] is complexity, irreducibility and integrated wholeness (the line's own parenthesis).

- It sets AEGIS against M as reductionism against holism: „AEGIS (Reduktionismus)“ ^[L220] and „AEGIS ist analytisch und kontrollierend“ ^[L225], the latter in a sentence that reports the blueprint as its source.

## 6 · Moonshine and the K-J Verbindung

- It also offers a second interpretation: „könnte als eine dionysische, intuitive Kraft interpretiert werden“ ^[L176], a lens reading it proposes.
