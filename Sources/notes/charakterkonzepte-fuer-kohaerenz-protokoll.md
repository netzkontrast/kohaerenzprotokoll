# Notes — Charakterkonzepte für Kohärenz Protokoll

**This is a character bible, not a report of what the manuscript does.** Every
one of the ten Alters and eleven Nebencharaktere gets the same field set —
Rolle/Funktion, Psychologie (Ziele/Motivationen/Ängste/Bedürfnisse/Weltbild/
Prägende Traumata/Blinder Fleck), Beziehungen, Manifestation/Erscheinung,
Welten-Link, Themen-Link, Arc-Potenzial — filled once per character rather than
stated once as a schema. `profile.py`'s repeated-label counter only catches
three of these labels at ×3 because the rest recur inside distinct prose blocks,
not as literally identical lines ^[terms:structural profile].

**191 hedging words in 15,436** and 52 question marks run through it
^[terms:stance]. This document proposes a cast; it does not assert one.

## `Wächter` never names the Guardians — it names four different, smaller things

The document's only proper name for AEGIS's five collective agents is the
English loanword `Guardians` — first introduced at line 148, "Die Guardians
(Systemagenten: LogOS, Mnemosyne, Cerberus, Kairos, Sophia)". The German root
`Wächter`/`Wärter` is never applied to that collective. Instead it surfaces four
times, for four unrelated referents:

> „Limina (Die Wächterin / Schwellenhüterin)" ^[L263] — an Alter's own epithet,
> not a Guardian.

> „…oder als bedrohliche Wächterfiguren an den Grenzen seines Bereichs." ^[L181]
> — one possible visual manifestation of the Guardian Cerberus, not a name for
> the class of Guardians.

> „Kann in KW1 als bürokratischer Kontrolleur, in KW3 als aggressiver Wächter
> auftreten." ^[L551] — the minor character Einheit 734 (Regel-Exekutor), one of
> two possible local guises.

> „Kann sich als Krieger, Wächter oder starkes Tier sehen." ^[L436] — Praetor's
> (an Alter's) own internal self-image, one option among three.

A fifth, related compound is Terminus's epithet `Der Grenzwärter`, line 575, and
the document glosses it itself as a smaller-scale stand-in for one Guardian:

> „Repräsentiert die Funktion von Cerberus auf einer spezifischeren Ebene."
> ^[L578]

So this document gives no support for treating `Wächter` as the term for the
collective AEGIS agents — every occurrence of the root names something smaller
and more local than the Guardians, and the closest the document comes to
equating one is to say explicitly that Terminus is Cerberus's local expression,
not a Wächter in his own right.

## `Echo` names two unrelated things

The Alter `Echo (Der Kleine)`, a Child Alter with a full ten-field profile
starting at line 315, and — separately — `Echo / Lichtfunke`, one of two
proposed names for `Junas Fragment`, a manifestation of Juna in a Kern-Welt:

Line 605 titles the profile "9. Junas Fragment (Manifestation in einer
Kern-Welt)"; its very next line gives the proposed name:

> „Name: Echo / Lichtfunke (oder ähnlich)" ^[L606]

Both are candidates in `03-candidates.md`; only reading the cited lines
separates them, since a plain string count folds them together.

## AEGIS's acronym, spelled out once

> „AEGIS (Autonomous Entropic Gatekeeper for Integrity Systems) ist das
> fundamentale Betriebssystem oder das grundlegende Gesetz, das die Überwelt und
> die darin eingebetteten Kern-Welten regiert." ^[L118]

Not repeated elsewhere in the document. Whether this expansion agrees with,
extends, or conflicts with any existing reading on the AEGIS page is a
reconciliation question — not answered here, since a census/note may not
consult the wiki.

## `Kohärenz Protokoll` used in-world, once, for something other than the novel

Five of the document's seven occurrences of `Kohärenz Protokoll` are its own
title or header text. One is plainly the novel (lines 659 and 671, in the
phrase Anwendung auf `Kohärenz Protokoll`). One is different:

> „AEGIS wird ausschließlich durch seine Kernprogrammierung, das "Kohärenz
> Protokoll", angetrieben." ^[L124]

Here `Kohärenz Protokoll` is AEGIS's own core programming, in-world, not the
title of the surrounding document. Flagged as a second reading of the phrase,
not merged with the first.

## The central conflict, stated once and plainly

> „Der grundlegende Antagonismus zwischen Juna (Verbindung, Emotion, das
> 'Echte') und AEGIS (Isolation, Logik, Kontrolle) dient als zentrale
> Externalisierung von Kaels tiefstem inneren Konflikt: Soll er sich der
> schmerzhaften, aber potenziell heilenden Verbindung zur Wahrheit und zu seinen
> Anteilen öffnen, oder soll er sich der scheinbar sicheren, aber letztlich
> leblosen Ordnung des Systems unterwerfen?" ^[L687]

## AEGIS's blind spot, tied explicitly to a named cybernetic law

> „Dies steht im Einklang mit kybernetischen Prinzipien wie Ashby's Law of
> Requisite Variety: AEGIS fehlt die notwendige interne Komplexität ('variety'),
> um die Komplexität des von ihm kontrollierten Systems (Kaels Psyche) effektiv
> zu steuern." ^[L128]

## The Guardians' isolation from each other is attributed to a named protocol

> „Aufgrund der segmentierten Natur des Systems (Zero-Trust-Protokolle, Kontext
> Pt 2) haben sie möglicherweise nur begrenztes Bewusstsein voneinander und
> operieren weitgehend isoliert in ihren jeweiligen Domänen…" ^[L205]

This is the same `Zero-Trust` surface the skill's own reasoning names as a
known false-conflict risk (`.claude/skills/ingest/SKILL.md`) — recorded here as
a claim this document makes, not as a judgement about any other document's use
of the term.

## The one-Alter-one-Kern-Welt mapping is provisional even in the document's own table

Kael's world-link is asserted plainly (line 502, Konstrukt-Stadt / KW1), but
the same summary table hedges every other Alter it locates:

> „Limina | Gatekeeper | Alterslos, analytisch | Grenzfeste (KW3)? | …" ^[L503]

Eos (line 507), Index (line 510) and Silas (line 511) carry the same trailing
`?` on their `Primärer Kern-Welt Link` cell. So this document does not itself
commit to a clean one-alter-one-world mapping beyond Kael, Nox, Echo, Flicker,
Oblivion and Praetor (the six without a `?`).

## What this document does not settle

It proposes ten Alter names and role-types, eleven Nebencharaktere, and one
possible AEGIS-acronym expansion — all as first-pass casting decisions (the
qualifier `oder ähnlich` follows every proposed name) rather than assertions
about a finished manuscript. It is `T3-work` and `charaktere`, the first
document read from that category, so nothing here is compared against a
sibling document or the wiki.
