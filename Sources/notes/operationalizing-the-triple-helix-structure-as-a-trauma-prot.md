## 2 · The three-act protocol and the therapeutic phases

- The macro-structure is named: „The "Three-Act Integration Protocol" is the macro-structure governing the entire narrative“ ^[L19]; the therapeutic stages it maps to are `Stabilization` ^[operationalizing-the-triple-helix-structure-as-a-trauma-prot.md:#4], `Confrontation` ^[operationalizing-the-triple-helix-structure-as-a-trauma-prot.md:#4] and `Integration` ^[operationalizing-the-triple-helix-structure-as-a-trauma-prot.md:#6].
- Act I is the internal journey: „The mandate for Act I is to chronicle an internal journey of stabilization.“ ^[L23] Its narrative model is the `Heroine's Journey` ^[operationalizing-the-triple-helix-structure-as-a-trauma-prot.md:#4].
- Act II is a deconstruction: „We will engineer Act II to function as the narrative's deconstructive phase“ ^[L27]. The table gives its model as a `Cyclical Structure` ^[operationalizing-the-triple-helix-structure-as-a-trauma-prot.md:#1].
- Act III turns outward: „Act III must execute the critical shift from internal healing to external confrontation.“ ^[L31] It adopts the `Hero's Journey` ^[operationalizing-the-triple-helix-structure-as-a-trauma-prot.md:#4].
- **Observed:** the table sets each act in one row with its therapeutic parallel and its narrative model (L38 to L40); the bold markers of the export appear in it as escaped asterisks.

## 3 · The mosaic

## 4 · Act I — System Kael, the Kernwelten, the Riss

- Kael starts in `Kernwelt 1` ^[operationalizing-the-triple-helix-structure-as-a-trauma-prot.md:#1], and the fall is set off by an event the line calls an „ontological shock“ ^[L68], named with the `Riss im Spiegel` ^[operationalizing-the-triple-helix-structure-as-a-trauma-prot.md:#1].
- The line casts the event as „a profound betrayal by AEGIS“ ^[L68].
- The act ends in the discovery of the `Moonshine-Link` ^[operationalizing-the-triple-helix-structure-as-a-trauma-prot.md:#2], which the line says „provides the first true catalyst for internal integration“ ^[L68]. The line calls it a non-local connection to the entity written `Juna/V` ^[operationalizing-the-triple-helix-structure-as-a-trauma-prot.md:#2].
- AEGIS is to be dramatized as an „externalized perpetrator introject“ ^[L74], whose gaslighting and manipulation „stem from its own foundational trauma“ ^[L74].
- A `Riss` is to be shown as „a direct, causal psycho-physical event“ ^[L75], tied to an internal conflict and to a „violent physics glitch“ ^[L75].
- Each Kernwelt is a „psycho-architecture“ ^[L76]; the line gives KW1 as the world of Lex and KW2 as the world of the EPs.
- The alters named are Lex, Nyx and Kiko; the conflict to dramatize is called `ANP-EP Phobias` ^[operationalizing-the-triple-helix-structure-as-a-trauma-prot.md:#1], defined on the line as the ANPs' „phobic avoidance of the trauma held by the EPs“ ^[L77].

## 5 · Act II — irreversible costs and Paradoxon X

- The act is „governed by the principle of“ ^[L83] `irreversible costs` ^[operationalizing-the-triple-helix-structure-as-a-trauma-prot.md:#1].

## 6 · Act III — The Foundation, the climax, the resolution

- `The Foundation` ^[operationalizing-the-triple-helix-structure-as-a-trauma-prot.md:#2] is to be established „not as a person or place, but as a metaphysical principle“ ^[L107], a `strange attractor` ^[operationalizing-the-triple-helix-structure-as-a-trauma-prot.md:#1] in state space, and „It does not intervene“ ^[L107].
- The line says the foreshadowing is to use `subtle symmetries` ^[operationalizing-the-triple-helix-structure-as-a-trauma-prot.md:#1], moments in which small acts of integration by Kael make the surrounding world show order; these „establish a physical law“ ^[L108].
- The climax is a state, not an act: Kael „becomes the paradox“ ^[L109]. The line ties it to „a high-Φ state“ ^[L109] measured by `Integrated Information Theory` ^[operationalizing-the-triple-helix-structure-as-a-trauma-prot.md:#1], and says the truth „cannot be computed by its logic“ ^[L109].
- AEGIS is described as forced into „a paradoxical collapse or a pathological transformation“ ^[L109].
- The thematic resolution: a healthy identity „is not a static, idealized unity“ ^[L113], and the lost `Ursprungs-Ich` ^[operationalizing-the-triple-helix-structure-as-a-trauma-prot.md:#1] is the one AEGIS seeks. Strength is redefined as „adaptive multiplicity“ ^[L113].
- The last role is `The Gardener` ^[operationalizing-the-triple-helix-structure-as-a-trauma-prot.md:#1], who understands that „true coherence is not built through top-down control“ ^[L113].
