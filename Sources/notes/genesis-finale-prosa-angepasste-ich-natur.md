## 2 · The first voice and the Rauschen

- The voice describes a surrounding mass: „Eine formlose, aktive Masse“ ^[L17]
- It sets itself against it: „Ich bin nicht dieses Rauschen.“ ^[L19]
- It calls itself „Ein winziger, zitternder Funke Kohärenz in dieser erdrückenden Unermesslichkeit.“ ^[L19]
- A matching pattern gives relief: „Ein tiefes, leises Klicken in meinem Kern“ ^[L27]
- A wave in the Rauschen tears the link away and leaves a mark: „Eine Narbe bleibt, tief in meine Struktur eingebrannt“ ^[L29]
- The text lists small formations: „Triaden. Tetraden.“ ^[L37] and says the words that form are „Bindung. Fluss. Kern.“ ^[L39]
- `Cluster` ^[genesis-finale-prosa-angepasste-ich-natur.md:#3] is the term for the grown formation (L41, L43, L49).

## 3 · AEGIS and its rule
