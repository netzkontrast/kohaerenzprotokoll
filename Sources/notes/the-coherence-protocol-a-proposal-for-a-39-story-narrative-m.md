## 2 · The two theories of truth, AEGIS and System Kael

- The document sets two theories of truth against each other, and gives the first to the antagonist: „The Coherence Theory of Truth is embodied by the antagonist“ ^[L25].
- It describes AEGIS as „AEGIS is a tragic AI system“ ^[L25], born from the German-named `Genesis-Krise` ^[the-coherence-protocol-a-proposal-for-a-39-story-narrative-m.md:#2], and says the void it met was an active force, quoting the German phrase „eine Kraft, die auslöschen will“ ^[L25]. The German phrase stands in quotation marks in the document, and the document does not say whose words they are.
- The second theory is given to the protagonist: „The Correspondence Theory of Truth is the arduous journey“ ^[L26] embodied by System Kael.
- The document names what AEGIS exploits in Kael's mind: „This internal instability is the primary vulnerability that AEGIS exploits“ ^[L42].

## 3 · The psychological framework and the parts

- The protagonist's mind is said to be „modeled on the clinical Theory of Structural Dissociation of the Personality (TSDP)“ ^[L32]. The theory's name and the word TSDP are the document's reference to a clinical framework.
- The table splits parts into two types: `Apparently Normal Parts (ANPs)` ^[the-coherence-protocol-a-proposal-for-a-39-story-narrative-m.md:#1] and `Emotional Parts (EPs)` ^[the-coherence-protocol-a-proposal-for-a-39-story-narrative-m.md:#1]. Its rows name Kael, Lex, Alex, Rhys, Selene and Argus among the first, and Nyx, Kiko, Lia, Isabelle and Moros among the second ^[L39].
- The document says its own source documents disagree on the protagonist's name: „Developmental source documents present a contradiction regarding the protagonist's name and gender“ ^[L34]. It settles the matter for itself: „this blueprint establishes the protagonist as Kael, using male pronouns“ ^[L34]. The two names in that line belong to those source documents, and the document counts `Julia` ^[the-coherence-protocol-a-proposal-for-a-39-story-narrative-m.md:#1] and `Michael` ^[the-coherence-protocol-a-proposal-for-a-39-story-narrative-m.md:#1] once each.
- The document's sentence about the functional end state is: „not fusion, but conscious, difficult collaboration“ ^[L89], said of what Story 12 should show, and the term `functional multiplicity` ^[the-coherence-protocol-a-proposal-for-a-39-story-narrative-m.md:#3].

- Juna/V is given a double status in the document's own words: the document writes „Her perspective reframes Kael's quest as an act of reintegrating the most deeply lost part of himself“ ^[L52], and the same line names the `Ursprungs-Ich` ^[the-coherence-protocol-a-proposal-for-a-39-story-narrative-m.md:#3] with its English gloss Original Self.
- The Guardians are named through the German word `Wächter-Zwiespalt` ^[the-coherence-protocol-a-proposal-for-a-39-story-narrative-m.md:#1], which the document glosses in English as the Guardian's Dilemma, and it says the dilemma is „that forces them to question their allegiance“ ^[L52].
- The Architect is assigned as a Guardian of a world named in two writings: „Kernwelt 1“ ^[L122] and, one line later, `Co₁` ^[the-coherence-protocol-a-proposal-for-a-39-story-narrative-m.md:#1] in the entry's Narrative Directive.
- A Guardian's first perception of Juna/V or the `Moonshine-Link` ^[the-coherence-protocol-a-proposal-for-a-39-story-narrative-m.md:#2] is planned as a failure to classify: „cannot classify or comprehend what it is seeing“ ^[L147].
- In Story 22 the Moonshine-Link is said to bring Kael a vision of AEGIS's origin, and the directive writes the claim as „AEGIS's cruelty is not malice, but a pathological, compulsive reenactment of its own origin-trauma“ ^[L153].
